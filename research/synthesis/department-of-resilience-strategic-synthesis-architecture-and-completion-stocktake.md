Every chapter must close the same five gates:

1. **Requirement:** a public or strategic question and falsifiable denominator are explicit.
2. **Architecture:** one controlling answer is selected and superseded answers remain traceable.
3. **Quantification:** at least one reconciled quantity retains unit, scope, horizon and evidence state.
4. **Integration:** adjacent institutional, force, technological and fiscal decisions reconcile.
5. **Reader asset:** the chapter works without requiring pass chronology or repository archaeology.

A chapter is complete only when all five close. Strong prose cannot compensate for a missing denominator; a model cannot compensate for a missing argument; a diagram cannot substitute for evidence.

## What becomes the manuscript

The [strategic study](strategic-study.md) is the publication spine. The root [README](../../README.md) remains the reader map, not a second manuscript. The [active frontier](../frontier.md) states current uncertainty. The research log records sequence, the supersession map records reversal, and individual papers and workbooks remain the reconstructable basis for claims. One claim has one controlling chapter and may have many supporting decisions; it may not acquire authority by repetition.

Twelve compression rules govern the rewrite. Lead with the latest controlling decision; retain an earlier result when it explains a reversal; do not use chronology as causal exposition; make evidence state travel with every quantity; never promote design, cost or shared service into observation; keep failure visible when it changes the surviving architecture; assign one owner to each top-level claim; reconcile denominators across finance, workforce, force and technology; treat international capacity as an invited service rather than domestic surplus; and render only after the representation class, requirements and evidence state are explicit.

The strongest four chapters establish the central proposition, public-service contract, Departmental constitution and force grammar. The largest substantive deficit is international projection: the repository has concurrency and consent rules, but not yet a full strategic doctrine of partnership, host sovereignty, logistics, non-recall, local industrial participation and exit. The largest editorial deficit is the integrated synthesis itself. The visual atlas correctly remains at zero until form follows requirement and the study can distinguish a concept plate from a tested configuration.
