# Technical visual atlas constitution

**Authority:** `TVA-0`, Pass 249  
**Status:** representation requirement and plate architecture closed; plates not yet issued  
**Scope:** final reader atlas for the integrated strategic study  
**Rule:** a visual may clarify an earned relationship but may not manufacture technical, evidentiary or production maturity

## Why the atlas exists

The Department of Resilience is difficult to understand because its strategic value arises from relationships rather than a single iconic platform. The atlas must make four things visible:

1. the causal path from public promise to accepted civilian effect;
2. the difference among institution, mission system, formation, platform and receiver;
3. the physical accounts and failure boundaries that shape purpose-built systems; and
4. the existence of serious rival forms before evidence permits selection.

The atlas is not promotional art, a fleet catalog or an acquisition announcement. It is a compact visual argument. Every plate must help a senior reader answer either **what must be true**, **how the pieces connect**, **where the system can fail**, or **which rival physical forms remain live**.

## Representation classes

| Class | What it may depict | Required content | What it may not imply |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PUB-0` | Editorial synthesis of already controlling relationships | source path, exact denominators, evidence-state label | new technical authority or measured performance |
| `REP-0` | Public-service requirement and boundary | population/function, service, deadline, duration, damage, rights | a machine or selected solution |
| `REP-1` | Causal topology | flows, interfaces, receiver, safe stop, weakest link | closed geometry or buildability |
| `REP-2` | Instrumented build-to-test section | root ID, configuration owner, conserved accounts, cut sets, instrumentation, unknowns | as-built article or favorable result |
| `REP-3` | Equal-salience rival whole-form set | at least three live forms, shared requirement, unlike failure logic, no winner | preferred exterior, procurement baseline or fleet |
| `REP-4` | As-built experimental record | actual serial, configuration hash, measurements and deviations | **held: no qualifying article exists** |
| `REP-5` | Evidence-qualified candidate | accepted event chain and bounded inference | **held: no candidate has qualified** |
| `REP-6` | Complete mission system | multi-family service proof and operational acceptance | **held: no complete system has qualified** |
| `REP-7` | Production and fleet depiction | admitted configuration, serial state and fielded inventory | **held: no production fleet is authorized** |

`PUB-0` is an editorial class, not a shortcut around the representation ladder. It may restate a controlling table or topology; it cannot introduce a machine, geometry, performance figure or causal relation absent from the corpus.

## Visual grammar

All plates use one restrained publication system:

- landscape 16:9 master with a print-safe inner frame;
- neutral background, dark structure, blue for public service, amber for energy, teal for water, red only for hazard or fatal cut sets, violet for protected civil state and gray for held or unknown states;
- one title, one-sentence claim, main visual field, evidence strip and source footer;
- quantities displayed at no more than three significant figures unless copied as direct source arithmetic;
- human figures and communities shown at credible scale, never as decorative silhouettes that conceal access burdens;
- no military insignia, weapon cues, national triumphalism or humanitarian-victim spectacle;
- no photorealistic setting that implies an actual built or deployed article.

Technical plates may be richly rendered, but their richness must come from legible systems: cutaways, flow paths, handling envelopes, receiver interfaces, maintainability zones, residual routes and instrumentation—not cinematic atmosphere.

## Mandatory plate footer

Every plate carries this six-field footer:

```text
CLASS · CONFIGURATION OWNER · EVIDENCE STATE
CONTROLLING REQUIREMENT · PROHIBITED INFERENCE · SOURCE PATH
```

For Group B and C, the evidence state is `DESIGN / UNBUILT / UNMEASURED`. The prohibited inference is respectively `NOT AN AS-BUILT ARTICLE` or `NO FORM SELECTED`. Group A uses the editorial or planning state of its controlling source.

## Issue and quality gates

A plate issues only if all eight gates pass:

1. its claim already exists in the controlling manuscript or source;
2. its class is eligible and correctly labeled;
3. every displayed quantity reconciles to the controlling register;
4. all required service, receiver and residual boundaries are visible;
5. unknowns and fatal cut sets are at least as salient as attractive features;
6. rival forms receive equal visual treatment where required;
7. source, evidence state and prohibited inference are readable at publication size; and
8. an independent reader can state what the plate does **not** establish.

The atlas closes only when every plate passes, the complete set has no contradictory legend or denominator, and the manuscript links to the final files. Partial issue does not create partial rendering authority for omitted classes.

## Authority boundary

`TVA-0` closes atlas requirement, architecture, quantification and integration. It authorizes creation and editorial issue review for `A01`–`C06`; it does not declare any plate issued. It authorizes no `REP-4`–`REP-7` object, experimental fact, preferred form, procurement baseline, production program or fielded fleet.

The next and only remaining publication question is whether the twenty-two plates can be produced, audited as one set and linked into the manuscript without violating this constitution.
