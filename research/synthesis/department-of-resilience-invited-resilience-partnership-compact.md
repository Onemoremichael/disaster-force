## Decision

Adopt an **Invited Resilience Partnership Compact**. The United States does not export a temporary substitute government. It accepts a bounded public-service obligation, enters through host-controlled receivers, keeps civil purpose separate from technical and operational authority, and exits only after service and sovereignty close together.

A standing compact permits preparation but does not activate the force. Only an authenticated sovereign request followed by U.S. acceptance binds a campaign. Disaster severity, silence, a treaty, available equipment or military access cannot manufacture consent. Where no competent civil authority can invite service, the Department cannot invite itself; any protective intervention requires a separate political and legal instrument and cannot be relabeled as invited resilience.

Eighteen compact clauses govern invitation, purpose, civil admission, technical safety, rights, family-complete reservation, dual corridors, separate security, non-recall, effect, data, evidence, finance, partner production, transfer, residuals, exit and public accounting. Fourteen exclusive authorities issue the corresponding artifacts. No new sixth house or international super-command is created.

## The service promise

Two design campaigns carry 334 Indo-Pacific and 341 Atlantic/Americas formation-equivalent commitments. The 675 total commitments require 1,350 primary and differently caused alternate corridor claims. Seven segments per corridor produce 9,450 reservations from origin through reverse flow and reset. These are three unlike units: campaigns test strategic concurrency, formations claim force, and corridors govern access.

Each campaign contracts all nine strategic effects separately: catastrophe denial, economic continuity, alliance reliability, partner sovereignty, industrial mobilization, technology competition, standards power, democratic legitimacy and strategic restraint. Every record must state baseline, population, geography, service floor, deadline, duration, distribution, externalities and evidence path before acceptance. Money spent, mass delivered, equipment transferred, favorable attention and aggregate avoided loss remain prohibited substitutes.

The forward posture remains a host-owned receiver commons: two networks, twelve sovereign districts, thirty-six unlike precincts, 842 family-specific reception slots, 192 partner production roots, ninety-six public assurance cells and twelve academies. Eighteen days of bridge capacity cover request-to-arrival delay. The 0.878–1.49 Mt forward-material range remains a planning boundary pending item-level overlap, not a stockpile order.[^1]

## Non-recall is the credibility mechanism

Before U.S. acceptance, invited capacity is allocatable. Acceptance plus family-complete reservation converts exact serials into a bilateral obligation. A later domestic catastrophe draws the 502-formation protected reserve and discloses any uncovered deficit; it does not recall accepted foreign service.

A technical stop suspends unsafe operation but does not dissolve custody. Host revocation stops new issue and orders safe transfer or withdrawal but does not create a unilateral American recall right. Only bilateral release ends the service obligation, and released serials return to allocatable stock only after regeneration and requalification. Replacement is lawful if service continuity and configuration equivalence remain intact; substitution that merely frees the original serial is not.

## Sovereign finance, industry and knowledge

The United States pre-funds standing readiness. Host inability to pay cannot terminate accepted life-safety service, create debt leverage or compel a tied purchase. Marginal campaign cost, title transfer and recovery finance remain public, separately accounted transactions. Reference partner-market activity never offsets appropriation or readiness requirements.

The USD1.28T receiver-commons capital and USD102B/year operating screens remain nested in Department accounts. The USD67.5B activation system is nested inside the receiver estate; the USD44.6B evidence system is nested inside activation. The USD150B strategic-power technology jurisdiction is inside the USD1.00T technology program. None is additive.[^2]

Partner production is a second sovereign production system, not a foreign extension of U.S. readiness. Every network must pass ten gates: domestic reserve conserved; separate serials; recipient civil control; accepted custody, safety and rights; funded configuration support; repair and evidence rights; trained workforce; independent ancestry; no mandatory U.S. key, cloud, supplier or operator; and funded residual return. Partner output earns no U.S. formation credit.

Four knowledge planes travel differently. Open physical reality is jointly releasable. Protected civil state remains with its originating host or person-authorized custodian. Ephemeral service state contains only expiring incident fields. Sealed evidence permits independent replay but not operational population search. Fourteen civil rights, including service without permanent identity, correction, purpose expiry, sovereign custody and remedy, bind all four.

## Transfer and exit

Eleven states run from standing compact through request, service vector, acceptance, reservation, bridge, employment, effect acceptance, transfer, bilateral release and regeneration. Exit requires nine conjunctive closures: contracted effects; rights and remedy; host operating authority; workforce; spares and configuration support; data return or expiry; title and finance; residuals and end of life; and U.S. release with regeneration. Equipment handover, ceremony, departure or market sale closes none by itself.

This doctrine redirects innovation. Priority technologies include partition-tolerant treaty compilers, machine-verifiable dual-key custody, offline bilateral operating pictures, reversible receiver utilities, purpose-expiring civil-data enclaves, partner microfactories and portable metrology, noncoercion monitors, autonomous residual accounting and exit-state compilers. The military analogue is not a platform. It is an engineered ability to keep a difficult promise without acquiring political control.
