# Synthesis

Synthesis states the best integrated answer the research can currently support. It is organized by the logic of resilience power, not by pass number, platform family, or folder inventory.

Read [Disaster Force: resilience as a coequal instrument of national power](strategic-study.md) for the present strategic judgment. The [canonical force allocation](../force/generation/canonical-force-allocation-and-fiscal-reconciliation.md) supplies the current Department-scale quantitative boundary. The [active frontier](../frontier.md) separates that durable synthesis from the live experiment and next decision. Consult [research history](../history/README.md) for how the judgment developed and [research assurance](../proving/assurance/README.md) for the evidence needed to strengthen or overturn it.

Pass 246 replaces accumulation as the completion standard. The [strategic synthesis architecture and completion stocktake](department-of-resilience-strategic-synthesis-architecture-and-completion-stocktake.md) defines fourteen final chapters and five noncompensatory gates per chapter. Forty-nine of seventy gates close: 70.0% editorial completion, with international doctrine, cross-chapter reconciliation, integrated synthesis and the held technical atlas carrying the largest burdens.
