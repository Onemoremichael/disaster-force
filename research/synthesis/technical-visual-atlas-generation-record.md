# Technical Visual Atlas Generation and Provenance Record

**Record:** `TVA-GR-1`  
**Issue:** Pass 250  
**Mode:** built-in image generation for six new bitmap section studies; deterministic SVG authoring for ten system diagrams; audited reissue for six corrected rival-form plates

This record makes the image-production step reproducible and rejects any inference that the generation process supplied engineering evidence. The prompts asked for concept sections, not photorealistic proof. No API model identifier or unrecorded performance parameter is claimed.

## Shared prompt constitution

All six Group B requests used a landscape technical concept-illustration grammar: orthographic or three-quarter cutaway; pale neutral field; restrained navy, teal, amber and safety-red accents; credible human scale; visible structure, flows, access, residual routes and instrument points; no cinematic atmosphere; no brand, military insignia, flag, weapon or promotional slogan; and no claim of as-built status.

## Final prompt set

### B01 — theater entry

> Create a landscape 16:9 technical concept plate, not a photograph, for an unbuilt purpose-built civil disaster-response “pelagic exchange” test object. Show a large twin-body coastal service carrier in clean three-quarter cutaway, with two genuinely independent heavy-service paths, modular water/power/cargo cells, cranes, a degraded-anchorage system, a replaceable shore-transition test fixture, neighborhood utility discharge and a clear reverse-logistics/residual path. Make candidate-owned hardware and public test-fixture hardware visually distinguishable by material tone. Show a few credible workers for scale, instrument points, flow arrows without text, safety isolation zones and maintainability access. Pale warm-gray background, dark navy structure, teal water, amber energy, red only for fatal cut sets. Serious engineering illustration, restrained, highly detailed, no cinematic ocean, no flag, no military insignia, no weapons, no logos, no readable labels, no implication that it has been built.

### B02 — civil terrain

> Create a landscape 16:9 technical concept plate, not a photograph, for an unbuilt civil-terrain disaster-response test system. Show a cutaway of occupied damaged urban ground with a coordinated family of low-ground-pressure tracked work cells: excavation, debris separation, bridge/path placement, pipe deployment and accessible building-entry support. Include autonomous sensing and remote operation but also visible manual authority stations and workers. Show buried utilities, unstable soil, contaminated spoil custody, an accessible temporary route, exclusion boundaries, instrument points and replaceable public test terrain. Candidate hardware and public fixture ground must be visibly different materials. Pale neutral background, navy machines, teal service lines, amber power, red only for hazard/cut sets. Orthographic three-quarter engineering illustration, high detail, no cinematic destruction, no military cues, no logos, no readable text, no implication of a built fleet.

### B03 — hazard control

> Create a landscape 16:9 technical concept plate, not a photograph, for an unbuilt purpose-built atmospheric hazard-control aircraft test object. Show a very large high-endurance civil aircraft in three-quarter cutaway with separated suppressant tanks, redundant pump and delivery paths, high-temperature/high-particulate propulsion protection, wide-area sensing apertures, refill interfaces, crew refuge and independently safeable sections. Below it show a sparse public test range with sensor towers, a controlled plume/fire-line surrogate, mobile refill cell and ground safety control, clearly not an actual disaster. Use arrows and colored paths without readable labels to reveal matter, energy, information and contaminated-residual custody. Include instrument points and workers for scale. Pale neutral field, navy structure, teal suppressant, amber energy, red only for hazard and fatal cut sets. Precise engineering concept illustration, no dramatic flames, weapons, flags, insignia, logos or claim of measured performance.

### B04 — lifeline restoration

> Create a landscape 16:9 technical concept plate, not a photograph, for an unbuilt firm-energy and rapid-utility restoration test system. Show a cutaway geothermal or other firm-source field with two independent bore/source loops, modular conversion and black-start islands, separate heat rejection, independently valved water/power/thermal distribution trunks, receiver isolation skids and swarms laying temporary pipe and cable through damaged civil terrain. Make every branch independently safeable and show a public receiver test block, measurement stations, manual controls, maintenance access, flow paths and residual/working-fluid custody. Avoid one central active header. Pale neutral background, dark navy structure, teal fluids, amber energy, red only for fatal cut sets, violet protected receiver state. Detailed orthographic engineering illustration, credible human scale, no dramatic disaster, no logos, no readable labels, no implication of a proven plant.

### B05 — human continuity, corrected issue

> Edit the prior human-continuity concept plate by removing the red cross emblem and any protected, medical-organization, national, military, company, institutional, or brand symbol. Replace the emblem with a plain colored orientation panel containing no icon or text. Keep the twin-hull floating clinical test object, separated clinical towers, cutaway decks, patient and supply routes, decontamination boundary, independent utility paths, waste custody, receiving pier and the existing restrained technical illustration style. Do not add any logo, flag, wordmark, readable label, weapon, or claim that this unbuilt concept is an actual hospital or protected medical unit.

The rejected first B05 generation used the same technical brief but requested a “universal civil-care emblem.” It produced a protected red-cross-like mark and was excluded before publication. Only the corrected, emblem-free asset is retained.

### B06 — regeneration

> Create a landscape 16:9 technical concept plate, not a photograph, for an unbuilt disaster-force regeneration test system. Show two parallel rail-compatible mobile industrial trainlets at a damaged civil logistics yard, each with separate feedstock quarantine, robotic disassembly, material assay, cleaning, additive and subtractive manufacture, heat treatment, metrology, qualification and output custody. Include intake of damaged pumps/vehicles/utility modules, modular microfactory cells, independent power and heat rejection, a public reference lab, manual workstations, instrument points and an explicit unrecoverable-residual route. Reveal flow and ancestry with colored paths and symbols but no readable labels. Pale neutral background, navy structures, teal recoverable material, amber energy, red only for rejected/quarantined material. Precise three-quarter cutaway, credible workers for scale, no military cues, logos or implied production readiness.

## Acceptance record

All ten SVGs parse and render at 1,600 × 900. The six new B plates open as RGB PNGs at 1,672 × 941. The corrected lineage plates retain their native dimensions: C01 is 1,774 × 887 and C02–C06 are 1,536 × 1,024. Visual review covered crop, typography, relative salience, protected marks, unintended weapons or insignia, receiver context, human scale and non-photographic status. The corrected B05 is the only selected variant; the rejected emblem-bearing variant is not in the repository.
