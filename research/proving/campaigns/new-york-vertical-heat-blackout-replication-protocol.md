# New York vertical heat-blackout replication protocol

**Protocol:** `DF-NYC-HB-PR38-v1`

**Status:** preregistered design; operator `L0` rehearsal complete; formal `L0` and all campaign outcomes remain pending

**Decision object:** whether the frozen `S3` Neighborhood Continuity and Access Cell merits advancement over frozen `A2`

**Campaign duration:** 168 hours

**Geography:** twelve provisional outcome-blind reference catchments and three rehearsal commitments preserved by the operator street-topology audit; independent reproduction and salted custody remain pending

## Executive judgment

The Harris County campaign established a severe result, but not a portable one. Its strongest protected-local architecture still failed its complete rule in 44.7 percent of broad uncertainty worlds, and the failure concentrated at the seam between buildings, household knowledge, local service, collection and released roads. Pass 37 configured that seam as a 35,000-person Neighborhood Continuity and Access Cell. The conventional `A2` cell closed every declared physical component-loss case but verified only 79.6 percent of households by hour 36 when commercial communications disappeared. The `S3` rival closed the arithmetic only by assuming unproved gains in disconnected records, field productivity, vertical access and prepared-district interfaces.

The next research act must therefore be replication, not elaboration. This protocol moves the same two frozen architectures into a deliberately antagonistic metropolitan geometry: a seven-day New York extreme-heat event coupled to prolonged power and commercial-communications loss. Most road pavement remains physically present, although street service degrades. The critical damaged network moves inside buildings: cooling, domestic water pressure, elevators, medical devices, access control, communications and civil records share an electrical dependency. The dominant path to harm moves indoors and upward.

No architecture result appears in this paper or its workbook. Pass 39 completed an outcome-blind operator selection rehearsal, producing twelve provisional references and three commitment hashes; demand has not been synthesized and neither architecture has been run. The rehearsal also showed that polygon adjacency did not satisfy the registered local-street-component rule and that unsalted deterministic commitments do not create holdout secrecy. Pass 41 corrects the topology with the official `LION 26b` node-and-level graph and preserves every reference and commitment across three graph screens. The protocol remains open at `L0` while preserving the questions, candidate designs, selection order, design space, complete success rule, failure injections, measurement authorities and interpretation before those outcomes exist.

This is a portability test with a real possibility of rejecting both architectures. `S3` does not pass because it beats `A2`; it must produce a material advantage, pass an absolute robustness floor in every built-form archetype, meet every public-effect clock, preserve rights and safety, and retain a second-mission force at hour 168. If it fails any co-governing condition, its technology assumptions do not enter a national force baseline.

## Why New York is the required contrast

Replication is informative when it changes the mechanisms most likely to invalidate a prior finding. New York is not selected because it is merely large or visible. It reverses four structural conditions embedded in the Harris campaign.

First, the exposure clock changes. Harris emphasized flood depth, damaged access, collection queues and route restoration. The New York campaign emphasizes indoor heat accumulation while cooling, power, elevators, domestic water pumps, communications and medical support degrade together. New York City defines a heat emergency around forecast heat-index thresholds and publishes a Heat Vulnerability Index that combines environmental, housing and socioeconomic conditions. The index is useful for neighborhood stratification, not for predicting an individual's illness or converting heat directly into campaign demand ([NYC Heat Vulnerability Index](https://a816-dohbesp.nyc.gov/IndicatorPublic/data-features/hvi/)).

Second, the relevant geography is three-dimensional. A tract can appear connected to a street while a resident remains separated from service by thirty floors, an unavailable elevator, a locked or dark circulation system, insufficient water pressure, a mobility device, a caregiver relationship or an unsafe interior thermal zone. Building footprints, tax-lot records and floor counts can identify candidate built forms, but they cannot establish occupancy, internal circulation or post-event operability ([NYC Building Footprints](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Housing-Development/Building-Footprints/nqwf-w8eh/about_data); [NYC MapPLUTO](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/City-Government/Primary-Land-Use-Tax-Lot-Output-Map-MapPLUTO-/f888-ni5f/about_data)).

Third, electricity becomes a common-mode civil dependency. Building backup requirements identify code-defined emergency loads; they do not prove that a particular building has sufficient generation, maintained fuel, operable switching or resident-serving capacity during a prolonged outage ([NYC emergency backup power systems](https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/industry/project-requirements-design-professional-emergency-backup.page)). Annual energy and water benchmarking can help stratify large buildings, but it is not a measurement of emergency thermal performance or pump survival ([NYC building energy and water disclosure](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Environment/NYC-Building-Energy-and-Water-Data-Disclosure-for-/5zyy-y8am/explore)).

Fourth, movement changes character. The main burden is not long-distance evacuation from a flooded road network. It is lawful, accessible movement through a building and the last block into named cooled, powered and clinically appropriate service. The U.S. Access Board treats an accessible means of egress as a continuous route to a public way, with explicit roles for areas of refuge and qualifying elevators. That boundary is adopted here as a rights-and-access requirement, while recognizing that code guidance does not demonstrate incident operability ([U.S. Access Board](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/guides/chapter-4-accessible-means-of-egress/)). NIST stair-evacuation research informs the trial factors for mobility impairment, communication and procedure; office-fire observations do not establish heat-blackout productivity ([NIST TN 1923](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.TN.1923)).

Together these changes create a hard transfer test: can a formation derived from a Gulf Coast flood campaign preserve complete service in a vertical city where the building itself becomes the damaged network?

## Campaign object

The unit of analysis is a **reference catchment**, not a borough, incident-command boundary or political district. Each catchment is a contiguous group of residential census tracts whose synthetic seven-day demand first enters 32,000–38,000 people. The catchment is large enough to exercise one `NCAC-35` class and small enough to expose building and neighborhood heterogeneity.

The public-effect state for person (i) at time (t) is:

\[
S_{i,t}=L_{i,t}\lor D_{i,t}
\]

where (L) means the person is in a verified safe local bundle and (D) means the person has been admitted to a named destination providing the required bundle. Departure, vehicle loading, arrival at an unverified site and administrative closure do not satisfy (D). A person cannot be credited twice.

The minimum local bundle is thermal safety, potable water access, sanitation, necessary medical power and care continuity, communications or an accessible in-person substitute, lawful household disposition and a viable route to assistance. Its detailed clinical and thermal thresholds remain an external evidence gate. The protocol uses an eighteen-hour mean unsafe-indoor-exposure ceiling as a design requirement, not as a validated medical threshold.

## Outcome-blind catchment selection

Selection must finish before either architecture produces an outcome.

The [Pass 39 `L0` rehearsal](new-york-l0-catchment-rehearsal.md) implements this sequence against seven frozen official extracts. It exposes 844 polygon-connected candidate groups, twelve provisional references and three rehearsal commitments in the [selection workbook](../../../models/nyc-l0-catchment-selection.xlsx). The [Pass 41 correction](new-york-l0-street-topology-audit.md) produces 847 street-connected candidates and preserves every selected composition in the [topology workbook](../../../models/nyc-l0-street-topology-audit.xlsx). These are not the formal freeze: independent reproduction, canonical-method registration and salted holdout custody remain required.

1. Freeze every official source file, schema, retrieval time, row count and SHA-256 digest.
2. Limit eligibility to land tracts with residential population and a connected local-street component. Marine-only islands remain a declared exclusion stratum rather than disappearing from the record.
3. Grow contiguous tract groups until synthetic demand first enters 32,000–38,000 people. Retain the overshoot; do not trim it to improve fit.
4. Assign candidate groups to one of three built-form archetypes using only pre-architecture covariates.
5. Order candidates by the SHA-256 of the GEOID set concatenated with `DF-NYC-HB-PR38-v1`.
6. Take the first four eligible non-overlapping catchments in each archetype, subject to declared covariate balance.
7. Permit replacement only for a documented source or topology defect found while blind to architecture results.
8. Seal one additional eligible catchment per archetype until the primary pipeline, exclusions and thresholds pass independent audit.

The three archetypes are intentionally mechanistic:

| ID | Archetype | Pre-outcome eligibility logic | Falsification purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `V1` | tower-intensive core | top resident-weighted quintile for floor and elevator-device measures; density at or above city 75th percentile | elevator, pressure, refuge and vertical-team saturation |
| `V2` | mid-rise heat-burden grid | predominantly three-to-eight-floor fabric; HVI 4–5 share at or above city 75th percentile | distributed contact and cooling without tower concentration |
| `V3` | outer-borough elevator-dependent campus | multi-building campus or superblock; high elevator dependence; protected-service walking access below city median | campus common modes and last-block access |

The elevator administrative source records post-2017 application activity and device details; it is not treated as a complete installed-base or incident-availability inventory ([NYC elevator device details](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Housing-Development/DOB-NOW-Build-Elevator-Device-Details/juyv-2jek)). Candidate classification must therefore carry an explicit missingness and field-verification flag.

## Preregistered questions and hypotheses

The main estimand is the paired difference in complete-rule success between `S3` and `A2` across the same catchment-world rows. Pairing prevents an architecture from benefiting from easier synthetic conditions. Inference clusters at the catchment level; individual people and time steps are not independent experimental replicates.

Five conditions co-govern advancement:

- `H1`: `S3 – A2` complete-rule success is at least fifteen percentage points overall and positive in every archetype.
- `H2`: `S3` succeeds in at least 80 percent of the declared broad worlds in each archetype.
- `H3`: every reference catchment reaches 100 percent authoritative household disposition by hour 36 and at least 95 percent high-risk contact by hour 18.
- `H4`: every reference catchment reaches at least 95 percent total safe-service closure and at least 95 percent mandatory-movement destination admission by hour 72.
- `H5`: `S3` causes no attributable rights or safety sentinel event and is not worse than `A2` on timed exception queues.

`H6` is a separate readiness condition: the candidate retains at least 80 percent of its next-24-hour capacity at hour 168, measured as the minimum of qualified people, mission-capable equipment, utility capability, essential stocks and reconcilable records.

There is no compensating score. A favorable average cannot erase a failed archetype, an uncontacted high-risk population, coercive movement, household separation, an inaccessible destination, a serious attributable injury or a force that cannot operate again.

## Declared broad design space

Each of twelve reference catchments receives 1,000 Latin-hypercube worlds shared by both architectures, producing 24,000 architecture-world rows. The worlds span declared coordinates; they are not assigned occurrence probabilities.

| Factor | Range | Dependency that must be preserved |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| design demand multiplier | 0.80–1.30 | positively associated with indoor heat burden |
| indoor heat accumulation | 0.70–1.40 | positively associated with heat-sensitive demand |
| grid outage | 24–168 h | negatively associated with elevators, pumps, communications and cooling |
| building backup availability | 0.20–0.80 | shared within building/tax-lot clusters |
| elevator availability | 0.05–0.60 | conditional on grid and backup |
| domestic-water-pump availability | 0.20–0.80 | conditional on grid and backup |
| commercial communications through hour 36 | 0.00–0.50 | conditional on grid duration |
| field workforce availability | 0.65–0.95 | negatively associated with heat burden |
| street-operability productivity | 0.60–1.00 | signal, curb, transit, congestion and heat degradation |
| prepared cooling-service availability | 0.50–0.90 | positively associated with building backup |
| cell utility realized output | 0.70–0.95 | common source, controller and fuel faults injected separately |
| heat-sensitive illness arrival | 0.80–1.50 | positively associated with heat accumulation |

The dependence rules matter more than the marginal ranges. Independently drawing grid, elevator, pump, communications and cooling availability would manufacture diversification that the physical system does not possess. The analysis must publish the target rank-correlation matrix, achieved matrix and rejected draws. It must also repeat the primary comparison across a set of prespecified alternative dependence strengths. Those runs test structural sensitivity; they do not acquire confirmatory status by producing a preferred answer.

## Complete campaign success rule

A world succeeds only if all eight conditions hold:

| Requirement | Threshold |
| --- | ---: |
| authoritative household disposition | 100% by hour 36 |
| high-risk household contact | 95% by hour 18 |
| total safe-service closure | 95% by hour 72 |
| mandatory-movement admission | 95% by hour 72 |
| worst-reference-catchment closure | 90% by hour 72 |
| mean unsafe indoor exposure | no more than 18 hours/person |
| next-mission capacity | at least 80% at hour 168 |
| rights and safety sentinels | zero attributable events |

An authoritative household disposition is verified service, documented refusal, not-found status after the declared escalation process, or lawful inaccessibility with independent review. Silence is not disposition. A mandatory movement counts only after named destination acceptance. Rights sentinels include attributable death or serious injury, coercive movement, household separation, material privacy breach and arrival without the promised service.

## Analysis plan

The primary pipeline is frozen before catchment unsealing.

For `H1`, calculate the paired binary complete-rule difference for every catchment-world pair. Report the overall mean difference, the three archetype-specific differences and catchment-cluster bootstrap intervals. `H1` fails if the overall advantage is below 0.15 or if any archetype difference is zero or negative.

For `H2`, calculate `S3` success within each catchment and archetype. Report the raw design-space share and a catchment-resampled lower interval. `H2` fails if any archetype's raw share is below 0.80. Because the worlds are a declared design space rather than probability-weighted forecasts, this is a coverage measure, not an expected reliability estimate.

For `H3` and `H4`, analyze record-level service clocks and publish the full catchment distribution. Every reference catchment must pass. Missing records remain in the denominator unless their disposition is resolved under the prespecified lawful-inaccessibility process.

For `H5`, a separate authority adjudicates sentinel events without access to sponsor preference. A zero-tolerance event blocks advancement. Timed accessibility, consent and household-continuity exceptions are compared as queues: incidence, age, remedy and unresolved burden at each six-hour boundary.

For `H6`, calculate the minimum available share across required force functions. An arithmetic average is prohibited because excess vehicles cannot substitute for an absent qualified crew, missing record authority or depleted utility treatment train.

Secondary analysis reports first failure by architecture, archetype and time; the interaction between architecture and verticality strata; queue peaks; person-hours in unsafe service; worker heat load; utility shortfalls; and the marginal public effect of each formation component. These analyses explain the result but cannot change the primary decision.

## Missing data, defects and amendments

Source missingness remains missing. It cannot be silently converted to no demand, no elevator, intact backup or successful contact. Every missing field carries its source, reason and operational consequence into the synthetic population generator. Model imputations are labeled, versioned and subjected to a missing-not-at-random stress case.

A source or topology defect discovered after selection can justify replacement only when the defect criterion was declared in advance, the architecture outcomes remain blinded and an independent data board approves the action. The rejected catchment, reason and replacement stay in the public record.

Any change to architecture, eligibility, outcomes, denominators, correlation structure, failure definition, code or analysis creates a new protocol version. Exploratory successors are valuable, but they do not inherit confirmation from `v1`.

## Interpretation matrix

| Result | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| both fail absolute rules | reject national admission; identify the common failed service chain and open rival architectures |
| `S3` beats `A2` but misses an absolute or rights rule | continue technology development; no force credit |
| `S3` passes, `A2` fails | advance frozen `S3` to representative mission confirmation; retain `A2` as control |
| both pass, advantage under fifteen points | prefer the lower-complexity development path unless lifecycle and common-mode evidence justify `S3` |
| archetypes diverge | reject universal cell doctrine; develop bounded variants without counting them as this replication |
| reference set passes, sealed holdout fails | treat the result as overfit; reopen selection and representation |
| all campaign rules pass | authorize the next evidence stage only; do not authorize procurement or rendering |

The final row is intentionally strict. A metropolitan simulation cannot establish field productivity, human safety, utility reliability, lawful data handling, maintainability or second-mission readiness. It can determine whether an architecture is coherent enough to earn expensive representative testing.

## Institutional consequence

This protocol is a small example of how a Department of Resilience must behave differently from an equipment program. It commits a rival design, an adversarial geography, independent measures and a public failure rule before favorable outcomes can harden into doctrine. It makes civil rights and service admission part of system performance. It treats a city not as a map onto which assets are placed, but as a coupled population–building–utility–street–institution system.

The campaign also reveals a new form of national power. In a vertical heat blackout, the decisive capacity may be neither strategic lift nor a spectacular generator. It may be the ability to maintain lawful knowledge of every household; carry trustworthy service through dark buildings; create local islands of cooling, water, medical power and communication; move only those who must move; and restore the formation quickly enough to act again. That is a technological and organizational frontier worthy of a national innovation system.
