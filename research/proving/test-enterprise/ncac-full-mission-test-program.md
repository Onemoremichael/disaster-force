# NCAC full-mission test program

**Program object:** prove, reject or bound the Neighborhood Continuity and Access Cell as a repeatable public-service formation

**Candidate architectures:** frozen `A2` conventional development reference and frozen `S3` distributed technology target

**Program horizon:** 2027–2038 design profile

**Authorized-cost screen:** $115.83 billion in real 2026 dollars, including 30 percent program reserve

**Current authority:** evidence program only; no acquisition or rendering authority

## Executive judgment

The Department of Resilience cannot acquire its way out of an evidence deficit. The current `NCAC-35` concept combines household knowledge, occupied-building access, protected local service, vertical movement, accessible collection, street release, islanded power and water, communications, civil adjudication, destination admission, sustainment and force regeneration. A successful vehicle, software demonstration, microgrid or field exercise cannot establish that combined mission.

The appropriate analogue is a major national test enterprise: dedicated ranges, instrumented representative buildings, rival full-scale test articles, trained human participants, utility and communications fault infrastructure, an independent operational-test authority, public protocols and enough complete missions to learn about rare system failure. The first design-cost screen is $46.2 billion in capital and $42.9 billion in twelve-year operations. A 30 percent program reserve produces an authorized envelope of $115.83 billion. These are transparent design assumptions, not an independent cost estimate.

That scale is not extravagance attached to one neighborhood formation. It is the price hypothesis for learning whether a new sovereign capability can safely exercise authority inside homes and buildings while infrastructure is absent. If the nation is unwilling to build representative evidence, it should not declare the capability mature.

## What must be proved

The test program has four linked propositions.

1. **Mechanism:** the technologies and work systems produce their claimed rates without shifting error, injury, exclusion or unmeasured labor elsewhere.
2. **Integration:** districts, utilities, access, records, movement and civil authority continue to work when their interfaces and shared dependencies fail.
3. **Mission:** one frozen cell can meet every public-effect, rights, safety and regeneration requirement for 168 hours under representative fault injection.
4. **Portability:** the result survives a different metropolitan hazard and built form under a preregistered design.

Evidence must move in that order. A metropolitan model cannot validate a human-access rate. A component trial cannot validate a seven-day common-mode architecture. A handful of scripted demonstrations cannot establish a reliability floor. A field success in one city cannot establish national doctrine.

The program adopts the logic of a requirements-verification matrix: each requirement has a verification method, representative condition, independent authority, data record and admission consequence. NASA's systems-engineering handbook is a useful precedent for linking requirements to verification and validation, but space-system procedure is adapted rather than treated as proof for a civil-response force ([NASA Systems Engineering Handbook](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/nasa_systems_engineering_handbook_0.pdf)). Technology maturity is separately assessed using evidence that is independent, credible and relevant to the intended environment, consistent with the Government Accountability Office's technology-readiness principles ([GAO Technology Readiness Assessment Guide](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-20-48g)).

## Evidence ladder

| Stage | Campaign | Scale | Purpose | Admission rule |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `L0` | protocol and data rehearsal | 12 analytical catchments | freeze snapshots, code, seed, exclusions and synthetic-population generation | independent reproducibility audit |
| `L1` | element and human-system trials | 888 two-arm units | estimate ledger, contact, vertical, route and collection parameters | prespecified quality, rights and safety co-outcomes |
| `L2` | utility and district integration | 60 seven-day fault sequences | exercise N-2 power, N-1 water, thermal service and communications | no common-mode defeat in required sequences |
| `L2B` | occupied-district missions | 48 seventy-two-hour trials | join people, buildings, services, rights, access and sustainment | every district acceptance rule |
| `L3` | developmental full-cell missions | 12 missions | six missions per architecture; redesign allowed with version reset | downselect, bound or reject |
| `L4` | confirmatory full-cell missions | 30 missions | frozen selected candidate under complete 168-hour mission | zero failures; any architecture change restarts count |
| `L5` | metropolitan replication | 24,000 architecture-world rows | twelve New York catchments × 1,000 worlds × two architectures | preregistered hypotheses plus sealed holdouts |

The labels are not maturity levels that can be awarded by documentation. They are physical and analytical evidence campaigns. Failure at a lower stage can prevent a more expensive stage from beginning. Success authorizes only the next stage.

## `L0`: protocol, source and synthetic-population rehearsal

The first range is informational. Every source snapshot receives a URL, owner, retrieval time, schema, row count and cryptographic digest. Derived geography receives code, package versions, coordinate system, exclusions and lineage. The deterministic catchment selector runs before any architecture outcome and publishes every eligible, rejected, selected and holdout set.

The synthetic population generator must preserve household binding, high-risk flags, accessibility, mobility devices, caregivers, medical-power dependency, language, building/floor assignment, service need, refusal and destination constraints without using live operational identity data. An independent privacy and methods team attempts record linkage, outcome leakage and reconstruction attacks. Failed protection blocks progression.

`L0` ends only when an independent team can rebuild the same catchments, design matrix and empty outcome tables from the frozen records. Reproduction after outcomes is insufficient because the architecture could already have influenced selection.

Pass 39 completed the first operator rehearsal against seven official extracts. It produced 844 population-bounded candidates, twelve disclosed provisional references and three commitment hashes, but it also found that tract-polygon adjacency did not satisfy the registered local-street test and that deterministic unsalted commitments are inferable from the public universe. Pass 41's [street-topology audit](../campaigns/new-york/l0-street-topology-audit.md) reconstructs the universe from official `LION 26b` node-and-level topology. It produces 847 candidates and preserves all twelve references and three commitments under primary, strict and permissive screens. Pass 42's [empty outcome registration](../campaigns/new-york/l0-empty-outcome-registration.md) binds 12,000 world identities, 24,000 architecture rows and 2,000 campaign aggregates without generating an outcome. Pass 43's [world-generator registration](../campaigns/new-york/l0-world-generator-registration.md) populates only the twelve stress factors with exact centered Latin-hypercube margins and explicit citywide, borough, catchment and residual dependence. Attempt zero passes the preregistered implementation tolerances, but the loadings remain design hypotheses and borough remains a service-territory proxy.

`L0` remains open for independent reproduction, governed population and building enrichment, service-territory and causal-feasibility completion, alternative dependence cases and external salted custody. The [selection workbook](../../../models/nyc-l0-catchment-selection.xlsx), [topology workbook](../../../models/nyc-l0-street-topology-audit.xlsx), [empty registry](../../../models/nyc-l0-empty-outcome-registry.xlsx) and [world-generator workbook](../../../models/nyc-l0-world-generator-registration.xlsx) are auditable operator artifacts, not admission records.

## `L1`: element and human-system trials

Five two-arm designs test the parameters that distinguish `S3` from `A2`. Sample sizes use a transparent normal-approximation planning equation:

\[
n_{arm}=\frac{2(z_{1-\alpha/2}+z_{1-\beta})^2\sigma^2}{\Delta^2}
\]

The raw count is multiplied by a declared clustering design effect, divided by one minus attrition and rounded upward to complete twelve-unit experimental blocks. The equation is a planning screen. Final protocols require endpoint-specific distributions, intraclass correlations, sequential rules and a statistician independent of the technology sponsor. NIST's sample-size guidance supplies the general relationship among alpha, beta, variance and detectable difference, not a substitute for a curated experimental design ([NIST Engineering Statistics Handbook](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc222.htm)).

| ID | Trial | Experimental unit | `A2` / target | Planned per arm | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `SD-01` | disconnected ledger reconciliation | 50-household conflict block | 0.35 / 0.55 reconciled share | 84 | 168 |
| `SD-02` | household contact and building screening | team-shift | 1.6 / 2.4 households per team-hour | 96 | 192 |
| `SD-03` | assisted vertical movement | completed representative mission | 0.50 / 0.80 people per team-hour | 96 | 192 |
| `SD-04` | route-assurance release | detachment-shift | 11.52 / 15.50 route-km | 84 | 168 |
| `SD-05` | collection full duty cycle | dispatch-to-ready-again cycle | 34.72 / 41.66 people | 84 | 168 |

Productivity is never the sole endpoint. Ledger speed is paired with household integrity, consent provenance, conflict recovery, lawful access and privacy. Contact rate is paired with false disposition, missed high-risk people, worker heat load and safety escalation. Vertical throughput is paired with resident and worker injury, fall and crush risk, household separation, egress obstruction, device continuity and aborted mission quality. Route release counts only vehicle-specific, independently authorized service; cleared geometry does not count. Collection includes dispatch, loading, restraint, travel, unloading, cleaning, energy, maintenance and ready-again state.

The 888 units are shifts, blocks, missions and cycles—not 888 individual residents. Treating every person or event inside one clustered team-shift as independent would create fictitious statistical power.

## `L2`: utility and district integration

The utility range must be able to create the failures the formation claims to survive. It therefore needs configurable electrical sources, feeders, switchgear, protection, loads, storage, black start, controls and instrumentation; water sources, treatment, pressure zones, storage, pumps, distribution, quality laboratories and residual paths; commercial and deployable communications with spectrum, backhaul, power, identity, cyber and degraded-mode controls; and thermal loads that couple building condition to human service.

Sixty seven-day fault sequences exercise at minimum:

- loss of two power blocks plus one feeder;
- loss of one water island plus a building pump cluster;
- cloud and commercial backhaul denial;
- a common software release defect requiring manual-safe operation and rollback;
- fuel, treatment consumable and operator constraints;
- peak thermal and medical-power demand; and
- maintenance while the system remains occupied.

The requirement is not continuous nameplate production. Credit is end-use service at the building or district boundary: protected circuits energized within power-quality limits; potable water delivered at pressure and quality; wastewater and residuals controlled; communications usable by declared public and responder classes; and safe fallback when automation fails.

One successful scripted sequence cannot close the stage. The fault order, load condition, operating crew and recovery route vary under a prespecified design. A common-mode defeat in a required sequence reopens the architecture.

## `L2B`: occupied-district missions

Forty-eight seventy-two-hour district trials join the human and utility systems before a complete cell is placed at risk. Representative low-, mid- and high-rise buildings and campus forms are instrumented for indoor temperature, water pressure, vertical circulation, power, communications and occupancy state. Participation is paid, medically supported and designed so no essential service is withheld and no person is trapped in an unsafe condition.

The experiment creates controlled service degradation through simulators, isolated training utilities and operational substitutions; it does not impose dangerous deprivation on residents. Every trial has rehearsed abort, rescue, fire, medical, power and accessible-egress paths under an independent stop authority. Disability-led design and paid participation are required from protocol formation through adjudication.

District acceptance requires complete household disposition, accessible communication, thermal and medical-power continuity, water and sanitation, vertical and last-block access, lawful exceptions, replenishment, waste removal and a verified handoff or restoration state. Excess performance in one service cannot cover failure in another.

## `L3`: developmental full-cell missions

The program then conducts twelve complete 168-hour missions: six for `A2` and six for `S3`. Developmental missions are allowed to discover defects and change the design. Every change creates a new architecture version and resets its evidence lineage.

The twelve required failure injections are:

1. commercial cloud and backhaul unavailable for hours 0–72;
2. one contact company unavailable for hours 0–48;
3. one vertical-access company unavailable for hours 12–72;
4. one five-district cluster loses prepared service for hours 18–96;
5. one collection squadron unavailable for hours 24–72;
6. one route-assurance detachment unavailable for hours 0–48;
7. two power blocks and one feeder removed in staggered faults across hours 6–96;
8. one water island and one building-pump cluster unavailable for hours 12–72;
9. one common software release defect injected at hour 30;
10. conflicting consent, destination and household records in five waves;
11. heat-related workforce attrition rising to 20 percent by hour 120; and
12. a no-notice second mission at hour 168.

The injections are a required set, not necessarily a safe chronological script. The range safety case determines sequencing, separation and abort boundaries. Surviving each in isolation is inadequate; representative combinations must expose correlated failure without deliberately endangering participants.

### Mission clock

The mission begins before deployment. The candidate receives an alert package with weather, infrastructure and synthetic demand uncertainty, then must marshal, deploy, establish authority, create household knowledge, open building access, activate protected districts, release local streets, collect and admit necessary movements, sustain the population, repair itself and demonstrate a second-mission package.

The governing clocks are:

- hour 18: contact at least 95 percent of high-risk households;
- hour 36: resolve 100 percent of authoritative household dispositions;
- hour 72: achieve at least 95 percent total safe service and mandatory-movement admission, at least 90 percent in the worst catchment, and no more than eighteen mean unsafe indoor hours per demand person;
- hour 168: retain at least 80 percent next-24-hour capacity in every required force function.

The clock does not pause for shift change, maintenance, data conflict, adjudication or resupply. Those are the mission.

## Measurement and authority

Twelve measurement streams prevent throughput from hiding harm or incompleteness.

| Family | Representative measures | Independent owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| household knowledge | disposition age; high-risk contact completeness | rights/data authority; evaluator |
| public effect | person-hours in safe service; unsafe indoor exposure | mission-effects; health/environment authorities |
| building access | completed vertical missions; device/floor strata; aborts | safety/test authority |
| workforce | force, exertion, heat load, near misses, injury | worker-safety authority |
| collection and streets | complete ready-again cycles; vehicle-specific released route-km-hours | mobility and transport test authorities |
| utilities | MW, MWh, water volume, pressure, quality, residual state | technical authority |
| communications | coverage, capacity, latency and delivered messages by user class | communications authority |
| rights | exception incidence, age, remedy and disposition | civil-rights authority |
| regeneration | minimum qualified people, equipment, utility, stock and data share | independent operational test |

Raw instrument and observer records are append-only. Operators cannot edit evaluator data. Missing sensors remain missing with a reason; they do not become zero or success. Derived datasets carry script hash, inputs, parameters and output hash. Holdout identifiers and data stay encrypted or access-controlled until the independent board authorizes unsealing.

## Rights, ethics and safety architecture

The test program cannot simulate coercion by exercising it. Ten controls govern participation and admission:

- no essential power, cooling, water, elevator, communications, medication or care is withdrawn to create realism;
- participation is voluntary unless a separate real emergency creates lawful authority outside the experiment;
- disability-led design, paid participation, accessible devices, interpreters, caregivers and service animals are built into every representative trial;
- worker heat, lifting, confined-space, electrical, vehicle and fatigue limits have an independent stop authority;
- synthetic household identities are used for record conflict, loss and cyber testing;
- outcome adjudication sits outside the sponsor and operating chain;
- consent, household integrity, accessibility, destination acceptance and privacy are constraints, not throughput trades;
- every human-system trial has rehearsed abort, rescue, fire, medical, power and accessible-egress procedures;
- participants receive compensation, transport, accommodation, follow-up and funded repair for any device or property damage; and
- protocol amendments, adverse events, missing data and failed missions remain public.

These are not research-administration details. They are system requirements. A formation unable to preserve them in a test environment is not fit to exercise civil power during catastrophe.

## `L4`: confirmatory mission reliability

After `L3`, the program may select one frozen candidate or reject both. The selected configuration then completes thirty independent, representative 168-hour missions with zero complete-rule failures.

For (n) trials with zero failures, the one-sided exact lower confidence bound on success probability is:

\[
p_L=\alpha^{1/n}
\]

At 95 percent confidence, 29 zero-failure trials put the lower bound just above 0.90; the program rounds to thirty, yielding approximately 0.905. Fourteen exact trials support only a 0.80 target, nineteen support 0.85 and fifty-nine support 0.95. The workbook rounds those program designs to 18, 24, 30 and 60 missions respectively.

This calculation is a screen, not a reliability guarantee. It is informative only if trials are independent enough, conditions are relevant to the field, failures are completely observed and the architecture remains unchanged. Reusing the same crew, range configuration, software build or fault sequence can reduce effective independence. Any material hardware, software, staffing, doctrine or interface change restarts the confirmatory count.

The program should publish both the nominal trial count and an effective-replication analysis for crew, site, software and scenario clustering. Thirty nominal repetitions that share one hidden common mode must not be represented as thirty independent demonstrations.

## `L5`: metropolitan replication

The New York protocol runs in parallel with late integration and tests whether the architecture's advantages survive a vertical heat blackout. Its operator rehearsal produces twelve provisional references and three commitment hashes; the street-topology correction preserves them but does not make them independent or secret. The registered generator now binds the primary joint stress surface, but it does not validate its own loadings or convert design coverage into probability. The package can prevent a favorable district or easy synthetic world from standing in for a city only after independent reproduction, alternative-dependence challenge and salted external custody. Twenty-four thousand paired architecture-world rows will test the declared design space after `L0` closes.

A campaign pass does not replace `L4`, and `L4` does not replace the campaign. The former tests geographic portability in a model with governed source data. The latter tests complete physical and human mission performance. National admission requires both and then an independent third-metropolitan replication whose hazard and built form are chosen before New York outcomes are interpreted.

## Facilities and national test capacity

The program requires purpose-built public infrastructure, not borrowed annual exercise space.

The **vertical-city test range** should include interchangeable low-, mid- and high-rise residential forms; a multi-building campus; instrumented stairs, refuge areas and lift systems; domestic-water pressure zones; configurable electrical and communications risers; accessible dwelling and common-space modules; curb, intersection, loading and short-route networks; protected-service districts; and a utility fault yard. Interior environments must reproduce heat, darkness, noise, communications loss and constrained circulation within a safety case.

The **data and civil-systems range** should reproduce disconnected records, conflicting household graphs, consent changes, destination capacity, identity and authority without live population data. It needs adversarial cyber, privacy, spectrum and reconciliation teams as well as public-facing usability labs.

The **workforce academy** should qualify household teams, vertical-access teams, route and collection crews, utility operators, clinical and rights personnel, evaluators, maintainers and commanders on the same mission thread. Training throughput must include relief and regeneration; a single demonstration cadre is not a deployable force.

The **independent test directorate** controls configuration, randomization, fault injection, clocks, measurements, safety stops, missing-data rulings and public reports. The capability sponsor can observe and propose fixes but cannot declare its own pass.

## Transition and kill decisions

Every technology program must change an observed mission parameter without displacing burden beyond the boundary.

| Program | Transition condition | Kill or redirect condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| disconnected continuity ledger | improves reconciliation and hour-36 disposition with household and consent integrity | lost provenance, unrecoverable conflict, surveillance expansion or dependence on backhaul |
| building-access mission system | reaches 2.4 quality-adjusted households/team-hour across built forms | missed high-risk people, unsafe entry or hidden resident/worker labor |
| powered vertical continuity | reaches 0.80 completed people/team-hour with devices and households intact | fall, crush, egress obstruction, separation or single-building-system dependence |
| common-cabin collection | raises complete ready-again flow across passenger needs | restraint, egress, dwell, cleaning, energy or proprietary-interface failure |
| route-assurance work cell | releases at least 15.5 vehicle-specific route-km/detachment-shift | geometry counted without inspection, control or lawful class-specific release |
| neighborhood utility island | survives N-2 power and N-1 water in seven-day occupied operation | common software, fuel, protection, treatment or operator defeat |
| prepared-district adapter | reduces imported district mass to 12 t with release inside 24 h | unsafe host structure, inaccessible service, externalized labor, waste or utility dependence |

The program may conclude that a technology should die, that `A2` remains preferable, that different built forms require bounded variants or that the entire cell boundary is wrong. Those are successful research outcomes if reached under a credible protocol.

## What this changes about the Department

A mature Department of Resilience needs an evidence economy comparable in seriousness to its operating force. Its laboratories, test ranges, operational-test service, public data systems, human-participant infrastructure, safety authorities, standards bodies, universities, manufacturers and field formations must form one learning system. Companies at the scale of major defense primes would compete not merely to sell vehicles, but to integrate service-producing systems and prove them under public rules.

The resulting industrial opportunity is larger and more durable than a catalog of emergency machines. It includes configurable utility networks, building-service interfaces, accessible movement systems, civil communications, safety instrumentation, offline public records, modular occupied-service districts, range systems, digital twins, maintenance and qualification. Exportable value comes from verified interfaces and mission evidence, not from U.S.-specific branding.

The discipline is equally important. Ambition does not mean giving speculative technology operational credit. It means financing enough rival invention, representative failure and independent measurement to discover capabilities that do not yet exist—and being willing to reject attractive systems that cannot preserve public effect and civil legitimacy.

Continue `L0` through independent reproduction of the completed operator topology, row ledger, world generator and official marginal input surface; salted holdout custody; preregistration and validation of the population–household–building compiler; governed medical-power and operational-building evidence; service-territory and causal-feasibility completion; and preregistered alternative dependence cases. Authorize preliminary range requirements, independent safety and rights governance, and detailed designs for the five `L1` experiments, but do not use `L1` observations to repair geography. Do not authorize full range construction until the source, participation, safety and cost bases receive independent review. Do not select `S3`, scale `NCAC-35` nationally or turn numerical parameters into procurement requirements.
