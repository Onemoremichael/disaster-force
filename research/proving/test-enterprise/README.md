# Innovation and test enterprise

Innovation begins where a complete mission thread fails. It does not begin with an invention seeking a disaster use.

[The resilience technology frontier](technology-frontier.md) converts operational failures into rival research programs with measurable transition conditions. [The National Resilience Proving Network](national-proving-network.md) defines the laboratories, environmental ranges, flagship facilities, instrumentation, independent replication and acquisition evidence needed to turn prototypes into public knowledge.

The [NCAC full-mission test program](ncac-full-mission-test-program.md) applies that architecture to one formation across powered human-system mechanisms, utility and occupied-district integration, developmental missions and frozen-configuration confirmation. Its cost is a design-study envelope; its purpose is to earn rejection-quality evidence before acquisition.

Novel capability receives force credit only when representative evidence changes a visible system parameter or uncertainty distribution. A successful component demonstration does not mature a service chain. A materially changed candidate begins confirmation again. The [active frontier](../../frontier.md) records the live campaign-to-technology handoff without turning this enduring method into a progress log.
