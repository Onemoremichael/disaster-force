# Innovation and test enterprise

[CSAC-1](../../force/projection/csac1-compound-service-article-and-range-campaign.md) is the current complete-system application of this doctrine. It assigns twenty-five campaigns to eight physical stages, protects qualification articles from mission consumption, and carries the service chain through carrier, unprepared receiver, casualty, reset and independent second-lot reproduction. Its four range classes are functional requirements rather than geographic site selections.

[MESA-1](../../force/projection/mesa1-measurement-and-destructive-evidence-architecture.md) is the measurement constitution beneath those campaigns. It separates control, independent physical evidence and civil-effect witness; registers the observable data burden; constrains uncertainty before verdict; and assigns destructive articles, calibration, custody and no-crutch failure insertions to a sovereign metrology enterprise.

[MESA-1R](../../force/projection/mesa1r-representative-evidence-hardware-and-hazard-envelope.md) converts that constitution into five representative physical node classes. Its 168-hour autonomy, local custody, pressure-skin and removal-paired requirements define a USD 6.8B, eight-campaign prototype program inside F01. The test enterprise must now compete supplier-neutral implementations and freeze serial configurations before full-mission use.

Innovation begins where a complete mission thread fails. It does not begin with an invention seeking a disaster use.

[The resilience technology frontier](technology-frontier.md) converts operational failures into rival research programs with measurable transition conditions. [The National Resilience Proving Network](national-proving-network.md) defines the laboratories, environmental ranges, flagship facilities, instrumentation, independent replication and acquisition evidence needed to turn prototypes into public knowledge.

The [NCAC full-mission test program](ncac-full-mission-test-program.md) applies that architecture to one formation across powered human-system mechanisms, utility and occupied-district integration, developmental missions and frozen-configuration confirmation. Its cost is a design-study envelope; its purpose is to earn rejection-quality evidence before acquisition.

Novel capability receives force credit only when representative evidence changes a visible system parameter or uncertainty distribution. A successful component demonstration does not mature a service chain. A materially changed candidate begins confirmation again. The [active frontier](../../frontier.md) records the live campaign-to-technology handoff without turning this enduring method into a progress log.
