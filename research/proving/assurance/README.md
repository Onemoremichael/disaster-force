# Research assurance

This layer answers one question: **what warrants belief in the force design?**

The [source ledger](source-ledger.md) records the provenance and bounded use of external evidence. The [evidence ledger](evidence-ledger.md) records the observations, experiments, engineering closure, and independent review that could confirm, change, or reject major claims.

The distinction matters. A source can justify an empirical boundary without validating a proposed system. A model can reconcile internally without establishing a probability distribution. A prototype can demonstrate a component without proving a formation. Assurance therefore follows the complete public-service thread and advances only when representative evidence closes the relevant failure modes.

The maturity scale is `R0` verbal mission, `R1` functional architecture, `R2` bounded concept, `R2+` first-order configuration, `R3` closed physics with representative evidence, and `R4` repeatable formation demonstration. Rendering is blocked until the relevant complete system reaches `R3`.
