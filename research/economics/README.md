# Economics

Economics makes the consequences of the promise visible: capital, recurring operations, workforce, readiness, industrial capacity, incident expenditure, recovery transfers, and public value are different accounts.

[Force and resource modeling](force-and-resource-model.md) preserves the original Department-wide top-down screen and the first $1 trillion constraint test. Later bottom-up allocation supersedes its headcount and capital totals, but not its central discipline: costs cannot be reconciled by hiding personnel, maintenance, training, bases, stocks, replacement, or risk outside the program.

Current figures are design-study scale hypotheses, not estimates or budget recommendations. The [model catalog](../../models/README.md) identifies which workbook owns each current quantitative claim.
