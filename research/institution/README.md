# Institution

Institution owns the question that coordination reforms leave unanswered: **who is accountable for the national promise when local systems and markets fail together?**

[Department of Resilience](department-of-resilience.md) defines the zero-based cabinet institution, its seven operating services, geographic and functional commands, civil authority, science and acquisition system, industrial base, professions, force components, and domestic and international employment rules.

[National Utility Projection System](national-utility-projection-system.md) tests that institutional logic against one strategic function. It shows how anchor sites, mobile formations, command, basins, workforce, industry, sustainment, and funding must combine before water and power modules become national capacity.

Institution sets authority and accountability. The [force-design spine](../force/README.md) determines what must actually be ready.
