# Research architecture

This corpus is organized as a **proof architecture**, not as a list of hazards, platforms or passes. Each layer owns one kind of claim, inherits constraints from the layer above and is allowed to reject the proposed answer below it. The folder tree therefore separates normative obligation, force design, technical closure and evidence rather than mirroring an incumbent bureaucracy.

```text
MANDATE
What must remain true for people, and who owns that promise?
        │
        ▼
FORCE
What people, formations, readiness and fixed capacity must exist beforehand?
        │
        ▼
MISSION SYSTEMS
What complete physical and human chains can actually produce the service?
        │
        ▼
PROVING
Does the design survive real geography, coupled failure and representative test?
        │
        ▼
SYNTHESIS
What judgment is warranted now?

Programmatics asks what the warranted design would demand of money, labor and industry.
History preserves how the answer changed without granting old answers authority.
```

## I. Establish the mandate

[Mandate](mandate/README.md) begins with the civilian condition to be preserved, not with an organization or asset. It defines population–service–time, credible design missions, service levels and the mission-engineering method. Only then does it derive the Department of Resilience and its enduring responsibility.

This layer can require a force. It cannot declare that a proposed force works.

## II. Generate and employ the force

[Force](force/README.md) connects national ambition to ready capacity. It treats generation, projection, protected-local continuity, civil mobility, reception, return and regeneration as one operating system. Its core objects are formations and complete paths, not inventories of attractive equipment.

This layer can close a campaign on declared assumptions. It cannot convert an uncertain coefficient into evidence.

## III. Close the mission systems

[Mission systems](systems/README.md) works backward from safely used public effect through source, production, distribution, access, staffing, waste and civil transfer. Water, power, fire, flood and population continuity share a common technical architecture so that modularity does not hide incompatible interfaces or national common modes.

This layer can establish first-order physics and configuration. It cannot infer representative reliability from arithmetic closure.

## IV. Try to break the answer

[Proving](proving/README.md) joins three functions that should not be separated: campaigns expose the force to geography and compound failure; the test enterprise turns failed mission threads into rival technology and representative trials; assurance controls sources, uncertainty, maturity and claim admission.

The active chain runs from deterministic Cascadia closure, through covariance failure, to the Harris County building-to-return campaign and now to a preregistered New York vertical heat-blackout replication. Campaigns live in geographic dossiers so that protocol, correction, registration and eventual results share one bounded context. The New York `L0` work has completed operator topology correction, empty result registration and a deterministic hierarchical stress-world generator. Independent reproduction, governed population and building inputs, observed service territories, alternate dependence cases and external custody keep the stop binding.

## The supporting layers

[Programmatics](programmatics/README.md) owns capital, recurring operations, workforce, readiness, industrial capacity and public-value accounts. It is downstream of force design: the budget must reveal the consequence of the promise rather than quietly resize the promise.

[Synthesis](synthesis/README.md) states the best integrated judgment the evidence presently supports. [History](history/README.md) records discovery order and supersession. Neither substitutes for the paper or model that owns a bounded technical claim.

## Authority rules

The repository has no implied “latest file wins” rule. Current authority follows the bounded decision:

- the mandate owns the required public effect;
- the force paper owns formation and employment logic;
- the mission-system paper owns complete-service physics;
- the campaign owns joint geographic closure;
- the workbook owns the declared arithmetic and sensitivity surface;
- assurance owns the maturity and evidence statement;
- synthesis owns only the integrated judgment that those lower-level records warrant.

Claims progress from a verbal mission (`R0`) through functional architecture (`R1`), bounded concept (`R2`), first-order configuration (`R2+`), closed physics with representative evidence (`R3`) and repeatable formation demonstration (`R4`). No major platform is at `R3`; no rendering is authorized.

## Active frontier

The next research obligation is not another architecture result. The operator street topology, row universe and experimental-world design are now auditable: 12,000 factor vectors occupy every one of 1,000 centered strata within each of twelve catchments, and explicit citywide, borough, catchment and residual loadings govern their dependence. All 24,000 architecture rows and 2,000 campaign aggregates remain empty.

A separate implementation must reproduce the 126,779 canonical edges, 7,129 tract pairs, 847 candidates, twelve references, row ledgers, factor values and four registered hashes. In parallel, the program must acquire governed age, disability, medical-power, household, accessibility, occupancy and building-condition inputs; replace or independently defend the borough service-territory proxy; freeze alternative dependence strengths and causal feasibility rules; and establish salted external holdout custody. Only then may the frozen conventional and technology architectures enter the metropolitan contest.
