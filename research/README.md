# Research architecture

This corpus is a proof architecture for one large claim: the United States can create a coequal instrument of national power whose purpose is to preserve civilian life-support through catastrophe.

The repository does not organize the inquiry as a list of hazards, agencies, or imagined vehicles. It follows the causal chain from public obligation to demonstrated effect. Each layer owns a different burden of proof, and a lower layer is allowed to reject the preferred answer above it.

```text
MANDATE                What is owed, to whom, under what conditions?
   │
   ▼
FORCE                  What capacity must exist and be ready before impact?
   │
   ▼
MISSION SYSTEMS        What complete chains can produce the effect?
   │
   ▼
PROVING                Does the design survive geography and evidence?
   │
   ▼
SYNTHESIS              What judgment is warranted now?

PROGRAMMATICS runs beside the chain: what industry, labor, and money does the
warranted design require? HISTORY sits behind it: what changed or was superseded?
```

The quantitative library now mirrors this structure under [`models/`](../models/README.md). A workbook and its paper are two views of the same decision: the workbook owns arithmetic and sensitivity; the paper owns interpretation, boundaries, and consequences.

## From obligation to institution

[Mandate](mandate/README.md) begins with the civilian condition to be preserved. It defines population–service–time, design missions, service levels, and the method for tracing a public effect backward through its dependencies. Only then does it derive the Department of Resilience, its authority, and its enduring responsibility.

Mandate can require a force. It cannot declare that a force works.

[Force](force/README.md) converts that obligation into people, formations, commands, bases, stocks, mobility, reception, readiness, and regeneration. Its central object is not an inventory but a formation able to complete a service thread under loss.

Force design can close a campaign on declared assumptions. It cannot turn uncertain coefficients into evidence.

[Mission systems](systems/README.md) works backward from safe human use through source, production, distribution, access, staffing, sustainment, waste, and civil transfer. Population continuity, water, power, fire, flood, mobility, medical support, and information systems share a common technical architecture so that attractive modules cannot hide incompatible interfaces or national common modes.

Mission engineering can establish physics and first-order configuration. It cannot infer representative reliability from a balanced spreadsheet.

## From hypothesis to evidence

[Proving](proving/README.md) keeps three functions together: geographic campaigns that expose the integrated force to real places; a test enterprise that turns failure into rival mechanisms and representative trials; and research assurance that governs sources, uncertainty, reproducibility, rights, safety, and claim admission. Proving is allowed to return no winner.

[Programmatics](programmatics/README.md) owns the capital, recurring operations, workforce, readiness, industrial capacity, and public-value consequences of whatever force survives. It is downstream of the promise: budgets may compare or phase designs, but they may not silently reduce the guaranteed service.

[Synthesis](synthesis/README.md) states the integrated judgment the evidence presently warrants. It cannot override the bounded paper, model, campaign, or assurance record that owns a technical claim.

[History](history/README.md) preserves discovery order, amendments, and supersession. It is evidence of learning, not the navigation structure for the current argument.

## Three ways into the work

For the national proposition, read [first principles](mandate/first-principles.md), the [Department of Resilience](mandate/department-of-resilience.md), and then the [strategic study](synthesis/strategic-study.md).

For the mature operating system, move from [mature force design](force/generation/mature-force-design.md) through [force composition](force/generation/force-composition.md) and [mission systems](systems/README.md). The [joint force allocation](force/generation/joint-force-allocation-and-order-of-battle.md) is the adversarial scale check, not the conceptual starting point.

For the live research problem, begin at the [active frontier](frontier.md), then enter the [New York campaign](proving/campaigns/new-york/README.md) and the [assurance system](proving/assurance/README.md). That path shows why the project has stopped before generating architecture outcomes and what must be learned before it may proceed.

## Authority and maturity

There is no “latest file wins” rule. Mandate owns required effect; force papers own formation and employment; mission-system papers own physical closure; campaigns own joint geographic performance; workbooks own arithmetic and sensitivity; assurance owns evidence maturity; and synthesis integrates only what those records permit.

Claims progress from a verbal mission (`R0`) through functional architecture (`R1`), bounded concept (`R2`), first-order configuration (`R2+`), closed physics with representative evidence (`R3`), and repeatable formation demonstration (`R4`). No major platform is at `R3`; no rendering is authorized.

The [active frontier](frontier.md) is the single repository-wide statement of current status. Section landing pages describe enduring intellectual responsibilities rather than repeating the latest campaign update.
