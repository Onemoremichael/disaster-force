# Research map

This corpus is a proof architecture for one large claim: the United States can create a coequal instrument of national power whose purpose is to preserve civilian life-support through catastrophe.

The folders do not mirror an agency chart or a catalog of hazards. Each owns a different burden of proof. A lower layer may reject the preferred answer above it; no layer may certify itself.

```text
MANDATE                         What is owed, to whom, under what conditions?
   │
   ▼
FORCE                           What must exist and be ready before impact?
   │
   ▼
MISSION SYSTEMS                 What complete chains can produce the effect?
   │
   ▼
PROVING                         Does the design survive geography and evidence?
   │
   ▼
SYNTHESIS                       What judgment is warranted now?

PROGRAMMATICS runs beside the chain: what industry, labor and money does the
warranted design require? HISTORY sits behind it: what was learned and superseded?
```

## Follow the claim

### Mandate: define the obligation

[Mandate](mandate/README.md) starts with the civilian condition to be preserved. It defines population–service–time, design missions, service levels and the method for tracing a public effect backward through every dependency. Only then does it derive the Department of Resilience, its authority and its enduring responsibility.

Mandate can require a force. It cannot declare that a force works.

### Force: create usable capacity

[Force](force/README.md) connects national ambition to ready formations. It joins generation, projection, protected-local continuity, selective movement, reception, return and regeneration. The central object is not an inventory but a formation that can complete a service thread under loss.

Force design can close a campaign on declared assumptions. It cannot turn uncertain coefficients into evidence.

### Mission systems: close the chain

[Mission systems](systems/README.md) works backward from safe human use through source, production, distribution, access, staffing, sustainment, waste and civil transfer. Water, power, fire, flood and population continuity use a common technical architecture so attractive modules cannot hide incompatible interfaces or national common modes.

Mission-system work can establish physics and first-order configuration. It cannot infer representative reliability from a balanced spreadsheet.

### Proving: try to break the answer

[Proving](proving/README.md) keeps campaigns, technology maturation and research assurance together. Campaigns expose the integrated force to real geography and coupled failure. The test enterprise converts failed mission threads into rival mechanisms and representative trials. Assurance governs sources, uncertainty, reproducibility, rights, safety and claim admission.

Proving is allowed to return no winner. That is a feature of the institution being designed, not a research inconvenience.

### Programmatics and synthesis: reckon with the consequence

[Programmatics](programmatics/README.md) owns capital, recurring operations, workforce, readiness, industrial capacity and public-value accounts. It is downstream of the promise: budgets may compare or phase designs, but may not silently reduce the guaranteed service.

[Synthesis](synthesis/README.md) states the integrated judgment the evidence presently warrants. It cannot override the bounded paper, model or assurance record that owns a technical claim.

## How authority works

There is no “latest file wins” rule. Authority follows the decision being made.

| Claim | Owning record |
| --- | --- |
| required public effect | mandate and service-level record |
| formation and employment logic | force-design paper |
| physical service closure | mission-system paper and workbook |
| joint geographic performance | campaign protocol and result |
| arithmetic and sensitivity | workbook |
| evidence maturity | assurance record |
| integrated judgment | synthesis, bounded by all of the above |

Claims progress from a verbal mission (`R0`) through functional architecture (`R1`), bounded concept (`R2`), first-order configuration (`R2+`), closed physics with representative evidence (`R3`) and repeatable formation demonstration (`R4`). No major platform is at `R3`; no rendering is authorized.

The [active frontier](frontier.md) is the single repository-wide statement of current status. The [research log](history/research-log.md) records work by pass, while the [supersession map](history/supersession-map.md) shows how later findings changed earlier conclusions. Section landing pages describe enduring intellectual responsibilities rather than repeating the latest campaign update.
