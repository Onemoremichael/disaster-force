# Research architecture

This corpus is one falsifiable argument: the United States can create a coequal instrument of national power whose purpose is to preserve civilian life-support through catastrophe.

The folders are not topic bins. They are stages in a proof. Each stage owns a different question and can reject the preferred design produced above it.

```text
PUBLIC OBLIGATION
       │
       ▼
    MANDATE ───────── What condition does the nation guarantee?
       │
       ▼
     FORCE ────────── What capacity must exist before impact?
       │
       ▼
MISSION SYSTEMS ───── What complete chains can produce the condition?
       │
       ▼
    PROVING ───────── Does the design survive place, loss and evidence?
       │
       ▼
   SYNTHESIS ──────── What judgment is warranted now?

PROGRAMMATICS runs alongside the proof and prices only what survives it.
HISTORY records learning and supersession without controlling the current design.
```

## The proof spine

[Mandate](mandate/README.md) begins with population, service, time, duration and damage. It derives the Department of Resilience from a public obligation rather than from today’s organizational chart. Its claims can require a force, but cannot declare that a force works.

[Force](force/README.md) converts the obligation into echelons, professions, formations, commands, bases, stocks, mobility, reception, readiness and regeneration. Its [canonical architecture](force/force-architecture.md) separates generating services, civilian effects, formations, employment commands and components so an organization chart cannot substitute for a complete force. The [National Formation Registry](force/generation/national-formation-registry.md) controls the family inventory and its evidence gaps; the [canonical allocation](force/generation/canonical-force-allocation-and-fiscal-reconciliation.md) makes the resulting concurrency, workforce, capital and entry burdens explicit; the [Household Collection Group configuration](force/generation/household-collection-group-formation-design.md) divides one complete service system between an organic formation and binding prepared or shared capacity; [shared-capacity allocation](force/generation/shared-capacity-allocation-and-claim-denial.md) establishes conservation, reservation and denial; [canonical cargo closure](force/projection/canonical-cargo-and-transport-closure.md) translates formation mass into position class, cube, deadline and mode while denying public-effect credit at the unquantified receiver; the [carrier–node program](force/projection/carrier-node-requirements-and-industrial-program.md) converts that burden into rival early-activation, node and 25-year industrial envelopes; the [`WRG-50` serial compiler](force/projection/wrg50-serial-manifest-and-configuration-admission.md) tests the first complete mission formation against object, position, node and receiver admission; the [first-reach competition](force/projection/wrg-fr10-first-reach-water-system-and-technology-competition.md) applies that denial to four clean-sheet water-conveyance rivals inside one complete ten-kilometre utility mission; the [degraded-service architecture](force/projection/wrg-fr10-degraded-service-architecture.md) makes charged storage, route separation, bypass time and receiver state part of force readiness; and the [federated receiver architecture](force/projection/wrg-fr10-federated-receiver-architecture.md) decomposes receiver identity into separately survivable service boundaries. The central object is an effect-complete formation, not an equipment inventory. Force design can expose a campaign contradiction on declared assumptions; it cannot turn uncertain rates into evidence.

[Mission systems](systems/README.md) work backward from safe public use through source, production, distribution, access, staffing, sustainment, waste and civil transfer. They enforce shared interfaces and expose national common modes. A balanced configuration can establish first-order physical closure; it cannot establish representative reliability.

The current water-force proof now reaches [unknown-agent assurance](force/projection/wrg-fr10-unknown-agent-water-assurance.md). It converts the federated receiver's final no-issue world into a bounded five-state release architecture without treating a negative field screen as proof of universal safety. Independent provenance and sealed reserve become complete service chains with their own source, process, power, consumable, residual, laboratory, custody and authority boundaries.

The [assurance-source family competition](force/projection/wrg-fr10-assurance-source-family-competition.md) now resolves those chains into coastal nuclear, protected-groundwater, variable-feed inland and sealed-transfer formations. The portfolio exposes two remaining universal gaps—residual custody without discharge and thirty-day operation without consumable resupply—while keeping every configuration and rendering denied.

The [closed-loop water endurance competition](force/projection/wrg-fr10-closed-loop-water-endurance.md) converts those gaps into four rival technology and force architectures. It keeps high-grade service, use-specific service, liquid residual, solid residual, consumables, power and second-mission state in separate accounts. All four close the original cases; only regeneration/dewatering and the circular source cell close both denials together, and neither survives a 25-percent power derating. Seventeen full-scale evidence gates remain open.

The [end-to-end independence study](force/projection/wrg-fr10-end-to-end-independence.md) reconnects that endurance module to the complete service path. It registers twelve source, movement, receiver, industrial, personnel, depot, control and mobilization boundaries. Two different sources inside one common support chain preserve service in only four of sixteen worlds. Separating field operations reaches eleven but leaves process-material, reset-depot and base common modes. Only a three-cell force with all twelve boundaries separated reaches all sixteen declared service worlds; the result remains an unproved upper comparator with twenty open gates.

The [cellular power and thermal study](force/projection/wrg-fr10-cellular-power-and-thermal-independence.md) then replaces the graph's abstract power nodes with rival physical and institutional utilities. A common plant, separate generators and hybrid black-start cells can preserve formation service while qualifying no complete cell. Only a multi-vector architecture with `N+1` electrical and thermal modules, local storage, independent energy lineages, offline control and separate reset pathways closes all three declared cells. Its $796.88 billion whole-program comparator remains unadmitted, and its 1,247–2,142 MWh campaign-energy claim remains outside wet-mass closure.

[Proving](proving/README.md) is where the design is allowed to fail. Geographic campaigns place the integrated force in real terrain and built form. The test enterprise converts decisive failures into rival mechanisms and representative trials. Assurance governs sources, uncertainty, privacy, safety, reproducibility and claim admission. Proving may return no winner.

[Programmatics](programmatics/README.md) translates a surviving design into capital, recurring operations, workforce, readiness, industrial capacity and public value. Budgets may compare and phase architectures. They may not silently weaken the service guarantee because the required institution is large.

[Synthesis](synthesis/README.md) states the strongest integrated judgment that the lower layers permit. It cannot overrule the paper, workbook, campaign or assurance record that owns a claim. [History](history/README.md) preserves the discovery sequence, amendments and displaced conclusions so chronology does not masquerade as authority.

## The present proof attempt

The live chain is the New York vertical heat–blackout campaign. It tests whether a neighborhood formation derived after the Harris County flood campaign transfers into a city where the decisive damaged network lies inside occupied buildings.

```text
FROZEN A2 AND S3 RIVALS
          │
          ▼
OFFICIAL GEOGRAPHY + EMPTY OUTCOME REGISTRY + 12,000 STRESS WORLDS
          │
          ▼
OFFICIAL MARGINS + BUILDING PROXIES + PUMS DONOR CUSTODY
          │
          ▼
TARGET ENGINE v1 ── FAIL: post-draw repair creates coherence
          │
          ▼
STRUCTURAL TARGET ENGINE v2
          ├── 36,000 coherent integers · negligible repair
          ├── FAIL: tail stability at registered K=1,000 ceiling
          └── FAIL: published-margin uncertainty calibration
          │
          ▼
MOMENT-RECONCILED ENGINE v3A
          ├── official covariance + SEs + linear identities coexist
          ├── FAIL: Gaussian states violate nonlinear civil bounds
          └── FAIL: repaired centers + selected-tail stability
          │
          ▼
ATOMIC BASIS v3B0
          ├── 1,557 lawful nonnegative cells · exact point witnesses
          ├── 1,525 latent directions · no tested joint identified
          └── STOP: feasible support is not a calibrated distribution
          │
          ▼
ATOMIC MOMENT-CONE AUDIT v3B1A
          ├── authoritative under-five extension · rank 33
          ├── PASS: all 468 published points reconstruct
          ├── FAIL: seven zero-mean positive-variance targets are impossible
          └── STOP: estimator covariance is not state covariance
          │
          ▼
ATOMIC POSTERIOR PREREGISTRATION v3C0
          ├── 4 likelihoods × 3 state priors · 12 retained cases
          ├── 1,152 chains · 57.6M registered transitions
          ├── 25 co-governing gates · 23 deliberate failures
          └── ZERO posterior states until execution and attestation
          │
          ▼
POSTERIOR PREFLIGHT v3C0 → v3C0A
          ├── PASS: construction, propriety and reduced-support moves
          ├── FAIL: G04 passes only 37 of 48 fixed-state paths
          ├── FREEZE: whitened familywise-calibrated successor gate
          └── ZERO posterior transitions or architecture reads
          │
          ▼
STOP — NO DONOR FIT, SYNTHESIS, BUILDING ASSIGNMENT OR ARCHITECTURE RESULT
```

The stop is part of the proof architecture. `v2` repairs the first failure but exposes cross-table spread distortion. `v3A` proves that the published linear moments can coexist, then fails because its Gaussian support is not civil support. `v3B0` registers that support and proves exact point feasibility. `v3B1A` adds the missing under-five authority, but proves that the proposed exact state-moment calibration is impossible. `v3C0` preregisters four likelihood cases, three state-prior cases, constrained computation, nested retention, predictive checks and the architecture firewall before sampling. The Pass 55 preflight then catches a statistically ill-posed covariance gate before the 57.6-million-transition run: all 1,584 diagonal ratios pass, but 11 of 48 paths fail a spectrum- and fourth-moment-dependent Frobenius cutoff. The next method must rerun the frozen `v3C0A` whitened, familywise-calibrated preflight; it may not start posterior chains or fit donors first. The [active frontier](frontier.md) is the single repository-wide authority for that state and the next admissible work.

## Two views of every quantitative decision

The model tree under [`models/`](../models/README.md) mirrors this research tree. A workbook owns formulas, uncertainty, sensitivity and integrity controls. Its paired paper owns interpretation, boundaries and consequences. Neither can silently expand the authority of the other.

The [model catalog](../models/CATALOG.md) is the exhaustive inventory. This architecture is intentionally not. It should remain a map of the argument, while section landing pages explain enduring responsibilities and the frontier records current status.

## Authority and maturity

There is no “latest file wins” rule. Mandate owns required effect; force papers own formation and employment; mission-system papers own physical closure; campaigns own joint geographic performance; workbooks own arithmetic and sensitivity; assurance owns evidence maturity; and synthesis integrates only what those records permit.

Claims progress from a verbal mission (`R0`) through functional architecture (`R1`), bounded concept (`R2`), first-order configuration (`R2+`), closed physics with representative evidence (`R3`) and repeatable formation demonstration (`R4`). No major platform is at `R3`. No rendering is authorized.
