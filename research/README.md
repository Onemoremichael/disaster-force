# Research atlas

This corpus is a systems argument, not a chronology. It begins with the human and physical consequences of catastrophe, derives time-bounded public effects, composes formations that can deliver them, and then asks what national institution, technology base, industrial system, and evidence regime could keep those formations real.

The pass sequence remains useful as an audit trail in the [research log](ledgers/research-log.md). The directory structure is the durable intellectual architecture.

## A guided route through the argument

### 1. Establish the design discipline

The [first-principles framework](foundations/first-principles.md) explains why capability must precede platform. The [force-design framework](foundations/force-design-framework.md) defines service portfolios, operational functions, force layers, readiness bands, and the platform gate. The [empirical baseline](foundations/empirical-baseline.md) establishes all-hazard demand and denominator guardrails without pretending that incompatible loss datasets are one ledger.

The [mission-engineering method](foundations/mission-engineering-method.md) converts catastrophe into population-service-time demand. The [design reference missions](foundations/design-reference-missions.md) provide ten compound campaigns and a national concurrency stress case. The [service-level library](foundations/service-level-library.md) defines what water, power, health, shelter, sanitation, communications, mobility, and public works mean across response phases.

These documents prevent a seductive object—a ship, aircraft, reactor, robot, or mega-base—from becoming its own justification.

### 2. Derive the institution

The [Department of Resilience](institution/department-of-resilience.md) is a zero-based cabinet design with six operating services, joint commands, separate civil and technical authority, science, acquisition, and industrial mobilization. [Force composition](institution/force-composition.md) defines deployable effects, unit-building rules, components, and professions. [Readiness and force generation](institution/readiness-and-force-generation.md) adds concurrency, basing, rotation, maintenance, reserves, and global posture.

The [mature force design](institution/mature-force-design.md) supplies the organizational middle that equipment catalogs usually omit: permanent formation families, joint effect commands, theater-opening and sustainment formations, employment echelons, force packages, and regeneration. It distinguishes owning assets from being able to employ them repeatedly.

### 3. Give the force a technical constitution

The [Resilience Arsenal](architecture/resilience-arsenal.md) organizes system families around mission effects rather than legacy procurement categories. [Information, autonomy, and common enablers](architecture/information-autonomy-and-enablers.md) defines observation, communications, logistics, maintenance, robotics, and sovereign offline operation.

The [common technical architecture](architecture/common-technical-architecture.md) goes deeper: ten common primitives, fifty controlled boundary contracts, four degraded modes, five machine-action authority classes, configuration provenance, and containment of correlated fleet failure. The [platform requirement register](architecture/platform-requirement-register.md) is the control point between ambition and form. It records admitted, conditional, held, and rejected concepts and the evidence each must obtain before `R3`.

### 4. Engineer complete capability chains

The broad portfolio papers establish the landscape:

- [Water, energy, and utility systems](capabilities/water-energy-and-utility-systems.md) treats source, treatment, distribution, wastewater, power, thermal safety, and nuclear water–energy options as coupled systems.
- [Hazard control, access, and earthworks](capabilities/hazard-control-access-and-earthworks.md) links fire, flood, debris, heavy work, corridors, and crossings.
- [Maritime, air mobility, and medical systems](capabilities/maritime-air-mobility-and-medical.md) separates access, sustainment, lift, patient movement, and clinical tiers instead of concentrating them in one universal carrier.

Each developed domain then has a deliberate pair: a force-engineering paper derives the complete formation; a configuration paper explains the linked workbook and exposes its open gates.

| Domain | Force engineering | Configuration analysis | Central finding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Water | [Water restoration force](capabilities/water/force-engineering.md) | [WRG-50](capabilities/water/configuration-model.md) | Distribution, pressure, storage, quality, wastewater, construction, and lift dominate the idea of a treatment plant. |
| Power | [Power restoration force](capabilities/power/force-engineering.md) | [PRG-100](capabilities/power/configuration-model.md) | Reuse, bypass, or rebuild of the receiving network can matter more than the generator technology. |
| Fire | [Fire response force](capabilities/fire/force-engineering.md) | [Fire configuration](capabilities/fire/configuration-model.md) | Distributed interception and campaign closure are different force problems connected by a common control system. |
| Flood | [Flood response force](capabilities/flood/force-engineering.md) | [Flood configuration](capabilities/flood/configuration-model.md) | Early action, hydraulic reversal, interior drainage, breach control, access, and dewatering are separate tests. |

Flood is currently the deepest domain thread. [Flood research, prototyping, and independent test](capabilities/flood/research-test-and-evaluation.md) converts its twenty unresolved evidence gates into rival technology paths, 46 experiment packages, purpose-built ranges, complete mission threads, and a protected independent evaluation authority.

### 5. Build an invention state

The [technology frontier](innovation/technology-frontier.md) asks what military-like technological ambition means when directed at resilience. It defines twelve mission-science directorates, sixty campaigns, an eight-stage maturity funnel, laboratories, manufacturing transition, and three alternative 25-year scale cases. Technology is admitted only when it changes an operational coefficient or distribution—not because it can be described as autonomous, nuclear, artificial-intelligence-enabled, or advanced.

The [National Resilience Proving Network](innovation/national-proving-network.md) designs the evidence infrastructure: ten national flagships, twenty-one regional ranges, twenty-seven conformance nodes, 448 mobile test assets, a professional test force, experimental-power screens, independent data custody, maximum-credible-event safety cases, and environmental closure. A successful demonstration is not the same as reproducible knowledge.

### 6. Reconcile strategy and scale

The [force and resource model](economics/force-and-resource-model.md) explains the original Department-wide workbook: mission demand, concurrency, readiness, inventory, capital, workforce, industrial learning, operating cost, benefits, and sensitivities. The workbook intentionally preserves the gap between the $1T reference capital tranche and the larger illustrative bottom-up force.

The [strategic study](synthesis/strategic-study.md) is the cumulative manuscript. It is the best single file for reading conclusions across the full program, while the modular papers retain the derivation and technical detail that a polished report would otherwise hide.

## Maturity and visualization gate

| State | Meaning | Permitted claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `R0` | Verbal mission | A consequential problem may exist. |
| `R1` | Functional architecture | Major functions and interfaces are identified. |
| `R2` | Bounded concept | Capacity, access, endurance, staffing, and environment have credible ranges. |
| `R2+` | Configuration model | First-order linked balances exist, but representative evidence remains open. |
| `R3` | Physics closed | Mass, energy, flow, heat, structure, stability, and lifecycle balances close. |
| `R4` | Formation closed | Crew, maintenance, connectors, bases, sustainment, and rotation are demonstrated. |

No major platform is yet `R3`. Model `PASS` states mean that formulas and declared architecture relationships reconcile; they do not certify engineering performance. Rendering begins only after physics closure and must depict the whole operating formation, including the unglamorous interfaces that make the public effect possible.

## Research control

The [source ledger](ledgers/source-ledger.md) records the provenance and intended use of evidence. The [evidence-gap ledger](ledgers/evidence-ledger.md) records what each claim family still needs. The [research log](ledgers/research-log.md) records why each pass changed the program, how its models were checked, and where work deliberately stopped.

New work should enter the corpus by intellectual function, not by pass number. A pass may change several layers, but every new claim should have one authoritative home, one visible evidence state, and one path into the synthesis.
