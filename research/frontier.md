# Active research frontier

**Decision state:** 5 September 2026  
**Purpose:** one repository-wide statement of what is believed, what is sealed and what must be learned next

This is not a release note or a queue of disconnected papers. It is the present edge of the argument: the narrow set of uncertainties whose resolution would most change the mature Department of Resilience, its force and its industrial program.

## The current design judgment

The research presently supports a [Department of Resilience](mandate/department-of-resilience.md) built as a permanent force-generation institution, not a larger coordinating agency. Its operational doctrine combines four things that cannot substitute for one another:

1. prepared local continuity that keeps occupied communities alive through early network failure;
2. hazard control and lifeline restoration that change the damaged environment;
3. projected service capacity that crosses broken geography; and
4. civil mobility, reception and return when the building or place cannot safely support continued occupancy.

The strongest revision to the original concept is the importance of the neighborhood seam. National fleets and utility modules fail if the force cannot identify households, enter buildings, preserve individual support relationships, establish local service, move only those who must move and maintain lawful civil custody. The first [`NCAC-35` configuration](force/mobility/neighborhood-continuity-and-access-cell.md) makes that seam a permanent formation problem rather than an improvised local task.

The conventional `A2` reference and technology-forward `S3` rival remain hypotheses. Neither is an acquisition baseline; neither has earned a rendering.

Inventing those relationships would determine the answer before the architecture test begins.

## The next connected moves

### 1. Implement and independently reproduce the civil-state compiler

The method is now preregistered with zero synthetic records. The immediate task is to acquire and freeze the PUMS donor surface, implement the target engine and household-preserving compiler, exercise all deliberate failure tests, and have a second team reproduce it from the source commitments. It must distinguish observed margins from modeled joints, carry published uncertainty into a nested ensemble, enforce building-class capacity, retain `UNKNOWN` where evidence is absent and expose donor distortion and privacy risk. Architecture outcomes remain unavailable while fit, convergence and disclosure are evaluated.

This is larger than a campaign-data problem. A mature Department would need a standing **civil-state production system** that can reconcile protected local records, official statistics, building systems and field observations during network failure without creating a population-surveillance regime. The compiler is therefore an early specification for operational information infrastructure, rights architecture and a new public technical profession.

### 2. Replace administrative proxies with operability evidence

Building form does not establish whether elevators, domestic-water pumps, cooling, refuge areas, backup power, communications or clinical equipment work during a prolonged outage. Borough boundaries do not establish electric, water, communications or transport dependency. The campaign needs governed building-system evidence and observed or defensible service territories before it can make a causal claim.

This exposes a national technology and measurement program: instrumented building archetypes; privacy-preserving condition reporting; standardized continuity interfaces; prepared-building adapters; and disconnected reconciliation between household, utility and response records. These capabilities would change both the `NCAC` formation and the civilian built environment it supports.

### 3. Turn campaign failures into force design—not isolated fixes

New York is not valuable merely as another score. Its purpose is to decide whether the neighborhood formation is portable, which functions belong permanently at neighborhood, regional and national echelons, and which demand preparation rather than mobile mass. A failed architecture should change the common formation grammar, readiness system, deployment network and acquisition portfolio. A successful model should still remain below representative evidence maturity.

The same logic must then move to a sealed third metropolitan holdout with independent geography and custody. Portability, not local optimization, is the bridge from one formation to a national order of battle.

### 4. Reopen the national force and industrial economy after the seam closes

The current Department-wide scale is provisional because neighborhood access, fixed local preparation, reception and human-service labor are not yet fully propagated through the order of battle. Once the formation grammar stabilizes, the program must recompute active, reserve, state, tribal, auxiliary and industrial components; bases and depots; training and rotation; production surge; replacement; and concurrent domestic and invited international campaigns.

Only then can the economic study mature from large illustrative envelopes into a 25-year industrial transformation: shipyards, aircraft and vehicle lines, modular utility production, construction robotics, grid and water interfaces, medical manufacturing, technical education, regional employment, export systems and allied interoperability. Budget parity is a strategic commitment to capability generation, not a license to allocate a trillion dollars before the force is understood.

## Gates that remain binding

The New York contest cannot run until all of the following are frozen or independently accepted:

- an independently reproduced population–household–building compiler, accepted uncertainty ensemble and disclosure controls;
- medical-power and operational-building coverage with explicit missing-state rules;
- operator results reproduced from canonical source snapshots by an independent team;
- actual service territories, or a formally bounded decision to retain the borough proxy;
- causal-feasibility and alternative-dependence cases;
- salted external holdout custody and countersignature.

The wider force cannot claim representative maturity until its critical service chains survive instrumented full-mission trials, independent replication, safety review, degraded-mode operation, maintainability and regeneration. A reconciled workbook is necessary evidence, never sufficient evidence.

## What this structure protects against

Centralizing the frontier prevents progress reporting from overwhelming the argument. More importantly, the research controls prevent three forms of self-deception: choosing data after seeing which architecture wins, mistaking synthetic precision for observed civil state, and scaling a locally optimized solution into a national force before portability is demonstrated.

The next pass should implement the registered compiler through its public building-class layer, beginning with immutable PUMS custody and the coherent target engine, while keeping architecture outcomes sealed and public relational records prohibited. In parallel research terms—not in outcome access—the Department-scale agenda remains force composition, civil-state infrastructure, building operability, technology maturation and the industrial economy. These are one coupled design problem.
