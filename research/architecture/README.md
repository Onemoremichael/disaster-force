# Architecture

Architecture governs the boundaries shared by independently designed forces. Its rule is **standardize interfaces while diversifying critical implementations**: interoperability must not create national common-mode failure.

[The Resilience Arsenal](resilience-arsenal.md) organizes acquisition around complete effect threads. [Information, autonomy, and enablers](information-autonomy-and-enablers.md) defines observation, communications, edge computing, logistics, maintenance, robotics, and connected-to-manual-safe operation. [The common technical architecture](common-technical-architecture.md) allocates physical and digital interfaces, degraded modes, authority, and provenance. [The platform requirement register](platform-requirement-register.md) records admission, holds, conditions, rejected universals, and the evidence required before form or rendering.

Architecture does not choose a vehicle because it is compelling. It makes independently acquired systems compose into service and makes their shared dependencies visible.
