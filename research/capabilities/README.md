# Capabilities

Capabilities owns complete-service physics. Each study begins at a public effect and works backward through source, production, distribution, access, safety, waste, staffing, sustainment, and civil transfer. The directory is not a platform catalog.

The configured domains are:

- [Population continuity](population-continuity/README.md), from cohort requirements to a complete occupied district;
- [Water](water/force-engineering.md), including `WRG-50`, nuclear water–energy, and prepared utility-anchor alternatives;
- [Power](power/force-engineering.md), including the `PRG-100` temporary utility across reuse, bypass, and rebuild states;
- [Fire](fire/force-engineering.md), joining ignition interception to sustained control and community protection; and
- [Flood](flood/force-engineering.md), joining forecast, exclusion, rescue, hydraulic control, access, drainage, and recovery.

[Hazard control, access, and earthworks](hazard-control-access-and-earthworks.md), [maritime, air mobility, and medical systems](maritime-air-mobility-and-medical.md), and [water, energy, and utility systems](water-energy-and-utility-systems.md) preserve broader portfolio architecture where detailed configuration has not yet separated every formation.

Capability studies propose bounded systems. The [common architecture](../architecture/README.md) governs their interfaces, and [research assurance](../assurance/README.md) governs whether they are believable.
