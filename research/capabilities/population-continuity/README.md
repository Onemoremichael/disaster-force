# Population continuity

Population continuity is where the Department's separate water, power, food, sanitation, shelter, health, communications, logistics and civil-authority systems either become one human outcome or fail together.

This domain is organized by scale, not by equipment:

> protected person-hour → PCC-D1 district → PCC-50 complex → PSP-10 pod → Service Entry Cell → regional campaign

Read [the PCC-50 configuration](configuration-model.md) for the cohort, topology, food, water, sanitation, energy and operating-workforce system. Then read [the PCC-D1 district configuration](district-configuration.md) for the physical occupied unit, including mass, packed volume, site work, installation, interfaces, commissioning and release.

The district study is authoritative for shelter-system mass. Its 78.71-tonne transformational case supersedes the earlier 26.05-tonne district target as a complete configuration. The prepared-receiver result is a dependency-bounded candidate, not a validated capability.

The open architectural question is now the division between mobile and fixed capacity. The next research object is a National Receiving Lattice that can be counted as force only when sites, structures, interfaces, records, stocks, people, inspection and exercises remain ready after correlated hazards.

All work in this domain remains R2+. Vehicle and facility renderings are blocked.
