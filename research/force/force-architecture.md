# The Department of Resilience force architecture

## Executive judgment

The United States should treat resilience projection as a second great instrument of national power: a permanent capacity to prevent catastrophic civil-system collapse, restore the material conditions of ordinary life and stabilize partners by invitation. The mature institution is not an enlarged disaster agency, a collection of hazard bureaus or a fleet of spectacular machines. It is a **force-generating and force-employing system** able to convert a constitutional public promise into safe service under damaged conditions, sustain that service through a campaign and return both the community and the force to readiness.

The architecture has three institutional objects and five non-interchangeable design axes.

The **Department of Resilience** owns public obligation, policy, budget, civil authority and accountability. The **National Resilience Establishment** owns the services, schools, laboratories, acquisition organizations, bases, depots, reserves and industrial relationships that create capacity. The **Resilience Force** is the certified subset of formations, stocks, transport and headquarters available for employment.

Within that structure:

- seven operating services generate people, doctrine and equipment;
- nine civilian-effect portfolios define what must be delivered;
- formations combine service contributions into complete deployable chains;
- joint commands allocate and employ those formations across campaigns; and
- components determine how capacity is recruited, paid, mobilized and regenerated.

Those distinctions resolve a central ambiguity in the earlier corpus. Fire, flood, drought, earthquake and storm are not branches of the force. They are damage environments against which cross-service formations are designed and tested. Water, power, care, access and civil continuity are not automatically services either. They are effects that often require several professional services to close. A maritime service may generate a floating water-production formation, but safe water ashore also requires infrastructure, land engineering, health, community-distribution and civil-authority capacity. The force must be organized around that truth.

At mature scale the institution would be comparable to defense in standing, not mechanically identical in structure. Its parity standard is whether it possesses a sovereign mission, permanent professions, concurrent-force planning, strategic mobility, operational commands, a research and acquisition system, industrial depth, bases and depots, independent test, reserve components, global access and a durable public theory of success. Dollar parity is one consequence of that architecture, not its definition.

## 1. The strategic object

Military force projection makes organized coercive power available across distance and time. Resilience projection makes organized **civil-system survival and recovery power** available across the same dimensions. Its strategic effects are different but no less real.

Domestically, it limits mortality, displacement, institutional failure, regional economic fracture and the compounding loss that occurs when water, power, care, access, communications and public legitimacy fail together. Internationally, it offers a form of statecraft based on invited stabilization: restoring life-support systems, expanding partner competence and leaving behind usable infrastructure, standards and professional networks rather than dependency on a permanent American presence.

The strategic unit is not aid delivered. It is a verified change in a civilian condition:

> **A defined population or critical civil function receives a defined bundle of safe service by a stated time, for a stated duration, under stated damage, with lawful authority and a viable transfer path.**

This is the force-design equivalent of an operational end state. It prevents impressive intermediate outputs from becoming false victories. Megawatts at a generator do not count if unsafe wiring prevents use. Treated water at a ship does not count if no lawful conveyance, pressure, quality assurance or public distribution exists. Clinical beds do not count without staff, oxygen, sterile supply, patient regulation and discharge. Passenger lift does not count unless people can be contacted, collected accessibly, admitted to a named receiver and supported through return or durable transition.

The force therefore projects **closed service chains**, not commodities or platforms.

## 2. First-principles design axioms

Seven axioms govern the architecture.

**Service precedes organization.** The public condition is specified before deciding which service, command, contractor or platform supplies it.

**Time is an independent requirement.** Eventual restoration cannot compensate for lethal delay. Alert, first safe effect, initial operating capability, full service, endurance and transfer are separately measured.

**Concurrency sizes the establishment.** The nation maintains capacity for simultaneous domestic campaigns, an enduring recovery, training and maintenance demand, and a bounded invited international mission. A unit counted twice is not capacity.

**The chain closes at use.** Source, production, distribution, access, labor, safety, waste, sustainment and transfer remain inside the mission boundary. Dependencies may be declared; they may not disappear.

**Civil authority and technical authority remain plural.** Affected governments set legitimate priorities. Operational commanders integrate action. Licensed technical authorities release systems for safe use. Independent assurance authorities decide what evidence and public claims are admissible. None inherits the others’ power merely by possessing equipment.

**Readiness includes regeneration.** A formation is not ready because one crew and one asset can depart. The count includes complete teams, movement, reception, sustainment, replacement, maintenance, stocks, home-station continuity and the path back to certification.

**Mission failure creates the technology portfolio.** Novel systems are pursued when they change a binding service coefficient or remove a dangerous dependency. Technical glamour, commercial availability and visual persuasiveness are not requirements.

These axioms turn the proposal from “large emergency response” into a distinct theory of national capacity.

## 3. One architecture, five coordinates

The force cannot be represented by one organization chart. Every deployable capability has five coordinates, each answering a different question.

| Coordinate | Governing question | Canonical unit | What it must not be confused with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Generating service | Who builds the profession and owns the lifecycle? | service, school, acquisition sponsor, depot | the effect delivered in one campaign |
| Civilian effect | What condition must become true for the public? | service level and effect portfolio | a permanent chain of command |
| Formation | What indivisible package can close the effect? | team, module, group, command, task force | a platform or loose resource list |
| Employment command | Who allocates and integrates formations in time and geography? | geographic, global or functional joint command | the service that generated the unit |
| Component | Under what readiness, authority and mobilization terms does capacity exist? | permanent, reserve, state/territorial, tribal, auxiliary, industrial, partner | a percentage that receives identical operational credit |

The architecture is therefore a matrix. A high-flow flood-control group may be generated mostly by the Land Engineering and Infrastructure Restoration Services, deliver hazard-control, access, water and public-works effects, deploy under a regional command, receive mobility and sustainment from functional commands and combine permanent, reserve, state and industrial components. No single label describes the formation adequately.

This matrix is deliberately resistant to bureaucratic stovepipes. Services retain professional depth and lifecycle accountability, while joint formations and commands prevent them from optimizing a machine at the expense of the public-service chain.

### 4.1 Department of Resilience

The Department is the constitutional and fiscal principal. It defines the national service guarantee, requests and allocates resources, maintains intergovernmental and international compacts, governs civil rights and privacy, establishes readiness policy and reports risk to the public. It should not manage every unit or certify its own technical claims.

The **Civil Resilience Authority** is the institutional interface with governors, tribal governments, territories, municipalities, infrastructure owners, health authorities and invited foreign governments. It converts lawful priorities into public-effect objectives, records unresolved conflict and defines transfer conditions. It is not a second operational headquarters.

The **Resilience Staff** integrates requirements, force design, readiness, global posture and joint doctrine. Its purpose is to prevent seven services from building seven self-contained response systems and to expose where the national mission promise exceeds available complete capacity.

### 4.2 National Resilience Establishment

The Establishment is everything that must exist before a force can truthfully be called ready: recruiting systems, academies, technical colleges, service schools, operational units, laboratories, design bureaus, program offices, proving grounds, bases, depots, shipyards, arsenals, stockpiles, reserve institutions and certified industrial capacity.

It owns force generation but does not automatically control force employment. This separation protects long-term readiness from the pressure to commit every nominal asset and prevents campaign commands from hollowing the schools, depots and home stations that create future capacity.

### 4.3 Resilience Force

The Force is not the department’s workforce or equipment inventory. It is the set of headquarters and complete formations that have satisfied their readiness vectors and are assigned, preparing for assignment or held in the national concurrency reserve. Contractor capacity counts only when its people, equipment, priority rights, movement, spares, data and cold-activation performance are governed. Local capacity counts only to the extent that its survival and availability are evidenced for the relevant damage case.

## 5. Seven generating services

Services are durable professional and acquisition homes. They organize, train, equip and sustain; joint commands employ.

| Service | Lifecycle responsibility | Boundary that forces joint employment |
|---|---|---|
| Land Engineering | access, earthworks, bridging, debris, temporary works, ground hazard control and heavy construction | does not own utility operation, civil priority or the complete distribution chain |
| Air Response | strategic and tactical aviation, aerial fire control, sensing, austere air operations, relay and uncrewed aviation | aircraft output does not equal ground effect, reception or sustained service |
| Maritime Response | ocean, coastal, river and lake access; sealift; offshore production; port opening; salvage and floating bases | shipboard production does not equal shore distribution, public access or transfer |
| Infrastructure Restoration | power, water, wastewater, communications, controls, fuel, thermal systems and deployable public works | technical restoration does not determine population priority or lawful service access |
| Health Protection | prehospital through definitive care, public health, clinical logistics, chronic-care continuity and workforce replacement | clinical capacity depends on mobility, utilities, receiving systems and public authority |
| Community Stabilization | shelter, food, essential goods, public information, education, temporary administration, housing transition and economic restart | social service cannot substitute for physical access, safe utilities or hazard control |
| Civil Mobility and Continuity | household contact, accessible collection, passenger movement, transfer nodes, destination continuity, reunification and return | passenger motion depends on common-user lift, route assurance, receivers and health regulation |

The seven-service settlement is canonical at the institutional level. It does not imply seven equal budgets or seven copies of every enabling function. It assigns one lifecycle owner while preserving the fact that operational effects are joint.

Hazard expertise is organized differently. Fire, flood, seismic, drought, storm, heat, cold, biological and contaminated-environment centers develop doctrine, forecasts, test envelopes and campaign plans across the services. They do not become independent fleets. A megafire campaign may require air response, land engineering, water, power, health, community stabilization, civil mobility and national sustainment at once.

## 6. Nine civilian-effect portfolios

Effects are the stable vocabulary of public outcomes. They are not another set of services.

| Effect portfolio | Verified output | Typical generating services |
|---|---|---|
| Civil coordination, safety and public information | lawful priorities, usable warnings, safe work and accountable public records | Community, Health, all technical services |
| Food, shelter and essential-goods continuity | safely supported person-days and a durable exit from emergency support | Community, Infrastructure, Mobility |
| Health and medical continuity | staffed acuity-specific care and intact patient pathways | Health, Air, Mobility, Infrastructure |
| Energy and thermal continuity | critical loads continuously served at required quality | Infrastructure, Land, Maritime, Air |
| Communications, computation and public-service access | priority users and areas connected at declared availability in degraded modes | Infrastructure, Community, Air |
| Mobility, access and distribution | people or materiel delivered from true origin to complete service | Mobility, Land, Air, Maritime |
| Water, sanitation and hygiene | compliant water at use and wastewater safely managed | Infrastructure, Land, Maritime, Health |
| Hazard and environmental control | measured reduction in the active damaging process and its residuals | Land, Air, Maritime, Infrastructure, Health |
| Housing, public works and economic continuity | safe occupancy and locally governed civil functions restored | Community, Land, Infrastructure |

This table is a planning crosswalk, not a claim that one service contribution is always primary. Its purpose is to preserve the outcome when organizations change. A formation’s service contract may contain several effects, but each effect retains its own unit, time band and acceptance condition.

## 7. Formation grammar

The force fights system collapse through **effect-complete formations**. Every formation record contains the same ten fields:

1. supported population, civil function or geography;
2. required effect and point of safe use;
3. alert, entry, initial, full and transfer time bands;
4. damage and degraded-mode envelope;
5. organic people, equipment, stocks, command and data;
6. declared supporting formations and prepared local interfaces;
7. movement, reception and operating-base requirement;
8. sustainment, waste, replacement and maintenance burden;
9. civil, technical and independent release authorities; and
10. regeneration standard after employment.

The record is indivisible for readiness credit. A pump without hose, discharge, crew, power, maintenance and environmental release is not a fraction of a flood-control formation. It is unavailable potential.

### 7.1 Echelons

Echelons describe increasing integration and endurance, not prestige.

| Echelon | Operational object | Independent burden |
|---|---|---|
| Capability team or detachment | one licensed or bounded task | immediate safety, communications and local support |
| Mission module | one measured effect at a site or short route | 72-hour entry load and immediate interface tail |
| Response group | several modules closing one consequence chain | organic command, maintenance, data and seven-day endurance |
| Metropolitan or watershed response command | several groups across one connected civil system | theater distribution, replacement and 30-day campaign control |
| Regional resilience force | multiple simultaneous incidents or one major campaign | standing joint headquarters, reception, sustainment and reserve integration |
| Catastrophe stabilization force | multi-region collapse or several linked campaigns | national mobility, scarce systems, industrial activation and regeneration depth |
| Global stabilization group | invited international mission | sovereign liaison, austere independence, partner interoperability and transfer |

The existing [`NCAC-35`](mobility/neighborhood-continuity-and-access-cell.md) result demonstrates why names alone are dangerous. Once household contact, vertical access, route release, utilities, rights, accessible collection and seven-day sustainment are included, one neighborhood continuity cell screens at 7,587 deployed people and 6,388 packed tonnes in its conventional architecture. That is not a validated table of organization. It is evidence that a team-sized label cannot absorb a formation-sized service contract.

The [Household Collection Group configuration](generation/household-collection-group-formation-design.md) now resolves the first echelon inside that contract. `NCAC-35` remains the complete neighborhood-system test; `HCG-35B` is the seven-company deployable origin formation. Prepared districts and heavy route, power and water capacity move to named supporting owners but remain serial mission requirements. The distinction makes the mobile formation lighter without allowing the architecture or budget to erase the rest of the system.

The [shared-capacity allocation study](generation/shared-capacity-allocation-and-claim-denial.md) supplies the governing consequence. A supporting object is capacity only when a named source can satisfy a named claim in configuration, place, time and authority without violating another obligation or protected reserve. The [canonical cargo study](projection/canonical-cargo-and-transport-closure.md) applies that rule to movement: physical mass, chargeable cube, position class, deadline, mode, complete-package identity and receiver state must remain visible together. The Joint Capacity Authority maintains the reservation boundary; generating services, civil leadership, technical release authorities, campaign commands and independent assurance retain deliberately separate powers.

### 7.2 Campaign force

A **Resilience Campaign Force** is task-organized around a vector of service deficits. Its headquarters owns the integration of effects, not the professions that generate them. At activation it receives:

```text
SERVICE-DEFICIT VECTOR
population × effect × geography × time × duration × damage
                         │
                         ▼
JOINT FORCE ALLOCATION
ready formations + lift + entry + receivers + sustainment + reserve
                         │
                         ▼
CAMPAIGN FORCE
local protection + hazard control + lifeline restoration
projected service + selective movement + receiving capacity
                         │
                         ▼
TRANSFER AND REGENERATION
local ownership restored · force returned to certified readiness
```

The campaign commander cannot waive structural, electrical, clinical, aviation, maritime, environmental or worker-safety release. The affected civil authority cannot create capacity by priority declaration. A technical authority cannot select the public objective. Independent test and rights authorities cannot command production. The design relies on explicit friction among legitimate powers rather than the fiction that one commander can own every truth.

## 8. Command architecture

The mature command system has three categories.

**Geographic commands** maintain continuous knowledge, plans, relationships and assigned forces for domestic regions. Boundaries follow correlated hazard, lifeline, mobility and receiving geography rather than inherited administrative maps. A Global Resilience Command maintains access, partner exercises, forward stocks and invited stabilization plans.

**Operational functional commands** govern national networks too scarce or interconnected for geographic ownership. Strategic Mobility Command operates the national deployment-and-distribution enterprise for common-user force and cargo movement plus theater entry. Civil Mobility and Continuity Command controls the person-to-receiver chain. Strategic Sustainment Command controls national stocks, depots, repair, replacement, regeneration and reverse flow. Resilience Medical Command regulates scarce patients, staff and definitive capacity. Information and Forecast Command maintains hazard, civil-state, infrastructure and readiness pictures in degraded conditions. Joint Campaign Assurance Command owns dependency models, common-mode trials and force-closure certification while remaining outside public-priority and technical-release authority.

**Generating institutions** should not masquerade as operational commands. Industrial Mobilization Administration maintains supplier, tooling, materials, surge and government-fallback capacity. Training and Doctrine Command is an administrative generating command that runs joint schools, collective certification and lessons. Science, Engineering and Acquisition governs laboratories, design bureaus, programs and test articles. They generate options and readiness; they do not become incident commanders.

This classification reduces headquarters proliferation. A new command is justified only when it controls a national allocation problem that no service or geographic commander can close. A new service is justified only when a distinct profession, lifecycle and acquisition system cannot be maintained elsewhere. A new formation is justified only when an effect requires an indivisible operating package.

## 9. Readiness and force generation

Readiness is the minimum of complete-formation capacities, not an average of impressive percentages:

\[
R_f=\min(R_{people},R_{material},R_{movement},R_{reception},R_{sustainment},R_{authority},R_{regeneration}).
\]

These are correlated capacities, not independent probabilities. If a clinical group has beds and staff but lacks receiving oxygen, power quality or patient movement, its readiness for that campaign is zero regardless of its internal average.

Every formation resides in one of four force pools: **available**, **preparing**, **committed** or **regenerating**. Deployment ends operationally only when equipment, people, stocks, data and home-station obligations return to their certification standard. The national allocation authority must preserve a residual reserve explicitly; otherwise the first catastrophe consumes the response to the second.

Components supply different kinds of depth. The permanent federal component holds immediate, scarce and globally deployable systems. The federal reserve supplies endurance and specialist replacement. State and territorial components provide rooted mass and domestic legitimacy. Tribal compacts provide sovereign, place-specific capability without subsuming tribal authority. A civil auxiliary contributes governed skills and local continuity. The certified industrial force provides production, repair, construction, transport and licensed surge under enforceable availability terms. Partner capacity is credited only under agreement and never treated as U.S.-owned reserve.

The decisive readiness product is not the number of assets. It is the number of complete formations that can be employed concurrently without consuming the institutions that must produce their replacements.

## 10. The capability-development enterprise

The Department must be able to invent, not merely buy. Its innovation system should resemble a continuous competition among rival ways to close mission chains.

The unit of research is a **binding operational coefficient**: liters delivered per unit of energy and residual burden; route-kilometers lawfully released per crew-hour; occupied floors reached per team-hour; megawatts delivered per tonne through a damaged entry network; staffed care-hours sustained per clinical replacement stream; hectares contained per safe air-and-ground cycle; or days of autonomous utility operation before specialist intervention.

Every technology program must identify:

- the failed service chain and governing coefficient;
- a baseline and at least one rival mechanism;
- the complete-system boundary, including labor and residuals;
- the representative damage envelope;
- interfaces with other formations and prepared local systems;
- a manufacturing, maintenance and degraded-mode concept;
- a test sequence able to kill the program; and
- the condition under which the result changes force structure.

### 10.1 Mission threads, not showcase platforms

The present corpus identifies six coupled mission threads that deserve defense-scale competition.

| Mission thread | Binding problem | Families of novel capability—not predetermined solutions |
|---|---|---|
| Enter a broken theater | national capacity cannot become local effect through damaged ports, airfields, roads and networks | no-pier transfer, austere heavy lift, autonomous cross-dock, rapid survey and node-opening systems |
| Reach occupied civil terrain | streets, buildings, vertical circulation and records fail below the scale of national logistics | robotic access, temporary vertical systems, route-release work cells, disconnected civil-state infrastructure |
| Control an active hazard | the damaging process continues faster than conventional crews can observe or intervene | persistent fire aviation, megaflow pumping, breach arrest, autonomous earthworks, atmospheric and hydraulic sensing |
| Produce and distribute lifelines | large production assets lack rapid, safe last-mile networks | mobile fluid, power and thermal buses; self-forming pipe and cable corridors; district adapters; residual-control systems |
| Preserve care and human continuity | hospitals, households, caregivers and receivers fail as connected networks | distributed definitive care, clinical workforce replacement, accessible collection, continuity modules and verified receivers |
| Sustain, transfer and regenerate | early output collapses under maintenance, waste, supply and personnel exhaustion | modular depots, field-qualified repair, autonomous inventory, circular materials, replacement formations and transfer systems |

A nuclear-powered desalination carrier belongs in the fourth thread, but its requirement does not begin with a hull. It begins with a water deficit, source chemistry, energy and heat balance, residual disposition, coastal access, survivability, maintenance, licensing, crew, throughput to shore, rapid pipeline deployment and the operating geography that makes a floating source preferable to inland alternatives. The same method governs a purpose-built firefighting aircraft: the requirement is a safe, sustained reduction in fire behavior across a complete sensing, basing, loading, airspace, maintenance and ground-integration cycle—not maximum liquid carried on one sortie.

### 10.2 Common primitives

The Department should fund common primitives beneath those mission threads: observation and provenance; causal mission models; extreme-environment autonomy; mobile power/fluid/thermal interfaces; advanced separations; rapid infrastructure fabrication; adaptive structures and materials; distributed biomanufacturing; industrial digital threads; and degraded communications and edge compute. The [common technical architecture](../systems/common-technical-architecture.md) governs how those primitives cross mission systems without creating one national common mode.

Commonality is earned only when a primitive survives at least three distinct mission threads and does not create a single national failure mode. Open physical and information interfaces should permit several implementations, fuels, suppliers and control paths. Standardization should reduce integration burden while preserving diversity in critical mechanisms.

## 11. Acquisition and the resilience industrial economy

The acquisition object is a **formation product line**, not a platform purchased in isolation. A program baseline contains the production article, crews, training devices, software, data rights, handling equipment, spares, depot plan, base interfaces, environmental and safety cases, supply-chain map, mobilization tooling and receiving-side adapters required for force credit.

That rule changes industrial structure. The Department would need first-tier integrators in response shipbuilding, severe-duty aviation, mobile utilities, autonomous construction, distributed health systems, civil communications, temporary infrastructure and resilience logistics. It would also need public design bureaus and technically expert program offices capable of controlling interfaces and rejecting vendor-defined mission boundaries.

Competition should occur at three levels:

1. rival mechanisms compete before a platform form is fixed;
2. qualified integrators compete to deliver open-interface system families; and
3. suppliers, depots and alternate production lines compete and rehearse within the mature family.

Government-owned fallback production is justified where correlated demand, long tooling lead or national dependence makes commercial availability noncredible. Private firms remain central, but “available on the market” receives no readiness credit without enforceable priority, trained labor, material access, configuration control and demonstrated cold activation.

Exports should be complete sovereign capability packages: equipment, open interfaces, local production rights, training, maintenance, spares, exercises, data governance and certification. The strategic outcome is a partner able to command and sustain the system without permanent American operation. That creates durable interoperability and an industrial economy without turning disaster dependence into a business model.

## 12. The resource architecture of parity

Parity with defense is a vector rather than one appropriations number.

| Dimension | Evidence of genuine parity |
|---|---|
| Mission | a statutory national objective and explicit concurrency promise |
| People | permanent professions, reserve depth, schools and replacement systems |
| Operations | standing geographic and functional commands with ready formations |
| Mobility | owned or assured strategic lift, entry, onward movement and reception |
| Capital | purpose-built fleets, utilities, bases, depots, arsenals and prepared nodes |
| Innovation | laboratories, advanced projects, rival prototypes, ranges and independent test |
| Industry | stable demand, supplier depth, mobilization tooling, public fallback and export finance |
| Evidence | destructive trials, readiness reporting, independent cost and public claim control |

Current quantitative work supplies only a scale screen. The [canonical allocation](generation/canonical-force-allocation-and-fiscal-reconciliation.md) carries a provisional `$15.179 trillion` capital and `10.484 million` affiliated-billet contradiction; the [shared-capacity audit](generation/shared-capacity-allocation-and-claim-denial.md) holds that headline because ownership credits are not yet item-allocated. The cargo compiler adds a force-design constraint rather than a cost: the inherited enterprise delivers 25.5 percent of physical mass but only 13.0 percent of complete formation equivalents, before any receiver earns admission. The [technology-frontier case](../proving/test-enterprise/technology-frontier.md) separately examines a `$2 trillion` invention enterprise, and the [proving-network case](../proving/test-enterprise/national-proving-network.md) examines `$320 billion` in test infrastructure. These are not additive appropriations recommendations and do not yet share an independent work breakdown. They establish that equal seriousness does not fit inside a symbolic `$1 trillion` one-time fund if the nation also expects permanent crews, bases, maintenance, replacement, strategic mobility and global availability.

A mature fiscal constitution should separate five accounts: formation and infrastructure capital; standing operations and personnel; research, development, test and evaluation; industrial and strategic reserve readiness; and incident employment plus recovery transfer. Keeping them separate prevents a severe season from consuming modernization, a development overrun from hollowing current readiness or reconstruction grants from being counted as deployable force.

The next Department-wide cost model must reconcile every program exactly once, phase workforce and industrial learning, distinguish procurement from lifecycle replacement and expose which public guarantee is reduced when a fiscal constraint binds. Budget balance cannot be achieved by silently moving distribution, waste, maintenance or local receiving capacity outside the model.

## 13. A 25-year force-building sequence

Full political authorization does not eliminate physical maturation time. Shipyards, laboratories, schools, licensed professions, test ranges, suppliers and depots must still be built and learned.

| Epoch | Force-building result | Governing proof |
|---|---|---|
| Years 0–5: constitute | Department, civil authority, services, design bureaus, initial commands, interface standards and first proving capacity | authority boundaries work; inherited capabilities are measured rather than assumed |
| Years 5–10: field first increments | certified neighborhood, entry, utility, medical, hazard-control and receiving formations; initial regional commands and depots | complete service threads close in representative regional campaigns |
| Years 10–15: create national depth | reserve and state components, strategic lift, production learning, multiple suppliers, large fleet increments and replacement pools | two concurrent domestic campaigns close without exhausting the reserve |
| Years 15–20: achieve campaign endurance | mature sustainment, regeneration, prepared national receiving geography and continuous technology refresh | one prolonged catastrophe plus a second major event and routine readiness remain supportable |
| Years 20–25: establish global resilience power | partner-certified formations, forward access, co-production, export support and allied campaign interoperability | an invited overseas stabilization does not reduce the domestic guarantee below its floor |

This sequence is not a transition plan for preserving current agencies. Existing organizations are sources of people, authorities, facilities and evidence. Their functions may be absorbed, divided or retired. The target architecture is governed by the public guarantee, not by the need to give every predecessor a box.

## 14. How the force knows it is succeeding

The primary campaign measure is the time integral of unmet safe service across affected people and critical functions:

\[
L=\sum_{e,z,t} w_{e,z,t}\,P_{z,t}\,
\max\left(0,S^{required}_{e,z,t}-S^{verified}_{e,z,t}\right)\Delta t.
\]

`L` cannot stand alone. The force also reports mortality and morbidity, unsafe displacement, distribution across vulnerable groups, responder harm, environmental residuals, local institutional burden, consent and remedy failures, duration to civil transfer, cost, and the readiness consumed from the next campaign.

Three measures are explicitly rejected as strategic success: assets deployed, commodities delivered and gross output at the production source. They remain useful logistics diagnostics. They do not establish that a person received safe service or that the force left the community and itself more stable.

## 15. Failure doctrine

The architecture is designed to reveal failure before national multiplication.

A service may not certify its own operational test. A geographic commander may not double-count a nationally allocated formation. A campaign may not consume the protected concurrency reserve without an explicit reduction in the public promise. An industrial contract may not count as capacity until cold activation succeeds. A platform may not receive force credit before its distribution, sustainment, waste and transfer chains close. An algorithm may not replace accountable civil priority or professional release. A successful average may not conceal systematic failure in hard-to-reach populations. A rendering may not harden an exterior form while mass, energy, interfaces and operating concept remain open.

The Department should publish unsupported missions as readiness risk. The authority to say “the nation cannot yet guarantee this service under these conditions” is part of strategic credibility. A force that claims universality by hiding dependencies will fail precisely when correlated catastrophe makes those dependencies binding.

## 16. Decisions established and questions still open

This architecture establishes a mature institutional grammar:

- Department, Establishment and Force are different objects;
- services, effects, formations, commands and components are different coordinates;
- seven services generate while joint commands employ;
- hazard expertise shapes doctrine and test rather than creating separate fleets;
- complete formations and closed service chains receive readiness credit;
- civil, operational, technical and assurance authority remain distinct;
- capability development begins with failed mission coefficients;
- acquisition includes the formation, industrial and receiving tails; and
- parity is measured across national-capacity dimensions, not one dollar comparison.

It does not prove the correct number of regional commands, the inventory of any formation, the final balance among active, reserve, state and industrial components, the performance of a novel platform, the nationwide basing plan or the authoritative 25-year budget. Those require linked quantitative and campaign evidence.

The governed force chain now reaches from the [National Formation Registry](generation/national-formation-registry.md) through [canonical allocation](generation/canonical-force-allocation-and-fiscal-reconciliation.md), [shared-capacity control](generation/shared-capacity-allocation-and-claim-denial.md) and [canonical cargo closure](projection/canonical-cargo-and-transport-closure.md). It identifies forty-eight families and compiles them into external transport positions, but still has no `R3` formation, serial national manifest or admitted receiver. The next force-design work should derive rival carrier–node requirement envelopes from one tagged representative formation and the full campaign compiler. In parallel, a **capability-development portfolio** should deepen the registry's mission-failure mapping into rival mechanisms, common primitives, proving facilities and force-structure consequences. A **single Department work breakdown** should then reconcile capital, operations, workforce, industrial learning and replacement across the force exactly once.

Only after representative configurations close should exterior renderings begin. At that point they will communicate a defensible system—intakes, pipe reels, flight cycles, cranes, thermal rejection, maintenance access, crew spaces, safety zones and interfaces—not merely an attractive object.
