# MESA-1C: supplier-neutral architecture and trial constitution

## Executive judgment

The Department of Resilience cannot build a trillion-dollar clean-sheet force with the evidentiary habits of an emergency-grant program. It requires a permanent technical constitution able to distinguish an audacious but testable architecture from an attractive drawing, a second vendor from a genuinely independent failure lineage, and a successful demonstration from a system that succeeded because the range, instrumentation or builder quietly helped it.

MESA-1C supplies that constitution for the representative evidence hardware defined in [MESA-1R](mesa1r-representative-evidence-hardware-and-hazard-envelope.md). It does not select production hardware. It selects eleven prototype baselines from thirty-three supplier-neutral architecture candidates, freezes the serial and interface records that each first article must satisfy, protects reference and destructive article banks, and registers sixty-eight trials and sixty-four co-governing evidence gates across eight campaigns. The program runs seventy-eight months and uses a USD 6.8 billion subportfolio already nested inside the USD 24 billion F01 metrology family. Every physical claim remains open.

The central decision is constitutional: **hard physical and institutional gates precede weighted merit**. Reliability, isolation, maintainability, integration and innovation matter only after a candidate proves seven-day independent energy, complete local retention, physical non-actuation, diverse decisive ancestry, representative mass and volume, outward heat rejection, hazard survival, field calibration, removal equivalence and independent custody. A candidate that misses one of those conditions is rejected even if it is cheaper, easier to integrate or more novel.

This order matters far beyond MESA. A mature Resilience Force will need unprecedented water-energy carriers, atmospheric heavy lift, variable-footprint terrain systems, fire aviation, autonomous construction, rapid utility corridors, hardened civil receivers and district-scale cooling. Many will initially exist only as competing physical hypotheses. The acquisition system therefore needs a way to reward radical technology while preventing a prototype’s conveniences from becoming hidden public-service dependencies. MESA-1C is an early model of that system.

## The problem is not instrumentation; it is epistemic sovereignty

A conventional development program often allows the prime contractor to own the vehicle, its sensors, its software, the test telemetry and much of the interpretation. That arrangement can work when the disputed question is incremental performance against a mature standard. It is inadequate when the Department is attempting a new category of national power and the decisive mechanisms do not yet have commercial benchmarks.

The Resilience Force must be able to answer five questions without asking the system under test for permission:

1. What physical event occurred?
2. Did the complete public-service chain remain inside its registered boundary?
3. Did the evidence system alter that result?
4. Can an independent authority reproduce the conclusion from original records?
5. Did apparently separate competitors share the same decisive technical cause?

Those questions require sovereign measurement capacity. The evidence formation must carry its own energy, time, storage, calibration and custody; it must observe governing physics through diverse mechanisms; it must survive communications loss; and it must fail before it can support the mission. Its presence must then be paired with a burden-matched emulator and a bare article so that mass, center of gravity, impedance, heat and operator behavior can be separated from measurement benefit.

This is why MESA-1C treats the measurement formation as a force system rather than an audit appliance. It has serial-controlled articles, protected stocks, independent authorities, range capacity, rival industrial lineages and a campaign plan. The same seriousness later applies to water production, fire suppression, energy projection, civil cooling and heavy access.

## Architecture constitution

### Admission before preference

Thirty-three candidates compete across eleven subsystem classes. Each class contains three contrasting hypotheses: a structurally credible reference, a more isolated or ambitious alternative, and a convenience architecture designed to expose a recurring failure such as streaming, shared power, central concentration or provider custody.

Ten hard gates determine admission:

| Gate | Constitutional purpose |
| --- | --- |
| 168-hour independent power | Network and mission power loss cannot erase the observation interval. |
| Complete local retention | The original record exists at the article or node before export. |
| No actuation path | Evidence can stop credit but cannot change vehicle or service behavior. |
| Diverse critical ancestry | Separate names do not hide a shared decisive component, process or firmware root. |
| Mass and volume envelope | The tested configuration includes the complete physical burden. |
| Outward heat path | Evidence heat leaves through its own boundary and cannot become useful mission heat. |
| Hazard survival | The node remains interpretable through the registered shock, contamination, water, fire and thermal worlds. |
| Field calibration | Traveling standards can test the measurement chain before and after exposure. |
| Removal equivalence | Instrumented, emulated and bare configurations can isolate measurement effects. |
| Independent custody | Builders and operators cannot alter or selectively release originals. |

Only an architecture passing all ten gates may enter the weighted comparison. Among admitted candidates, reliability and failure isolation each receive 25 percent; maintainability and integration each receive 15 percent; innovation receives 20 percent. The innovation weight is intentionally large enough to reward technical reach and intentionally too weak to compensate for a broken constitutional boundary.

Fourteen of the thirty-three candidates are admissible. Eleven are selected as prototype baselines. Several nonselected architectures remain valuable comparators: the battery-only energy pack, single-physics pressure mosaic and single-trailer receiver are not declared impossible. They lose because the selected competitors expose and isolate more decisive failure mechanisms.

### The selected chain

| System | Prototype baseline | First-principles reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Air tile cassette | `ATC-2` three-zone isolated vault | A local casualty cannot simultaneously erase sensing, utility-boundary and custody truth. |
| Terrain tile cassette | `TTC-2` four-pod conformal cassette | Evidence remains local through crush, contamination and loss of one pod. |
| Air carrier node | `ACN-2` triple zonal vault with passive witness ring | Lift, propulsion, contact and custody paths are physically separated. |
| Terrain carrier node | `TCN-2` twelve pod-local nodes with dual vaults | Contact evidence survives pod, ring and central-vault faults without a remote array. |
| Civil receiver | `CRS-2` three-module station | Assay, utility truth and lawful civil release do not share one casualty. |
| Pressure skin | `PSS-3` dual-physics mosaic with optical geometry | Force and contact boundary can be reconstructed from unlike physical observations. |
| Passive interface | `PIF-3` split-physics transducer and optical state mirror | Non-actuation is inspectable in hardware, not asserted by software. |
| Independent energy | `EPS-2` primary endurance core with rechargeable event buffer | Low standby loss and isolated peak power coexist without a mission-energy tie. |
| Denied time | `TKS-2` dual atomic roots with quartz witness | Three physical roots preserve event order and reveal common drift. |
| Immutable storage | `STG-2` dual cross-hashed vaults | Separate controller and media ancestries preserve originals through corruption and custody challenge. |
| Mass-and-heat emulator | `MHE-3` passive mass-CG body with outward heater and impedance loads | The comparator reproduces burden without sensing, control or useful service. |

These are architecture selections, not vendor selections. Their geometric form remains deliberately unresolved. A “three-zone” cassette identifies casualty separation and custody topology; it does not specify a case shape. A “twelve-node” terrain system identifies pod-local truth; it does not authorize an exterior layout. Geometry must follow representative weigh-in, scanning, access trials, calorimetry, contamination handling and removal equivalence.

## The technological ambitions

The clean-sheet force should not wait for today’s instrumentation market to solve tomorrow’s governing uncertainties. MESA-1C therefore creates development demand for several technologies that are not ordinary catalog purchases.

### Dual-physics structural skins

The terrain carrier needs to know normal pressure, shear, contact area, temperature, missing-cell state and changing soil geometry across roughly 285 square metres. The current planning reference contains 4,560 cells and 18,240 registered channels. The selected architecture couples a distributed force mosaic to an independent optical boundary reconstruction and subsurface/geotechnical witnesses. The aim is not merely more sensors. It is a measurement field in which different physics can expose a common inference error.

A mature program could drive printable rugged force-shear laminates, distributed self-test, replaceable branch segments, optical contact reconstruction under dust and floodwater, automated calibration platens and uncertainty propagation that preserves local invalid states rather than smoothing them away. This technology would later transfer to levees, temporary bridges, debris fields, wildfire dozer lines, unstable buildings and autonomous earthmoving.

### Physically non-actuating state observation

Software permissions are insufficient when the constitutional claim is that evidence cannot help the mission. The selected passive interface uses split physical transducers and optical state mirrors so the return-energy path can be inspected, challenged and destructively opened. A mature family would include optical and mechanical state witnesses, energy-limited conversion, visible isolation boundaries, tamper-evident calibration ports and standardized teardown coupons.

This becomes a general Resilience Force technology. It allows independent evidence on nuclear water-energy carriers, mobile hospitals, grid-forming stations and autonomous machinery without making a national measurement network part of their safety or operating loop.

### Seven-day autonomous evidence power

The evidence system must remain alive when the mission power bus is damaged, isolated or suspected. The selected architecture separates a primary-cell endurance core from a rechargeable high-rate event buffer. That is a demanding design problem at carrier-node scale: storage must survive shock, water, thermal cycling and fire exposure while rejecting its heat outward and remaining removable under quarantine.

The innovation program should pursue ultra-low-standby acquisition, safe primary chemistries, physically partitioned peak buffers, passive thermal propagation barriers, sealed state-of-charge witnesses and rapid exchange under contaminated conditions. The point is not to choose a chemistry on paper. Rival cell, pack, management and enclosure ancestries must face full-interval and destructive failure tests.

### Denied-time event truth

When networks and satellite time disappear, a carrier unload, soil failure, assay release and public handoff must still be ordered within a tight uncertainty allocation. `TKS-2` pairs two unlike atomic roots with an independent quartz witness and traveling reference. The program should treat fieldable time sovereignty as a national capability: hardened compact clocks, asynchronous event grammars, delay insertion tests, post-mission reconciliation and cryptographically sealed time-custody records.

### Immutable field originals

“Uploaded to the cloud” is not a custody architecture. Each node retains the complete original through network loss, controller corruption and selective export. Dual storage vaults use different controller and media ancestries and cross-hash short segments so partial loss is explicit. The technical agenda includes write-once field media, low-energy authenticated segmenting, physical custody seals, offline export and forensic reconstruction that never substitutes a derived national archive for the missing local original.

### Burden-equivalent test bodies

The least glamorous selected system may be the most important. A passive emulator must reproduce mass, center of gravity, mounting stiffness, electrical and fluid impedance, aerodynamic or terrain disturbance, and outward heat without sensing or contributing to the mission. If only total mass is matched, the test cannot isolate the physical effect of the evidence system. If the emulator is actively programmable, it may itself become a hidden mission aid.

The Department should build an entire discipline of certified equivalence bodies: configurable inertial matrices, passive thermal signatures, fluid and electrical impedances, failure-safe mounts and blinded swap procedures. Such bodies make honest testing possible across very large novel machines for which a few tonnes of instrumentation can change the phenomenon under observation.

## Serial configuration is a legal fact

Each prototype family receives a serial prefix. The serial record includes hardware, firmware, calibration ancestry, mass properties, heat rejection, wiring, access, seals and custody fixtures. A change after campaign start invalidates the affected campaign and every dependent result unless the protocol is prospectively amended and repeated.

The eleven planning records range from 10 kilograms for the denied-time module to 2.8 tonnes for the civil receiver and mass-and-heat emulator. The air and terrain tile cassettes remain 110 and 135 kilograms. Air and terrain carrier truth nodes remain 1.2 and 2.6 tonnes; the latter includes the 1.14-tonne pressure skin. These values are inherited design envelopes from MESA-1R, not measurements.

Serial control prevents a familiar failure: assembling the best result from several physically different prototypes. A successful endurance pack, a successful pressure skin and a successful custody vault do not together prove a configuration unless the serial record shows they coexisted with the declared mass, heat, access and interface burden.

## One-way interface law

Twelve interface classes connect the evidence system to tile structure, mission power, mission fluids, thermal boundaries, carrier lift, propulsion, terrain pods, pressure skin, receiver utilities, civil authority, evidence vault and environment. Every interface has four zeros in the governing direction:

- zero energy transfer to the mission;
- zero return data path;
- zero actuation authority; and
- zero useful mission contribution.

Evidence loss may stop trial credit. It may not create an unsafe mission state. Calibration ports remain physically disabled during credited execution. A detected reverse-energy path, return data route, control authority or useful service rejects the interface family rather than subtracting points from it.

This principle is essential for a future autonomous Resilience Force. The Department will need vast telemetry and civil-service accountability, but a communications outage must not turn water, cooling, medical power or evacuation machinery into a remote-controlled dependency. Local mission autonomy and independent national evidence should be designed as separate planes.

## Protected article bank

The prototype bank separates four categories that development programs often blur:

- protected references that preserve an untouched comparison;
- destructive articles that are expected to be cut, burned, flooded, shocked or contaminated;
- quarantine stock unavailable after an ambiguous exposure; and
- deployable stock for simultaneous campaigns.

Across eleven families the plan builds enough articles to preserve nonnegative margin at peak simultaneous need. The indicative article bank costs USD 1.764 billion and remains inside the broader USD 6.8 billion program. Cost is a planning allocation, not a quote.

Protected stocks are controlled by evidence authorities rather than builders or operators. That custody rule matters because a second-lot article is not independent if the first builder selected its components, repaired its hidden defects or chose which unit the second team received.

## P01–P08 trial constitution

The campaign sequence allows component learning to overlap while preventing the complete mission from outrunning prerequisite evidence.

| Campaign | Months | Trials | Budget | Governing purpose |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| P01 Power and storage endurance | 1–12 | 8 | USD 1.0B | Seven-day autonomy, peak capture, link loss, corruption and fire containment. |
| P02 Tile cassettes and passive interfaces | 7–24 | 10 | USD 0.7B | Burden, heat, contamination, ingress, reverse energy, common fault and replication. |
| P03 Air carrier truth node | 19–36 | 6 | USD 0.6B | Zonal integration and instrumented/emulated/bare cold-dense unload. |
| P04 Terrain carrier and pressure skin | 19–48 | 12 | USD 1.5B | Calibration, representative soils, pod and cell loss, three-way route equivalence and replication. |
| P05 Civil receiver truth station | 25–48 | 8 | USD 0.8B | Assay, power quality, occupied effect, access, isolation, release, rights and replication. |
| P06 Denied time and event order | 13–30 | 8 | USD 0.5B | Thermal, shock, cycling, root loss, delay insertion, substitution and replication. |
| P07 Removal and no-crutch equivalence | 37–54 | 8 | USD 0.6B | Tile removal, mass-only and mass-heat emulators, receiver withdrawal and blinded behavior. |
| P08 Frozen complete mission | 55–78 | 8 | USD 1.1B | Nominal, casualty, network/time, soil/power, cross-mode receiver and independent second lot. |

Every campaign faces the same eight co-governing evidence gates: configuration, energy, data, time, mechanism, civil effect, no crutch and independent replication. One failed gate yields zero campaign credit. That rule prevents a program from calling seven good answers and one missing answer “mostly successful.” It also makes safe refusal valuable: a pressure skin that declares its field invalid under damage may preserve evidence integrity even when the mission itself continues.

The sixty-eight-trial register fixes sequence, failure world, configuration, required article set, blinding, authority, state and schedule before results exist. All trials remain `OPEN` and carry zero credit.

## Industrial base: plurality below the prime

A Department operating at defense scale will attract large integrators. It should use them, but it cannot confuse prime-contractor plurality with technical independence. Three corporations buying the same conversion silicon, battery cell, storage controller, optical substrate or calibration root create one decisive failure lineage.

MESA-1C identifies twelve critical industrial chains and requires two or three rival design teams for each. Shared critical ancestry is allowed to equal zero at the decisive boundary. The register covers acquisition silicon, pressure-skin material, optical geometry, two distinct atomic-time roots, storage controllers, storage media, endurance cells and management, passive photonics, traveling standards, receiver assay and thermal containment.

Independence is established through ownership review, bills of process, firmware provenance, destructive teardown and divergent test response. Resale, relabeling and final assembly do not create a new root. When an apparently separate team shares a decisive wafer, process, cell, reagent, reference or code base, the affected chain reopens and is recompeted.

This approach suggests the mature economic structure of the Department. It would not merely purchase platforms from a few resilience primes. It would deliberately sustain plural sub-tier physics, public metrology, pilot lines, sovereign reference materials, test foundries, destructive facilities and technical data rights. The industrial policy objective is not autarky; it is the capacity to know which dependencies are real and to preserve alternatives where common failure would defeat a public guarantee.

## Fiscal architecture

The USD 6.8 billion program is allocated both by campaign and by workstream. The largest workstreams are range and trial operations at USD 1.17 billion, power/time/storage at USD 1.03 billion, pressure skins at USD 880 million, carrier truth nodes at USD 750 million, architecture competitions and receiver stations at USD 600 million each, and independent replication at USD 590 million. Tile articles, destructive evidence, data custody and reserve complete the account.

The campaign and workstream views reconcile to the same total. The allocation remains nested inside F01’s USD 24 billion and therefore does not increase the USD 1.050 trillion proving portfolio established in the parent force model. Awarded obligations, repeated campaigns or a depleted reserve would reopen the fiscal boundary. No line is a vendor estimate, appropriation forecast or probability distribution.

The ratio is instructive. The prototype article bank itself costs an indicative USD 1.764 billion; the rest buys independent ranges, destructive use, custody, replication, calibration, industrial competition and program reserve. A serious innovation institution spends heavily on the ability to discover that its preferred technology is wrong.

## Decision authority and stop rules

Twelve decision gates remain open. They ask whether the selected architectures pass independent review; serial records close; built articles meet burden; nodes survive 168 hours; local originals persist; denied time stays inside allocation; the terrain skin achieves the inherited 0.183-kPa U95 limit after test mass; interfaces remain physically non-actuating; evidence heat rejects outward; instrumented, emulated and bare missions agree; supplier ancestries are genuinely independent; and the P08 second lot earns authority to begin rendering.

Failure consequences are explicit. A miss can reopen host mass, energy, pressure margin, thermal architecture, custody, interface family or industrial competition. No sunk-cost doctrine allows a failed candidate to advance because it is already integrated.

The present authorization is narrow:

- architecture competition: selected for prototype;
- prototype construction: authorized within the nested subportfolio;
- production commitment: `HOLD`;
- internal and exterior rendering: `BLOCKED`.

The rendering prohibition is not aesthetic caution. Premature images harden arbitrary geometry, encourage requirements to fit a picture and make an unearned configuration feel inevitable. The first permissible internal visuals should come from measured article envelopes: mass-property sections, heat paths, maintenance access, contact maps, quarantine flows, custody zones and receiver interfaces. Exterior form follows those truths.

## What this pass changes

MESA-1R showed that independent evidence has mass, heat, energy, storage and terrain consequences. MESA-1C changes the next question from “what equipment should be bought?” to “what competing physical architectures and trial institutions can earn the right to define that equipment?”

Three conclusions become authoritative at the current research maturity:

1. The evidence formation requires eleven distinct architectural competitions, not a generic rugged computer procurement.
2. Supplier independence must be evaluated at the decisive technical ancestry, below corporate labels.
3. Removal equivalence requires instrumented, burden-emulated and bare configurations under a frozen protocol; paper assertions of non-interference are insufficient.

No technology coefficient, physical performance, cost estimate, vendor, production quantity or geometry is admitted. The workbook’s 1,183 passing controls prove internal reconciliation only.

## Next research boundary

The next pass should translate each selected baseline into a build-release and acceptance package without drawing the final vehicle. It should specify:

- article-level mass, center-of-gravity, volume, access and heat budgets;
- prohibited shared ancestry and minimum technical-data rights;
- reference, destructive and quarantine disposition;
- traveling calibration standards and pre/post exposure procedures;
- reverse-energy, teardown and physical non-actuation acceptance tests;
- contamination, shock, fire, water and thermal test envelopes;
- blinded instrumented/emulated/bare swap protocols;
- original-data segment, clock and custody formats;
- campaign range staffing, safe-stop doctrine and evidence-board procedure; and
- the exact measured artifacts required to unlock internal requirements diagrams.

After those packages exist, construction can begin across rival teams. Rendering still waits. The most ambitious technology program is not diminished by that discipline; it is made possible by it. A Department of Resilience capable of inventing new forms of public power must also be capable of proving when those inventions work, when they fail, and when two apparently different answers are secretly the same machine.
