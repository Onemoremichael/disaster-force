# MESA-1D prototype build-release and acceptance standard

## Executive judgment

MESA-1C selected eleven supplier-neutral architectures for prototype competition. It did not make them buildable. A named topology, a mass envelope and a trial register still leave too much freedom in the hands of an integrator: the thing built can differ from the thing reviewed; the article tested can differ from the article retained; a nominally independent team can inherit the same decisive component ancestry; and a reconciled campaign plan can quietly begin before the hardware, facilities and authorities required to execute it exist.

MESA-1D closes that institutional gap. It converts the eleven architecture baselines into a common build-release constitution:

- a physical allocation ledger that reserves 15 percent of every family envelope against integration and growth;
- fourteen controlled build-package records per family;
- twelve article-acceptance decisions per family;
- eight declared hazard worlds per family;
- six physical challenges across each of twelve one-way interfaces;
- twenty-three traceable calibration chains;
- ninety-six ancestry and technical-rights records below the prime-contractor level;
- thirty-three separately controlled article lots containing 254 articles;
- eight facility families scheduled over nine program years;
- a corrected 102-month build-to-evidence calendar;
- a nine-year USD 6.8 billion cost phase; and
- fifteen release gates that keep physical evidence, production and rendering at zero.

The decisive result is temporal as much as technical. MESA-1C's seventy-eight months described the P01–P08 trial sequence. It did not include the time required to freeze requirements, separate authorities, complete rival detailed designs, build engineering articles and release qualification lots. MESA-1D adds a twenty-four-month build-release lead. P01 begins at calendar month 25 and an accepted P08 cannot exist before month 102. This correction does not increase the USD 6.8 billion planning envelope, but it changes when facilities, article lots, independent replication and fiscal authority must exist.

The result is not a procurement specification. It is the standard that must exist before procurement can truthfully begin. It preserves technological ambition by defining required physical effects, observability, failure behavior and interfaces while leaving chemistry, material, manufacturing process, internal arrangement and exterior form open to rival teams. The Department of Resilience should be able to commission technologies that do not yet exist. It should also be able to know, before committing a fleet, whether two supposedly different inventions are the same dependency wearing different corporate labels.

## The missing layer between architecture and experiment

Large public technology programs often move from a concept selection directly to a demonstration. That shortcut is especially dangerous for a Resilience Force. The systems being tested are intended to operate amid power loss, communications loss, contamination, structural damage, uncertain civil authority and damaged access. An apparently minor difference in battery management, heat path, enclosure seam, clock source, firmware build, calibration ancestry or custody fixture can change the result.

The governing object must therefore be a serial article, not a generic type. A serial article is a legally and physically bounded configuration whose mass properties, energy, heat, interfaces, calibration, software, materials, ancestry, access, seals and custody are recorded together. A change to one of those properties after release reopens the affected acceptance decision and every dependent campaign. A program cannot assemble a successful narrative from the best energy test on one unit, the best pressure result on another and the best custody record on a third.

This standard creates four distinct authorities before construction:

1. **Requirements authority** owns the question and the non-substitutable threshold.
2. **Configuration authority** establishes what was built and whether a change created a new article.
3. **Evidence authority** owns calibration, exposure, original data, custody and test admission.
4. **Release authority** decides whether a specific lot may enter a specific campaign.

The builder and operator are deliberately absent from final evidence authority. Their technical participation is necessary; their ability to certify their own success is not.

This prevents the endurance claim from hiding an event-power deficit and prevents a nominally independent energy store from using the mission platform as its heat sink.

## Physical allocation before geometry

MESA-1D divides every mass, volume, average-power, peak-power and heat envelope across five technical functions:

1. sensing or physical burden;
2. acquisition and evidence computation;
3. independent energy;
4. storage and denied time; and
5. enclosure, isolation and calibration.

Those functions may consume no more than 85 percent of the family envelope. Ten percent remains an integration reserve and five percent remains a prototype growth reserve. The reserve is carried as a visible physical allocation rather than spread optimistically across components.

This is an innovation policy. A radical team may use a new cell chemistry, printable structural skin, optical boundary witness, passive transducer, field atomic clock, immutable medium or configurable inertial body. It may not claim that the novelty is weightless, cold, maintenance-free or already integrated. The 15-percent reserve gives new mechanisms room to exist while making their cost to the host architecture visible.

The allocation is intentionally function-based rather than component-based. A component list too early would encode the incumbent market. A function budget lets rival technologies decompose the problem differently so long as the total mass, volume, energy, heat and isolation close at the serial boundary.

Each family requires the same fourteen controlled records:

| Interface-control dataset | every physical, energy, fluid, optical and custody boundary |
| Mass-property ledger | component mass, moments, center of gravity and reserve |

| Thermal-rejection case | steady, peak, propagation and outward boundary |
| Materials and hazard declaration | composition, incompatibility and exposure disposition |
| Observable and channel map | measurand, location, sampling and invalid-state grammar |
| Calibration plan | traveling standards, uncertainty and pre/post sequence |
| Data-time-storage plan | segments, clocks, retention, loss and export |
| Physical non-actuation case | reverse energy, return data and teardown evidence |
| Access and maintainability plan | tools, reach, swap, inspection and safe isolation |
| Decontamination and quarantine plan | cleaning, exposure marking, custody and disposition |
| Ancestry and technical-rights package | ownership, facility, process, firmware and reproduction rights |
| Emulator-equivalence definition | mass, inertia, stiffness, heat and impedance pairing |
| Acceptance evidence index | artifacts, authorities, stop rules and second-lot record |

The package defines what another qualified team would need to build, inspect, reproduce and challenge the article. Proprietary treatment may protect a disclosed industrial right; it may not conceal a decisive root from the public evidence authority. If the Department cannot reproduce a safety-critical or evidence-critical function after the original provider withdraws, it does not yet possess that capability.

All 154 package records begin in DRAFT with approval OPEN and evidence credit zero. Drafting is authorized because it creates inspectable requirements. Construction waits for the requirements and authority gates to close.

## Twelve acceptance decisions per family

Every serial family faces twelve co-governing acceptance decisions:

1. mass and three-dimensional volume;
2. mass properties and reserve;
3. 168-hour independent endurance;
4. registered simultaneous peak capture;
5. outward heat rejection;
6. complete local original retention;
7. denied-time allocation;
8. physical non-actuation;
9. family-specific hazard survival or explicit invalidation;
10. calibration stability before and after hazard exposure;
11. blinded maintenance, safe removal and quarantine; and
12. independent reproduction from the frozen package.

These are not weighted attributes. A pressure skin that cannot survive contaminated removal is not partially acceptable. A seven-day logger that transfers useful energy into the mission is not independent. A receiver station that measures water quality but loses its original record when disconnected cannot authorize civil release.

Acceptance thresholds remain written as testable propositions. The mass and volume decision requires an independent weigh-and-scan record inside the frozen envelope, including fixtures. Endurance requires full interval operation plus reserve. Peak capture requires registered simultaneous injection without brownout, evidence loss or a hidden reset. The non-actuation test requires reverse-energy injection and teardown with zero conductive return, zero actuation and zero useful mission path. Reproduction requires a second team and distinct decisive ancestry to build the same decision under the frozen data package.

Every acceptance record remains OPEN, every decision remains HOLD and every credit remains zero.

## Eight hazard worlds

The common qualification grammar subjects each family to mechanical shock, random vibration, cold soak, hot soak, water ingress, dust and debris, salt and decontamination chemistry, and internal fire and propagation.

The thresholds in the workbook are research targets, not adopted standards. That distinction is material. The Department should eventually own a family of representative hazard profiles derived from actual missions: wave slam and pier impact for littoral systems; debris strike and smoke particulate for fire aviation; saturated weak soil and asymmetric contact for terrain carriers; contaminated washdown and salt fog for water-energy plants; repeated erection and damaged-building dust for mobile utilities.

MESA-1D supplies the grammar for that future science. A hazard result must say whether the article operated, failed safe, declared itself invalid, contained propagation, retained its sealed original and preserved a lawful custody state. Survived is insufficient if the measurement drifted invisibly or the article cannot be safely removed afterward.

## Proving a physically one-way evidence plane

The evidence architecture touches twelve interface classes: tile structure, mission power, mission fluid, thermal boundary, carrier lift, carrier propulsion, terrain pod, pressure skin, receiver utility boundary, civil authority, evidence vault and evidence enclosure.

Each interface receives six challenges:

1. visual and continuity survey for undeclared paths;
2. reverse-energy injection at the maximum registered potential;
3. coded return-data challenge;
4. command attempt across every evidence-side interface;
5. complete evidence-path severance; and
6. attempted calibration-port access during credited operation.

The required result is physical, not administrative: no undeclared path, no useful reverse transfer, no mission-side observation of coded evidence data, no mission-state change, safe local operation after evidence loss, and a calibration port that remains physically disabled.

This doctrine matters far beyond test equipment. A future Department of Resilience will operate autonomous aircraft, utility corridors, reactors, treatment systems, medical plants and civic-data networks at enormous scale. It needs architectures in which observation can be rich while remote dependence remains bounded. Local mission control, independent evidence and civil release should be capable of disagreeing without one plane secretly commanding another.

## Calibration and field truth

Twenty-three calibration chains bind structural load, electrical energy, fluid state, surface heat, lift support, propulsion force, ground reaction, pod pose, receiver assay, power quality, occupied thermal effect, optical contact geometry, reverse-energy detection, stored energy, event order, silent data loss and emulator equivalence.

Each chain has a design expanded-uncertainty allocation, a field-check target no greater than one half of that allocation, a drift limit, a traveling standard and a mandatory pre/post comparison. The half-allocation rule preserves room for transport, environmental exposure and method uncertainty rather than consuming the full decision margin in the laboratory.

The future proving establishment implied here is larger than a set of contractor labs. It includes public traveling standards, mobile calibration loops, geometry references, force frames, pressure-flow systems, calorimetry, assay standards, time transfer, known-pattern custody challenges and blinded artifact exchange. Metrology becomes an operational service of the Department: the ability to measure a shattered utility system, an improvised receiver or an autonomous machine in the field is part of national power.

## Industrial independence and technical rights

Twelve decisive chains each receive eight required rights or disclosures: complete bill of materials, process and facility identity, firmware and software bill of materials, ownership and beneficial-control map, calibration genealogy, destructive teardown right, second-team reproduction right, and perpetual interface and evidence-data right.

Ninety-six records therefore sit below the prime level. A nominal three-prime competition fails if all three buy the same cell, controller, wafer, reference reagent, optical substrate or firmware root. Shared decisive ancestry is grounds to reopen and recompete the chain.

This rule does not require the United States to manufacture every component domestically. It requires the Department to know the dependency, possess the rights needed to maintain public service, and create an independent root where common failure would defeat the guarantee. In a mature resilience economy this would support public test foundries, pilot lines, sovereign reference materials, component challenge programs, reproduction contracts and allied dual-source compacts.

It also changes corporate incentives. The Resilience Force's analogue to a major defense prime should earn durable value by integrating complete public-service systems and sustaining a plural supplier ecology, not by making the state permanently dependent on one opaque stack.

## Article lots, custody and destructive use

The eleven families produce thirty-three lots: engineering articles used to discover integration and access failures; qualification and destructive articles reserved for exposures, teardown and irreversible evidence; and campaign and protected-reserve articles that preserve independent comparison and second-lot replication.

The bank contains 254 articles at an indicative USD 1.764 billion. Every lot is released at least one month before need. Qualification stock cannot be borrowed to improve a campaign shortage; a quarantined unit cannot quietly return to service; and a protected reference cannot be repaired by the builder whose work it is intended to challenge.

Custody is a physical capability. It requires controlled storage, contamination segregation, battery and materials safety, sealed software and calibration states, independent transport, witnessable opening, and a disposition path for destroyed or ambiguous hardware. The Department should treat destructive evidence capacity as infrastructure, not overhead. An institution unable to cut open, burn, flood, overload and forensically reconstruct its most ambitious machines will learn only what its builders choose to show it.

## Facilities and the corrected calendar

Eight facility families carry the program: public metrology and calibration; energy and fire containment; materials and pressure-skin testing; environmental exposure; atmospheric carrier systems; terrain carrier and soil; civil receiver and occupied effect; and data, time and custody.

The nine-year capacity screen contains seventy-two facility-year records and preserves nonnegative reserve in every year. This is a planning sufficiency test, not site selection or observed availability.

| --- | ---: | --- |
| BR01 | 1–3 | frozen D01–D14 requirements and authority matrix |
| BR02 | 4–9 | independently reviewable rival detailed designs |
| BR03 | 10–18 | engineering lots |

The P01–P08 sequence then runs from month 25 through month 102. Component campaigns overlap the later build stages, but no campaign starts before its required article lot. P08 occupies months 79–102 and remains the earliest complete-mission, independent-second-lot decision.

This 102-month clock is the honest minimum under the present assumptions. It should not be compressed by relabeling development articles as qualification units, approving packages after test, substituting range telemetry for independent evidence or allowing early campaign results to select the second-lot configuration.

## Fifteen release gates

MESA-1D separates requirements freeze, authority separation, physical burden, energy, heat, data, time, non-actuation, hazards, calibration, access, ancestry, article bank, facility capacity, and complete-mission/form decisions.

Package drafting is authorized. Rival serial construction is held until gates G01 and G02 close. Campaign credit remains zero until the relevant evidence gates close. Production remains held through an accepted independent-second-lot P08. Rendering remains blocked through G15.

The final gate asks more than whether the system completed a demonstration. It asks whether a second lot, built from the frozen package by an independent technical root, passed the frozen complete mission without a hidden evidence crutch. Only then can the form be treated as earned rather than imagined.

## What MESA-1D changes

MESA-1C established which architectures deserved prototype competition. MESA-1D establishes the conditions under which those competitions may create evidence.

Five conclusions become authoritative at the present research maturity:

1. The seventy-eight-month campaign requires a preceding twenty-four-month build-release phase; full calendar closure moves to month 102.
2. Every family must preserve a visible 10-percent integration reserve and 5-percent growth reserve before geometry is allowed to harden.
3. Buildability requires fourteen controlled records, while acceptance requires twelve co-governing physical decisions; neither can be replaced by a concept selection.
4. Industrial independence is inseparable from technical data, destructive access and second-team reproduction below the prime-contractor level.
5. The Department's innovation system requires public metrology, destructive evidence, custody and representative ranges as permanent force infrastructure.

No physical performance, reliability distribution, observed cost, vendor, production quantity or geometry is admitted. The workbook's 4,109 passing controls prove internal completeness and reconciliation only.

## Next research boundary

Pass 95 should convert this common constitution into family-specific procurement data packages and a source-selection experiment. It should define:

- solicitation-level performance and interface data for each family;
- bidder questions that reveal hidden mass, heat, access and ancestry assumptions;
- three genuinely different technical roots where feasible;
- model-based design-review and hardware-in-the-loop entry conditions;
- government-furnished standards, emulators and custody fixtures;
- award partitions that prevent one integrator from controlling every decisive layer;
- earned-value measures tied to accepted physical artifacts rather than document completion;
- payment and stop rules for destructive failures;
- second-team reproduction lots and technical-data escrow;
- facility reservations and long-lead materials;
- a prospective method for updating hazard targets without moving them after results; and
- the exact internal requirements diagrams that measured articles may unlock.

That pass should remain form-neutral. The first new visuals should be engineering evidence: measured envelopes, mass-property sections, heat-flow boundaries, maintenance and quarantine volumes, contact maps and interface schematics. Exterior renderings remain deferred until an accepted P08 makes one integrated form defensible.
