# MESA-1E family procurement and source-selection experiment

## Executive judgment

The Department of Resilience cannot create a new technological economy by buying familiar equipment in unusual quantities. It must learn how to commission physical capabilities whose decisive mechanisms, production systems and evidence standards do not yet exist. That requires an acquisition constitution as ambitious as the machines. MESA-1E therefore asks a narrower but foundational question: how should the Department procure the eleven MESA evidence-system families so that innovation, industrial independence and falsifiable physical learning survive the first award?

The answer is not a conventional winner-take-most competition. MESA-1E buys thirty-three rival learning paths across eleven families. Two proposals per family reserve provisional serial build paths; a third receives a protected challenge contract to attack one declared blocking requirement. All proposals face twelve non-negotiable paper gates before six weighted merit dimensions are considered. Merit can rank useful learning among admissible paths. It cannot compensate for missing endurance, hidden reverse energy, incomplete custody, shared decisive ancestry, unavailable facilities or absent reproduction rights.

The modeled portfolio contains:

- eleven family-specific solicitation packages and 132 non-negotiable requirements;
- thirty-three fictional technical-root proposals;
- 396 hard-gate records and 198 post-gate merit judgments;
- twenty-two provisional build paths and eleven challenge-only paths;
- eleven independently disclosed root pairs below the integrator level;
- 264 technical-rights records;
- sixty-six root-specific article lots containing 254 articles;
- fifteen government-furnished standards and eight public facility families;
- twenty-two decisive long-lead reservations;
- 165 artifact-based milestones;
- sixteen progression gates that hold evidence, production and rendering authority at zero; and
- a nine-year, USD 6.8 billion portfolio allocation.

The experiment's most important conclusion is institutional. The unit of acquisition should be a **falsifiable learning path**, not a platform promise. A build contract is an option on physical evidence. It is not an entitlement to program continuity, consolidation or production. A challenge contract is protected technical dissent. It may pursue photonic measurement skins, printed sensing structures, mechanically legible recorders, new compact time roots, activated energy stores or century-scale immutable media. It receives no waiver. It either closes its declared blocker under the common standard or terminates without serial authority.

This design makes technological ambition governable. It also implies a new public industrial role. The Department must own the definition of success through standards, ranges, destructive access, custody, interface rights and independent reproduction. If a winning firm owns the only fixture, software root, calibration chain, test facility and legal right capable of judging its machine, the government has purchased dependence rather than power.

MESA-1E is a planning experiment, not a source selection. Its firms and proposals are fictional archetypes; its scores and prices are declared allocations; every evidence record remains open; every award and payment decision remains held. Rendering remains blocked. The pass defines how rival invention could be commissioned without pretending that invention has already succeeded.

## Procurement is part of force design

A military force does not emerge from isolated vehicle purchases. It depends on doctrine, test ranges, arsenals, technical orders, depots, standards, training, reserve components, industrial mobilization and a state capable of integrating them. A Resilience Force requires an equivalent acquisition ecology. Its distinctive difficulty is that a failed system may not merely lose a mission. It may contaminate drinking water, energize damaged conductors, destabilize soil, misclassify a civil release, create an invisible data gap or make a population dependent on a service that cannot be repaired.

The Department's acquisition system must therefore preserve five things simultaneously:

1. **Effect discipline.** Every program begins with a public-service effect, damage world, time boundary and release authority rather than an attractive platform category.
2. **Mechanism diversity.** Rival teams must be able to pursue substantially different physical roots rather than cosmetically different integrations of the same decisive supply chain.
3. **Evidence independence.** The builder cannot own the only observation plane, test interpretation, article custody or reproduction pathway.
4. **Serial accountability.** Configuration, ancestry, rights, calibration and physical disposition attach to articles and lots, not to a generic model name.
5. **Option preservation.** The state retains competing paths long enough to observe representative failure instead of consolidating at the paper-design stage.

These principles change what “efficiency” means. Early commonality can reduce near-term engineering cost while creating an unobservable common failure. A single prime can simplify coordination while erasing the government's ability to compare mechanisms. Proprietary test equipment can accelerate a demonstration while making the result irreproducible. MESA-1E treats those apparent savings as transfers of decision power away from the public institution.

Competition must survive the first award. That is the acquisition analogue of survivability.

## The acquisition object: a family learning package

Each of the eleven MESA families receives a solicitation package that freezes the public question while leaving internal solution space open. The package states the effect boundary, mass, volume, average and peak power, seven-day independent endurance, outward heat rejection, storage, article inventory, readiness date, acceptance authority and contract ceiling. It does not prescribe chemistry, detailed geometry, manufacturing method or corporate team.

The eleven packages cover atmospheric and terrain tile cassettes; atmospheric and terrain carrier nodes; the separate civil receiver; terrain pressure skin; passive evidence interface; independent energy; denied time; immutable storage; and a mass-and-heat emulator. These are not auxiliary purchases. They are the physical nervous system by which the Department can tell whether its most radical service machines work without asking the machine to grade itself.

Every package carries twelve hard questions:

| Boundary | Required answer |
| --- | --- |
| Public effect | observation and invalid-state grammar capable of falsifying the mission |
| Burden | installed mass, cube, moments and access with 10 percent integration and 5 percent growth reserve |
| Endurance | 168 hours plus 25 percent nameplate reserve without mission power or recharge |
| Heat | all evidence-system heat rejected across its own measured boundary |
| Original record | complete local originals and explicit invalid intervals through network and one-media loss |
| Time | decisive events ordered after external time and network loss |
| Non-actuation | zero useful reverse energy, return data, control authority or mission contribution |
| Hazard behavior | no silent state across mechanical, thermal, water, dust, chemical and fire exposure |
| Maintenance | blinded inspection, safe removal, decontamination and quarantine |
| Ancestry | disclosure of decisive components, process, firmware, facility and calibration roots |
| Rights | teardown, second-team reproduction, interface and evidence-data rights |
| Execution | qualified facilities, long leads, lots, milestones and constant-dollar ceiling |

A missing answer is a failed gate. It is not a weakness that can be offset by low price or high innovation score. This prevents the familiar pattern in which weighted source selection converts a necessary public condition into a preference.

## Thirty-three technical roots

MESA-1E uses three fictional architectures per family to test whether the requirement set admits genuinely different mechanisms. They are design probes, not claims about present commercial availability.

| Family | Build root A | Build root B | Challenge root C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Air tile cassette | zonal optoelectronic vault | distributed fiber-photonic skin | MEMS mesh with mechanical witness |
| Terrain tile cassette | pod-local fiber and force cassette | printed piezoresistive laminate | fluidic pressure-shear network |
| Air carrier node | triple isolated evidence vault | distributed analog event islands | photonic electromechanical witness ring |
| Terrain carrier node | pod-local dual vault | structural-wave acquisition lattice | fluidic mechanical recorder array |
| Civil receiver | separate assay, utility and release modules | microfluidic assay plus grid-forming witness | electrochemical and paper-optical fabric |

The challenge roots deliberately reach beyond ordinary acquisition comfort. Some may prove physically unattractive. That is acceptable if the experiment is bounded and its failure becomes public technical capital. The purpose of the challenge lane is not to guarantee radical technology a place in the fleet. It is to prevent the acquisition process from excluding it before a decisive experiment can occur.

Each challenge proposal declares one failed hard gate. Its contract pays for three artifacts: a registered blocking hypothesis, a bench coupon with failure evidence, and one controlled attempt to close the gate. Until closure, the proposal is excluded from serial build authority. This is more ambitious than preserving a small research office beside a dominant procurement system. It embeds high-risk invention inside every major family competition while preserving a single public evidence law.

## Hard gates before merit

The paper screen contains 396 records: twelve gates for each of thirty-three proposals. The twenty-two provisional build proposals pass all twelve in the modeled case. Each of the eleven challenge proposals fails exactly one. Those failures remain visible in the portfolio; they are the reason the challenge contracts exist.

Only after the hard screen does the model calculate six merit dimensions:

- mission learning, 25 percent;
- technical divergence, 20 percent;
- testability, 20 percent;
- field maintainability, 15 percent;
- manufacturing scale, 10 percent; and
- export portability, 10 percent.

This ordering is essential. “Innovation” is often invoked as permission to weaken safety, data or endurance boundaries. MESA-1E uses the opposite logic. A radical mechanism earns a protected experiment because it may enlarge the feasible set. It earns fleet consideration only when it satisfies the same non-substitutable public-service boundary.

Likewise, a high score does not create a winner. The fictional scores help test whether the ledger can rank learning value; they are not market findings. Real competition would require independent evaluators, registered conflicts, blinded technical review where possible, price evidence, facility verification and traceable reasons for every judgment.

## Two build roots and a protected challenge path

Every family allocates 45 percent of its family-contract ceiling to build root A, 45 percent to build root B and 10 percent to challenge root C. Across the eleven families, these contracts total USD 3.42 billion.

Maintaining two build paths is expensive by ordinary unit-cost logic. It is economical under uncertainty. The program is not yet buying repeat units; it is buying the right to observe unlike failures. If the state collapses to one design before representative stress, it loses the counterfactual needed to distinguish a bad component from a bad architecture, an integration failure from a mechanism limit, or a firm-specific defect from a shared industrial dependency.

The build paths are therefore retained through engineering, qualification/destructive and campaign/protected lots. The sixty-six lots contain 254 articles and reproduce the inherited USD 1.764 billion article-bank boundary. A root-specific lot cannot be repaired with components from its rival, and a qualification unit cannot be quietly relabeled as a protected reference.

The six fictional integrator archetypes face a ceiling of four provisional build paths. In the baseline, four integrators hold four paths and two hold three. Tightening the ceiling to three creates four explicit portfolio failures. This sensitivity is valuable: it proves that concentration is not merely prose. The allocation would have to rebalance before award.

An integrator cap alone is insufficient. Two primes may depend on the same wafer, optical substrate, controller, cell, reagent, facility, firmware or calibration process. The root-pair register therefore requires zero shared decisive ancestry at the paper stage and reserves authority to reopen the pair when ownership, provider control or serial design changes.

## The state must own the evidence commons

MESA-1E allocates USD 440 million to fifteen government-furnished standards. These include mass-property reference frames, traveling calorimeters, electrical-energy standards, pressure-flow loops, force platens, optical geometry fields, denied-time transfer kits, storage challenges, reverse-energy injectors, vibration profiles, environmental loops, fire-containment rigs, receiver assay references and sealed custody fixtures.

This is not generic laboratory overhead. It is a sovereign evidence commons. A public standard lets different mechanisms produce comparable results and lets a new entrant challenge an incumbent without renting the incumbent's definition of success. The Department should maintain the standards, publish interface and uncertainty rules, circulate traveling references, blind selected defects and preserve original measurements.

Eight public facility families receive USD 1.17 billion across nine years: metrology and calibration; energy and fire containment; materials and pressure skins; environmental exposure; atmospheric carriers; terrain carriers and soil; civil receivers and occupied effects; and data, time and custody. Seventy-two facility-year records preserve positive modeled reserve.

The facilities are part of industrial strategy. They lower the fixed cost of entry for new builders, keep destructive testing from being traded away in a contract negotiation, and let the Department reproduce a result after a provider exits. In a mature force, some would become national laboratories, some mobile range units, some shared pilot lines and some allied facilities operating under common custody and metrology compacts.

## Rights below the prime

Every proposal carries eight required rights or disclosures: complete bill of materials; bill of process and facility identity; firmware source and software bill of materials; ownership and beneficial-control map; calibration genealogy; destructive teardown right; second-team reproduction right; and perpetual interface and evidence-data right.

The 264-record ledger applies these conditions even to challenge contracts. Failed inventions can still yield valuable public knowledge. Without teardown and data rights, the Department may pay for a black-box failure whose cause remains proprietary. Without interface rights, the first integrator may turn an open family competition into a closed ecosystem. Without reproduction rights, “industrial base” may mean one corporate balance sheet.

This does not require indiscriminate publication of every trade secret. It requires the public institution to possess enough lawful access to maintain safety, compare alternatives, commission a second source and preserve service after corporate change. The exact treatment can include controlled technical-data repositories, escrow, limited-purpose licenses and protected government facilities. The governing test is practical: can an independent qualified team reproduce the decision without the original provider's discretionary assistance?

## Artifact-based contracts

The portfolio contains 165 payment milestones. Each provisional build path uses six artifacts: frozen package and conflict disclosure; critical design and burden closure; engineering-lot release; qualification-lot release; family acceptance artifact set; and independent reproduction artifact. Each challenge path uses the three blocking artifacts described above.

Payment shares sum to one for every proposal, but all payment authority remains held. The milestone date does not earn payment by itself. The responsible acceptance authority must receive the declared physical artifact, its original evidence, configuration record and custody state. A slide review deck may explain an article. It is not the article.

This approach also changes contractor incentives. Firms earn continuation by producing inspectable, destructible and reproducible capability—not by keeping a program narratively alive. Termination after a useful negative result should not be treated as acquisition failure. If a challenge experiment decisively rules out a mechanism and preserves its evidence, the state has acquired knowledge that protects larger programs.

## Long leads without premature lock-in

The twenty-two provisional build paths expose one decisive long lead each, including isolated acquisition wafers, optical substrates, printed sensing films, vault enclosures, microfluidic cartridges, grid-forming witness converters, magneto-optic isolators, primary-cell stacks, compact time references, immutable media and inertial-emulator modules.

The modeled order windows begin only after a month-four freeze and still arrive before the registered need. That is a schedule control, not supplier evidence. In execution, the Department would use capacity reservations, option contracts, public pilot-line slots, qualified substitute designs and government-furnished material buffers. It should avoid solving schedule risk by ordering a single root before requirements are frozen. Such a purchase may make the subsequent competition ceremonial.

At larger scale, long-lead governance becomes an industrial sensing function. The Department should maintain sub-tier maps, process-capacity models, ownership-change alerts, material inventories, workforce constraints and alternative qualification paths across its service systems. Its mobilization command should be able to add a pilot line or fund a new process before a brittle dependency becomes a crisis bottleneck.

## Financial architecture

MESA-1E holds the USD 6.8 billion planning ceiling established by the preceding MESA passes while reallocating it around competition and evidence and industrial option value:

| Workstream | USD billions |
| --- | ---: |
| Family implementation and challenge contracts | 3.42 |
| Government-furnished standards | 0.44 |
| Qualified facilities and ranges | 1.17 |
| Destructive evidence | 0.33 |
| Independent reproduction | 0.59 |
| Data custody | 0.20 |
| Acquisition and evidence workforce | 0.35 |
| Portfolio reserve | 0.30 |
| **Total** | **6.80** |

The structure places USD 3.38 billion outside the family contracts. That is a feature, not administrative drag. Standards, facilities, destructive evidence, reproduction, custody, workforce and reserve are what allow the government to remain an intelligent buyer after award.

The nine-year phase is front-loaded for family contracts and standards, sustained for facilities and workforce, and increasingly weighted toward independent reproduction and reserve in later years. This is consistent with a program moving from competing designs to testing, destruction, replication and decision. It is not an appropriation forecast. No price base, inflation series, vendor quote or observed facility capacity has been admitted.

The model also rejects the assumption that duplicated development is pure waste. At this maturity, the rival root is a risk-reduction asset. Its value includes failure discrimination, bargaining power, supplier mobility, export variants, surge capacity and the possibility that the favored mechanism encounters a physical boundary. Future passes should value those options explicitly, but should not manufacture a precise net-present value before empirical transition probabilities exist.

## Sixteen gates from solicitation to form

The source strategy is governed by sixteen stop rules:

1. solicitation requirements and authorities frozen;
2. requirements, builder, operator, evidence and release roles separated;
3. two disclosed roots retained for every family;
4. all provisional build paths pass twelve hard gates;
5. physical integration and growth reserves preserved;
6. cost and lot releases remain inside the USD 6.8 billion and month-102 boundaries;
7. technical rights complete;
8. independent facility capacity reserved;
9. long leads ordered after freeze and before need;
10. public standards funded and available;
11. no integrator exceeds four build paths;
12. engineering, destructive and protected lots remain separate;
13. payment depends on accepted artifacts;
14. representative family acceptance exists;
15. an independent second root passes the complete P08 mission; and
16. measured sections and accepted P08 evidence support form authority.

Every gate presently holds. The model authorizes drafting and experiment design, not construction, payment, evidence admission, production or rendering. This boundary matters because a polished source-selection model can create false momentum. Internal arithmetic can show that the planned portfolio is coherent. It cannot prove that a battery survives, a passive interface is physically one-way, a clock remains independent, a pressure skin measures dynamic soil or a supplier can deliver.

## Scaling beyond MESA

MESA-1E is a small prototype of a Department-wide institution. At maturity, the Department of Resilience should possess an **Acquisition and Industrial Mobilization Command** with functions analogous in seriousness to military materiel commands:

- mission engineering offices that translate public effects into non-substitutable requirements;
- family program offices responsible for rival technical roots rather than single platforms;
- a public standards and metrology service;
- destructive-test and forensic commands;
- government article arsenals and configuration depots;
- technical-data, interface and reproduction authorities;
- industrial-base intelligence and sub-tier mobilization cells;
- pilot plants and shared production infrastructure;
- challenge-contract offices embedded within major family programs;
- export and allied-interoperability offices built around common public-service interfaces; and
- an independent acquisition evidence board able to stop programs regardless of sponsor enthusiasm.

This would create the institutional demand needed for resilience-sector equivalents of major defense primes while reducing the risk that those firms become irreplaceable monopolies. Large integrators would assemble water, power, medical, access, communications and civil-release systems. Specialist firms would build advanced reactors, treatment trains, autonomous earthmoving systems, high-rate thermal equipment, field metrology, structural skins, robotic utility corridors and deployable manufacturing. Public standards and data rights would let new entrants compete at subsystem boundaries.

The same structure could support export power. The Department would not export one universal machine into every legal and climatic context. It would export certified service families, interface standards, training, spares, evidence methods, financing and joint production packages. Allied countries could contribute independent roots, host proving facilities and specialize in different hazard environments. The result would be an international resilience industrial base organized around the delivery and verification of public service.

## What this pass overturns

MESA-1D made eleven families buildable in principle. It still treated “rival construction” as the next step without defining how rivals would be admitted, funded, kept independent, paid or prevented from capturing the evidence system. MESA-1E narrows that authority.

No family proceeds directly from a frozen package to one selected builder. Each receives two unlike build paths and one challenge path. All twelve hard gates precede merit. Rights attach to every root. Public standards and facilities are budgeted. Article lots remain root-specific. Integrator concentration is constrained. Long leads cannot create pre-freeze lock-in. Payment waits for artifacts. Independent reproduction remains a condition of production.

The previous 102-month evidence calendar and USD 6.8 billion ceiling remain in force. MESA-1E changes the institutional content inside them, not their authority. The next research object is no longer a better bid-scoring sheet. It is the Department-wide acquisition and industrial-mobilization command capable of operating this pattern across reactors, aircraft, ships, terrain systems, medical plants, utility corridors, autonomous construction and civil-service platforms at national scale.

## Model integrity and research boundary

The companion [companion workbook](../../../models/force/projection/mesa1e-family-procurement-and-source-selection-experiment.xlsx) contains nineteen sheets and 4,983 deterministic controls. All controls pass in the restored baseline; the formula-error scan returns zero matches; the Open XML container validates; and every sheet is rendered and visually inspected. Tightening the maximum integrator allocation from four build paths to three produces four portfolio failures and four corresponding audit failures before the baseline is restored.

Those are model results only. The eleven deliberate challenge-gate failures remain visible by design. The workbook does not identify actual vendors, establish market availability, validate costs, qualify facilities, award contracts or admit physical performance. Evidence credit is zero. Production is held. Form and rendering remain blocked until measured sections, heat paths, access volumes, contact geometry, destructive evidence and independent complete-mission reproduction exist.
