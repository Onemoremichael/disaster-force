# CSAC-1: compound service article and range campaign

## Building the sovereign proving service behind a Department of Resilience

## Executive judgment

The Resilience Force cannot be designed by drawing a fleet and then asking laboratories to validate it. It must begin with a public obligation, turn that obligation into physical burdens and failure worlds, and build an institution capable of discovering that the preferred machine is wrong.

Pass 89 produced the first physically differentiated reference for the Expeditionary Service Kernel: energy, water, thermal, distribution and civil service. It also exposed the terrain formation's governing weakness. Modeled public service arrives at hour 53.569 against an absolute hour-54 deadline; reset completes at hour 167 against an hour-168 ceiling; modeled damaged-soil pressure is 34.249 kilopascals against a 35-kilopascal screen. Those are not comfortable margins. They are invitations to falsification.

This pass specifies the institution, articles, facilities, campaigns and decision rules needed to perform that falsification. It selects **CSAC-1**, a clean-sheet Compound Service Article Campaign with:

- twenty-five technology campaigns and eight ordered evidence stages, producing 200 gates;
- 480 development tiles, divided into a protected 240-tile qualification bank and a separate 240-tile full-mission and attrition bank;
- four paired air-and-terrain mission-range classes covering humid-salt, arid-dust, cold-freeze and weak-soil/flood/urban-debris environments;
- 200 registered full missions: 176 repetitions of admitted claims, eight edge-of-claim probes and sixteen blinded defect trials;
- eleven facility families linking materials, family articles, carrier interfaces, public receivers, reset and second-lot production;
- a permanent Service Systems Proving Command with 24,000 direct personnel, a 60,000-position affiliated ecosystem and a minimum continuous watch of 5,800;
- an earliest production decision in program year fourteen, after independent second-lot replication; and
- a USD 1.050 trillion technology portfolio allocated from, not added to, the program boundary established in Pass 89.

The campaign corrects an error in the previous physical reference. The 240 tiles allocated to family qualification cannot also be consumed in repeated, destructive, contaminated or blinded full-mission trials. Qualification evidence and mission evidence require separate serial-controlled populations. The development fleet therefore doubles from 240 to 480 tiles, and the total tile inventory rises from 5,916 to **6,156**. The operational fleet remains 4,730 loaded and 946 reserve tiles. Existing provisional development-carrier counts—fourteen air and twelve terrain—can support two concurrent formations in each mode, provided ranges alternate rather than attempt eight simultaneous vehicle missions.

CSAC-1 selects a proving architecture. It does **not** select ESK-40V for production, verify either physical lineage, predict an appropriation profile or authorize an exterior rendering. All 200 evidence gates remain open. The production state is **HOLD**.

## 1. Why proving is force structure

A coequal instrument of national power needs more than research grants, vendor demonstrations and regulatory compliance. It needs a sovereign capacity to ask whether a complete public-service effect survives distance, damage, casualty, contamination, software faults, organizational failure and reset.

Military establishments treat proving grounds, test squadrons, target ranges, operational evaluation, instrumentation, configuration custody and destructive articles as part of force generation. A Department of Resilience must do the same, but the protected object is different:

> a defined population receiving a defined bundle of safe service by a stated time, for a stated duration, under stated damage.

That statement changes what counts as evidence. A water machine can meet its flow rating while the mission fails because residuals cannot be contained. A carrier can complete a flight while public service fails because its receiver cannot unload the last energy tile. A thermal plant can achieve nameplate output while occupied buildings remain unsafe because distribution, access or civil release is absent. A fleet can appear redundant while both modes depend on one firmware root or sub-tier factory.

The proving system must own the causal chain from source to lawful public effect. It must distinguish modeled closure, representative physical evidence and public authority. No state substitutes for another. A workbook can expose a contradiction but cannot certify a membrane. A successful machine may produce water but cannot authorize public consumption. A regulator may define a permissible effect but cannot make an infeasible carrier fly.

## 2. The correction from 240 to 480 development tiles

Pass 89 allocated twenty-four qualification tiles to each of ten family-lineages.

| Lineage | Families | Articles per family | Qualification bank |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Atmospheric | EA, WA, TA, DA, CA | 24 | 120 |
| Terrain | ET, WT, TT, DT, CT | 24 | 120 |
| **Total** | **ten family-lineages** |  | **240** |

Each family bank contains six four-article populations: engineering, environmental, destructive, set integration, independent replication and preserved reference. The division is intentionally rigid. A tile damaged in a casualty trial cannot later become the pristine comparison article. A vendor cannot replace a failed configuration quietly. A successful early article cannot be rebuilt repeatedly until it represents no reproducible production state.

Full-mission proof creates a different demand. Two instrumented air formations require 120 tiles because each formation carries sixty. Two terrain formations require 100 because each carries fifty. A further twenty articles are required for hidden faults, quarantine, contamination and damage that makes immediate reuse inappropriate.

~~~text
qualification bank                         240
air mission bank       2 formations × 60  120
terrain mission bank   2 formations × 50  100
attrition and quarantine                    20
                                         -----
development fleet                          480
~~~

The full inventory becomes 4,730 operational loaded tiles, 946 rotating-reserve tiles and 480 development tiles: **6,156 articles**.

This is a general doctrine for the Department: **evidence assets are not readiness assets**. A destructive fleet must exist at meaningful scale or the institution will protect scarce prototypes, substitute simulation for damage and gradually lower the severity of its own questions.

## 3. Service Systems Proving Command

CSAC-1 creates a permanent **Service Systems Proving Command** within the National Resilience Proving Network. The Command is neither the acquisition executive nor an operating service. It is the public institution that maintains the ability to test coupled service systems at formation scale across decades.

| Authority | May do | May not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Builder | design, fabricate, disclose, repair and propose changes | accept its own evidence or alter a threshold during trial |
| Operator | execute blind protocols, maintain custody and declare an unsafe stop | reveal injected faults or trade services without authority |
| Evidence authority | control randomization, instruments, raw data, uncertainty and reproduction | redefine the public obligation to rescue a design |
| Civil authority | define acceptable public effect and release service | certify hidden machine performance it did not observe |
| Ancestry authority | trace factories, feedstocks, firmware, laboratories and update roots | treat different logos as independent without causal evidence |
| Rights authority | govern consent, accessibility, privacy, care, remediation and reporting | waive protections to preserve a schedule |

The four core parties—builder, operator, evidence authority and civil authority—must be institutionally separate. Ancestry and rights authorities cut across them. The Secretary of Resilience owns the final production decision but may not manufacture missing evidence by memorandum.

The Command maintains compound-service ranges and specialized laboratories; owns the evidence constitution and measurement chain; sustains destructive and second-lot article banks; trains operators and independent replicators; and continues block-level proof after production so software, suppliers and repair changes do not outrun evidence.

## 4. The evidence constitution

Every campaign proceeds through eight ordered stages.

| Gate | Question | Required physical basis | Decisive artifact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | Is the burden, hazard and authority claim complete? | registered requirement | burden-hazard-authority contract |
| G2 | Do materials and subcomponents survive? | coupons and subassemblies | raw data, calibration, uncertainty and failure dossier |
| G3 | Does a representative family article meet mass and output? | family tile | configuration, output, hazard and maintainability record |
| G4 | Do five families operate simultaneously? | five-family set | cross-coupling and public-service vector |
| G5 | Can the carrier accept, retain and release the set? | instrumented carrier | structure, center of gravity, restraint, transfer, fire and evacuation |
| G6 | Can an unprepared receiver create safe public service? | receiver complex | assembly, assay, handoff and public-release record |
| G7 | Does the claim survive casualties and denial? | survivor formation | blinded casualty, denial and safe-stop record |
| G8 | Can it reset, repeat and reproduce independently? | second lot and reset chain | repeated mission, lot trace, depot and replication record |

Five rules prevent evidence inflation.

- **No paper maturity.** Reports, simulations and reviews can prepare but cannot close a physical gate.
- **No borrowed evidence.** Evidence belongs to an exact configuration, condition and causal lineage.
- **No averaged deadline.** A fast nominal mission cannot compensate for a late casualty mission.
- **No unobserved substitution.** Hardware, software, reagents, calibration, crews and repairs are configuration events.
- **Truthful safe stop earns credit.** Outside an admitted claim, safe containment and an explicit narrower promise are better than dangerous improvisation.

## 5. The physical article system

The ten family-lineage campaigns are the program's center.

| Family | Atmospheric question | Terrain question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Energy | Can high-specific-output conversion, protection and wet energy custody fit a 22.375-tonne article? | Can an independently sourced multi-liquid lineage meet the civil boundary under shock, dust and field repair? |
| Water | Can compact separation and oxidation manage both product and residuals? | Can ceramic filtration and separate residual processing survive debris and contamination? |
| Thermal | Can high-lift equipment and dry rejection meet peak heat without receiver water? | Can segmented hybrid rejection work on weak ground and in confined layouts? |
| Distribution | Can reeled conductors, fluid lines and robots establish a safe utility corridor rapidly? | Can sectional conductors, reinforced hoses and tracked adapters tolerate debris and repeated repair? |
| Civil | Can local control, clinical/access, survey, assay and repair fit the air burden? | Can an independent wired-control and heavy-access lineage meet the same public obligation? |

Each article needs a complete measurement envelope:

- certified mass, three-axis center of gravity and mass-change history;
- structural load path, restraint forces, vibration, shock, fatigue and damage growth;
- electrical input, output, harmonics, fault energy, isolation, black start and protection;
- fluid flow, pressure, temperature, contamination, leaks, residuals and custody;
- thermal input, useful output, rejection plume, ambient derating, noise and hot surfaces;
- software build, control root, time source, degraded mode, local state and update ancestry;
- crew touch time, maintenance access, tools, consumables, lockout, recovery and accessibility;
- assay, public-release state, participant exposure and environmental discharge; and
- reset work, cleaning, quarantine, recalibration and configuration return.

The builder may instrument internal mechanisms, but an independent measurement layer owns public claims. Mobile reference instruments accompany articles between laboratories and ranges so transfer error becomes visible. Calibration changes are configuration changes.

Interfaces are physical systems with mass, people, time, tools and hazards. The Department should fund autonomous load transfer, robotic utility-laying, self-surveying anchors, rapid crossing protection, field metrology and offline configuration systems as aggressively as it funds prime movers.

The four mission ranges are environmental classes, not site selections:

1. **Humid coastal and salt:** aerosol, heat, humidity, surge, corrosion, wet faults and debris.
2. **Arid heat and dust:** extreme heat, dust, smoke, water scarcity, grid loss and long utility runs.
3. **Cold and freeze:** icing, snow, freeze-thaw, cold starts, frozen feeds and broken roads.
4. **Weak-soil, flood and urban debris:** saturation, low bearing, standing water, rubble and live utility conflicts.

Each class contains physically separate air and terrain receivers. Shared observation infrastructure cannot become a mission dependency. The range provides damaged geography; the formation must bring the service system.

## 8. Capacity and the 200-mission matrix

At 180 operating days per range-year, ten days per mission and a four-year full-mission window, four ranges provide 288 mission slots. Two concurrent air formations provide 144 air slots. Two concurrent terrain formations provide 144 terrain slots. Each mode needs 100.

The margin absorbs restoration, weather exclusions, participant protection, instrument failure and required reruns. A rerun never erases the first result.

Each mode executes:

- twenty-two admitted-claim cells repeated four times: nominal; carrier loss; carrier plus E, W, T, D or C loss; receiver denial; origin-loader denial; navigation denial; network denial; assay queue; climate extreme; wet containment; black start; simultaneous peak; certification-authority loss; sub-tier recall; common control-root defect; crew casualty; saddle quarantine; and reset followed by a second mission;
- four boundary probes: second carrier loss, two same-family tile losses, both access modes lost and no lawful receiver; and
- eight blinded defects: mass-tag mismatch, counterfeit component, common firmware signature, contaminated wet lot, wrong center of gravity, assay disagreement, receiver-adapter fault and falsified reset record.

Repetition is coverage, not a reliability estimate. Four successes can expose deterministic coupling and operator variance; they cannot establish a fleet failure probability.

## 9. Guardbands and the terrain problem

Passing at the absolute deadline is not an evidence strategy.

| Measure | Absolute boundary | Evidence threshold | Modeled value | Margin |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Air stressed service | 54 h | 53.75 h | 52.917 h | 0.833 h |
| Terrain stressed service | 54 h | 53.75 h | 53.569 h | **0.181 h** |
| Terrain soil pressure | 35 kPa | 34.75 kPa | 34.249 kPa | 0.501 kPa |
| Terrain reset | 168 h | 167.5 h | 167 h | 0.5 h |

The terrain service margin is approximately 10.8 minutes, smaller than plausible uncertainty in route classification, bearing preparation, saddle handling, utility connection, assay queues or crew variation. The correct interpretation is not “terrain passes.” It is:

> the terrain lineage is the first architecture CSAC-1 should try to break.

If representative missions cannot remain below 53.75 hours, the Department must reopen the formation: reduce saddle count through family productivity, add carriers, preposition receiver kits, change payload division, create a lighter first-service echelon or relax the public obligation explicitly. It may not quietly reinterpret hour 54.

## 11. Innovation and industrial consequence

The campaign is not limited to commercial equipment. Its purpose is to create options the mature force requires: integrated saddles for unlike hazards; high-specific-output firm power; water systems that own unknown feeds and residuals; low-mass high-lift thermal rejection; robotic power and fluid corridors; dynamic variable-footprint ground contact; autonomous route opening; offline control with diverse roots; public metrology; rapid receiver civil works; and production methods that preserve serial ancestry through repair and reset.

These are mission competitions, not technology prizes. Every candidate must state which decisive coefficient it changes—mass, cube, energy, heat, time, labor, casualty tolerance, reset, ancestry or safety—and survive the whole source-to-public chain.

The resulting industrial base spans structures, power electronics, energy storage and fuels, water membranes and ceramics, compressors, heat exchangers, medium-voltage equipment, robotics, tracked mobility, instrumentation, clinical systems, construction machinery, depots and public facilities.

Plurality must mean causal separation. Two primes using the same membrane factory, control kernel, rare-material refiner, calibration laboratory or signing authority are one failure lineage. Export variants may change voltage, water regulation, language, accessibility law and transport envelope, but may not inherit maturity without testing the altered chain.

## 12. Rights, environment and legitimacy

Civil proving grounds cannot treat communities as battlespace or people as passive loads. Before any participant mission, the rights authority controls consent, notice, accessibility, privacy, clinical escalation, worker protection, grievance, compensation and aftercare. Civil authorities may reject service even when program leadership wants a successful event.

Ranges own environmental state before and after each mission. Feed water, soils, air, noise, waste, residuals, spills and remediation are measured. Restoration time is part of mission occupation. A program that demonstrates rapid service by externalizing contamination has failed.

Tribal nations, states, territories and local governments require governance roles in range design, land and water protection, participant rights and public-service interpretation. This pass selects environmental functions only; geographic siting remains open.

## 13. Decision rules and research consequence

Series production remains on hold until:

1. article banks are serially separated;
2. facilities are accepted;
3. G1–G3 close for all ten lineages;
4. five-family simultaneous operation closes;
5. both carrier modes close;
6. both receiver modes create lawful service;
7. degraded missions close inside guardbands;
8. boundary probes demonstrate truthful safe stop; and
9. second lots and new crews reproduce the result independently.

Failure reopens every dependent claim. A receiver failure need not invalidate family physics, but it prevents public-service credit. A common-ancestry discovery can invalidate both modes retroactively.

The companion workbook closes the deterministic bookkeeping: 25 campaigns, 200 gates, 480 development tiles, 6,156 total tiles, 288 modeled range slots, 24,000 direct personnel, USD 1.050 trillion and 368 audit controls. It proves none of the physical claims. All gates remain open and all mission cells retain zero evidence credit.

The next pass should build the measurement and article-burn architecture beneath CSAC-1: sensor channels, bandwidth, time synchronization, independent measurement paths, destructive consumption, range observation that cannot become a mission crutch, uncertainty budgets for the terrain clock and soil screen, bottom-up facility quantities and supplier-ancestry graph requirements.

Exterior rendering remains blocked. The first permissible visuals are internal burden, hazard, maintenance, restraint, transfer and human-access diagrams derived from representative evidence. The purpose is not to make an implausible fleet look real. It is to create an institution capable of forcing an unprecedented fleet to become real—or killing the wrong design before the nation builds it.
