# `TAFG-1` second-region formation competition

## The least universal reinforcement is not a second fleet; it is a second complete physics

**Research status:** first-principles formation competition; behavior and program hypotheses only; no architecture, vehicle or procurement quantity admitted

**Decision date:** 7 September 2026

**Companion model:** [`tafg1-second-region-formation-competition.xlsx`](../../../models/force/projection/tafg1-second-region-formation-competition.xlsx)

## Executive judgment

The Department of Resilience should not answer a second simultaneous disaster by reflexively duplicating every element of the first-region force. It should first define what the public guarantee requires, distinguish guarantees that work anywhere from those that depend on a prepared receiver, and then buy the least formation that preserves the promised effect after credible loss.

Pass 80 established the expensive control. Two complete `TAFG-1` groups under dual-federated custody can serve two regions after losing one vehicle and an entire access mode or operating site. That `S0` full-duplication package fields 1,670 tonnes, ten vehicles and 546 additional deployed people per ready group. Across forty-three ready groups it adds a declared $963.4964 billion and places the whole-force boundary at $8.34663795 trillion. It remains an assured upper comparator, not a selected architecture.

This pass competes six leaner rivals against it. The governing distinction is between **universal unprepared assurance** and **conditional assurance**. Universal assurance assumes that the second region has no qualified standoff utility source, no usable local feedstock, and no prepared service cell. The reinforcement must carry a complete source-to-service chain and must retain one after losing an entire physics family or site. Conditional assurance allows one of those external resources to count, but only when its identity, capacity, route, authority and survival are independently established.

Under the declared service-cell grammar, the universal case has a registered fielded-payload lower bound of 1,270 tonnes. One complete physical cell weighs 635 tonnes at dispatch. If loss of one whole mode may remove every carrier in that family, one physical cell cannot survive the loss. Two independently governed complete cells, using dissimilar access physics, are therefore necessary. The `S1` dual-carriage package, `DCP-2`, attains that bound with one atmospheric cell carried by two `HBL-320` liftships and one terrain cell carried by four `ATUT-160` vehicles. It needs six vehicles, 486 incremental deployed people and an estimated 1,065 incremental affiliated positions. Its declared forty-three-package program adds $770.6333 billion and produces an $8.15377485 trillion national boundary—$192.8631 billion, or 20.0 percent, below full duplication.

This is a lower bound within the registered assumptions, not a theorem over every future technology. A new mechanism could change the mass of a complete cell, change what “carriage” means, or make a receiver-side effect possible without moving the present article. The point is to state what such a mechanism must displace and prove. It cannot receive credit merely for being novel.

Four conditional formations remain strategically important. VHL plus a robotic utility bridge, VHL plus local conversion, terrain carriage plus a utility bridge, and atmospheric carriage plus a prepared receiver cell can all preserve the second-region effect when their enabling condition survives. The least-cost declared conditional rival is `S4`, terrain carriage plus utility bridge, at $572.3871 billion. It is not a cheaper universal force. It is a force whose public claim is narrower. Conditional capacity is not deficient capacity; it is capacity whose public claim is narrower.

The seventh rival, two ten-aircraft VHL lattices, exposes a categorical error. Twenty aircraft can survive one aircraft loss and still fail the loss of their common atmospheric mode. Vehicle multiplicity is not physics diversity.

The practical force-design consequence is a two-tier second-region enterprise. `DCP-2` is the current research reference for universal reinforcement. Conditional detachments should be generated alongside it and assigned through a receiver-intelligence system that can prove standoff sources, local conversion inputs or prepared cells before dispatch. The Department should develop new technologies aggressively, but its innovation institution must be organized around eliminating a named service-chain term or common mode—not around producing impressive vehicles without an admissible effect.

All forty-four evidence gates remain open. Current and strict credit are zero. The deterministic second-region paths reach service between absolute hours 48 and 54 against an hour-54 deadline, but no timing distribution has been measured. Rendering remains blocked. The next pass must configure the `DCP-2` position by position, vehicle by vehicle and base by base before its physical form can be drawn responsibly.

## 1. The decision is a promise, not a platform

The force has reached a point where “more access” is too imprecise to guide investment. A national rescue institution could buy another aircraft family, another terrain vehicle or another bridge system and still fail the public promise because the added capacity shares a payload, base, authority, receiver or physics with the first region.

The decision in this pass is narrower and harder:

> What is the least declared second-region package that enters service within its own forty-eight-hour clock, survives the loss of one vehicle, and preserves both regions after the complete loss of one access mode or operating site?

The first region is held by the Pass 80 `F1-C1` reference: one tri-vector group with atmospheric carriage, autonomous terrain carriage and robotic utility bridging; separate payload arsenals; independent loaders, energy lineages, controls and technical releases; and one receiver echelon. Its declared whole-force boundary is $7.38314155 trillion for forty-three ready groups.

At absolute hour 6, a second region activates. It receives its own forty-eight-hour service clock, so its deadline is absolute hour 54. No rival receives credit for prelaunch, forward movement or a preexisting receiver unless the package explicitly owns and assures that condition. The second-region formation is therefore incremental to the one-region base.

The service effect remains the controlled `PTS-5` physical cell: 580 dry tonnes plus 55 tonnes of wet launch load, or 635 tonnes at dispatch. It contains firm power, black start, water treatment and quality, useful cooling, heat rejection, water and thermal distribution, wastewater and residual custody, communications, survey, medical and vertical service, crew safety, repair and building connection. A corridor system may project equivalent services from a qualified source; a local-conversion system may reproduce selected service capacity from qualified local matter and energy; a prepared cell may already hold part of the effect. None is equivalent merely by mass.

This grammar prevents the platform from becoming the unit of analysis. The unit is a complete, governed, accepted service chain at a specific receiver by a specific time.

## 2. Two assurance classes

### 2.1 Universal unprepared assurance

A universal package must serve a second region whose useful infrastructure state is unknown at dispatch. It may use terrain, airspace, littoral access and a receiver zone, but it may not assume a qualified external utility source, suitable local feedstock or a prepared service cell. Those absences are not pessimistic forecasts. They define the guarantee.

The package passes only if:

```text
complete carried service cell
× independent custody and release
× viable access mode
× receiver entry and connection
× service acceptance by absolute hour 54
= one second-region effect
```

It must retain that result after a permitted vehicle loss and after a separate world removes one entire mode or site. Losing the atmospheric mode removes every VHL aircraft and liftship in that domain. Losing the terrain mode removes the terrain train. Counting several vehicles inside one domain does not soften the mode-loss case.

### 2.2 Conditional assurance

A conditional package can substitute a verified receiver resource for part of carried capacity. Three bases are registered:

- `STANDOFF`: an independently qualified source survives outside the damaged area, a deployable corridor can reach it, and legal and technical authority exists to energize or pressurize the connection;
- `LOCAL`: adequate matter, energy, assay and conversion authority survive at or near the receiver; or
- `PREPARED`: an independently powered, maintained and accepted fixed service cell is already present and can combine with the arriving formation.

The condition is not a narrative assumption. It must have an identity, operating state, reserved capacity, route, authority, configuration record and loss result. If any one of these is unknown, the conditional path cannot support a universal dispatch promise.

Conditional formations are not failed universal formations. They can be the economically dominant choice in a prepared national lattice. But their readiness reports, public commitments and dispatch orders must state the condition. A Department that calls every conditional package “all-hazards” would recreate the same paper readiness that this project is designed to eliminate.

## 3. The registered lower bound

Let `m = 635 t` be the fielded mass of one complete source-independent physical service cell. Let an access mode be a set of carriers sharing a decisive physical operating domain. Let the assurance rule remove any one mode and still require one complete effect at the second region.

In the unprepared world, a corridor, conversion unit or dormant fixed cell cannot supply a complete effect because its external source condition is absent. At least one carried cell is required. If only one carried cell exists and its access mode is removed, no source-independent path remains. Therefore at least two carried cells must be assigned to independently losable modes. The fielded-payload lower bound is:

```text
2 complete cells × 635 t per cell = 1,270 t
```

The bound has four qualifications.

First, the cells must be physically complete after loading. Two partial manifests that require one another are one logical cell, even if they travel by different vehicles.

Second, their custody, release and receiver chains must be separately governed. Two payloads held behind one release authority can fail together.

Third, the modes must be dissimilar under the registered loss grammar. Two atmospheric lattices are one mode for this purpose.

Fourth, this is a bound on the present effect definition and mass architecture. It does not forbid a lighter future cell, a different service doctrine or an experimentally proved non-carriage mechanism. It establishes the evidence burden for claiming that advance.

`DCP-2` attains the registered bound. That makes it the least declared universal rival in this competition. It does not make it an admitted force.

## 4. Seven rival formations

### 4.1 `S0`: full `TAFG-1` duplication

`S0` sends another complete atmospheric cell, terrain cell and 400-tonne utility-bridge payload with two receiver echelons and dual-federated custody. It fields 1,670 tonnes through two liftships, four terrain vehicles and four corridor projectors. Because either physical carriage mode can complete the service without a standoff source, it retains a second-region effect after atmospheric or terrain denial. The bridge adds utility-gap specialization and a third dispatch option where a source survives.

Its strength is breadth. Its weakness is that the second region inherits a complete third path whether or not that path is necessary for the universal guarantee. `S0` is the exact Pass 80 upper control: 546 incremental deployed people, 1,217 incremental affiliated positions, $963.4964 billion of package program and an $8.34663795 trillion national boundary.

### 4.2 `S1`: `DCP-2` dual-carriage detachment

`S1` removes the second corridor fleet and keeps the two complete physical cells. Atmospheric cell `A` moves through two `HBL-320` liftships; terrain cell `B` moves through four `ATUT-160` vehicles. Each has its own payload custody, loading, energy, control, technical release and receiver work package. The formation fields 1,270 tonnes through six vehicles.

Its assurance comes from dissimilar complete carriage, not internal vehicle redundancy. Loss of one liftship makes the atmospheric cell incomplete, but the terrain cell remains. Loss of one terrain vehicle makes the terrain cell incomplete, but the atmospheric cell remains. Loss of the entire atmospheric or terrain mode likewise leaves the other complete chain.

The detachment needs an estimated 486 deployed people: operating crews, two separately governed payload and release cells, receiver entry, connection, medical and safety coverage, command, communications, maintenance, recovery and reset. It implies about 1,065 affiliated positions after relief, schools, depots and institutional depth. At forty-three ready packages, the industrial account contains 86 liftships, 172 terrain vehicles, 86 primary custody nodes and 43 receiver echelons.

`DCP-2` does not reproduce the utility bridge. It therefore lacks the specialized ability to span a long damaged utility gap from a surviving source without transporting the full service plant. The universal guarantee does not require that specialization because either carried cell can generate the effect. The larger force still needs corridor formations; `DCP-2` simply shows that every second-region reinforcement need not own one.

### 4.3 `S2`: VHL plus utility bridge

`S2` combines a ten-aircraft heavy-VTOL lattice carrying a 720-tonne functional-`N+1` payload with four robotic utility-corridor projectors carrying 400 tonnes. It fields 1,120 tonnes through fourteen vehicles. The VHL lattice retains 648 tonnes after one evenly allocated aircraft is lost, above the 571.5-tonne ninety-percent mass screen. Functional closure remains a hypothesis requiring load-by-load proof.

In the unprepared world, the bridge has no source to project and the VHL lattice is the only complete effect. Atmospheric denial therefore removes universal service. When a qualified standoff source and route survive, the bridge becomes an independent second path and the package passes the conditional mode-loss screen. Its declared package program is $922.6397 billion.

### 4.4 `S3`: VHL plus local conversion

`S3` joins the ten-aircraft VHL lattice to three local-conversion seed units. It fields 870 tonnes through thirteen vehicles and adds $922.2314 billion across the program. The conversion units are intended to turn locally available matter and energy into water, thermal, structural, process and repair capacity rather than transport every finished subsystem.

This is a high-leverage innovation direction, but not universal capacity. Local matter may be contaminated, chemically wrong, inaccessible, privately controlled or without sufficient energy. Quality assurance may take longer than conversion. The package becomes conditionally assured only where feedstock, energy, assay, process yields and public-service certification survive independently of the atmospheric path.

### 4.5 `S4`: terrain carriage plus utility bridge

`S4` combines one complete 635-tonne terrain cell with the 400-tonne corridor payload. Four terrain vehicles and four projectors field 1,035 tonnes. It is the least-cost declared conditional rival: 370 incremental deployed people, a $572.3871 billion package program and a $7.95552865 trillion national boundary.

Its economics are strategically important. Relative to full duplication it avoids $391.1093 billion, or 40.6 percent of the incremental program. But terrain denial removes its only physical cell, and corridor loss leaves no internal alternative after a terrain-vehicle loss. It passes only where a separately assured standoff source and route make the corridor a complete path.

### 4.6 `S5`: atmospheric carriage plus prepared receiver cell

`S5` carries one complete atmospheric cell with two liftships and pairs it with one independently maintained receiver cell. It fields 1,215 tonnes across the mobile and fixed account, requires 380 incremental deployed people, and adds $767.1056 billion.

The prepared cell can activate by absolute hour 18 in the deterministic schedule, providing deep margin. That result depends on the cell being outside the incident's decisive common modes: separate power, maintained consumables, current software, qualified operators, protected water and thermal paths, valid acceptance records and an authority able to release it. Without those properties, prepositioning is inventory, not readiness.

### 4.7 `S6`: two VHL lattices

`S6` assigns twenty heavy-VTOL aircraft in two ten-aircraft lattices. It fields 1,440 tonnes, employs 520 incremental deployed people and adds $1.399947 trillion—the most expensive rival in the competition.

It can retain two nominal atmospheric payloads after individual aircraft loss. It cannot retain the second-region effect after complete atmospheric-mode or site loss. The result is deliberately stark: more vehicles, more payload and more money do not create an independent physics family. A force optimized around a spectacular carrier can remain less assured than a smaller mixed-physics force.

## 5. The timing problem begins when the second region activates

Region B activates at absolute hour 6. Its forty-eight-hour deadline is therefore absolute hour 54. The deterministic chains are:

| Path | Relative entry | Absolute entry | Margin to hour 54 | Governing dependency |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `M1` buoyant atmospheric lift | 42 h | 48 h | 6 h | independent air release and receiver zone |
| `M3` autonomous terrain carriage | 45 h | 51 h | 3 h | independent land release and receiver zone |
| revised VHL lattice | 47 h | 53 h | 1 h | functional `N+1` allocation |
| `M5` utility bridge | 46 h | 52 h | 2 h | qualified source and corridor rights |
| `M6` local conversion | 48 h | 54 h | 0 h | qualified matter, energy and output release |
| `P0` prepared receiver cell | 12 h | 18 h | 36 h | cell remains operational and accepted |

These are arithmetic targets, not readiness findings. No path currently includes an empirical distribution for weather, queue contention, software mismatch, crew delay, contamination, route obstruction, casualty, rejected receiver, rework or failed service acceptance. The zero-margin local-conversion path is especially informative: even a successful process concept cannot support the hour-48 claim without substantial parallelization, prior characterization or a faster certification system.

The Department's technological ambition should therefore include **time architecture**. Autonomous record reconciliation, continuously rehearsed loads, machine-verifiable custody, prequalified receiver interfaces, digital but offline-capable authority, parallel safety work and modular test equipment may remove critical-path hours. The goal is not to pressure crews into bypassing controls. It is to redesign the system so safe release is intrinsically faster.

## 6. A force portfolio, not one universal formation

`DCP-2` should be treated as the universal research reference because it preserves the declared promise without receiver credit. It is the force that can be dispatched when national command knows only that a second severe region exists and that an entry zone can be established.

The conditional packages belong in the same force, but their assignment requires a receiver-state compiler. Before committing `S2` or `S4`, command must prove a source, route, capacity reservation, quality boundary and energization authority. Before committing `S3`, it must prove matter, energy, assay and conversion release. Before committing `S5`, it must prove the independent prepared cell.

This creates an operational distinction between **formation readiness** and **mission pairing**. A conditional formation can be fully ready for its designed condition while being unsuitable for a particular receiver. Readiness reporting should therefore publish both:

1. the package's internally assured paths; and
2. the set of externally verified receiver conditions that complete its mission chain.

A mature national portfolio could hold universal detachments at high-alert access bases, cheaper terrain-plus-bridge detachments near dense prepared utility networks, conversion formations near characterized material basins, and atmospheric-plus-prepared-cell teams within a nationwide fixed-service lattice. The precise mix cannot be selected from this pass because the joint distribution of incident geography, receiver state, mode denial and concurrent demand is not yet measured.

## 7. Industrial and workforce consequences

At the forty-three-ready-group planning point, `DCP-2` requires 86 clean-sheet liftships and 172 clean-sheet terrain vehicles. It also requires 86 separately governed custody nodes and 43 receiver echelons. Those are not repurposed military leftovers. They imply continuous production, overhaul, flight and terrain test, payload integration, public-service certification, depots, schools, simulation, base utilities and reserve-component depth.

The industrial logic differs from conventional defense in its acceptance boundary. A resilience carrier does not complete its mission when cargo is dropped. It completes when water, cooling, power, medical, communications and building services are safely accepted by civil authorities and people can use them. Manufacturers must therefore own integration evidence across the carrier, service module, receiver and reset cycle.

The Department should cultivate prime integrators at the scale of major aerospace, shipbuilding and energy firms, while preventing one prime from becoming a common failure across both cells. Program rules should require plural interface implementations, independently reconstructable configuration records, separate test articles and deliberate cross-prime compatibility. Public arsenals and test ranges should retain enough engineering authority to reject a vendor-defined system boundary.

Workforce is similarly physical. `DCP-2`'s 486 deployed positions cannot be reduced to pilots and vehicle operators. They include payload custodians, load engineers, energy and control technicians, public-health release, water and thermal specialists, receiver reconnaissance, building-interface crews, medical and safety coverage, communications, recovery, maintenance, command and reset. The estimated 1,065 affiliated positions acknowledge relief, training, depot, standards, experimentation and institutional depth. These remain staffing hypotheses until task times, queue loads, fatigue and casualty substitutions are measured.

## 8. A technology program designed around broken terms

Military innovation is powerful when doctrine, test, procurement and production all pull toward a demanding mission. A Department of Resilience should create the same machinery, but organize it around civil service-chain discontinuities.

### 8.1 Reduce the mass of a complete cell

The 1,270-tonne lower bound is driven by the 635-tonne cell. High-temperature heat pumps, compact thermal rejection, electrochemical treatment, high-power solid-state conversion, structural energy storage, membrane advances, autonomous distribution and self-deploying building interfaces could reduce that mass. The decisive test is not a component's specific power in isolation. It is whether a smaller article still closes every service function, carries its wet and consumable burden, survives loss, connects to real buildings and resets for a second event.

### 8.2 Make heavy buoyant lift an operational utility

The liftship hypothesis requires variable buoyancy, gust control, quiet high-authority propulsion, autonomous station keeping, degraded-navigation flight, unprepared mooring, safe heavy transfer, lightning and icing tolerance, maintainable envelopes and operations near populated damaged terrain. A national program should build instrumented subscale articles, full-scale propulsion and mooring rigs, destructive weather trials, autonomous cargo-transfer demonstrations and representative end-to-end service missions. It should not advance from attractive renderings to fleet quantities.

### 8.3 Create terrain mobility beyond roads

The terrain cell requires distributed ground pressure, articulation, obstacle negotiation, amphibious or saturated-soil operation, autonomous convoy behavior, recovery under self-weight, local bridge assessment, low-damage passage through communities and unloading without cranes. Novel wheel-track hybrids, walking supports, temporary load-spreading surfaces, robotic route preparation and cooperative vehicle recovery may matter more than top speed.

### 8.4 Turn utility corridors into robotic tissue

Conditional packages become more valuable if a corridor can discover, qualify and connect to sources rapidly. The research object is not a hose drone. It is a multi-service tissue carrying water, power, thermal exchange, communications and control through sectional isolation, fault location, protected crossings, autonomous laying and retrieval, pressure and voltage management, cyber-safe local operation and public-service release. Source assay and rights resolution must be designed into the deployment clock.

### 8.5 Make local conversion certifiable

Local conversion could collapse strategic mass if it can characterize uncertain inputs, reject unsafe material, adapt processes, manufacture repair parts and produce certified water, thermal media or structural elements. The enabling advances are rapid spectroscopy, autonomous sampling, portable process trains, feed-flexible chemistry, quality-by-construction controls, signed material genealogy and regulators capable of approving bounded emergency products. Conversion that produces output faster than it can be trusted has not closed the service chain.

### 8.6 Build a machine-verifiable receiver state

Conditional dispatch needs a living national receiver graph: sources, capacities, water quality, substations, pressure zones, heat sinks, rights, routes, building interfaces, fixed cells, spares, trained local teams and failure ancestry. It must operate under communications denial and distinguish stale declarations from measured current state. This is not a commercial digital twin. It is a readiness instrument with inspection, custody, adversarial testing and legal authority.

### 8.7 Test compound loss at full scale

The Department needs proving grounds where a complete payload begins in arsenal custody, loses a loader or vehicle, encounters a denied mode, enters a damaged receiver, connects under contamination and communications constraints, delivers accepted service, then recovers and resets. Trials should randomize faults and withhold their identity from operating crews. Success is measured at the household, clinic, shelter, water-quality and building-service boundary—not at vehicle arrival.

The economic opportunity is larger than procurement. A twenty-five-year program would create design, production, maintenance and export ecosystems for civil heavy lift, off-road infrastructure mobility, robotic utilities, modular treatment, firm mobile power, receiver instrumentation and public-service assurance. Exportable capacity should be governed as interoperable civil protection rather than stripped-down military surplus. Its value would lie in standards, training, spares, data custody, receiver preparation and joint exercises as much as vehicles.

## 10. Institutional design

The Department needs a formation generator that owns both universal and conditional packages. The existing Fourth Access Systems Command can remain the research-level employment home, but the second-region problem requires three functions to be institutionally explicit.

The **Universal Reinforcement Regiment** would generate `DCP-2`-class detachments, maintain two independent physical cells and certify compound-loss readiness. The name describes an obligation rather than freezing the current vehicles.

The **Conditional Access Regiment** would generate bridge-, conversion- and prepared-cell packages. Its readiness standard would include an approved set of receiver conditions, not an assertion of universal reach.

The **Receiver Intelligence and Pairing Center** would maintain the authoritative receiver graph, issue condition-specific mission pairings, and deny dispatch credit when sources, routes, feedstocks, fixed cells or authorities cannot be proved. It would sit beside operations, not beneath logistics, because it changes which formation can lawfully fulfill the public promise.

A separate Access Test Directorate should own representative trials and evidence admission. Program offices may build systems; employment commands may demand readiness; neither should certify its own success. The technical authority must be able to withhold current credit even when a formation is politically urgent or industrially mature.

## 11. Evidence, rendering and admission

The workbook registers forty-four evidence gates across vehicle physics, payload completeness, custody separation, timing, receiver conditions, workforce, reset, cost and institutional authority. All are open. The calculations therefore describe behavior under assumptions, not observed readiness.

Rendering remains blocked for substantive reasons. `DCP-2` still lacks:

- a thirty-two-position allocation across each physical cell;
- vehicle-by-vehicle mass, cube, center-of-mass and restraint closure;
- liftship transfer, mooring and receiver-zone geometry;
- terrain axle or contact loading, articulation, recovery and unloading geometry;
- separate base, arsenal, loader and energy layouts;
- crew duty cycles, queue discipline and casualty substitution;
- a two-receiver representative schedule; and
- recovery, decontamination, reload and second-event configuration.

Drawing before these choices would give accidental dimensions and visual coherence the status of requirements. The next pass may establish stable envelopes; only then should the project commission detailed renderings.

## 12. Pass stocktake

This pass makes five advances authoritative at research maturity `R2`.

First, it separates universal unprepared assurance from conditional assurance. Receiver-dependent capacity keeps its strategic value while losing an overbroad claim.

Second, it registers a 1,270-tonne lower bound for the current universal second-region effect: two separately governed 635-tonne physical cells in dissimilar modes.

Third, it establishes `DCP-2` as the least declared universal reference in the seven-package competition. It supersedes full duplication as the only practical research reference while retaining full duplication as the exact upper control.

Fourth, it shows that vehicle multiplicity cannot substitute for physics diversity. Two VHL lattices fail the compound mode-loss requirement despite carrying more mass and costing more than the universal reference.

Fifth, it turns conditional formation assignment into an institutional receiver-intelligence problem. A national resilience force should intentionally generate universal and conditional capacity, but it must never report them as the same promise.

Nothing is admitted for acquisition. Payload masses, vehicle counts, schedules, workforce, industrial quantities, costs and organizations remain hypotheses. Current and strict evidence credit are zero.

Pass 82 should configure `DCP-2` physically. It should divide all thirty-two payload positions across the `M1` and `M3` cells; allocate each position to each liftship and terrain vehicle; close mass, cube, balance, restraint, loading and unloading; separate bases and arsenals; compile crew duty and technical release; run two-receiver schedules under vehicle and mode loss; and carry both cells through recovery and reset. The result should either produce stable physical envelopes for first renderings or reopen the lower-bound formation.
