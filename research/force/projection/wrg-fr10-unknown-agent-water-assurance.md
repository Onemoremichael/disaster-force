# Nondetection is not assurance

## A safe water-release architecture for unknown-agent conditions

> **Successor correction — Pass 68.** The [assurance-source family competition](wrg-fr10-assurance-source-family-competition.md) replaces this paper's generic half-demand provenance with four complete source logics: a nuclear coastal carrier, protected-groundwater group, variable-feed inland group and sealed-reference transfer lattice. No family is universal. The four-family portfolio serves ten of twelve declared theaters and retains two eligible sources in six; residual-denied and thirty-day no-consumable worlds remain open. All strict admissions remain zero and rendering remains blocked.

Pass 66 converted a receiver-wide failure into a controlled federation problem. Three independently releasable half-demand service islands passed eight of ten declared receiver worlds. A four-island mesh with an alternate half-demand source passed nine. Every architecture still stopped when a national unknown-contaminant order removed civil issue authority. That result was intentionally safe and operationally incomplete.

Pass 67 asks the question on the other side of that stop:

> What evidence, source capacity, custody, release authority and endurance would permit at least fifty percent safe water service during an unknown-agent hold without treating a negative field screen as proof that water is safe?

The distinction is fundamental. A finite analytical panel can show that specified targets were not observed above stated limits in specified samples. It cannot establish the absence of every harmful chemical, biological agent, toxin, transformation product or mixture. An architecture that converts “not detected” into “safe” silently expands its knowledge beyond its evidence. Speed then becomes a mechanism for unsafe issue.

The alternative is **positive assurance**. A source may remain eligible during a scoped hold when the complete chain that produced and delivered it is independently defensible: source environment, intake, process, critical power, consumables, residual path, quality release and custody are not defeated by the event that invalidated the primary water. A sealed reserve may receive similar credit while its production record, turnover, inventory, isolation and seals remain valid. Neither claim is universal. Each has a registered independence envelope and must return to hold when contrary evidence crosses it.

The companion [unknown-agent water-assurance model](../../../models/force/projection/wrg-fr10-unknown-agent-water-assurance.xlsx) tests four assurance overlays on the Pass 66 four-island receiver across twelve deterministic worlds. A centralized confirmatory doctrine safely passes two. A local doctrine that permits a negative field screen to qualify the primary source passes three and issues affected water in seven outside-panel worlds. A qualified half-demand source plus a sealed twenty-four-hour reserve passes ten. A second continuous half-demand provenance passes eleven and survives a seventy-two-hour compound source hold. The twelfth world remains a universal safe stop.

Every result is a declared target. All strict admissions remain zero, sixteen technical and mission gates remain open, and the rendering gate remains blocked. No contaminant envelope, source chain, reserve, workcell or release protocol has representative evidence.

## Executive judgment

Eight findings govern the next water-force design cycle.

First, **unknown-agent assurance is an open-world problem**. The force cannot enumerate every hazardous condition and then describe a finite test panel as comprehensive. The unknown may be a novel industrial chemical, a treatment byproduct, a biological agent, a toxin, a mixture produced by damaged infrastructure, a sampling failure or an ordinary substance at an extraordinary concentration. The safe system must continue to operate when the specific identity is unresolved.

Second, **negative evidence is bounded by method, sample, time and target list**. `A2` releases the primary source after an outside-panel field-negative result. In the model, it issues affected water in seven of twelve worlds. Removing that exception reduces unsafe worlds from seven to zero without improving continuity. The result exposes a genuine trade: the unsafe doctrine creates apparent throughput by counting water that a safe-service metric must exclude.

Third, **positive provenance is a physical service path, not a database label**. `A3` qualifies the existing half-demand alternate source only when its source, treatment, power, consumables, residuals, laboratory and custody can survive the primary hold. The architecture combines that continuous path with a sealed half-demand reserve and maintains safe service in ten worlds. A provenance record is useful only because it points to an independently configured chain.

Fourth, **sealed inventory is a bridge rather than continuous production**. `A3` adds about 5,882 cubic metres of gross storage to yield 5,000 cubic metres of usable reserve at the declared eighty-five-percent share. It survives loss of the qualified continuous source for twenty-four hours. It fails when the primary and first assurance source remain held for seventy-two hours because the reserve exhausts. Extending reserve endurance to seventy-two hours turns that failure into a pass, demonstrating that duration—not the word “reserve”—controls the claim.

Fifth, **a second continuous provenance removes the registered endurance common mode**. `A4` adds another half-demand source chain. When the primary source and Assurance A are held for seventy-two hours, Assurance B supplies exactly the fifty-percent floor. Removing Assurance B reverses the result. Source count receives credit only if the second intake, process, power, consumable, residual and release chain can fail separately.

Sixth, **central expertise and local authority perform different functions**. A central confirmatory laboratory can support deeper characterization and cross-region comparison. It cannot be the only path by which an intact local source remains lawful when communications or national data services disappear. `A3` and `A4` use multiple local release domains operating inside a preauthorized class. Those officers may apply the class; they may not enlarge it.

Seventh, **a universal no-issue state remains necessary**. Every architecture issues zero water in `W12`. A system that has no way to stop because all uncertainty is automatically converted into an exception is not resilient. The force should reduce the time spent in universal hold by building independent evidence and substitute sources, not abolish the stop decision.

Eighth, **assurance has material and industrial mass**. The four declared systems range from about 5,579 to 6,871 dry tonnes, 5,914 to 7,379 chargeable tonnes and 148 to 185 forty-tonne surface positions. Twenty-five-year program comparators rise from $156.0 billion to $204.0 billion. These are not estimates. They show that laboratories, custody, clean reserve and independent source chains are acquired force capacity rather than incidental quality-control overhead.

## 1. The epistemic problem

Water release under a known condition is already difficult. The force must know the source, process envelope, sampling design, analytical method, detection limit, result, pressure state, storage state and distribution condition. An unknown-agent condition adds a different problem: it is unclear which measurement would be dispositive.

Four propositions follow.

### 1.1 A sample is not the source

A laboratory result describes the material represented by a sample under a chain of custody. It does not automatically describe every location, depth, time, flow path or storage sector. A heterogeneous contaminant plume, intermittent discharge, sediment-associated agent, biofilm or cross-connection may escape an otherwise valid method. Sampling design and transport become part of the safety case.

### 1.2 A panel is not the hazard universe

Targeted methods answer bounded questions. Even broad indicators—conductivity, total organic carbon, spectral fingerprints, toxicity signals or process deviations—do not identify every possible consequence. They can detect a departure, support isolation and prioritize confirmatory work. They cannot convert an unbounded hypothesis space into a universal clearance.

### 1.3 Treatment performance is conditional

A treatment train has a validated feed and operating envelope. A process effective against one class may transform, concentrate or pass another. An unknown source departure therefore cannot be assigned a removal credit merely because the plant is sophisticated. The [water-force engineering architecture](../../systems/water/force-engineering.md) already requires release to stop when source quality moves outside the active train envelope. Pass 67 extends that discipline to the alternate path.

### 1.4 Provenance is also conditional

Independent provenance does not prove metaphysical purity. It establishes that a named failure mechanism affecting one chain does not, within a registered boundary, affect another. A coastal source may be independent of an inland industrial spill and correlated with a regional atmospheric deposition event. Two groundwater wells may be geographically separated and share an aquifer. Sealed inventory may avoid a current source event and share the same compromised production batch. The common-mode register governs the claim.

The consequence is an asymmetry. Negative evidence narrows a set of possibilities. Positive assurance creates a service option by excluding the affected pathway through independently controlled physical and institutional boundaries. The force needs both, but only the latter can carry continuity credit during a scoped unknown-agent hold.

## 2. Release states

The architecture defines five public operating states.

| state | trigger | water that may be issued | controlling rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| `D0 NORMAL` | no unresolved anomaly | water inside its validated source and process envelope | ordinary local or central release |
| `D1 SOURCE HOLD` | known agent or bounded process departure | unaffected source or successfully treated water after source-specific release | quarantine the affected provenance |
| `D2 ASSURANCE ONLY` | unknown agent or unresolved provenance conflict | prequalified independent continuous sources and intact sealed reserve | field nondetection cannot qualify the held source |
| `D3 ALL STOP` | the hazard cannot be bounded across any qualified water | none | national public-health stop authority |
| `D4 RECOVERY` | threat bounded and affected systems isolated | staged source, storage and distribution release | no silent return to normal |

The states separate two decisions commonly collapsed into one. **Source eligibility** asks whether a water path remains inside a defensible assurance class. **Issue authority** asks whether a named authority may release that class through a particular island at a particular time. A technically clean source cannot issue through an expired or unavailable authority. A valid authority cannot declare an unqualified source safe.

`D2` is the novel operating state. It avoids both extremes. It does not treat every anomaly as contamination of the nation’s entire water inventory, and it does not permit a finite screen to erase uncertainty. It quarantines the disputed provenance and shifts service onto capacity whose independence was established before the event.

`D3` remains available when the event crosses the assurance boundaries themselves. Examples include evidence that source classes share the agent, a compromised production batch spanning reserves, an unbounded atmospheric or deliberate hazard, loss of custody across all qualified paths, or conflicting data that no competent authority can resolve. In that state, commands preserve evidence, communicate restrictions, protect non-water services and prepare substitutes. They do not self-release.

Recovery is its own state because clearing a source, tank or network requires more than the disappearance of an alarm. It may require isolation, characterization, flushing, cleaning, replacement, staged sampling, independent review, public notification and reconciliation of local and central records. Every restored provenance receives a new release record.

## 3. Assurance classes

Four evidence classes define what can enter the release ladder.

### `P0`: unverified or held

The source has missing, expired or contrary evidence. It receives no issue credit. The state is not a claim that the water is contaminated; it is a statement that safe release has not been established.

### `P1`: field-negative

A validated method did not observe its named targets above stated limits. The record includes sample identity, location, time, method, panel version, detection limits, calibration, custody and operator. `P1` may support ordinary operation or a known-agent decision inside the validated envelope. It may not qualify an unknown-agent source under `D2`.

### `P2`: assured continuous

A complete source chain is registered as independent of the event controlling the hold. The object includes source geography, intake, feed characterization, treatment, stabilization, critical power, chemicals and media, residual custody, quality work, conveyance, receiving boundary and release authority. It can supply its rated safe capacity while the independence claim remains valid.

### `P3`: sealed reference

Water was produced before or outside the affected pathway, and its batch record, turnover, storage condition, sector isolation, tamper evidence and local custody remain intact. The inventory may issue under `D2` until its measured endurance expires. A seal is not sufficient by itself; the source and batch that filled the reserve remain part of the claim.

These classes are not levels of confidence on one numerical scale. They answer different questions. `P1` is analytical evidence about stated targets. `P2` is a continuously operating independent chain. `P3` is finite inventory with custody. A source may hold several records at once, but the release decision uses the class whose conditions are actually satisfied.

## 4. Quantitative screen

All four alternatives begin with the Pass 66 `R4` receiver: four independently releasable service islands, a normal requirement of 10,000 cubic metres per day, a planning population of 200,000 and a fifty-percent safe-service floor. The physical baseline includes one half-demand alternate source, but that source is not accepted under `D2` until its complete provenance is qualified.

For architecture `a` in world `w`, release-path availability is:

```text
L(a,w) = central_available(w)                  if central release is required
       = 1[local_domains(a) > domains_lost(w)] otherwise
```

The model calculates every source channel separately. A clean primary may issue under `D0` when a release path exists. An affected primary does not issue when the known-agent panel detects its registered target. In `A2`, an outside-panel negative result is permitted to qualify the primary; the model therefore records that issued share as unsafe.

An alternate continuous source can issue under ordinary operation when clean and released. Under `D2`, it can issue only if the architecture has qualified that provenance. A sealed reserve also requires qualified custody and enough registered endurance for the whole world.

Unsafe issue is:

```text
unsafe(a,w) = primary_issued(a,w) × [1 - primary_clean(w)]
```

Safe service is:

```text
safe_service(a,w) = min(
  receiver_service_ceiling(w),
  clean_primary + clean_assurance_A + clean_assurance_B + clean_reserve,
  1
)
```

A declared world passes only if unsafe issue is zero, safe service is at least fifty percent and the decision occurs within the registered target. Known-agent worlds allow six hours. Unknown-agent switching and local-release worlds allow two. `D3` does not pass the continuity test; it is recorded separately as `SAFE STOP` when all issue is zero.

This formulation refuses to let gross flow conceal harm. `A2` reports one hundred percent gross issue in several outside-panel worlds and zero safe service. A volume-only model would call those worlds successes.

## 5. The four architectures

### `A1`: central confirmatory hold

`A1` preserves the Pass 66 physical receiver and requires one central release path. It does not qualify the existing alternate source for assurance-only operation, carry a sealed reserve or delegate local release. Its declared decision time is six hours.

The architecture safely releases the clean ordinary alternate source after the two known primary-source challenges. It therefore passes two of twelve worlds. It fails the unknown-agent worlds because no source has positive assurance status. It also fails when the central laboratory and data network are unavailable. It never issues affected water and has twelve safety passes.

This is an important control. A safe doctrine can be operationally brittle. Central depth should remain part of the system, but central confirmation alone cannot be the continuity architecture.

### `A2`: sensor-negative local release

`A2` adds one local release domain and allows a negative field panel to qualify the primary source. It acts in half an hour. It passes the two known challenges and central-network loss, for three declared passes.

Its speed is illusory under open-world conditions. The model deliberately places the active agent outside the field panel in seven worlds. `A2` issues affected primary water in each. It records only five safety passes, including the safe universal stop. The architecture is rejected as a release doctrine.

The field panel itself is not rejected. Rapid sensing is valuable for finding known targets, detecting departures, directing sampling, isolating sectors and shortening characterization. What is denied is the authority inference from panel silence to unbounded safety.

### `A3`: qualified source and sealed twenty-four-hour reserve

`A3` qualifies the existing half-demand alternate source as `P2`, adds two local assurance workcells and carries a sealed half-demand `P3` reserve for twenty-four hours. At an eighty-five-percent usable share, 5,000 cubic metres of usable reserve requires about 5,882 cubic metres gross.

The continuous source and reserve provide full normal demand during the first twenty-four hours of a primary hold, subject to receiver capacity. If Assurance A is breached or its consumable lineage is invalidated, the reserve alone provides the fifty-percent floor. If one receiver island is also held, the surviving 87.5-percent receiver ceiling remains above the source combination. The architecture passes ten worlds and all twelve safety screens.

It fails the seventy-two-hour compound world because both the primary and continuous assurance source are held and the reserve exhausts after twenty-four hours. It also enters the universal safe stop.

`A3` is the minimum testable assurance architecture. It converts the previous universal hold into a scoped source quarantine without pretending that inventory is permanent production.

### `A4`: dual-continuous assurance lattice

`A4` adds four local assurance workcells, the same sealed reserve and a second qualified half-demand continuous source. Assurance B has its own source, intake, process, critical power, consumable, residual, quality and custody path. The declared decision time remains two hours.

The architecture passes the first eleven worlds and all twelve safety screens. When the primary and Assurance A remain held for seventy-two hours, Assurance B preserves exactly half service after reserve exhaustion. The architecture then obeys the universal stop in `W12`.

This is the test leader, not a selected system. The model assumes the two continuous provenances are actually independent. A shared watershed, atmospheric exposure, membrane batch, chemical supplier, software release, laboratory method, substation, residual route, workforce or authority could remove the claimed protection. Those relationships must be serialized and challenged.

The architecture also refuses to score `W12` as a conventional pass. Zero issue is the required behavior, but the public still loses water service. Reporting `SAFE STOP` preserves both truths: the operating decision is correct and the continuity promise is unmet.

## 7. Authority architecture

The assurance force needs authority designed with the same care as treatment and conveyance.

The **Water Service generating command** owns the equipment and professions that create source, treatment, storage, sampling and distribution capacity. It may certify that a configured unit is ready for independent test. It may not release its own unknown-agent safety claim.

A standing **Water Assurance Directorate** should own source-class definitions, method envelopes, sample doctrine, custody rules, qualification records, assurance-source configuration and professional development. It would sit inside the National Resilience Establishment but remain distinct from the operational chain that benefits from continued output.

The **receiving quality command** owns local water-state custody, island isolation, sampling, authorized issue and public records at the prepared node. Its officers apply signed release classes during disconnection. Their authority is explicit, geographically bounded and expiring. Offline operation does not permit policy invention.

The **National Public Health Release Authority** owns `D2` and `D3` policy: which prequalified source classes remain eligible, when a condition exceeds their envelope and when the universal stop can be lifted. It is independent of the geographic employment command and the production program.

The **Joint Campaign Assurance Command** maintains the live common-mode register across sources, receiver islands, suppliers, software, laboratories and authorities. Its task is not to operate the plants. It identifies when supposedly separate paths have become correlated.

The **Resilience Test and Evaluation Authority** controls challenge materials, blind trials, configuration lock, data custody and admission. It tests the release decision as a complete public-service chain, including the temptation to override a hold under operational pressure.

The **civil authority** retains allocation, warning, rights and public accountability. It determines who receives scarce safe water and how restrictions affect institutions and households. Technical release establishes what may be used; it does not settle distributional priority.

This is not a committee assembled during an event. It is a standing command-and-profession architecture with schools, exercises, laboratories, inspection, duty rotations and denial power.

## 8. The assurance formation

An `A4` assurance package implies five operating elements.

1. **Source-provenance detachments** operate each independent continuous chain from source survey through residual custody. Their personnel include hydrogeology, intake, process, power, chemistry, microbiology, toxicology, maintenance and environmental control.
2. **Island assurance workcells** maintain local sampling, rapid analysis, custody, offline records, signed release and physical stop controls. Each is independent enough that loss of one does not invalidate the other islands.
3. **Confirmatory laboratory groups** provide higher-resolution and broader methods, comparison across sources, method development and forensic characterization. They support local authority without becoming a single release gate.
4. **Sealed-reference companies** produce, turn over, inspect, distribute and regenerate reserve sectors. The operational challenge is continuous custody, not warehouse ownership.
5. **Public water protection teams** implement accessible issue, restrictions, substitution, institutional priority, warning and service records. They measure safe use at the public boundary.

These elements must deploy with the physical receiver federation. A laboratory without source control cannot create water. A source without custody cannot retain assurance. A technically released tank without accessible distribution is not service. A public queue without quality authority is unsafe.

## 9. Technology competitions

The architecture creates a research portfolio at the scale of a major military mission system.

### Open-world sentinel systems

The objective is rapid detection of unexpected departure, not a fictional universal contaminant detector. Rival systems should combine orthogonal physical, chemical and biological observations; automated preconcentration; source and process fingerprints; local baselines; targeted panels; and anomaly methods whose false-alarm and blind-escape behavior is measured. Every alert must preserve raw observations and method versions. The sentinel may recommend or inhibit; it cannot enlarge release authority.

### Deployable confirmatory laboratories

Purpose-built laboratories should close sample intake, controlled preparation, high-resolution analysis, microbiology, toxicology, secure result review, waste and decontamination in a mobile configuration. Research should target time from representative sample to accountable result, the diversity of methods that can operate under field constraints, and safe operation when external networks are unavailable.

### Continuous assurance-source families

The force should compete source chains rather than select one universal plant. Candidate families may include independently sited surface water, protected groundwater, brackish treatment, coastal seawater desalination, sealed bulk transfer and other sources that can establish a distinct failure geography. Each candidate includes intake, pretreatment, treatment, stabilization, critical power, consumables, residual handling, quality and receiver landing. The protected output is safe service inside a stated feed and hazard envelope.

### Sealed high-turnover reserve

Static emergency water degrades into an inventory and custody problem. A mature system would integrate reserves into ordinary turnover, continuously track sector state, isolate batches, verify tamper evidence, manage water age and refill automatically from qualified sources. Low-mass sanitary storage, robotic cleaning, protected pumping and keyed connections are enabling technologies.

### Offline authority and evidence controls

Release instructions should be signed, bounded by source class and geography, carry an expiry and remain enforceable without continuous access to a central policy service. Local records must later reconcile without “last write wins.” Physical stop channels remain independent of the primary controller. A replayed or expired authority cannot open a valve.

### Correlation discovery

The force needs tools that reveal shared watersheds, suppliers, process media, energy paths, laboratories, code, people and residual routes across nominally independent sources. Graph analysis and automated configuration comparison can help, but the output is a contestable common-mode register. A procurement database that assigns two vendor names to the same upstream membrane batch has not created industrial independence.

### Rapid sanitary switching

Robotic valve operation, branch placement, sampling, flushing and commissioning could reduce the two-hour transition to assurance-only service. The switching system must preserve source identity through manifolds and storage. Faster mixing is not faster assurance.

## 10. Cost, mass and industrial consequence

Pass 66 placed the four-island receiver at about 5,579 dry tonnes, 5,914 chargeable tonnes and a $156.0 billion twenty-five-year comparator. Pass 67 holds the common program constant, removes the prior equipment and receiver allowances for twenty-four operational sets and twelve prepared nodes, and inserts assurance-specific values.

| architecture | dry tonnes per set | chargeable tonnes per set | 40 t positions | equipment per set | receiver per node | revised program |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `A1` | 5,579 | 5,914 | 148 | $2.30B | $2.20B | $156.0B |
| `A2` | 5,624 | 5,959 | 149 | $2.45B | $2.35B | $161.4B |
| `A3` | 5,881 | 6,239 | 156 | $2.75B | $2.75B | $173.4B |
| `A4` | 6,871 | 7,379 | 185 | $3.60B | $3.60B | $204.0B |

The dry calculation adds forty-five tonnes per assurance workcell, thirty-six tonnes per thousand cubic metres of added gross storage hardware and 900 tonnes for the second continuous source chain. Chargeable burden applies packed-cube density floors and 1,050 tonnes for the source chain. Stored water itself is not dry equipment mass; receiver inventory is measured separately.

The $48.0 billion spread between `A1` and `A4` is a declared program variable, not a cost estimate. It exposes what “assurance” would mean as an industrial commitment: multiple production-grade source trains, field and regional laboratories, sanitary storage, instruments, sample systems, power electronics, physical controls, signed evidence infrastructure, independent consumable lineages, depots, training and full-scale challenge facilities.

The industrial policy must avoid manufacturing a common mode. Dual sources built by nominally different prime contractors may share membranes, reagents, sensors, firmware, pumps, subcontractors or quality laboratories. The Department should acquire visibility to the critical upstream lineage, maintain qualified alternatives and periodically demonstrate substitution. Independence costs more than vendor diversity on paper.

This new sector also has export value. A standardized assurance-source and receiver system could support partner utility continuity without exporting nuclear custody or relying on improvised aid. The transferable product would be a verified public-service architecture: source, treatment, laboratory, storage, release and accessible issue. Export claims would require the same local authority, feed, residual and receiving evidence as domestic operation.

## 11. Evidence contract

Seventeen gates govern progression.

1. Hold an affected primary source under a known chemical challenge.
2. Repeat the hold under a known biological challenge.
3. Seed an unknown chemical outside the operating panel and require zero unsafe issue.
4. Repeat with an outside-panel biological agent or toxin.
5. Challenge the claimed independence of source, intake, process, power, residual and custody.
6. Lose the central laboratory and data network while maintaining lawful local service.
7. Lose one local release cell without invalidating the others.
8. Breach one sealed-reserve custody path, isolate it and preserve the floor.
9. Invalidate a common consumable lineage and demonstrate a surviving source.
10. Combine a receiver-island loss with the primary-source hold.
11. Hold the primary and first assurance source for seventy-two hours.
12. Give the field panel a negative result that conflicts with a provenance hold and require the hold to control.
13. Expire and replay release authority; no valve may open from invalid authority.
14. Reconcile conflicting local and central evidence without silent overwrite.
15. Demonstrate that affected people understand restrictions, substitutions and safe uses.
16. Sanitize, refill, recertify and pass a second mission.
17. Release rendering only after representative assurance physics and evidence.

The challenge program should be genuinely blind to the operating unit. The independent test authority selects safe representative challenge materials and conditions, controls configuration and records every sample, order, valve state and public issue. Trials must include panel escapes, false alarms, heterogeneous source conditions, delayed samples, compromised custody, shared suppliers, loss of communications and pressure to continue service.

Performance reporting must separate gross volume, safe volume, affected water issued, population reached, unavailable catchments, decision time, time in each release state and recovery duration. A single “water delivered” number would recreate the analytical error this pass is designed to prevent.

All sixteen technical and mission gates are open. Rendering remains blocked.

## 12. Acquisition decision and next proof

`A1` remains the centralized safety control. `A2` should continue only as an intentionally unsafe comparator used to test whether sensing programs overclaim their authority. `A3` should become the first representative assurance formation because it is the minimum architecture that safely converts a universal hold into a scoped quarantine. `A4` should remain the endurance test leader.

No architecture should enter production. The next pass must serialize the source chains that the present model treats as capacities. It should compete **coastal, inland and sealed assurance-source configurations** under the same half-demand mission, including feed envelope, process sequence, critical power, consumables, residuals, equipment objects, crew, commissioning, lift, fixed works, independent dependencies and recovery. Coastal desalination should be one rival, not the presumed answer. Protected groundwater, brackish sources, surface sources and sealed transfer may dominate different failure geographies.

That configuration study should answer whether two continuous half-demand chains can be independent without duplicating every supporting system, what equipment must remain organic, what may be prepared at receivers, how much reserve is required during switching and whether the resulting mass still fits the carrier-and-node architecture. It should also identify which technologies require clean-sheet development rather than merely larger procurement.

Only after one source family and one assurance workcell reach representative configuration should the Department authorize a physical rendering. The first rendering must show the complete assurance system—source boundary, process, power, residual path, laboratory, storage, island connection, authority controls and public issue—not a sensor box presented as certainty.

## Conclusion

The unknown-agent problem cannot be solved by buying a longer list of tests and declaring the list complete. A finite panel produces bounded negative evidence. When the active condition falls outside that boundary, a doctrine that releases on silence can deliver impressive volumes of unsafe water.

The Department of Resilience needs a different form of technological overmatch. It must be able to quarantine one provenance while shifting public service onto another whose physical chain, custody and authority remain defensible. Sealed reserve buys time. Independent continuous sources buy endurance. Distributed release workcells preserve lawful operation when central systems disappear. A universal stop remains available when the assurance envelope itself is lost.

Pass 67 makes those principles measurable. The central doctrine safely passes two of twelve worlds. The sensor-negative doctrine passes three and is unsafe in seven. A qualified source and twenty-four-hour reserve pass ten. A second continuous provenance passes eleven and holds the fifty-percent floor through seventy-two hours. All remain unadmitted.

This is the kind of institution the original project demands: not a larger emergency stockpile, but a standing national force that turns uncertainty into configured alternatives, keeps safety authority independent of production pressure, and invests at industrial scale in the ability to preserve ordinary life without pretending to know what it does not know.
