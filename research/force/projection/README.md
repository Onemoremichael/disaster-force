# Force projection

This layer explains how certified capacity moves from origin to operating public service through damaged networks.

The sequence narrows from enterprise to indivisible increment:

1. [Joint deployment and distribution](joint-deployment-and-distribution-enterprise.md) joins strategic movement, theater opening, sustainment, reverse flow and regeneration.
2. [The 72-hour Service Entry Force](72-hour-service-entry-force.md) creates six independently originated campaign paths and tests two-cell loss.
3. [Public-service pod configuration](service-entry-pod-configuration.md) replaces divisible tonnes with complete ten-district service packages.

Pass 60's [shared-capacity allocation study](../generation/shared-capacity-allocation-and-claim-denial.md) reopens the enterprise at canonical scale. Forty-eight formation families across ten campaigns present 6.677 million early tonnes after prepositioning against 2.463 million inherited effective 72-hour tonnes. The next projection pass must compile item-identified packages, origins, handling and mode compatibility, carrier and node objects, receivers, deadlines, protected reserve and regeneration rather than assigning aggregate tonnes to modes.

The projection subsystem can deliver only the mobile share of a complete service system. The [receiving subsystem](../reception/README.md) owns the fixed share, destination intake and population relocation problem.
