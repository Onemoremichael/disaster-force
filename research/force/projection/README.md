# Force projection

## From certified capacity to verified public service

Force projection is the causal bridge between a ready formation at origin and a population receiving safe service in damaged geography. It includes carriage, route or flight access, transfer, receiver admission, unloading, utility assembly, assay, civil release, sustainment, reverse flow and reset.

The unit of account is not delivered tonnage or vehicle arrival. It is:

> a complete public-service effect, released by civil authority, within the declared time and damage world.

That rule has repeatedly overturned the attractive answer. Fractional cargo did not equal a complete formation. A carrier did not equal a receiver. Two modes did not equal two independent service chains. Spare vehicles did not preserve specialized functions. The current architecture is valuable because it makes those failures visible.

## Current answer

The current answer is now a proving and measurement architecture rather than a vehicle selection. [CSAC-1](csac1-compound-service-article-and-range-campaign.md) establishes the permanent Service Systems Proving Command, campaigns and ranges. [MESA-1](mesa1-measurement-and-destructive-evidence-architecture.md) adds a three-plane shadow formation; [MESA-1R](mesa1r-representative-evidence-hardware-and-hazard-envelope.md) makes its independent planes physical. The selected planning articles are 110 kg and 135 kg tile cassettes, 1.2 t and 2.6 t carrier nodes and a separate 2.8 t receiver station, all with seven-day independent energy and local storage. The terrain node raises the instrumented carrier to 723.786 t and 34.384 kPa, so the earlier uncertainty allocation no longer passes; a 4,560-cell pressure skin targets 0.129 kPa U95 against a 0.183 kPa limit. Production remains on hold through independent second-lot replication, no earlier than program year fourteen.

Four sets close the 317.5-tonne air payload; two close the 158.75-tonne terrain payload. The change exposes a carrier-interface contradiction: the air carrier needs twenty compact tile saddles rather than eighteen inherited positions, and the terrain carrier needs ten rather than nine. The correction leaves only twenty-six minutes of modeled terrain mission margin and one hour of reset margin.

ESK-40V is a behavior and technology-development reference, not a production choice. Its $6.26079719 trillion twenty-five-year boundary is $256.92095 billion above full exact-cell duplication. It remains worth testing because it avoids 207 carrier articles and creates a modular, adaptable and exportable civil-systems industrial base. All 200 physical evidence gates remain open. Exterior rendering remains blocked.

## Design constitution

Every projection study follows five rules.

### Effect before platform

The population, service bundle, deadline, duration and damage world are registered before selecting a vehicle. A platform is admitted only if the complete source-to-public chain closes.

### Complete burden

Dry mass, wet mass, cube, deck, center of gravity, heat, energy, labor, tools, safety, waste, access, receiver and reset are part of the payload. A missing burden is not a future detail if it can change the architecture.

### Non-substitutable gates

Mass cannot compensate for absent water quality. Speed cannot compensate for an unavailable receiver. A second vendor cannot compensate for a shared sub-tier cause. Hard failures are not averaged into weighted scores.

### Common meaning, separate causes

Services need common units, custody states and public handoff. Compound assurance requires separate decisive machines, control roots, supply lineages, authorities and pre-release receiver dependencies.

### Correction propagation

A physical correction must propagate through vehicle, formation, mission clock, receiver, workforce, fleet, industry and cost. The model is not closed if one layer quietly retains the superseded assumption.

## Active decision boundaries

The next projection pass must compete supplier-neutral architectures for the five MESA-1R node classes, pressure skin and one-way interfaces; freeze serial prototype configurations; and write the P01–P08 execution protocol. Its first physical objective is to determine whether seven-day autonomy, denied-time custody, outward heat rejection, removal equivalence and the revised 0.129-kPa soil U95 survive representative hardware. No interior or exterior form is authorized before P08 closes measured mass, volume, access, heat and hazard envelopes.

The following claims remain prohibited:

- that either tile lineage is technically feasible at its design mass;
- that 0.5 hours is an observed interface time;
- that 34.249 kilopascals is representative dynamic soil performance;
- that the two lineages are industrially independent;
- that the $6.261 trillion boundary forecasts appropriations;
- that modularity is cheaper than duplication; or
- that a reconciled workbook admits fleet procurement.
