## Decision

1. A humanitarian adjunct makes rescue a residual user of military logistics.
2. An expeditionary basing command turns civil access into presence and command.
3. An export-statecraft enterprise lets sales substitute for public readiness.
4. A unified Strategic Power Command can define need, build capacity, employ it, measure itself and retain failure.

The selected compact contains seven articles: public guarantee; technology and industry; formation generation; readiness and allocation; domestic employment; invited activation; and evidence, renewal and regeneration. These articles join the five inherited Department circuits without collapsing their exclusive warrants.

## Constitutional mechanics

The lifecycle is: public obligation → technology and industry → formation → readiness → claim → request and acceptance → bridge → complete service → transfer and exit → evidence → disposition → regeneration.

Fourteen nonconversion laws prevent every attractive shortcut. Spending is not capability; industry is not formation; formation is not campaign; invitation or treaty is not activation; access is not command; security support is not mission ownership; bridge output is not strategic effect; partner capacity is not U.S. readiness; market revenue is not appropriation; historical success is not current authority; and prestige cannot renew failure. Accepted invited serials are not recallable for domestic urgency without bilateral release.

Nine exclusive authorities distribute public purpose, production, technical proof, formation generation, allocation, domestic employment, host consent, effect measurement and security support. No office may own the full causal chain. Strategic power therefore enters the inherited employment/transfer/regeneration circuit; it does not become a super-command above the Department.
