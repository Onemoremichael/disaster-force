# Pass 198 — Bilateral civil-service activation and access transaction

**Research object:** `SPC-3`  
**Decision level:** mature Department strategy  
**Verdict:** `CLOSE`

## Branch and resolution

The deepest active branch is `SPC-3`, the third of five permitted strategic-power passes.

**Resolution test:** Reversing the activation system would change who may invite or employ the force, whether accepted international serials can be recalled, whether territorial access implies coercive authority and whether the two receiver commons can produce service before main-force arrival, so it changes the mature architecture and central thesis.

## Decision

An invited campaign will execute as a **bilateral civil-service transaction**, not an executive humanitarian declaration, defense-led access operation, contractor task order or automatic treaty trigger. A standing compact creates the ability to ask; only an authenticated event request creates a claim. Silence, disaster severity and technical ability are not consent.

Twelve institutions retain separate positive powers. Host authorities issue invitation, population priority, receiver admission, stop and transfer acceptance. U.S. civil authority accepts or declines the bounded objective. The Joint Capacity Authority certifies family readiness; NMAC schedules exact serials; the International Custody Chamber binds dual-key issue, non-recall and bilateral release; the Rights and Scarcity Tribunal protects access and remedy; a bilateral cell compiles corridors and receivers; an external security authority protects access without inheriting the mission; Operational Command employs the force; and the Resilience Proving Service holds technical evidence.[^1]

No actor can manufacture the successor state it wants. Host consent cannot create U.S. readiness. A U.S. acceptance cannot create host access. A ready serial cannot issue its own civil purpose. Successful output cannot certify public effect.

## The executable transaction

The state machine runs from standing compact through authenticated request, compiled service vector, U.S. acceptance, campaign reservation, issued custody, bridge service, full employment, transfer, bilateral release and regeneration. Ten transitions each produce a bounded artifact and a rollback or hold.

Twelve event warrants govern one actual mission: sovereign request; public-service vector; rights and remedy; U.S. acceptance; complete family reservation; collision-free serial schedule; primary and alternate access; separate security; dual-key non-recall custody; independent evidence; host transfer acceptance; and bilateral release with regeneration. These do not duplicate Pass 196’s treaty seals: treaty seals constitute the standing relationship; event warrants prove that one campaign may advance.[^2]

The reference transaction registers all 675 invited formations. Each receives a primary and differently caused alternate corridor, producing 1,350 access claims. Every corridor has seven governed segments—origin, strategic movement, sovereign boundary, precinct, district distribution, receiver and reverse/reset—creating 9,450 segment reservations. A landing clearance or port berth is therefore not a complete access claim.

## The bridge-clock correction

Pass 197’s fourteen-day stock was sized against transit alone. That result is superseded. The harder campaign requires twelve hours to authenticate, compile, accept and reserve, followed by fourteen days of transit. Fourteen days of stock therefore expires twelve hours before nominal main-force arrival and carries no disruption margin.[^3]

`SPC-3` raises the common bridge to eighteen days. Coverage becomes 432 hours. The Indo-Pacific design case reaches nominal main arrival at hour 348 and retains eighty-four hours, or 24.1 percent; the Atlantic/Americas case arrives at hour 252 and retains 180 hours. The change raises two-network sustainment stock from about 0.683 Mt to 0.878 Mt, an increment of 195,000 tonnes. Combined with 25 percent forward early mass, the controlled forward-material boundary becomes 0.878–1.49 Mt depending on item-level nesting.

The target clock authenticates the request by hour two, compiles service and rights by hour six, completes U.S. acceptance and full-family reservation by hour twelve, issues admitted forward stock on the same boundary, produces the first host bridge effect by hour twenty-four and places advance integration by hour seventy-two. These are architecture requirements, not observed performance.

## Bridge service is real service, not fractional deployment

Nine host-led bridge threads cover civil authority, water and sanitation, power and thermal safety, health continuity, mobility and access, food and household continuity, immediate hazard stabilization, evidence, and residual/reverse flow. They may earn measured local effect before full-force arrival. They do not reduce the 675-formation reservation or turn partial output into campaign completion.

Consent loss, safety stop and recall are distinct. A host may revoke territorial consent; the force must stop new issue, safe hazardous systems and transfer or withdraw. A U.S. technical authority may stop an unsafe configuration while its international custody remains committed. Loss of security suspends or reroutes exposed movement; the Department does not assume combat. A domestic recall demand after acceptance is rejected unless bilateral release occurs.[^4]

## Economic and technological consequence

The transaction system screens at USD 67.5B of capital and USD 12.2B/year in the base case, with USD 39.8–104B capital and USD 4.77–26.1B/year sensitivities. It funds two network transaction centers, twelve district cells, thirty-six precinct control nodes, 675 serial custody kits, 1,350 corridor packages, offline evidence and adversarial activation ranges. The base lies inside Pass 197’s receiver-commons capital and Pass 194’s command-and-truth operating range; it is not additive.[^1][^3]

Technology must make constitutional separation physical: authenticated sovereign requests under partition; executable service-vector compilers; family-complete reservation; multi-cause corridor proof; dual-key stock controls; non-recall ledgers; civil-security interfaces; offline bilateral operating pictures; safe revocation; transfer proof; and public transaction replay. The objective is not faster paperwork. It is a system in which no emergency, network owner or executive can silently convert access into command.

The twenty-sheet workbook contains the complete serial and corridor registers, state machine, authorities, warrants, clocks, bridge services, stop rules, failure worlds, technology and financial model. Fourteen saved-file audits pass and five adversarial mutations restore. Every request, identity, corridor, effect and release remains source-empty; authority is zero and rendering remains blocked.
