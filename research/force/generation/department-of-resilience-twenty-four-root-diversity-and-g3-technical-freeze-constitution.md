# Department of Resilience twenty-four-root diversity and G3 technical-freeze constitution

**Pass:** 175
**Research object:** `C3-RDFC-1`
**Decision level:** six-system root architecture, industrial independence and `G3` release
**Verdict:** `CLOSE`

## Disposition before design

The deepest live chain is `C3-JRC-1 → C3-RSEP-1 → C3-RDFC-1`; this is its first pass at root-diversity resolution.

**Resolution test:** reversing the diversity law or the twenty-four-root slate would change the Department’s industrial structure, the systems admitted to Cascadia `G4`, the use of the first USD 500B and whether one corporate or technical failure can remove several mission systems.

It passes because root independence determines force survivability and national production power, not merely procurement scoring.

## Decision: common public seams, unlike execution roots

Four letters do not create four roots. The selected constitution separates commonality into three classes:

1. **Public seams** may span all twenty-four roots: service states, exchange records, units, physical reference frames, safety and rights rules, custody identifiers and evidence formats. Each seam requires at least four independent implementations and no live central broker.
2. **Substitutable pools** may share a commodity, spectrum band or standard component class, but no provider, lot, region, tool or control authority may support more than two roots. Every dependent root carries a qualified alternative.
3. **Decisive ancestry** is root-exclusive across the national slate. A root integrator, prime work physics, energy conversion, structural process, critical-feedstock conversion, control keys, communications/time implementation, calibration chain, production tooling, final assembly cell, repair route or evidence chain may serve only one of twenty-four roots.

This requires twenty-four root houses rather than four national primes each supplying six systems. A house may integrate several mechanisms inside one root; it cannot own another campaign root. Corporate ownership, beneficial control, facilities and critical subtiers remain part of the ancestry test. Commodity labels do not hide a common converter, and open-source code does not erase a common signer, build chain or runtime.

## The first complete root slate

Every root carries every route and all seven service stages in its system. The names are architecture theses, not product names or exterior forms.

| System | Root A | Root B | Root C | Root D |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Theater entry | Oceanic mass bridge | Buoyant air bridge | Austere surface bridge | Amphibious autonomous mesh |
| Civil terrain | Civic edge grid | Air-and-skin reach | Building utility spine | Neighborhood machine swarm |
| Hazard control | Atmospheric control wing | Fluid intervention lattice | Autonomous terrain corps | Distributed control field |
| Lifelines | Oceanic nuclear utility | Inland geothermal train | Renewable electrochemical swarm | Synthetic-fuel service fleet |
| Human continuity | Clinical continuity campus | Neighborhood care fabric | Mobile care network | Host-building conversion system |
| Regeneration | Heavy arsenal base | Distributed microfactory mesh | Exchange and remanufacture network | Closed-loop sustainment fleet |

The workbook expands these twenty-four theses into ninety-six root–problem-field assignments. The lifeline slate, for example, does not ask four firms to package the same generator and membrane. It tests a nuclear-thermal coastal producer, rapid-bore geothermal and brackish-water train, renewable/storage/electrochemical swarm and fuel-synthetic cogeneration fleet. All must deliver strategic production, potable water, local power, a grid bridge, thermal safety and residual closure. The hazard roots similarly cross fire, flood and debris rather than specializing one root into each hazard.

The 1,448 Pass 174 positions remain the campaign obligation: 248 entry, 192 civil-terrain, 96 hazard-control, 152 lifeline, 512 human-continuity and 248 regeneration positions. Four complete roots also create 112 root–route obligations. A root that omits a hard route is a component portfolio, not a root.

## What `G3` must freeze

Each root receives fourteen conjunctive release gates: complete routes; seven-stage state machine; four-field architecture; numeric service thresholds; closed mass, energy, flow and residual accounts; safety and rights; public interface control; declared decisive ancestry; executable build records; representative subsystem evidence; disconnected and manual control; root-native evidence and outside replay; repair, reset and second-team reproduction; and independent release.

The freeze owns configuration and the right to enter an integrated campaign. It does not prove `G4` service. None of the twenty-four draft roots currently passes a gate. All 288 decisive-ancestry declarations are `UNDECLARED`; all 336 `G3` gate records are `OPEN`; force credit remains zero. Filling a spreadsheet field cannot establish physical independence. The declaration must bind serial lots, facilities, keys, people, tools, calibration and controlled loss trials.

If a root mechanism dies, its evidence remains public and the system must constitute a new baseline. It may not borrow a rival’s decisive chain and retain its old letter. Public seams remain stable so a challenger can enter without rebuilding the other five systems.

## Financial and industrial boundary

Pass 171 assigns the first two waves 50 percent of the conserved program: USD 500B through `G3`. This contains USD 275B for mission-system work, USD 75.0B for service technical bases, USD 75.0B for national common capacity, USD 50.0B for the sovereign technical estate and USD 25.0B for failure reserve.

The mission share preserves the six system weights. Its cumulative `G0–G3` reservations are USD 44.5B entry, USD 45.9B civil terrain, USD 27.3B hazard control, USD 36.0B lifelines, USD 76.6B human continuity and USD 44.7B regeneration. Equal division inside each system gives each root an option-sized reservation of USD 6.83B–19.1B. These are ceilings for rival learning paths and reproducible roots, not platform prices or earned payments.

The industrial consequence is intentional: 24 root houses, 96 mechanism campaigns, 288 exclusive ancestry cells, public standards and destructive access. Concentrating the work into four cross-system primes would be administratively simpler but would turn one ownership, key, facility or balance-sheet failure into a six-system casualty.

## Closure

`C3-RDFC-1` closes the diversity taxonomy, first twenty-four architecture theses, exclusive-ancestry cap, complete-route obligation, `G3` release record and cumulative financial boundary. It releases no builder, mechanism, root or position.
