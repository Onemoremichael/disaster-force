## Decision: quarter the load, never a route

The complete `G3` article is a **system-native quarter-load service unit**. For each route, its bill is the ceiling of one quarter of the inherited Cascadia load. Ceiling by route matters: rounding the system total would permit a small route to vanish. Every unit retains every route, all seven service stages, public acceptance and residual custody.

Four alternatives were tested. One token per route suppresses coupled load. Equal article counts erase real system complexity. A full Cascadia root duplicates the integrated campaign before `G3`. The quarter-load ceiling alone preserves all routes, scales with the registered campaign and exactly reconstructs the ten-position lifeline sector already frozen by Pass 177.

## Six different public denominators

The shared arithmetic does not create shared evidence. Each proof unit must meet its own indivisible public contract:

- `MS-ET` accepts at least 5,630 tonnes/day of complete formation and cargo flow by hour 72, from denied strategic entry through dry staging and onward custody, for thirty days.
- `MS-CT` governs three `HCG-35B` origin workloads: about 105,000 people, 42,800 households and 180 lawfully released route-kilometres. Every household receives a verified disposition or remains in an explicit exception queue through 168 hours.
- `MS-HC` operates fire, hydraulic and debris pathways together. Fire-control and basin-removal margins must each remain above zero after loss; every debris and intervention residual has accepted custody; the civilian and responder envelope holds for thirty days.
- `MS-LF` preserves the inherited 500,000-person sector: 25,000 m³/day water, 20,000 m³/day wastewater or residual intake, 25 MW firm, 20 MWth, 25,000 m³ buffer and 60 MWh black start.
- `MS-HU` preserves 50,000 person–household–care relationships for 168 hours across origin, definitive care, patient transfer, relocation, receiver and workforce states. Motion without an accepted relationship-bearing destination is failure.
- `MS-RT` sustains one 99-position all-six quarter-load stack for thirty days, accepts every return and residual, and restores or replaces every critical configuration within 168 hours of return. A commodity subtotal cannot compensate for one missing class.

These thresholds are controlled acquisition hypotheses. No workbook status converts them into observed performance.

## One proof grammar, six native campaigns

Every root receives `P1/P2` complete units for simultaneous challenge and clean control, plus a separately built `R1` reproduction. Each root also owns ten support units: three evidence cells, two repair/reset units, two civil/configuration units, two closed-loop bases and one protected replacement bank.

The resulting root bills are 61 entry, 52 civil-terrain, 31 hazard, 40 lifeline, 112 human-continuity and 61 regeneration units. Across four roots each, the proof estate contains **1,428 top-level article units**.

Each system writes eighteen native worlds: twelve route-physics or public-service challenges and six cross-route authority, control, ancestry, receiver, repair and regeneration challenges. Every world receives one challenged `P` event and one matched clean control; six decisive worlds repeat on `R1`. The common denominator is therefore forty-two valid events per root, 168 per system and **1,008 across twenty-four roots**. Nine invalidity-only reserves per root raise the maximum to 1,224 attempts but cannot erase valid adverse evidence.

Evidence scales with the bill. Article records, events, replays, ancestry, calibration, repair, reproduction, safety, release and route dossiers produce 4,276 required packages. Nine root-native proving functions per root create 216 function records. All remain source-empty.
