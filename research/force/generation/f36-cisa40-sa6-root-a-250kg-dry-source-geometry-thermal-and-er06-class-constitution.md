# RBM-1 — Root A 250 kg dry-source geometry, thermal architecture and ER-06 class constitution

## Decision abstract

`RBM-1` defines the sixth physical rung of the `AM-06` entry ladder created by [RBG-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-am06-source-water-ignition-entry-ladder-reset.md). It begins only after the 100-kilogram dry flame class in [RBK-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-100kg-dry-source-flame-state-quadrature-and-er04-class-constitution.md) and the 100-kilogram source–water coupling class in [RBL-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-100kg-4p8m3-water-action-coupling-and-er05-class-constitution.md) are physically accepted. Its question is:

> What public field geometry, atmosphere boundary and thermal evidence architecture can admit 250 kilograms of dry energetic source across five square metres, deliver one measured ignition opportunity, preserve the four-root combustion quadrature, end safely and return after 1,008 hours without treating the 100-kilogram cell as a scalable component?

The answer is not two `FRC-100` cells placed side by side. Source inventory rises by 2.5 while registered area rises by 2. The five-square-metre state therefore carries 50 kilograms per square metre rather than 40, a 25 percent increase in areal loading and mean source depth. At the inherited burning-flux screen, heat-release rate doubles to 11.7117 megawatts, source energy rises to 10,647 megajoules, notional duration rises to 15.152 minutes and equivalent circular field diameter rises by the square root of two. The inherited 40-millimetre source route also reaches 2.5 times its 100-kilogram velocity. No single scale factor describes the event.

`RBM-1` creates four rival `FGX-5` field architectures: a monolithic field plane, a twin-cell bridge, a four-tile matrix and a radial annular field. Two independent lineages at three campuses produce twenty-four candidate articles. They execute 192 nonclass geometry trials and 144 blinded pairwise comparisons before one architecture can populate an eighteen-module `FRC-250` bank. Selection is conjunctive. A hard failure in source-field truth, combustion-root independence, atmosphere capture, boundary heat, safeing, recovery or replay cannot be offset by a score.

Sixteen serviceable modules then execute ninety-six integrated qualification cycles. Each module traverses a six-state bridge from the accepted 100-kilogram cell through intermediate areal loading, expanded geometry, the exact 250-kilogram state, atmosphere perturbation and one-root denial. The program preserves 100-, 125- and 250-kilogram results as distinct observations rather than fitting an assumed scale curve.

The exact `ER-06` class contains twelve campus-by-source-lineage-by-module-lineage cells and four invalid-attempt reserves. Every possible class event carries 250 kilograms of source, numeric-zero candidate water, one accepted igniter opportunity, five square metres of surveyed field, fourteen separately decided propositions, 104 gates, 416 evidence records, 896 active channels, twenty-eight safeing paths, four combustion-root packets, geometry and atmosphere accounts, two outside replays and a fixed 1,008-hour return.

Required qualification and maximum class issue govern 21.6 tonnes of energetic event source. Tiered evidence provision is approximately 80.52 terabytes. The primary child calendar is ninety-four weeks and the maximum is ninety-eight. With four later rungs retained at provisional 17/21-week allowances, the ten-rung floor becomes 479 primary and 513 maximum weeks.

The existing `RAS-3R3` parent technically contains that calendar but does not contain the scientific formation or technology program. `RBM-1` therefore converts the program into a permanent three-campus hazard-physics establishment and issues `RAS-3R4`: 624 weeks, 2,400 concurrent positions, USD 7.5 billion capital, USD 500 million per year and USD 2.6 billion cross-classified technology. The child assigns 900 execution positions, USD 1.35 billion capital, USD 165 million per year and USD 500 million technology.

An accepted `ER-06-QUALIFIED` class establishes only the 250-kilogram dry-source reference on the selected five-square-metre geometry. It does not establish 12 cubic metres of candidate water, a 500-kilogram fire, candidate performance, production, deployment, procurement, export or exterior form. The next admissible object is `RBN-1`, the `ER-07` 250-kilogram / 12-cubic-metre water-action coupling repair.

## 3. Four `FGX-5` candidate architectures

### 3.1 `MFP-5`: monolithic field plane

`MFP-5` uses one continuous 2.5-by-2.0-metre surveyed insert. It minimizes seams and gives the product and calorimetric roots one continuous boundary. Its principal risks are long drain paths, central access, thermal bowing, large replacement mass and a single distorted plane affecting every root.

### 3.2 `TCB-2`: twin-cell bridge

`TCB-2` joins two 2.5-square-metre fields beneath one common atmosphere boundary. Each side can receive independent source and root witnesses, while a registered bridge region reveals plume and product interaction. Its principal risks are seam retention, asymmetric ignition, false duplication, shared exhaust capture and treating two 125-kilogram subevents as one 250-kilogram event without proving interaction.

### 3.3 `QTM-4`: four-tile matrix

`QTM-4` uses four 1.25-square-metre source-field tiles with synchronized source issue and independently witnessed final states. It shortens local drain and exchange distances and permits controlled tile denial. Its principal risks are excessive internal boundaries, phase offsets, preferential source routing, seam flames and a high common-interface count.

### 3.4 `RAF-5`: radial annular field

`RAF-5` places the five-square-metre field around a one-metre-diameter central evidence and safeing well. The annulus supports protected central ion, optical, product and thermal references with radial drains. Its principal risks are inner-edge recirculation, nonuniform radial issue, annular flame topology and a geometry that may be unusually favorable to the reference instrumentation.

## 8. Atmosphere and exhaust boundary

Combustion cannot be treated as source-only mass closure. Oxygen enters, carrier gases traverse the field, products leave, and heat changes density and flow. `RBM-1` requires separate source, atmosphere and product accounts.

The workbook carries a declared stoichiometric oxygen sensitivity rather than a material fact. At a 3.5-kilogram-oxygen-per-kilogram-source planning case, 23.2 percent oxygen mass fraction and 50 percent excess-air factor, the 0.275-kilogram-per-second burning screen requires approximately 6.223 kilograms or 5.186 cubic metres of air per second at the declared density. This is an input-range calculation pending source assay.

A separate thermal exhaust screen assumes that 65 percent of the heat-release screen enters a controlled gas stream, gas heat capacity is 1.01 kilojoules per kilogram-kelvin and accepted temperature rise is 200 kelvin. It produces 37.686 kilograms or 31.405 cubic metres per second. Applying a 1.5 capacity factor yields 47.108 cubic metres per second. This is a facility-sizing hypothesis, not a predicted plume flow.

The two screens cannot be merged. Oxygen sufficiency does not prove thermal capture; a large exhaust flow does not prove the source received known oxygen or that products were measured without bypass.

## 23. Stop, amendment and release law

RBM-1 stops or narrows if:

- no geometry closes all hard gates;
- a geometry changes its decisive field after selection;
- source area, depth, route hold-up or terminal destination is unknown;
- ignition coverage is inferred from flame;
- PHO or ION loses decisive field access;
- MOL cannot bound bypass, wall retention or transit delay;
- CAL cannot separate field, structure and exhaust energy;
- atmosphere supply and exhaust capture share one unverified estimator;
- source-account or fixed class limits fail;
- public intervention becomes reference performance;
- a valid adverse result is repeated;
- a reserve crosses cell, source lineage or module lineage;
- exchange transfers hidden source, product or thermal state;
- outside replay requires origin services; or
- parent capacity is absent.

Architecture selection authorizes module construction only. Module qualification authorizes exact class planning only. One event credit authorizes no other cell. `ER-06-QUALIFIED` authorizes constitution of `RBN-1`; it does not issue 12 cubic metres of water or admit a candidate system.

## 24. Research stocktake

RBM-1 changes the program's understanding in eight ways.

First, equal areal heat-release screens do not create geometric similarity. Total energy, duration, field diameter, source depth and route rate still change.

Second, a larger fire is an atmosphere system. Oxygen supply, carrier gas, exhaust heat, product capture and bypass require their own conserved boundaries.

Third, modularity is a hypothesis rather than a virtue. Seams, phase offsets and shared exhaust paths can make a tiled field less interpretable than a continuous one.

Fourth, intermediate states are scientific controls. The 125-kilogram bridge helps separate areal loading from field expansion without claiming a universal scale law.

Fifth, the four-root quadrature survives only as a principle. Every root needs new geometry-specific calibration, field coverage and return evidence.

Sixth, safeing expands with the controlled physics. Source, ignition, atmosphere and exhaust final states must remain independently observable.

Seventh, repeated source-scale research requires permanent public facilities, professions and industrial interfaces. A temporary project cannot sustain long-return modules, rival architectures and outside replay across successive hazard states.

Eighth, ambitious technology funding is part of epistemic honesty. Large-field tomography, atmosphere accounting and robotic recovery are not optional enhancements when the event cannot otherwise be known.
