# RBO-1 — Root A 500 kg dry-source field-time, atmosphere and ER-08 class constitution

## Decision abstract

`RBO-1` defines the next admissible research system after the 250-kilogram / 12-cubic-metre coupling class in [RBN-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-250kg-12m3-water-action-coupling-and-er07-class-constitution.md). It does not double `FRC-250`, enlarge a source bin or assume that a 500-kilogram unignited transfer establishes a 500-kilogram fire. The new state places 500 kilograms of dry source on 6.25 square metres and introduces one registered ignition opportunity with numeric-zero candidate water.

The source inventory doubles relative to `ER-06`, but field area rises only 25 percent. At the registered 800-kilogram-per-cubic-metre density screen, mean source depth rises from 62.5 to 100 millimetres and source per area rises from 50 to 80 kilograms per square metre. Both increase by 60 percent. Equivalent field diameter rises only 11.8 percent, while source energy doubles to 21,294 megajoules and energy per equivalent circular boundary rises 78.9 percent.

The inherited burning-flux screen produces a 14.639625-megawatt heat-release value, only 25 percent above `ER-06`, because active area grows by 25 percent. The same screen produces a 24.242-minute notional duration, 60 percent longer than `ER-06`. The scale discontinuity is therefore not mainly width or peak power. It is deep inventory, long temporal exposure, evolving internal topology, atmosphere accumulation, sensor drift, thermal soak and post-event recovery.

`RBO-1` creates four rival `FGT-625` field-time architectures. `DBS-625` uses a continuous deep batch shell with remote measurement. `PIL-625` introduces a removable penetrating instrument lattice. `SCM-25` divides the active field into twenty-five conserved control volumes. `RAF-625` forms an annular source field around a protected central reference well. Every candidate must receive the complete 500-kilogram inventory before the ignition opportunity. Serial feed, reduced inventory and post-selection geometry repair are prohibited.

Two independent builder lineages at three campuses produce twenty-four candidate articles. Each executes ten nonenergetic or thermal-surrogate trials, for 240 candidate trials. All six architecture pairs then execute twenty-four blinded comparisons, producing 144 matched comparisons. Candidate and comparison work governs 84 tonnes of nonenergetic source-like simulant before any architecture may populate the `FRC-500` bank.

Sixteen serviceable modules execute 128 eight-state scale-bridge cycles. The bridge crosses the 100-kilogram and 250-kilogram accepted anchors, a 250-kilogram field-expansion state, a 400-kilogram high-areal-load state, the exact 500-kilogram state, atmosphere accumulation, root or service denial, and thermal-soak return. Qualification governs 48 tonnes of energetic source.

The exact `ER-08` class contains twelve campus-by-source-lineage-by-module-lineage cells and four invalid-attempt reserves. Maximum qualification and class issue governs 56 tonnes of energetic source. Each possible class event binds sixteen propositions, 128 hard gates, 512 evidence records, 1,152 active channels, thirty-six safeing paths, four conserved terminal accounts, one field-material exposure ledger, two outside replays and a fixed 1,008-hour module return.

The child calendar is 123 primary weeks and 127 maximum weeks. With two later rungs retained at provisional 17/21-week allowances, the ten-rung floor becomes 679 primary and 713 maximum weeks. `RAS-3R5` can contain only the primary child and would leave no credible correction or final-rung margin. `RBO-1` therefore issues `RAS-3R6`: 832 weeks, 4,200 concurrent positions, USD 14 billion capital, USD 900 million per year and USD 6.2 billion in mission-led technology.

An accepted `ER-08-QUALIFIED` class establishes only the selected 500-kilogram dry-source field-time reference on 6.25 square metres. It does not establish a 24-cubic-metre coupled-water event, candidate performance, operational suppression, production, deployment, procurement, export or exterior form. The next admissible object is `RBP-1`, the `ER-09` 500-kilogram / 24-cubic-metre water-action coupling and class constitution.

## 2. The hidden discontinuity

A scale description based only on total source and active area would miss the decisive change. The field becomes only 0.298 metre wider in equivalent diameter, yet it must contain twice the source energy and remain observable through a substantially longer interval.

Five mechanisms can change independently:

1. **Depth and exposed surface.** A deeper inventory can shield interior material, change void structure, delay exposure and create local collapse or redistribution.
2. **Time.** A 60-percent longer notional interval increases sensor drift, liner heat soak, deposit growth, filter loading, calibration loss and causal ambiguity.
3. **Boundary burden.** Total energy doubles while equivalent boundary grows only 11.8 percent. A local boundary cannot be credited from the average.
4. **Atmosphere accumulation.** Oxygen demand, carrier flow, exhaust heat, products and bypass persist through a longer event and may change as the source field evolves.
5. **Recovery.** Residue, absorbed source, transformed products and field deformation can occupy three-dimensional locations that a surface map cannot close.

`RBO-1` therefore treats source inventory topology and causal time as primary research objects.

## 5. Four `FGT-625` architectures

### 5.1 `DBS-625`: deep batch shell

`DBS-625` places the complete inventory on one continuous 2.5-by-2.5-metre field. PHO, ION, topology and thermal observations originate from protected perimeter, overhead and under-field paths. The field has no internal physical discontinuity.

Its value is a continuous source boundary with simple gross custody. Its risks are remote inference of internal state, central blind volume, long heat paths, large liner deformation and common loss if the continuous plane changes shape.

### 5.2 `PIL-625`: penetrating instrument lattice

`PIL-625` preserves one continuous outer field but introduces a removable six-by-six lattice of protected penetrants. Each penetrant contains independent topology, temperature and ion interfaces, with surveyed insertion depth and post-event mass and deformation records.

Its value is direct depth-resolved observation. Its risks are intrusive alteration of the source field, penetrant shadowing, thermal conduction, deposit bridging and the possibility that the measurement system changes the phenomenon it must observe.

### 5.3 `SCM-25`: segmented control-volume matrix

`SCM-25` divides the 2.5-by-2.5-metre active field into twenty-five 0.25-square-metre conserved cells. Each cell receives a separately weighed source fraction, independent final-element state, local topology witness and terminal source account. The cells share one registered atmosphere boundary.

Its value is local conservation and fault isolation. Its risks are twenty metres of internal discontinuity, seam flames, false independence, common exhaust coupling and a field that behaves as twenty-five adjacent experiments rather than one event.

### 5.4 `RAF-625`: radial annular field

`RAF-625` places 6.25 square metres of active source area around a one-metre-diameter protected central reference well. Radial topology, ion, product, thermal and safeing paths connect the inner and outer boundaries. The excluded central well increases the outer footprint but does not reduce active source area.

Its value is protected interior access and two-sided radial observation. Its risks are annular recirculation, favorable geometry for the instruments, inner-edge heat concentration and source behavior that may not transfer to a simply connected field.

## 8. The new topology root

`TOP-500` is a fifth physical root. It does not decide flame, transformation or energy. It observes the source inventory as a three-dimensional, time-varying object.

The root must measure or bound:

1. initial occupied volume and vertical distribution;
2. exposed surface and internal void fraction;
3. local settlement, collapse, spread and ejection;
4. time of material entry into each active zone;
5. subsurface temperature and phase where physically meaningful;
6. source leaving the active field by route and time;
7. blind volume and its uncertainty; and
8. terminal source position before recovery.

The root may use surveyed mass cells, depth tomography, removable penetrants, active acoustics, radiometry or other new technology. It cannot infer its decisive state from PHO, ION, MOL or CAL labels. Each candidate must disclose how its measurement method perturbs the field.

## 9. Five-root constitution

### 9.1 `PHO-500T`

`PHO-500T` reconstructs emission, absorption, obscuration, occupancy and extinction over the longer field-time interval. It carries protected short and long paths, independent obscuration references and explicit unresolved volume.

### 9.2 `ION-500T`

`ION-500T` provides independent reaction-zone evidence at the surface, depth interfaces and field boundaries. Local witness loss remains local. It cannot use a photon-derived flame label.

### 9.3 `TOP-500`

`TOP-500` owns inventory geometry, depth, void, redistribution and terminal position. It reports perturbation caused by its own sensors and cannot fill blind volume with a symmetry assumption.

### 9.4 `MOL-500T`

`MOL-500T` observes source-derived gas, particulate, condensate, wall deposit, liner absorption and residue. Each path carries transit time, retention, sample fraction and analytical loss.

### 9.5 `CAL-500T`

`CAL-500T` closes field heat, radiant transfer, perimeter consequence, structure storage, exhaust enthalpy, residual energy and cooldown. It must separate a lower peak from a longer integral.

PHO and ION jointly decide flame. TOP decides source inventory state. MOL decides transformation. CAL decides energy and consequence. No root repairs another after observation.

## 10. Atmosphere and exhaust institution

The declared oxygen screen uses a 0.34375-kilogram-per-second burning rate, 3.5 kilograms oxygen per kilogram source, 23.2-percent oxygen mass fraction and 50-percent excess-air factor. It produces 7.779 kilograms or 6.482 cubic metres of air per second at the declared 1.2-kilogram-per-cubic-metre density.

The separate thermal-exhaust screen assigns 65 percent of the 14.639625-megawatt heat-release value to a controlled gas stream with 1.01 kilojoules per kilogram-kelvin heat capacity and 200-kelvin rise. It produces 47.108 kilograms or 39.256 cubic metres per second. Applying a 1.5 facility factor produces 58.885 cubic metres per second.

These screens answer different questions. Oxygen sufficiency does not establish heat or product capture. Exhaust capacity does not establish oxygen provenance, field entrainment, transit delay or bypass.

The longer interval creates new obligations:

- supply composition and flow must remain known as the field evolves;
- exhaust capture must be spatially resolved, not only measured at a common duct;
- thermal expansion, smoke loading and deposits must not silently change calibration;
- condensation and wall retention must remain in the product account;
- bypass must be observed independently of the controlled stream; and
- entry state must remain a separate safety decision.

## 28. Research stocktake

`RBO-1` changes the program's understanding in nine ways.

First, a larger source state can become a time problem before it becomes a peak-power problem. Area-driven power rises 25 percent while source energy doubles.

Second, source depth becomes a public measurement obligation. A surface outline cannot establish internal inventory, void, collapse or terminal location.

Third, the experiment needs a fifth root. Topology is neither flame, product nor energy and cannot be reconstructed honestly from those labels.

Fourth, field architectures must disclose measurement perturbation. Embedded sensors may improve observability while changing the event.

Fifth, boundary burden can rise much faster than field diameter. Total energy per equivalent boundary rises 78.9 percent.

Sixth, atmosphere and exhaust are time-varying systems. A single peak flow cannot establish a long event with changing products, density, deposits and bypass.

Seventh, deliberate intermediate states are more informative than a straight scale jump. The 250-kilogram area expansion and 400-kilogram areal-load state isolate different mechanisms.

Eighth, module return must include material exposure, not only recalibration. Liners, penetrants, cells, deposits and absorbed source can change the future event.

Ninth, mission-led technology is part of the scientific constitution. Deep-inventory tomography, adaptive exhaust, reversible refractory fields and high-temperature robotics are required to know the event at all.
