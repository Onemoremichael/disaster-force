## Disposition before design

The deepest live chain is `NIFS-1 → MSPT-1 → HCC-1 → PHCS-1 → MSPT-1R`, entering its fifth pass since national force sizing returned the portfolio question. This pass must therefore close or return it.

**Resolution test:** reversing the development sequence would change which of the six mission systems can exist together, when industry may reproduce them, whether the USD 1.00T program buys complete public service or disconnected machinery, and when any of the 5.25k formation equivalents can earn force credit.

It passes. Sequencing is a force-design decision, not project management.

## Decision: one coupled wavefront, not six queues

The Department will develop all six mission systems through one coupled wavefront. “Balanced” means equal constitutional survival, evidence access and obligation to complete the joint mission—not equal dollars, identical maturity dates or permission to hide weakness inside a portfolio score.

Five alternatives were tested by hard gate. Demand-ranked serial development starves low-volume enabling systems. Physics-first serial development delays the people-facing definition of success. Six independent parallel lanes preserve budgets but discover incompatibility too late. Campaign-specific bundles dissolve stable owners and buy the same interfaces repeatedly. Only the coupled wavefront keeps all six systems alive in every phase, retains rival roots, shares public interfaces, uses independent evidence authority and requires an all-six mission thread before scale.

The selected law is:

1. Every phase funds `MS-ET` theater entry, `MS-CT` occupied-terrain reach, `MS-HC` hazard control, `MS-LF` lifeline production, `MS-HU` human continuity and `MS-RT` sustainment and regeneration.
2. Each system keeps its Pass 168 allocation and acquisition owner. Failure money may move among rival roots inside that system or return to the protected reserve; it does not become a reward for the leading portfolio.
3. A component or system may close `G0–G3` independently. No system enters `G5` production readiness until every system can supply at least one configuration-controlled root to a common `G4` mission thread.
4. A `G4` judgment is focal, not solitary: one system is judged while the other five are fixed dependency conditions in the same campaign. Failure of a dependency denies the focal claim rather than becoming an exclusion.
5. `G6` force credit remains system- and configuration-specific. A successful joint stack does not grant blanket readiness to sibling roots, fleets or campaigns.
6. `G7` renewal must preserve a technically unlike challenger; incumbency is not evidence.

Each receives the same phase shares of 20, 30, 25, 15 and 10 percent. Balance is therefore a rule against starvation and premature victory, not an accounting symmetry.

## The joint demonstrator is the unit of truth

The twenty-four Pass 168 technology programs remain parent problem fields; the twelve `MS-HU` competitions refine four of them without creating new money. Wave 1 opens at least four unlike mechanisms per parent field: ninety-six root opportunities. Wave 2 must retain at least two independently reproducible mechanisms per field and assemble four complete, unlike system roots for each of the six systems.

Wave 3 combines those roots into four rotating all-six stacks. Each stack crosses all ten inherited campaign archetypes at three independent sites. The minimum registered estate is therefore 120 complete missions, 720 focal system judgments and 1,800 pair-interface observations. Root assignment rotates; a permanent “winning team” is forbidden because correlated suppliers, software or energy paths would masquerade as six-system evidence. Every mission includes cold arrival, service production, population acceptance, handoff, residual custody and regeneration. The recent `HCDN-50` campaign supplies one human-continuity root family, not permission to omit the other five systems.

The sovereign technical estate owns validity and configuration lineage. Effect ledgers own public acceptance. Mission offices own outcome requirements. Services own professional and article-family release. Manufacturing authorities own reproducibility. None may waive another authority's failure.

## What closes

`MSDW-1` closes the returned sequencing question. The Department should not choose a first service to finish and then repeat the process five times. It should mature six systems concurrently through shared evidence waves, while staggering only local technical decisions. The all-six demonstrator—not the vehicle, factory, program office or effect score—is the first object permitted to make a Department-level maturity claim.

The companion workbook makes the five candidate sequences, five fiscal waves, six system allocations, twenty-four parent fields, eight interlocked gates, 120-mission demonstrator estate and non-additive account reconciliation auditable. It creates no procurement, site, readiness or exterior-form authority.
