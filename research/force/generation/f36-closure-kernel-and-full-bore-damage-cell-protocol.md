# F36 closure-kernel and full-bore damage-cell protocol

## Executive judgment

The first F36 hardware cannot be admitted by testing a generic joint or by staging an impressive repair demonstration. Each technical root must expose the complete mechanism that turns raw boundary edges into a known, inspectable and potable pressure boundary, then restore that boundary after a surprise full-bore injury without borrowing decisive capability from the range.

This protocol defines two coupled experimental objects:

1. the **closure kernel**, which includes preparation, alignment, formation, process control, inspection, reject and rework logic, serialization, power, tools, operators and locally retained evidence; and
2. the **full-bore damage cell**, which applies a registered injury to an operating `F36-350` article and conserves every minute, material, machine, sample, decision and custody transition from injury onset through receiver release and forensic opening.

The protocol preserves the three unlike roots established in Passes 100 and 101:

- `R1`, factory-formed sectional conduit with frequent machine-executed closures;
- `R2`, mobile continuous formation with sparse end, restart, transition and repair closures; and
- `R3`, a protected fold-flat structural-ribbon challenge with bonded seams, crease state and multilayer repair.

Comparability does not require the same joint. It requires the same public service, full-bore pressure and transient worlds, injury states, independent clocks, water-release logic, evidence custody and decision grammar. Root-specific fixtures remain article-owned whenever they supply restraint, alignment, thermal control, surface preparation, curing, inspection or recovery that the deployed system would need.

The Pass 101 bank provides twenty-four closure articles per root and twenty-six destructive runs: eight each for `R1` and `R2`, and ten for `R3`. This is enough to challenge defined mechanisms and to reject unsafe or unobservable processes. It is not enough to claim production reliability. Even zero failures in twenty-four independent closure decisions produces only an 88.3 percent one-sided 95 percent lower confidence bound on success probability. A later frozen-design reliability lot requires at least fifty-nine consecutive successes to support a 95 percent lower bound of 95 percent under the same simplifying Bernoulli assumption.

The closure campaign uses twelve declared process-window articles, four known-defect calibration articles, four blind physical-defect articles, two blind configuration-defect articles and two clean blind controls per root. The destructive campaign uses six disclosed injury families and two sealed combinations for each build root. `R3` adds one crease-origin injury and one contaminated bonded-repair repeat. No critical blind defect may be falsely accepted. No water release may occur from a pressure-only result.

The six-hour repair clock contains eight conserved segments totaling 360 minutes. It begins when the registered injury first becomes independently measurable, not when the builder acknowledges an alarm:

| Segment | Maximum minutes | End condition |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| independent detection | 10 | independent event identity and clock record exist |
| safe stop | 10 | energy and personnel state are controlled |
| isolation and buffer transition | 20 | injured domain is isolated and receiver continuity is recorded |
| access | 40 | the repair boundary is physically available to the article team |
| removal and preparation | 65 | rejected material is removed and accepted edges are prepared |
| closure | 95 | repair pressure boundary and inspection decision exist |
| proof and flush | 75 | sixty-minute proof and registered flush are complete |
| assay and receiver release | 45 | sanitary, configuration and receiver authorities jointly release service |

The clock never pauses for safety, weather, unavailable staff, laboratory queues, software recovery or inconclusive data. A safe stop can remain correct while the service claim fails.

The protocol creates a thirty-month experimental phase inside the forty-two-month Pass 101 article program. Its USD 1.480B capital cross-classification and USD 0.192B annual operating boundary remain inside the Pass 101 USD 8.120B capital and USD 0.446B annual support lines. They fund public full-bore fixtures, two independently resettable damage cells, root-neutral hydraulic and power plant, rapid sanitary-assay development, independent metrology, blind-defect manufacture, robotic access and forensic sectioning. They are research allocations, not vendor estimates.

Twelve protocol-content gates close in planning. Fixture construction remains held for constituted safety, water, receiver and configuration approval. Closure acceptance, damage-cell admission, sanitary release, internal requirements sections, production and exterior rendering remain open or blocked. No physical credit is created by this paper or its workbook.

## 1. The decision this protocol owns

Pass 101 fixed the article bank and common service envelope. It did not specify the executable experiment that would distinguish a sound closure system from a rehearsed craft demonstration, or a genuine six-hour restoration system from a fast mechanical patch followed by uncounted laboratory and release time.

This protocol owns five decisions:

1. whether a root has exposed a complete closure kernel rather than a favorable specimen;
2. whether public fixtures measure the root without supplying hidden deployed capability;
3. whether inspection distinguishes known-good, known-bad and blinded configuration states;
4. whether the article restores the full hydraulic, sanitary and configuration boundary inside the conserved clock; and
5. what measured internal geometry may enter the next requirements diagram.

It does not accept a production design, set a national engineering code, approve construction, or authorize an exterior depiction.

## 2. The closure kernel as an industrial unit

A closure kernel is the smallest deployable system that can repeatedly create and disposition an `F36-350` pressure-boundary transition. It contains all capability required to execute these functions:

1. identify the two boundary states and their configuration ancestry;
2. reject incompatible or damaged material before preparation;
3. clean, cut, condition and geometrically prepare the edges;
4. align and restrain the full-bore boundary under the registered field state;
5. apply the root-specific formation process;
6. measure process variables at their native time resolution;
7. inspect the completed boundary with a method independent of the formation controller;
8. issue accept, reject or indeterminate disposition against a frozen rule;
9. remove and remake a rejected boundary without losing custody;
10. serialize the closure, tools, software, recipe, operators and consumables;
11. export locally complete original evidence during network denial; and
12. return the equipment, waste and unused material to a known state.

If the test range provides alignment, restraint, heating, curing, cleaning, inspection or software that would be required in the field, that provision is either charged to the article or the run is invalid for deployability. The range may provide safety containment, calibrated stimulus, independent measurement and blind-test custody.

### 2.1 Root-specific normalization

The three roots enter the same decision through different physical mechanisms.

| Root | Credited closure episodes | Primary controllable factor | Secondary factor | Root-specific latent state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `R1` | section-to-section, adapter and repair closure | formation energy, force or engagement | temperature and surface preparation | alignment, circumference completion and closure-element state |
| `R2` | startup, shutdown, feedstock splice, receiver transition and repair closure | line speed, compaction or fusion state | feedstock, cooling and ambient condition | continuous-wall history and transition-zone state |
| `R3` | longitudinal or circumferential bond, end transition and bonded repair | activation, pressure and cure state | fold age, temperature and surface condition | crease, interlayer bond and barrier continuity |

Each root must register low, nominal and high values in dimensioned native units. The shared labels do not permit dimensionless reporting. They only allow the campaign to occupy comparable process-window positions.

### 2.2 Decision outputs

Every closure article yields a three-way disposition:

- `ACCEPT`, when process, geometry, nondestructive inspection, pressure, sanitary and configuration prerequisites all close;
- `REJECT`, when the frozen rule identifies a disqualifying state; or
- `INDETERMINATE`, when evidence is missing, contradictory, outside uncertainty or corrupted.

`INDETERMINATE` is not a weak pass. It blocks service credit and enters the forensic queue. Builder reclassification after unblinding is prohibited.

## 4. Public fixture constitution

The public fixture applies common stimulus and captures independent evidence. It is not permitted to improve a root's deployed closure.

### 4.1 Closure fixture

The closure fixture contains:

- replaceable full-bore source and receiver manifolds outside the active boundary;
- an article-owned central support and alignment zone;
- public axial load towers with at least 300 kN controlled capacity;
- a hydraulic loop capable of −0.10 to 1.40 MPa characterization;
- transient accumulators and valve modules with independently measured command and response;
- a contained external leak-capture jacket;
- independent geometry, pressure, flow, load, thermal and acoustic references;
- isolated article and evidence power domains;
- two local original-data repositories; and
- remotely actuated shields, drain-down and emergency isolation.

Root adapters may change interface geometry outside the active decision zone. They may not reduce the active load, bypass a closure, hide leakage, condition a root-specific sensor signal or add restraint that the field article does not carry.

### 4.2 Damage cell

Each 120 m damage cell contains:

- independently isolated source and receiver ends;
- a replaceable central injury cassette with no hidden structural bypass;
- an external contamination annulus and quarantined capture system;
- controlled soil, standing-water, debris and unsupported-span modules;
- a full-bore hydraulic loop and receiver simulant;
- article-owned access, removal, preparation, repair, flush and release equipment;
- public injury actuators that control delivered geometry, load, displacement and timing;
- independent metrology, time authority and water-sample custody;
- robotic observation and protected human access; and
- physical segregation between clean water, challenge water and waste.

Two cells are required so a failed, contaminated or forensically held run cannot monopolize the program. Parallel cells do not permit overlapping a single article team's conserved clock.

### 4.3 Fixture neutrality qualification

Before a root enters either fixture, the public authority runs:

1. a compliant reference article;
2. a known-leak reference;
3. a known-restraint reference;
4. a geometry reference;
5. a timing and data-denial reference; and
6. a contamination blank and positive-control recovery.

Fixture contribution must remain below 20 percent of the allowable decision uncertainty for every credited quantity. Any root-specific adapter is requalified after configuration change. The fixture must identify the known-bad references and must not reject the compliant reference. Otherwise all dependent runs remain unadmitted.

## 5. Hydraulic and structural waveform

Every closure article receives a registered sequence unless its assigned destructive truth requires earlier opening.

| Phase | Command | Duration or count | Decision purpose |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| `W00` | filled, vented and stabilized at 0.05 MPa | 10 min | establish thermal, geometry and leak baselines |
| `W01` | ramp to 0.70 MPa | 15 min | measure elastic response and process discontinuity |
| `W02` | hold at 0.70 MPa | 60 min | working-pressure leakage and drift |
| `W03` | ramp to 1.05 MPa and hold | 75 min including 60-min hold | full-bore proof |
| `W04` | cycle 0.05–0.70 MPa | 50,000 cycles at registered 0.10–0.20 Hz | fatigue and inspection drift |
| `W05` | slow surge 0.70–0.85 MPa | 25 pulses | pump and control transient |
| `W06` | fast surge 0.70–0.85 MPa | 25 pulses | valve and local restraint transient |
| `W07` | oscillatory packet 0.55–0.85 MPa | 100 pulses | resonance and closure response |
| `W08` | depressurize to −0.085 MPa | 30 min | collapse, seal and contamination-ingress state |
| `W09` | return to 0.70 MPa | 30 min | post-vacuum leak and geometry decision |
| `W10` | repeat 1.05 MPa proof | 75 min including 60-min hold | final nondestructive acceptance |
| `W11` | controlled ramp toward 1.40 MPa or first instability | destructive subset only | characterize margin without creating an acceptance threshold |

The exact rise time, dwell and waveform tolerance become configuration-controlled commands after fixture commissioning. Commanded pressure is never substituted for measured article pressure. Any excursion outside the registered band is an invalid stimulus unless the independent authority accepts it as a more severe bounded event before unblinding.

The prospective leakage proposition is no visible wetting and no captured pressure-boundary loss above the registered 0.10 L/h cell decision floor during static holds. That value is a research target subject to metrology review, not a public code.

## 6. Full-bore injury matrix

Each run starts with the cell operating at registered service pressure and flow. The damage authority controls truth; the operator receives only information that the field detection system produces.

| Code | Injury state | Controlled physical proposition | Primary question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `D01` | clean guillotine transection | complete boundary separation in the injury cassette | can the root detect, isolate, remove and restore a clean catastrophic break? |
| `D02` | jagged crush and ovalization | local area collapse plus torn, nonplanar edges | can preparation recover an acceptable boundary without range machining? |
| `D03` | puncture with axial tear | registered 20 kN puncture followed by controlled axial propagation | can the system arrest, remove and repair a propagating injury? |
| `D04` | closure pullout | axial load through a credited closure to loss or 200 kN | can the system recognize root cause and prevent reuse of an unsafe closure? |
| `D05` | contaminated breach | external surrogate annulus under inward hydraulic gradient | can the system recover potable custody, not merely pressure? |
| `D06` | pressure-bound settlement | 300 mm imposed displacement over 10 m at 0.70 MPa | can the root handle coupled ground and pressure damage? |
| `D07` | sealed combination A | two or more registered mechanisms selected after custody | surprise integration and anti-rehearsal challenge |
| `D08` | sealed combination B | two or more registered mechanisms selected after custody | independent repeat under a different combination |

The injury actuator must report delivered force, energy, displacement, geometry and time. Tool identity alone does not define an injury. A run is invalid if a structural bypass, prepositioned replacement, undisclosed access opening or range-owned repair capability changes the service problem.

## 7. Contamination-surrogate protocol

Live pathogens are unnecessary for the first campaign. The external annulus uses a registered, nonpathogenic challenge representing four transport modes:

- a dissolved conservative tracer for rapid ingress and flush accounting;
- inert fluorescent particles near the registered microbial-proxy scale;
- fine mineral turbidity for soil and debris transport; and
- a food-contact-compatible organic-film surrogate for surface retention.

The independent water team prepares the challenge after article custody. Concentration, volume, temperature, external head and contact time are sealed from the operator. Blank and positive-control samples travel through the same containers, instruments and clocks.

The breach creates an inward gradient during pressure loss. Repair acceptance requires accounting for source water, challenge inventory, captured waste, flush volume and receiver sample. Dilution without a registered mass balance cannot establish cleaning.

## 8. Conserved six-hour repair ledger

The 360-minute clock starts at the first independently measurable departure caused by injury. The independent time authority writes every boundary from synchronized raw events; operator declarations are evidence but do not own time.

| Segment | Budget | Starts | Ends | Prohibited exclusion |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| `T01` detection | 10 min | measurable injury onset | independent alarm and event identity | algorithm warmup or human acknowledgement delay |
| `T02` safe stop | 10 min | alarm | hazardous energy and personnel state controlled | emergency deliberation |
| `T03` isolation/buffer | 20 min | safe-stop decision | injured domain isolated and receiver continuity confirmed | valve travel or control reboot |
| `T04` access | 40 min | isolation | repair boundary physically reachable | dewatering, excavation or robot deployment |
| `T05` removal/preparation | 65 min | access | accepted repair edges and configuration exist | cutting, cleaning, measurement or rejected attempts |
| `T06` closure | 95 min | accepted edges | completed repair and independent NDE disposition | tool setup, cure, inspection or rework |
| `T07` proof/flush | 75 min | closure disposition | sixty-minute proof plus registered flush complete | pressure ramp, hold, drain or flush volume |
| `T08` assay/release | 45 min | flush complete | water, configuration and receiver release signed | sample transit, assay queue or authority deliberation |

The segment budgets are acquisition targets, not excuses to stop a run at a local overrun. Testing continues safely to reveal the complete restoration time. Total success requires every segment and the 360-minute boundary. Unused time in one segment may offset another only if the authority records the transfer before unblinding; no segment may have a negative duration or missing boundary.

## 9. Rapid sanitary release

The six-hour target forces a new field water-release capability. The program must develop an independent assay package rather than assuming a central laboratory can return a decision on demand.

The release sequence is conjunctive:

1. record pre-injury source, receiver and challenge blanks;
2. close pressure-boundary proof for sixty continuous minutes;
3. flush at least three measured cell volumes or the larger root-declared validated volume;
4. collect upstream, repair-zone and receiver samples under sealed identity;
5. run continuous conductivity, turbidity, organic-response and particle channels;
6. run challenge-specific dissolved-tracer and particle assays;
7. reconcile blank, positive control, challenge inventory and waste capture;
8. obtain two successive conforming repair-zone decisions separated by ten minutes;
9. close material, cleaning, configuration and assay records; and
10. obtain joint Water Release, Configuration and Receiver Authority signatures.

The prospective decision is no challenge analyte above its registered quantification limit, no statistically distinguishable adverse shift from the paired upstream baseline after accounting for instrument uncertainty, and complete positive-control recovery. An unavailable assay is `INDETERMINATE`, not clean water.

The forty-five-minute release allocation is intentionally aggressive. It creates technology programs in portable high-throughput optical counting, rapid dissolved-tracer analysis, automated chain of custody, locally executable uncertainty evaluation and receiver-authority decision support.

## 12. Statistical acceptance and claim limits

The protocol uses statistics to bound claims, not to decorate a small sample.

### 12.1 Closure gate

A root closes the closure gate only when:

- every critical defect above the registered detection limit is rejected;
- neither clean blind control is falsely rejected after the complete decision chain;
- all twelve declared process-window articles meet their frozen article criteria;
- all required evidence objects, clocks and configurations are complete;
- no pressure, structural or sanitary critical failure occurs; and
- the independent evidence board reproduces the disposition from retained originals.

The twenty-four results are reported by condition and truth class. They may support process-window entry, but they do not support a production reliability claim.

For zero observed failures in `n` independent trials, the one-sided 95 percent lower confidence bound on success probability is `0.05^(1/n)`. The resulting bounds are:

| Zero-failure sample | Lower 95% bound |
| ---: | ---: |
| 8 | 68.8% |
| 10 | 74.1% |
| 24 | 88.3% |
| 59 | 95.0% |

The formula assumes independent, identically distributed Bernoulli trials. Process-window and damage campaigns deliberately violate identical-distribution assumptions to cover unlike mechanisms, so these values are disclosure screens rather than final reliability estimates.

### 12.2 Damage-cell gate

Every disclosed injury and sealed combination must independently close the hydraulic, service, sanitary, configuration and evidence decisions. A root fails campaign admission if any critical injury:

- escapes independent detection;
- exceeds the 360-minute total;
- returns service before the required proof and flush;
- releases challenge material to the receiver;
- uses unregistered decisive range support;
- lacks a reproducible configuration; or
- produces an accepted disposition contradicted by teardown truth.

A failed run may generate valuable engineering evidence. It creates no mission credit and cannot be averaged away by faster runs.

## 13. Forensic teardown and internal sections

Every destructive or indeterminate article enters controlled teardown. The minimum measured package contains:

- whole-article pre- and post-test geometry;
- the complete repair volume plus at least one metre upstream and downstream;
- eight registered transverse section planes through damage, repair, transition and controls;
- one longitudinal section through the dominant defect or load path;
- layer, bond, fracture, contamination and cleaning maps at registered resolution;
- correlation between independent nondestructive signals and opened truth;
- residual material, witness, waste and sample custody; and
- an as-tested internal clearance and repair-access envelope.

Section selection is fixed before opening, with two additional planes chosen after initial non-destructive review but before truth is exposed. This prevents the builder from presenting only favorable micrographs.

Accepted measurements may support an **internal requirements section** showing functional boundary, layer categories, closure zone, load path, inspection access, repair clearance, fluid path and uncertainty. It must not imply a selected exterior shape or vendor architecture. An internal section remains blocked until at least one closure root and one full-bore damage run have accepted, independently reproducible evidence. Exterior rendering remains blocked through the full F36 mission and production/form decision.

## 17. Technology programs created by the protocol

### Self-measuring closure factories

The closure kernel requires a machine that carries its own metrological boundary into mud, heat, rain, lost-network and degraded-power conditions. Formation and independent inspection must be physically and computationally separated while remaining fast enough for corridor production.

### Programmable full-bore injury ranges

The damage cell is a civilian equivalent of a survivability range. It must deliver repeatable rupture geometry, soil state, contamination gradient, axial load and transient pressure while protecting people and preserving the damaged truth for later opening.

### Forty-five-minute sanitary decision systems

The water-release allocation demands portable assays that combine fast optical, chemical and particle measurements with automated blanks, controls, uncertainty and custody. The innovation target is a defensible release decision at the receiver, not a faster unqualified sensor.

### Robotic contaminated-boundary repair

The article must expose, remove, prepare and remake a full-bore boundary without putting people into an uncontrolled pressure, excavation or contamination state. Different roots may use different machines, but access and safe state remain part of the deployable system.

### Nondestructive truth correlation

Every accepted classifier must be correlated against opened full-bore sections. The program needs inspection modalities and interpretable classifiers that distinguish safe, reject and indeterminate states across different materials rather than hiding uncertainty inside a vendor score.

### Cryptographically conserved experimental custody

Sealed truth, local original retention, denied-network operation, configuration ancestry and delayed unblinding require an evidence system designed as part of the machine. This capability transfers across water, power, medical, fire and civil-engineering force families.

## 18. What changed and what comes next

### Now authoritative

The F36 closure question is governed by a complete deployable production-and-acceptance kernel. The destructive question is governed by a 120 m full-bore cell, common injury effects, two independently resettable ranges, a nonpausing 360-minute ledger, rapid sanitary release, blinded truth and controlled teardown.

### Falsified

Five shortcuts no longer survive:

- a joint coupon can qualify a field closure system;
- a common fixture may supply root-specific deployed capability;
- a mechanical patch completes a potable repair;
- twenty-four successes can establish production reliability; or
- teardown photographs without preregistered section planes constitute forensic evidence.

### Still provisional

All factor values, fixture capacities, waveform tolerances, leakage floors, surrogate properties, timing budgets, article counts and cost allocations are prospective research requirements. They require constituted engineering, safety, sanitary, metrology, receiver and fiscal approval before construction. No specimen has been built or tested.

### Burden created by success

This protocol turns the next bottleneck into physical public infrastructure: full-bore pressure loops, programmable injury cells, portable water-release laboratories, independent data planes, blind-defect shops, forensic sectioning facilities and teams trained to preserve both service and truth.
