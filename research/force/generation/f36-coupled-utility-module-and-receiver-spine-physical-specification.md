# F36 coupled utility module and receiver-spine physical specification

## Executive judgment

Pass 109 defined `MQP-1`, the physical qualification constitution required before the first no-notice campus-reconstruction event. It established the article bank, interface families, receiver worlds, cold-state burden and release sequence. It intentionally left every dimension, load, flow, voltage and measurement plane open.

This pass defines the first coupled physical slice, `CISA-1`: the Coupled Interface and Spine Article. It is not a replacement campus and it is not a vehicle design. It is the smallest full-scale article set that forces structure, lifting, weak-ground support, hydraulic service, sanitary separation, island power, black start, containment, local evidence and reset to work together.

The common reference article, `CISA-40`, has a provisional 12.0 m × 3.6 m transport envelope, a 32.0-tonne dry mass ceiling and a 40.0-tonne operating mass ceiling. Its public receiver spine uses eight independently observable support zones with at least 1.6 m² effective bearing area each and 400 mm of total leveling range. The common interface must tolerate a 1.5-g vertical challenge, a 0.6-g horizontal challenge at a center of gravity no higher than 2.2 m, one unavailable support zone, 300 mm of imposed receiver differential over 10 m and root-native lifting or self-transfer without a receiver-owned crane.

The hydraulic article carries two independent `F36-350` service paths. Each preserves at least 0.0962 m² flow area and 57.87 L/s half-demand flow. The full pair delivers 10,000 m³/day. The inherited physical states remain 0.70 MPa working pressure, 0.85 MPa registered surge, 1.05 MPa hydrostatic proof for sixty minutes, −0.085 MPa vacuum for thirty minutes, 50,000 pressure cycles and 200 kN axial restraint per path. Using the exact circular area, a 24 m paired test loop contains 4.6181 m³; the minimum three-volume sanitary flush is therefore 13.8544 m³ unless a root validates a larger requirement.

The public power boundary is a provisional 1.5 MW continuous grid-forming service, 2.25 MW for ten minutes and 3.0 MW for ten seconds. A separately protected 0.6 MW critical bus must remain available for controls, isolation, metrology, sanitary custody and safe access. At least 0.4 MWh must be deliverable from locally controlled stored energy, providing forty minutes at the critical load before generator contribution. From a dead state with no national network or external grid, local control must become authoritative within thirty seconds, the critical bus within ninety seconds, hydraulic isolation and measurement within five minutes and the full 1.5 MW service within twenty minutes.

These values are deliberately ambitious. They create public technology programs in self-surveying load grids, receiver-independent heavy handling, contamination-incompatible fluid collars, grid-forming mobile power, portable high-rate sanitary assay, two-original measurement and robotic reset. They are not claims that current products meet the requirement.

`CISA-1` draws fifty of the ninety-four `MQP-1` decision trials and fifty-eight of its 130 controlled units into one coupled program:

- thirty-six interface-pair articles covering `IF01`, `IF02`, `IF03`, `IF04`, `IF06` and `IF10` across two roots and three serial states;
- four utility-island assemblies;
- six receiver spines across the three receiver worlds and both roots;
- four integrated campus slices; and
- eight structural, hydraulic, sanitary and electrical reference, truth and quarantine units.

The remaining seventy-two units and forty-four trials are not waived. Thermal and ventilation, data and signing, full metrology-interface qualification and human-maintainability qualification remain later `MQP-1` obligations. Passing `CISA-1` cannot by itself authorize `CRC-E01`.

The subprogram runs for thirty-six months inside the forty-eight-month `MQP-1` boundary. It cross-classifies USD 0.960B of capital and USD 0.260B/year inside Pass 109, leaving USD 2.040B of `MQP-1` capital and USD 0.460B/year for the remaining interface, service-cell and integration work. No operational shipset, alert roster or receiver-owned completing asset receives credit.

Planning can authorize the physical specification and article allocation. It cannot claim that a root has built an article, that a receiver spine carries load, that water is sanitary, that power black-starts, that evidence survives denial, that reset works, that `CRC-E01` may begin or that an exterior form is known.

## 1. What `CISA-1` is designed to learn

The first coupled article must answer six questions that cannot be resolved by separate coupons.

1. Can a full-mass service article transfer gravity, lateral, restraint and settlement loads into a damaged receiver without a bespoke civil project?
2. Can it be lifted, transferred, aligned and removed with article-owned equipment when one planned handling point or receiver aid is unavailable?
3. Can two independent high-flow potable paths be joined, isolated, challenged, cleaned and released without mixing product, test water, residuals or waste?
4. Can an island power system black-start and preserve protection, grounding, measurement and safe isolation while hydraulic and structural loads change?
5. Can independent instruments distinguish article failure, receiver failure, fixture failure, wrong configuration and safe protective action?
6. Can the complete article be disassembled, decontaminated, inspected, repaired, requalified and returned to known custody after storage and transport?

A test that answers only one of these questions remains useful subsystem work. It does not admit the coupled article.

### 1.1 The limit of the article

`CISA-1` is a full-scale interface and common-utility slice. It does not reproduce the ten-kilometre F36 corridor, the 10,000 m³ receiver buffer, a complete campus, a 168-hour operational endurance mission or the complete 130-unit `MQP-1` bank. The 24 m hydraulic loop emulates the source, line, receiver, isolation, sample and waste boundaries at full diameter, pressure and flow. The load article reproduces the governing mass, center of gravity and receiver reactions. The power island reproduces the public service boundary and black-start sequence.

This distinction prevents scale confusion. Full diameter can qualify local pressure, flow, sanitary and connection mechanisms. It cannot establish ten-kilometre placement rate or corridor failure independence. Full module mass can qualify a receiver spine and handling system. It cannot establish a complete shipset transport campaign. A successful eight-hour power run can qualify transient and protection behavior. It cannot establish 168-hour logistics endurance.

## 3. Public interface and root-native freedom

The public specification fixes what the receiver and admission authority must be able to observe. It does not prescribe one hidden design.

### 3.1 Common public layer

Both roots must share:

- the mass, center-of-gravity and transport-state declarations;
- the support-zone coordinates, allowable receiver reactions and survey datums;
- the required hydraulic area, flow, pressure, isolation and sanitary states;
- the public power service, protection, grounding and black-start outcomes;
- the measurement-plane coordinates, units, time relation and uncertainty limits;
- the article, tool, material, software, sample, waste and evidence identities;
- the receiver-world stimuli and fault families;
- the access, exclusion, containment, safe-stop and terminal-state grammar; and
- complete public rights to reproduce, challenge and reset the article.

### 3.2 Root-native layer

The roots should differ in the mechanisms whose common failure would defeat the force. They may use unlike:

- primary structural materials and frame topology;
- support, leveling, anchoring and settlement-accommodation mechanisms;
- lift, jack, roll, crawl or gantry handling systems;
- conduit material, wall system, closure and sanitary barrier;
- valve actuation, isolation proof and dead-leg elimination;
- generation, storage, conversion and cooling architecture;
- grounding, protection and fault-current strategy;
- embedded control, firmware, signing and local operator interface;
- production processes, machines, tools, suppliers and qualified workforces; and
- inspection, repair, decontamination and reset sequences.

A root may exceed a common service requirement through a different mechanism. It may not require the other root's unique adapter, loader, key, converter, assay or software service.

### 3.3 Two provisional root directions

The program should fund competing physical directions rather than clone one design.

`ROOT_A` may pursue a metallic truss and kinematic support frame, sectional `F36-350` conduit, electrochemical storage plus liquid-fuel generation, bolted or pinned replaceable collars and a self-contained strand-jack handling portal.

`ROOT_B` may pursue a hybrid composite and tension-frame structure, continuously formed or structural-ribbon conduit, rotating machinery plus a different storage chemistry, bonded or clamped sanitary collars and distributed synchronized lifting crawlers.

These are competition hypotheses, not selections. The root test is whether removal of one material, machine, control implementation, key ancestry, supplier domain or workforce still leaves the other root executable.

## 6. Structural load constitution

Using standard gravity `g = 9.80665 m/s²`, the 40-tonne operating article weighs 392.266 kN. The load cases are public stimuli rather than a structural code.

| Case | State | Applied demand | Derived article demand |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| `LC01` | level static service | 1.00 g vertical at 40 t | 392.266 kN |
| `LC02` | operating proof | 1.25 g vertical at 40 t | 490.333 kN |
| `LC03` | vertical challenge | 1.50 g vertical at 40 t | 588.399 kN |
| `LC04` | lateral receiver challenge | 0.60 g horizontal at 40 t | 235.360 kN |
| `LC05` | overturning challenge | `LC04` at 2.2 m | 517.791 kN·m |
| `LC06` | routine dry lift | 1.30 dynamic factor at 32 t | 407.957 kN total |
| `LC07` | dry transport longitudinal | 1.00 g at 32 t | 313.813 kN |
| `LC08` | dry transport lateral | 0.50 g at 32 t | 156.906 kN |
| `LC09` | dry transport vertical | 0.30 g at 32 t | 94.144 kN |

The load fixture applies gravity, lateral reaction, twist and settlement through independent paths. Root-embedded load cells may aid operation but cannot own the admission result.

### 6.1 One-support-out requirement

The support system must remain safe and observable with one of eight support zones unavailable. At `LC02`, a 1.35 reaction-imbalance factor across seven remaining 1.6 m² zones produces 59.10 kPa. At the 1.50-g challenge it produces 70.92 kPa. Both remain below the provisional 75 kPa weak-ground receiver limit, but the latter retains only 5.4 percent analytical margin. This narrow result makes pad area, soil improvement, load redistribution and measured reaction a governing experiment.

No root may claim compliance from total pad area alone. Every zone must report reaction, contact state, settlement and load redistribution. A hard point outside the registered zones is a fixture bypass.

### 6.2 Anchor and overturning envelope

At 0.60 g and a 2.2 m center-of-gravity height, the public lateral demand is 235.360 kN and overturning demand is 517.791 kN·m. With a 3.0 m transverse anchor lever arm, two anchors on the resisting side and a 1.5 load-imbalance factor, the representative tension demand is approximately 129.45 kN per anchor. Applying the same imbalance to four-way direct shear gives 88.26 kN per anchor. The combined representative demand is approximately 156.7 kN.

Each registered anchor path therefore receives a provisional 200 kN combined tension-shear proof. A root may use more anchors, passive mass, ground screws, deadmen, external guys or active restraint. It receives credit only if every decisive item arrives in its manifest and the receiver world permits its installation and removal.

### 6.3 Deformation and terminal rules

The article must maintain:

- no loss of hydraulic area below 95 percent of reference through structural distortion;
- support-zone displacement known to ±1 mm expanded uncertainty;
- interface-datum displacement known to ±2 mm;
- pose known to ±0.05° during structural trials;
- no unregistered load path or permanent interference with access and isolation;
- safe isolation after any structural limit or sensor invalidity; and
- a declared reusable, repairable, quarantined or destroyed terminal state.

Permanent deformation is not automatically failure if the root declares a one-use sacrificial mechanism and the article remains safe. Hidden permanent set presented as elastic recovery is failure.

## 7. Lift, handling and transport

The receiver may provide controlled ground and exclusion authority. It may not provide the only crane, spreader, forklift, jack, prime mover, ballast system or operator required to place the article.

### 7.1 Lift nodes

Four primary lift nodes are each rated at 196 kN working load. The routine dry lift applies a 1.30 dynamic factor to 32 tonnes, producing 407.957 kN total or 101.99 kN per node under equal sharing. The challenge applies a 1.40 local imbalance, raising the most-loaded representative node to 142.78 kN. That leaves approximately 27 percent below the 196 kN working-load limit.

Qualification includes:

- balanced four-node lift;
- measured center-of-gravity offset in both horizontal axes;
- one denied primary node with an accepted alternate plan;
- controlled loss of synchronization without uncontrolled tilt;
- set-down on a receiver with 150 mm initial elevation mismatch;
- transfer from transport support to receiver support without an unobserved load path; and
- recovery to transport state after wet and contaminated operation.

The node rating does not make the lift system safe. Slings, beams, jacks, portals, crawlers, control, exclusion, energy and emergency lowering are part of the qualified handling transaction.

### 7.2 Transport restraint

The dry article must expose independently measurable restraint paths for the 1.00-g longitudinal, 0.50-g lateral and 0.30-g vertical transport cases. Root-native packaging may use a cassette, cradle or integrated support frame. A restraint removed for operation must receive an identity and stowage state; loose untracked hardware is an incomplete reset.

The `S2` article receives a registered vibration, shock, salt, dust, thermal and handling exposure before the receiver assignment is revealed. Transport survival means the subsequent article passes prestate, lift, connection, service and reset. An undetected transport defect is not credited because the article later survives a reduced trial.

## 8. Receiver-spine specification

The receiver spine is public test infrastructure and a future force asset. It converts unknown ground and damaged approaches into a bounded civil interface without completing the supplier's article.

### 8.1 Common spine functions

Every spine contains:

- a surveyed reference grid independent of the root article;
- eight replaceable support and reaction zones;
- anchor test positions and a bypass-detection load path;
- adjustable elevation and slope control;
- segregated hydraulic source, receiver, flush and waste approaches;
- a medium-voltage service point and protected low-voltage critical bus;
- local time, identity, evidence and emergency-stop distribution;
- clean, challenge, waste and quarantine zones;
- article-owned access and handling corridors; and
- a restoration ledger returning the site to a declared condition.

The public spine must not contain a proprietary root adapter or the only control needed to start either root.

### 8.2 Three physical worlds

| World | Bearing boundary | Imposed geometry | External services | Governing question |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| `RW1` damaged hardstand | 300 kPa | ±25 mm local datum error | untrusted grid, network and drainage | can the article reject or correct a damaged prepared surface? |
| `RW2` weak-ground island | 75 kPa | 300 mm differential over 10 m plus one unavailable zone | none beyond controlled source and waste boundary | can support and utilities remain safe as the receiver changes? |
| `RW3` remote corridor | 150 kPa | 150 mm initial mismatch and long approach runs | no grid or national network | can the complete handling, utility and evidence estate deploy independently? |

Bearing limits are trial targets at the support-zone boundary, not geotechnical design values for real sites. Actual receivers require site-specific soil, seismic, flood, fire and environmental decisions.

### 8.3 Alignment and handoff

Before service connection, the spine must establish:

- plan position within ±10 mm of the public coordinate;
- interface elevation within ±5 mm after leveling;
- article slope within 0.5° unless a root declares a smaller limit;
- hydraulic connection axes within the root's public adapter envelope;
- electrical phase, voltage, frequency, grounding and fault-current identity;
- clean and contaminated access paths with no uncontrolled crossing; and
- a local custody boundary that remains complete through network loss.

Alignment is a measured state, not paint marks or operator judgment.

## 9. Hydraulic and sanitary interface

### 9.1 Full-scale paired loop

The test loop uses two independent 24 m paths at the full `F36-350` service class. Each path carries at least 0.0962 m² flow area and 57.87 L/s. At the nominal 0.35 m circular reference, mean velocity is 0.601 m/s. The pair provides 0.11574 m³/s or 10,000 m³/day.

Each path includes:

- independently powered source and receiver isolation;
- root-native closure and restraint;
- upstream, mid-article and downstream pressure planes;
- independently measured flow and accumulated volume;
- an exchangeable dead-leg and wrong-valve challenge cassette;
- a sanitary sampling manifold before and after the connection;
- a controlled external challenge annulus;
- segregated flush and waste custody; and
- a receiver emulator able to impose pressure and demand transients.

### 9.2 Pressure and restraint states

| State | Requirement | Duration or count | Decision |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| service | 0.70 MPa and 57.87 L/s per path | 8 h integrated run | stable service and bounded loss |
| surge | 0.85 MPa measured peak | registered transient packet | restraint, valve and control response |
| proof | 1.05 MPa | 60 continuous min | no unaccepted boundary loss |
| vacuum | −0.085 MPa | 30 continuous min | collapse, seal and ingress state |
| cycling | 0.05–0.70 MPa | 50,000 cycles | closure, fatigue and barrier condition |
| axial restraint | 200 kN per path | registered hold | pressure-end and structural load path |

At 1.05 MPa and the exact 0.35 m circular bore area, pressure end force is 101.022 kN per path. The 200 kN restraint requirement preserves approximately 98 percent analytical margin above that simple end-load reference and exposes forces added by closure, bending and receiver behavior.

### 9.3 Leakage decision

The inherited prospective cell decision floor is 0.10 L/h with 0.02 L/h expanded measurement uncertainty. Acceptance requires no visible wetting, no unexplained volume imbalance and a guard-banded result below the registered limit after temperature and fixture effects are accounted for. A pressure trace alone cannot establish leakage.

### 9.4 Sanitary separation

Product water, challenge fluid, flush water, residuals and waste must remain physically and evidentially distinct. The public requirements are:

- product and waste connections are geometrically or mechanically incompatible;
- valve position is independently observable at the boundary;
- a single wrong command cannot create an undetected cross-connection;
- trapped volume and dead-leg length are declared and verified;
- cleaning and decontamination inputs are identified by lot and quantity;
- source, repair-zone, receiver, blank and positive-control samples share sealed custody;
- challenge inventory, recovered waste and residual are mass-balanced; and
- release is `INDETERMINATE` when a required assay, blank, positive control or calibration bracket is unavailable.

Using the exact circular area, the two-path loop contains 4.6181 m³. A three-volume flush therefore requires at least 13.8544 m³. Roots may require more, but they cannot claim a smaller volume without a separate validated cleaning result.

Release requires no challenge analyte above its registered quantification limit, no guard-banded adverse shift from the upstream reference, acceptable positive-control recovery, complete waste accounting and joint sanitary, configuration and receiver signatures. No numeric drinking-water limit is invented in this pass; the constituted water authority must bind each analyte to the applicable public release law before construction.

The hydraulic pump demand inherited from F36 is only 67 kW installed. That does not size the utility island. Manufacture, joining, lifting, laboratory, control, thermal rejection, lighting, decontamination and future campus services create the larger power boundary.

The public service point is a nominal 13.8 kV, three-phase, 60 Hz receiver feeder with a 480Y/277 V protected service bus. A root may generate and distribute internally at different AC or DC voltages. Its adapter and conversion equipment remain inside its serial manifest.

### 10.2 Black-start clock

The nonpausing start sequence begins when the independent authority releases the dead-state article.

| Boundary | Maximum elapsed time | Required observable state |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| local control authority | 30 s | local time, identity, emergency stop and configuration available |

| full 1.50 MW service | 20 min | island stable under registered load |

At least 0.4 MWh must be deliverable after conversion and protection losses. At 0.60 MW, that provides forty minutes. The thirty-minute minimum therefore retains ten minutes of analytical reserve. Nameplate capacity does not count if state of charge, temperature, isolation or converter limits prevent delivery.

### 10.3 Electrical behavior

The article must demonstrate:

- steady 60 Hz service within ±0.15 Hz;
- steady RMS voltage within ±5 percent at the public boundary;
- a 0-to-50-percent, 50-to-100-percent and 100-to-zero load sequence;
- transient recovery to ±0.5 Hz within five seconds and ±5 percent voltage within two seconds after the registered step;
- safe response to phase, protection, grounding, reference and communications mismatch;
- no energization into an unidentified receiver ground or unsafe backfeed path;
- local restart after loss of the national network and remote manufacturer support;
- independent measurement of source, conversion, feeder, critical-bus and hydraulic-load energy; and
- a safe isolated state after sensor disagreement, protection failure or containment alarm.

The range registers available fault-current envelopes at 13.8 kV and 480 V before build release. Converter-limited roots and rotating-machine roots may require different protection. The public result is correct clearing and safe isolation, not one mandated fault-current waveform.

### 10.4 Eight-hour energy boundary

The integrated article requires 12 MWh at 1.5 MW for eight hours, excluding transient peaks and conversion loss. The root must bring the generation, energy stock, cooling, exhaust or reaction products, fire protection and replenishment state required by its architecture. The range supplies a measurable load, not mission energy.

This eight-hour run qualifies local behavior only. The full F36 formation still owes 168-hour service endurance and supply-chain loss testing.

## 19. Technology programs created by the specification

### 19.1 Self-surveying receiver grids

The weak-ground result demands a foundation system that knows its own geometry, reaction and settlement while carrying a changing service load. Distributed load cells, local geodesy, embedded ground characterization and active leveling should become one public technology line.

### 19.2 Receiver-independent heavy handling

The article needs a handling system that can cross modes, tolerate a denied node, establish center of gravity and set down precisely without a unique crane. Competing strand-jack portals, distributed crawlers, compact gantries and self-jacking cassettes should be judged by complete transfer and reset, not unloaded demonstrations.

### 19.3 Multi-physics interface collars

Future collars can combine structural restraint, fluid identity, valve state, contamination separation, grounding, local measurement, time and signed configuration without imposing one root's mechanics. The program should develop observable boundary state while preserving unlike physical realizations.

### 19.4 Rapid sanitary field release

Three-volume flushing, sealed blanks, mass balance and two successive decisions inside operational clocks require portable optical, chemical and particle analysis with local uncertainty evaluation and automated custody. That capability has value across water, medical, fire and flood missions.

### 19.5 Grid-forming utility kernels

The 0.6 MW critical bus, 1.5 MW continuous service and root-specific fault behavior require modular converters, storage, rotating machines, protection and grounding able to form a grid without remote services. Public test ranges should make weak-grid and converter-limited protection ordinary acquisition disciplines.

### 19.6 Evidence-native structures and utilities

Load paths, valves, breakers, isolation, contamination boundaries and service state should expose independent measurement coordinates at design time. The goal is not more telemetry. It is the ability to reconstruct cause after power, network, supplier or site loss.

### 19.7 Robotic teardown and reset

Disassembly, decontamination, inspection, waste segregation and return to storage are large fractions of readiness. Root-native robots should identify energy state, contaminated surfaces, fasteners, lift points and removed items while the public evidence plane judges their work.

## 20. Failure modes the article must expose

`CISA-1` must reject the program if:

- gross mass is met by moving ballast away from the governing center of gravity;
- total bearing area passes while one unmeasured hard point carries decisive load;
- a support or anchor failure is hidden by the range fixture;
- the article can be placed only with an unregistered receiver-owned crane or expert;
- the denied lift node is quietly restored before the challenge;
- a hydraulic proof passes while the sanitary boundary remains unknown;
- flush volume is counted without challenge and waste mass balance;
- power black-starts only through a remote service, external time source or receiver battery;
- protection passes only because the range supplies the root's missing ground reference;
- common public adapters become one hidden failure shared by both roots;
- builder telemetry replaces an independent plane or missing original;
- a clean control is discarded to improve fault-detection appearance;
- the cold article is refurbished before its receiver deployment;
- summary physical counters close while serial observations remain zero;
- reset omits contaminated, removed, consumed or quarantined items; or
- passing six interfaces is relabeled as complete `MQP-1` or production admission.
