# National Industrial-Mobilization Exercise Constitution

## Executive judgment

The Department of Resilience does not possess a mobilizable force merely because it has signed contracts, funded factories, counted inventory or witnessed prepared demonstrations. Industrial capacity exists only as a timed, configuration-specific transition from a cold state to independently accepted public service—and back to a state able to answer the next demand.

This report defines the national exercise system required to prove that transition across the forty-eight force families and the industrial constitution established for the [Acquisition and Industrial Mobilization Command](department-resilience-acquisition-and-industrial-mobilization-command.md). It is deliberately more demanding than an acquisition review, vendor readiness audit or emergency-management exercise. A valid result must conserve the same family-specific output across twelve physical stages: alert, workforce, facility start, material release, supplier activation, production, independent acceptance, loadout, strategic transport, receiving admission, commissioned service and regeneration. Paper capacity cannot replace a lost lot. Gross production cannot replace conforming output. Delivered equipment cannot replace functioning public service. First-wave success cannot replace readiness for the next wave.

The core unit is a **mission service lot**: a configuration-specific, serially traceable increment of one force family's public effect. It is defined separately for every family. A medical-service lot, utility-service block, civil-works package and command-and-data set are not interchangeable and must never be added into a fictitious national total. National admission is therefore conjunctive. Every required family must close its own chain; surplus in one family cannot offset failure in another.

The exercise design contains six progressively harder damage worlds, a 4,256-node industrial register, an 896-node annual unique sample, seven nested event classes, twenty sealed inject types, twenty-four evidence-package classes and twenty-four decision gates. At the declared planning margins, all forty-eight families close the ten-campaign M04 case; only twenty-six close M05 after simultaneous grid and transport-region loss; none close M06 when a follow-on catastrophe removes two industrial domains. Those figures are planning screens, not readiness findings. All observed production, evidence acceptance and physical admission remain zero. The model consequently admits zero families.

The exercise program receives a design allocation of USD 62 billion per year, or USD 1.55 trillion over twenty-five years. This amount is nested inside the USD 310 billion mature annual operating design for AIMC, not added to the force-capital envelope. The scale is intentional. A nation cannot learn whether a continental industrial system truly mobilizes by exercising only headquarters and leaving factories, consumables, transport, receivers and replacement stock untouched.

One constitutional correction emerges. The seven authorities defined in the preceding program-office pass are sufficient for acquisition decisions but not for hazardous national activation. The exercise constitution requires an eighth body: an **Independent Safety and Environmental Authority** with unconditional stop power, custody of exposure and residual evidence, and no power to award mission success. The authority may preserve people and environment without organizational pressure to keep a test running; a safe stop remains a valid failure, not a passing performance.

The resulting standard is severe by design:

> **The force is not mobilized when factories run. It is mobilized when every required civil effect reaches accepted service under surprise and the industrial chain can answer the next demand.**

No exercise has yet occurred. No supplier, process, facility or output has been accepted. No platform form is authorized for rendering.

## 1. Mobilization is a state transition

The ordinary language of surge capacity compresses several different claims into one phrase. A supplier may possess floor area, a workforce roster, dormant tooling, reserved material, contractual obligations or a plausible production schedule. Each can be useful, but none proves that a configured public service will exist at the required place and time.

The Department instead treats mobilization as a controlled state transition:

`declared cold state -> authenticated activation -> conforming output -> admitted receiver -> commissioned public service -> regenerated readiness`

This definition binds industrial action to the civil effect the force exists to create. It also makes failure localizable. A chain may fail because the order was not authenticated, qualified personnel did not report, a plant could not start without the grid, a nominally independent supplier shared a hidden process, metrology drifted, a transport corridor disappeared, a receiver refused the imposed burden, or replacement stock was never rebuilt. Calling each condition “insufficient capacity” would hide the mechanism and invite the wrong investment.

The transition must begin from a frozen pre-state. Before the no-notice order, participating firms and institutions disclose their people, stock, software, facilities, process state, custody and configuration. They may not quietly preposition material, select a prepared demonstration crew or infer the activation window. The exercise is not an ambush: rules, safety boundaries, compensation and sampling obligations are known. The timing, selected nodes and loss conditions are not.

The transition ends only after regeneration. A force optimized to expend its protected reserve on the first campaign can create a visually impressive success while making the nation less ready for the second. Because compound disasters and international commitments overlap, the ability to restore people, stock, tools, process state and receiver capacity is part of the mission result rather than a later logistics concern.

## 2. The mission service lot

The mission service lot, or MSL, is the smallest unit that can retain one identity from industrial production through public service. Each of the forty-eight families receives a family-specific lot definition, receiver criterion, response deadline, planning margin and readiness tier.

Examples include a configured utility-service block for deployable power, a licensed and staffed medical-service unit for acute care, a civil-works package for a rapid water trunkline, a distributed service cell for an autonomous ground-logistics convoy and a command-and-data set for civil continuity. The noun alone is insufficient. A valid MSL binds:

- the family and required public effect;
- the exact hardware, software and process configuration;
- material and component ancestry;
- production and acceptance records;
- custody through loadout and movement;
- receiving interface, consent and support burden;
- commissioned effect and measurement; and
- consumed resources and regeneration state.

The lot is therefore neither a procurement “unit” nor a universal measure of value. Twelve lots of public-access communications cannot compensate for one missing medical lot. Four commissioned power blocks cannot satisfy a water-treatment requirement. Even within a family, two lots are comparable only if their configuration and admission criteria are the same or an authorized equivalence has been established.

This prevents two recurrent distortions. The first is counting factory output as mission output. The second is constructing a portfolio average that allows abundant, easy-to-produce families to conceal the absence of scarce decisive effects. The Department may publish volumes, rates, costs and utilization for analysis, but the readiness decision is made family by family.

## 3. The twelve-stage conserved chain

Every active family passes through the same logical chain while retaining its own physical definition and deadline.

| Stage | State transition | Admission object | Typical falsifier |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | alert authentication | valid order and synchronized chronology | conflicting order or lost time source |
| 2 | workforce muster | qualified, non-duplicated people in assigned roles | absence or credential-authority loss |
| 3 | facility utility and safe start | lawful operation under declared utility loss | grid, cooling or heat-rejection denial |
| 4 | material, tooling and instrument release | serial stock and production means in custody | quarantined feedstock or missing fixture |
| 5 | supplier and process activation | independent industrial paths operating | hidden common process or ownership change |
| 6 | conforming production | serialized candidate output | yield collapse or wrong software state |
| 7 | independent acceptance | accepted lots with method and uncertainty | instrument drift or disputed original record |
| 8 | loadout and custody transfer | accepted lots prepared and sealed for movement | restraint, hazard or custody defect |
| 9 | strategic transport | same accepted lots through the damaged route | corridor loss or broken seal |
| 10 | receiving-node admission | receiver accepts configuration and burden | refusal, capacity deficit or interface mismatch |
| 11 | commissioned public service | measured family-specific effect | equipment delivered but unable to operate |
| 12 | regeneration and replacement | people, stock, process and reserve restored | second demand arrives before recovery |

For family `f`, stage `s`, required lots `R(f)`, certified conforming lots `C(f,s)`, prior-stage admitted lots `A(f,s-1)`, deadline gate `T(f,s)` and accepted-evidence gate `E(f,s)`, observed admission is:

`A(f,s) = min[R(f), C(f,s), A(f,s-1)] × T(f,s) × E(f,s)`

For the first stage, the prior-stage term is omitted. Both gates are binary. A late or unevidenced quantity receives zero admitted credit even when it exists physically. This is not a claim that late output has no humanitarian value. It is a statement that a guaranteed response deadline has not been proved.

The minimum makes loss irreversible inside one exercise chain. If twelve lots are required and only eleven cross the strategic-transport stage, no downstream declaration may restore the twelfth. A receiver may commission at most eleven. The missing lot becomes a causal finding attached to a responsible root and reopened baseline.

Family admission requires both stages eleven and twelve to meet the requirement:

`family admitted(f) = [A(f,11) >= R(f)] AND [A(f,12) >= R(f)]`

National admission is the logical conjunction across every family required by the exercise order:

`national admitted = AND family admitted(f)`

There is no weighted score. The force either supplied the declared set of civil guarantees or it did not.

## 4. Damage-world grammar

The exercise series uses six registered worlds rather than a single “stress scenario.” Each combines demand, concurrent campaigns, stage-specific industrial availability and protected reserve. The worlds are not forecasts. They are design adversaries used to expose where the force's declared architecture ceases to close.

| World | Demand share | Concurrent campaigns | Defining condition | Planning result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| M01 | 0.10 | 1 | authentication and custody probe | bounded probe |
| M02 | 0.35 | 4 | simultaneous domestic campaigns with routine friction | planning screen |
| M03 | 0.55 | 6 | four domestic and two invited international campaigns | planning screen |
| M04 | 0.80 | 10 | full declared campaign concurrency | 48 of 48 adequate |
| M05 | 1.00 | 10 | concurrent grid and transport-region loss | 26 of 48 adequate |
| M06 | 1.25 | 12 | follow-on catastrophe plus two lost industrial domains | 0 of 48 adequate |

M04 tests whether the designed margin can support the force's stated ten-campaign ambition without compound industrial damage. M05 removes the favorable assumption that the industrial base and national movement system remain outside the disaster. M06 introduces the condition the mature force must ultimately confront: a second demand wave before full recovery, with two industrial domains unavailable.

The active planning case is M05. Its twenty-six adequate families are the H1 high-readiness lines; twenty-two slower S1, W2 and W1 lines fail before commissioned service. The result is not a proposal to relax M05. It is an investment map. A family that matters during grid and transport loss must be redesigned until the chain closes or its public guarantee must be explicitly narrowed.

M06 defeats every declared family plan. This is analytically useful. A model that always produces a passing portfolio has probably encoded aspiration rather than scarcity. M06 identifies the technology frontier needed to decouple production from regional utilities, specialized labor, unique process assets and fixed transport corridors.

## 5. Sampling the national industrial organism

The industrial base cannot be proved by repeatedly activating the same showcase factory. AIMC therefore maintains persistent identity for 4,256 certified nodes across six classes:

| Node class | Registered | Unique annual sample | Maximum interval | Five-year coverage |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| primary system-integrator ecosystems | 16 | 16 | 1 year | 16 |
| independent technical-root firms | 96 | 48 | 2 years | 96 |
| subsystem and process firms | 480 | 96 | 5 years | 480 |
| certified production nodes | 2,400 | 480 | 5 years | 2,400 |
| regional service suppliers | 1,200 | 240 | 5 years | 1,200 |
| allied lead firms | 64 | 16 | 4 years | 64 |
| **Total** | **4,256** | **896** |  | **4,256** |

The sample is unique at node level. Repeating multiple events at one facility does not increase coverage. Selection is stratified by family, technical root, process ancestry, ownership, facility domain, geography, transport dependence and receiver relationship. The white cell may deliberately select a line whose nominal rivals share a coating, controller, furnace, model, source material or calibration facility.

Sampling follows three principles.

First, every system integrator activates annually because integration authority is itself a scarce production resource. Second, decisive technical roots receive tighter coverage than general production nodes; a small laboratory or process firm may control a whole family despite low revenue or headcount. Third, regional service suppliers are part of industrial readiness because commissioning, repair and receiving labor cannot be teleported from a factory gate.

Allied firms are exercised as genuine production and transfer paths, not symbolic observers. Their output must carry compatible configuration, rights, metrology, custody and receiving admission. An allied source that cannot lawfully transfer its process or cannot meet the Department's evidence constitution remains diplomatic access rather than mobilizable capacity.

## 6. A standing exercise force

The program is continuous rather than episodic. Seven nested event classes create learning at different physical scales:

| Event class | Annual cadence | Principal object |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| alert-authentication and custody probes | 192 | cold-state truth and order integrity |
| technical-root engineering restarts | 48 | reconstruction of one decisive root per family |
| pilot-line activations | 24 | repeatable conforming production and acceptance |
| complete family-chain exercises | 12 | all twelve stages for one family |
| regional compound campaigns | 4 | multiple families through a damaged receiver system |
| national ten-campaign exercise | 1 | concurrent force and industrial claims |
| two-domain-loss capstone | 0.2 | one no-notice capstone in each five-year cycle |

The architecture avoids two extremes. Exercising only complete national campaigns would be prohibitively destructive and too infrequent to diagnose technical roots. Exercising only small components would never expose transport, receiver and concurrency failure. The tiers share identifiers and evidence so a root restart informs the next pilot activation, which informs the family chain, which informs the regional and national result.

The exercise begins only after a cryptographically and institutionally authenticated order. The white cell freezes and escrows the pre-state, selects nodes and injects, and holds decryption keys. Participants know the possible inject grammar but not the selected condition. Substitute demonstration teams are prohibited. If an organization receives privileged warning, the affected result is invalidated rather than statistically adjusted.

Twenty inject classes attack order, time, workforce, credentials, utilities, cooling, material, tooling, ownership, supplier ancestry, process yield, software state, metrology, evidence custody, transport, seals, receiver consent, utility interface, follow-on demand and safety. These are controlled falsification instruments, not theatrical disruptions. Each inject has an owner, activation record, observation plan, safe-state boundary and causal question.

## 7. Evidence before score

The exercise package contains twenty-four evidence classes extending from sealed order custody to the public causal record. Evidence must retain the identity of people, facility, material, tooling, software, method, instrument, lot, carrier, route, receiver, public effect and regeneration state. A conclusion without reconstructable originals is not accepted evidence.

Evidence submission and evidence acceptance remain separate. Participating firms may submit records; they cannot certify their sufficiency. The Independent Evidence and Data Authority owns methods, observers, raw originals, uncertainty and disagreement. The Independent Cost and Capacity Authority owns randomized selection, activation economics and capacity findings. Receiving authorities own consent, interface and service admission. Public metrology and configuration-control functions preserve their respective chains.

Three rules prevent evidentiary laundering:

1. evidence acceptance without physical output yields zero admitted lots;
2. physical output without accepted evidence yields zero admitted lots; and
3. a downstream success cannot replace the missing identity or quantity of an upstream lot.

The after-action report is consequently causal rather than narrative. Every failure must name the mechanism, affected serials, public consequence, responsible root, authority owner, baseline reopened, corrective experiment and date of retest. “Coordination,” “communications” and “supply chain” are domains, not root causes. A valid finding identifies what dependency failed, why the architecture did not isolate it and which physical or institutional change can falsify the proposed correction.

## 8. Authority constitution and the eighth authority

Eight institutions divide positive action, independent judgment and negative stop power.

| Institution | Affirmative power | Forbidden power |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Civil Guarantee Authority | orders purpose and accepts public effect | cannot select a builder or rewrite failure |
| Resilience Staff | allocates families, campaigns and reserve | cannot grade physical evidence |
| AIMC and activated industry | mobilizes people, facilities, material and production | cannot see sealed injects or accept its own output |
| Independent Evidence and Data Authority | owns methods, observers, originals and findings | cannot manage participant performance |
| Receiving Service Authority | accepts consent, local burden and service | cannot waive national effect or safety |
| Independent Cost and Capacity Authority | owns randomization, cost and capacity findings | cannot improve observed output administratively |
| Production Release Board | releases bounded lots and protected reserve | cannot manufacture missing evidence |
| Independent Safety and Environmental Authority | stops exposure and requires safe state | cannot convert a safe stop into mission success |

The eighth authority is an emergent correction to the [common program-office constitution](aimc-common-program-office-operating-system-and-technical-data-constitution.md). During acquisition, safety evidence can be separated within test and release decisions. During no-notice activation, hazardous factories, reactors, energetic systems, aircraft, medical processes, contaminated sites and damaged receivers are operating simultaneously under deliberately inserted loss. Someone must possess immediate and unconditional stop power without owing the schedule, output or score.

That authority observes exposure, bystander state, residuals, waste, quarantine, safe shutdown and restart conditions. It may stop one node, one family chain or the whole exercise. It cannot declare that a safely terminated operation met its mission requirement. This separation matters because an organization responsible both for safety and program success will experience pressure to reinterpret exposure; an organization able to stop but also award success can erase the informational cost of unsafe design.

The constitutional pattern is not bureaucratic redundancy. Each authority controls a different transformation of fact. None can make the complete readiness decision alone, and none can be bypassed by a composite vote.

## 9. Financial architecture

The annual exercise design allocates USD 62 billion across nine workstreams:

| Workstream | Annual USD B | Share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| no-notice activation payments | 18 | 29.0% |
| consumed lots, scrap and replacement | 12 | 19.4% |
| transport, receiving and commissioning | 8 | 12.9% |
| independent evidence and metrology | 6 | 9.7% |
| workforce backfill and credentialing | 5 | 8.1% |
| facility utility and safe-start operations | 4 | 6.5% |
| data custody and public after action | 2 | 3.2% |
| allied-source activation | 2 | 3.2% |
| exercise contingency and safety reserve | 5 | 8.1% |
| **Total** | **62** | **100.0%** |

The twenty-five-year exercise allocation is USD 1.55 trillion in constant dollars. It equals twenty percent of AIMC's mature USD 310 billion annual operating design and is nested inside that prior envelope. It is not additional force capital, a vendor estimate or an assertion of present federal cost.

Almost half of annual exercise resources pay for activation and the articles consumed, scrapped or replaced. That is a first-principles consequence. A test system that never disturbs operating stock, makes scrap, consumes shelf life, pays worker backfill or forces line restart will systematically overstate readiness. The cost of discovering that a process does not restart is part of owning mobilizable capacity.

Activation payments buy a callable obligation, data access and disruption tolerance. They do not buy a passing result. Firms are compensated for exposing true cold-state performance, including valid failure. This aligns incentives toward accurate disclosure rather than staged success. Repeated nonperformance changes contract structure, capital allocation and root competition, but a failed exercise is not treated as fraud unless the underlying declaration or evidence was false.

## 10. Findings from the executable constitution

The companion model turns the constitution into an auditable workbook with seventeen sheets, 576 family-stage records and 2,123 deterministic controls. Its baseline is deliberately empty: every observed gross quantity, conforming yield, elapsed-time gate and evidence-acceptance field is zero. The result is zero observed admitted lots and zero admitted families.

Four sensitivity tests establish the intended behavior.

- Accepting evidence while leaving physical output at zero produces zero admission.
- Completing all twelve stages for one family at its required twelve lots admits that family.
- Reducing the transport stage to eleven lots constrains every downstream stage to eleven and denies admission.
- Activating M06 produces zero planning-adequate families.

The planning screen also reveals a specific fracture. M04 closes because the modeled 0.80 demand share and generally 0.85 stage availability remain within declared family margins. In M05, transport and loadout availability fall to 0.75 while process and supplier availability also decline. Only the twenty-six H1 families retain enough declared margin to deliver and regenerate the requirement. The twenty-two remaining lines are not “almost ready.” They are missing one or more conserved lots under a declared national damage world.

The dominant planning loss at the transport boundary is an architectural finding, not an argument to buy transport alone. Upstream designs may be too massive, geographically concentrated, hazardous to move, dependent on a unique transload node or unable to separate into receiver-ready lots. The correction can therefore involve product architecture, distributed manufacturing, alternate routes, packaging, local completion, receiver adapters or movement capacity. The exercise must preserve the causal path rather than assigning every loss to the stage where it becomes visible.

## 11. Innovation pulled by failure

The M05 and M06 gaps define a technology program more ambitious than hardening today's factories. The Department should use repeated failure to create new technical roots in at least nine areas.

**Islandable industrial utilities.** High-consequence plants require modular power, heat, cooling, water treatment, inert gases and safe shutdown independent of regional networks. This may include standardized nuclear-heat and electric interfaces, long-duration storage, closed-loop cooling and portable environmental control designed as production infrastructure rather than emergency backup.

**Transferable process cells.** Tooling, fixtures, software, inspection and environmental control should move as qualified cells between certified shells. The objective is not a generic container factory. It is a process whose critical variables remain known when geography changes.

**Self-describing metrology.** Instruments should carry calibration genealogy, uncertainty, firmware identity and tamper-evident custody in forms that independent authorities can verify offline. Acceptance should not fail merely because one proprietary cloud or national timing source is unavailable.

**Robotic cold restart.** Remote and autonomous systems can inspect dormant lines, recover material, configure tooling, reproduce process windows and perform hazardous first runs. Their value is measured in safe time-to-conforming-lot, not nominal labor displacement.

**Process-flexible materials.** Designs should qualify families of feedstocks and manufacturing routes without erasing configuration truth. Computational materials, in-process sensing and rapid coupon qualification may widen the substitution space while preserving known behavior.

**Damage-aware transport architecture.** Lots should expose mass, hazard, interface, route and commissioning requirements to a network that can recompute movement under lost corridors. Modular separation must be designed around receiver service, not shipping convenience.

**Universal receiver adapters.** Power, water, data, clinical, structural and logistics families need bounded interface kits able to translate among regional standards without transferring unsafe burdens. Receiving refusal is evidence about system architecture, not local disobedience.

**Portable commissioning and public-effect metrology.** The force needs independent instruments and methods that measure delivered water quality, stable power, clinical throughput, communications access, structural condition and hazard reduction at the service boundary.

**Regenerative production.** Material recovery, component remanufacture, additive repair, distributed spares and protected workforce replacement should be designed into the mobilization baseline. A line that creates first-wave output by cannibalizing its future is not ready.

These programs should be competed as materially independent technical roots. The exercise system then becomes an innovation engine comparable to military operational testing: it produces hard demand for new physics, manufacturing methods, autonomy, infrastructure and standards while preventing an attractive demonstration from becoming doctrine.

## 12. What this pass does not claim

The scenario factors, lot quantities, margins, node counts and costs are strategic design hypotheses. They make the constitution executable and expose its implications; they are not empirical estimates of current U.S. industry. The planning model does not yet contain measured facility distributions, route networks, correlated failure probabilities, family bills of process, workforce substitution graphs or receiver capacities.

The 896-node sample defines coverage scale, not a final statistical design. Actual sampling will require a registered industrial graph and probability weights that preserve rare but decisive roots. The M04–M06 results are deterministic screens rather than probabilistic forecasts. A later empirical model must distinguish aleatory variation, epistemic uncertainty, adversarial concealment and structural dependence.

No family has completed a physical exercise. No data package has been accepted. The workbook's passing audit controls establish internal consistency only. They do not establish industrial readiness, legal adoption, budget authority or operational suitability.

## 13. Next research boundary

This pass opens two connected tasks.

First, the twenty-two M05 planning gaps must become **family-specific industrial redesign portfolios**. Each needs a process ancestry, facility and utility topology, workforce graph, material routes, transport form, receiver interface, protected reserve and technology experiments sufficient to move it from its current readiness tier toward H1 without fabricating a readiness claim.

Second, the Department needs its first complete no-notice exercise package for one representative family. That package must specify the frozen pre-state, sampled nodes, sealed injects, safety envelope, mission service lot, twelve-stage instrumentation, receiver agreement, independent authorities, consumed articles, cost, causal after-action schema and regeneration test. The choice of family should maximize institutional learning rather than visual spectacle.

Those tasks precede representative vehicle geometry. Form will be rendered only after a family requirement, coupled internal architecture, receiving burden and physical evidence have earned that authority.
