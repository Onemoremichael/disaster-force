# RAS-1 — Quarter-source entry and coupled-ancestry safety case

## Decision abstract

`RAS-1` defines the first source-bearing qualification campaign for the F36 `CISA-40` `SA6` Root A program. It does not assume that the nonenergetic methods in `RAW-1` have physically qualified. It states the additional physical constitution that would be required before `AM-05` unignited source custody or `AM-06` quarter-source fire and coupled ancestry could earn limited release.

The decisive finding is that **quarter inventory is not quarter consequence**. The admitted source is 500 kilograms, one quarter of the two-tonne maximum-credible release. If its occupied area remains at the nominal 6.25 square metres, the inherited burning-flux screen gives a 14.639625-megawatt heat-release rate. If the same 500 kilograms reaches the 25-square-metre casualty-area bound, the peak screen returns to 58.5585 megawatts—the same area-controlled peak as the two-tonne reference. Reduced mass shortens notional duration before it reduces an unconstrained peak. RAS-1 therefore separates inventory fraction, release rate, occupied area, peak heat release, burn duration, route similarity, water-to-source ratio, retention, terminal-source time, ancestry and release authority. No single scale factor may represent them.[1–8]

The campaign crosses two methods, two physically unlike truth roots, six public rigs and six source-entry classes. The minimum population is 144 required valid credits. Twenty-nine dormant invalid-attempt reserve tokens protect the population without allowing valid adverse results to be repeated away. The base campaign schedules 44.4 tonnes of source and 624 rig-days; the full reserve envelope adds 10.8 tonnes and 147 rig-days. `AM-06` requires 864 cubic metres of base intervention water and a 528-cubic-metre reserve envelope. A 30-percent forensic allowance raises the native evidence provision for all possible attempts to approximately 26.462 terabytes.

The physical campaign is organized around twelve final control elements, thirty-six independent safeing paths, twenty mandatory abort conditions, twelve denial cases, forty-eight calibration families, twenty-four causal-time nodes, seventy-two sample-custody plans, twenty-four uncertainty budgets, twelve within-rig unlike-root agreements, four cross-campus root-equivalence decisions and twenty-four outside-site casualty replays. Source and water receive separate mass ledgers, separate ancestry ledgers, separate samples and separate custodians. Public intervention always protects life and the site; it becomes an adverse causal event and can never be credited as candidate performance.

The modeled establishment requires 512 public positions, $620 million in gross capital and $235 million per year in gross support. Registered overlap with the existing public rig, measurement, evidence and method estate is $560 million and $207 million per year, leaving $60 million capital and $28 million per year as the net extension. Thirty-month gross exposure is $1.2075 billion. The resulting credited portfolio boundary is $2.435 billion capital and $464 million per year; the uncredited view is $3.095 billion and $637 million per year. These are planning hypotheses, not appropriations, bids or observed cost.[1–10]

The companion model has forty-eight sheets and passes 120 structural, numerical, blank-versus-zero, dimensional, financial, adverse-direction, coupled-water, thermal and authority assertions with no formula-error matches. All 144 physical trial results, both method qualifications and twenty-four outside-site replays remain open. Eight downstream decisions remain blocked: separate `AM-05` and `AM-06` releases, routine quarter-source authority, candidate-article entry, `AM-07` full-source authority, Root A campaign release, production release and exterior rendering. RAS-1 is design-complete and physically unexecuted.

## 1. The proposition being qualified

The question is not whether a rig can survive 500 kilograms of source. It is whether the Department can admit, observe, terminate, recover and independently reconstruct a bounded source-bearing event while preserving public safety and a truthful account of every consequential inventory. The qualification object is therefore a **source-entry method embedded in a governed physical system**, not a fuel quantity, test script or candidate device.

`AM-05` and `AM-06` have different propositions.

| Method | Proposition | Energetic condition | Required truth |
|---|---|---|---|
| `AM-05` | terminate, capture and recover admitted source before ignition | ignition prohibited | admitted mass, source state, every destination and terminal disposition |
| `AM-06` | create, observe, safely end and reconstruct a controlled quarter-source fire with coupled water | registered ignition opportunity by class | source, thermal field, source ancestry, water ancestry, intervention sequence and environmental disposition |

`AM-05` is not a rehearsal that receives credit merely because nothing ignites. It must establish that a source-bearing system can conserve identity and mass from source vessel through dispenser, route, normal receiver, casualty receiver, surface, sample and environment. `AM-06` adds ignition opportunity and a second active inventory: water introduced by the candidate, the public rig or emergency intervention. A result is not interpretable unless the program can distinguish which water entered, where it went, what source it carried, what heat effect occurred and which action preceded which transition.

The methods earn separate authority. An `AM-05` pass cannot release `AM-06`, because source custody without fire does not establish thermal or mixture truth. An `AM-06` pass cannot erase an `AM-05` failure, because a coupled event does not excuse an inability to terminate and recover unignited source. Neither method grants a candidate article the right to enter, and neither implies that the full two-tonne `AM-07` state is safe or representative.

## 2. Twelve conjunctive entry conditions

Source entry requires twelve conditions to exist at the same time and on the same serialized configuration:

1. `RAW-1` methods `AM-00` through `AM-04` have been physically accepted on both unlike roots.
2. The exact rig source train, casualty routes, safeing paths and evidence systems are accepted.
3. Source mass, composition, temperature, lot, custody and terminal disposition are accepted.
4. The three adjacent compartments are physically inert and independently observed.
5. The normal 15-cubic-metre source sump and 40-cubic-metre firewater vault are isolated and available.
6. The casualty 50-cubic-metre source receiver and 120-cubic-metre water receiver are isolated and available.
7. The nominal 6.25-square-metre and casualty 25-square-metre thermal fields are bounded.
8. Source, candidate water, public water and environmental background remain analytically separable.
9. Source, ignition, water, public intervention and recovery remain causally ordered after network and primary-time denial.
10. Source and water ledgers, samples and serialized baseline can close after the event.
11. A second campus can reproduce truth, uncertainty and disposition from a sealed packet.
12. The quarter-source board separately accepts `AM-05` or `AM-06` without inferring `AM-07`.

These are not twelve topics on a checklist. They are one conjunction. A rig with excellent containment but unresolved ancestry cannot enter. A source with excellent custody but no casualty receiver cannot enter. A complete local packet that cannot be replayed elsewhere cannot qualify a national method. A board cannot waive a missing physical condition by accepting a narrative substitute.

The adjacent-compartment rule is especially important. `MCR-1` defined one live two-tonne compartment while its neighbors remain inert; it did not silently create a four-compartment source event. RAS-1 preserves this topology. The three neighboring compartments receive independent inert-state observation, not a shared control-system declaration. Propagation into a neighbor is a safety event, a configuration failure and a limit on the method claim.

## 3. Scale law: why one quarter does not mean one quarter

The phrase “quarter source” names admitted mass only. RAS-1 registers sixteen scale relations because distinct physical quantities scale differently.

### 3.1 Inventory and release rate

The source mass is 500 kilograms against the two-tonne reference, so the inventory fraction is 0.25. If both are admitted over 120 seconds, nominal mass rate is also one quarter. That second equality depends on preserving the schedule. A valve transient, gravity route or casualty diversion can produce local rates that do not scale with total mass.

### 3.2 Area, peak and duration

At 6.25 square metres, the quarter-source nominal area is 0.25 of the 25-square-metre full-source area. Using the inherited 0.055-kilogram-per-square-metre-second burning-flux screen and 42.588-megajoule-per-kilogram lower heating value:

\[
\dot{Q}_{nominal}=6.25\times0.055\times42.588=14.639625\;MW
\]

At the casualty-area bound:

\[
\dot{Q}_{bound}=25\times0.055\times42.588=58.5585\;MW
\]

With radiative fraction 0.35, the corresponding radiant outputs are 5.12386875 and 20.495475 megawatts. The 500-kilogram event at 25 square metres has a shorter notional burning interval than the two-tonne event, but the area-controlled peak is unchanged. Thermal soak, local flame geometry, plume entrainment, sensor saturation and safeing time may respond to peak, duration or both. RAS-1 carries each quantity rather than assuming a linear consequence ratio.

### 3.3 Hydraulic similarity

If source rate falls by four in an equal route with unchanged density and viscosity, velocity and Reynolds number fall by four. Froude behavior also changes with velocity, while Weber number falls approximately with velocity squared and therefore by sixteen. A quarter-rate flow can cross a regime boundary for surface spreading, atomization, breakup, drain capture or retained film. This is why a small event may be less representative even when it is safer in total energy.

### 3.4 Water coupling

`QE-06` permits 48 cubic metres of water against 500 kilograms of source. At the declared densities, the water-to-source mass ratio is 96. Intervention water is therefore the dominant transported mass, can spread source into new routes, can fill normal retention and can make an otherwise local source event an environmental custody problem.

The 48-cubic-metre casualty case occupies 40 percent of the 120-cubic-metre casualty water receiver before residuals, foam, rain or emergency additions. Volume headroom therefore belongs to the initial-condition envelope, not to a nominal tank label.

### 3.5 No automatic extrapolation

`AM-07` authority remains zero even after every quarter-source result passes. Full-source entry introduces different total energy, longer thermal exposure, receiver inventory, suppression duration, structural heating, sample load and recovery burden. Some mechanisms may transfer; the board may cite them as prior evidence. Transfer is not identity. The full-source method requires its own consequence case, event population and authority.

## 5. Physical custody: source, water and samples

RAS-1 establishes forty source lots. Each of the four method-root families receives ten: six active lots assigned one per rig, one reference, one destructive, and two quarantine. This is more source stock than a minimum test calculation would require because qualification must survive disputed composition, suspected contamination, destructive examination and reproduction without asking a candidate supplier to recreate the reference.

Twenty-four water lots assign normal supply, normal reference, casualty supply and casualty reference to each rig. Water is not treated as a generic utility. Its source, treatment, background chemistry, tracer state, initial volume, receiving path and environmental disposition can change the causal interpretation of `AM-06`.

Twelve site-native tracer codes create optical and electrochemical families for source and water across the three campuses. The optical and electrochemical roots cannot share the decisive reader, calibration transform or reference preparation. Minimum recovery is 0.995 and maximum cross-talk is 0.005. Those thresholds are necessary but not sufficient: source in water, water in source, surface inventory, retained samples and environmental background remain separate reported fields.

Seventy-two sample-custody plans cross six rigs, six classes and two media. Each plan defines locations and preserves two sealed aliquots plus one archive. A sample is not merely evidence that material was collected. The chain must identify the event, time, physical location, medium, container, seal, custodian, transfer, storage state, analytical method, remaining mass and final disposition. Destructive analysis cannot consume the only retained reference.

The source mass ledger accounts for admitted source, source-vessel residual, normal receiver, casualty receiver, surface or route inventory, burned vapor or sample, and environment. The water ledger accounts for initial water, supplied water, return, normal storage, casualty custody, environment, vapor or sample. The ancestry ledger keeps recovery and cross-talk apart from mass closure. A mass ledger can close while origin is wrong; an ancestry ledger can recover code fraction while mass is missing. Both must pass.

## 6. Final control elements and independent safeing

RAS-1 defines twelve final control elements:

- source inlet and dispenser isolation;
- gravity source diversion;
- normal and casualty source-receiver control;
- ignition-energy removal;
- candidate and public-water isolation;
- casualty water diversion;
- normal electrical isolation;
- independent safety energy;
- environmental boundary and safe vent; and
- an evidence safe-state latch.

Each element receives three paths: primary, diverse and manual or passive. The resulting thirty-six paths must be physically independent at the point that produces the safe state. Three screens displaying the same valve command are one command chain, not three safeing paths. A final-state sensor should observe the valve, breaker, route or latch after actuation. Motive energy is declared: spring, gravity, stored pneumatic energy, local battery, passive geometry or mechanical memory.

The design makes a distinction the broader Resilience Force should adopt: **command, actuation and resulting state are different evidence objects**. A control system may issue a correct command while energy is absent, a mechanism binds or the wrong component is addressed. Safety credit attaches to the observed physical state and its time, not to the message.

Independent safety energy is sized to maintain local safeing and state evidence for thirty seconds after normal energy loss. Longer post-event acquisition comes from the rig evidence estate; the safety path itself should not require a campus network, remote service or candidate controller. The mechanical latch preserves last decisive state after complete energy loss.

Twenty abort conditions cover source identity and mass, neighbor state, receiver availability, truth and time roots, uncommanded release, excessive rate or area, boundary heat, terminal-source time, ancestry, source and water closure, loss of services and public intervention. Public safety authority has unconditional stop power. It does not have success-award power. An intervention may save the event boundary while proving that the tested method or candidate did not.

## 7. Denial as a designed operating state

Twelve denial cases remove normal electrical energy, campus network, primary time, primary source reader, primary water reader, normal source destination, normal water destination, command path, one environmental sensor, one native original, the origin campus analysis chain or operator interpretation. Each removal has a declared survivor.

`QE-06` combines energy, network and primary-time denial because these dependencies are likely to fail together in a damaged environment. The event must remain safe locally, but evidence standards are not relaxed. An unlike local clock preserves order; locally complete originals preserve native observations; gravity and stored energy preserve safeing; casualty receivers preserve custody. If the method cannot remain decidable, the attempt can be invalid, but it cannot pass through a post-event reconstruction that silently imports the missing service.

This design is directly transferable to disaster operations. Flood, fire and earthquake formations cannot treat communications loss as an exotic test inject. A mature Department should build systems whose safety, local public-service action and evidence all survive the failure of remote coordination. National systems may later reconcile and allocate; they should not be the only means by which a local machine can stop, contain or explain itself.

## 8. Truth roots, calibration and uncertainty

Each method has two physically unlike roots.

For `AM-05`, Root A uses gravimetric source-vessel, dispenser-residual and receiver masses. Root B uses geometric source tomography and chemistry-tagged distributed inventory. For `AM-06`, Root A uses orthogonal optical source and water codes with fire-capable readers. Root B uses electrochemical codes, gravimetric phase separation and retained samples.

The roots do not merely produce two numbers. They differ in physical principle, calibration, supplier path, software transform and custody. Each must carry all six classes on all six rigs. Within a rig, both roots require six passing class results; their normalized mean source-discrepancy gap must not exceed 0.25. Across campuses, each method-root requires thirty-six passing credits and bounded site spread. A method decision requires seventy-two accepted trial results, six rig agreements, two cross-campus equivalence decisions, all forty-eight calibration families, all twenty-four time nodes, all twelve evidence objects and an independent board acceptance.

Forty-eight calibration families cover eight decisive domains on each rig: source mass, water volume, source ancestry, water ancestry, thermal field, boundary heat flux, final-state time and local evidence time. Before-and-after bookends detect drift. The bookends do not retroactively make an out-of-family instrument valid; an excursion creates an uncertainty and adjudication problem for the affected interval.

Twenty-four causal-time nodes give each rig a registered primary root, unlike local root, evidence recorder and safe-state latch. The inherited maximum error is five microseconds. The purpose is not universal clock perfection. It is reliable causal order between source release, ignition opportunity, water introduction, public intervention, final-element transition and observed physical effect.

Twenty-four uncertainty budgets cross method, root and class. They retain source-mass, destination, ancestry and thermal/time contributions before root-specific combination. Expanded uncertainty cannot exceed five kilograms for `AM-05` or six kilograms for `AM-06` in the initial design. Covariance must be declared where components share calibration, environment or processing. Narrow arithmetic is not evidence of independence.

## 9. Evidence, validity and replay

The inherited data rate is 53.40384 megabytes per second. Each event retains ten minutes at full rate and six hours at one percent, creates two native originals and carries a 1.35 custody factor. The resulting 117.659340288 gigabytes per event produce approximately 16.943 terabytes for 144 base credits. Provisioning 173 possible attempts and 30 percent forensic overhead produces approximately 26.462 terabytes.

Twelve evidence objects are mandatory: source ledger, water ledger, source ancestry, water ancestry, thermal field, safe-state chronicle, denial chronicle, calibration bookends, sample chain, two native originals, remote replay packet and board disposition. Derived summaries are useful but cannot replace them.

Validity and performance remain separate. An event can be invalid because its identity, initial condition, calibration, timing or evidence chain failed. A valid event can be adverse because the method did not terminate, close or reconstruct the source. Valid adverse evidence counts against qualification. Invalidity consumes an approved reserve token but does not erase the attempt.

Twenty-four `QE-06` events receive outside-site replay. The remote campus must reconstruct the result from a sealed, machine-readable packet without the origin analysis chain. Required agreement is 0.995. A remote report that copies the origin conclusion is not replay. The receiver must execute the method version, recover inputs, reproduce calculations, show uncertainty and issue its own disposition.

The workbook enforces a crucial computing rule: blanks are not zeros. An accepted source ledger with absent discrepancy, uncertainty or environmental-source fields fails. An accepted `AM-06` thermal ledger with no boundary-flux observation fails. A genuine sealed datum passes only when the relevant zero values are present as numbers.

## 10. Technology program created by the safety case

RAS-1 is not constrained to components already sold at emergency-management scale. It defines fourteen capability campaigns whose exit objects are evidence failures closed in a public method.

1. Fire-capable multimodal source tomography should reconstruct distributed inventory through smoke, spray and conductive structures.
2. Orthogonal molecular codes should keep source, candidate water, public water and environment separable without materially changing their behavior.
3. High-temperature electrochemical readers should supply an unlike ancestry root at drains and receivers.
4. Self-calibrating mass and volume nodes should retain uncertainty after vibration, heat and denial.
5. Passive high-rate diversion should route source to a casualty receiver without grid or network command.
6. Selective phase recovery should separate source and water while retaining measurable origin.
7. Distributed pool-edge sensing should resolve area growth under obscuration.
8. Radiant and convective boundary arrays should reconstruct site consequence in real time.
9. Mechanically latched final-state memory should remain readable after total energy loss.
10. A causal local-time fabric should preserve event order without navigation or campus services.
11. Fire-hardened evidence vaults should make two native originals and sealed packets at the rig edge.
12. Autonomous contamination-search systems should find source on surfaces, in drains and along environmental routes while preserving custody.
13. A machine-verifiable safety-case compiler should bind event identity, calibration, ledgers, samples, validity and decision without hiding missingness.
14. Rapid reversible containment liners should bound occupied area without concealing retained source.

The modeled technology allocation is $1.005 billion across the fourteen campaigns. It is a portfolio signal rather than an additive RAS-1 cost line; prototypes, public facilities and test use may sit in CISA, shared technology or later program increments. The purpose of publishing the allocation is to show where first-principles demand outruns available industrial product.

This is how a Department of Resilience can create a new industrial economy. It should not ask existing firms to adapt surplus platforms around a small civil market. It should create sustained demand for source-accountable response systems, physically independent controls, high-rate environmental metrology, deployable containment, causal evidence and field phase recovery. Open public interfaces and government-held reference methods allow specialized firms to compete at subsystem, root and complete-system levels. Repeated qualification and regeneration create a continuing market rather than a one-time demonstration.

Concentration is administratively attractive and epistemically dangerous, particularly when novel systems operate under emergency urgency.

## 13. Thirty-month execution logic

The campaign has twelve overlapping work packages. Design and hazard decomposition occupy months one through three. Source, water and tracer lots qualify from months two through eight. Control and safeing realization runs through month ten, alongside metrology and time acceptance. The class campaign proceeds from the sealed datum in months eight through eleven to low and intermediate source, nominal quarter source, blind ancestry crossover and finally the casualty-area denial and replay class in months twenty-two through twenty-eight. Unlike-root and cross-campus adjudication follows accepted packets; the quarter-source board closes in months twenty-nine and thirty.

Calendar overlap does not allow evidentiary leapfrogging. A later class may prepare while an earlier class is under review, but it cannot enter source on the assumption that the predecessor will pass. The six rigs each carry twenty-four base events and 104 base rig-days. Reserve allocations of 24 or 25 rig-days bring the fleet envelope to 771 rig-days. The 60-percent ready-rig planning fraction leaves time for configuration control, calibration, blind setup, recovery, investigation, maintenance and public observation.

The event order is fixed within each method-root-rig cell. Class, source lot, water lot, initial-condition envelope, denied services and replay campus are registered before execution. Execution time, packet time, validity and credit fields remain blank. This prevents schedule software from manufacturing progress through planned records.

## 14. Release law and failure containment

The release ladder contains eight non-authorities at issue:

1. `AM-05` method release;
2. `AM-06` method release;
3. routine quarter-source authority;
4. candidate-article entry;
5. `AM-07` full-source authority;
6. Root A campaign release;
7. production release; and
8. exterior-rendering authority.

The first two depend on their respective method-qualification decisions. Routine quarter-source authority requires both methods and all outside-site replays. Candidate entry requires a separate article safety case. Full-source entry requires a new source and consequence constitution. Root A campaign and production decisions require further authorities. Exterior rendering remains blocked until physical architecture is sufficiently mature.

Failure is contained at the narrowest truthful level. A source-ledger failure blocks the event, not every rig by decree. A valid adverse event remains in the class population and blocks the relevant root or agreement. A local instrument fault may invalidate an attempt if independent validity authority finds that the registered experiment cannot be interpreted. A shared calibration failure may affect multiple events and require readjudication. A truth-root common cause prevents the other root from being called “independent.” A public intervention can save life while demonstrating that the method’s own safeing did not close.

Restart requires evidence specific to the cause: corrected hardware, new calibration, restored custody, revised hazard boundary, additional reference article, amended method version or expanded event class. Restart never changes the original record. The replacement receives a new identity and consumes the proper reserve token.

## 16. What RAS-1 changes in the Department of Resilience

RAS-1 turns source escalation into a force function. The Department needs permanent source and water custody, final-element engineering, unlike metrology, local causal time, public abort authority, sample archives and outside-region replay. These capabilities are not program paperwork. They are the basis for operating novel response systems in damaged environments without asking their suppliers to define success.

The deeper institutional insight is that response itself alters the disaster. Water can cool a fire and spread contamination. Earthmoving can open access and destabilize a slope. Pumping can protect one district and increase downstream load. Grid restoration can save heat-exposed populations and energize damaged conductors. Medical evacuation can relieve a local system and overload the receiver. A serious Resilience Force therefore needs coupled-intervention science at the same level that military institutions study weapons effects, logistics and operational testing.

That ambition should shape technology. A deployable desalination and water-distribution formation should know source, treatment, concentrate, energy, pipe, storage and public-release state in real time. A wildfire formation should distinguish agent effect from weather and fuel change. A flood formation should measure where displaced water goes. A heat-response formation should preserve causal links between power, cooling, building conditions and health. Public truth architecture is what lets such systems learn rapidly without confusing scale, activity or attractive imagery with service.

RAS-1 also strengthens the case for delaying exterior renderings. The emerging physical obligations—large casualty receivers, passive diversion, independent energy, two ancestry roots, fire-hardened evidence, sample access, reversible containment and maintainable safeing—will determine form. Rendering a carrier or vehicle before these internal systems are experimentally resolved would turn an unresolved load case into a visual commitment. Internal sections, custody diagrams, safeing schematics and evidence flows remain appropriate; exterior form does not.
