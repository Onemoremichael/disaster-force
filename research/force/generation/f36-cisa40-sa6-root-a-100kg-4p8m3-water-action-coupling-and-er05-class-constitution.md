## Decision abstract

`RBL-1` defines the fifth physical rung of the `AM-06` entry ladder created by [RBG-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-am06-source-water-ignition-entry-ladder-reset.md). It begins only after the `ER-04` dry-source flame class in [RBK-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-100kg-dry-source-flame-state-quadrature-and-er04-class-constitution.md), the water-only reference in [RBI-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-water-only-reference-system-and-er02-class-constitution.md) and the dual-ancestry reference in [RBJ-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-dual-ancestry-reference-architecture-competition-and-er03-class-constitution.md) are physically accepted. Its question is:

> What public reference architecture can introduce 4.8 cubic metres of candidate-path water into a verified 100-kilogram flame, distinguish the water action from public intervention, measure the interaction rather than merely the input, account for every source and water destination, and preserve the dry combustion roots after steam, runoff and phase transfer change what they observe?

The answer is not a larger valve on `FRC-100`. Volume alone does not define a water action. The same 4.8 cubic metres can arrive as a coherent jet, a distributed droplet field, a moving sheet or a sparse pulse pattern. Those states differ in rate, momentum, droplet or ligament scale, occupied volume, source contact, surface impact, entrainment, vapor production and runoff. A reference that knows only total volume cannot attribute an effect and cannot later judge a candidate with a different field.

The scale law changes the meaning of the event. At the registered 998-kilogram-per-cubic-metre density, 4.8 cubic metres is 4,790.4 kilograms of water, 47.904 times the 100-kilogram source inventory. If introduced over the 120-second reference window, water mass rate is 39.92 kilograms per second. That is 47.904 times the scheduled source-admission rate and approximately 290.33 times the inherited area-controlled burning-rate screen.

Water also carries a larger theoretical heat sink than the fire carries source energy. Heating the registered water from a 20-degree-Celsius reference to 100 degrees and vaporizing it requires approximately 12,416.14 megajoules, 2.915 times the source's 4,258.8-megajoule lower-heating-value input. Even the physically impossible limiting case in which all source energy enters the water can vaporize only about 1,643.13 kilograms, or 1.646 cubic metres. At least 3.154 cubic metres therefore remains liquid under the source-energy-only ceiling. `ER-05` is intrinsically a runoff, contamination and recovery experiment, not merely a flame-control experiment.

RBL-1 creates a public `WAE-4.8` water-action emulator. “Candidate water” names the future candidate-owned route and causal position; it does not admit a candidate supplier. Four unlike delivery architectures enter through two builder lineages at three campuses, creating twenty-four articles. They execute 192 water-field trials and 144 blinded matched comparisons. Two maximally unlike reference morphologies must survive hard gates: one dispersed field and one coherent field. Retaining two prevents a single spray form from becoming a false universal reference.

Sixteen serviceable cassettes execute ninety-six integrated qualification cycles. Each cassette performs three dry/wet pairs—baseline, denied-service and residue/return—so the integration campaign contains forty-eight dry and forty-eight wet 100-kilogram events. Dry requalification is necessary because the new water cassette, sensing apertures, drains and thermal boundary change the dry architecture.

The exact `ER-05` class crosses three campuses, two selected water morphologies and two cassette lineages in twelve cells. Source-lot lineages and igniter lineages are balanced within that grid rather than added as hidden factors. Four invalid-attempt reserves retain exact-cell identity. A valid event without an established pre-water flame cannot earn water-effect credit. A valid wet event with bounded public consequence but failed runoff, ancestry, thermal, root-survival or return remains adverse and cannot be repeated away.

Each wet event must close twelve separate propositions: source entry, ignition opportunity, pre-water flame, water issue, water-field state, flame response, source transformation, thermal and phase-change consequence, atmosphere state, source-carrying runoff, safe end and 1,008-hour return. The dry `PHO`, `ION`, `MOL` and `CAL` roots do not automatically survive. Steam can obscure the photon field; conductive water can alter ion response; water can scrub products from the gas route; and latent heat can move energy outside the dry calorimetric account. ## 1. Water action is a field, not a quantity

Four equal-volume events can be physically unlike:

| Water form | Governing local variable | Likely interaction difference | Required measurement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| distributed droplets | number, size, velocity and occupied volume | evaporation, plume cooling and distributed surface contact | three-dimensional dispersed-phase field |
| coherent fan | sheet thickness, velocity, sweep and impact line | source displacement, surface impulse and directed runoff | sheet geometry, momentum and contact trace |
| annular curtain | perimeter continuity, inward angle and closure | radiation interception and edge confinement | curtain gaps, inward transport and source boundary |
| sparse pulse lattice | impulse, interval, location and recovery | repeated disruption with dry intervals | pulse-resolved momentum, overlap and response |

Total volume is necessary for custody. It is insufficient for causality. The reference must observe at least volume, mass, temperature, issue rate, local momentum, dispersed-size or sheet-thickness distribution, three-dimensional occupied field, source/flame overlap, surface impact, vapor, runoff and terminal disposition.

This distinction has force-design consequences. A future wildfire aircraft, industrial-fire ground unit or urban high-rise suppression system cannot be qualified by gallons delivered alone. Its useful action depends on where the agent goes, what physical state reaches the hazard, what is displaced, what is cooled, what is carried away and whether the public can account for the new downstream hazard.

## 2. Candidate-path water without candidate admission

`RBG-1` calls the 4.8-cubic-metre input “candidate water” to separate it from public quench. At `ER-05`, however, no candidate drainage or suppression article has been admitted. RBL-1 resolves the ambiguity with a public water-action emulator.

Its performance establishes only that the registered water field occurred. It cannot establish that a future candidate would create the field, control the fire, retain runoff or return safely.

Public quench remains a different system. It may act at any time required for life or site safety. Its water, command, route, effect and disposition remain separately identified. If public quench supplies missing candidate-path water or completes extinction, it cannot improve emulator or class credit.

## 3. First-principles scale law

### 3.1 Inventory and rate

Using 998 kilograms per cubic metre:

\[
M_w=4.8\times998=4{,}790.4\ \text{kg}.
\]

The water-to-source inventory mass ratio is:

\[
R_{w/s}=4{,}790.4/100=47.904.
\]

For a 120-second issue:

\[
\dot{M}_w=4{,}790.4/120=39.92\ \text{kg/s}.
\]

Scheduled source admission is 0.8333 kilogram per second, so the issue-rate mass ratio is also 47.904. The inherited burning-rate screen is 0.1375 kilogram per second; water rate is approximately 290.33 times that screen. These ratios must not be conflated. One compares registered inputs; the other compares water input with a notional burning limit.

Across 2.5 square metres, the nominal applied volume flux is 0.016 cubic metre per square metre-second. This value sizes the emulator and it is not assumed uniform at the source. Field nonuniformity is one of the observations.

### 3.2 Enthalpy ceiling

At the declared 20-degree-Celsius reference, heating water to 100 degrees requires approximately 0.33488 megajoule per kilogram. Adding a 2.257-megajoule-per-kilogram vaporization screen gives 2.59188 megajoules per kilogram. Thus:

\[
E_{w,heat+vap}=4{,}790.4\times2.59188=12{,}416.14\ \text{MJ}.
\]

The source lower-heating-value input is 4,258.8 megajoules, so the water enthalpy capacity is 2.915 times larger. The source-energy-only vaporization ceiling is:

\[
M_{vap,max}=4{,}258.8/2.59188=1{,}643.13\ \text{kg}.
\]

That is 1.646 cubic metres or 34.3 percent of the water. Heat lost to structure, gases, radiation, residue and remaining source reduces actual vaporization. RBL-1 therefore designs for at least 3.154 cubic metres of liquid disposition under the declared energy ceiling, before rain, public water, cleaning water or measurement additions.

The calculation is not an extinction prediction. It proves that gross water volume can overwhelm source energy while local flame persists, and that most water may remain a transport medium for source and products.

### 3.3 Receiver and custody fractions

The 4.8-cubic-metre issue is twelve percent of the 40-cubic-metre normal firewater vault and four percent of the 120-cubic-metre casualty-water receiver. Those fractions appear comfortable only before source-carrying water, foam, public quench, prior retained inventory, rain and uncertainty are included. Free volume must be measured before each event. A tank name is not capacity evidence.

## 10. Water-field truth

Water issue retains the unlike gravimetric and swept-volume roots qualified in RBI-1. RBL-1 adds three field accounts:

1. a kinematic account for local velocity, direction, impulse, dispersed-size or sheet-thickness distribution and issue continuity;
2. an occupied-field account for three-dimensional water presence, flame/source overlap, obscuration and surface contact; and
3. a terminal account for vapor, retained liquid, runoff, receiver, samples and environment.

The field accounts use physically unlike observations where a conclusion is consequential. Optical methods cannot be the only decisive path because steam and droplets also alter the photon flame root. Momentum and surface-impact witnesses cannot be the only path because they miss suspended water. The architecture therefore combines protected multi-view field imaging with distributed impact/impulse surfaces, local conductivity and temperature witnesses, and custody measurements at each terminal route.

No field estimator receives access to the event's expected morphology or outcome before its native disposition seals.

## 12. Thermal, vapor and atmosphere accounts

The 4,258.8-megajoule source input remains a screen. The wet `CAL` account adds:

- sensible energy gained by liquid water;
- latent energy assigned to vapor;
- water-carried energy leaving the source field;
- condensate energy returned to surfaces or receivers;
- source, structure and cassette storage;
- radiant and convective boundary transfer;
- gas and particulate exhaust enthalpy; and
- unaccounted energy with uncertainty.

Temperature, vapor fraction and mass must be jointly observed. A steam cloud is not an energy measurement. A lower gas temperature does not prove that water absorbed the missing energy; it may reflect incomplete transformation, altered plume direction or measurement obscuration.

Atmosphere authority separately records steam, smoke, oxygen, toxic products, visibility, pressure and entry state. Steam can reduce optical visibility while increasing burn or scald hazard. Human entry remains a measured safety decision, not a fixed time after water ends.

## 21. Stop, amendment and release law

RBL-1 stops or narrows if:

- candidate-path water and public water share a final element, command identity or decisive ledger;
- total volume is used as a substitute for water-field state;
- one reference morphology is treated as universal;
- the pre-water flame gate is missing or inferred from igniter command;
- a wet root uses another root's conclusion to survive steam or water;
- gas-only products stand in for gas, liquid and residue custody;
- water recovery is called source recovery;
- vapor mass or latent energy is inferred from visible steam alone;
- runoff free volume is assumed rather than measured;
- public quench improves reference credit;
- a valid no-prewater-flame, self-terminated, runoff-adverse or other adverse event is repeated away;
- a reserve changes morphology, cassette lineage, campus, source lineage, igniter lineage or root versions;
- the 0.10-kilogram environmental-source or 0.75-kilogram unaccounted-source limits are inflated by attempts;
- return occurs before 1,008 hours;
- outside replay needs the origin campus; or
- `RAS-3R3` is not adopted before physical campaign issue.

Architecture selection does not issue source. Cassette qualification does not issue a class event. One wet event does not release another exact cell. `ER-05-QUALIFIED` does not admit a candidate, raise source inventory, authorize public deployment or settle exterior form.

## 22. Research stocktake

RBL-1 changes the program's understanding in seven ways.

First, water application is a field problem. Volume without momentum, distribution, overlap and terminal disposition is not a repeatable intervention.

Second, the nominally smaller 4.8-cubic-metre rung is a mass-dominant event. Water outweighs source by 47.904 and most of it must remain liquid under the source-energy ceiling.

Third, the dry truth architecture does not automatically survive wet operation. Water changes the observation physics of every combustion root and can make unlike systems share an obscuration or transport failure.

Fourth, a public emulator is necessary to occupy the candidate causal position without admitting candidate performance. This separates reference action from supplier claims.

Fifth, two unlike reference morphologies are necessary. A single spray geometry would turn one implementation into the definition of suppression.

Sixth, the paired dry/wet campaign is the first causal experiment in the ladder. It measures several noncompensatory effects rather than collapsing them into one “suppression success” label.

Seventh, the parent program must grow when the physics grows. The `RAS-3R3` amendment is not administrative optimism; it is the explicit institutional cost of learning source-water interaction without hiding uncertainty.
