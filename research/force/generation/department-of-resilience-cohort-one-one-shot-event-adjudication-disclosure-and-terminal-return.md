## Invalidity requires causal proof

The party seeking facility invalidity carries nine affirmative burdens: timely petition, a named facility cause, temporal priority, an independent trace, a causal counterfactual, candidate non-causation, absence of outcome selection, preservation of the whole record and a bounded remedy. The petition freezes within 72 hours of event close. Unproven or ambiguous cause leaves the event `VALID / UNRESOLVED`, supplies no favorable inference and creates no retry. Proven facility causation may create one new slot and a new warrant; it never edits the old record.

This is the key anti-laundering rule: operational mistakes are not automatically candidate failures, but administrative uncertainty cannot be converted into a favorable null result.
