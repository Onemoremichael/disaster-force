# F36 campus-regeneration capital and industrial exercise constitution

## Executive judgment

Surviving the loss of a proving campus is not the same capability as rebuilding one. Pass 105 proved only that a first campaign can continue through a declared campus denial. Pass 106 showed that the two surviving campuses can absorb the residual campaign in a synthetic conformance case. Neither pass created the capital, people, industrial rights, prepared land, utility plant, metrology or independent acceptance authority required to replace the destroyed institution.

The inherited 180-day regeneration target is therefore rejected as an unfunded calendar assertion. A USD 1.340B installed campus cannot be recreated in 180 days by waiting for a loss, appropriating money, acquiring a site, redesigning its services, recompeting the work and borrowing the staff of the two campuses that must remain operational. The clock has to begin with assets, rights and institutions already in existence.

This pass defines that standing force as the Campus Regeneration System (`CRS-1`). Its selected planning reference is not a spare fourth campus. It is a distributed arsenal capable of becoming an admitted campus after loss: four complete root-coherent campus shipsets; two unlike industrial roots; four separated module arsenals; six prepared but non-operating receiver sites; a 300-person public Campus Regeneration Group (`CRG-1`); 1,400 retained industrial affiliates; independent mobile commissioning and reference systems; controlled technical data and signing roots; and annual partial plus triennial full-chain exercises.

Four complete shipsets are not an arbitrary reserve factor. One shipset at each arsenal is the minimum integer architecture that can lose either industrial root and any one arsenal while retaining one root-coherent complete campus. Treating the inventory as 2.667 divisible campus-equivalents would be cheaper but physically false for singleton services. A forensic line, custody authority, calibration chain or utility island cannot be replaced with a fraction in a financial cell.

The reference architecture carries USD 10.000B of standing capital and USD 0.620B of annual support. Only USD 0.100B of the inherited USD 0.250B national reset stock is recognized as compatible adapters and reference packs. The remaining USD 0.150B of reset stock and all USD 0.210B of controlled program reserve remain protected for operations. Incremental capital authority is therefore USD 9.900B. The twenty-five-year incremental program boundary is USD 25.400B; the gross boundary, including recognized inherited stock, is USD 25.500B. These are constant planning dollars, not an estimate from vendor bids.

The exercise model gives `CRS-1` a 162-day worst declared reconstruction path. The day-180 target carries an eighteen-day management reserve rather than being treated as a point estimate. Eleven failure worlds must close at or before day 162. A second active-campus loss during reconstruction does not become a fictional pass; it triggers a lawful safe stop, protects the surviving range and requires national reprioritization.

This is a planning constitution, not proof that `CRS-1` exists. No receiver site, shipset, industrial root, roster or reference system has been physically accepted. The model supports an architecture-selection and funding decision followed by a cold reconstruction demonstration. It does not admit a rebuilt campus, release production hardware, fix module geometry or release rendering.

## 1. The capability being created

Campus regeneration is a force function, not an insurance transaction. Its public effect is:

> After permanent physical loss of one F36 proving campus, create at a previously non-operating site an independently commissioned replacement that can perform the complete campus bill of service within 180 days, while the two surviving campuses retain their campaign and safety obligations.

The replacement must be able to accept future F36 articles. It is not enough to erect buildings, restart selected stations or recreate the nominal amount of floor area. The effect requires all of the following at the new site:

- closure and restoration exposure;
- representative damage testing;
- sanitary and unknown-agent laboratory work;
- forensic separation of article, range and reference cause;
- calibration, time, geometry and traceability services;
- native evidence storage, replay and two-original custody;
- blind configuration and challenge authority;
- hydraulic, source, receiver, power, waste and decontamination services;
- public safety, admission and release authority; and
- a qualified workforce that was not removed from the two surviving campuses.

The unit of readiness is the admitted bill of service. Buildings, modules, money and people are inputs. None independently counts as a replacement campus.

## 2. The regeneration clock

The 180-day clock begins when the permanent-loss authority authenticates that the campus cannot return through ordinary repair within the target. It does not begin after Congress acts, a contract is signed, land is acquired or debris is cleared.

Five states must remain distinct:

| State | Meaning | Governing response | Counts as regeneration? |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| `DENIED` | the campus exists but is unavailable to the campaign | residual-force and access law | no |
| `REPAIRABLE` | the campus can return inside its registered repair envelope | campus repair formation | no |
| `MODULAR_LANE_LOSS` | one service lane requires replacement modules | national reset inventory | no |
| `PERMANENT_CAMPUS_LOSS` | site or service estate cannot return inside 180 days | activate `CRS-1` | starts the clock |
| `REPLACEMENT_ADMITTED` | an independent board accepts every required service and common chain | transfer to range command | yes |

Cause investigation continues after the clock begins. The loss authority may select a receiver site and isolate suspect roots without waiting for a complete forensic narrative. It may not, however, reuse a suspect root merely to protect schedule.

## 3. The anti-hidden-campus law

Prepositioning can easily become a fourth campus disguised as reserve. That would distort range capacity, staffing, appropriations and readiness claims. `CRS-1` therefore separates stored capability from operating authority.

A dark receiver site may contain completed earthwork, drainage, foundations, anchors, protected corridors, utility interfaces, environmental permits, custody boundaries and surveyed expansion areas. It may not contain admitted F36 stations, maintain a resident test roster, conduct credit-bearing root trials or count toward routine range capacity.

A module arsenal may preserve, rotate and inspect equipment. It may not conduct credit-bearing article tests. The full-scale regeneration exercise range may prove transport, assembly, utilities, calibration transfer and independent commissioning. It may not issue F36 technical credit or become the routine overflow campus.

These restrictions cost money because readiness cannot be financed by quietly using reserve assets for normal production. They also protect the claim: an asset is available for regeneration only if ordinary operations did not consume it.

## 5. Architecture competition

Four architectures were tested against the same loss definition. Cost cannot compensate for failure of the 180-day deadline, complete-service requirement, no-borrow law or root independence.

| Architecture | Standing proposition | Capital, USD B | Annual support, USD B | Worst declared completion | Judgment |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `R0 BESPOKE_REBUILD` | fund and contract after loss | 1.340 event-contingent | 0.025 | 420 days | reject: no standing capacity |
| `R1 CENTRAL_SHIPSET` | one stored shipset and one receiver site | 2.620 | 0.160 | 238 days | reject: central-node and schedule failure |
| `R2 DISTRIBUTED_SINGLE_ROOT` | two shipsets, four sites and distributed storage under one technical root | 4.400 | 0.286 | 176 days | reject: root failure and no 10% schedule reserve |
| `R3 CRS-1` | four root-coherent shipsets, dual roots, four arsenals, six sites and separate formation | 10.000 | 0.620 | 162 days | selected planning reference |

`R2` looks attractive if the sole question is whether nominal reconstruction can occur before day 180. That is the wrong question. It has only four days of deadline margin and cannot recover from a common production-control, technical-data, signing-root or process defect. Its distribution is geographic, not epistemic.

`R3` is selected because it is the first architecture that preserves a complete path after the registered compound cut. Selection means it becomes the architecture to falsify in a physical program. It does not mean its cost, schedule or technology has been observed.

## 6. Why the reserve contains four complete shipsets

Two unlike roots each produce two complete, internally coherent campus shipsets. The four shipsets reside at four separated arsenals. Root A owns ancestry of shipsets `A1` and `A2`; Root B owns `B1` and `B2`. Public interface and evidence standards make both roots admissible, but a replacement campus does not have to mix suspect modules across roots.

The registered compound loss is:

1. either industrial root is denied by defect, compromise, insolvency or evidence failure; and
2. any one arsenal is inaccessible or physically lost.

After Root A is denied, `B1` and `B2` remain. Loss of either B arsenal leaves the other complete B shipset. If the arsenal loss falls on an already denied A shipset, both B shipsets remain. The same logic holds in reverse.

A spreadsheet-level reserve factor of 2.667 campus-equivalents would appear sufficient if every service were continuously divisible among four arsenals. It is not. The campus includes singleton chains and root-coherent systems. Unless a later technology program proves cellular decomposition and cross-root mixing for every service, the inventory must be integer. Four complete shipsets are the minimum conservative first-generation solution.

Every stored item therefore carries:

- root and lot ancestry;
- interface and configuration baseline;
- preservation state and shelf-life clock;
- last exercise and calibration history;
- installed mate and replacement identities;
- two controlled technical-data originals;
- signing and software dependency state; and
- a declared receiver-site compatibility set.

Cannibalization creates an immediate readiness debit. A shipset is complete only when every bill-of-service line and its common utilities, evidence, safety and custody chain are present and qualified.

## 7. Receiver sites are prepared options

Six dark receiver sites create geographic choice without creating six idle campuses. Each site has a different hazard, grid, water, transport, waste and communications posture. At least three remain eligible after the permanent-loss region and its correlated infrastructure are excluded.

The minimum pre-loss site condition includes:

- public control of the land or an irrevocable emergency-use interest;
- completed geotechnical, flood, seismic, fire and contamination envelopes;
- graded pads, drainage, foundations and standardized anchor grids;
- two heavy approaches with surveyed bypass and bridge classifications;
- protected source and receiver corridors that do not assume one water body;
- islandable power, communications and fuel interfaces;
- permitted waste, decontamination and emergency discharge concepts;
- protected evidence and custody zones; and
- an annually renewed environmental and community operating compact.

The site does not remain frozen in time. Each year can change adjacent development, hydrology, routes, utilities, population and permitting assumptions. A receiver whose survey, agreements or hazard envelope expires becomes ineligible and cannot be counted in the six-site portfolio.

## 8. The Campus Regeneration Group

`CRG-1` is a permanent 300-person public formation. It owns mobilization, receiver selection, public safety, technical-data custody, site acceptance, independent commissioning and transfer to the range. Its members are not an emergency roster borrowed from the operating campuses.

| Detachment | Public billets | Primary responsibility |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| command, loss authentication and logistics | 45 | declare the branch, select the site, control configuration and movement |
| civil works and receiver activation | 50 | survey, earthwork, foundations, drainage and custody zones |
| power, hydraulic, thermal and waste utilities | 65 | establish island services and source/receiver safety |
| station assembly and integration | 75 | assemble closure, damage, laboratory and specialist chains |
| metrology, evidence and commissioning | 45 | establish references, native records and independent challenge |
| safety, workforce qualification and transfer | 20 | protect stop authority, rosters and range handover |
| **total** | **300** |  |

The formation is supported by 1,400 retained industrial affiliates distributed across both roots, arsenals, transport providers, civil partners and instrument suppliers. Affiliates perform skilled industrial work. They do not declare public safety, classify evidence, admit a service or accept a campus.

No more than registered liaison teams may come from the two surviving campuses. Those teams remain on their home rosters and cannot be double-counted as reconstruction labor. The no-borrow ledger blocks regeneration if it depends on operating-campus technicians, calibration masters, software keys, spares, heavy movers or utility capacity.

## 9. The 180-day reconstruction campaign

The planning schedule is a dependency network, not a list of simultaneous aspirations. The worst declared case closes by day 162, preserving eighteen days—ten percent of the program target—for unmodeled interaction, weather and evidence rework.

| Work package | Earliest window, days | Terminal artifact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| permanent-loss authentication and receiver selection | 0–6 | signed loss and site branch |
| roster muster, root isolation and shipset release | 0–12 | independent people and item ledger |
| receiver revalidation and custody activation | 4–24 | site release |
| civil reactivation and utility corridor preparation | 8–42 | surveyed pads and corridors |
| distributed transport and identity receipt | 12–52 | reconciled module custody |
| grid-forming power and communications nucleus | 38–72 | island black start |
| hydraulic source, receiver, thermal and waste chains | 44–88 | safe wet utility loop |
| station and laboratory assembly | 58–116 | mechanically complete service estate |
| evidence, metrology, blind and forensic chains | 52–102 | independent reference estate |
| configuration reconciliation and dry integration | 116–134 | signed configuration baseline |
| black-start, wet-reference and containment trials | 134–150 | common-service acceptance packets |
| known-truth article brackets and failure insertion | 148–158 | service-specific evidence packets |
| independent replay, cause challenge and board admission | 156–162 | replacement-campus admission record |

Some packages overlap, but their gates do not. Mechanical completion cannot start a wet trial without utilities. A wet trial cannot admit a station without reference evidence. The admission board cannot infer a complete campus from individually favorable subsystem packets if the common utility, identity or custody chain remains open.

| `W04` | one industrial root denied | unlike-root shipset admitted | day 158 |

The day values are design hypotheses. Physical exercises must replace them with distributions and measured tails. The model's release gate is deliberately stricter than the nominal deadline: any mission-world path above day 162 fails the planning architecture even if it remains below day 180.

## 11. Independent commissioning

A rebuilt station is not accepted by the group that assembled it. Commissioning proceeds through four independent evidence layers.

1. **Identity and configuration.** Every module, reference, key, executable, drawing and interface reconciles to a signed baseline.
2. **Common services.** Power, time, communications, source, receiver, thermal, waste, decontamination and custody survive black start and declared failure insertion.
3. **Known truth.** Portable references and sacrificial known-truth articles test measurement direction, magnitude, timing and cause classification.
4. **Independent replay.** A separate authority reconstructs the decision from native records without the builder's interpretation.

Admission is conjunctive. A campus with nine of ten capital lines installed but no independent reference hierarchy is zero campuses. A complete service whose evidence cannot survive the national network cut is not admitted. Schedule pressure cannot convert a missing original, open safety disposition or indeterminate cause into a provisional pass.

The mobile commissioning estate never depends on the destroyed campus. It has two unlike signing roots, offline configuration records, traveling time and geometry references, sacrificial known-truth sets, clean evidence hardware and two-original storage.

## 14. Technology portfolio created by the requirement

The first-generation design uses conservative integer redundancy, but it should also fund technologies capable of changing the force coefficient after proof.

### 14.1 Cellular service decomposition

Singleton utility, forensic, blind and custody chains drive four complete shipsets. A research program should test whether each can be decomposed into interoperable cells with explicit quorum and failure behavior. The objective is not generic modularity. It is a measured reduction in integer reserve without creating cross-root common cause.

### 14.2 Self-registering physical interfaces

Modules need surveyed structural, hydraulic, power, time, data, waste and custody interfaces that prove their own geometry, seal condition, ancestry and configuration on arrival. Robotic survey and machine-readable physical datums should shorten integration while preserving independent verification.

### 14.3 Portable utility nuclei

The rebuilding force needs grid-forming generation, black-start controls, high-flow hydraulic conditioning, heat rejection, water-source adaptation, waste isolation and clean communications as one composable service. It cannot assume the receiver grid, municipal water, public network or intact fuel distribution.

### 14.4 Traveling metrology and known truth

References must survive the campus they measure. Portable physical standards, known-truth sacrificial articles, offline time distribution, geometry transfer and deterministic replay should be designed as deployable force packages rather than laboratory afterthoughts.

### 14.5 Autonomous site conversion

Receiver sites are prepared, not finished. Robotic earthmoving, autonomous heavy transport, digital terrain reconciliation, rapid foundation finishing and machine-verified utility corridors can compress the civil path without relaxing public safety.

### 14.6 Executable public technical data

Drawings alone do not create a second root. The public package must include process windows, software source, build tooling definitions, calibration methods, test fixtures, failure examples, suppliers, material substitutions and signed reference outputs. Annual challenge builds should prove that a root can execute without the incumbent's private memory.

Technology may reduce the future reserve only after a physical campaign demonstrates the same complete-service, compound-loss and independent-admission result. A prototype interface or fast assembly video does not earn force credit.

## 15. Industrial and economic consequence

`CRS-1` turns reconstruction into a standing industrial demand signal. Two roots must preserve tooling and qualified labor even when no campus has been lost. Four arsenals need preservation engineering, instrumentation, software custody, logistics and inspection. Six receiver communities receive civil preparation and recurring exercises but not an idle production campus.

The industrial policy is deliberately different from emergency contracting. Suppliers are paid to retain executable options, publish interfaces, maintain public technical data, accept destructive challenge and demonstrate substitution. The Department buys the ability to form capacity under loss, not merely equipment at rest.

The exportable product is therefore broader than a modular laboratory or mobile power plant. It is an institutional package: receiver-site standards, cellular utilities, independent commissioning, technical-data custody, exercise methods, roster systems and industrial option contracts. Allied versions could regenerate hospitals, water works, grid nodes, communications hubs and logistics bases without copying the F36 mission.

## 16. Release gates

Planning may advance only when all gates in the relevant layer pass.

### Program authority

- the permanent-loss and 180-day public effect are adopted;
- USD 9.900B incremental capital and USD 0.620B annual support have distinct authority;
- operating reset stock and controlled reserve remain protected;
- `CRG-1` billets and affiliate retainers are constituted; and
- no operating campus receives a readiness debit to make the program appear complete.

### Industrial release

- both roots execute the public technical-data package;
- four root-coherent shipsets reconcile as complete integer inventory;
- each arsenal can load out without another arsenal's unique equipment;
- all six receiver options are current; and
- software, signing, materials and metrology have no undeclared common root.

### Exercise admission

- the selected receiver and shipset are chosen after the no-notice start;
- the reconstruction force meets the no-borrow ledger;
- the exercise includes at least one declared root, arsenal, route, grid, network or workforce cut;
- every service and common chain reaches independent commissioning; and
- the complete evidence packet closes by day 162 in planning and day 180 in execution.

### Campus admission

- every bill-of-service line is complete;
- public safety and evidence authorities have no open disposition;
- native records replay independently;
- known-truth failures are detected and correctly classified;
- the campus survives black start and island operation; and
- the receiving range command accepts custody without relying on temporary borrowed authority.

Failure of one conjunctive gate blocks admission. A weighted average cannot compensate for a missing campus service.

## 17. What this pass establishes

### Established by derivation

- one complete campus has USD 1.340B of installed local estate;
- ordinary repair, campaign denial and physical regeneration are different states;
- an 180-day clock must start from pre-loss standing capacity;
- four root-coherent integer shipsets are required to survive one root plus any one arsenal loss without assuming divisible singleton services;
- dark sites, arsenals and the exercise range cannot count as routine campus capacity;
- a separate public formation and independent acceptance chain are necessary to obey the no-borrow law; and
- the selected planning architecture needs an eighteen-day management reserve inside the 180-day target.

### Provisional design hypotheses

- six receiver sites, four arsenals, 300 public billets and 1,400 affiliates are sufficient;
- the work-package network can close the worst declared mission world by day 162;
- the capital boundary is USD 10.000B and annual support is USD 0.620B;
- USD 0.100B of inherited reset inventory is genuinely compatible; and
- the eleven registered mission worlds cover the first-order reconstruction problem.

### Still held

- live campus-loss frequency and expected-value benefit;
- site selection, community consent and environmental acceptance;
- root qualification and public technical-data completeness;
- actual transport, assembly, commissioning and rework distributions;
- cross-root modular mixing or any reduction below four complete shipsets;
- physical campus admission, F36 root credit and production; and
- exterior form, internal arrangement and rendering.

## 18. Next decisive experiment

The next pass should not draw the campus or optimize individual modules. It should compile a no-notice cold reconstruction exercise from immutable items, people, routes, receiver constraints and acceptance events.

That exercise should:

1. choose the denied root, lost arsenal and receiver only after the start;
2. force the two surviving campuses to continue their registered workload;
3. reconcile every moved item and worker against the no-borrow ledger;
4. execute island utilities without the public grid or national network;
5. insert known faults into configuration, geometry, calibration and evidence;
6. require independent replay and cause classification;
7. record the full distribution of rework and queue time; and
8. reject the architecture if the complete packet closes after day 180 or its modeled P90 cannot preserve the eighteen-day reserve.

Only after representative module interfaces and service sections survive that campaign should the project consider form studies or renderings.

## 19. Workbook verification

The companion workbook makes the architecture competition, integer reserve rule, receiver posture, work-package dependencies, failure worlds, no-borrow ledger, workforce, commissioning, capital, support and release gates explicit.
