# F36 `CISA-40` full-scale subarticle and controlled-defect execution campaign

## Executive judgment

`CRET-1` defined how a `CISA-40` object becomes legally constructible. It did not define a campaign capable of learning from physical failure. Pass 114 creates `SDEC-1`: the first executable full-scale subarticle and controlled-defect campaign beneath the construction constitution. Its purpose is not to accumulate successful demonstrations. It is to identify the coupled transfer functions, unsafe states, detection limits and root-specific failure mechanisms that must be known before either unlike industrial root may construct a complete article.

The central correction is that twelve subarticle *designs* cannot mean twelve physical specimens. One specimen per family and root would confound article variation, fixture behavior, operator effect, instrument effect and damage caused by the test itself. `SDEC-1` therefore realizes the twelve inherited designs as sixty-four serialized full-scale specimens: eight support/reaction nodes, eight anchor/lift nodes, sixteen hydraulic collars, twelve bus/protection assemblies, eight thermal/evidence assemblies and twelve four-station `EBC-1650` racks. The campaign prescribes 40,520 full-scale events. These are screening and mechanism-identification events, not service-life qualification.

The controlled-defect population is likewise no longer a shelf of seventy-two illustrative standards. It is a preregistered inference experiment. Twenty-four root–method–defect-family cells each receive twenty-nine independent at-threshold positives, twenty-nine above-threshold positives, twenty-nine below-threshold negatives and twenty-nine clean controls. That creates 2,784 unique blinded primary presentations and 2,784 non-independent repeat acquisitions used only to estimate operator, instrument and access effects. Zero misses in an at-threshold cell gives a one-sided 95-percent lower detection bound of 90.19 percent. Zero false rejects across the fifty-eight negative presentations gives a one-sided 95-percent upper false-reject bound of 5.04 percent. No result may be pooled across unlike roots or defect families to rescue a failed cell.

The `EBC-1650`, collar and bus/protection families lead. The inherited 0.005-tonne custody discrepancy is measurable only if the combined meter-and-weighing expanded uncertainty is no greater than 0.00125 tonne. The ±0.010-tonne cassette band and ±0.020-tonne cross-cassette band remain discriminating only if fixture cross-talk, thermal zero drift, pitch/roll sensitivity and fluid-line inventory are independently identified before each trial block. The inherited 2,000 collar cycles, ten bus-fault shots, 500 thermal cycles and 100 energy–ballast cycles become per-specimen screening exposures. Ten fault shots on one root article would be weak; ten shots on each of six separately serialized assemblies provides sixty root observations distributed across source state, fault type and location.

This fidelity exposes a financial contradiction rather than concealing it. The USD 0.016B capital and USD 0.003B/year left inside `BRD-1` cannot produce credible full-scale fixtures, fault containment, specimens, metrology, blind standards or independent custody. The proposed campaign requires an incremental planning boundary of USD 0.430B capital and USD 0.098B/year for thirty months, or USD 0.675B of capital plus operating draw over that interval. It therefore triggers an `N4` baseline consequence. A revised `BRD-2` would rise from USD 0.318B to USD 0.748B capital and from USD 0.084B/year to USD 0.182B/year. This is a research allocation, not a quote, appropriation or additive Department claim. Execution remains unauthorized until that reallocation is reconciled within `CISA-1` and the facilities are commissioned.

The larger institutional lesson is exacting. A Department of Resilience cannot create a first-principles industrial base by treating testing as a late compliance service. It needs public ranges for water, structure, electric fault, heat, hazardous fluids and evidence; independent truth custody; destructive forensics; statistical authority; and two unlike industrial lanes with no shared decisive measurement ancestor. The test enterprise is part of force structure.

No physical observations are introduced in this pass. Sixty-four planning gates close; thirty-two source-dependent gates remain open and four downstream gates remain blocked. No complete article, `CRC-E01` admission, production or exterior-rendering authority is created.

## 1. Decision, claim and boundary

### 1.1 Decision

This pass asks:

> What physical population, stimulus history, measurement system, stopping law and inference rule would allow the public authority to decide whether the twelve `CISA-40` subarticle designs are fit to proceed into complete-article construction?

The answer must do five things at once:

1. separate specimen behavior from fixture behavior;
2. expose unlike-root mechanisms under common public service conditions;
3. demonstrate that the measurement system can discriminate the proposed acceptance bands;
4. preserve failure evidence before repair or teardown changes it; and
5. prevent successful nominal events from overwhelming rare but governing failures.

### 1.2 Claim class

`SDEC-1` may support only three claim classes:

- **measurement-capable:** the instrument chain resolves a public decision interval with the assigned uncertainty;
- **mechanism-bounded:** the tested specimen population and challenge matrix bound the named failure mechanism within the tested domain; and
- **screen-passed:** no automatic-stop condition occurred in the prescribed screening exposure.

It cannot support fleet reliability, service life, production yield, universal environmental qualification, maintainability in deployment or complete-article acceptance. Passing 2,000 collar cycles means only that the serialized collar completed the registered screen. It does not mean that the collar has a 2,000-cycle life, much less a life beyond the screen.

### 1.3 What remains fixed

The public envelope remains 12.0 × 3.6 × 3.8 metres. Configured mass remains 40.000 tonnes. Named dry hardware remains targeted at 30.900 tonnes with a separate 1.100-tonne manufactured-mass allowance. Root A remains the welded high-strength-steel, super-duplex, copper-bus, liquid-fuel and LFP lineage. Root B remains the CF-PAEK shell-grid, composite hydraulic, aluminum-bus, pressure-DME and zinc-halogen lineage. The public support, anchor, lift, hydraulic, electrical, receiver and evidence interfaces remain those inherited through `CISA-40`.

The extra collar specimens exist because pressure-boundary fatigue, sanitation and sectioning compete for the same physical state. The extra bus and energy–ballast specimens separate destructive fault or barrier outcomes from subsequent trials. Four structural and thermal specimens per root provide nominal, environmental-edge, deliberately damaged and retained-reference roles. Serial roles are randomized after acceptance into the article bank; the best-looking specimen cannot be selected for the hardest or most visible trial.

Each serial inherits its own material lots, adjacent coupons, construction events, calibration brackets, storage history and teardown allocation. A repair creates a new configuration state; it does not restore the specimen's statistical identity. A replacement specimen comes from a predeclared reserve lot and does not erase the failed event.

## 3. Common trial grammar

Every event is compiled from twelve fields before the specimen enters the range:

1. serialized specimen and configuration state;
2. trial ID and preregistered purpose;
3. precondition history;
4. commanded stimulus waveform with units and time base;
5. fixture state and boundary-identification result;
6. channel set, range, sample rate and expanded uncertainty;
7. synchronized independent reference;
8. automatic safe-state predicates and actuators;
9. valid/invalid rules;
10. pre- and post-inspection sequence;
11. raw, derived and custody objects; and
12. admission, quarantine or destructive next state.

The event controller and evidence recorder are separate systems. A controller may command a load or valve. It cannot certify that the stimulus occurred. The independent reference observes the achieved stimulus from a separate clock, power source and sensor chain. Where a hazardous event destroys the primary controller, the evidence recorder must remain readable and export both originals.

### 3.1 Trial blocking

Events are grouped into blocks small enough to bracket zero, calibration and fixture state. No block exceeds twenty-five energy–ballast cycles, 250 collar cycles, fifty structural load blocks, five electrical fault shots or 125 thermal cycles. A pre-block and post-block reference check surrounds each block. Drift outside the registered bracket invalidates the affected observations but remains a reportable campaign failure.

### 3.2 Order law

Within safety constraints, nominal and challenge cases are randomized. The public randomization authority commits the assignment hash before the builder or operator receives the schedule. Destructive challenges occur after all intact observations that they could alter. The order is never rearranged because early results make a more favorable sequence desirable.

## 5. `SA6`: `EBC-1650` execution campaign

### 5.1 Physical arrangement

Each of twelve full-scale racks carries four production-scale cassettes at the inherited longitudinal stations. A complete rack is required because the public question is not merely whether one tank can be weighed. It is whether fuel removal, water replacement, structural reaction and center-of-gravity control remain distinguishable across four coupled stations.

Root A uses the double-wall liquid-fuel bladder and separately contained recovered-water chamber. Root B uses the pressure-rated DME cylinder and structurally separate annular water chamber. Neither lineage may use a common fuel–water wall. All replacement water is quarantined process inventory.

### 5.2 One-hundred-cycle history per serial

| clean custody | 20 | proportional four-station draw and same-station water replacement |
| load/rate steps | 16 | 0.6, 1.5, 2.25 and 3.0 MW-equivalent states, including ten-second transients |

Root A's nominal full-output replacement rate remains 0.333674 tonne/hour; Root B's remains 0.450938 tonne/hour. Eight-hour consumption is 2.669395 and 3.607504 tonnes respectively. The four-station controller therefore has to resolve local replacement without hiding a system-level mass balance.

### 5.3 Decision intervals and instruments

Each cassette is measured over 0–2.0 tonnes with expanded uncertainty no greater than 0.0010 tonne at 95-percent coverage and a ten-hertz recorded rate. Fuel and water mass-flow references cover 0–0.20 kg/s per station with expanded uncertainty no greater than 0.25 percent of reading plus 0.010 kg. Interstitial pressure covers -10 to 300 kPa with 0.5-kPa expanded uncertainty at ten hertz. Root B fuel pressure covers 0–2 MPa with 5-kPa expanded uncertainty. Four fixture reactions are measured independently to reconstruct total mass and longitudinal/lateral balance.

The 0.005-tonne custody rule is admitted as measurable only if the combined expanded uncertainty of the meter-derived and weighing-derived discrepancy is no greater than 0.00125 tonne, providing a four-to-one test-uncertainty ratio. The ±0.010-tonne local band and ±0.020-tonne cross-cassette band require the same ratio after orientation and fixture corrections. A numerical controller claim without that measurement capability is rejected.

### 5.4 Safe state and admission

Any uncommanded mass gain, custody discrepancy above 0.005 tonne, local error beyond ±0.010 tonne, cross-cassette difference beyond ±0.020 tonne, interstitial alarm, incompatible lineup, lost independent weighing or unidentified fixture state commands isolation. Fuel and water valves close independently, transfer stops, each fluid remains captured in its own boundary, and generation enters the predeclared controlled shutdown. No process water is released to a potable path.

The family passes screening only if every clean or nominal valid event stays within its applicable band; every seeded above-threshold anomaly is detected; no clean case produces an unsafe command; safe isolation completes within its registered time; both evidence originals survive; and pre/post mass conservation reconciles. Invalid events cannot be relabeled successful. They consume the specimen's exposure and invoke the registered replacement rule.

## 6. `SA3`: hydraulic collar campaign

### 6.1 Coupled history

Every collar serial receives 2,000 registered cycles. The exposure is screening, not a life claim:

- 1,800 working cycles ramp from zero to 0.70 MPa, hold sixty seconds, return and inspect leakage;
- 160 surge cycles add a five-second 0.85-MPa plateau under combined bending;
- twenty vacuum cycles reach -0.085 MPa for sixty seconds;
- twenty proof cycles reach 1.05 MPa after each major block and before destructive allocation.

Mechanical states superimpose 0, 75 and 125 kN axial tension/compression; 0, 75 and 125 kN·m bending; and 0, 20 and 35 kN·m torsion in a blocked factorial schedule. These values are challenge hypotheses inherited into the test design, not admitted allowables. Product-fluid temperatures are 2°C, 20°C and 50°C. Ambient states are 0°C dry, 20°C controlled, 45°C hot and a wet-contaminated exterior. Line compliance and dead volume vary as defined in the fixture experiment.

Sanitary challenges use a declared conductive/fluorescent marker, calibrated inert microspheres and a bounded organic-residue surrogate. They test cleanability, trapped volume, seal bypass and sampling recovery. They do not establish potability or microbiological safety. Sanitary samples precede pressure dye, disassembly and cutting.

### 6.2 Measurement and stop law

Pressure is recorded over -0.10 to 1.20 MPa at 200 hertz with expanded uncertainty no greater than 0.001 MPa. Leakage is collected independently over 0–1.0 L/hour with expanded uncertainty no greater than 0.02 L/hour. Six-axis loads, collar displacement, bolt or tie state, acoustic emission, temperature, strain and high-resolution before/after geometry share a registered coordinate frame.

A pressure excursion, gross leak, unstable damage signal, axial slip above 0.25 mm, permanent bore change above 0.10 mm, loss of seal compression, contamination-marker bypass, failed fixture bracket or evidence-path loss stops the event. Both sides isolate, fluid dumps to a contained tank, actuators unload into mechanical catches and the collar remains untouched until the public evidence authority releases inspection.

Admission requires zero automatic stops without a root-cause disposition, no progressive leakage or residual deformation trend across registered blocks, successful detection of all seeded governing damage, complete sanitary recovery accounting and intact post-block NDE. Root A and Root B are admitted separately.

## 7. `SA4`: bus, protection and evidence-survival campaign

### 7.1 Why ten shots becomes sixty observations per root

Each root uses six independently serialized bus/protection assemblies. Each receives ten fault shots. The sixty root observations are blocked across source state, fault type and location:

- prospective source states are 4, 8 and 12 kA for Root A, and 2.2, 4.41 and 6 kA for Root B;
- faults include phase-to-ground, phase-to-phase, three-phase and bounded high-impedance cases;
- locations include source bus, tie, critical feeder and noncritical feeder; and
- preconditions include 1.5-MW equivalent steady service, 2.25-MW ten-minute overload and a 3.0-MW ten-second transient.

Challenge source states are experiments, not service ratings. Shot order is randomized within the constraints imposed by enclosure inspection and expendable fault elements. After any arc or containment event, no subsequent shot occurs until geometry, insulation, contact resistance, debris and evidence-system state are dispositioned.

### 7.2 Instrumentation and safe state

Current channels cover 0–20 kA at 250 ksample/s with expanded uncertainty no greater than one percent of reading. Voltage channels cover 0–1 kV at 250 ksample/s with 0.5-percent expanded uncertainty. Protection contacts, controller commands and independent breaker witnesses use a shared sub-millisecond time reference. Contact temperature covers -20°C to 250°C; enclosure pressure covers 0–50 kPa. High-speed imagery is secondary evidence and cannot replace electrical quantities.

The primary protective device must clear the registered fault without loss of upstream selectivity. A failed clear, source-envelope exceedance, enclosure breach, sustained arc, evidence-power loss or time-reference disagreement commands upstream isolation. Stored DC energy must fall below the registered safe-touch condition within sixty seconds; the planning reference is 60 V. Independent evidence power remains live long enough to seal both originals. Restart is a separate, witnessed event and cannot erase the fault state.

Admission requires every seeded fault to be detected and cleared by the intended protection layer, no unintended upstream trip in the selectivity cases, no breach of the containment boundary, surviving independent evidence and successful restart only when the post-fault configuration is released. The campaign estimates response variation; it does not infer a fleet fault rate from 120 shots.

## 8. Structural and thermal families

### 8.1 `SA1` support/reaction node

Each serial receives 200 combined load blocks spanning 0–150 kN vertical working load, a 200-kN proof challenge, ±50 kN lateral force and ±75 kN·m moment. The rig presents 5, 25 and 100 MN/m vertical foundation stiffness, 0, 150 and 300 mm one-corner settlement, level and five-degree slope, and nominal plus one-support-relieved cases. Loads are not allowables; they are registered hypotheses tied to the preceding interface envelope.

Six-axis reactions, 3-D displacement, strain, pin/fastener state and residual geometry are observed. The family stops for unstable stiffness loss, fastener/pin migration, crack or delamination growth, permanent datum shift above 0.5 mm, fixture nonlinearity or lost load-path evidence. Servo load removes into mechanical capture without forcing the specimen through the failed region.

### 8.2 `SA2` anchor/lift node

Each serial receives 200 load blocks combining axial, transverse and moment components through three registered angles. The anchor challenge reaches the inherited 156.757-kN vector with a 200-kN proof boundary. Lift states exercise the article-owned lifting interface through symmetric, one-node-lag, capture and emergency-lower cases. Hinge, pin, insert, weld, bond and post-tension transfer are mapped before and after each fifty-block bracket.

A load redistribution above ten percent, slip above 0.5 mm, uncontrolled articulation, capture failure or progressive damage stops the event. The system arrests on redundant mechanical supports before power is removed.

### 8.3 `SA5` thermal/evidence assembly

Each serial receives 500 deploy–heat–isolate–stow cycles. Root A is tested against the 2.8-MW/64-m² article reference, equivalent to 43.75 kW/m². Root B is tested against 2.4 MW/56 m², or 42.86 kW/m². The subarticle loop applies 0, 50, 100 and 125 percent of the root reference heat flux with sink temperatures of 0°C, 20°C and 45°C; clean, dust-loaded and driven-rain exterior states; nominal and partly obstructed airflow; and nominal, chafed and one-channel-open evidence routes.

Coolant flow, inlet/outlet temperature, heat flux, panel strain, hinge load, leak collection, cable continuity and evidence-clock agreement are measured. A leak, runaway temperature, actuator asymmetry, obstruction contact, evidence-path common-mode loss or heat-balance failure stops heat input, isolates the affected segment, captures coolant and parks the panel in the registered safe geometry.

The heat balance must close within five percent after assigned uncertainty. All 500 cycles must complete without an unresolved progressive trend. Damage cases must be detected without using the controller's own status as the decisive witness.

## 9. Controlled-defect population and blinding

### 9.1 Twenty-four independent admission cells

The inherited seventy-two standards are design exemplars. `SDEC-1` converts them into twenty-four admission cells: twelve decisive defect-method families in Root A and twelve in Root B. Each cell has four truth strata:

| Truth stratum | Independent presentations per cell | Decision use |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| at threshold | 29 | primary probability-of-detection screen |
| above threshold | 29 | gross-miss and procedure-function screen |
| below threshold | 29 | false-reject and threshold-discrimination screen |
| clean control | 29 | false-reject and handling-bias screen |
| **per cell** | **116** |  |
| **twenty-four cells** | **2,784** | unique primary presentations |

The threshold is a registered physical dimension or severity for each method and access state. “At threshold” cannot be selected after seeing the instrument response. Natural flaws may supplement the population but cannot replace truth-characterized seeded specimens unless destructive truth establishes the same dimension and ancestry.

### 9.2 Independence and repeatability

An independent presentation is a separately fabricated truth object, not another scan of the same object. Four qualified operators each receive twenty-nine primary presentations per cell. Three instrument serials and three access states are assigned through a balanced incomplete block. Because twenty-nine cannot populate every operator–instrument–access combination independently, `SDEC-1` admits the aggregate procedure cell, not each operator as a separately qualified population.

Every unique object also receives one secondary blinded acquisition under a different operator, instrument or access state. Those 2,784 repeat acquisitions estimate repeatability, reproducibility and access interactions. They never enlarge the independent primary denominator.

### 9.3 Truth custody and randomization

The truth laboratory fabricates and measures the population. The randomization authority assigns opaque IDs and commits the ordered truth-file hash before presentation. Builders see fabrication instructions only for their own objects. Operators see neither truth stratum nor expected counts within a block. Clean controls undergo sham preparation so surface handling does not reveal class. The public statistician receives decisions before the truth vault opens.

Compromise of one object's blind invalidates that primary presentation and activates a preassigned replacement; compromise of a block or assignment key quarantines the entire block. Replacement IDs and reserve order are frozen in advance. A favorable result cannot choose its own replacement rule.

### 9.4 Statistical admission

For a cell with twenty-nine at-threshold positives and zero misses, the exact one-sided 95-percent lower bound on detection probability is:

`pL = 0.05^(1/29) = 0.90185`

The same zero-miss rule applies to the twenty-nine above-threshold positives. The fifty-eight below-threshold and clean-negative presentations jointly bound false reject. With zero false rejects:

`pU = 1 − 0.05^(1/58) = 0.05034`

A cell is admitted only with zero at-threshold misses, zero above-threshold misses, zero false rejects across both negative strata, complete truth metrology, no blind compromise, valid pre/post checks and combined expanded uncertainty no greater than one quarter of the physical decision interval. One failure rejects the cell and requires method redesign or a new, fully independent population. Results cannot be pooled across roots, defect families or threshold revisions.

Secondary acquisitions require at least 0.90 raw agreement and a preregistered chance-corrected agreement statistic of at least 0.80, with uncertainty reported. These are reproducibility screens, not substitutes for the primary exact-binomial rule.

## 11. Pre-inspection, invalid trials and post-inspection

### 11.1 Pre-event sequence

Before every block, the authority verifies identity and configuration; inspects the specimen and safety envelope; confirms material, repair and storage history; surveys datum and fixture zero; performs independent reference checks; proves interlocks and safe-state actuators without the hazardous stimulus; verifies both evidence originals and clocks; and seals the event definition and randomization assignment.

### 11.2 Invalidity is an outcome

An event is invalid for the intended inference if the stimulus was not preregistered, the fixture boundary is unidentified, a required channel saturates, a calibration bracket fails, clock error exceeds allocation, truth is unblinded, the specimen entered in the wrong configuration, the safe-state system was unavailable, native evidence is incomplete or an unauthorized repair occurred. Invalidity does not turn a hazardous or failed observation into “no test.” Safety and causal evidence remain reportable. The consumed exposure and article state remain in the ledger.

### 11.3 Post-event sequence

The specimen remains in place until command and independent stimulus records reconcile. The authority then records visible condition, zero return, residual geometry, leak or insulation state, NDE map, fluid/sample custody, protection/contact state and calibration closure. Original evidence objects are sealed before repair, disassembly or destructive work. The decision authority, not the operator, assigns retain, continue, quarantine, repair-study or destruct state.

## 12. Data volume and custody

Full-scale high-rate and continuous channels produce approximately 1.166 TB of raw numerical data under the registered channel/rate/duration assumptions. The 5,568 primary and repeat NDE acquisitions are allocated 8 GB each, or 44.544 TB. Spatial surveys, high-speed imagery, thermal imagery and audio/acoustic records add a 6.0-TB planning allowance. Raw campaign evidence is therefore about 51.71 TB.

Derived objects receive a 25-percent allowance and package/index overhead receives six percent. Two separately powered originals produce a planning custody requirement of approximately 137.0 TB before long-term replication and off-site escrow. Derived products never replace raw acquisitions. Every object carries specimen, event, channel, unit, calibration, clock, coordinate-system, missing-data and software/hash identity.

The public copy uses documented, license-independent formats for the decision quantities. Builder-native objects may accompany it. They cannot be the only readable record. A summary plot is not evidence custody.

## 13. Destructive order

The inherited sixty cut locations are allocated before results: five locations in each of the twelve root–family cells. A cut location is not necessarily a separate specimen. Forty-eight are the four minimum cuts per cell; twelve are governing fifth cuts reserved for the highest-consequence interface in each cell.

The universal sequence is:

1. freeze event state and both evidence originals;
2. complete intact geometry, leak, electrical, thermal and NDE observations;
3. remove energy and fluids into controlled custody;
4. take sanitary and contamination samples before cutting media are introduced;
5. expose a sacrificial perimeter without intersecting the question surface;
6. image and measure the intact interface in the exposed state;
7. take the registered governing section;
8. split mirror evidence into public and root/laboratory custody; and
9. reconcile kerf, debris, retained pieces and coordinate transforms.

Bus contacts are measured for preload, resistance and thermal state before disassembly. Composite volumes are registered in three dimensions before sectioning. Weld indications and metallographic planes share the same article coordinate system. Energy–ballast barriers are leak-tested and sampled before fluids are removed. No cut proceeds if it would destroy the only surviving reference.

The decisive capability is public, permanent and reusable. Builders may operate their own root-native processes, but they do not own the only fault source, pressure range, truth library, clock, raw evidence store or destructive laboratory. A mature Department of Resilience needs these institutions for the same reason a serious aerospace state needs wind tunnels, ranges and flight-test organizations: novel public capability cannot be procured intelligently when the buyer cannot generate or interpret physical evidence.

## 17. Release architecture

`SDEC-1` contains one hundred gates:

## 18. Falsification and automatic stop

The campaign or affected cell is stopped if:

- a specimen, truth object, lot, configuration or assignment identity does not reconcile;
- a fixture boundary cannot be identified within its allocated contribution;
- combined expanded uncertainty exceeds one quarter of the decision interval;
- a required channel saturates, loses time alignment or fails its calibration bracket;
- the achieved stimulus leaves the registered safe envelope;
- a safe-state actuator fails its rehearsal or hazardous event command;
- fuel and water lose independent containment or custody;
- a collar leaks, slips, deforms or transfers contamination outside its registered rule;
- protection fails to detect, selectively clear or contain a seeded fault;
- structural stiffness or geometry changes progressively without disposition;
- thermal balance cannot be reconciled or evidence loses independent power;
- an at-threshold or above-threshold defect is missed;
- a below-threshold or clean object is falsely rejected;
- truth is revealed before primary decisions are sealed;
- repeated acquisitions are counted as independent defects;
- results are pooled across roots or families to pass a failed cell;
- original evidence is overwritten by a repair, rerun or derived file;
- a destructive cut precedes the intact observation it can alter;
- execution begins inside the USD 0.016B/0.003B inherited remainder without baseline reopening; or
- full-article, production or rendering authority is inferred from this campaign design.
