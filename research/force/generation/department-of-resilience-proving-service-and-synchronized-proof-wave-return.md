## Decision: create the Resilience Proving Service

Four institutional alternatives were tested against eight conjunctive gates. Root-house test departments fail sovereign truth, adverse publication and budget independence. A single mega-range creates a national bottleneck and cannot cold-reproduce itself. A contract-lab federation offers capacity but leaves custody and disposition purchasable. Only a **Resilience Proving Service** passes all eight.

The Service is a permanent public operating institution inside the Department of Resilience, coequal with the mission-system commands but barred from owning candidate designs. It receives nine exclusive warrants:

1. Mission System Commands freeze the public-service denominator and four-root cohort.
2. Independent Root Houses design candidates and disclose complete ancestry and technical data.
3. Worlds Command creates decisive denial states and operates challenge rigs.
4. Counterfactual Command operates matched controls and quantifies background burden.
5. Reconstruction Command cold-builds and replays candidates from public technical data.
6. Standards and Custody Command owns instruments, uncertainty, samples, clocks and the evidence vault.
7. The Public Protection Inspectorate owns the independent safety, rights and intervention stop.
8. The Root Disposition Board alone applies `HOLD`, `RESIZE`, `KILL` and `BUILD PROOF`.
9. The Program Comptroller releases stage authority and returns unused balances.

No actor may design a candidate, create its evidence, judge its result and release its money. Thirty-two non-collapsible separations govern beneficial control, executive command, decisive tooling and evidence custody. The offices are constitutionally distinct, but every implementation identity remains source-empty.

The five non-lifeline mission systems each receive three system-native stations: challenge, matched control and cold reconstruction. Every station contains four isolated campaign lanes, one for each root, plus one regeneration lane. The national reference is therefore:

The three station classes must not share beneficial control, executive command, decisive toolchains or evidence custody. Cold reconstruction uses only public technical data and labor outside the candidate’s craft tradition.

These are functional stations, not selected sites. A theater-entry challenge station must reproduce port denial, receiver loss, terrain fracture and custody break. A civil-terrain station must create occupied-building, utility, rotor, facade and access casualties. Hazard control requires full-scale fire, flood, debris, contaminant and aftershock worlds. Human continuity requires relationship-bearing care, labor, identity and receiver failure. Regeneration requires material ancestry, qualification, residual and disconnected-production truth systems.

## Authority and industrial economy

The USD 43.0B early ceiling is cross-classified by institutional jurisdiction: USD 17.2B for candidate-system articles and technical data, USD 12.9B for proving stations, USD 8.60B for metrology and public custody, and USD 4.30B for replacement and regeneration reserve. These are reservations, not cost estimates or added appropriations.

Authority remains staged: 25 percent for method and station qualification, 50 percent for paired challenge/control, and 25 percent for cold reconstruction and final disposition. The jurisdiction mix changes by stage so no root house controls the majority of truth infrastructure. No accepted warrant, commissioned station, qualified lane, conflict record or stage gate currently exists; released authority remains zero.

This arrangement creates an industrial sector distinct from platform production: programmable casualty fields, receiver emulators, full-scale counterfactual environments, distributed force and fluid metrology, relationship-state instruments, cold digital-to-physical build cells, adversarial evidence vaults, disconnected causal clocks and regenerable calibration roots. The Service drives these technologies because ordinary commercial testing has no reason to build public truth systems capable of killing its largest customer’s product line.
