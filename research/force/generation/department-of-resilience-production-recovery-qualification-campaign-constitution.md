## Frontier and resolution

The deepest live branch is `MPC-3`, the third of five permitted market-and-production passes; `MPC-2` fixed the recovery service but did not define how any route could earn credit.

**Resolution test:** reversing this campaign would change whether the Department accepts contractor demonstrations, attempts every possible joint combination, or requires independent complete-unit evidence across every private and public route before national recovery capacity exists, so the object passes at Department scale.

## Decision: qualify routes, then the lattice

Five architectures face twelve noncompensatory gates. Contractor declaration fails eleven. One demonstration per route fails clean counterfactual, compound denial, cold replay, cell qualification, concurrency and adverse permanence. Complete route triads without joint execution fail six-system concurrency. Testing all 4,096 cohorts passes the evidence gates but breaches the fiscal boundary. Only **complete route triads plus eight balanced joint rotations** passes all twelve.

The campaign qualifies production as a service, not a factory. Every one of seventy-two routes—twenty-four private survivor networks and forty-eight public cold paths—must complete three year-long events:

- `C0` freezes a nominal production counterfactual.
- `D1` imposes the route’s governing compound loss. A private route loses its largest site, decisive supplier and 25.0 percent of qualified workers. A public route loses the Root House, its alternate cell, decisive supplier, largest bay and 25.0 percent of workers.
- `R1` gives an outside-origin team only the signed public rights, tooling, material, metrology and process record. Tacit incumbent assistance invalidates the event.

Each private event must demonstrate black-start in seventy-two hours, a critical lot in thirty days, one complete unit in 120 days and three by day 365. Each public event owes 168 hours, sixty days, 240 days and two units by day 365. A partial assembly, accepted subsystem or tonne of output earns zero units.[^1]

## The joint test is finite but complete

The route triads create 216 valid events. Eight joint runs then add forty-eight public-path activations. Four `P1` cohorts run in the first wave and four `P2` cohorts in the second. Every public path appears exactly once; every cell operates once in each wave; every root appears twice; and the 358 kt governing cohort runs once through each path set.

This does not pretend to observe all 4,096 root combinations. It tests every route, both roles of every cell and the maximum mass-and-rate envelope. Compositional credit for the unbuilt combinations is legal only while route, site, operator, supplier, utility, workforce, tooling, transport and receiver independence remain accepted. One shared decisive identity removes every dependent combination.

## Evidence and decision law

Each of 264 activations requires ten packages: authority, configuration, independence, workforce, tooling and material, utility, process and metrology, clock and output, complete-unit conformity, and receiver–residual–regeneration closure. Eight cohorts add twelve joint packages each. The evidence estate therefore contains 2,736 packages.

Five separately signed judgments govern every activation: validity, configuration, clock and throughput, complete-service acceptance, and reliance on adverse evidence. That creates 1,320 judgments. Seventy-two route, twenty-four cell, twenty-four root, six system and one national dispositions add 127 serial decisions. No majority vote, average score or successful sibling can close a missing dependency.[^2]

Only integrity invalidity permits exact-event replacement. A 20.0-percent planning reserve creates fifty-three dormant attempts. A valid adverse result remains permanently in the denominator; a terminal safety, rights or ancestry breach kills the lineage.

## Physical and fiscal scale

Because an event must prove the full year-one promise, the campaign completes 600 units: 216 through private triads, 288 through public triads and ninety-six through joint rotations. Their inherited bills turn 27.9 Mt of controlled dry configuration through the estate. This is an industrial mobilization program, not a laboratory exercise.

The ten-year plan reserves the existing USD 200B proof account and USD 230B initial-formation account, totaling USD 430B without adding authority.[^3] USD 280B is assigned to direct route and cohort production; USD 150B governs evidence, denial, restoration, replenishment, recycling, invalidity, command and renewal. Accepted retained units transfer at carried value and reduce formation authority; they are not purchased twice. The USD 250B productive estate supplies titled sites, bays, tooling and reference capacity but is not added to the campaign envelope.[^4]

At the reference direct-cost case the envelope has zero headroom. A 25.0-percent direct-cost shock creates a USD 70.0B deficit. The lawful response is circular production, lower retained-output cost or redesign—not automatic appropriation.

Fourteen technology programs become force requirements: adaptive heavy-production bays, self-restoring metrology, live material genealogy, portable credential twins, qualification-aware process compilers, robotic disassembly, autonomous yard logistics, distributed utility black-start, blind defect insertion, evidence-preserving production twins, large-article modular joining, receiver surrogates, rapid tool regeneration and six-root resource scheduling. Their fatal criteria are campaign failures, not modernization delays.
