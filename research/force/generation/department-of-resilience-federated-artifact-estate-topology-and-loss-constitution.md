## The topology

Each of the six roots occupies three sites in three different failure domains. Each failure domain hosts three different roots and never contains a complete root.

| Root | Site-position failure domains |
| --- | --- |
| `ET-A` | `FD-A`, `FD-C`, `FD-E` |
| `CT-C` | `FD-B`, `FD-D`, `FD-F` |
| `HC-A` | `FD-A`, `FD-D`, `FD-E` |
| `LF-B` | `FD-B`, `FD-C`, `FD-F` |
| `HU-A` | `FD-A`, `FD-D`, `FD-F` |
| `RT-B` | `FD-B`, `FD-C`, `FD-E` |

Every root receives six role enclaves. Position 1 carries active Native replay and standby Divergence; position 2 carries active Clean reconstruction and standby Native; position 3 carries active Divergence and standby Clean. Thus Native, Clean and Divergence each have two separately sited paths. Standby activation transfers a bounded authority; it does not transfer personnel, keys or beneficial control.

Every Root Artifact Court has one seat at each root site. Two of three seats plus a Public Artifact Exchange witness may issue an Artifact Validity Warrant. No national office owns a root's technical judgment, and one root's staff may not rescue another root's quorum.

Six regional Exchange nodes hold full public mirrors. Performance escrow, cryptographic lineage and the disconnected archive use four-of-six threshold custody. One office, root or region cannot unseal protected performance. The builder is not an operating node: vendor exit leaves public custody and Clean reconstruction intact.

## Loss constitution

The companion [workbook](../../../models/programmatics/department-of-resilience-federated-artifact-estate-topology-and-loss-constitution.xlsx) evaluates thirty architecture worlds: eighteen individual-site losses, six failure-domain losses, vendor exit, compromise of each reconstruction role, one court-seat compromise and one Exchange-share compromise. Those worlds generate 540 root-function judgments.

A single site loss leaves one Native, one Clean and one Divergence path and two court seats for the affected root. A domain loss removes one site from each of three roots and one Exchange share; all roots retain their three functions, court quorum and at least five custody shares. Role compromise removes one path per root but leaves the dissimilar path. Court or Exchange compromise remains below the constitutional threshold.

Continuity is not validity. These are counterfactual topology results, not drills, observations or evidence. A second root-site loss, common beneficial control, lineage leakage, builder dependency or displaced truth-station capacity forces `HOLD` or termination. It never authorizes cross-root borrowing or a central takeover.

## Institutional and economic boundary

Physical co-location is allowed only behind separate employment, administration, keys, threshold ceremonies, technical ancestry and adjudication. Twelve incompatibility rules prevent one actor from authoring, replaying, reconstructing, adjudicating, releasing escrow and paying the same package. Common control kills the cell; it does not become an efficiency waiver.

`FAE-1` treats the existing USD540M public-method replay and cold-run reservation as a maximum co-use capital ceiling, not a new appropriation. The planning allocation is USD189M for host and failure-domain hardening, USD135M for role-separated enclave compute, USD81.0M for offline custody and threshold escrow, USD54.0M for court seats and key ceremony, USD54.0M for cross-region restoration and USD27.0M held for recovery. The [Pass 232](department-of-resilience-source-capsule-and-dual-reconstruction-grammar.md) operating ceiling remains USD41.3M across reconstruction and public escrow.

This accounting is conditional. Artifact-estate use has no claim on capacity needed for physical truth trains. A later commissioning warrant must prove non-displacement under simultaneous cold-run demand; failure returns the co-use claim to zero.

The workbook contains fourteen sheets, 2,656 formulas, fifty terminal audits, eighteen detected-and-restored corruptions and one chart after independent office recalculation. All thirty worlds and 540 detailed judgments pass. Formula-error and external-link scans are empty. Named sites, stewards, keys, commissioned enclaves, validity warrants, payments, observations, selections, `MAKE` and `REP-4` remain zero.

Rejected but unconsumed alternatives are a single national campus, six monolithic root estates, thirty-six dedicated function sites, a fourteen-site lattice and a vendor cloud with public backup. None is a killed branch.
