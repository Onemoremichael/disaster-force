# RBA-1 — Root A first QE-02 source event and source-exposed regeneration constitution

## Decision abstract

`RBA-1` defines the first lawful physical entry of energetic source into the Root A program. It begins only after `RAZ-1` has qualified the twenty-four-cell `QE-01` null-event class. It does not treat that class as source evidence. Instead it creates a new, source-specific eligibility field, a fresh public selection, one serialized 125-kilogram source package, one 100-kilogram event aliquot, a separately governed casualty-water reserve, a source-bearing event clock, a terminal mass account and a new post-exposure credential.

The first-principles distinction is decisive. `QE-01` proved that six rigs, two methods and two unlike truth roots could observe and reconstruct zero. It did not prove that any cell could accept fuel, survive an ignition opportunity, distinguish source from water, preserve a thermal boundary, recover material, classify public intervention or return a contaminated article to service. The first `QE-02` event is therefore a new constitutional object, not the next checkbox in the null campaign.[1–4]

All twenty-four cells must close sixteen source-specific eligibility gates before selection. Six independent custodians then publish hash commitments; after the eligibility freeze they reveal their salts and integers, whose modular sum selects one of the twenty-four cells. A cell may not enter the draw without its exact source package, current article–rig baseline, accepted 40-millimetre insert, containment geometry, twelve final control elements, thirty-six safeing paths, casualty-water package, thermal boundary, source and water ancestry roots, qualified crew, rescue posture, waste route and evidence capacity. This prevents a favorable subset from being created after source properties are known.

The event uses the government-owned `REA-100` low-source article. One hundred kilograms at the inherited planning density occupies 0.125 cubic metres. The 40-millimetre, 12-metre insert holds approximately 12.064 kilograms, produces a nominal velocity of 0.82893 metres per second over 120 seconds and creates an observable filled-route problem. The rejected 150-millimetre route would hold approximately 169.646 kilograms and cannot be filled by the aliquot. `RBA-1` does not revive that invalid scale analogy.[2,5]

The source package contains 125 kilograms divided before issue into 100 kilograms for the event, 10 kilograms retained for assay, 10 kilograms for conditioning and 5 kilograms held in quarantine. Ten characteristics bind identity and admissibility. During the event, the 100-kilogram ledger closes across mutually exclusive terminal bins. Expanded mass uncertainty must not exceed 1.5 kilograms; environmental source must not exceed 0.10 kilograms; unaccounted source must not exceed 0.75 kilograms; source ancestry recovery must be at least 0.995 and cross-talk no more than 0.005. Numerical closure cannot replace ancestry, and ancestry cannot replace mass closure.

`AM-06` carries a ten-second ignition opportunity at `T+15 s`; flame is observed rather than presumed. The planning upper screen is 5.85585 megawatts thermal and 2.04955 megawatts radiant over the 2.5-square-metre pan. A valid no-flame event remains evidence and cannot be repeated into a flame result. It may earn the bounded source-admission credit but no thermal-performance claim. Public quench, casualty diversion or rescue can protect people and the site while making the research outcome adverse.

Two attempt tokens exist. `QE02-A01` is primary. `QE02-A02` is dormant and may activate only if an independent board finds the registered proposition invalid. It must repeat the same selected cell with a new event issue, packet root and same-family reserve source package. A valid adverse result cannot activate it. The invalid original remains in every attempt, source and evidence denominator.

The event consumes its pre-event credential. Source-exposed re-entry is materially stricter than null-event regeneration. It requires forensic hold, inventory mapping, sampling, controlled decontamination, waste disposition, replacement of event-specific wetted articles, calibration bookends, a dry zero, a water blank, a source blank, complete safeing regression, outside-origin replay and a new serialized baseline. Return cannot occur before day fourteen. A successor event would then require a new 96-hour freeze, so the minimum source-exposed same-pair interval is 432 hours from one `T0` to the next.

The model contains sixty-four sheets. It preserves every physical observation as open. Ninety-six executable threshold cases exercise selection, lot allocation, uncertainty, source closure, environmental custody, ancestry, receiver capacity, water capacity, replacement, credit and source-exposed return. They include valid unignited, flame and no-flame dispositions; adverse ancestry, environmental and public-action cases; and invalid-attempt replacement. One hundred eighty planning-coherence audits pass; the saved-workbook scan finds no formula-error match, every sheet renders for visual review and the Open XML package validates. The 673,608-byte workbook has SHA-256 `aae5ab9f9fdedf2f6ed7348a195c84a92fcd2bf7efe06ba5dc9a3cbe6cd79b46`. Model completion does not assert that source has entered a rig.

`RBA-1` allocates USD 36 million in capital, USD 12 million per year in support and USD 80 million in cross-classified technology work. Its six-month stage exposure is USD 42 million. The allocation is nested inside the unassigned `RAS-1` parent capacity. After RBA-1, that parent retains USD 224 million capital, USD 55 million per year and USD 455 million of technology authority for later source-bearing work. The release-aware chain reaches month 72, compared with month 96 if every stage waited for its predecessor to finish. The 24-month difference remains revocable preparation time.

An accepted first event grants, at most, one exact `QE-02` cell credit and authority to constitute the full `QE-02` class. It grants no `QE-03` entry, no 150-millimetre transfer, no candidate admission, no production authority and no exterior form. Rendering remains blocked.

## 2. The registered proposition

The event proposition is:

> One publicly selected, source-qualified Root A cell can receive one registered 100-kilogram aliquot through the `REA-100` article, execute its assigned public method, reach bounded physical states, preserve source and water identity, close mass and evidence accounts, classify every intervention and produce a distinct source-exposed post-event credential.

This proposition has four inseparable layers:

1. **Issue truth:** the selected cell, package, baseline, crew and authorities existed before source release.
2. **Event truth:** source admission, ignition opportunity if assigned, final-element actions, intervention and physical states were observed in causal order.
3. **Custody truth:** source, water, samples, emissions and waste retain identity and reconcile quantitatively.
4. **Return truth:** the used pair does not regain availability until source-exposed regeneration creates a new baseline.

Failure in one layer cannot be offset by strength in another. Perfect mass closure with cross-contaminated ancestry is not success. A clean thermal result with an unobserved final element is not success. A safely quenched event may be an operational safety success and an adverse method result at the same time.

Every commitment must verify; every reveal is retained. The draw fails rather than silently excluding a late or inconvenient cell. If the selected cell later loses readiness before issue, the board does not redraw. The event pauses until that exact cell is restored or the constitution is amended prospectively.

## 5. Physical event envelope

### 5.1 Hydraulic reference

For admitted mass \(m=100\;kg\), density \(\rho=800\;kg/m^3\), time \(t=120\;s\), diameter \(D=0.04\;m\) and length \(L=12\;m\):

\[
V_s=\frac{m}{\rho}=0.125\;m^3,
\qquad
\dot V=\frac{V_s}{t}=0.00104167\;m^3/s.
\]

The insert area is approximately 0.00125664 m², route volume 0.0150796 m³ and planning hold-up 12.0637 kg. Nominal velocity is 0.82893 m/s. With \(\mu=0.004\;Pa\cdot s\), \(\sigma=0.025\;N/m\) and gravity 9.80665 m/s²:

\[
Re\approx6{,}631,
\qquad
Fr\approx1.324,
\qquad
We\approx879.5.
\]

These values define the event article; they do not claim similarity to the 150 mm route. The insert and wetted liner are controlled consumables after energetic exposure unless forensic review authorizes a narrower disposition.

### 5.2 Containment

The inner pan measures 2.0 by 1.25 metres with a 0.2-metre wall. Its 2.5 m² area produces a nominal 0.05-metre depth for 0.125 m³. The closed receiver has 0.5 m³ gross volume and a 0.8 usable fraction, yielding 0.4 m³ of usable capacity and 0.275 m³ margin above the source aliquot.

The surrounding 25 m² catchment and 120 m³ casualty receiver are public safety systems. Their use can preserve life, the rig and the environment but cannot improve the method result. Any source crossing the inner boundary receives a terminal bin, time, ancestry result and disposition.

### 5.3 Thermal opportunity

Only `AM-06` includes ignition. At `T+15 s` it receives one ten-second opportunity from the registered igniter. The event does not presume flame. If flame occurs, the planning screen is:

\[
\dot Q=2.5\times0.055\times42.588=5.85585\;MW.
\]

At a 0.35 radiative fraction the point-source screen is 2.04955 MW radiant. The notional 100 kg burning interval is 12.12 minutes. A simple 1.6 kW/m² point-source distance is about 10.10 metres, but the 200-metre operational boundary remains because wind, flame tilt, smoke, runoff, public action and model error are not represented by the scalar screen.[2,6]

No flame is a valid observed state when igniter energy, timing and geometry are all accepted. It may earn a low-source admission and containment credit. It cannot populate a flame-performance field, and the program cannot repeat it merely to obtain flame.

### 10.2 Mutually exclusive terminal bins

The 100 kg event aliquot closes across dispenser residual, route residual, pan/surface inventory, normal receiver, casualty receiver, burned or transformed source, event samples, environmental release and unaccounted source. A physical quantity may occupy only one terminal bin. Recovered source is calculated from eligible physical bins rather than entered as a second overlapping total.

For `AM-05`, burned or transformed mass must be observed zero. For `AM-06`, it is a measured terminal bin and must reconcile to thermal, gas, residue and sample evidence. No estimate may be inserted merely to force the total to 100 kg.

## 17. Closure and the next admissible object

Four final decisions exist:

1. close the selected attempt record;
2. award or deny one exact `QE-02` cell credit;
3. return or quarantine the source-exposed article–rig pair; and
4. permit or block constitution of the full twenty-four-cell `QE-02` class.

All four decisions begin open. A valid success or valid no-flame result can award the exact cell credit if every custody, safety, evidence and regeneration condition closes. A valid adverse result closes the event without credit or replacement. An invalid result may activate the one reserve token but does not erase the original.

The next admissible object after successful RBA-1 closure is `RBB-1`, a full `QE-02` class execution and source-transfer qualification constitution. It must govern twenty-four source packages, source-exposed reuse, no-flame strata, method and root contrasts, reserve-package depletion, outside-origin reconstruction and class-level environmental totals. It may not assume that one event transfers.

`QE-03`, larger source routes, candidate mechanisms, operational fleet design, production and exterior rendering remain blocked.

## 18. What would falsify this constitution

RBA-1 should be amended or stopped if physical work shows any of the following:

- the 40 mm insert cannot create a stable, observable filled-route event at the registered aliquot;
- source properties cannot be held inside the declared measurement envelope;
- both roots share an unrecognized common dependency;
- final-element state cannot be established independently of command;
- terminal bins cannot close without overlapping quantities;
- public water destroys source ancestry or environmental custody;
- ignition opportunity cannot be separated from assumed flame;
- post-exposure blanks remain contaminated beyond registered limits;
- remote campuses cannot reconstruct the event without origin interpretation; or
- source-exposed return routinely exceeds the planned institution’s regeneration capacity.

The appropriate response is to narrow, redesign or stop—not to widen tolerances retrospectively.
