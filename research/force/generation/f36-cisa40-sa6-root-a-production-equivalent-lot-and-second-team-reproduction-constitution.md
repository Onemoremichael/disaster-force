# RAQ-1 — Root A production-equivalent lot and second-team reproduction constitution

## Decision abstract

RAQ-1 defines how the Department of Resilience would create the eighteen production-equivalent articles already reserved by RAP-1 without allowing a successful development prototype, a preferred builder, a favorable material lot or an unexamined repair to become the basis of public acceptance. It converts the construction and development evidence in RAC-1 and RAB-1 into a six-cell industrial experiment: three independent builders, two unlike construction lineages and three consecutive articles in every builder–lineage cell. The resulting bank contains six operational articles, six functional twins, three calibration articles and three destructive articles. Roles, build sequence, material splits and destinations are committed before fabrication begins.[1–8]

The principal decision is narrow:

> May a complete, frozen, eighteen-role article bank constructed by three qualified teams enter the RAP-1 physical campaign?

An affirmative answer would mean that the public build package was reproduced across three organizations, that two unlike lineages remained separately identifiable, that material ancestry crossed builders instead of being confounded with them, and that every article passed its own construction gates. It would not mean that the line is ready for series production, that field reliability is known, that source entry is authorized or that any article is a deployable candidate.

The experiment uses three consecutive articles per cell because a production claim cannot rest on one hand-built exemplar. Yet three is still a small denominator. If all three articles in a cell conform on first pass, the exact one-sided 95-percent lower bound on cell yield is only 36.84 percent. Even eighteen clean articles produce a lower bound of only 84.67 percent. Conversely, zero failures in three units leaves a 63.16-percent upper bound on the defect probability; zero failures in all eighteen leaves 15.33 percent. Fifty-nine clean independent units would be needed to place the corresponding upper defect bound at or below 5 percent. RAQ-1 can therefore establish reproducible bank construction. It cannot establish mature production capability.

The model makes this distinction operational. Controlled rework remains visible in the first-pass denominator. A repaired article may become conforming only through an accepted disposition and retained branch identity. It cannot be relabeled as first-pass work. A cell with repaired articles requires an explicit yield disposition even when all three articles ultimately pass. A missing article cannot be replaced by assigning a favorable article from another role or lineage. Eighteen dormant replenishment authorizations preserve the exact role, lineage and bank position while requiring a new material lot.

The physical record is intentionally large. Thirty-two build-capsule records, thirty-six builder-qualification records, seventy-two production-tool units and 1,728 measurement-transfer observations precede article work. Fabrication then creates 360 section travelers, 720 adjacent-process coupons, 360 NDE records, ninety joint records, 216 final control elements, 2,592 installed channel records and 2,592 factory calibration records. Each article faces 390 construction gates, for 7,020 gates across the bank. All are separate from later RAP-1 operating and source-event gates.

RAQ-1 assigns $66 million of capital, $16 million per year of support and $98 million of 24-month stage exposure. Together, RAC-1, RAB-1 and RAQ-1 allocate the full $248 million RAP-1 capital boundary, the full $112 million annual-support boundary and the full $310 million technology map. The apparent financial closure reveals a schedule error: the three stages require sixty sequential months—eighteen for RAC-1, eighteen for RAB-1 and twenty-four for RAQ-1—against the original 24-month RAP-1 establishment duration. The chain is therefore thirty-six months over its inherited calendar. Financial headroom cannot be treated as time.

The strongest RAQ-1 outcome is bank-entry authority. Campaign mobilization remains a separate object. `RAX-1` must assign the accepted articles to rigs, establish pre-source operational readiness, reconcile the 60-month establishment chain and issue the physical handover conditions under which the RAP-1 campaign can begin. Exterior rendering remains withheld because no operational candidate, deployment envelope or service layout has been accepted.

## 1. Research stocktake

The Root A sequence now answers five different questions that should never be collapsed into one prototype demonstration.

| Stage | Governing question | Maximum decision |
|---|---|---|
| `RAE-1` | what is the lowest physically honest source event? | define an executable low-source dossier |
| `RAP-1` | what bank and proof institution could judge a reference article? | define the eighteen roles and campaign gates |
| `RAC-1` | what exactly is the public construction object? | release two unlike development build packages |
| `RAB-1` | can the mechanisms, measurements and defect screens survive development qualification? | admit a lineage to production-equivalent research |
| `RAQ-1` | can independent teams reproduce the frozen package as a complete bank? | admit all eighteen positions to campaign mobilization |

This sequencing is more than administrative caution. Each stage changes the unit of evidence. RAE-1 is an event dossier. RAP-1 is a campaign institution. RAC-1 is a configuration and construction constitution. RAB-1 is a development qualification population. RAQ-1 is a crossed production-equivalence experiment. A strong result at one level cannot answer the next level's question.

RAB-1 may reveal a valid process window in controlled development work. That result does not show that another organization can interpret the released package, establish its own tooling, transfer the measurement standard, preserve material ancestry and repeatedly build an accepted article. RAQ-1 inserts that missing industrial layer.

The Department of Resilience concept needs this layer across every major capability family. A high-capacity firefighting aircraft, autonomous flood barrier, mobile heat-rejection plant, nuclear desalination vessel or rapid water trunkline will fail as a national capability if it can be fabricated only by one heroic team. The state must be able to reproduce the physical object and its evidence across more than one industrial site. Public force design therefore includes production epistemology: how government knows that the thing built in another plant is still the thing it authorized.

## 2. Production equivalence as a public claim

RAQ-1 defines production equivalence as a conjunction of five conditions:

1. the configuration issued to each builder is identical at the public interface and explicitly different only where lineage permits;
2. each builder has independently qualified people, tools, measurement chains and controlled work areas;
3. every article is fabricated through the released sequence with complete native evidence;
4. the population crosses builder, lineage and parent material bundle so those effects can be distinguished; and
5. an authority outside the builder accepts the article, the cell and the bank in that order.

Production equivalence is not visual similarity. It is not dimensional inspection at final assembly. It is not possession of a supplier certificate. It is not compliance with a drawing while using an unregistered process. It is the controlled reproduction of geometry, material state, joint state, instrumentation, safeing state, evidence identity and configuration ancestry.

The distinction is important for resilience equipment because these systems often spend long periods staged, then operate under damaged infrastructure, remote supervision and severe environmental conditions. A hidden difference in seal compression, heat history, channel mounting or safe-state energy may remain invisible during warehouse inspection and appear only during the emergency. Production evidence must retain the causal path from raw material and tool state to the accepted article.

RAQ-1 also separates production equivalence from production capability. The former asks whether the frozen object can be reproduced under a controlled lot. The latter asks whether a stable industrial process can repeatedly meet requirements at intended rate, cost and yield. RAQ-1 supplies evidence for the first claim and inputs for the second. It does not claim the second.

## 3. Why eighteen articles cannot establish mature yield

The eighteen-article bank is large as an integrated physical campaign and small as a production sample. That apparent contradiction is central to the design.

For an all-success observation with (n) independent units and one-sided significance level \(\alpha=0.05\), the exact lower bound on yield is:

\[
L=\alpha^{1/n}
\]

The corresponding upper bound on defect probability after zero observed failures is:

\[
U=1-\alpha^{1/n}
\]

| Population | Clean units | Yield lower bound | Defect upper bound | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| one builder–lineage cell | 3 | 36.84% | 63.16% | confirms three specific constructions only |
| complete RAQ-1 bank | 18 | 84.67% | 15.33% | supports bank reproduction, not series yield |
| minimum zero-failure screen for 5% defect upper bound | 59 | 95.05% | 4.95% | still a narrow zero-failure screen |
| minimum all-success population for 90% yield lower bound | 29 | 90.19% | 9.81% | insufficient for high-consequence fleet reliability |

The calculations assume independent, identically governed units. RAQ-1 deliberately introduces known structure—builders, lineages, material bundles and sequences—so naïve pooling can be misleading. Eighteen successes could hide a builder-specific mechanism, a lineage-specific weakness or a material interaction if the crossing were poorly designed. The model preserves cell-level results and prohibits pooling to rescue a failed cell.

First-pass yield is descriptive. It reports how many of the committed articles met all construction requirements without controlled rework. Rework does not disappear because the repaired article eventually conforms. The original result stays in the denominator, the repair receives a branch identity and the cell board must explain why the mechanism is bounded. This prevents a perfect final-inspection number from concealing an unstable build process.

If leadership wants a statistically stronger production claim, it must authorize a later series-demonstration population with a registered sampling frame, stable rate, repeated shifts, maintenance intervals, supply substitutions and sufficient units. That is not a reason to inflate RAQ-1. It is a reason to state honestly what RAQ-1 is for.

## 4. Six-cell industrial design

The experiment contains three builders—`BLD-A`, `BLD-B` and `BLD-C`—and two unlike lineages—`BL-X` and `BL-Y`. Their crossing creates six cells. Each cell fabricates three consecutive articles under one released lineage capsule.

| Cell | Builder | Lineage | Article roles |
|---|---|---|---|
| `LOT-A-X` | `BLD-A` | `BL-X` | operational, functional twin, calibration |
| `LOT-A-Y` | `BLD-A` | `BL-Y` | operational, functional twin, destructive |
| `LOT-B-X` | `BLD-B` | `BL-X` | operational, functional twin, calibration |
| `LOT-B-Y` | `BLD-B` | `BL-Y` | operational, functional twin, destructive |
| `LOT-C-X` | `BLD-C` | `BL-X` | operational, functional twin, destructive |
| `LOT-C-Y` | `BLD-C` | `BL-Y` | operational, functional twin, calibration |

Every builder therefore produces six articles: two operational articles, two functional twins, one calibration article and one destructive article. Every lineage contributes nine articles. The special roles are balanced across builders and lineages without assigning every rare role to one site.

Three consecutive articles matter because the first unit often receives exceptional attention and the later units expose whether the package is actually executable. The planned sequence differs across cells so article role is not perfectly confounded with first, second or third build. The operational article is not always first; the destructive or calibration article is not always last. Builders may not reorder the sequence after seeing intermediate results.

The design does not create a competitive shootout in which the most favorable six articles are selected. All eighteen positions are mandatory. The bank fails to constitute if any position is missing, reassigned across lineage or released without its cell. Competition occurs through transparent causal learning and future procurement, not favorable-unit selection inside the proof population.

## 5. Role commitment and identity conservation

Each article receives a locked role, destination, builder, lineage, material bundle and build sequence before fabrication. Those fields form its master identity.

The six operational articles are destined for the active RAP-1 campaign. The six functional twins carry controlled-defect and denial work without contaminating the operational population. Three calibration articles create known geometry, state and timing references. Three destructive articles provide terminal physical truth. The assignments preserve the population architecture defined by RAP-1.

Role precommitment prevents four forms of selection bias:

- a clean destructive article cannot be promoted into an operational slot;
- a repaired operational article cannot be hidden by substituting a cleaner twin;
- a failed lineage cannot borrow an article from the unlike lineage; and
- a builder cannot choose its best unit after final inspection.

An article that becomes unavailable may invoke its reserved replenishment authorization. The replacement must retain the same bank position, builder responsibility, lineage and role, but it receives a new article identity and a new material lot. The failed or missing article remains in the evidence ledger. Replenishment restores a bank position; it does not erase a result.

## 6. Material crossing and ancestry

Each lineage begins with three parent material bundles. Every parent bundle is divided into three sealed splits, one for each builder. Within a builder–lineage cell, its three articles use the three different parent bundles. The complete design therefore crosses parent material with builder.

This arrangement prevents the common error in which builder A receives material lot A, builder B receives lot B and builder C receives lot C. Under that design, a difference between builders could actually be a material difference. RAQ-1 makes each parent bundle appear at every builder so a later analysis can separate site and material effects.

Every split requires parent-lot identity, split identity, custody root and acceptance. Article records then link the split to the built serial and final configuration root. Material from an unaccepted split cannot enter a qualifying article. An unidentified substitution creates quarantine, not an engineering note.

The crossing is not a claim that three material bundles characterize the entire supply market. It is an identification design for the RAQ-1 bank. Later series authority must include more heats, suppliers, batch ages, transport histories and accepted substitutions.

## 7. The executable build capsule

RAC-1 defines the construction object. RAB-1 may physically challenge and revise its process window. RAQ-1 begins only after one frozen build capsule is issued for each lineage.

Each capsule contains sixteen controlled components:

1. configuration root and released drawing set;
2. material specifications and approved ancestry fields;
3. process windows and hold points;
4. work instructions;
5. tooling list and commissioning standard;
6. measurement and uncertainty plan;
7. joint execution and acceptance plan;
8. inspection and NDE plan;
9. final-element and safeing plan;
10. instrument mount and channel plan;
11. calibration plan;
12. software and firmware manifest;
13. evidence and causal-time schema;
14. RFI and assistance protocol;
15. nonconformance and change protocol; and
16. released public interface package.

Two lineages produce thirty-two capsule records. A capsule is complete only when all sixteen components have version, release hash, issuer, release time and accepted state. The builder cannot begin qualifying work from a draft package. Any discrepancy discovered during interpretation enters the sealed RFI process so all builders receive the same authoritative clarification.

The capsule must be machine-readable as well as human-readable. Future resilience production will combine people, programmable tools, robotic fixtures, autonomous inspection and software-defined evidence. A build instruction that exists only as prose cannot reliably govern that system. RAQ-1 therefore treats executable configuration, validation rules and record schemas as technology objects in their own right.

## 8. Builder independence and the assistance ledger

The three builders are organizationally distinct. `BLD-A` is the public reference arsenal. `BLD-B` and `BLD-C` are independent production cells. Each receives the same public package and must create its own qualified workforce, work area, tooling, measurement chain and evidence process.

Independence does not mean silence. It means that assistance is visible and symmetric. A builder may ask a question. The configuration authority answers through a sealed record, determines whether the answer changes the capsule and distributes any generally applicable clarification to all cells. Undocumented coaching is prohibited because it converts the supposedly executable package into tacit knowledge held by the origin team.

Each builder faces twelve qualification records covering package receipt, workforce authorization, tooling and environmental control, material custody, measurement transfer, configuration control, evidence custody, safeing, nonconformance control, independent acceptance, technical-capacity isolation and incident readiness. All twelve must pass before the first qualifying article begins.

The public reference arsenal is not privileged at acceptance. It may know the design history, but its articles face the same capsule, ancestry, traveler, calibration and board requirements as the outside builders. If the public builder needs undocumented origin-team intervention, the package is not yet transferable.

## 9. Tooling and measurement transfer

Each builder commissions twenty-four production-tool families, creating seventy-two serialized tool units. The families correspond to the article’s decisive construction and verification acts: public datum transfer, autonomous bore mapping, surface state, contact state, cutting and preparation, orbital joining, purge control, clamping, seal compression, monitored interspace, liquid proof, leak measurement, thermal exposure, cyclic hydraulic work, draining, spring work, stored energy, safeing state, final-element installation, instrument mounting, channel calibration and related evidence functions.

Tool equivalence is functional, not brand-based. Two builders may use different equipment if both demonstrate the same governed output, uncertainty, boundary and record. This invites industrial competition while keeping the public claim stable.

Measurement transfer uses twenty-four families, six standards, three sites, two operators and two repeats:

\[
24 \times 6 \times 3 \times 2 \times 2 = 1{,}728
\]

raw observations.

The observations feed seventy-two family–site decisions. Each decision requires all twenty-four records for its site and family, a bounded site-bias share, an accepted gage repeatability-and-reproducibility result, a registered analysis identity and independent acceptance. A builder cannot pass because its local readings are internally consistent while shifted from the public reference. It also cannot pass because the three-site average is correct while one site is unacceptable.

The transfer network creates a reusable Department capability. If mobile reactors, desalination trains, firefighting aircraft and hospital platforms are to be built across a broad industrial base, public standards must travel between plants without losing their physical meaning. The standards network becomes national infrastructure for resilience production.

## 10. Production evidence native to the article

RAQ-1 does not wait for final inspection to discover what was built. Evidence is created at the act that changes physical state.

Each of the eighteen articles contains twenty governed sections, forty adjacent-process coupons, twenty NDE records, five joint records, twelve final control elements and 144 installed channels. Across the bank this becomes:

| Evidence object | Per article | Bank total |
|---|---:|---:|
| section travelers | 20 | 360 |
| adjacent-process coupons | 40 | 720 |
| NDE records | 20 | 360 |
| joint records | 5 | 90 |
| final control elements | 12 | 216 |
| installed channels | 144 | 2,592 |
| factory calibrations | 144 | 2,592 |

A section traveler links the public datum, material identity, process act, operator, tool, observed state and acceptance. The two coupons adjacent to every section provide separate physical witnesses of the local process state. They do not prove the entire section, but they create inspectable evidence at the same article, builder, material and time context.

NDE records remain distinct from destructive truth and from joint acceptance. A favorable scan cannot replace a missing process record. A complete process record cannot replace the scan. A joint record integrates the mechanism-specific evidence and preserves the governing variables defined for its lineage.

Every installed channel has article, builder, lineage, family, measurement, instance, sensor serial, mount datum, power path, time path, calibration identity and evidence root. Factory calibration is article-specific. A generic instrument certificate cannot show that the installed sensor, mount, cable, acquisition path and time identity work together.

## 11. Unlike lineage evidence

The two lineages remain intentionally unlike because common interfaces should not create common hidden failure mechanisms.

`BL-X` uses the fused-metallic route. Its decisive evidence concerns preparation, fit, purge state, joining energy, travel, internal geometry, heat-affected condition, leak integrity and post-process inspection. Its joint is permanent and later intervention must respect that permanence.

`BL-Y` uses the keyed, monitored double-barrier route. Its decisive evidence concerns face condition, key registration, preload, seal compression, interspace integrity, retained state and serviceability. Its joint is assembled rather than fused and therefore carries different installation and relaxation risks.

The common public capsule contains interface, safety, measurement and evidence requirements. The lineage capsules contain the mechanism-specific path to those requirements. Acceptance cannot average the two. A strong result in `BL-X` provides diversity value but cannot rescue an unacceptable `BL-Y` cell, and vice versa.

This pattern scales beyond Root A. A Department of Resilience should often keep unlike technical roots alive for high-consequence functions: air-cooled and water-cooled heat rejection, membrane and thermal desalination, tethered and free-flight communications, mechanical and power-electronic isolation. Diversity is useful only when each root has its own truthful evidence and both meet a common public interface.

## 12. Nonconformance, repair and first-pass truth

Fifty-four pre-established exception records cover the eighteen articles across nonconformance, assistance and configuration classes. The records are dormant until used. Each retains subject, builder, description, disposition, classification flag, evidence root, closure and state.

An exception can lead to use-as-is, controlled repair, scrap, replacement or change review. None is self-authorizing. The builder records the condition; configuration, quality, metrology, safeing or the article board decides according to the affected claim.

Controlled repair follows four rules:

- the initial condition remains visible;
- the repair receives its own approved instruction and configuration branch;
- the repaired feature repeats every affected inspection and calibration; and
- the article remains a reworked unit in first-pass reporting.

The cell board sees required articles, released articles, first-pass-conforming count, controlled-rework count and yield disposition. All three articles must release. If any article required rework, the yield disposition must be explicit before cell release. A decision may accept a bounded, understood repair for bank use while still refusing to call the process mature.

This is an institutional design choice. Public programs often reward a green final status and thereby suppress the path by which green was achieved. RAQ-1 instead treats the history of correction as part of the product. That history guides later production investment, automation, supplier development and inspection intensity.

## 13. Article, cell and bank gates

Acceptance proceeds through three nested levels.

### Article release

Each article faces 390 gates. The gates verify lineage capsule, builder qualification, material split, build sequence, twenty travelers, forty coupons, twenty NDE records, five joints, twelve final elements, 144 channel installations, 144 factory calibrations and the remaining article-master decisions. All 390 must pass, first-pass disposition must be visible and the article board must record acceptance.

The 7,020 gates are generated from the native ledgers. They do not create new evidence. Their purpose is to prevent a summary cell from turning green when an underlying record is missing, failed or attached to another article.

### Cell release

Each builder–lineage cell requires all three of its articles. It also requires a cell-specific first-pass yield disposition and independent board acceptance. A cell cannot release with two articles and a promise to replace the third. It cannot borrow an article from another cell.

### Bank release

The bank board verifies eighteen released articles, six released cells, nine articles from each lineage, six articles from each builder, six operational roles, six twin roles, three calibration roles, three destructive roles and two accepted lineage baseline releases. Twelve bank gates must pass.

The bank decision is constitutional rather than merely additive. Eighteen individually accepted objects do not constitute the planned bank if their population balance, lineage identity or role commitments have changed.

## 14. Replenishment and change control

Eighteen replenishment authorizations are reserved, one for each bank position. They begin dormant. A missing or rejected article can be replaced only through its matching authorization. The new article keeps the same builder responsibility, lineage, role and destination but uses a new material lot and new identity. Every affected gate reruns.

Twelve change classes cover public interface, lineage geometry, material specification, process window, joint mechanism, safeing mechanism, instrument mount, calibration method, software or firmware, evidence schema, production tool and supplier or parent lot.

A change is classified before work resumes. Minor documentary correction may require bounded record revision. A change to a critical physical or evidentiary mechanism triggers renewed reproduction for the affected lineage and cells. The authority cannot declare a critical change equivalent by narrative alone.

This discipline matters during rapid innovation. The Department should encourage better robotics, sensors, materials and processes, but it must know when the improved object is no longer the qualified object. Change control is not a brake on invention. It is the mechanism that lets invention enter a public force without dissolving the meaning of prior evidence.

## 15. Stop, quarantine and public authority

Sixteen automatic stop rules cover material mismatch, tool-boundary error, measurement transfer failure, lost ancestry, unauthorized assistance, calibration failure, unregistered configuration, section or joint failure, software mismatch, missing native evidence, unsafe final-element state, replenishment misuse and bank-role inconsistency. A stop preserves the last known safe state and sends the affected object to the named authority.

Twelve quarantine classes keep suspected conditions from spreading across the bank. They include technical-data mismatch, tool-boundary unknown, measurement disagreement, material-ancestry break, joint or surface nonconformance, safeing failure, channel or calibration failure, evidence or clock loss, undisclosed assistance, unauthorized repair, role or lot-selection bias and personnel or environmental event.

Twelve separated authorities govern the work: configuration, public design, each builder, material, tooling, metrology, quality, safeing, evidence, independent article acceptance and bank acceptance. Their powers are intentionally incomplete. The builder may construct but not accept its own article. Metrology may accept measurement transfer but not waive a physical failure. Configuration may release a package but not select favorable bank roles. The bank board may constitute the bank but not issue the RAP-1 source campaign.

The future Department of Resilience will need permanent public institutions of this kind. A procurement office can buy equipment. It cannot by itself create known defects, transfer standards, reconstruct causal evidence, stop a plant or decide whether unlike mechanisms remain equivalent at a public interface. Those are sovereign technical functions.

RAQ-1 uses twenty-four months. The stage sequence includes capsule freeze and builder mobilization, qualification, tooling, measurement transfer, material release, staggered three-article cells, article acceptance, cell acceptance and bank constitution.

This is the most consequential emergent finding of the pass. Earlier models allocated money by stage but did not reconcile the stages as a sequential critical path. The capital and annual-support limits close; the calendar does not.

Some work may later overlap, but overlap must be earned by an interface and risk analysis. Production-equivalent fabrication cannot begin before the relevant RAB-1 process window, measurement system and capsule are accepted. Builder facility preparation, long-lead tool acquisition and workforce development may start earlier under revocable authority. `RAX-1` should identify which activities can proceed in parallel without prejudging physical qualification.

These programs are not conveniences around a conventional factory. They are the research agenda for a new industrial mode. Military investment historically creates deep competence in propulsion, sensing, navigation, materials, production and logistics because the mission pays for capabilities that commercial demand alone will not mature. A coequal resilience force should do the same for rapid infrastructure, environmental control, medical utilities and recovery systems.

The build-capsule compiler could make a public configuration executable across human and robotic work. Self-verifying tools could refuse an act when calibration, material or released parameters do not match. Autonomous internal inspection could turn inaccessible pipes, tanks and joints into observable state. A national standards network could let a plant in one region reproduce a critical object whose design originated elsewhere. Evidence-native factories could make later forensic reconstruction a design property rather than an archival scramble.

At scale, these advances create prime integrators comparable in depth to major defense firms, but focused on stabilization systems: high-rate water, temporary power, cooling, autonomous earthwork, public-health logistics, fire suppression and resilient communications. Their export product would include equipment, training, calibration, digital configuration, acceptance services and interoperable public interfaces. The United States would project power partly by making recovery capacity available, reproducible and trusted.

RAQ-1 offers a small but concrete industrial doctrine: government owns the public interface and evidence regime; multiple builders retain room to innovate beneath it; qualification crosses organizations and materials; and acceptance is a public technical function. That doctrine can scale to the larger force.

## 20. Acceptance state and next object

RAQ-1 is analytically complete and physically unexecuted. The companion workbook contains forty-six populated sheets. Its baseline audit contains 120 passing assertions. A dynamic verifier executes sixty-four baseline and adversarial cases, including incomplete capsules, failed tool transfer, site bias, unacceptable gage results, missing material ancestry, build-sequence violations, calibration failures, unauthorized assistance, article-identity faults, incomplete 390-gate release, cell incompleteness, rework disposition, bank-role reassignment, critical change, financial propagation and formula-error search.
