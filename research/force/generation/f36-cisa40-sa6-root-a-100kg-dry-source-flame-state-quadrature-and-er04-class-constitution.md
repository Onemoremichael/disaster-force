# RBK-1 — Root A 100 kg dry-source flame-state quadrature and ER-04 class constitution

## Decision abstract

`RBK-1` defines the fourth physical rung of the `AM-06` entry ladder created by [RBG-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-am06-source-water-ignition-entry-ladder-reset.md). It begins only after the dry igniter class in [RBH-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-dry-igniter-reference-architecture-competition-and-er01-class-constitution.md), the water-only reference in [RBI-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-water-only-reference-system-and-er02-class-constitution.md) and the dual-ancestry class in [RBJ-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-dual-ancestry-reference-architecture-competition-and-er03-class-constitution.md) are physically accepted. Its question is:

> What new public architecture is required to admit 100 kilograms of dry energetic source, deliver one measured ignition opportunity, establish whether a flame existed, account for source transformation and thermal consequence, end the event safely, and return a serialized module after 1,008 hours without allowing any one observation to impersonate the others?

The answer is not an igniter attached to the accepted low-source article. Ignition command, energy delivery, flame, oxidation products, heat, source disappearance, extinction and safe return are different propositions. A bright discharge can exist without a sustained flame. A hot surface can remain after flame loss. Oxygen depletion can occur without resolving the reaction zone. A closed source mass account can still assign material to the wrong process. Public quench can bound consequence while demonstrating that the reference article did not end the event itself.

RBK-1 therefore constitutes a **combustion quadrature** with four physically unlike truth roots. A photon-field root and an ion/reaction-zone root must independently establish flame state. A source-derived product root must establish transformation. A distributed calorimetric root must establish thermal consequence. None can be dropped because another root appears persuasive. No weighted score can compensate for a missing root.

Four root programs, each built by two separated lineages across three campuses, create twenty-four candidate articles. They execute 192 root trials and 144 pairwise interference trials before one lineage in each root can enter an eighteen-module `FRC-100` bank: twelve active modules, four spares and two untouched forensic references. The sixteen serviceable modules then execute ninety-six full-scale qualification cycles. The exact `ER-04` class contains twelve campus-by-source-lineage-by-module-lineage cells and four invalid-attempt reserves. A valid no-flame event earns source-admission and safeing evidence but does not earn a flame-state credit and cannot be repeated under the same proposition merely to produce a flame.

At the registered 2.5-square-metre area, the inherited 0.055-kilogram-per-square-metre-second burning-flux screen and 42.588-megajoule-per-kilogram lower heating value produce 5.85585 megawatts of notional heat release, 2.0495475 megawatts of radiant output at a 0.35 fraction and 12.1212 minutes of notional duration. These are design screens, not predicted observations. Candidate water is a required numeric zero. Public water remains physically available under separate custody; any use is a public intervention and cannot become reference performance.

The primary child calendar is eighty-two weeks and the maximum is eighty-six. Together with the 143- and 149-week pre-fire programs and six remaining provisional rungs, the revised floor becomes 327 primary weeks and 361 maximum weeks inside the 364-week `RAS-3R2` boundary. Only thirty-seven primary and three maximum weeks remain. RBK-1 therefore does not request another parent rebase, but it eliminates the fiction that the later fire campaign retains comfortable calendar reserve.

The modeled establishment adds 575 execution positions while retaining 560 prior-class positions. It requires USD 720 million capital, USD 105 million per year during the stage and USD 300 million of cross-classified technology. Concurrent annual support is USD 197 million and concurrent staffing is 1,135. After RBK-1, the parent retains USD 2.12 billion capital, USD 160 million technology, USD 18 million per year and sixty-five positions. These are research hypotheses, not appropriations, bids or observed cost.

An accepted `ER-04-QUALIFIED` class establishes only the low-source, dry, reference-flame proposition on the selected module constitution. It does not qualify water application, coupled source-water ancestry, suppression performance, a candidate article, higher source classes, procurement, deployment, export or exterior form. The next admissible object is `RBL-1`, the 100-kilogram source-plus-water coupling architecture and `ER-05` constitution. It must begin with a parent-capacity decision because three weeks of maximum calendar reserve is not a credible unknown-physics allowance.

## 1. The composition defect

The inherited components were qualified against different propositions:

| Inherited object | What it can establish | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AM-05` low-source article | source admission, route, termination and source-equivalent account | ignition, flame, source transformation or heat |
| `RBH-1` dry igniter | commanded and delivered opportunity within a measured space-time envelope | whether source ignited or a flame persisted |
| `RBJ-1` ancestry pair | origin and phase assignment under nonenergetic source-like simulant and water | survivability or interpretation after combustion |
| public fire rig | safe site boundary, intervention, custody and evidence services | reference-article performance |

Joining accepted objects can create a new unqualified system. The igniter changes electromagnetic, thermal and timing conditions. Flame changes the source, tracers, atmosphere, surfaces and sensor fields. Product sampling changes flow and custody. Thermal instruments can disturb the plume or create hot surfaces. Public protection can change the very event being measured. RBK-1 treats these interactions as a new architecture, not integration paperwork.

Seven propositions must close independently:

1. the correct 100-kilogram source lot entered the registered article;
2. the dry igniter delivered the registered opportunity;
3. a flame did or did not exist in the registered volume and interval;
4. source-derived matter did or did not transform through combustion;
5. the thermal field and boundary consequence were measured;
6. new source, ignition energy and flame ended in the required order; and
7. the module returned to its serialized baseline after the prescribed observation interval.

This decomposition is constitutional. A later estimator may study relations among the propositions, but it may not merge their authority.

## 2. The combustion quadrature

### 2.1 Four truth roots

| Root | Decisive physical variable | Required conclusion | Shared-dependency exclusion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `PHO` | time-resolved emission and absorption through a surveyed volume | luminous or absorbing reaction volume, onset, persistence and end | no ion electrode, gas sampler or heat-flux transform may decide the root |
| `ION` | local charge-carrier response in a guarded reaction-zone lattice | ionized reaction-zone presence, propagation and disappearance | no optical classifier or `PHO` clock may decide the root |
| `MOL` | source-derived elemental or molecular products in time-registered custody | fraction and timing of source transformation | no source-loss inference or thermal estimate may replace collected product evidence |
| `CAL` | distributed incident flux, surface enthalpy and exhaust-energy boundary | thermal power, partition and integrated consequence | no flame-state label may generate the heat result |

`PHO` and `ION` are conjunctive flame-state roots. `MOL` is the transformation root. `CAL` is the consequence root. The quadrature is intentionally asymmetric: two unlike roots decide the fragile state proposition, while transformation and consequence each retain a dedicated independent account.

The program does not assume that present instruments can satisfy this architecture. It funds high-dynamic-range optical tomography through smoke and glare, replaceable ion lattices that survive contamination, source-derived product collection with bounded transport delay, distributed calorimetry with reconstructable surface storage, and a causal-time fabric that survives loss of campus services.

### 2.2 Candidate articles and builder separation

Each root has two builder lineages and one article at each of three campuses. The result is twenty-four articles:

\[
4\ \text{roots}\times2\ \text{lineages}\times3\ \text{campuses}=24.
\]

Lineages may share the public mechanical and safety envelope. They may not share the decisive transducer, calibration material, estimator, firmware release authority or analyst. The government holds interface definitions, reference stimuli, raw formats and verification methods. A lineage failure does not grant the other lineage a monopoly; it opens a successor competition against the public interface.

### 2.3 Root trials

Each candidate article executes eight trials: sealed zero, registered reference stimulus, weak field, strong field, spatial offset, temporal discontinuity, denied primary service and contaminated-return state. This creates 192 root trials. The trials establish numeric zero, detection bounds, location and time error, false declaration, recovery after saturation, denial survival and return state. They do not use the `ER-04` 100-kilogram source event as a discovery tool.

### 2.4 Pairwise interference

All six root pairs execute twenty-four cohabitation trials:

\[
\binom{4}{2}\times24=144.
\]

The purpose is not to select the best pair. It is to discover whether illumination corrupts charge sensing, electrodes alter optics or flow, product extraction changes the thermal boundary, calorimetric surfaces bias the reaction zone, or any root injects a clock, heat source, obstruction or chemical background that changes another root. A pairwise pass requires both roots to retain their individual bounds while installed together.

## 3. The `FRC-100` physical article

`FRC-100` is a removable fire-reference cell built around the inherited 2.5-square-metre source field. It is neither a generic pan nor a miniature candidate system. Its physical constitution has nine sections:

1. a serialized source admission and isolation head;
2. a surveyed source-field insert with bounded area and depth;
3. a replaceable photon-view volume with protected optical paths;
4. a guarded ion/reaction-zone lattice outside the source-custody path;
5. a source-derived product capture and transit-time section;
6. an inner and outer calorimetric boundary;
7. a residue, surface and drain custody cassette;
8. four public-safe-state final functions with local state memory; and
9. three separated native evidence trunks with unlike local time.

The geometry is designed around replaceable hot-zone cassettes rather than a permanent instrumented room. Fire-exposed surfaces, product routes, optical windows, ion elements and residue traps can be removed as one registered set. This turns contamination and accumulated thermal history into controlled article state. It also creates a plausible industrial product family: source-field cassettes, sensing frames, product-account modules, calorimetric boundaries and robotic exchange equipment can be competed separately under public interfaces.

An active module cannot be reassigned to a different exact cell after results are visible. A spare inherits no credit from the withdrawn module; it repeats the applicable qualification and receives a new class identity.

### 3.2 Four public-safe-state functions

Each module carries four independent paths for each of five final functions: source isolation, casualty-source diversion, ignition-energy removal, public-quench admission and evidence safe-state latch. Twenty paths result. Each path has a distinct command or passive trigger, motive-energy declaration, final-state witness and time record. Shared displays or common software do not create independence.

Public quench is armed and available but not part of reference performance. If it acts, the event may remain valid and the site may remain safe, but the flame-end and thermal-control propositions are adverse unless a separately registered rule says the public action occurred after reference completion.

## 4. Exact `ER-04` population

The class grid crosses three campuses, two source-lot lineages and two module build lineages:

\[
3\times2\times2=12\ \text{exact cells}.
\]

Each cell receives one active module, one preassigned source lot, one dry-igniter assembly from the accepted bank and one blinded event identity. The source-lot and module-lineage axes prevent a single material preparation or manufacturing path from defining the class. The campus axis prevents a local facility from becoming the hidden reference.

Four reserve tokens protect only independently adjudicated invalid attempts. A token preserves the exact campus, source lineage, module lineage, root versions and event proposition. The original packet remains permanent.

### 4.1 Valid no-flame law

A valid ignition opportunity followed by no flame is a physical result. It may establish:

- source admission and initial state;
- igniter delivery and safe removal;
- numeric-zero flame observations;
- source recovery and disposition; and
- safe-state and return behavior.

It does not earn the cell's flame-state, transformation or thermal-consequence credit. It does not activate an invalid-attempt reserve. The program must create a prospective repair object with a new configuration version and a new proposition. The original event remains in the class history. Repeating an unchanged cell until flame appears would convert a physical outcome into selection bias.

## 5. Source and opportunity constitution

The registered event source is 100 kilograms. Using the inherited 800-kilogram-per-cubic-metre planning density, it occupies 0.125 cubic metre. The earlier `REA-100` planning article uses a 40-millimetre-diameter, twelve-metre insert; its nominal hold-up is 12.0637 kilograms, nominal schedule is 120 seconds, velocity screen is 0.82893 metre per second, Reynolds screen is 6,631, Froude screen is 1.324 and Weber screen is 879.5. These values identify the inherited source-transfer proposition; RBK-1 does not claim that the physical article has achieved them.

Each event lot contains 125 kilograms: 100 for the event, ten for assay, ten for conditioning and five for quarantine. Event source-mass expanded uncertainty may not exceed 1.5 kilograms. Environment may receive no more than 0.10 kilogram source-equivalent. Unaccounted source-equivalent may not exceed 0.75 kilogram. Ancestry recovery must be at least 0.995 and cross-assignment no greater than 0.005 until RBK-1 testing supports a tighter limit.

The dry igniter receives one ten-second opportunity from `T+15` through `T+25` seconds. The accepted RBH-1 reference limits remain in force: commanded-to-delivered energy ratio 0.95 to 1.05, onset error no more than ten milliseconds, duration error no more than fifty milliseconds, location error no more than five millimetres, repeat-coordinate error no more than two millimetres, abort within 100 milliseconds, residual temperature no more than 80 degrees Celsius at sixty seconds and no more than 45 degrees at ten minutes, and no precommand or uncommanded energy.

Igniter conformance is a prerequisite to interpret the event. It is not flame evidence.

## 6. Thermal screen and consequence account

At the registered 2.5-square-metre field:

\[
\dot{m}=2.5\times0.055=0.1375\ \text{kg/s}
\]

\[
\dot{Q}=0.1375\times42.588=5.85585\ \text{MW}
\]

\[
\dot{Q}_{rad}=5.85585\times0.35=2.0495475\ \text{MW}
\]

\[
t_{screen}=100/0.1375=727.273\ \text{s}=12.1212\ \text{min}.
\]

The source lower-heating-value input is 4,258.8 megajoules. `CAL` must independently account for incident radiant energy, convective transfer, surface and structure storage, exhaust enthalpy, residual chemical energy and unaccounted energy. The screen supplies instrument range and public boundaries. It cannot populate observed flux, heat partition, duration or efficiency.

The module uses an inner calorimetric ring to resolve local field variation and an outer boundary to resolve consequence. A single remote heat-flux gauge is insufficient because a source area can remain constant while flame geometry, wind, optical thickness and surface storage change. Thermal reconstruction carries uncertainty and covariance rather than converting every sensor to one apparent power curve.

## 7. Source-derived transformation account

Combustion imports oxygen and creates products whose gross mass is not source mass. RBK-1 therefore forbids a naive product-mass closure. `MOL` uses a source-equivalent account based on the accepted source assay and source-derived elemental or molecular signatures. Required destinations are:

1. untransformed source in dispenser and route;
2. untransformed source in the field and residue cassette;
3. recovered liquid or condensed source;
4. source-equivalent in collected gas and particulate products;
5. source-equivalent in surfaces, filters and product routes;
6. source-equivalent in samples;
7. source-equivalent in environment; and
8. unaccounted source-equivalent.

Ambient oxygen, nitrogen, water vapor and background carbon remain separate carrier accounts. The workbook deliberately leaves material-specific stoichiometric coefficients open until source assay exists. A physically observed zero is numeric. An unknown coefficient is blank. Neither may be coerced into the other.

`MOL` must register product-route transit time, dispersion, wall retention, condensation, sampling fraction and destructive-analysis loss. A late product observation cannot be placed at flame onset by subtracting an assumed constant unless that route's delay distribution is calibrated under the event state.

## 8. Candidate water is zero; public water is separate

`ER-04` candidate water is exactly 0 cubic metre. The value must be present as numeric zero in the event record, candidate-water ledger, route-state record and module baseline. A blank fails.

Public water remains available in the casualty system. Its initial issued quantity is also numeric zero, but the public authority may introduce water to protect people, the site or the environment. If it does, gross public water, returned water, retained water, vapor, casualty custody, source carried by water and environmental disposition receive a separate ledger and causal time. The action cannot be relabeled as candidate water or ignored because the class was intended to be dry.

This separation anticipates the next rung. `RBL-1` will add candidate water as an experimental input; RBK-1 creates the dry reference against which that coupled system can later be interpreted.

## 11. Return, exchange and the 1,008-hour boundary

Return is part of the experiment because fire can create latent seal damage, absorbed source, changed emissivity, sensor drift, product-route deposits, embrittlement and false-clean baselines. Each module receives twenty-eight work packages and twenty-four independent return decisions across source custody, residue, products, filters, optics, ion elements, calorimetry, wiring, safeing, evidence, structure and environmental state.

The fixed boundary is 1,008 hours. A provisional 504-hour reuse route may be studied only through ninety-six separate exchange trials that compare a removed hot-zone cassette with a known clean replacement. It cannot shorten the class boundary. If the exchange program fails, the 1,008-hour rule remains without schedule relief.

Two outside-origin reconstructions per module lineage must show that another campus can determine retained source, calibration state, safeing state and baseline identity from the sealed dossier and physical witnesses. Local maintainer judgment may trigger a hold but cannot issue return authority.

## 12. Architecture qualification and hard gates

Root selection is lexicographic. A lineage must first pass every hard condition: numeric-zero specificity, bounded false declaration, required range, time and location accuracy, denied-service survival, saturation recovery, contamination discrimination, calibration closure, native evidence, outside replay and return. Only lineages that pass may be compared on burden such as exchange time, service consumption, evidence rate and lifecycle cost.

The integrated module must then pass all ninety-six full-scale cycles. Each serviceable module executes:

1. a registered baseline event;
2. a photon-root dynamic-range challenge;
3. an ion-root residue-state challenge;
4. a product-route transit and collection challenge;
5. a denied primary-time and network challenge; and
6. a heat-exposed cassette exchange and return regression.

Every integrated cycle admits 100 kilograms of energetic source; zero, igniter-only and nonenergetic reference work has already occurred in the root campaign. The qualification bank therefore governs 9.6 tonnes of event source. The maximum class population adds 1.6 tonnes. Assay, conditioning and quarantine allocations remain separate from event mass.

Hard module failure cannot be offset by another cycle. A common cause across builder lineages reopens the root program. A module withdrawal reduces available bank capacity until a fully qualified spare receives a new assignment.

## 17. Stop, amendment and release law

RBK-1 stops or narrows if:

- either flame-state root depends on the other root's clock, classifier or calibration;
- `MOL` infers products only from missing source;
- `CAL` derives heat from a flame label rather than independent measurements;
- a root changes source area, source route or ignition opportunity beyond the registered envelope;
- a bright igniter or hot surface can satisfy both flame roots;
- source-derived product transport delay is unbounded;
- candidate water is blank or nonzero;
- public water is unregistered, combined with candidate performance or omitted from custody;
- a valid no-flame or adverse event is repeated under the same proposition;
- a reserve changes the exact cell or root versions;
- root packets exchange dispositions before seal;
- source-equivalent or energy accounts use assumed observations;
- environment exceeds 0.10 kilogram source-equivalent or unaccounted exceeds 0.75 kilogram;
- a module returns before 1,008 hours;
- outside replay needs the origin campus; or
- the parent capacity ledger is absent or overrun.

Release authority remains narrow. Root qualification does not issue source. Module qualification does not issue a class event. One passing class event does not release another cell. Twelve credits do not qualify water or a candidate. `ER-04-QUALIFIED` has no authority over `ER-05`, higher source, procurement, operational deployment, export or exterior rendering.

## 18. Research stocktake

RBK-1 changes the program's understanding in six ways.

First, fire reference is not a sensor-selection problem. It is a compound public architecture whose state, transformation, consequence, custody and return authorities must remain separate.

Second, integration creates new physics. Accepted source, igniter and ancestry systems do not remain accepted when combustion makes them interact. Clean-sheet module and root competition are justified by the new proposition, not by institutional preference.

Third, the dry class is the only credible baseline for later water claims. If the Department cannot decide a 100-kilogram dry event, it cannot attribute what candidate water changes.

Fourth, valid no-flame evidence is valuable and inconvenient. Protecting it from repetition bias is more important than keeping the schedule visually complete.

Fifth, post-fire return is not maintenance overhead. The 1,008-hour observation is part of truth because latent material and contamination state determine whether a later event is independent.

Sixth, the seven-year parent is now nearly full at maximum schedule. The research has converted a generous-looking program into an explicit scarcity of time, annual support and people. That is analytical progress: the next decision can no longer hide unknown fire-water physics inside a provisional line.
