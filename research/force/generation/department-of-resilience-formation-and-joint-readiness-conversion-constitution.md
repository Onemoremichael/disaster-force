# Pass 191 — Formation and joint-readiness conversion constitution

**Research object:** `DFG-1`  
**Decision level:** Department force generation  
**Verdict:** **CLOSE**

## Branch and resolution

The deepest live branch is `DFG-1`, the first of five permitted passes after `MPC` returned industrial availability to Department force generation.

**Resolution test:** reversing the conversion law would let factories, accepted machinery or paper task forces satisfy the national force promise, changing the meaning, size and authority of the entire Department; the object passes at Department scale.

Pass 190 proved an industrial availability service. It deliberately did not create a formation.[^1] This pass seals the boundary.

## Decision: five states, not one inventory

The Department shall use a **serial conversion lattice**. Five objects remain legally, fiscally and operationally distinct:

1. **industrial availability** — reproducible configuration, workforce, suppliers and production path;
2. **accepted article set** — controlled equipment belonging to one prospective formation;
3. **service-ready formation** — the article, people, command, stocks, movement, receiver and regeneration system proved as one serial;
4. **joint mission contribution** — that formation integrated into one mission-system and campaign world; and
5. **campaign credit** — a concurrent stack accepted for public effect, rights and transfer.

Only the fifth object satisfies a national campaign claim. Industry is the force’s regeneration source, not a reserve component. An article can exist without a crew; a crewed article can lack a receiver; a ready formation can be promised twice; a collection of ready formations can fail together. No lower state may be renamed upward.

Five rivals face ten conjunctive gates. Industrial-equivalent accounting, accepted-equipment inventory, service self-certification and paper joint aggregation all fail. Only the serial conversion lattice preserves distinct states, one formation identity, complete crews and sustainment, receiver closure, independent trials, exclusive reservation, regeneration, family-specific `N−1` and joint-world proof.

## One formation is a ten-warrant conjunction

A formation serial earns service-ready status only when ten current warrants bind the same object and interval:

- accepted article and genealogy;
- complete named crew, relief and household support;
- technical, safety and professional release;
- sustainment stocks, maintenance, residual custody and replenishment;
- mobility and broken-theater access;
- named receiver, interface and transfer conditions;
- lawful activation, civil priority, rights and stop authority;
- no-notice complete-formation trial under denial;
- exclusive reservation of article, people, stock, movement and receiver; and
- a cold regeneration path for the exact configuration.

The operating service owns article, profession and formation release. Strategic Sustainment owns stocks and regeneration. A Mission System Command owns end-to-end integration. The Civil Resilience Authority owns priority, lawful admission and transfer. The Resilience Proving Service owns the hostile trial. The Joint Capacity Authority owns serial reservation. None can compose the others’ warrants or waive an absence.[^2]

Rollback is immediate and specific. Configuration drift removes dependent evidence. Crew expiry, depleted stocks, lost access, receiver withdrawal or reservation collision returns the serial to `HOLD`. A failed joint trial removes joint credit without pretending the underlying article disappeared. History remains visible.

## The national denominator

The companion model instantiates all **5,251** heterogeneous formation equivalents from the forty-eight-family national inventory.[^3] It assigns ten source-empty warrant cells to every serial: **52,510** evidence cells. Actual service-ready formations and actual joint credit are therefore both zero.

The inherited family readiness factors imply an instantaneous obligation of **3,667** service-ready formations, the exact sum of forty-eight family-level ceiling operations. This is not a target percentage applied to a fungible fleet. Every family must meet its own obligation. The model distributes the planning obligation across seven readiness districts and evaluates 384 intact or single-district-loss family worlds. Every planning case retains protected demand; none earns authority because no serial evidence exists.

Joint admission is separate. Six mission systems across ten simultaneous campaign worlds create sixty admission cells carrying **2,420** concurrent formation contributions. Each cell requires a complete interface definition, concurrent joint trial and civil admission. Success in one campaign world does not create universal readiness.

## Capital and regeneration consequence

The conversion law exposes a fiscal category error. The USD 230B initial-formations account is one learning subaccount inside the USD 1.00T technology program. The inherited capital screen for all 5,251 formations is USD 9.41T, leaving USD 9.18T beyond that initial account before common capital and program reserve. Spending either amount earns no warrant. The same force carries 11.5 million direct affiliated billets; equipment delivery cannot substitute for their trained, current and supportable assignment.[^4]

A 15–30-year formation-life sensitivity implies roughly 176–351 formation replacements and USD 314–627B of formation capital throughput per year. These are burden screens, not selected service lives. They show why regeneration must be designed as an operational mission rather than deferred maintenance.

The enabling technology is accordingly ambitious: independently reconstructable formation truth twins; dynamic reservation graphs spanning articles, people, stocks, carriers and receivers; autonomous acceptance cells; synthetic receiving societies; no-notice mission worlds; portable crew authority; self-describing sustainment sets; receiver-interface compilers; public regeneration threads; rights-aware command runtimes; formation-fatigue observatories; and adversarial joint-stack compilers. These are physical truth and mobilization systems, not a readiness dashboard.

The [companion workbook](../../../models/force/generation/department-of-resilience-formation-and-joint-readiness-conversion-constitution.xlsx) contains twenty-one sheets, 5,251 formation serials, 384 denial worlds, sixty joint admission cells, 5,707 passing audits and four restored mutations. Every sheet is rendered and inspected; all evidence remains source-empty.
