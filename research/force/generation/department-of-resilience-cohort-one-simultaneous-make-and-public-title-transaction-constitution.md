## Decision

Adopt one atomic issue slate with 408 severable contract lots. All 144 serial `MAKE` warrants issue together only after every inherited Experiment Definition Record gate closes and every required lot is validly constituted. Partial issue is prohibited. This locks comparative intent without requiring all fabrication to start on one day: the six predetermined work-start waves remain weeks zero, five, ten, fifteen, twenty and twenty-five, all before the first event opportunity at week 104.[^1]

The 408 lots comprise 144 serial articles, 144 serial tooling-use burdens, forty-eight public fixtures and seventy-two metrology-and-custody scopes. Each technology order therefore carries seventeen independently priced, accepted, payable, step-in-capable and cancellable lots. One portfolio prime, one prime per order, serial-only awards and government self-build all fail: each respectively centralizes outcome leverage, preserves root common control, omits public proving infrastructure or collapses builder, host, measurement and acceptance authority.

Atomic issue and severable performance are complements. Before issue, one missing lot holds the whole slate. After issue, one supplier failure holds or replaces only the affected lot, serial generation or declared incurable root pair. Cross-default across siblings or fiscal jurisdictions is otherwise prohibited.

## Public material title without premature risk transfer

Every serial carries six title states from reservation through residual custody: serial reservation, accepted source material, tagged work in process, incorporated subassembly, completed article and custody-or-residual transfer. Public beneficial title begins when tagged material is accepted, follows every transformation, and survives rejection, step-in, wreck, salvage and destruction. Contractor risk of loss nevertheless persists through construction acceptance; public title cannot excuse care or convert progress into public acceptance.

A post-title change of contractor, configuration or material creates a new serial generation. The prior generation remains in the public ancestry record. Insolvency permits a clean successor to use publicly held material and the frozen dossier, but does not transfer the origin builder's people, keys, tools or live coaching. This preserves regeneration as an independent claim rather than assisted repair.[^2]

## Performance-blind payment and construction acceptance

Each lot has eight bounded milestones: slate reservation, public-title ledger, configuration freeze, witnessed fabrication, construction complete, construction acceptance, custody-and-dossier transfer and non-performance closeout. Their shares are 5%, 15%, 10%, 20%, 20%, 15%, 10% and 5%. Across 408 lots they create 3,264 planned claims. A claim pays verified work, custody and reconstructability; no fee, share or remedy depends on an experimental result.

Construction acceptance is a separate 1,152-record ledger: eight gates for each serial cover title chain, frozen configuration, material account, interface-and-fixture fit, safe transport, metrology installation, as-built reconstruction and custody transfer. These gates may test dimensional conformance and safe handling. They may not operate the service-effect path, judge event readiness, supply an accepted observation or create capability credit. Factory completion is therefore neither acceptance nor evidence.

Valid adverse experimental performance is final: no cancellation, clawback, warranty conversion, lost-profit penalty or automatic retry follows. Latent construction nonconformance is different and may be rejected against the frozen build record. Invalid opportunities preserve earned construction payment but require a new event warrant. Cost overrun remains inside the affected lot; it cannot borrow from a sibling, another jurisdiction or protected proof reserve.

Thirty common-control prohibitions separate the two builder roots, origin and reconstruction teams, candidate and fixture operator, primary and witness metrology, construction and performance acceptance, finance and result disposition, and archive and procurement. An incurable root-control conflict terminates the root pair; it does not invite centralization.
