## Decision: a federated state vector with typed exchanges

The Department will govern each mission as six asynchronous service contracts, joined by typed bilateral exchange records. It will not use a universal incident phase, six independent completion claims, a mesh of bespoke handshakes or a central digital twin.

One scalar phase is false: hazard control may still be stabilizing while lifeline production operates and human continuity transfers households. Independent contracts permit each system to report success while another waits on an undisclosed dependency. Pair-specific handshakes create thirty incompatible dialects. A central broker makes the force depend on one network, one data model and one surveillance surface.

The selected object is a `6 × 7` mission state vector. Every system occupies one evidenced service stage at a time, while directed records convey what another system may rely upon. The mission does not “advance” as a whole. It closes only when all six contracts reach an accepted terminal state and every residual has an owner.

## One seven-stage law, six system meanings

Every contract uses the same verbs, but its evidence remains system-specific:

1. **Establish state and authority:** identify the affected service, population, place, uncertainty, invitation, rights and accountable owner.
2. **Bound immediate failure:** prevent uncontrolled worsening within the system’s declared safety or continuity envelope.
3. **Assign service:** bind a provider, receiver, capacity, validity interval and non-service consequence.
4. **Prepare the path:** configure origin, route, endpoint, access, custody and fallback.
5. **Deliver or produce:** move the person, object, energy, material or information—or project the service to the origin.
6. **Accept and sustain:** the receiver verifies usable service under declared quality, rate, duration and exception limits.
7. **Transfer and regenerate:** return authority, close residuals, repair or replace consumed capability and preserve configuration history.

`MS-ET` applies these stages to access paths and nodes; `MS-CT` to authority and public truth; `MS-HC` to hazard pathways; `MS-LF` to utility service; `MS-HU` to relationship-bearing people and care; and `MS-RT` to stocks, repair, civil transfer and force renewal. A system may move backward when evidence expires. No local stage number implies priority over another system.

## The joint service exchange record

Each of the fifteen system pairs carries two directed records. Every record must explicitly resolve six domains, even when the lawful value is “none”:

1. operational state and uncertainty;
2. authority, consent, rights and limitations;
3. service or capacity, with quantity, rate and duration;
4. physical flow and custody of people, mass, energy, data and residuals;
5. evidence, calibration, provenance and configuration;
6. return, residual ownership and regeneration.

The record has twenty mandatory fields: transaction and parent-interface identifiers; campaign and mission; provider and receiver system/root/configuration; provider release stage; receiver prerequisite stage; issue time and validity; origin and destination; state and uncertainty; authority and limitations; service quantity and units; flow class; custody signatures; evidence method and provenance; configuration version/hash; residual owner and deadline; and acceptance, correction, revocation or rejection.

A provider may issue `PROVISIONAL` information for planning. Only `RELEASED` evidence can satisfy a recipient prerequisite, and only `ACCEPTED` service earns joint credit. `EXPIRED` or `REVOKED` records remove dependent credit. Mutually dependent releases use a joint-release barrier: both sides prepare, validate independently and commit together; timeout triggers a declared safe rollback. Disconnection is normal. Records must be locally verifiable, append-only, mergeable after reconnection and readable without a Department-wide cloud service.

The thirty directions encode material obligations. Entry gives hazard control delivered formations and receives safe operating windows; lifelines give human continuity usable water, power and thermal service and receive protected-cohort loads; civil terrain gives every operator lawful destinations and receives verified availability and effects; regeneration gives all five systems fuel, replacement, repair and return capacity and receives residuals, failures and transfer conditions. The workbook states all thirty rather than hiding them inside a generic “coordination” interface.

## Evidence burden and industrial consequence

One mission now contains forty-two system-stage judgments and thirty directed exchange records covering 180 domain judgments: 222 governed decisions. The Pass 171 estate of 120 missions therefore produces 5,040 stage judgments, 3,600 exchange records and 21,600 domain judgments—26,640 decisions before any replicated-force claim. Twenty fields create 72,000 required record instances.

This is not chiefly a software procurement. It establishes an industrial interface law for pumps, aircraft, bridges, shelters, reactors, clinics, robots and public offices. Every future article must expose its service state, capacity envelope, custody events, evidence provenance, residuals and safe rollback in the same grammar while retaining mechanism-specific physics. Novel technology is invited; semantic opacity is not.

The companion model traces every family to one route, every route through seven stages, every system pair in both directions and every campaign mission to its evidence burden. It creates no readiness, procurement, supplier, location or exterior-form authority.
