# RAC-1 — Root A reference-article construction and build-release constitution

## Decision abstract

`RAC-1` converts the `REA-100` low-source reference article from a campaign requirement into a controlled construction hypothesis. It defines the physical topology, common interfaces, two unlike build lineages, drawing system, part families, material-lot splits, manufacturing routes, dimensional stacks, pressure boundary, surface-state controls, safeing-work budget, instrument interfaces, article travelers and release predicates needed before the Department can authorize a physical build. It does not issue a fabrication drawing, release an article, accept hardware or authorize source entry.[1–6]

The central physical correction is simple but consequential. The inherited article has a 2.4-metre by 1.6-metre by 0.6-metre transport envelope and a 12-metre controlled fluid path. Those dimensions cannot describe one rigid state. RAC-1 therefore makes the article a six-module transport kit. Six protected two-metre modules stow in the transport frame, leave it in a controlled sequence and deploy on six surveyed saddles as one straight 12-metre line. The public fluid experiment exists only in the deployed state.

Both lineages retain a 40-millimetre bore, 12-metre reference length at 20°C, common endpoint datums, common port stations, bounded internal steps, matched surface-state requirements and identical source, receiver, measurement and safeing interfaces. `BL-X` creates a fused path through five permanent orbital girth welds. `BL-Y` creates a serviceable path through five keyed double-barrier mechanical joints with monitored interspaces. The lineages are not cosmetic alternatives. They expose different failure mechanisms under a common experimental geometry.

The nominal 100-kilogram transfer over 120 seconds produces approximately 0.829 metres per second in the 40-millimetre bore and a Reynolds number near 6,631 under the declared surrogate properties. The 60-second high-rate bracket reaches 1.658 metres per second and Reynolds number 13,263. A conservative instantaneous surge screen at a 1,000-metre-per-second wave-speed bound reaches 1.326 megapascals. RAC-1 therefore adopts a 2-megapascal design boundary and a 3-megapascal liquid proof, despite expected steady losses of only about 9.9 kilopascals at the high-rate condition. Normal control remains at 0.1 megapascals and relief at 0.25 megapascals; the larger boundary protects against a failed surge-control assumption rather than becoming a normal operating target.

Thermal behavior changes the geometry more than fabrication tolerance does. Across −20°C to 50°C, the 12-metre `BL-X` path changes free length by approximately 13.44 millimetres; `BL-Y` changes by 10.92 millimetres. The inherited ±2-millimetre length tolerance is therefore a reference-temperature build condition, not an operational restraint target. Each lineage fixes the path at the six-metre midpoint datum and gives both ends at least 15 millimetres of sliding travel. The accepted line must expand without transferring hidden axial load into valves, instruments or receivers.

RAC-1 defines ninety-six controlled drawing sheets, seventy-two part families, seventy-two conserved material/process lot splits, thirty-two lineage process operations, 360 article-section travelers, twenty-four joint families, forty-eight worst-case tolerance stacks, 192 signed contributors and 288 article build gates. Every drawing, part, lot, process, traveler, joint, surface record, gate and article release begins `OPEN`. Planning completion cannot convert those states into physical evidence.

The construction institution requires 180 positions over an 18-month program. Its gross allocation is $64 million in capital and $38 million per year in support, producing $121 million of 18-month exposure. The full amount is nested inside `RAP-1`, leaving an explicit parent balance of $184 million in capital and $74 million per year. Ten technology programs carry a separately visible $125 million cross-classified allocation. They address bore metrology, surface-state mapping, monitored cartridge joints, weld execution records, passive-safe actuation, physical state memory, low-state energy autonomy, evidence-native sensor mounts, robotic internal inspection and machine-reproducible build compilation.

The next research object is `RAB-1`: one development article from each lineage, the fixtures that create and interrogate them, and a destructive build-qualification campaign. Exterior rendering remains withheld. RAC-1 defines an internal research instrument and its deployment geometry, not a field vehicle.

## 1. Research stocktake

The Root A chain now has three distinct layers. `RAE-1` defines an executable low-source event dossier and corrects the mismatch between a 100-kilogram event and the 169.646-kilogram static inventory of the inherited 150-millimetre route. `RAP-1` defines the article bank and physical campaign needed to establish whether a 40-millimetre reference article, its safeing paths, measurements, containment and evidence can be accepted. RAC-1 now defines how that article could be constructed without collapsing two unlike build mechanisms into one supplier design.[1–3]

The earlier campaign established eighteen article roles: six operational articles, six matched functional twins, three calibration skeletons and three destructive witnesses. It also established twenty controlled article sections, 216 final control elements, 648 safeing paths, 3,348 path trials, 192 inert-fluid route trials, 2,592 installed channels and 15,252 physical acceptance gates. Those populations are inherited. RAC-1 does not resize the campaign to make construction easier.

What remained absent was the definition between nominal geometry and an accepted built state. A 40-millimetre bore did not specify how six modules share a datum, how joint steps are measured, how weld heat is retained, how clamp preload is reconstructed, how thermal growth avoids hidden restraint, how ports avoid unmeasured inventory, how spring work reaches the final element, or how an article release fails when someone signs an incomplete record. RAC-1 makes those questions explicit.

This is a microcosm of the larger Department of Resilience. A force able to deploy desalination plants, long water routes, mobile substations, fire aircraft and autonomous engineering systems needs more than procurement specifications. It needs public technical objects against which materials, machines, sensing, safeing and industrial methods can be tested. The construction authority described here is the seed of that sovereign engineering capacity.

## 2. The physical-state correction

### 2.1 Stowed and deployed are different configurations

The article’s 2.4-metre transport length cannot contain a straight 12-metre pipe. A coiled or serpentine line would fit, but it would add curvature, secondary flow, high and low points, joint clustering and drain behavior that do not belong in the low-source reference question. A telescoping line would add sliding seals and varying internal diameter. Folding hard pipe would add hinges and internal discontinuities. RAC-1 rejects all three as hidden changes to the experiment.

The adopted topology is a modular straight line:

| State | Physical definition | Governing datum |
|---|---|---|
| stowed | six protected two-metre modules, support rail, valves and service kits inside one transport frame | transport base and restraint faces |
| extraction | one module leaves the frame at a time under controlled lift and identity | module serial and lift points |
| assembly | modules occupy surveyed saddles; joints remain accessible and unaccepted | deployed bore origin |
| deployed reference | one straight 12-metre centerline with fixed midpoint and sliding supports | upstream seal face, midpoint and downstream seal face |
| teardown | joints, witnesses and modules retain their event and configuration identity | last accepted deployed baseline |

The transport envelope therefore governs handling, restraint and stowed center of gravity. It does not govern the deployed experimental footprint. The deployed line requires approximately 12 metres of axial run plus source, receiver, manual-access and rescue volumes. Those volumes belong to the public rig, not to an imagined self-contained field vehicle.

### 2.2 Six modules preserve inspectability

Six two-metre modules create five internal joints and two endpoint interfaces. That count is enough to expose repeated joint behavior while keeping every preassembly surface accessible. Each module can receive full bore mapping, roughness observation, port-depth measurement and retained witness coupons before assembly. An internal robot can then traverse the complete 12 metres after joining and compare pre-joint and post-joint state.

The module count also creates a practical destructive program. A witness article can preserve one upstream module, one midpoint-joint module pair and one downstream module for terminal sectioning without treating an arbitrary cut as representative of the complete line. The destructive order is frozen before trial results to prevent investigators from selecting only favorable regions.

## 3. Authority and claim boundary

RAC-1 asks:

> Is the public definition of both `REA-100` construction lineages complete, internally closed and falsifiable enough to authorize development-article qualification?

It does not ask whether either lineage is accepted. A workbook row labeled `PASS` means that a declared planning calculation reconciles. An `OPEN` physical record means that the object has not been built or accepted. The maximum future RAC-1 decision is release of a controlled construction package into development-article qualification. Even that decision remains unavailable at the current baseline.

| Decision | RAC-1 contribution | Still-required authority |
|---|---|---|
| technical definition | coordinates, dimensions, joints, processes, measurements and release predicates | accepted controlled sheets and effect review |
| development fabrication | explicit build route and evidence plan | physical drawing, lot, process and fixture release |
| article build release | sixteen gates per serial | accepted as-built, NDE, proof, surface, energy and configuration evidence |
| RAP-1 execution | defined internal article architecture | accepted article bank and commissioned fixtures |
| source event | no authority | event dossier, source package and named issuer |
| field system or exterior | no authority | candidate, crew, mobility, deployment, production and service research |

The boundary is intentionally severe. A public research institution loses value if its definitions quietly become procurement endorsements. `BL-X` and `BL-Y` are research roots. They may be revised, separated or rejected by later physical evidence.

## 4. Common public interface

Both lineages share one `COMMON-40` interface class. The public class contains the following controlled quantities:

- 40.000-millimetre reference bore with ±0.050-millimetre diameter tolerance at 20°C;
- 12.000-metre deployed reference length with ±2-millimetre total tolerance at 20°C;
- six two-metre nominal modules and five internal joints;
- no internal joint step above 0.100 millimetres;
- common source-vessel, dispenser, pan, normal-receiver, casualty-receiver and water-boundary interfaces;
- common instrument stations and direct final-state witness volumes;
- common normal, relief, design and proof-pressure definitions;
- a fixed midpoint at six metres with free thermal travel toward both ends;
- common surface roughness, contact-state, deadleg and drained-residual observables; and
- common evidence, time, calibration and configuration identities.

These are not all equivalence conditions. Equal bore and station position support controlled comparison. Equal surface-state bands reduce one source of bias. Different joint mechanisms are intentionally retained. Analysis must keep lineage as a blocking variable until physical results justify pooling.

The public coordinate system separates stowed and deployed datums. `D00–D02` define the transport base, forward face and lateral face. `D03` is the upstream deployed bore origin. `D04` is the fixed six-metre midpoint. `D05` is the downstream seal face. Separate datums govern pan low point, both receivers, source vessel, evidence vault, manual access, lift pairs, optical slope and the outside-origin survey monument. A supplier coordinate system may exist, but every accepted measurement must transform to these public datums.

## 5. `BL-X`: fused metallic reference

`BL-X` uses six electropolished austenitic stainless modules with a 40-millimetre bore and 2-millimetre nominal wall. At the declared 8,000-kilogram-per-cubic-metre density, the 12-metre bare tube mass is approximately 25.33 kilograms. Pressure does not govern that wall: thin-wall hoop stress is about 20 megapascals at the 2-megapascal design boundary and 30 megapascals at the 3-megapascal proof. Handling stiffness, weld execution, port preparation, internal inspection and damage tolerance govern the preliminary wall instead.

The five girth joints are permanent within a released configuration. Each joint receives:

1. matched and serialized end preparation;
2. recorded purge state before arc initiation;
3. native current, voltage, travel and timing records;
4. internal root imaging before any blend or surface recovery;
5. external volumetric or surface examination appropriate to the joint state;
6. complete-bore mapping after all five joints are present; and
7. a retained adjacent-process joint made from the same parent lot, operator, machine and window.

The weld record is an observed physical process, not a machine program. A commanded current or travel rate cannot substitute for the recorded arc state. Any root intrusion, lack of fusion, excessive heat, purge loss or post-weld dimensional miss creates a configuration branch before repair. Repair evidence retains the original condition and cannot overwrite it.

`BL-X` exists because permanent fused construction may minimize crevice volume, preload maintenance and repeated-assembly variation. Its liabilities are equally important: heat distortion, hidden root geometry, inaccessible surface recovery and permanent loss of pre-joint state. Those mechanisms justify a separate lineage rather than an assumption that welded pipe is the neutral default.

## 6. `BL-Y`: serviceable cartridge reference

`BL-Y` uses six super-duplex cartridges with the same 40-millimetre bore and a 2.5-millimetre nominal wall. At the declared 7,800-kilogram-per-cubic-metre density, the bare tube mass is approximately 31.24 kilograms. The larger wall supports repeated end preparation, joint handling and instrument integration rather than pressure necessity.

Each of the five mechanical joints has a keyed radial alignment, two independent sealing barriers and a monitored interspace. The outer clamp supplies structural retention. The keys control relative rotation and limit radial step. The seals create distinct inner and outer leakage boundaries. The interspace gives a direct location for detecting which barrier failed. Joint acceptance requires measured end-face condition, seal identity, seal compression, preload distribution, axial gap, radial step, interspace volume and liquid leak state.

The 3-megapascal liquid proof produces approximately 3.77 kilonewtons of axial thrust on the 40-millimetre bore. The preliminary 15-kilonewton retention requirement therefore provides a factor near 3.98 before local load and cyclic knockdowns. That ratio is not a fastener selection. `RAB-1` must measure preload loss, seal relaxation, repeated assembly, contamination, temperature and asymmetric loading before any service interval exists.

`BL-Y` may permit module replacement, repeated internal access and faster causal localization after a defect. Its liabilities are seal aging, preload loss, joint step, crevice inventory and assembly dependence. The monitored interspace is therefore not an optional convenience. It is the physical feature that makes a double-barrier claim observable.

## 7. Hydraulic, pressure and surge constitution

The 40-millimetre bore has an area of approximately 0.001257 square metres. Under the declared 800-kilogram-per-cubic-metre density and 0.004-pascal-second viscosity, the planning screens are:

| Case | Mass rate | Velocity | Reynolds number | Friction loss | Slope contribution | Instantaneous surge screen |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| nominal level | 0.833 kg/s | 0.829 m/s | 6,631 | 2.886 kPa | 0 | 0.663 MPa |
| nominal ±2° | 0.833 kg/s | 0.829 m/s | 6,631 | 2.886 kPa | ±3.286 kPa | 0.663 MPa |
| high-rate level | 1.667 kg/s | 1.658 m/s | 13,263 | 9.895 kPa | 0 | 1.326 MPa |
| low-rate level | 0.417 kg/s | 0.414 m/s | 3,316 | 0.928 kPa | 0 | 0.332 MPa |

The Darcy factors are declared screening values rather than measured truth. The calculation is useful because it separates steady hydraulic demand from closure surge. A pump sized only for the approximately 10-kilopascal high-rate friction loss could still create a severe transient if a boundary closes instantaneously. Conversely, sizing every operating component for the conservative surge without a control hierarchy would add mass and obscure whether controlled closure works.

RAC-1 therefore sets four distinct boundaries:

- 0.1 MPa normal control boundary;
- 0.25 MPa relief set point;
- 2 MPa structural design boundary; and
- 3 MPa liquid proof boundary.

The 2-megapascal design boundary exceeds the conservative 1.326-megapascal instantaneous screen by approximately 51 percent. The relief system limits slower overpressure; compliant volume and measured valve travel limit surge. The structural boundary remains the final protection when those controls underperform. Proof uses liquid because a pneumatic failure would store and release materially more energy.

## 8. Temperature and dimensional control

Reference dimensions close at 20°C. For `BL-X`, the declared linear expansion coefficient of 16 micrometres per metre-kelvin produces −7.68 millimetres at −20°C and +5.76 millimetres at 50°C. For `BL-Y`, the 13-micrometre coefficient produces −6.24 and +4.68 millimetres. Extreme-to-extreme changes are therefore 13.44 and 10.92 millimetres.

If either line were fixed at both ends, the resulting restraint load could migrate into valve bodies, instrument bosses, clamps, receiver scales and the public rig. RAC-1 fixes one saddle at `D04` and permits the remaining saddles to slide. Each endpoint receives 15 millimetres of one-direction travel from the reference state, leaving 8.28 millimetres of travel margin for `BL-X` and 9.54 for `BL-Y` against half of the extreme span.

This distinction matters to the tolerance constitution. The ±2-millimetre deployed-length tolerance governs the measured line at the reference temperature after configuration freeze. It cannot be imposed at −20°C or 50°C. Every dimensional record retains object temperature, measurement time, thermal stabilization condition and the transform to 20°C.

## 9. Surface state, hold-up and recovery

The nominal line inventory is approximately 12.0637 kilograms. That value follows directly from bore area, length and surrogate density. It is small enough to create a valid 100-kilogram low-source event, but it is not negligible against mass-accounting limits.

The bore has approximately 1.508 square metres of wetted area. A uniform 0.1-millimetre film contains about 0.1206 kilograms of surrogate. Joint pockets, instrument ports and low points add discrete inventory beyond that film. The preliminary port architecture assigns 116 millilitres of total trapped volume, equivalent to 0.0928 kilograms at the declared density, against a 250-millilitre ceiling.

RAC-1 therefore refuses to use roughness as a proxy for recovery. Each of twelve fluid-path segment classes records four independent quantities:

- arithmetic roughness against a preliminary 0.8-micrometre ceiling;
- surrogate contact angle against a 50°–60° planning band;
- joint and port geometry from internal mapping; and
- drained residual against a 0.2-kilogram article ceiling, divided into six segment allocations whose sum cannot exceed the article limit.

The contact-angle band is a research hypothesis, not a material certificate. If the two alloys cannot reach a stable common wetting band without coatings that change inspection or contamination behavior, later work may separate the lineages rather than forcing nominal equality. The purpose of the common band is to keep construction mechanism visible, not to hide an incompatible surface treatment.

## 10. Safeing work and independent energy

The twelve final control elements are built as boundary assemblies rather than assumed single valves. Each has a primary route, an unlike diverse route and a local manual or passive route. Safe direction comes from released mechanical energy: springs close isolations, open casualty routes, trip electrical boundaries or seal evidence. Normal power may command or hold an operating state, but it does not supply the decisive final action.

The model calculates rotational work as torque multiplied by travel angle, then applies a 2.5 load factor. Across all twelve assemblies, required final-action work is approximately 666.3 joules. Installed spring energy is 1,332.6 joules. At the inherited 60-percent low-energy condition, approximately 799.5 joules remains available, leaving about 133.3 joules above required work. Each element also retains an individual positive margin; aggregate surplus cannot rescue one deficient element.

The electrical survival system has a different purpose. It must keep final-state sensing, the local evidence recorder, the causal-time root and release electronics alive after normal services disappear. The declared load requires 156.25 watt-hours before reserve and 203.125 watt-hours after a 30-percent allowance. A 400-watt-hour independent pack provides 240 watt-hours at the 60-percent state, leaving 36.875 watt-hours of margin.

These calculations establish two frequent ambiguities. Mechanical springs create the safe action. The battery observes, timestamps and seals it. Treating the battery as the final actuator would make a low-charge or electrical fault a common failure. Treating a spring as sufficient without direct state evidence would make a commanded release indistinguishable from a jammed mechanism.

## 11. Instrument, time and evidence interfaces

RAC-1 retains the twenty-four measurement families established in RAP-1: vessel mass, transfer mass, pressure, temperature, hold-up tomography, pan level, two receiver masses, geometry/slope, three path-state witnesses, final-element reference, causal time, two motive-energy states, two power states, leak collection, occupied-area state, water flow, two ancestry roots and evidence health. Six instances from each family appear on every article, producing 144 channels per article and 2,592 across the bank.

The construction package governs physical interface, not calibration result. Each family specifies mounting datum, orientation, insertion or stand-off, service envelope, maximum power, time interface, normal and independent power paths, cable route, local acquisition identity, calibration access and evidence destination. A channel cannot inherit location from a cable label or acquire calibration from another serial.

Wetted interfaces use flush diaphragms, swept tees or sealed low-volume sample valves. The port-volume ledger is physical: count multiplied by measured internal volume. An unused port remains in the ledger. A capped branch cannot become zero inventory by description.

The evidence-native mount is an important technology object. It should bind the physical mount, sensor serial, local datum, calibration identity, cable endpoint, power path, clock path and configuration hash at installation. If successful, later resilient-force equipment could carry a machine-readable measurement constitution from factory through deployment, repair and outside-origin replay.

## 13. Parts, lots and construction routes

Seventy-two part families divide into twenty-four common, twenty-four `BL-X` and twenty-four `BL-Y` families. Common families own public interfaces and service definitions. Lineage families own joint, material, mount and process mechanisms. Every family states whether it is publicly built, purchased from a qualified supplier or retained through two supply routes; all remain under public configuration identity.

Material conservation uses seventy-two lot splits. Each of twelve material or process families in each lineage is divided before state-changing work into:

1. article material;
2. adjacent-process evidence; and
3. retained archive.

The parent identity remains common across all three. The adjacent evidence must share heat, batch, machine, operator, preparation, environmental state and process window with the article. Archive material preserves later chemistry, microscopy, surface and dispute work. A perfect laboratory specimen from another lot has no article credit.

The two lineages each have sixteen ordered process states: authority freeze; lot receipt and split; datum survey; segment bore mapping; surface preparation; boss preparation; lineage joint execution; post-joint internal mapping; pan/receiver assembly; final-element installation; local-energy installation; instrument installation; time/evidence integration; liquid pressure proof; clean drain/residual mapping; and configuration freeze. The functional order is common. The evidence inside joint execution remains unlike.

Eighteen articles times twenty sections produce 360 travelers. A traveler closes only when the as-built observation, NDE result and independent acceptance are present. The timestamp is not the result. The workbook deliberately turns an accepted timestamp beside missing observations into `FAIL`.

## 14. Tolerance constitution

RAC-1 creates twenty-four functional stacks per lineage. Each stack has four signed contributors representing source geometry, process effect, assembly and measurement. Worst-case absolute sum governs release. Root-sum-square is retained only as a process diagnostic.

The stacks cover deployed length, bore diameter, endpoint coaxiality, joint step, slope, instrument stations, port orientation, valve alignment, spindle angle, seal compression, gasket intrusion, deadleg allocation, pan low point, drain grade, receiver datum, load-cell axiality, sensor depth, state-witness gap, actuator stop, manual access, rescue removal, lift interface, evidence-route separation and water-shed gap.

The preliminary `BL-X` bore stack uses 0.040 millimetres of the 0.050-millimetre allowance. Its internal-step stack uses 0.085 of 0.100 millimetres. `BL-Y` uses 0.045 millimetres of bore allowance and 0.095 millimetres of step allowance. Those two `BL-Y` stacks are closest to closure and receive priority in development fixtures.

No stack may be rescued by adjustment unless the adjustment range, lock, measurement and configuration consequence appear on the drawing. Rework changes the contributor history. A new measurement after rework does not erase the prior state.

## 15. Nonconformance and build release

Twelve nonconformance classes cover material identity, bore, weld root, clamp geometry, surface band, proof leak, safeing work, battery state, instrument interface, mass/CG, evidence continuity and unauthorized drawing change. Each class names an immediate physical action, decision owner, permitted branch and affected claim.

The governing rule is:

> A nonconformance is a new configuration branch with preserved ancestry, not a comment attached to the old configuration.

Every article has sixteen build gates: authority; drawing baseline; material ancestry; coupon evidence; process route; as-built geometry; joint evidence; pressure/leak; surface/recovery; safeing work; independent energy; instrumentation; mass/CG; access/rescue; nonconformance closure; and configuration freeze. Eighteen articles produce 288 gates.

An article release remains `OPEN` until a release authority acts. If an authority timestamps the release while any gate is open or failed, the release becomes `FAIL`. Only sixteen explicit passes can support a passing release. This logic prevents a signature from changing the meaning of missing evidence.

## 18. Technology and industrial consequences

The $125 million cross-classified technology program is not a collection of optional instruments. It attacks the construction mechanisms that currently prevent public equipment from carrying a complete, inspectable physical identity.

Automated bore metrology and robotic internal inspection should map diameter, ovality, step, port depth, surface state and retained contamination through a complete assembled line. The double-barrier cartridge joint should make seal state and failure location observable without creating unacceptable trapped inventory. A weld execution twin should link energy input, purge, travel and geometry to the final root state without treating a machine command as evidence.

Passive-safe actuation and mechanical state memory should preserve final action and direct evidence after total electrical loss. Low-state energy autonomy should retain sensing, time and two native evidence originals at a physically measured partial state. Evidence-native mounts and a machine-reproducible build compiler should carry configuration identity from drawing through part, lot, operation, inspection, calibration and article release.

At force scale, these capabilities support more than one small fluid article. The same public industrial base could qualify rapid water lines, desalination skids, mobile cooling plants, high-flow fire systems, temporary power networks, medical utility modules and environmental recovery equipment. The strategic export is not only hardware. It is a repeatable public method for proving what was built, what it can safely do and what remains outside its claim.

## 19. Acceptance state and next research object

The companion workbook contains forty-two populated sheets, 2,050 formula records, 120 passing assertions and no formula-error matches. Its dynamic challenge suite confirms that a tolerance overrun fails the owning stack and audit; incomplete accepted travelers and surface records fail; a premature article release fails; sixteen explicit gate passes can close that release; a reduced design-pressure boundary fails the high-rate screen; excess mass fails the handling closure; and financial changes propagate through the parent balance, exposure, summary and audit.

Those results validate the model’s logic, not the article. Ninety-six drawings, seventy-two part families, seventy-two lots, thirty-two process routes, 360 travelers, twenty-four joint definitions, twelve surface records, 288 build gates and eighteen article releases remain open. All eight downstream releases remain blocked.

`RAB-1` should now build one development article from each lineage. It should commission the bore-mapping, joining, preload, liquid-proof, drain, safeing-work, battery, instrument-mount and evidence fixtures; execute the complete lot and traveler chain; insert known construction defects; cut precommitted joints and surfaces; and determine which RAC-1 tolerances and process windows survive physical work. Only then should production-equivalent articles enter RAP-1.

Exterior rendering remains withheld. RAC-1 resolves the internal research object and its deployment footprint. It does not resolve a field platform’s mobility, crew, setup equipment, weather enclosure, operator protection, transport class, sustainment or production architecture.
