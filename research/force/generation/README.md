# Force generation

This layer explains how the Department of Resilience turns national resources into certified formations before a disaster.

The sequence is deliberate:

1. [Force composition](force-composition.md) defines components, professions and effect portfolios.
2. [Readiness and force generation](readiness-and-force-generation.md) defines when those components are actually employable.
3. [Mature force design](mature-force-design.md) assembles services, commands, schools, depots, reserves and authorities.
4. [Joint force allocation and order of battle](joint-force-allocation-and-order-of-battle.md) sizes the establishment against the concurrent national mission.

This subsystem ends at certified origin capacity. It does not claim that the force can traverse damaged networks or become public service at the destination.
