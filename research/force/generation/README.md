# Force generation

Force generation is the conversion of national resources into complete, certified formations before a campaign begins. It owns professional communities, unit design, recruiting, training, acquisition, bases, stocks, maintenance, readiness cycles, reserves and regeneration. It ends at certified origin capacity; projection and reception still determine whether that capacity can become public service.
