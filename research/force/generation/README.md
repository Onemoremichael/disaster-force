# Force generation

Force generation is the conversion of national resources into complete, certified formations before a campaign begins. It owns professional communities, unit design, recruiting, training, acquisition, bases, stocks, maintenance, readiness cycles, reserves and regeneration. It ends at certified origin capacity; projection and reception still determine whether that capacity can become public service.

## The governing sequence

```text
PUBLIC SERVICE REQUIREMENT
        │
        ▼
FORCE ARCHITECTURE
service owner · effect · formation · command · component
        │
        ▼
FORMATION CONFIGURATION
people · equipment · stocks · interfaces · sustainment · authority
        │
        ▼
READINESS GENERATION
train · certify · stage · maintain · replace · regenerate
        │
        ▼
NATIONAL ALLOCATION
concurrent demand · residual reserve · movement · reception · endurance
```

The [Department force architecture](../force-architecture.md) defines the common grammar. [Force composition](force-composition.md) expands the nine effect portfolios, formation building blocks, professions and components. [Mature force design](mature-force-design.md) develops the detailed unit families, package library and command relationships. The [National Formation Registry](national-formation-registry.md) governs forty-eight canonical family hypotheses and their ten-field service contracts. The [canonical allocation and fiscal reconciliation](canonical-force-allocation-and-fiscal-reconciliation.md) is the first registry-based national screen: forty-eight lines, explicit shared-capacity credits and renewed movement, reception, workforce and fiscal contradictions. The [Household Collection Group formation design](household-collection-group-formation-design.md) configures the largest line from workload upward, creates seven `HCC-5` companies and prevents organic, prepared and shared capacity from being counted as interchangeable. [Readiness and force generation](readiness-and-force-generation.md) governs posture, force pools, rotation and basing. The Pass 23 [joint force allocation and order of battle](joint-force-allocation-and-order-of-battle.md) remains the predecessor and arithmetic reconciliation control.

These papers are successive levels of one problem, not a list of alternative organizations. The canonical distinction is:

| Object | What generation owns |
|---|---|
| Service | profession, doctrine, lifecycle and acquisition sponsor |
| Effect portfolio | requirement vocabulary used to size formations |
| Formation | indivisible package receiving readiness credit |
| Component | availability, authority and mobilization terms |
| Force pool | available, preparing, committed or regenerating state |

Operational commands employ the result. They cannot waive the personnel, material, movement, reception, sustainment, authority or regeneration dimensions that made the formation ready.
