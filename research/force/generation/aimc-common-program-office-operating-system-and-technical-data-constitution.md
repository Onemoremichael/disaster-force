# AIMC Common Program-Office Operating System and Technical-Data Constitution

## Executive judgment

The Department of Resilience cannot create a new instrument of national power by applying ordinary program management to unfamiliar equipment. Its intended capabilities—mobile nuclear utilities, high-rate water systems, autonomous construction, distributed medical production, persistent atmospheric access, civil communications under infrastructure loss and other still-unsettled technologies—combine scientific uncertainty, public consequence, industrial concentration and operational dependence at a scale conventional acquisition is not designed to govern.

The institutional problem is therefore not merely how to buy large systems. It is how to let a public institution pursue radical technical possibility without allowing ambition, urgency, expenditure or organizational prestige to become substitutes for truth.

This pass defines the common operating system for the [Acquisition and Industrial Mobilization Command](department-resilience-acquisition-and-industrial-mobilization-command.md), or AIMC. Its central judgment is:

> **The unit of program control is an authority-bearing physical artifact attached to a configuration—not a date, review, expenditure percentage or platform narrative.**

A valid program decision exists only when the mission effect, tested configuration, evidence, technical rights, custody, receiving burden, cost finding and lawful release authority converge on the same serial state. If any element is absent, the decision remains open regardless of schedule pressure or money spent.

The mature architecture carries forty-eight Family Program Offices, sixteen cross-portfolio Technical Integration Offices, 192 concurrent programs, 576 materially distinct technical roots, ten earned physical artifacts per program, twenty-eight complete technical-data classes per root and seven configuration baselines per program. This produces 1,920 artifact transactions, 16,128 logical root–data obligations and 1,344 baseline records. The sixty-four offices contain 128,000 people, preserving the personnel boundary established in the preceding institutional pass while replacing its generic office assignment with a functional topology.

The complete USD 15.179 trillion, twenty-five-year force-capital envelope is allocated across the ten artifact classes. Calendar progress earns zero capital. The baseline model releases zero because this report creates no physical article, accepted evidence, complete data package or lawful production finding. This is not timidity. It is the distinction between a design for authority and the exercise of authority.

The architecture permits technological audacity. A program may pursue new physics, processes, forms, autonomy or industrial methods. It may not hide coupled burdens, self-grade evidence, deny the public enough data to reproduce the result, or convert emergency urgency into an undocumented waiver. Independent judgment is the containment structure that makes mission-led innovation at trillion-dollar scale governable.

No program, supplier or platform is selected here. All artifact evidence remains open, all decisions remain held and vehicle-rendering authority remains blocked.

## 1. The program office as a constitutional machine

Most program offices are organized around management functions: requirements, engineering, contracting, test, cost, schedule and logistics. That division is necessary but insufficient. A large organization can possess every specialty and still make invalid decisions if its internal transactions do not bind those specialties to one object.

The AIMC program office is designed as a **constitutional machine**. Its product is not coordination. Its product is a sequence of decisions that remain valid under personnel turnover, supplier failure, technical change, political urgency and later forensic review. Every consequential transaction must answer seven questions:

1. What civil condition is being guaranteed, for whom and within what distributional floor?
2. Which exact configuration, article, process and software state is under judgment?
3. What physical evidence supports the claim, under which damage world and uncertainty statement?
4. Does the public possess the data and rights required to inspect, repair, reproduce and replace it?
5. Can a receiving jurisdiction use, refuse, sustain and integrate the service without hidden burden?
6. What capital, workforce, production capacity and alternative opportunity are being committed?
7. Which institution has lawful authority to accept this specific finding or release this specific state?

The operating system rejects any decision that cannot bind all seven answers. A test report without serial configuration is historical information, not production evidence. A complete drawing set without process identity does not reproduce a product. A successful prototype without receiver acceptance does not produce civil service. A signed release without an independently accepted evidence chain is an administrative act without technical validity.

This design changes the meaning of management. Schedule, budget and risk registers remain important for forecasting institutional demand. They cannot manufacture evidence. A late program may still possess a valid artifact; an on-time review may possess none. Earned value becomes physical and authority-bearing rather than calendar-bearing.

## 2. A corrected office topology

The preceding AIMC design assigned 192 programs round-robin across sixty-four generic program offices. That allocation established scale but not organizational purpose. This pass supersedes that simplification with two complementary office types.

### 2.1 Family Program Offices

Each of the forty-eight force families receives one Family Program Office, or FPO. The office owns four overlapping increments of the same civil-effect family. A water-trunkline FPO, for example, carries the existing production increment, a field-learning increment, a representative-test increment and a new architecture competition at the same time.

This continuity prevents the familiar pattern in which production, sustainment and next-generation development become isolated organizations with incompatible evidence and data. Field failure from one increment must alter the damage worlds, interfaces and challenge questions of the next. The family, rather than an individual platform, is the durable acquisition object.

Each FPO is assigned 2,000 people. The staffing model allocates 120 to executive, legal and public-authority functions; 760 to mission and systems engineering; 440 to evidence liaison, technical data and configuration; and 680 to industrial mobilization, cost, contracting, operator integration and field learning. These are design allocations, not current personnel claims.

### 2.2 Technical Integration Offices

Sixteen Technical Integration Offices, or TIOs, each span twelve programs. Their job is not to impose common platforms. It is to expose cross-family physical contradictions early enough to change designs.

A mobile desalination utility, rapid pipeline, electric microgrid, littoral carrier and receiving water system may belong to different families while sharing flow, voltage, control, residual, transport and public-release interfaces. An individual FPO can optimize its machine and externalize system failure. The TIO holds the coupled budgets and representative integration states that no single family owns.

Each TIO also contains 2,000 people with the same broad staffing envelope. Its authority is different: it may stop an interface claim, require a cross-family article or open a coupled burden. It may not rewrite the civil effect owned by a family or select a preferred builder.

The resulting network contains sixty-four offices and 128,000 people. Forty-eight offices preserve longitudinal family knowledge; sixteen preserve lateral system truth. Neither can accept its own evidence or release serial production.

## 3. Ten artifacts that earn physical value

The common lifecycle contains ten sequential artifacts. They are not document titles. Each represents a change in the physical and institutional state of knowledge.

| Sequence | Artifact | Minimum accepted CTDP classes | Capital allocation | Governing question |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | Civil effect charter | 0 | $0.750T | What condition must become true, and what residual state is unacceptable? |
| 2 | Requirement and root-independence package | 8 | $1.550T | Are the burdens falsifiable and the technical paths materially unlike? |
| 3 | Architecture and burden closure | 14 | $1.300T | Do interfaces and coupled mass, energy, heat, flow, labor and access budgets close? |
| 4 | Engineering article acceptance | 18 | $0.900T | Does a serialized article match its declared build baseline? |
| 5 | Qualification and destructive evidence | 21 | $2.300T | Have limits, invalid states and causal failure modes been exposed physically? |
| 6 | Representative mission result | 23 | $3.800T | Does the effect survive declared damage and receiving conditions? |
| 7 | Independent reproduction | 28 | $3.300T | Can a second team reproduce the bounded result from public data? |
| 8 | Pilot-process qualification | 28 | $0.600T | Does a bounded line produce repeatable yield and accepted output? |
| 9 | Production release package | 28 | $0.250T | Do configuration, cost, capacity, custody and receiving findings converge? |
| 10 | Mobilization activation | 28 | $0.429T | Can no-notice output reach receivers as conforming service? |
|  | **Total** |  | **$15.179T** |  |

The first artifact has no root-data threshold because it precedes technical roots. That does not make it administrative. It is accepted by the Civil Guarantee Authority and fixes the public effect, population floor, lawful exceptions, invalid-state grammar and distributional burden against which every later artifact will be judged.

The next two artifacts create the competitive and architectural problem. A requirement package is incomplete if its nominally rival roots share decisive physics, process, facility, firmware, ownership or calibration ancestry. Architecture closes only when its internal and external burdens coexist in one state. A reactor cannot be accepted as a power capability if its exclusion, heat-rejection, water, security, residual and receiver requirements live in separate favorable scenarios.

Artifacts four through seven turn claims into reproducible evidence. The engineering article establishes what physically exists. Qualification and destructive trials establish where it fails. The representative result establishes whether the entire service chain works in a declared damage world. Independent reproduction establishes whether the state owns knowledge or merely witnessed a vendor performance.

Artifacts eight through ten distinguish a demonstrable system from a force. A pilot process proves repeatable manufacture and acceptance. Production release binds the exact process and configuration permitted to enter series. Mobilization activation proves that people, tools, materials, suppliers, transport, acceptance and receiving capacity can expand under surprise.

No artifact can jump the chain. The capital-control equation is:

`eligible tranche = tranche capital × accepted artifact × authority signature × prior-chain closure × data-threshold closure`

Every Boolean term must equal one. Expenditure, percent complete and calendar year are absent.

## 4. The complete technical-data constitution

Data is not a closing deliverable. It is force structure. If a decisive technical state exists only in the original builder's tacit knowledge, proprietary instrument, unrecorded process window or unavailable source environment, the Department does not possess a mobilizable capability.

Each of the 576 roots therefore carries a Complete Technical Data Package, or CTDP, with twenty-eight classes:

1. civil effect charter;
2. damage-world set;
3. receiving and operational-suitability statement;
4. invalid-state grammar;
5. system architecture and boundary definition;
6. interface-control definition;
7. mass, volume, energy, heat and flow budgets;
8. hazard and safety case;
9. full bill of materials;
10. bill of process and facility identity;
11. source code, software bill and executable models;
12. material and process specifications;
13. drawings, tolerances and three-dimensional definition;
14. calibration genealogy;
15. test methods, instruments and uncertainty;
16. serial, lot and as-built record;
17. deviation and waiver record;
18. production route, tooling, fixtures and acceptance;
19. supplier ownership and technical ancestry;
20. alternates and substitution qualification;
21. capacity and readiness plan;
22. packaging, transport and storage definition;
23. maintenance, repair, diagnostics and spares;
24. training, human factors and accessibility;
25. cyber, offline and failure behavior;
26. residual, waste and decommissioning plan;
27. rights, license and continuity escrow; and
28. the independent reproduction artifact.

The list is deliberately broader than engineering drawings. It joins product definition to evidence, industrial process, public consequence and lawful use. A technical package that cannot identify which furnace, coating route, training state, source repository or calibration ancestry produced an article is incomplete even if its geometry is exact.

Registration, submission and acceptance are separate events. The baseline root register requires twenty-eight classes, records zero submitted and zero accepted, requires eight technical-right categories and records zero delivered. A supplier's assertion of completeness produces no credit. Acceptance belongs to the independent data custodian or the named domain authority, and the accepted object retains immutable identity.

The CTDP also changes competition. Rivalry is measured below the prime contractor. Two corporate bidders are not independent if they share the same decisive process, controller, specialty material, model, test facility or beneficial owner. Conversely, a public pilot line or sovereign design arsenal can make a new entrant credible by supplying facilities without supplying a favorable finding.

The decisive practical test is independent reproduction: can a qualified second team reconstruct the decision and reproduce the bounded result without the original provider's discretionary help? If not, the Department has acquired access, not control.

## 5. Seven baselines and one serial truth

Every program maintains seven configuration baselines:

- **B0 Effect** — the public condition and unacceptable residual;
- **B1 Requirements** — burdens, invalid states and allocated performance;
- **B2 Architecture** — interfaces and coupled system budgets;
- **B3 Build** — the exact configuration used to create an engineering article;
- **B4 Trial** — the configuration, method, instrumentation and damage world that produced evidence;
- **B5 Production** — the process, tooling, software, acceptance and supply state authorized for serial output; and
- **B6 Mobilization** — the people, material, facility, route and receiver state that reproduces conforming service under activation.

The model creates 1,344 baseline records. Every one remains version `0.0.0`, evidence `OPEN` and decision `HOLD`. The notation is intentional. It prevents a planning document from silently creating a released configuration.

The baseline chain addresses a common category error: treating “the design” as one stable object. The configuration tested may differ from the one produced; the process produced may differ from the one mobilized; the software loaded in a fielded serial may differ from the source tree used to generate evidence. AIMC treats those differences as governed objects rather than administrative noise.

Every accepted finding carries the identities of the article, components, lots, software, process, facility, instrumentation, method and custodian. Later analysis can therefore ask whether a failure challenges one serial, one lot, one process window, one software state, one supplier lineage or the architecture itself.

## 6. Divided authority

The program operating system separates seven institutions.

| Institution | Affirmative power | Forbidden combination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Civil Guarantee Authority | accepts the public effect and burden | cannot select the builder or release production |
| Resilience Staff | sets requirements and portfolio priority | cannot grade evidence or waive missing data |
| AIMC | designs, buys, builds and mobilizes | cannot accept its own evidence |
| Independent Evidence and Data Authority | owns methods, originals, configuration findings and reproduction | cannot manage program schedule or builder |
| Receiving Service Authority | accepts operational suitability and local burden | cannot rewrite the national guarantee |
| Independent Cost and Capacity Authority | certifies cost, capacity and opportunity cost | cannot accept the mission effect |
| Production Release Board | integrates valid findings into serial or mobilization release | cannot create missing evidence by vote |

Authority separation is not a sequence of advisory reviews. Each institution can withhold a finding that the final transaction requires. The Production Release Board is powerful because it integrates complete findings, and limited because it cannot cure an incomplete one.

The design also separates positive and negative power. AIMC has strong positive power to create options: it can finance rival roots, commission public facilities, build articles, negotiate rights, mobilize production and fund discontinuous experiments. It lacks the negative power to redefine failure after seeing a result. The evidence authority has strong negative power to reject a claim but cannot use rejection to choose policy or manage the builder.

This is the enabling constitution for high-consequence innovation. The Department can pursue an audacious mechanism without requiring one institution to be simultaneously entrepreneur, safety authority, customer, judge and historian.

## 7. Change control as causal propagation

Change control is usually treated as a board deciding whether a modification is major or minor. AIMC instead asks what causal claims the modification reaches.

Ten change classes cover mission effect; damage world; interface; technical root and ancestry; facility, process and tooling; evidence method; cost; schedule; serial deviation; and receiving condition. Each class names an adjudicator, the baselines reopened and the mandatory consequence.

A schedule change reopens capacity and portfolio assumptions but earns no value. A test-method change can invalidate comparability even if the physical article is untouched. A facility or process change can reopen build, trial, production and mobilization findings because geometry alone does not preserve material behavior. An interface change propagates to every dependent family and receiver. A civil-effect change reopens the entire chain.

No change is minor merely because it is inexpensive. No change is major merely because it is visible. Consequence follows causal reach.

Waivers remain visible unresolved-risk objects. They identify affected serials, receivers, evidence and custody. A waiver may authorize a bounded use where law permits; it does not transform nonconforming evidence into a conforming finding. Emergency conditions can accelerate testing, convene authorities continuously and narrow a temporary use. They cannot allow the builder to self-approve or erase the record.

## 8. Failure, replacement and institutional memory

A force built for an increasingly hostile environment should assume that technical roots, suppliers, facilities, evidence methods, software states and distribution routes will fail. Replacement must therefore be part of the architecture, not an improvised response to embarrassment.

Twelve trigger classes produce automatic consequences: a failed nonnegotiable effect boundary; rights defect; shared ancestry; ownership change; process or facility loss; sustained schedule variance; material cost growth; invalid test; safety failure; production-yield collapse; mobilization-exercise failure; and receiver-burden failure.

The governing rule is:

> **Failure removes entitlement, not memory.**

The affected root may lose capital eligibility, production authority or ownership of the program. Its articles, data, deviations, raw evidence, causal findings and custody records remain public assets. The Department can activate an alternate root, fund a protected challenge, requalify a process or recompete an integrator without pretending that the prior path never existed.

This reverses the political economy of sunk cost. A failed program is not required to survive in order for its expenditure to have public value. Its value lies in accepted learning, preserved options and faster exclusion of invalid mechanisms. Equally, past expenditure creates no entitlement to future production.

Replacement credibility depends on the surrounding industrial design: public data custody, sovereign engineering competence, public test facilities, qualified alternate processes and protected technical roots. Without those assets, termination authority is nominal because the state cannot replace what it stops.

## 9. Financial architecture

The operating model assigns the entire USD 15.179 trillion force-capital boundary to the ten artifact classes. The allocation is a strategic planning decomposition, not an appropriation forecast, vendor price or claim that each program has identical physical cost structure.

Every program receives the same portfolio shares while absolute tranches scale to the capital boundary of its force family. This makes unlike programs comparable at the level of institutional uncertainty without pretending that a biological process line and a littoral carrier consume the same resources.

The model distinguishes three financial ideas:

1. **planned capital** reserves institutional capacity to pursue an artifact;
2. **spent capital** records cash, labor and asset use; and
3. **eligible capital** records value whose physical artifact and authority chain have closed.

Only the third enters physical earned value. Spending is never erased, but neither is it mistaken for accepted learning.

Public facilities require special treatment. The USD 2.3 trillion design, proving, foundry and pilot infrastructure established in the preceding AIMC pass remains nested in the force envelope. When a program uses a shared range or pilot line, the corresponding capital draw is traced through that program's artifact tranche. The facility is not added again as an independent total. This prevents the industrial commons from becoming a hidden double count.

The twelve-year program phasing model distributes capital for capacity planning, with the largest shares in middle years when engineering, qualification and representative work overlap. That phasing does not appear in the eligibility equation. Calendar time can reveal an institutional bottleneck; it cannot prove a mission effect.

## 10. Innovation under a hard evidence constitution

The Department's technological mandate should exceed adaptation of existing equipment. Existing markets will not reliably create mobile systems optimized for civil effect, public burden, damaged infrastructure, receiver diversity and rare compound demand. AIMC should deliberately create new technological and industrial possibility.

The program operating system allows technical roots to challenge inherited coefficients rather than merely propose new forms. A water program can seek a separation mechanism that changes energy, residual or intake dependence. A fire program can seek new sensing, suppressant, propulsion or mixed-airspace control. An energy program can seek passive nuclear heat and power architectures designed as arriving utilities. A medical program can seek sterile distributed production that changes cold-chain dependence. A construction program can seek autonomous manipulation whose safe-state behavior reduces exposed labor.

The mission charter fixes what cannot be traded invisibly: public safety, distributional floor, residual disposition, receiving burden, refusal, accessibility, degraded behavior, custody and evidence. Within that space, the technical roots are free to be discontinuous.

This combination changes the incentive structure. Builders are rewarded for falsifiable learning and reproducible capability, not for preserving a preferred platform. Challenge teams receive a protected route to decisive experiments but no promised production. Public facilities lower the capital barrier to testing a new process while independent evidence prevents those facilities from becoming advocacy organizations.

The result resembles the innovation role historically associated with national-security investment, but its objective function is different. The value is continuity of water, health, shelter, mobility, energy, communications, food, environmental control and local public authority under compound loss. Export power follows from systems that allied societies can inspect, operate, repair and locally produce—not from permanent dependence on the original supplier.

## 11. What the model demonstrates

The companion workbook operationalizes the constitution across seventeen sheets. It contains:

- sixty-four office records and a 128,000-person staffing reconciliation;
- 192 programs and 576 root records;
- 1,920 artifact transactions with formula-driven release eligibility;
- twenty-eight CTDP classes and 16,128 logical obligations;
- 1,344 configuration baselines;
- ten change classes and twelve replacement triggers;
- a complete USD 15.179 trillion artifact allocation;
- twenty-four decision gates; and
- 6,302 terminal audit controls.

All 6,302 controls pass at baseline, with zero spreadsheet error values. That result establishes internal coherence only. Evidence and decisions remain open or held.

Four adversarial tests probe the actual control logic. First, an artifact marked accepted but lacking its authority signature releases zero capital. Second, adding the valid signature to the first artifact releases exactly its $3.088 billion tranche for the selected example program. Third, accepting and signing the second artifact while reopening the first leaves the second ineligible. Fourth, changing the declared CTDP requirement from twenty-eight to twenty-nine classes produces exactly 577 failures: one global class-count control and all 576 root-constitution controls. Restoring the assumption restores all controls.

These tests matter more than the baseline pass count. They demonstrate that the workbook can distinguish a coherent empty state from a positive transaction and can fail loudly when a governing assumption diverges from the root ledger.

The model is available as [`aimc-common-program-office-operating-system.xlsx`](../../../models/force/generation/aimc-common-program-office-operating-system.xlsx).

## 12. Limits and next research boundary

This pass establishes a planning constitution, not an implementation manual. It does not determine legal placement of the seven authorities, the geographic distribution of offices, specific information systems, classification rules, workforce pipelines, supplier identities, program start dates or the technical merit of any root. The capital allocations are portfolio hypotheses. The staffing composition is a mature-state design. None is evidence of present capacity.

The artifact chain is deliberately common across force families, but later work may discover family-specific sub-artifacts. A nuclear utility, biological production system and public communications mesh will require different safety and evidence detail. Any extension should preserve the common constitutional transaction rather than fragment it into incomparable acquisition cultures.

The most important open question is now physical activation. A complete data package and production finding can still coexist with an industrial network that fails under surprise, transport loss, workforce absence, power interruption or receiving congestion.

The next force-generation pass should therefore define a **national industrial-mobilization exercise constitution**. It should specify no-notice activation, protected observation, serial and lot sampling, supplier and process-domain cuts, workforce muster, material release, utility interruption, transport and receiver throughput, acceptance yield, repair, replenishment, demobilization and public after-action custody. It should distinguish produced units from accepted units, shipped units from received service and nominal capacity from sustained conforming output.

That pass should also determine how exercise findings propagate into the 1,344 configuration baselines, 1,920 artifact transactions, readiness contracts, strategic material reserves and next program cohorts.

Vehicle rendering remains blocked. The project should first possess measured representative subsystem sections, credible internal arrangements, accepted coupled burdens and destructive evidence. The eventual renderings should express technical truth earned by the research rather than establish a form that later analysis feels compelled to defend.
