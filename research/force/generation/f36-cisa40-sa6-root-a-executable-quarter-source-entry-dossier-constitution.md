# RAE-1 — Root A executable quarter-source entry dossier constitution

## Decision abstract

`RAE-1` converts the low-source edge of the Root A safety program from a conceptual campaign into an executable physical evidence constitution. Its subject is deliberately narrow: the first 100-kilogram source-bearing event that a public proving institution could issue after the nonenergetic wave, but before any candidate mechanism, quarter-source route or exterior vehicle form receives authority. This is the point at which a research program either becomes a real institution—with owned articles, serialized states, independent acceptance and a lawful event—or remains an attractive sequence of test ideas.

The central engineering finding is that a low-source event cannot be created by turning down the full-source system. A 100-kilogram aliquot occupies 0.125 cubic metres at the inherited planning density. A 12-metre, 150-millimetre route holds approximately 0.212 cubic metres, or 169.646 kilograms. The proposed low-source inventory therefore cannot fill that reference route. Over the inherited 120-second interval it would produce a nominal velocity of only 0.0589 metres per second and a Reynolds number near 1,768. The event would primarily test an unfilled, near-laminar line, trapped inventory and uncertain initial wetting—not the source-entry proposition intended by the program.

RAE-1 consequently creates a public reference article, `REA-100`, around a replaceable 40-millimetre, 12-metre insert. Under the same 100-kilogram and 120-second conditions, nominal velocity becomes 0.8289 metres per second, Reynolds number approximately 6,631, Froude number 1.323 and Weber number 879.5. Line hold-up falls to 12.064 kilograms. This does not make the insert dynamically identical to the 150-millimetre route, and it grants no authority over that route. It creates a measurable control article for the low-source regime and makes the non-transfer claim explicit.[1–7]

The reference campaign contains forty-eight required valid events: two methods, two unlike truth roots, six public rigs and two entry classes. `QE-01` is a zero-source physical datum; `QE-02` admits 100 kilograms. Twenty-four `QE-01` events require forty-eight rig-days. Twenty-four `QE-02` events require seventy-two. Six invalid-attempt reserve tokens add fifteen rig-days and at most three additional source-bearing attempts. Base admitted source is 2.4 tonnes. Twenty-eight serialized 125-kilogram source packages create 3.5 tonnes of gross custody: twenty-four active packages and four root-protected reserves, although only three source-bearing reserve tokens may activate without amendment.

Execution is governed through 5,050 physical acceptance records. They include six reference articles, 120 cell sections, six configuration masters, twenty-eight source packages, 280 source-characteristic records, twelve water packages, six containment proofs, twenty-four receiver proofs, seventy-two final control elements, 216 safeing paths, 216 path proofs, 192 walkdown records, 144 neighbor proofs, 1,152 event-phase records, 1,536 hold records, 576 evidence obligations, 336 samples, forty-eight event issues, forty-eight results, twenty-four uncertainty budgets and eight technology campaigns. Every physical record begins `OPEN`. Planning completion cannot change that state.

The establishment is modeled at 384 positions, $360 million in gross capital and $168 million per year in gross support. Twenty-four-month gross exposure is $696 million. These amounts are fully nested within the already registered `RAS-1` program, so the net portfolio extension remains zero. A $470 million technology portfolio is cross-classified within the parent program rather than added to RAE-1 capital. Eight downstream releases remain blocked, including candidate entry and exterior rendering. RAE-1 is design-complete, physically unexecuted and intentionally incapable of authorizing a vehicle silhouette.

## 1. Research stocktake: from campaign logic to an issuing institution

The preceding work established the Root A chain in successive layers. `RFA-1` defined the drainage, firewater and recovery technology competition. `RFP-1` established a candidate-neutral public rig. `RFE-1` imposed a causal event clock, conserved the event denominator and separated intervention from performance. `RAM-1` defined method cards and estimators. `RAW-1` created the nonenergetic known-truth wave. `RAS-1` then defined the quarter-source safety case, including separate source and water ancestry, final control elements, unlike safeing paths and outside-site replay.[1–6]

That body of work was coherent but not yet executable. It could say that a source package was required without saying how many packages existed, what mass each contained, which event owned each aliquot or how quarantine survived an invalid attempt. It could require a safeing path without binding the path to a final element on a named rig. It could prescribe a first event without defining the physical article that made 100 kilograms observable. It could call for an acceptance dossier without enumerating the records that constituted one.

RAE-1 closes this gap by treating event issue as a sovereign institutional act. A public issuer does not authorize “testing” in general. It authorizes one registered proposition on one rig, with one article, one source or zero-source state, one water state, one configuration, one set of accepted final elements, one set of path proofs, one clock, one evidence plan and one rescue posture. Every field is joined by serialized identity and common baseline. The event exists only if the conjunction exists.

This is a broader lesson for the Department of Resilience. A rescue-industrial economy cannot be created solely through ambitious procurement targets. It needs public institutions able to define severe but lawful environments, own neutral reference articles, measure performance independently, preserve adverse results and certify bounded capabilities. Military innovation is supported by ranges, test squadrons, arsenals, standards, operational evaluation and configuration control. A coequal resilience force requires equivalent epistemic infrastructure. The public proving estate is not overhead around the machine; it is the institution that makes a trustworthy machine market possible.

## 2. The proposition and its authority ceiling

The RAE-1 proposition is:

> A public rig can create, safely terminate, contain and independently reconstruct a zero-source or 100-kilogram Root A entry event using a known reference article, while preserving source identity, water identity, causal order, physical final states, environmental custody and a valid adverse outcome.

The proposition is intentionally smaller than “the method works” and much smaller than “a candidate system is ready.” It allows the program to learn whether its public instrumentation, source custody, containment, safeing and evidence architecture are capable of issuing the smallest source-bearing class. It does not award performance credit to a candidate mechanism because no candidate article is present.

The authority ceiling matters because research institutions often gain unintended authority through vocabulary. A successful “quarter-source precursor” may be cited later as quarter-source qualification even if its route, hold-up and flow regime were different. RAE-1 prevents that drift by assigning four distinct objects:

| Object | RAE-1 status | What would be required next |
|---|---|---|
| public low-source reference article | designed; physical acceptance open | build, instrument and accept `REA-100` on all six rigs |
| 100 kg entry event | constitution complete; issue open | accept every event-specific physical record and name an issuer |
| 150 mm quarter/full-source route | not qualified | separate physical similarity, hold-up and escalation program |
| candidate Root A mechanism | absent and unauthorized | candidate constitution, admission law and independent campaign |

This boundary also protects exterior rendering. The research now constrains an internal reference article: envelope, pan, insert, receiver, control elements, measurement and access. It does not yet constrain a field vehicle’s external geometry, mobility system, operator arrangement, production interfaces or deployment form. Rendering those features now would turn unearned assumptions into apparent architecture.

## 3. First-principles selection of the low-source article

### 3.1 Why the 150-millimetre line fails as a low-source reference

Let admitted source mass be (m=100\;kg), density (\rho=800\;kg/m^3), event interval (t=120\;s), route diameter (D) and route length (L=12\;m). Source volume and nominal volumetric rate are:

\[
V_s=\frac{m}{\rho}=0.125\;m^3
\]

\[
\dot V=\frac{V_s}{t}=0.0010417\;m^3/s
\]

For the 150-millimetre route, cross-sectional area is 0.01767 square metres and line volume is 0.21206 cubic metres. Line hold-up at the planning density is therefore 169.646 kilograms. The source mass is 69.646 kilograms below the amount required to fill the route. Nominal velocity is 0.05895 metres per second.

Using the inherited dynamic-viscosity hypothesis of 0.004 pascal-seconds:

\[
Re=\frac{\rho vD}{\mu}\approx1,768
\]

The low-source event has crossed into a different hydraulic problem. Initial gas fraction, residual wetting, slope, pockets, drain-down and surface condition could dominate the observed state. Repeating the event would characterize this partially filled route, not establish a clean lower rung toward the reference source path.

### 3.2 The 40-millimetre insert as an honest control article

At 40 millimetres, line volume becomes 0.01508 cubic metres and planning hold-up becomes 12.064 kilograms. Nominal velocity becomes 0.82893 metres per second. With the inherited density, viscosity and surface-tension hypotheses:

\[
Re\approx6,631,\qquad Fr\approx1.323,\qquad We\approx879.5
\]

The insert is not chosen because these values reproduce the full-source route. They do not. It is chosen because the source inventory fills the article, produces an observable transient, leaves sufficient mass beyond static line volume, and makes hold-up measurable before escalation. The article therefore supports three narrow questions: can the public system admit the registered aliquot, can it observe and terminate the source state, and can it close custody afterward?

This is a model of ambitious but disciplined innovation. The force should develop purpose-built reference machines whose geometry is selected for measurement and causal learning, not improvise with surplus production hardware. The resulting technology—replaceable flow articles, embedded inventory sensing, self-identifying source capsules and local causal clocks—can later become a national industrial standard. The public research object drives private capability without pretending that the research object is itself the operational fleet.

## 4. `REA-100`: the government-owned reference system

Each of the six public rigs receives one `REA-100` article. The planning envelope is 2.4 by 1.6 by 0.6 metres with a dry-mass ceiling of 2.5 tonnes. The source article includes the 40-millimetre insert, a 2.0-by-1.25-metre inner pan, a 0.2-metre wall, and a 0.5-cubic-metre closed receiver. The pan provides 2.5 square metres of known occupied area. Gross contained volume is 0.5 cubic metres; at the declared 0.8 usable fraction, usable volume is 0.4 cubic metres. The 100-kilogram source produces a nominal depth of 0.05 metres and leaves 0.275 cubic metres of usable margin.

The pan is a class boundary, not a cosmetic catch tray. `QE-02` is valid only if the physical geometry limits occupied area to 2.5 square metres. The surrounding 25-square-metre catchment remains a public casualty system. It may protect life, the rig and the environment, but it cannot improve the candidate or method result. Because no candidate is present in RAE-1, the distinction is established here as institutional habit before commercial incentives appear.

The article is divided into twenty controlled cell sections per rig. They cover public datum, source conditioning, capsule docking, low-volume routing, dispensing, pan geometry, normal and casualty source routes, water isolation, casualty-water path, final control elements, local safety energy, evidence latches, environmental sampling, rescue access and recovery interfaces. Each section requires an as-built record. A design drawing cannot populate the observed field. Six configuration masters then bind each article, its twenty sections, twelve final control elements, thirty-six paths, thirty-two walkdowns and three neighbor identities to a single accepted rig state.

Public ownership is essential. If the first known-truth article were supplied by a future candidate vendor, the Department would import vendor-specific assumptions into the measurement root. `REA-100` is a method reference: simple enough to understand, instrumented enough to reconstruct and neutral enough to expose failures in the public system itself.

## 5. Event population, source custody and reserve law

The base campaign crosses two methods (`AM-05`, `AM-06`), two unlike truth roots (`TR-A`, `TR-B`), six rigs and two classes. The result is forty-eight required valid events.

| Class | Required valid events | Source | Ignition | Rig-days per event | Purpose |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---|
| `QE-01` | 24 | 0 kg | prohibited | 2 | prove that zero is an observed physical state rather than a blank |
| `QE-02` | 24 | 100 kg | `AM-06` opportunity only | 3 | prove low-source admission, termination, containment and reconstruction |

`QE-01` is not a rehearsal. It exercises article identity, sealed source state, receiver inventories, control elements, safeing paths, neighbor state, clock, evidence, samples and board process while preserving numeric zero. It establishes whether the institution can distinguish “none occurred” from “no observation exists.”

`QE-02` uses one active source package per method-root-rig combination. Each package contains 125 kilograms: a 100-kilogram event aliquot, 10 kilograms for retained assay, 10 kilograms for conditioning and 5 kilograms for quarantine. Ten source-characteristic records attach to each package, covering gross mass, event mass, density, lower heating value, temperature, water fraction, foreign solids, ancestry recovery, custody seal and retained samples.

Four physical reserve packages protect the four method-root families. Reserve law is narrower: only three `QE-02` reserve tokens exist. This intentional mismatch leaves one physical root-protected package untouched after the maximum authorized invalid-attempt replacement set. The campaign cannot consume the last independent source reference through administrative convenience.

Three `QE-01` and three `QE-02` reserve tokens yield fifteen reserve rig-days. A token activates only after an independent board finds that the registered proposition did not occur or decisive evidence was invalid. A valid adverse result remains part of the denominator. No program office may repeat it into disappearance.

## 6. Water, thermal consequence and public intervention

Both entry classes plan zero candidate water. That does not permit water to be ignored. Each rig-root pair receives a separately identified 120-cubic-metre casualty-water and background package. Initial casualty-receiver inventory may not exceed 5 cubic metres. The planning adverse provision is 48 cubic metres of emergency quench, 45 cubic metres of controlled rain over 600 square metres, 12 cubic metres of foam and rinse, and 5 cubic metres unassigned. Total initial and adverse provision is 115 cubic metres, leaving a minimum 5-cubic-metre margin.

The point is not that every event will receive 110 cubic metres of intervention. The point is that the event remains physically safe and analytically decidable if public actions or weather introduce water. Source and water retain unlike ancestry codes, separate custody and separate destinations. Public water can save the site while causing an adverse method result. It never becomes candidate performance.

Only `AM-06 QE-02` carries an ignition opportunity. With 2.5 square metres, inherited burning-flux screen 0.055 kilograms per square metre-second and lower heating value 42.588 megajoules per kilogram:

\[
\dot Q=2.5\times0.055\times42.588=5.85585\;MW
\]

At a 0.35 radiative fraction, the screen is 2.04955 megawatts radiant. The 100-kilogram notional burning interval is approximately 12.12 minutes. A simple isotropic point-source calculation reaches 1.6 kilowatts per square metre at approximately 10.10 metres. This is a planning calculation, not a boundary reduction. The inherited 200-metre public operational boundary remains in force because flame geometry, wind, smoke, runoff, intervention and uncertainty are not represented by the point source.

## 7. Safeing architecture: proof attaches to physical state

Each rig has twelve final control elements: source inlet isolation, dispenser isolation, gravity source diversion, normal receiver isolation, casualty source diversion, ignition-energy removal, candidate/public-water isolation, casualty-water diversion, normal electrical isolation, independent safety energy, environmental boundary/vent and evidence safe-state latch. Across six rigs this creates seventy-two controlled elements.

Every element receives three physically unlike paths:

- a primary registered command with independent energy and direct final-state sensing;
- a diverse local trip with unlike energy and unlike state indication; and
- a manual or passive path that does not depend on the network.

The result is 216 path allocations and 216 separate path-proof records. A path proof contains the trigger, energy condition, final-state observation, action time, reset state and independent acceptance. Three screens showing the same commanded bit do not create three paths. A path earns credit only at the mechanism that produces and preserves the safe state.

The evidence latch is especially important. It retains the last decisive state through primary power and communications loss using mechanical memory and an isolated recorder. This technology is useful far beyond the rig. Disaster machines routinely operate amid damaged utilities and fragmented communications. A pump, mobile substation, water processor or autonomous earthmover should be able to state locally what it did, when it did it and which physical state resulted—even before national systems reconnect.

## 8. The event dossier as a causal object

Each event issue names minimum accepted content: one reference article, twenty cell sections, one configuration master, one water package, twelve final control elements, thirty-six safeing paths, thirty-six path proofs, thirty-two walkdowns, three neighbor proofs, thirty-two event holds, twelve evidence objects and six or eight samples. `QE-02` additionally requires one source package and ten source-characteristic records. Counts do not themselves issue the event; the issuer must bind the accepted identities and states to one frozen baseline.

Twenty-four causal phases run from the 96-hour baseline freeze through the day-seven validity and outcome board. The sequence records source and water custody, configuration, environment, rescue, calibration, receiver inventory, neighbor state, evidence arming, final control poll, command, physical actuation, ignition opportunity, terminal-source decision, public intervention class, recovery, ledger closure, immutable packet freeze and board disposition.

Thirty-two holds cross eight domains—configuration, source, containment, water, safeing, evidence, environment and authority—with four questions in each: identity, capacity, state and accountable acceptance. This produces 1,536 event-specific hold records. The apparent repetition is intentional. A hold accepted for one event cannot silently cover another event after source handling, calibration, weather, configuration or personnel changes.

Twelve evidence obligations per event preserve the registered baseline, source command and mass, final control states, path timing, receiver inventories, thermal field, water state, ancestry, environmental boundary, neighbor state, intervention and validity packet. Native originals remain authoritative. A compiled dossier may index and test them but cannot replace them.

The inherited evidence rate produces approximately 117.659 gigabytes per event after the full-rate interval, one-percent tail, two originals and custody factor. The forty-eight-event base requires approximately 5.648 terabytes. Provision for all fifty-four possible attempts plus a 30-percent forensic allowance is approximately 8.260 terabytes. These quantities are modest by defense-test standards; the difficult part is identity, synchronization and admissibility rather than storage volume.

## 9. Uncertainty, ancestry and invalidity

Each of the twenty-four `QE-02` method-root-rig combinations receives its own source-mass uncertainty budget. The design allocations are 0.25 kilograms for the load-cell chain, 0.20 for transfer, 0.30 for hold-up, 0.30 for samples and 0.20 for time association. Root-sum-square standard uncertainty is approximately 0.5679 kilograms; using a planning coverage factor of two gives (U_{95}\approx1.1358\) kilograms against a 1.5-kilogram ceiling. The remaining design margin is approximately 0.3642 kilograms.

These are allocations, not accepted performance. Every calibration record remains open. Physical acceptance must also show unaccounted source no greater than 0.75 kilograms, environmental source no greater than 0.1 kilograms, ancestry recovery at least 0.995 and cross-talk no greater than 0.005. An uncertainty calculation cannot compensate for the wrong source identity, and ancestry recovery cannot compensate for missing mass.

Invalidity is causal, not merely statistical. Sixteen restart families cover wrong article, configuration, custody, source characteristics, water cross-talk, receiver inventory, control state, path independence, time disagreement, evidence loss, sample custody, neighbor state, environment, unregistered intervention, rescue deviation and issuer defect. Initial quarantine expands according to mechanism, shared service and custody ancestry. An event, rig, campus or method-root set may be affected depending on the common cause. Restart requires root-cause evidence, a newly frozen baseline and fresh authority.

## 10. An innovation portfolio built around measurement limits

RAE-1 defines eight technology campaigns with a combined $470 million cross-classified allocation inside `RAS-1`:

| Campaign | Required advance | Why ordinary procurement is insufficient |
|---|---|---|
| traceable source capsule | serialized source, assay, seal and contained disconnect | commodity containers do not preserve event-grade ancestry and state |
| 40 mm low-volume insert | replaceable article for a filled, observable 100 kg transient | throttling the large route creates the wrong experiment |
| micro-hold-up tomography | nonintrusive inventory before, during and after admission | mass closure cannot assume empty piping |
| reversible containment pan | known geometry, closed receiver and rapid cleanout | generic spill capture does not preserve class geometry or custody |
| mechanical final-state memory | local state after network and power loss | command logs do not prove physical final state |
| local causal-time system | bounded independent clock and immutable timing packet | cloud or campus time can disappear with the event |
| zero-state leak/background sensing | distinguish true zero from tiny release against background | conventional alarms are too coarse for method qualification |
| machine-verifiable dossier compiler | test identities, states, time and custody while preserving originals | manual packet assembly cannot scale to thousands of physical objects |

This portfolio shows what “military-scale innovation for resilience” means in practice. It is not limited to larger pumps or tougher trucks. It funds new sensing physics, reference architectures, failure-tolerant control, evidence systems and public test methods. The Department becomes a demanding lead customer whose problems create supplier depth in metrology, robotics, controls, materials, energy systems and field infrastructure.

The export opportunity follows from trust. Nations and cities may buy disaster equipment, but they also need evidence that systems perform under utility loss, contamination, public intervention and degraded communications. A Department able to supply reference methods, certification services and interoperable evidence standards can shape a global resilience market without requiring foreign customers to duplicate the entire U.S. proving estate.

## 11. Operating institution and industrial allocation

The 384-position establishment is organized by owned capability rather than inherited bureau boundaries. Campaign integration owns sequence and interfaces. Article/configuration teams own reference hardware and masters. Source/ancestry teams own custody, conditioning and assay. Controls/safeing teams own final elements and unlike paths. Metrology/time teams own calibration and causal order. Evidence teams own originals, custody and compilation. Environment/recovery teams own background, receivers and restoration. Safety/rescue teams own exclusion, abort and entry. Independent acceptance teams own witness, issue and validity. Program operations own finance, acquisition, schedule and records.

This composition is neither a temporary project office nor a small safety branch attached to a production program. It is the seed of a permanent public test and evaluation profession inside the Department of Resilience. Its people rotate between laboratory method, range operation, disaster deployment and acquisition evaluation. The institution develops careers in source accounting, field causal inference, degraded-system safety, environmental recovery and large-scale public acceptance—the resilience equivalents of mature defense test disciplines.

Capital allocation is $48 million for reference articles, $46 million for source systems, $42 million for containment, $26 million for receiver proof, $50 million for final controls, $32 million for safeing paths, $28 million for metrology, $22 million for evidence/time, $24 million for casualty-water and thermal systems, $14 million for rescue, $12 million for samples/environment and $16 million contingency. Annual support is $96 million workforce, $12 million source/media, $18 million rig operations, $8 million calibration, $8 million evidence, $7 million safety/rescue, $5 million environment, $4 million independent boards, $6 million technology campaigns and $4 million contingency.

RAE-1 is a nested tranche. Its $360 million capital and $168 million annual support are already inside the $2.435 billion/$464 million-per-year credited `RAS-1` boundary. They are exposed here because a credible institution must show what the parent allocation purchases. They are not added again. The uncredited parent view remains $3.095 billion and $637 million per year. This distinction prevents detailed technical work from creating false portfolio growth while still exposing whether the inherited reservation is plausible.[6–8]

## 12. Maturity assessment and next research object

RAE-1 passes 120 workbook assertions. It contains forty-six sheets, forty-eight event records and 5,050 linked physical acceptance gates. The formulas reproduce the hydraulic, thermal, water, uncertainty, evidence and financial calculations without formula-error matches. This establishes internal design coherence.

It does not establish readiness. Six reference articles have not been built. One hundred twenty cell sections have not been surveyed. Twenty-eight source packages have not been accepted. Safeing paths have not actuated. Clocks have not been calibrated. Samples have not been sealed. No issuer has authorized an event, and no validity board has ruled. All physical acceptance gates therefore remain open.

The next object should be `RAP-1`, a physical reference-article and safeing proof campaign. It should specify build articles, materials, internal drawings, instrument installation, calibration fixtures, controlled defects, path-actuation trials, receiver-volume trials, pan release/recovery trials, background-sensing trials, independent clock denial and dossier replay. It should culminate in dry and inert-fluid acceptance of `REA-100`—not source entry. Only after RAP-1 closes should the Department consider issuing the first `QE-01` event, followed by `QE-02` under separately granted authority.

Exterior rendering remains premature. RAP-1 will begin to reveal service clearances, access zones, sensor lines, isolation geometry, rescue interfaces, thermal separation, receiver placement and maintainability. Later candidate research will add deployability, mobility, crew, field power and production constraints. At that point exterior form can emerge from the physical constitution rather than lead it.
