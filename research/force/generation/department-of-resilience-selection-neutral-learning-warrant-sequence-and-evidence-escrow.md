## Resolution test

If this result were reversed, the Department would either require an eighteen-form physical surge or permit activation order and early results to choose the first portfolio before comparable evidence exists, changing a first-order acquisition decision.

The deepest active branch is `FSA`, with one prior pass consumed. It passes the resolution test because the sequence determines which candidate forms receive executable access to the first physical program and whether the selection covenant remains credible.

## Decision

Issue all eighteen legal learning warrants at week 0, reserve each warrant's full `FRAME` share, freeze its event identity and configuration, and activate work in three sequential cohorts of six. Each cohort contains one form from every root, exactly two `A`, two `B` and two `C` variants, and one-third of the USD 538M ceiling. Results remain in role-separated escrow until all three cohorts complete. Only symmetric safety orders may cross a cohort boundary.

This separates five states that a simple “start date” would collapse:

1. **Authorized:** Pass 226 made a complete warrant eligible.
2. **Issued:** the Department creates equal legal standing and reserves the form's ceiling.
3. **Activated:** the form's five work packages enter the bounded production lane.
4. **Observed:** a later evidence authority accepts or rejects a physical record.
5. **Selected:** a still-later comparative warrant disposes among root rivals.

Pass 227 advances only the second state. Obligations, payments, accepted observations and selections remain zero. `MAKE`, `BREAK` and `REP-4` remain held.

## Alternatives

| Sequence | Failure |
| --- | --- |

| **Rotating one-per-root cohorts** | **Selected.** Limits peak work to six forms while balancing root access, variant position and money in every wave. |

Each root's three sibling shares are reserved at issue. An early underrun cannot accelerate a later sibling; an overrun cannot consume its reserve; and a failed early form cannot release its balance to another form before the comparative round closes. This is not fiscal idleness: it is the price of making “all rivals remain live” true in an acquisition system that otherwise rewards first movers.

## Evidence escrow

Twelve rules separate safety learning from performance learning. Primary event identity, denial world, disqualifier, instruments, custody chain and reconstruction rule freeze before activation. Raw records move to a custodian that cannot select a form. Cohort staff receive no sibling performance results. The Evidence Court may transmit a safety order only when it is expressed as the same constraint, measurement or safeing requirement for every still-unactivated sibling in that root. It may not transmit a relative result, redesign hint, cost comparison, maturity judgment or candidate rank.

A safety change therefore preserves the contest; a performance change contaminates it. Contamination freezes the affected root and returns all altered siblings to a common configuration baseline. Cohort 3 completion releases records for evidence adjudication, not selection. The Pass 225 noncompensatory domains and separate selection warrant continue to govern.
