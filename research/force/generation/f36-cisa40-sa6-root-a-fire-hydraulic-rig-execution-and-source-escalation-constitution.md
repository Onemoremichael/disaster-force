Neither document answered the final question before method cards can be released: how does a six-rig public institution execute the campaign without letting schedule, safety action, invalidity, missing evidence or recovery discretion rewrite the experiment after results are visible?

This pass creates `RFE-1`, the Root A execution and source-escalation constitution. Its governing decision is:

> Execute one immutable 306-event population through a twenty-four-phase public clock. Separate event validity, candidate outcome, public intervention and recovery status. Permit reserve attempts to replace invalid trials only; never permit them to erase valid adverse evidence.

The exact population is sixty safe development events, twelve quarter-source development anchors, ninety-six quarter-source paired events, ninety-six full-source paired events, twenty-four destructive events, twelve reset events and six cross-site replays. The ninety-six matched pairs remain conserved. Every row has a candidate, builder, campus, rig, method, protocol, source tier, water interval and registered order before physical execution begins.

One unignited and one ignited 500 kg development anchor for each of six roots add six tonnes. Base provision is therefore 294 tonnes.

Seven class-specific prospective invalid rates create sixty-six dormant reserve tokens: nine safe-development, three quarter-development, seventeen quarter-paired, twenty-four full-paired, eight destructive, three reset and two replay tokens. Source-bearing reserve provision is 74 tonnes. Base and reserve together require 368 tonnes and 2,559 rig-days. Against approximately 5,084.7 available rig-days during forty-eight months at a 0.58 ready-rig fraction, nominal utilization is 50.3 percent. The unused interval is not excess. It carries recovery variance, maintenance, source conditioning, environmental holds, reconfiguration and repetition after invalidity.

Every event follows the same twenty-four-phase clock from `T-96 h` configuration freeze through day-seven disposition. Within that clock, `T+30 s` is the candidate terminal-uncontrolled-source decision, while `T+120 s` is the scheduled public-dispenser end. These are not competing specifications. The first asks whether newly uncontrolled source has ended or entered a bounded candidate route. The second ends the registered external source schedule unless public isolation acted sooner. An ignition command at `T+15 s` is an opportunity, not an assumed flame. If the opportunity is valid and no flame occurs, “no flame” is candidate evidence rather than automatic invalidity.

Public action is deliberately scored. Twelve intervention paths protect people, site, neighboring source, environment and evidence, but cannot make candidate performance successful. A public quench can save the rig and still establish candidate thermal-control failure. Casualty-water capture can prevent uncontrolled runoff and still establish candidate retention failure. Immediate casualty extraction can take precedence over evidence collection while remaining in the packet.

Validity is decided before outcome. A valid candidate failure answers the registered question adversely. A facility-caused invalid event does not answer it. A safe refusal can be a valid outcome if the candidate entered its declared safe state. An unresolved event remains blank; zero is reserved for an observed numeric quantity. Ten pair rules preserve a valid member when its mate is invalid and prohibit replacement of a valid adverse member.

Recovery is also evidentiary. Ten ordered states begin with termination of new source and end with a restored serialized baseline. Elapsed time alone cannot authorize entry, cleaning, repair or the next event. Twelve root-site reset clocks and six outside-origin replays turn restoration and reconstructability into campaign observations.

## 2. The experiment is the force unit

Military test enterprises distinguish test articles, ranges, instrumentation, safety commands, trial cards and independent evaluation. A Department of Resilience needs the same institutional seriousness, but its decisive object is civilian effect under damaged conditions. In Root A, the proposition is not that a drain can pass liquid in a laboratory. It is that an integrated article can terminate a source, bound fire, distinguish source from suppression water, protect neighboring inventory, preserve environmental custody and enter a measured recovery state under registered damage.

That proposition can be corrupted in four ways:

1. the event population can drift toward cases a favored candidate handles well;
2. the public plant can silently help a candidate through common infrastructure;
3. safety intervention can be hidden from candidate scoring; or
4. invalidity can be used to delete an adverse observation.

The constitution prevents each. Population is frozen by row. Candidate and public actions use separate commands and inventories. Every intervention has a trigger and scoring consequence. Reserve tokens can replace only invalid attempts, while original packets remain permanent.

## 4. Source escalation and reserve law

### 4.1 Source provision

Base source is:

\[
12(0.5)+96(0.5)+96(2)+24(2)=294\ \mathrm{t}.
\]

Reserve source is:

\[
3(0.5)+17(0.5)+24(2)+8(2)=74\ \mathrm{t}.
\]

Total provision is 368 tonnes. “Provision” means bounded acquisition, conditioning, custody, sampling and terminal disposition capacity. It does not mean source is admitted to the test estate at program start.

### 4.2 The ladder

The authorized logical sequence is:

1. geometry and blank-field acceptance;
2. water-only hydraulics;
3. sealed tracer;
4. safe surrogate;
5. quarter source without ignition;
6. quarter source with registered ignition opportunity;
7. quarter-source matched pairs;
8. full-source matched pairs;
9. destructive full-source cases; and
10. recovery and outside-origin reconstruction.

Progression requires the prior method’s validity, source closure, environmental custody, evidence reconstruction and restoration regression. Calendar date, article seniority or management confidence cannot substitute.

### 4.3 Reserve-token constitution

A reserve token begins dormant. It receives a triggering event only after independent invalidity disposition. Activation creates a new replacement-event identity. The original event, source lot, interventions, fault, evidence and cause remain visible.

A token may not:

- replace a valid candidate failure;
- convert public intervention into facility invalidity without causal evidence;
- merge two incomplete attempts into one synthetic success;
- change the registered challenge after seeing the original; or
- improve a confidence claim by omitting the original packet.

These rules make schedule reserve compatible with scientific integrity.

## 5. The common twenty-four-phase clock

### 5.1 Pre-event phases

From `T-96 h` to `T-2 h`, independent authorities accept configuration, environmental baseline, source lot, recovery capacity, terrain, challenge realization plan, candidate state, services and rescue posture. Thirty-two holds cover four questions—identity, capacity, state and authority—across configuration, source, terrain, candidate, water/heat, safety, measurement and environment/recovery.

The test conductor coordinates the poll but cannot clear another authority’s hold. Silence is not consent.

### 5.2 Evidence and source phases

At `T-30 min`, native evidence is armed. At `T-10 min`, the final authority poll is recorded. At `T-60 s`, 576 channels enter the registered full rate. At `T-10 s`, the blind challenge reaches its measured state. `T0` is the single public source command.

For fire methods, the igniter receives a ten-second registered opportunity beginning at `T+15 s`. This definition avoids two errors: treating commanded ignition as observed flame, or treating lack of flame as a facility invalidity when the opportunity was valid.

### 5.3 The two source times

At `T+30 s`, the campaign asks a candidate question: has new uncontrolled source ended or entered a bounded, accountable candidate route? At `T+120 s`, the public dispenser reaches its scheduled end unless isolation acted earlier.

The distinction is essential. A candidate does not pass merely because the external source schedule ended. It must control its own terminal state inside the stricter thirty-second requirement. Conversely, an early public stop remains an intervention and cannot be mistaken for candidate control.

### 5.4 Water, rescue and tail phases

`T+15 min` classifies initial thermal and water state. `T+30 min` classifies the compound `A08` interval and any public quench or casualty custody. `T+60 min` establishes a remote-entry class; it does not grant human entry. At `T+6 h`, evidence tail closure is permitted only if thermal, environmental and inventory states allow it.

Mass and water ledgers are due at `T+24 h`. The evidence packet freezes at `T+72 h`. At day seven, the independent board decides validity first and candidate outcome second.

## 6. Command, abort and public intervention

### 6.1 Twenty command transactions

The command grammar distinguishes acceptance, arming, candidate action, public action, evidence freeze and restoration release. Each transaction has one issuer, one temporal location, one observable action and one predetermined response to failure. Candidate controllers issue candidate source, water and robot actions. Public authorities issue source isolation, casualty diversion, quench, rescue and evidence-preservation actions.

This separation permits attribution. A shared “stop” button with no issuer or final-element record could end the event safely while destroying the scientific answer.

### 6.2 Twenty abort channels

Abort observations include source identity, time skew, loss of native originals, indeterminate challenge, terminal source, boundary crossing, vault level, secondary-basin admission, pool extent, thermal limits, neighbor recruitment, ancestry loss, safeing-path loss, robot corridor violation, casualty location, breathing-air reserve, air release, structural reaction, manual stop and uncertain casualty capacity.

Every channel requires its own drill-acceptance record. General rig acceptance cannot stand in for proof that a particular observation reaches its authority and final element. This pass caught and removed that exact form of authority leakage during workbook verification.

An abort classifies immediate public action, not final validity or candidate outcome. “Facility invalidity,” “valid candidate failure” and “cause review” are provisional labels until the independent board examines the chain.

### 6.3 Twelve public interventions

The public institution owns source isolation, casualty-source diversion, casualty-water capture, two unlike quench paths, secondary-basin admission, robot lockout, remote reconnaissance, casualty extraction, shelter or evacuation, quarantine transfer and evidence-preservation safe state.

The scoring rule is noncompensatory. Public action can reduce consequence, but it cannot improve the candidate result. The candidate receives no terminal-time credit after public source isolation, no retention credit for casualty-water capacity and no recovery credit for public quarantine transfer.

### 6.4 Safe state

Ten ordered actions stop new source, isolate the candidate route, divert uncontrolled inventory, bound water, remove ignition, preserve beneficial cooling, retain remote evidence, maintain rescue posture, freeze configuration and admit recovery only by measured state. Safe state protects people and preserves the answer; it does not declare success.

## 7. Validity, outcome and pair logic

### 7.1 Validity answers whether the proposition was tested

Twelve rules require accepted configuration, source identity, initial state, challenge realization, decisive measurement, time integrity, public-boundary integrity, mass/water closure, intervention identity, custody continuity, rescue integrity and independent adjudication.

A facility-caused source transient may invalidate attribution. A candidate-created pool crossing under an otherwise valid event is not invalid simply because the result is severe. This distinction prevents safety from becoming a mechanism for removing failures.

### 7.2 Outcome answers how the candidate performed

Fourteen outcomes include clean success, bounded degraded success, safe refusal, terminal-time failure, source-capture failure, fire-growth failure, water-retention failure, source–water mixing failure, environmental failure, robot/recovery failure, public casualty intervention, reset-time failure, unsafe excursion and no candidate outcome for invalid events.

No weighted score permits excellence in one dimension to compensate for violation of a hard public boundary. Diagnostic features can explain a result; they cannot erase it.

### 7.3 Pair disposition

A pair exists only when both event-validity records and the pair signature are accepted. If one member is invalid, the valid member remains and only the invalid member may receive a reserve replacement. Valid adverse control behavior is retained and may hold the contrast. Site disagreement and builder disagreement remain visible at their own levels.

The registered contrast is challenge minus control with uncertainty and intervention flags. Pass 126 must define each method-specific feature, tolerance and causal estimator before any of these cells can produce an admissible effect.

## 9. Recovery is an experimental phase

Ten recovery states are ordered:

1. new source terminated;
2. candidate and public paths isolated;
3. casualty status resolved;
4. remote map accepted;
5. accountable inventories captured;
6. atmosphere and thermal state accepted;
7. mass and water ledgers closed;

The ordering prevents cleanup from destroying source ancestry and prevents repair from changing the article before the evidence authority releases it. Reset ceilings differ by candidate hypothesis—72, 96 and 84 hours—but meeting time alone is insufficient. Every state must be accepted.

## 14. Failure semantics and learning policy

The program must learn from four different events without confusing them:

| Event state | Meaning | Permitted action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| valid success | candidate satisfied the registered rule | retain and compare |
| valid adverse outcome | candidate violated or declined a registered rule | retain; do not replace |
| invalid attempt | the registered proposition was not established | retain original; replacement may use reserve |
| unresolved | required evidence or authority is incomplete | remain blank; do not score |

Public intervention can coexist with validity. If a candidate creates a valid adverse state and the public plant then protects the site, the event remains informative. Facility invalidity requires a demonstrated public cause that prevented attribution, not the mere fact that staff acted.

This policy is foundational for a Department of Resilience technology system. Catastrophe-response machines operate near hard civil boundaries. A research institution that treats every safety action as “bad data” will select brittle designs. One that awards the public plant’s performance to the candidate will select dependent designs. Root A must do neither.

## 15. Findings and unresolved questions

### Finding 1 — Exact execution changes the physical interpretation

The six rigs are not interchangeable storage slots. Exact allocation reveals an operational-campus peak of sixty-nine events and a destructive-campus burden of twelve long energetic cases per rig. Staffing, maintenance, environmental custody and evidence architecture must follow those identities.

### Finding 2 — The source budget must include learning events

The 288-tonne paired-plus-destructive view omitted six tonnes of root-native quarter-source development. An execution constitution must budget the path to knowledge, not only the final comparison.

### Finding 3 — Reserve is a scientific resource

Sixty-six tokens and 495 rig-days are required to protect the valid denominator under prospective invalidity. Their legitimacy depends on immutable originals and a prohibition on replacing valid adverse evidence.

### Finding 4 — Safety and scoring must coexist

The public plant needs enough source, water, quench, rescue and environmental capacity to survive candidate failure. That capacity remains scientifically neutral only if every activation has a causal record and an adverse scoring rule.

### Finding 5 — Recovery determines throughput and validity

Source ancestry, water custody, samples, article state and responder access are decided during recovery. Treating cleanup as post-test support would make both event closure and campaign tempo unknowable.

### Finding 6 — The next pass must make methods executable

`RFE-1` fixes population and constitutional law, but it does not yet define method-specific nominal values, tolerances, dwell, sampling, commands, automatic logic, missing-data disposition or causal feature equations for Root A’s coupled liquid/fire cases.
