# Pass 168 — Six Mission-System Portfolio Topology and Technology Competition Constitution

**Research object:** `MSPT-1`  
**Decision level:** Department mission-system portfolio  
**Verdict:** `CLOSE`

## Research disposition

The deepest live branch is `NIFS-1 → MSPT-1`. `MSPT-1` is on its first pass after force sizing returned the frontier to the Department portfolio decision; no technical sub-branch is active.

**Resolution test:** Changing this topology changes Department program offices, industrial integrators, budget ownership and the representative campaigns used to admit the force.

The test passes because those are first-order institutional choices, not details of a vehicle or subsystem.

## Decision

The Department shall acquire through **six end-to-end mission-system portfolios**, while maintaining **nine independent civilian-effect acceptance ledgers**. The [Pass 166 fiscal constitution](department-of-resilience-joint-mission-system-appropriation-reservation-and-accountability-constitution.md) is preserved, but its provisional nine effect-mirrored mission offices are superseded as program and appropriation boundaries. Effects remain the public claims that must be proved; they do not become acquisition organizations.

The selected topology is:

| `MS-ET` | Enter a broken theater | 9 | 369 | 827 | USD 89.0B |

Every canonical family has exactly one mission-integration owner. The seven services retain professional, lifecycle and technical-release authority over their families. The nine effects retain separate acceptance questions, so gains in water cannot excuse failure in care, access or lawful civil continuity. Joint commands employ the resulting formations; none becomes a program owner merely by commanding a campaign.

## Why six chains, not nine effects

An effect is a test dimension. A mission system is the stable chain of failures that must be integrated to pass it. Safe water may require theater entry, hazard control, lifeline production, last-mile civil reach and regeneration. A nine-office effect structure therefore fragments physical chains, creates repeated interfaces and leaves sustainment as someone else’s obligation.

Four rivals fail noncompensable gates. Seven service portfolios allow lifecycle owners to optimize inside professional boundaries. Ten campaign offices duplicate families and create domestic and international variants. Air, sea, ground and fixed-plant portfolios mistake physical hosts for public service. The six-chain topology alone passes unique family assignment, effect independence, service–mission separation, ten-campaign portability, mechanism-neutral competition, explicit regeneration, common-capacity conservation and invited-use sovereignty.

This is not a renaming exercise. `MS-ET` owns the problem of making complete formations operational through broken ports, airfields, roads, nodes and bases. `MS-LF` owns service from source through point of use and residual custody—not a generator, desalination plant or pipe. `MS-RT` owns campaign endurance, public transfer and restoration of the force itself, preventing logistics from disappearing into every program’s margin.

## What the load case reveals

The [Pass 167 force promise](department-of-resilience-national-and-invited-international-concurrency-geography-and-force-sizing-constitution.md) changes the intuitive center of gravity. Human continuity carries 879 of 2,420 simultaneous formation demands, 1,944 of 5,251 inventory equivalents and USD 153B of the mission-technology envelope. Hazard control—the visually dominant firefighting, flood and containment domain—carries 170 demands, 370 inventory equivalents and USD 54.6B.

This does not subordinate hazard technology. It prevents the force from being designed around machinery while treating clinical labor, household integrity, shelter, essential goods and accessible movement as residual services. Entry plus sustainment and regeneration account for 1,659 inventory equivalents and about USD 178B of technology exposure. Projection and return are therefore productive capabilities, not overhead.

The topology also crosses 22 active service work-package pairs. All six systems operate in every one of the ten representative campaigns. That is why no service or hazard bureau can substitute for a mission integrator.

## Technology as a governed competition

The model conserves the USD 550B mission-system share of the existing USD 1.00T technology-and-proving envelope. Each mission system receives a USD 30B floor; the remaining USD 370B is allocated by declared pressure: 30% concurrent demand, 25% selected inventory, 20% formation capital, 15% direct workforce and 10% unclosed demand. These are planning exposures, not budget estimates or evidence of feasibility.

Each system opens four **problem-field competitions**, producing 24 competitions without selecting a mechanism or exterior form. The fields include purpose-built persistent firefighting aircraft; megaflow flood intervention; autonomous terrain and debris work; firm-source water–energy carriers and inland service barges; robotic pipe and conductor corridors; grid-forming power and thermal cells; distributed definitive care; heavy buoyant and austere-surface lift; autonomous node opening; building and vertical access; civil communications meshes; closed-loop bases; robotic regeneration; and circular residual control.

These are not 24 favored platforms. Every competition must state the binding public-service coefficient it changes, preserve the complete-system boundary, fund unlike physical roots, expose a rival mechanism, and contain a kill condition. Government-owned interfaces and a public test estate prevent six mission integrators from becoming six proprietary monopolies. Service-family markets, common-interface suppliers and challenger roots remain distinct industrial entry points.

No common title, interface or apparent multifunctionality earns quantity credit. The [common technical architecture](../../systems/common-technical-architecture.md) may reduce integration friction; only exclusive reservation and representative substitution evidence can reduce the force.

## Closure and next admissible resolution

`MSPT-1` closes the sole inherited parent question: Department mission-system portfolio topology. The next admissible resolution is inside `MS-HU`, the highest-pressure system: define the complete care-and-human-continuity service contract and its rival technology campaign without collapsing it into a hospital, shelter or mobility program. That successor is not opened here.

**Remaining unresolved at this decision level:** none. Physical rival results, production release, procurement and exterior rendering remain subordinate and unadmitted.

**Open-item ledger:** opened 0; closed 1 (`MSPT-1` topology); net −1; running parent-level total 0.

**Authoritative:** six mission chains own integration; nine effects own independent public acceptance; seven services own professional and family lifecycles.  
**Died:** nine effect-mirrored program offices as appropriation and acquisition boundaries.  
**Burden moved:** from naming portfolios to proving complete rival systems inside the highest-pressure mission chain.  
**Parent question:** what complete `MS-HU` service contract should govern the first representative technology competition?
