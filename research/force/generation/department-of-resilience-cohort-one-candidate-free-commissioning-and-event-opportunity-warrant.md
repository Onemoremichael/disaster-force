## Resolution test

The deepest active branch is `EXR`, with three prior passes consumed. Reversing this result would let construction acceptance, candidate-bearing rehearsal or site-level readiness acquire evidentiary force, changing the campaign architecture and the Department's first-order rule for what counts as an event.

## Decision

Adopt `EOW-1`: no accepted article may enter a live event merely because it exists, fits a fixture or survived transport. Readiness belongs to one serial, generation, fixture, site, receiver, configuration digest and live window. Twelve conjunctive gates create one expiring **Event Opportunity Warrant** (`EOW`). The warrant is permission to expose an article once; it is not an event, observation, result, payment claim or capability finding.

The full proving estate must commission before the first live epoch. All eighteen anonymous node dockets, forty-eight primary berths, forty-eight third-node alternate berths and seventy-two primary, witness and custody scopes must close. This campaign-wide threshold prevents early articles from teaching later sites or letting an apparently strong candidate monopolize the best fixture. It also makes the alternate route physical before its capacity is credited.[^1]

Each node docket has twelve non-performance gates: structural reaction, independent utilities, thermal rejection, containment and residuals, responder egress, local manual stop, emergency power and communications, custody zoning, environmental baseline, civil and receiver access, alternate-berth route, and recovery. Every berth separately closes eight gates, including foundation, datum, isolation, complete load path, manual stop, residual route, measurement interface and removal. Construction acceptance may supply geometry and declared boundary loads; it cannot supply service-effect behavior.[^2]

## Candidate-free commissioning

Each of the eighteen site positions receives a public **Commissioning Surrogate Article**. A surrogate reproduces the bounding mass, center, attachment, reaction, hazard class and synthetic heat, flow, power, force and signal envelopes of the eligible article family. It contains no candidate mechanism, candidate controller or candidate output path.

All 144 serial opportunities receive a full-dress rehearsal using those public surrogates. Ten checks exercise candidate exclusion, blind placeholder identity, inert mass, synthetic load injection, primary and unlike witness chains, manual stop, emergency response, custody handoff, and reset. Candidate presence and candidate-output injection must both remain zero. Energizing a decisive candidate path, representative loading of its mechanism or injecting its output before an `EOW` is an event exposure; it can never be relabelled rehearsal.

This rule deliberately rejects builder-led wet commissioning. The builder may certify construction and provide a frozen interface dossier, but cannot tune the installed path, operate the rehearsal or observe a candidate-like outcome. The rehearsal authority receives only the interface, hazard and envelope record.

## Transport, receiver and warrant

Every serial travels under a sealed dispatch digest, public title and custody record, conflict-cleared route and append-only shock, thermal, humidity, power and access history. Arrival permits seal reconciliation, passive damage inspection and cold mating. Mechanical, utility, safeing and metrology interfaces may close without energizing the decisive mechanism. Any change to configuration creates a new generation under the inherited title law.[^3]

The receiver owns eight serial-specific records before warrant issue: denominator, matched counterfactual, exclusions, rights and access, safety and abort receipt, residual custody, data and disclosure, and independent acceptance. The receiver may reject an incomplete service definition; it may not improve a candidate's record by changing the population or duration after seeing behavior.

An independent `EOW` court sees gate closure, conflicts, frozen identities and the exact window—not candidate performance, cost, schedule reputation or builder identity. It issues at `T-72 hours`, remains valid for thirty-six hours and expires if site, fixture, receiver, digest or window changes. The commissioning clock begins at `T-26 weeks`; berth and metrology qualification start by `T-20`, the receiver packet freezes at `T-12`, candidate-free rehearsal closes by `T-8`, dispatch occurs at `T-4`, arrival at `T-2` and cold mating at `T-1`.

## Invalidity and recovery

A prestart abort before decisive operation may receive a new warrant only after cause closure and proof that configuration and custody are unchanged. Facility-caused invalidity after start preserves all observed behavior but creates neither candidate credit nor penalty; recovery requires a new slot and new `EOW`. Candidate-caused protective abort or accepted adverse performance is a valid final result. Neither gains an automatic retry or rehearsal label. Loss of a primary site invokes the predetermined third-node migration route rather than a favorable substitute.

Eighteen explicit dispositions cover estate holds, failed berths or chains, candidate contamination of rehearsal, seal mismatch, cold-mate defects, conflicts, aborts and post-start invalidity. Safety action is unconditional; evidentiary treatment follows only after the person-and-environment protection state is secure.
