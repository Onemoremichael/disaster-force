## Resolution test

The deepest active branch is `EXR`, with one prior pass consumed. Reversing this result would replace a federated campaign with a common proving cause, change the six-year public clock and alter how USD25.0B is sequenced across four jurisdictions; those are first-order changes to the proof-wave architecture.

## Decision

Adopt an eighteen-node campaign lattice: six form-native federations, each containing three anonymous proving-site slots. Allocate two public fixture cores to each of twenty-four technology orders. The paired cores occupy different nodes, split the two builders and three serial roles across unlike fixtures, and retain the third node as a relocation destination.[^1]

The slots are constitutional positions, not geographic selections. Actual sites, operators, hazards, utilities and permits remain source-empty. A national campus fails because site, safety, metrology and schedule become one cause. Six form campuses fail because one site still controls both builders inside a form. Forty-eight fixture-specific sites preserve custody but destroy migration and comparable capacity. Contractor-hosted ranges collapse candidate and host control.

## Six-by-six campaign clock

The 144 serials enter six 32-week epochs through a six-by-six Latin-square order. Every epoch carries exactly one serial from each technology order: twenty-four events, twelve per builder, eight per role and four per form. Each of the six serial classes—`B1-INT`, `B2-INT`, `B1-BRK`, `B2-BRK`, `B1-RCN` and `B2-RCN`—appears four times per epoch. Calendar position therefore cannot favor one builder, role, order or form.[^2]

Each epoch contains four six-week live windows followed by an eight-week reset. Six simultaneous events occupy six different site slots; no site hosts two live events in one window. A 104-week preactivation interval precedes the first event, and a sixteen-week closeout follows the last epoch. Total campaign length is 312 weeks, approximately six years.

The six construction-release waves begin at weeks zero, five, ten, fifteen, twenty and twenty-five. All are fixed before the first event at week 104. Performance remains sealed until campaign close. Only source-blind safety burdens may cross epochs under the inherited root-epoch protocol; observed performance, cost, repair experience and imagery may not.[^3]

## Reserve and loss law

The lattice contains 432 node-windows. The campaign schedules 144 and leaves 288 dormant. Dormancy is not slack available to management: it is constitutionally reserved for holds, requalification and migration. A valid adverse result is final and cannot be retried. An invalid opportunity remains attached to its original record; a new slot requires a separate warrant.

All 108 single-site-loss-by-epoch cases retain a third-node route without co-locating paired fixtures or exceeding four windows at a destination node. This proves only combinatorial capacity. It does not prove relocation time, site suitability, fixture installation or event validity.

## Separated workforce and control

The campaign requires at least 354 separated duty cells: eighteen site cells, forty-eight fixture custodians, seventy-two primary-or-witness measurement roles, 144 receiver-witness cells, forty-eight clean-reconstruction teams and twenty-four order courts. These are accountable control appointments, not additive jobs or billets. Identity, credential and staffing sources remain unassigned.

Using the inherited USD0.650M exposure per direct position-year and a 1.25 peak factor, the six-year USD25.0B exposure screens to about 6,420 average and 8,030 peak direct-position equivalents. This is an industrial workload measure, not an employment forecast.[^4]

Twenty-four prohibited common-control edges separate builders, fixtures, sites, primary and witness metrology, receiver denominators, safety, scheduling, result release, finance, clean reconstruction, public archives and courts. An unresolved curable edge holds release; an incurable edge terminates the serial or root pair. Eighteen nonconversion laws prevent schedule from becoming `MAKE`, cash draw from becoming payment, dormant capacity from becoming retry, safety from becoming performance credit, or topology from becoming rendering authority.
