## Decision: lifelines go first

`MS-LF` is the only system to pass all eight noncompensable selection gates. It owns source-to-user production, joins water, sanitation, electricity and thermal safety, has both maritime and inland roots, changes primary energy physics across all four rivals, supplies all five other systems and inherits physical source, corridor and receiver work from earlier research. Human continuity has the larger budget but already has a complete physical target; entry and hazard control do not close a civilian service chain by themselves.

The first root campaign therefore freezes a common public vector for a two-million-person utility zone. By hour 72 it must deliver at least 25,000 m³/day of quality-released water, 25 MW of firm electricity and 20 MWth of safe heating or cooling. By day seven those values double. By day twenty-one it must deliver 100,000 m³/day, 100 MW and 80 MWth, accept 80,000 m³/day of wastewater or equivalent residual load, and hold one day of product water. It must sustain that state for thirty days.

Loss of one source position and one corridor must leave at least half service after the buffer interval. Cloud, wide-area timing and remote vendor control may be absent for twenty-four hours. Every intake, concentrate, emission, cleaning stream and returned article retains an owner. These are acquisition thresholds, not evidence that any mechanism works.

## Configuration control and industrial consequence

The bill fixes article classes, quantities, service capacities, mass hypotheses, workforce, interfaces, failure worlds and evidence fields. It does not fix exterior form. Root-specific mechanisms remain behind common water, power, thermal, custody and evidence seams.

All forty-eight decisive-ancestry declarations remain `UNDECLARED`. All fifty-six inherited `G3` release gates remain `OPEN`. Configuration identifiers are frozen so prototypes can be built and adverse evidence cannot be answered by silently changing the root. A root reaches `G3` only after complete balances, representative subsystem trials, disconnected control, independent replay, repair and second-team reproduction are accepted.

The lifeline share through `G3` remains USD 36.0B inside the conserved program. Each root receives the same USD 8.99B option: 40 percent for root mechanisms and prototypes, 20 percent for public ranges and receivers, 20 percent for root-native tooling, 15 percent for independent evidence and 5 percent protected failure reserve. Equality prevents the familiar root from starving the novel one; unspent or failed-root authority returns to the mission account.
