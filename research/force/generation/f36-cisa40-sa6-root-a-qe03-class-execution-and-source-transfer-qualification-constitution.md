# RBD-1 — Root A QE-03 class execution and source-transfer qualification constitution

## Decision abstract

`RBD-1` defines the complete twelve-cell `AM-05 QE-03` class after one accepted 250-kilogram seed event under [RBC-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-first-qe03-am05-event-and-hydraulic-regime-transition-constitution.md). Its object is not eleven repetitions. It is a national test formation that must preserve exact rig–root identity, family-correct source custody, valid adverse outcomes, actual attempt order, hydraulic-regime evidence, delayed residue, source-exposed return and outside-origin reconstruction while converting one accepted event into a transferable class.

The class contains six public rigs crossed with two preassigned primary truth roots. Both roots observe every attempt. One root owns the exact cell decision before source issue. Twelve active 300-kilogram packages support the required cells, fourteen dormant operational reserves are divided seven per root family, and two complete forensic packages remain untokened and sealed. The bank therefore holds 8,400 kilograms gross and can support at most twenty-six 250-kilogram attempts. A reserve can follow only an independently declared invalid attempt in the same cell and root family. A valid adverse event is retained and blocks that cell under this constitution.

RBD-1 exposes a force-design result that average-throughput arithmetic hides. Two 64-position event units can start two events every seven days. Six source-exposed return lanes can hold six articles for the 504-hour minimum. Six rigs, each reusable no more often than once every twenty-one days, also yield two starts per week. The three capacities are deliberately balanced. Source preparation and evidence replay are sized to the same rate.

The balanced system completes the primary schedule with the last event at day 42, the last physical return at day 63 and class review at day 77. If all fourteen reserves are consumed but distributed across the six rigs, the last event can occur at day 91 and class review at day 126. The binding case is concentrated invalidity. Seven replacements consumed by one cell delay that cell’s last event to day 154; the other root cell on the same rig must then execute at day 175, placing final return at day 196 and class review at day 210. If the seven reserves in each root family are consumed by the two cells on one non-seed rig, that rig must execute sixteen attempts in sequence. Its last event occurs at day 322, its last return at day 343 and class review at day 357. National event, laboratory and evidence capacity is then mostly idle. Exact-cell continuity, not aggregate capacity, determines the calendar.

The twelve-month campaign envelope is therefore a physical reserve against the maximum authorized concentration of invalid attempts. It is not a claim that common-cause quarantine, facility loss or a new scientific question can be solved within one year. Those conditions stop or amend the campaign.

Every attempt remains the complete RBC-1 event: forty-two gates, 217 dossier bindings, thirty-six safeing-path proofs, sixty root-specific measurements, ten samples, twenty regeneration work packages, fourteen return decisions and twenty-four outside-origin replay decisions. Required execution therefore creates 504 event gates and 2,604 dossier bindings. Maximum capacity creates 1,092 and 5,642 respectively. Evidence grows from approximately 2,304.739 gigabytes native and 2,996.160 gigabytes allocated for twelve attempts to 4,993.601 gigabytes native and 6,491.681 gigabytes allocated for twenty-six. Invalid packets coexist with every later packet.

Class budgets remain fixed at the twelve-credit design, regardless of attempts: no more than 3.000 kilograms of environmental source and 22.500 kilograms of unaccounted source across every issued event. Each credit must independently meet ancestry recovery of at least 0.995, cross-talk of at most 0.005, the five-square-metre capture boundary, measured receiver capacity and the 2.5-kilogram expanded-uncertainty ceiling. A clean replacement cannot erase an earlier loss.

Class qualification requires twelve exact cell credits, six unlike-root contrasts, six rig decisions, three campus decisions, one `AM-05` treatment decision, complete package custody, accepted return for every issued attempt, two non-origin reconstructions of every packet and one immutable class record. The result can authorize only constitution of the first 500-kilogram `AM-05 QE-04` event. It does not authorize that event directly.

The next scale cannot inherit the current hydraulic system by multiplication. Five hundred kilograms over 120 seconds through the 40-millimetre route would raise the conservative closure-surge screen from 1.658 to approximately 3.316 megapascals, beyond the 2-megapascal design boundary. Nominal source volume would become 0.625 cubic metres, exceeding the current 0.40-cubic-metre usable receiver. `RBE-1` must therefore compete longer source schedules, new route geometries, compliant energy management, shaped metering and larger receiver architectures before source issue.

RBD-1 uses the existing 384-position public establishment. Two event units use 128 positions; six independent regeneration lanes use ninety-six; source custody and two preparation laboratories use forty-eight; evidence, replay and boards use forty; and relief, scheduling, environment, waste, safety and command use seventy-two. The twelve-month stage allocates USD 52 million capital, USD 14 million annual support and USD 120 million of cross-classified technology. Stage exposure is USD 66 million. The `RAS-1` parent retains USD 70 million capital, USD 9 million annual support and USD 90 million technology authority. Cumulative child-stage exposure becomes USD 857 million against the parent’s USD 1.2075 billion gross exposure.

The companion workbook separates planning capacity from physical evidence. Its twenty sheets begin with every execution register open and the physical class state blocked. All 144 synthetic boundary cases and 180 terminal planning audits pass. Interval tightening, complete return, early-return denial, cross-root reserve denial, ordered reserve issue and valid-adverse retention recalculate correctly in a disposable verification copy. The saved-workbook scan finds zero formula errors, every sheet has been rendered for review and the OpenXML package validates without compression errors. The 65,159-byte artifact has SHA-256 `9b8e0f509b5c442329f88ceb9fc99ca356a02dcf67ba7fc5c83376d018dbc42b`. These checks test the schedule, reserve, source, identity, evidence and finance rules without asserting that RBC-1 closed or that any additional event occurred. Exterior rendering remains blocked.

## 1. Stocktake: the class is a return system

The Root A sequence has progressively changed the evidence object.

| Constitution | Evidence object | Question resolved | Maximum authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RBA-1` | first 100 kg event | can one cell admit, account for and recover source? | one `QE-02` cell |
| `RBB-1` | complete 100 kg class | does low-source handling transfer across methods, roots, rigs and campuses? | bounded `QE-02` source transfer |
| `RBC-1` | first 250 kg unignited event | does the 40 mm system remain testable in the higher-rate state? | one `AM-05 QE-03` cell |
| `RBD-1` | complete 250 kg unignited class | can the higher-rate result transfer while every exposed system returns honestly? | bounded `AM-05 QE-03` transfer |
| later work | first 500 kg event | what architecture replaces the current hydraulic and receiver boundary? | separately constituted `QE-04` entry |

The governing proposition is:

> Can all twelve public rig–primary-root cells execute the accepted 250-kilogram unignited event, preserve every issued attempt and source package, return each source-exposed boundary no earlier than 504 hours, reproduce the evidence outside its origin and close one fixed class budget without borrowing identity or allowance across cells?

This proposition makes return part of performance. An event that ends safely but leaves the rig, receiver, capture surface, source ledger or evidence root unable to establish a new baseline has not completed the class service cycle.

## 2. Four identities that cannot be merged

RBD-1 preserves four populations.

- A **cell** is one exact rig–preassigned-primary-root proposition under `AM-05 QE-03`.
- An **attempt** is one issued 250-kilogram event with its own package, baseline, source ledger and packet.
- A **credit** is the one valid result that can close an exact cell.
- A **return cycle** joins an attempt’s incoming credential, event, terminal state, delayed observations, regeneration and new credential.

Every issued attempt enters the attempt and return-cycle denominators. A valid contained event can create one credit after its return closes. A valid adverse event remains scientifically valid, earns no passing credit and cannot receive a replacement. `INVALID` means the registered proposition did not occur or cannot be interpreted because identity, initial condition, calibration, time, evidence, issue authority or unregistered intervention failed. Only invalidity can activate the next root-family reserve.

The distinction prevents three errors: deleting failed attempts from validity, repeating an unfavorable physical result, and treating a clean article as equivalent to a source-exposed article that completed its delayed evidence.

## 3. The class bank and token law

### 3.1 Twenty-eight packages

Each 300-kilogram package keeps the RBC-1 split.

| Allocation | Mass per package | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| event aliquot | 250 kg | exact source issue |
| retained assay | 15 kg | property and ancestry reconstruction |
| conditioning and route verification | 20 kg | source-family-specific pre-event work |

| **gross package** | **300 kg** | complete custody object |

The bank contains twelve active cell packages, seven `TR-A` reserves, seven `TR-B` reserves and two complete family references. The references remain sealed even if every operational reserve is used. Gross custody is 8,400 kilograms. Required event issue is 3,000 kilograms. Maximum event issue is 6,500 kilograms. The remaining mass retains assay, conditioning, quarantine and forensic functions and is reconciled separately.

### 3.2 Ordered family queues

Operational reserves are family-bound but not assigned to a particular cell before invalidity. Each family publishes a serial order before class issue. When a cell becomes invalid, the next unconsumed package in that root-family queue can activate for that same cell. The board cannot inspect package properties and then choose a preferred reserve.

One cell may consume all seven family reserves. That is necessary for the capacity probability claimed by RBC-1, but it creates the concentrated calendar case. If an eighth invalidity occurs after the primary and seven replacements, the family has no lawful capacity for that cell. The sealed reference does not become an event package.

### 3.3 Capacity probability is not qualification probability

Conditional on the accepted seed, one root family needs five remaining credits and the other needs six. With seven reserves in each family and independent attempt validity (p), the probability of enough family-correct attempts is:

\[
P_{5,7}(p)P_{6,7}(p),\qquad
P_{s,r}(p)=\sum_{j=0}^{r}{s-1+j\choose j}p^s(1-p)^j.
\]

| Attempt-validity hypothesis | Conditional family-capacity probability |
|---:|---:|
| 0.60 | 85.0617% |
| 0.65 | 92.9470% |
| 0.70 | 97.2461% |
| 0.75 | 99.1585% |
| 0.80 | 99.8174% |
| 0.90 | 99.9988% |

The calculation assumes independent validity and excludes valid adverse outcomes and common causes. It cannot predict class success. It says only whether the source bank contains enough family-correct attempts under the stated abstraction.

## 4. A seed-normalized schedule

### 4.1 Public role transform

RBC-1 selected one of twelve exact cells through a six-custodian modulo-twelve draw. RBD-1 does not redraw. After acceptance, the schedule renames the selected rig `S0` and the selected root `RS`. Its same-campus mate becomes `S1`. The other two campuses contribute ordered rig pairs `S2/S3` and `S4/S5` by their frozen public serials. `RO` means the other preassigned primary root.

This is a label transform, not a change in cell assignment. Actual rig serial, campus, article, package and root remain in the execution register. The normalized schedule makes the rule readable without assuming which cell won the predecessor draw.

### 4.2 Primary calendar

| Day from accepted seed `T0` | Event 1 | Event 2 | Active return lanes after issue |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 0 | `S0–RS` accepted seed | — | 1 |
| 7 | `S1–RO` | `S2–RS` | 3 |

Every rig’s two primaries are separated by twenty-one days. No campus hosts two source events on one date. The first six cells contain three `RS` and three `RO` assignments; the second six reverse them. Root balance is therefore preserved without pretending that the accepted seed occurred concurrently with later cells.

The active-return count assumes a predecessor lane releases at its exact 504-hour minimum before a successor issues. A delayed decision postpones the successor. It never compresses the interval.

Four capacity chains must agree.

Evidence ingest supports two simultaneous 53.40384-megabyte-per-second full-rate streams. Replay and board capacity supports two new attempt packets per seven days while prior packets continue their 504-hour physical return.

This balance is intentionally conservative. A third event unit would not increase class pace unless the Department also added rigs or shortened a physically qualified return. A seventh regeneration lane would not help while six rigs retain twenty-one-day credentials. More source packages do not accelerate a cell whose exact rig is still exposed.

## 6. Four calendars, one source bank

### 6.1 Primary-only case

With no invalidity, the class has twelve attempts. The last event occurs on day 42. Its minimum return closes on day 63. Two non-origin replay windows and the class board consume a fourteen-day closeout tail, placing class review on day 77.

### 6.2 All reserves distributed across the rig pool

If all fourteen reserves activate but attempt demand remains balanced across rigs, the system can use its two-per-week national pace. After the seed, twenty-five attempts occupy thirteen weekly start positions. The last event occurs on day 91, the last return on day 112 and class review on day 126.

This is the result an aggregate queue produces. It is useful for storage, staffing and source-laboratory sizing. It is not a maximum calendar.

### 6.3 One family concentrated in one cell

Suppose one non-seed primary becomes invalid and each of the seven replacements in that family also precedes the final credit. The same rig cannot start the next attempt until 504 hours after the prior `T0`. If the primary occurs at day 7, the eight attempt dates for that cell are days 7, 28, 49, 70, 91, 112, 133 and 154. Its return closes on day 175. The rig still owes its other primary-root cell, which can now issue at day 175, return at day 196 and enter class review at day 210.

Five other rigs, the other event unit, unused return lanes and the second source-preparation line cannot replace the missing exact-cell time.

### 6.4 Both families concentrated on one non-seed rig

The maximum authorized local concentration assigns all seven `TR-A` reserves to one root cell and all seven `TR-B` reserves to the other root cell on the same non-seed rig. The rig must first complete or exhaust the earlier scheduled cell, then execute the second. Sixteen source events occur at twenty-one-day intervals beginning at day 7. The last event occurs on day 322, return closes on day 343 and class review occurs on day 357.

The class still uses only twenty-six attempts, but average national use over the complete 357-day window is approximately 0.510 attempts per week, 25.5 percent of the installed two-per-week start capacity. This is not inefficiency that a scheduler can remove. It is the cost of retaining the exact causal proposition under family-concentrated invalidity.

The twelve-month stage leaves eight days beyond this constructed boundary. It does not include common-cause investigation, article reconstruction or campus loss. Any of those conditions invokes a prospective amendment.

## 9. Source-exposed return and transition truth

Each attempt creates a pair-cycle record containing incoming baseline, package issue, event `T0`, terminal source, 72-hour residue observation, 336-hour residue observation, twenty work packages, fourteen decisions, two non-origin replays and the new baseline. `AVAILABLE` is the conjunction of those records and elapsed time. A clean appearance or elapsed day twenty-one alone does not return the rig.

Required execution creates twelve pair cycles and six between-cell transitions. Maximum execution creates twenty-six pair cycles and twenty actual transitions if all six rigs issue at least one attempt. Within-cell replacement and between-cell progression remain distinct transition types.

A replacement follows the invalid attempt in the actual sequence, not a primary placeholder in the nominal calendar.

## 11. Class inference and transfer decision

### 11.1 Unlike-root contrasts

The two roots observe every event but own different exact cells. Each rig produces one registered contrast after both root cells credit. The comparison includes terminal-source time, final source bins, environmental source, unaccounted source, ancestry recovery, cross-talk, occupied area, receiver state, peak pressure, closure transient, uncertainty and delayed residue.

A contrast does not pool terminal bins or force unlike instruments to report identical point values. It asks whether both roots support compatible decisions within their registered uncertainties and whether the difference has a reconstructed physical or measurement cause.

### 11.2 Rig, campus and treatment decisions

Each rig decision requires two exact credits, one accepted between-cell transition, both return cycles and one unlike-root contrast. Each campus decision requires four exact credits, all issued local return cycles and complete non-origin reconstruction of its originating packets. The `AM-05` treatment decision requires all twelve credits and all six root contrasts.

The final source-transfer decision requires:

1. twelve exact cell credits;
2. no valid adverse cell;
3. every issued attempt retained in validity and source denominators;
4. complete return for every issued attempt;
5. six accepted unlike-root contrasts;
6. six rig, three campus and one treatment decision;
7. fixed environmental and unaccounted-source budgets closed;
8. complete package, sample, residue and waste custody;
9. two non-origin reconstructions of every active packet;
10. all changes, exceptions and quarantines resolved; and
11. one immutable class record accepted by separate safety, validity, source and portfolio authorities.

No majority rule or favorable subset can substitute for an exact missing cell.

## 15. Why `QE-04` is a new architecture question

The first possible successor is a constitution for one 500-kilogram `AM-05 QE-04` event. It is not a class and it is not yet authorized to issue.

At nominal density, 500 kilograms occupies 0.625 cubic metres. The current receiver has 0.40 cubic metres of usable volume before uncertainty and initial inventory. At the 792-kilogram-per-cubic-metre accepted low-density edge, source volume is approximately 0.6313 cubic metres. Receiver replacement is mandatory.

If the 120-second schedule and 40-millimetre route were retained, velocity would rise to approximately 4.145 metres per second and the conservative instantaneous surge screen to approximately 3.316 megapascals. That exceeds the 2-megapascal design boundary before measured closure behavior is considered. Extending the schedule to 240 seconds would hold nominal velocity and surge at the `QE-03` level, but it changes duration, control, hold-up and receiver dynamics. A larger route lowers velocity while increasing static inventory and source retained inside the line.

`RBE-1` must therefore compare at least five architecture families: longer-duration 40-millimetre transfer; an intermediate-bore replaceable route; distributed compliant volume; shaped source metering with bounded stop energy; and a larger modular receiver with independent casualty capacity. The competition must preserve the two public roots and current class evidence while treating each new geometry as a new experimental object.

This is how ambitious technology enters the Department. The answer is not limited to equipment already on the market, but every novel capability still has to earn authority through physical evidence.

- an invalid attempt disappears from any denominator or source sum;
- environmental source exceeds 3.000 kilograms across issued attempts;
- unaccounted source exceeds 22.500 kilograms across issued attempts;
- a credit averages away ancestry, area, receiver, pressure or uncertainty failure;
- an event packet lacks two non-origin reconstructions;

- the maximum authorized local sequence cannot close by day 357;

- any decision is used to release `AM-06`, a 500-kilogram event, candidate comparison, production or exterior form.

Common-cause evidence can quarantine a family, rig, campus, laboratory or the entire bank before reserve exhaustion. That is a scientific result, not a schedule variance.

## 17. Research stocktake

RBD-1 adds five conclusions to the Department design.

First, campaign capacity is topological. Equal aggregate rates do not guarantee an executable class when identity-constrained work can concentrate on one physical path.

Second, regeneration is an experimental resource with locality. Six national return lanes close average demand, but one exact rig can still govern the year.

Third, reserves consume calendar as well as inventory. Source-bank probability and schedule probability are different claims and require different models.

Fourth, the correct response to a local bottleneck is a technology program with a new test object. The Department should pursue removable wetted boundaries, faster residue observation and robotic recovery, but it cannot credit their future promise inside the present class.

Fifth, scale escalation must reopen architecture. The next source state exceeds both the current receiver and conservative surge boundary. A mature public research institution treats that contradiction as the origin of a competition, not a reason to stretch a qualified label.

An accepted RBD-1 class can authorize only `RBE-1`, the first 500-kilogram `AM-05 QE-04` architecture and event constitution. `AM-06 QE-03`, source-water crossover, casualty-area fire, the 150-millimetre production route, candidate articles, production, fleet procurement, export release and exterior rendering remain blocked.
