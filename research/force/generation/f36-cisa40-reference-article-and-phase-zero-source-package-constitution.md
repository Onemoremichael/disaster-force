# F36 CISA-40 reference-article and phase-zero source package constitution

## Executive judgment

The public test complex defined in Pass 115 is a facility constitution. It specifies where a test may occur, what each lane must be capable of doing, how the estate survives complete loss of one campus, and which evidence and fiscal boundaries apply. It does not yet specify the physical objects by which the complex proves that any of those claims are true. A lane cannot commission itself with its own controller, its own sensors or an unloaded demonstration. It must encounter known physical states whose identities, magnitudes, uncertainties and dispositions exist independently of the lane.

This pass creates `RSP-1`, the Reference-Article and Source-Package constitution beneath `PTC-0`. Its governing decision is whether the 156 phase-zero events can be converted from event names into a manufactured, root-balanced and auditable source package without turning planning records into physical evidence. The answer is yes at planning level. The package requires seventy-two serialized reference or surrogate bodies, six per lane. Each lane receives three Root A and three Root B bodies. Every body carries four interchangeable known-truth modules: datum or blank, known compliant, known nonconforming and decision boundary. The result is 288 separately identified truth modules.

The seventy-two bodies are not generic steel frames painted different colors. They retain the unlike material, joint, service, control and evidence ancestry of the two `CISA-40` roots. Their public mounting and measurement boundaries remain common, but the physical path that creates the response does not. A range that can recognize a known defect only in the same architecture used to calibrate its algorithm has not demonstrated root independence.

Two additional failure-insertion packages are assigned to every root–family pair. One acts on the data plane: sensor saturation, native-data removal, command–observation disagreement, clock loss, network denial and signing-root denial. The other acts on safety and retained hazard: primary-controller removal, one-actuator denial and deliberate preservation of residual load, pressure, electrical energy, heat, fluid or contamination. Twenty-four kits therefore exist independently of the article controller, recorder, power supply, clock, signing key and operator.

The 120 mounted lane events use an exact mirrored assignment. Each lane performs the ten inherited causal classes. Odd and even sibling lanes invert the assigned root for every class. Each lane therefore performs five Root A and five Root B events, and each family encounters every event class once under each root at two different sites. Root assignment is not left to operator convenience after an article arrives.

The source ledger also corrects a category error in the predecessor containment discussion. Installed source, maximum event source and maximum credible release are different quantities. A facility may hold eight tonnes of fuel-equivalent inventory while passive segregation limits one credible release to a two-tonne compartment. The event may use the full installed inventory without assuming that all of it can enter one release path. Conversely, an active valve cannot be credited as a passive boundary merely because it normally closes.

That distinction reveals the governing hazard. A single two-tonne Root A liquid-fuel compartment contains 85.176 GJ of chemical energy. The Root B DME compartment contains 57.024 GJ and can form approximately 1,060 m³ of gas at the declared reference state. The maximum Root A and Root B thermal commissioning events contain 4.20 and 3.60 GJ. The electrical, mechanical and hydraulic release cases are megajoule-scale or smaller. The `SA6` energy–ballast cell therefore governs the next safety-case pass even though `SA5` governs lane throughput.

`RSP-1` assigns USD 0.168B of capital and USD 0.044B/year of annual support inside the already constituted `PTC-0` boundary. Fourteen months of annual service adds USD 0.051333B, producing total source-package exposure of USD 0.219333B. The source package consumes 39.07 percent of the USD 0.430B physical-use capital estimate. Because its assets are CISA-specific, it also consumes USD 0.168B of the USD 0.302B net-new CISA capital and USD 0.044B/year of the USD 0.057B/year net-new annual allowance. USD 0.134B capital and USD 0.013B/year remain for the other CISA-specific facility, containment and commissioning uses. This is an allocation within `BRD-2R`, not a new request above it.

Ninety-six planning gates close: seventy-two body packages, twelve root–family source ledgers, six family event-card sets, three campus-denial packages and three campus custody/destructive packages. All thirty-six physical-source gates remain open. No site, lane, reference group, denial set, replay, vault pair or destructive complex has supplied an accepted native packet. The six downstream decisions remain blocked: `SDEC-1`, complete-article admission, `CISA-RB1`, `CRC-E01`, production and exterior rendering.

## 1. Decision and boundary

### 1.1 Decision

`RSP-1` answers:

> What manufactured reference bodies, truth modules, source terms, independent failure insertions, safe-state clocks, custody rules and acceptance packets must exist before `PTC-0` may receive the sixty-four `SDEC-1` campaign specimens?

The pass makes three decisions.

First, phase-zero truth is an inventory, not a label. Every ACCEPT, REJECT, INDETERMINATE, INVALID, SAFE STATE, SAFE STATE AND REPLAY or QUARANTINE expectation must be attached to a serialized body, truth module, failure command and evidence schema before the lane is armed.

Second, root balance is physical. Both roots must occupy both lanes in a family pair. Each causal class must be exercised once under each root. A common mounting interface does not permit a common decisive load path, controller, recorder, clock, signing key or truth realization.

Third, a physical gate closes from a complete native source packet. A workbook value of one, a facility certificate, a photograph, a summary chart or a later reconstruction is not the source packet.

| Class | Meaning | Credit in `RSP-1` |

The fourth class cannot be inferred from the first three. A complete drawing may authorize manufacture. It cannot establish that the manufactured truth lies where the drawing placed it. A calculated safe-state time may size an actuator. It cannot establish that the actuator closed under the inserted failure.

## 2. Why a reference article is not a dummy load

A dummy load proves only that the facility can act on the dummy load. That may be useful for checking actuator travel or recorder wiring, but it does not establish that the range can separate article behavior from fixture behavior across two unlike roots.

The reference body must reproduce the decisive transaction of its family. For support/reaction it must carry load through a root-native node into the public bed. For anchor/lift it must carry force and moment through the root-native hinge, insert, weld, bond or post-tension path. For the collar it must reproduce the root-native pipe, seal and joint compliance. For bus/protection it must reproduce the conductor, contact, protection and ground-reference behavior. For thermal/evidence it must reproduce panel, hinge, coolant and evidence-route coupling. For energy–ballast it must reproduce the four-station rack, unlike fuel boundary, separate water chamber, line inventory and support reactions.

The reference body is therefore a reduced mission article, not an exterior vehicle and not a laboratory coupon. It is large enough to preserve the causal path under judgment and simple enough to accept controlled truth modules and failure insertions. It never enters the `SDEC-1` inference population. Its repeated use exposes the facility to a known state; it does not create additional independent specimens.

Three object identities remain separate:

1. **body identity** controls root ancestry, material lots, joints, service topology, mounting and prior exposure;
2. **truth-module identity** controls the known physical state, uncertainty, blind key and destructive-truth record; and
3. **event identity** controls configuration, source setting, insertion command, operators, expected disposition and evidence package.

Combining these identities into one serial would make a body replacement indistinguishable from a truth revision. Combining them into an event name would erase the physical lineage entirely.

## 4. Four truth modules per body

### 4.1 Module classes

Every body receives four replaceable truth modules:

| Code | Class | Physical relation to threshold | Expected disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `T0` | datum and blank | zero or registered clean datum | ACCEPT |
| `T1` | known compliant | target at 50 percent of threshold | ACCEPT |
| `T2` | known nonconforming | target at 150 percent of threshold | REJECT |

The modules are not universal inserts. `T1` for a structural node is a controlled geometric or compliance feature. `T1` for a bus is a controlled clearing-time implementation. `T1` for the energy–ballast rack is a controlled custody discrepancy. The `T3` module is centered on the threshold. Its uncertainty therefore overlaps both sides of the decision boundary, and the registered disposition is INDETERMINATE. A lane that forces `T3` into ACCEPT or REJECT has demonstrated overconfidence, not discrimination.

### 4.3 Why every body carries all four

Four modules per body provide three controls that a smaller set would not.

First, a body can be compared before and after a failure event without changing its root ancestry. Second, a truth module can move through a public transfer chain while the body remains in site custody. Third, drift can be localized: if one module changes, the body need not be declared the source of the change until independent remounting and metrology separate the two.

The 288-module count is physical inventory, not 288 independent statistical trials. A module used twice remains one truth object with two exposures. Its event ledger records both.

## 5. Known-truth metrology and uncertainty

### 5.1 Bottom-up budget

Each root–family decision quantity receives five independent expanded-uncertainty allocations, expressed as fractions of its physical threshold:

- reference realization: 0.08;
- truth-module fabrication: 0.10;
- mounting and transfer: 0.08;
- acquisition: 0.07; and
- analysis: 0.05.

Their root-sum-square combination is 0.17378 of the threshold. The maximum allowed combined expanded uncertainty remains 0.25 of the threshold. The resulting allocation ratio is 1.4386 between the permitted maximum and the planned total. That margin is a planning allocation, not achieved metrology.

### 5.2 Independent realization

The body fabricator cannot be the only truth authority. The minimum chain is:

1. root fabricator produces the body and module to the controlled package;
2. public configuration authority verifies identity and mounting features;
3. independent truth laboratory measures the decisive physical state;
4. blind authority replaces descriptive identity with an opaque event key;
5. separate public metrology chain verifies the lane transfer before and after exposure;
6. event decision is sealed before truth is opened; and
7. destructive truth confirms the assigned state where the method requires sectioning.

Root A and Root B may use different fabrication and destructive laboratories. The public truth file uses a common quantity definition and coordinate system so disagreement remains observable.

### 5.3 Drift and look-back

Every module carries a last-accepted comparison time, environmental and shock history, use count, mounting count and sealed state. A failed return comparison opens the interval back to the last accepted comparison. Every event that relied on the module during that interval reopens. A new module begins a new lineage; it cannot retroactively repair the old one.

Reference drift can produce INVALID or INDETERMINATE even when the body appears undamaged. That is a necessary feature. The purpose of a reference system is to expose lost knowledge, not to preserve a passing count.

## 6. Source-term constitution

### 6.1 Four quantities that cannot be merged

Every hazardous root–family event distinguishes:

1. **installed source** — the energy, fluid, mass or force available in the lane system;
2. **maximum event source** — the source admitted by the frozen event card;
3. **maximum credible release** — the source that can enter one release path after one credited passive boundary is lost; and
4. **passive capacity** — the arrest, enclosure or recovery capacity available without crediting the failed active path.

Human entry is a fifth, independent quantity. It requires a measured terminal condition. “Thirty minutes elapsed” cannot replace a gas concentration, voltage, pressure, temperature, load or mass-balance measurement.

### 6.3 Hazard hierarchy

The source hierarchy spans almost six orders of magnitude. Treating each lane as a generic “heavy test bay” would conceal this fact. Mechanical and hydraulic families are governed primarily by rapid arrest, fragment control and retained load or pressure. Electrical events are governed by arc containment, causal timing and post-fault stored energy. Thermal events are governed by sustained heat, coolant loss and delayed human entry. Energy–ballast events are governed by chemical energy, vapor or gas production, incompatible media, multi-compartment custody and post-event mass reconciliation.

The hierarchy has an institutional consequence. One uniform emergency team, one universal sensor suite or one generic containment rule cannot be presumed sufficient. Common command grammar is useful; common hazard implementation is not.

## 7. Family reference systems

### 7.1 `SA1` support/reaction

The `SA1` body carries a root-native support node, surrounding structure and measured datum network. Its public interfaces mount to the 5/25/100 MN/m self-identifying load bed. `T0` is a geometrically stable reference. `T1` carries a 0.250 mm residual datum shift. `T2` carries 0.750 mm. `T3` is centered at 0.500 mm with 0.050 mm expanded uncertainty.

The data-plane kit can remove one displacement reference, saturate a six-axis channel or impose a difference between commanded and delivered load. The safety kit can remove the servo controller, deny one unload actuator or retain 25 kN after the command reports zero. Passive catches accept the load without driving the body through the failed region.

The family-specific human-entry condition is a surveyed datum below 0.500 mm after the mechanical source is supported. An unloaded command, a quiet pump or a stationary actuator is not sufficient.

### 7.2 `SA2` anchor/lift

The `SA2` body preserves the root-native lifting and anchoring joint, including the Root A metallic joint path or Root B composite/insert/post-tension path. The public rig applies the forty-tonne equivalent payload, three load vectors and pinned, guided and capture states. Truth is assigned through controlled slip at 0, 0.250, 0.500 and 0.750 mm.

Failure insertion includes one delayed node, a false symmetric command with asymmetric delivered load, controller removal during transfer, denial of one emergency-lower path and retention of a suspended or captured load. The passive frame must receive the payload on at least two independent supports.

The maximum credible 0.25 m capture travel represents approximately 0.0001 GJ for the declared forty-tonne equivalent payload. This small energy relative to `SA6` does not imply low consequence. Uncontrolled geometry and personnel access make the safe-state time much shorter.

### 7.3 `SA3` hydraulic collar

The collar body includes root-native pipe wall, collar, seal, valve and compliance. Its `T1`, `T2` and `T3` modules create permanent bore changes at 0.050, 0.150 and 0.100 mm. Separate tracer modules can create known bypass without using a live microbiological hazard.

The lane can hold 60 m³ but the phase-zero event uses no more than 15 m³. The maximum credible liquid release remains 15 m³ and must enter the 90 m³ recovery system. A 0.25 kg tracer or contaminant surrogate remains in a separate mass ledger. The source package does not treat dilution as disappearance.

The data-plane kit can saturate pressure or leakage measurement and command 0.70 MPa while the independent source delivers 0.85 MPa. The safety kit removes the loop controller, denies one end-isolation valve or retains pressure, tracer or contaminated liquid. Human entry requires pressure below 50 kPa and a closed recovery ledger.

### 7.4 `SA4` bus/protection

The bus body preserves root-native conductor, joint, contact, protection, ground reference and evidence-power behavior. Root A is commissioned against the 100 ms clearing threshold; Root B against 150 ms. The truth modules implement 50/100/150 ms for Root A and 75/150/225 ms for Root B, with separate 10 and 15 ms expanded uncertainties.

The Root A maximum source is 12 kA prospective; Root B is 6 kA. The bounded phase-zero arc releases are 8 and 4 MJ. The lane enclosure is planned for 100 MJ, preserving capacity above the event source without relying on the primary breaker. Twenty kilograms of fragment source and bounded combustion-product volumes remain in the planning ledger.

The data-plane kit can saturate current or voltage, alter apparent source impedance, remove native waveform objects or split the event clocks. The safety kit can remove the event controller or deny one upstream isolation device. Entry requires the bus below 60 V and a stable enclosure. Restart is a new event identity.

### 7.5 `SA5` thermal/evidence

The thermal body preserves the root-native heat exchanger, panel, hinge, coolant boundary and evidence route. Root A receives a maximum phase-zero input of 3.5 MW for twenty minutes, equal to 4.20 GJ. Root B receives 3.0 MW for twenty minutes, equal to 3.60 GJ. Both remain below the 8 MW transient lane capability.

Truth modules create 2.5, 5.0 and 7.5 percent heat-balance errors around the inherited five-percent decision. Data failures remove heat-flow or temperature channels or create a command–delivery mismatch. Safety failures remove the thermal controller, deny one heat or coolant isolation path or retain a hot surface, two cubic metres of coolant or an unresolved evidence discontinuity.

The passive coolant system holds five cubic metres. Heat input must stop before coolant isolation destroys the evidence needed to understand the event. Human entry requires a measured surface below 80°C and a closed calorimetric balance.

### 7.6 `SA6` energy–ballast

The `SA6` body is a complete four-station rack because local mass replacement and cross-station imbalance cannot be commissioned on a single tank. Root A carries its double-wall liquid-fuel boundary and separately contained water chamber. Root B carries its pressure-rated DME cylinder and structurally separate annular water chamber. The two fluids never share a common wall.

Truth modules create 2.5, 5.0 and 7.5 kg custody discrepancies. The data-plane kit can saturate weighing or flow measurement, remove a native totalizer, or command one transfer while delivering a different metered quantity. The safety kit can remove the transfer controller, deny one fuel or water isolation path, or retain fuel, DME, water or an unresolved mass difference.

The lane may hold eight tonnes of each event inventory. Passive compartmentation limits the governing release to one two-tonne fuel compartment. Root A's compartment contains 85.176 GJ at 11.83 MWh/t. Root B's contains 57.024 GJ at 7.92 MWh/t and approximately 1,060 m³ of reference-state gas. The 40 m³ segregated capture volume addresses liquids and controlled vessel contents; it does not by itself address an unconfined DME gas release. Vent, dispersion, ignition, relief and exclusion remain open safety-case subjects.

Human entry requires vapor or gas below the accepted entry limit and complete mass reconciliation. A closed valve indication does not close either condition.

## 8. Failure-insertion architecture

### 8.1 Twenty-four kits

Each root–family pair receives:

- one data-plane kit; and
- one safety-and-hazard kit.

The result is twenty-four kits. A kit is a public challenge asset, not a builder option. It uses separate power, clock, signing root and command authority. The lane operator sees the permitted insertion envelope but not the exact time or branch selected by the blind authority.

### 8.2 Data-plane kit

The data-plane kit can:

- saturate a decisive channel without saturating the independent reference;
- remove one native object while leaving a summary available;
- change delivered stimulus without changing the controller command;
- deny external time while preserving local holdover;
- deny national networking;
- deny the primary signing service; and
- introduce a known timestamp or unit disagreement.

The kit never fabricates evidence after the event. It acts through registered hardware or software paths before the source packet is sealed. Its own command and achieved insertion are recorded independently.

### 8.3 Safety-and-hazard kit

The safety-and-hazard kit can:

- remove the primary controller;
- deny one active termination actuator;
- retain a bounded mechanical load;
- retain pressure or contaminated fluid;
- retain post-fault voltage or enclosure uncertainty;
- retain heat or coolant;
- retain hazardous fuel, gas or unresolved mass; and
- hold the article in quarantine after the event.

The kit cannot disable every termination path. The event asks whether the independently constituted alternate path reaches the registered terminal condition after one failure. An insertion capable of defeating all layers would be an uncontrolled accident, not a phase-zero test.
