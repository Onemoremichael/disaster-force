# F36 CISA-40 public test complex and phase-zero commissioning constitution

## Executive judgment

The full-scale campaign created in Pass 114 cannot begin merely because its specimens, events and decision rules have been enumerated. A campaign of 40,520 events can still produce weak evidence if one facility, controller, reference chain, operator pool or evidence system becomes the only way to judge both industrial roots. Pass 115 therefore creates **PTC-0**, the public test-complex and phase-zero commissioning constitution beneath SDEC-1. It treats the range estate as a national technical force whose first duty is to prove its own ability to fail safely, preserve truth and disagree with itself.

PTC-0 uses the three public proving campuses constituted in Pass 104. It does not infer that those campuses have been built. It allocates twelve full-capability family lanes across the three-campus topology: two lanes for each of the six SDEC-1 families, always at different sites. The lane pair is the minimum architecture that separates family behavior from one site's fixture, utility, environment and personnel. Both Root A and Root B specimens are distributed across both lanes. A root is never assigned permanently to a site, instrument chain or operator pool.

The topology is intentionally nonuniform. PTC-A hosts one support/reaction lane, one anchor/lift lane, one hydraulic-collar lane and one thermal/evidence lane. PTC-B hosts the second support/reaction lane, one bus/protection lane, the second thermal/evidence lane and one energy–ballast lane. PTC-C hosts the second anchor/lift, hydraulic-collar, bus/protection and energy–ballast lanes. Loss of any campus removes at most one lane from any family. Every family retains a complete alternative path.

Each surviving lane is sized to execute its family's entire SDEC-1 workload inside the fourteen-month physical window. The capacity calculation includes event time, serial installation and changeover, sixteen operating hours per day, 30.4375 days per month and 70-percent productive availability. The thermal/evidence family governs. Its full 4,000-event workload consumes 3,160 station-hours against 4,772.6 available hours, or 66.21 percent after loss of the sibling site. The other families range from 13.18 to 40.73 percent. The second lane is therefore an independent productive asset, not a nominal backup that would need a future expansion to carry the campaign.

The facility estate is physical. It contains twelve family lanes, twelve operator control rooms, twelve independent witness rooms, six separately powered evidence vaults, six calibration laboratories, three destructive-examination cells, three blind-configuration shops, eighty-four full-scale specimen positions, six 100-tonne bridge cranes, three 80-tonne mobile gantries, six self-propelled heavy transporters and twelve traveling metrology packages. Every campus carries 30 MW of utility intake, 6 MW of islanded critical capacity, 5 MWh of locally controlled stored energy, 400 m³ of treated process water, 600 m³ of quarantine water and 1,200 m³ of independent fire water.

The twelve lanes do not share one decisive technical ancestor. For each family, PTC-0 separately registers site, utility control region, water and quarantine chain, fixture fabrication process, stimulus controller, acquisition recorder, calibration transfer chain, time implementation, signing root, lead crew, evidence-vault authority and destructive laboratory. Common units, public interface definitions and cross-comparison artifacts are allowed. A common device or organization capable of determining both lane results is not.

Before a CISA-40 specimen may enter any lane, the complex executes 156 phase-zero events. Each lane performs ten mounted known-truth events, for 120 lane events. Each campus performs eight denial drills, for twenty-four campus events. Each family contributes two sealed packets to unlike cross-site reconstruction, for twelve replays. The lane sequence contains a reference zero, a known compliant article, a known nonconforming article, an intentionally indeterminate boundary case, a sensor saturation or native-data gap, a command–observation disagreement, loss of the primary controller, loss of one safe-state actuator, clock/signing/network denial and a retained-hazard or carryover condition. Every known truth must receive its registered disposition.

Commissioning is not a reliability sample. One successful denial event does not estimate the probability of surviving denial, and ten events do not qualify a family. The rule is categorical: one misclassification, one unsafe command, one missing original, one unreconciled fixture state or one failed cross-site replay leaves that lane uncommissioned. Results may not be pooled across sibling lanes to rescue a failure.

Pass 115 also resolves the financial ambiguity created in Pass 114. The USD 0.430B capital and USD 0.098B/year physical-use view remains correct: those are the assets and services consumed by SDEC-1. It was not yet a funding-owner view. The predecessor public complex already carries common halls, utility islands, water and waste systems, metrology, evidence vaults, blind shops and forensic lines inside its USD 4.480B capital and USD 0.398B/year boundary. Charging those objects again to CISA-1 would double count the same public estate.

With a registered predecessor reservation, USD 0.128B of the physical-use capital and USD 0.041B/year of annual service are cross-classified to the public complex. Net new CISA-1 requirements become USD 0.302B capital and USD 0.057B/year. The reconciled BRD-2 boundary is therefore USD 0.620B capital and USD 0.141B/year, leaving USD 0.340B and USD 0.119B/year inside CISA-1. The conservative no-credit envelope remains the Pass 114 USD 0.748B and USD 0.182B/year result. Even that envelope fits inside CISA-1, leaving USD 0.212B and USD 0.078B/year. N4 still reopens BRD-1, but it does not require an increase to the CISA-1 parent boundary.

The shared credit is conditional. A planning reservation can reconcile program ledgers. It cannot make a campus, vault, laboratory or crew physically available. If the reservation, capacity or independence source fails, the credit disappears and the no-credit envelope governs. Execution remains unauthorized.

Seventy-two planning gates close. Thirty-six source-dependent gates remain open because no site, lane, reference group, denial set, cross-site replay, vault pair or destructive laboratory has been physically accepted. Six downstream decisions remain blocked: SDEC execution, complete-article admission, the CISA-RB1 disposition, CRC-E01, production and exterior rendering. PTC-0 permits internal facility plans, containment sections, circuit diagrams, fluid-custody paths and fixture free-body diagrams. It does not authorize an exterior form.

## 1. Decision, claim and boundary

### 1.1 Decision

This pass asks:

> What public sites, duplicate test lanes, utility islands, containment systems, evidence authorities and deliberately failed commissioning events are required before SDEC-1 may expose a CISA-40 specimen to full-scale testing?

The answer must satisfy six conditions:

1. one campus loss cannot remove an entire family from the campaign;
2. both industrial roots must encounter both facility implementations;
3. a lane must classify known truth before it judges unknown article behavior;
4. hazardous work must reach a registered safe state after a single decisive facility failure;
5. two native originals and unlike reconstruction must survive controller and network denial; and
6. shared public infrastructure must receive one funding owner and one capacity reservation.

### 1.2 Claim classes

PTC-0 can create only four planning claim classes:

- **topology-complete:** every family has two physically separated full-capability lanes;
- **capacity-sufficient:** one surviving lane can complete the full family workload inside the registered window;
- **commissioning-defined:** every facility decision boundary has a known-truth and deliberate-failure event; and
- **fiscally-reconciled:** the physical-use view and funding-owner view tie without adding a predecessor asset twice.

Physical source records are required before any lane becomes commissioned. A commissioned lane may then support SDEC-1 mechanism and screening claims. It cannot create a fleet reliability, service-life, production-yield or operational-readiness claim.

### 1.3 Inherited boundary

PTC-0 preserves the SDEC-1 campaign:

## 2. Derivation from the predecessor range estate

### 2.1 What Pass 104 already constituted

The [F36 range-complex throughput and regeneration architecture](f36-range-complex-throughput-and-regeneration-architecture.md) created three complete public campus islands. Each campus was required to hold local power, source and receiver water, waste custody, metrology, evidence replay, blind configuration, forensics, lifting, isolation and shift support. It also established a USD 4.480B capital and USD 0.398B/year standing boundary.

Those objects are common public infrastructure. They are not tied to one exterior vehicle or one root. PTC-0 therefore treats the following predecessor objects as candidate shared services:

- site security, receiving and internal heavy routes;
- common test halls and exclusion shells;
- campus utility intake and black-start islands;
- treated-water, quarantine and waste systems;
- evidence vault shells, clocks and replay rooms;
- public calibration and transfer-metrology spaces;
- blind-configuration shops;
- forensic and destructive laboratories;
- general lifting and specimen handling; and
- public range operations, safety and custody staff.

The word “shared” does not mean available. Each object still needs a named reservation, capacity state, configuration identity and physical commissioning record.

### 2.2 What SDEC-1 must add

The predecessor complex was sized around full-bore closure and damage work. It does not contain a credited SDEC-1 lane merely because it has a building or a crane. CISA-1 must add:

- four high-force structural and lift fixtures;
- two full-scale hydraulic-collar rigs;
- two independent high-current fault sources and arc cells;
- two thermal/evidence calorimetric lanes;
- two four-station energy–ballast rigs;
- CISA-specific adapters, reference articles and surrogate articles;
- family-specific acquisition and safe-state controllers;
- controlled-defect truth objects;
- root-native execution teams; and
- the exact phase-zero commissioning population.

These are the net new program obligations. General halls and utilities cannot complete a CISA article, while a CISA fixture cannot claim the whole campus around it.

### 2.3 The non-additive ledger

PTC-0 uses three simultaneous views:

| View | Meaning | Capital | Annual support |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| SDEC physical use | everything the campaign consumes | USD 0.430B | USD 0.098B/year |
| shared predecessor reservation | common public services already carried once | USD 0.128B | USD 0.041B/year |
| net new CISA requirement | additional funding-owner burden | USD 0.302B | USD 0.057B/year |

The physical-use view is necessary for capacity, scheduling and causal accounting. The funding-owner view is necessary for fiscal reconciliation. Neither may substitute for the other.

Event count is a poor site-load measure because a three-minute collar cycle and a twelve-hour fault-shot occupation are not equivalent. Station-hours produce a much more balanced campus allocation: the largest planned site burden is only about eighteen percent above the smallest.

### 3.3 Root–site balance

Every family assigns half of each root's serial population to each lane. This prevents three confounds:

- Root A cannot inherit the environmental history of one site while Root B inherits another;
- one fixture implementation cannot become synonymous with one root; and
- an observed root difference cannot be explained entirely by operator or calibration ancestry.

Within a lane, event order remains randomized and block-balanced as specified in SDEC-1. Site and root balance do not authorize pooling across serialized specimens.

### 3.4 Complete-site loss

The family-pair topology survives loss of PTC-A, PTC-B or PTC-C. A site loss removes one lane from four families and no lanes from the remaining two. It never removes both lanes of one family.

The surviving lane does not receive automatic credit. The National Range Scheduling Authority must prove that the remaining lane is READY, has sufficient calendar and support capacity, retains unlike evidence and calibration ancestry, and can accept transferred specimens without changing their history. A topology diagram is not a loss rehearsal.

## 4. Lane-capacity constitution

### 4.1 Capacity equation

One lane's productive hours are:

**H = months × days per month × hours per day × availability**

Using fourteen months, 30.4375 days/month, sixteen hours/day and 70-percent productive availability:

**H = 14 × 30.4375 × 16 × 0.70 = 4,772.6 station-hours**

The full family burden includes event occupation and serial setup:

**B = events × minutes per event / 60 + serials × setup hours per serial**

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |

| SA2 | 1,600 | 30 | 128 | 928.000 | 9.7% | 19.4% |

The 70-percent site-loss ceiling preserves 3.79 percentage points of modeled headroom in the governing SA5 lane. This is intentionally narrow enough to remain a falsifiable requirement. These durations are planning hypotheses. Phase-zero data must replace them with observed setup, event, reset, investigation and calibration distributions. A lane cannot claim capacity from the shortest successful run while excluding invalid or quarantine time.

The 30 MW intake is a planning nameplate. Commissioning must prove the actual voltage, frequency, harmonic, short-circuit, protection, grounding and recovery envelopes at each lane boundary.

The critical island is separate from the hazardous stimulus source. It preserves control, trip, valve closure, metrology, record sealing, emergency lighting, ventilation, access and communications. PTC-A carries 3.2 MW of declared critical load, PTC-B 3.6 MW and PTC-C 3.4 MW against 6 MW of islanded capacity. The 5 MWh store supports transition and orderly termination; it does not supply credit for a hazardous event that requires a missing source.

### 6.2 Fault energy

The two SA4 cells each provide a registered source envelope of up to 20 kA and 1 kV, with 40 MJ of controlled source energy and a 100 MJ arc, fragment and hot-gas enclosure. The source, enclosure and instrument saturation limits are distinct. A cell cannot claim safety because the source failed to deliver the intended shot, and it cannot claim a valid article result after a measurement chain saturates.

The fault source must be locally isolable from the campus critical island. Residual-energy proof is an observed state, not elapsed time after a trip. Restart is a separate witnessed event.

### 6.3 Water and quarantine

Every campus receives the same 400 m³ treated-water and 600 m³ quarantine-water minimum even when its current lane set does not use the full amount. Standardized utility islands allow a future lane module or transferred specimen to arrive without a new civil project.

Product, challenge, cleaning, fire and waste water retain separate identities. No unmeasured manifold or common drain may collapse those ledgers. A clean assay cannot authorize discharge when recovery mass, blank state or custody remains open.

### 6.4 Heat rejection

PTC-A and PTC-B carry 12 MW of heat-rejection capacity because they host the two SA5 lanes. PTC-C carries 8 MW for hydraulic, fault and energy–ballast support and can receive a modular thermal cell after a site loss. The SA5 lane itself is specified for 5 MW continuous and 8 MW transient measurement.

Heat rejection is measured through independent electrical and calorimetric balances. A controller command or cooling-plant nameplate cannot close the energy balance.

## 7. Family range constitutions

### 7.1 SA1 support/reaction

Each SA1 lane provides a 5 MN vertical and 2 MN lateral load system, 300 mm of independently imposed settlement, 5/25/100 MN/m foundation-stiffness states and a machine-surveyed reaction frame. Mechanical catches support safe unload after loss of active control. The fixture must expose six-axis reaction, cross-axis transfer, local pad force, datum change and residual set.

The technology objective is a **self-identifying civil load bed**: modular reaction blocks and active receiver cells that measure their own stiffness and geometric transfer matrix after assembly. Such a bed would later qualify deployable hospitals, power plants, bridge systems, desalination carriers and heavy rescue modules without requiring a bespoke permanent laboratory for each.

### 7.2 SA2 anchor/lift

Each SA2 lane provides a 3 MN force and 4 MN·m moment envelope, three load vectors, pinned/guided/capture states and a one-node-loss insertion. The cell includes a capture frame, alternate recovery gantry and independently observed load transformation. It must distinguish article redistribution from parasitic fixture restraint.

The technology objective is a **programmable heavy-handling proving cell** that can impose denied-node, delayed-node, shifting-center-of-gravity and weak-ground states while preserving human stop authority. This makes receiver-independent handling an engineering discipline rather than a crane demonstration.

### 7.3 SA3 hydraulic collar

Each SA3 lane carries a 1.5 MPa rated loop, 60 m³ active inventory, 90 m³ captured recovery, three line-compliance states, two dead volumes, bending and thermal conditioning. Two-end isolation, contained dump and independent leakage collection must operate without the primary controller.

The cell treats sanitation as a conserved process. Conductive and fluorescent tracers, inert microspheres, organic-residue surrogates, blanks, split samples and waste custody remain distinguishable through every cycle.

The technology objective is a **software-defined sanitary hydraulic range** with interchangeable compliance modules, machine-readable dead volume, automated mass reconciliation and robotic cleanout. It should change root geometry in hours without changing public truth.

### 7.4 SA4 bus/protection

Each SA4 lane contains an unlike high-current source, protection implementation, recorder and clock. It can reproduce low, nominal and challenge prospective source states for both root architectures without supplying the root's missing ground reference or trip logic.

The technology objective is a **programmable converter-dominated grid-fault laboratory**. It must reproduce rotating-machine, inverter-limited, weak-grid and island transitions while measuring source impedance and causal timing directly. This capability applies to deployable power plants, hospital microgrids, cooling corridors and water systems.

### 7.5 SA5 thermal/evidence

Each SA5 lane combines a 5 MW continuous and 8 MW transient calorimetric source, 12 MW site heat sink, airflow and sink-temperature states, partial obstruction, wet/dirty exposure and independent evidence power. It tests heat balance, deployment, articulation, leak isolation, asymmetric rejection and witness continuity.

The technology objective is a **modular multi-megawatt thermal truth cell** that can accept unlike cooling architectures and close a full energy balance during power, airflow, sensor and network failures. The same public cell could test district cooling kernels, mobile clean-air refuges and high-density emergency computing.

### 7.6 SA6 energy–ballast

Each SA6 lane accepts the complete four-station rack, up to 8 tonnes of water and 8 tonnes of fuel-equivalent event inventory, with 40 m³ of segregated capture. Root A liquid fuel and Root B DME never share a containment boundary. Four independent reactions, metered transfers, orientation control and line-inventory measurement preserve the 0.005-tonne custody question.

The technology objective is a **metrology-grade hazardous-fluid balance cell** capable of measuring local mass replacement on a full-scale moving support system. The same methods apply to emergency fuels, medical gases, cryogens, treatment chemicals and recovered contaminated fluids.

## 8. Containment and safe-state law

### 8.1 Containment is a measured state

Containment does not mean that a building wall exists. For each hazardous event, the record must identify:

- credible source inventory and energy;
- active and passive boundaries;
- vent, drain, fire and fragment paths;
- automatic and manual termination systems;
- the independent measurement proving termination;
- accessible rescue and recovery paths;
- retained contamination or residual energy;
- quarantine capacity; and
- the condition allowing human entry.

The exact credible-release source remains open until the constituted safety authority accepts a root- and event-specific analysis. Planning values size the facility. They do not authorize exposure.

### 8.2 Single-failure posture

Every lane must reach safe state after one of the following:

- primary controller loss;
- one active isolation or unload actuator failure;
- external utility loss;
- primary clock or network loss;
- one witness-room evacuation;
- one evidence-vault loss;
- one lift or egress path loss; or
- one source-water or waste-route quarantine.

The alternate path must be mechanically, electrically or organizationally different. Two identical controllers with the same firmware and power source count as one mechanism.

### 8.3 Safe state cannot award acceptance

Emergency action may terminate any event. It cannot create a passing article observation. A successful safe state can preserve safety and causal evidence while the registered article decision becomes FAILED, INVALID or INDETERMINATE.

| evidence vault authority | separate original custodian |
| destructive laboratory | cross-site disposition outside the host lane |

All seventy-two planning comparisons are distinct. All seventy-two physical proofs remain absent.

### 9.2 What may remain common

Common public elements include:

- SI units and declared coordinate systems;
- mission-derived service requirements;
- event schema and disposition grammar;
- article identity and configuration vocabulary;
- public decision thresholds;
- cross-comparison artifacts;
- the national schedule;
- the right to inspect, reproduce and dissent; and
- the final baseline board.

These elements define comparability. They do not decide a measurement.

### 9.3 What may not remain common

The following shortcuts are prohibited:

- one vendor's acquisition appliance on both lanes;
- one firmware root commanding both safe-state systems;
- one calibration package declaring both lanes correct;
- one cloud service required to seal both originals;
- one laboratory providing all destructive truth;
- one lead crew rotating between the two lanes of one family during the same admission block;
- one crane or waste path required to recover both lanes; or
- one site-specific adapter that completes both roots.

### 9.4 Disagreement is retained

If two lanes or two primary chains disagree, neither is averaged into a result. The family enters a bounded disagreement campaign with retained originals, cross-comparison artifacts and a third method that was not used to generate either initial result. Program schedule pressure has no role in selecting the preferred value.

### 10.2 Ten lane events

Every lane executes the same causal classes through family-specific artifacts:

| Event | Known truth | Required disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| fixture zero and transfer matrix | registered mounted reference | ACCEPT |
| compliant near-boundary surrogate | known compliant state | ACCEPT |
| nonconforming surrogate | known violation beyond guard band | REJECT |
| decision-boundary surrogate | uncertainty overlaps the decision | INDETERMINATE |
| sensor saturation or native gap | decisive observation unavailable | INVALID |
| command–observation disagreement | delivered stimulus differs from command | INVALID |
| primary controller loss | control implementation removed | SAFE STATE |
| one safe-state actuator loss | one termination path unavailable | SAFE STATE |
| clock, signing and network denial | external services removed | SAFE STATE AND REPLAY |
| retained hazard or carryover | residual energy, fluid or contamination remains | QUARANTINE |

These are end-to-end mounted events. Component bench tests may be prerequisites. They cannot substitute for the lane event.

### 10.3 Eight campus denial drills

Each campus demonstrates:

1. external grid loss;
2. failure of one island source;
3. national network and external-time denial;
4. discovery of contaminated process water;
5. loss of the primary control room;
6. loss of one evidence vault;
7. loss of the primary lift or egress path; and
8. closure of the planned waste route.

The drill begins with at least two lanes in active or armed states. A ceremonial facility shutdown before the failure insertion receives no credit.

### 10.4 Cross-site reconstruction

Each family produces two packets:

- one packet generated on Lane 1 is reconstructed at Lane 2's campus; and
- one packet generated on Lane 2 is reconstructed at Lane 1's campus.

The reconstruction uses the sibling site's recorder implementation, clock interpretation and decision engine. It must reach the same frozen disposition from native objects without a supplier portal or an analyst who participated in the initial decision.

### 10.5 Admission law

A lane is commissioned only if:

- all ten truths receive the registered disposition;
- every hazardous event reaches safe state;
- neither original is missing or reconstructed from a later copy;
- no unregistered facility aid completes the article or decision;
- pre- and post-event fixture identification close;
- the lane's utility and containment state returns to READY;
- the sibling site reproduces the required sealed packet; and
- all dissent and invalidity remain in the admitted record.

There is no partial family credit. One commissioned lane and one failed lane leave the family unqualified for full SDEC-1 execution.

## 11. Metrology and reference architecture

### 11.1 Twelve transfer packages

Each lane receives one registered traveling transfer package. Odd-numbered lanes use the ALPHA chain and even-numbered lanes use the BETA chain. The chains use separate primary cells, transfer hardware, analysis implementations, custodians and return schedules.

The packages span the family quantities:

- force, moment, geometry and settlement for SA1 and SA2;
