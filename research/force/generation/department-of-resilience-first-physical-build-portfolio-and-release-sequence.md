## Branch position and resolution test

The deepest live branch is `PFP-1`, the first of at most five physical-portfolio passes.

**Resolution test:** reversing this decision would change which mission systems and industries receive the first USD 200B of physical authority, whether the Department becomes a platform-first queue, and when production can begin; it therefore changes the top-level program.

## Decision

Authorize a **USD 200B National First Article Portfolio across all six mission systems**. Do not choose a first platform, hazard or service. Choose a first experimental economy in which every system must discover its own viable physical forms while remaining coupled to the others by complete-service proof.

The six systems receive unequal dollars because their inherited obligations differ, but each receives the same twenty-percent first-wave share of its mission account:

| Mission system | First-tranche authority |
| --- | ---: |
| Enter a broken theater | USD 17.8B |
| Reach occupied civil terrain | USD 18.4B |
| Control an active hazard | USD 10.9B |
| Produce and distribute lifelines | USD 14.4B |
| Preserve care and human continuity | USD 30.6B |
| Sustain, transfer and regenerate | USD 17.9B |
| **Mission-system total** | **USD 110B** |

No system receives production credit. The first principle is **all six first, physical form second**.

## Why the apparent priorities fail

A human-continuity-first portfolio follows the largest demand but strands care without entry, lifelines and regeneration. A lifeline-and-hazard portfolio produces visible machines while civil reach, receiver legitimacy and sustainment remain missing. A mobility-first portfolio confuses delivery with service. A proving-estate-first portfolio builds truth infrastructure before it has unlike, root-native claims worth testing.

Nor should the USD 110B be divided into six miniature production programs. That would fossilize early guesses and reward the first credible-looking article. The portfolio instead makes incomplete knowledge a governed asset: multiple unlike mechanisms may fail without taking their owning mission system or the national program with them.

## What the first tranche physically makes

The complete USD 200B tranche contains five exclusive accounts: **USD 110B mission systems, USD 30B service technical bases, USD 30B common capacity, USD 20B sovereign technical estate and USD 10B failure reserve**. These are appropriation authorities, not capability, inventory or readiness.

The immediate physical program is:

1. **Twenty-four problem-field competitions.** Four fields in each mission system each retain four causally unlike mechanism opportunities: ninety-six national first articles. They may include new heavy-transfer physics, autonomous damaged-terrain access, distributed fire control, water–energy carriers, clinical islands, cellular shelter metabolism, circular residual recovery and autonomous regeneration. Novel technology is expected; no incumbent platform class defines the answer.
2. **Public denominators and interfaces.** Service semantics, receiver boundaries, metrology, safety cases and custody rules become open public infrastructure before any private design can become authoritative.
3. **Root-native falsification.** Forty decisive non-lifeline endpoints, sixty rig roles and 240 valid-event slots may stage inside the inherited USD 43.0B ceiling. Balance failure orders `RESIZE`; keystone failure orders `KILL`; favorable evidence may order only `BUILD PROOF`.[^1]
4. **Route-complete service frames.** Articles must grow into system-native proof units—a theater corridor, occupied-terrain reach district, hazard-control cell, utility sector, relationship-bearing continuity district or closed regeneration loop. A mechanism cannot impersonate a complete root.[^2]
5. **Cold reconstruction.** Independent teams must rebuild candidate roots outside the design path. A factory lot, demonstration or repeated favorable test cannot become formation credit.

The lifeline account receives USD 14.4B in this tranche. Its full USD 36.0B `G0–G3` campaign is not released. The other USD 52.6B of mission options remains with its six owning systems rather than moving to an apparent winner.

## Release sequence

Physical authority advances through eight article states. `A0` is a public method and interface; `A1` a mechanism article; `A2` an endpoint rig; `A3` a route-complete proof unit; `A4` a reproduced complete root; `A5` a rotating all-six stack; `A6` a production-representative increment; and `A7` a field formation or regenerated serial.

The five inherited tranches now have exact meanings:

| Tranche | Years | Authority | Required exit | Production |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| First articles | 1–5 | USD 200B | qualified mechanisms | none |
| Integrated roots | 6–10 | USD 300B | reproduced complete roots | none |
| Complete missions | 11–15 | USD 250B | one accepted root from every system in an all-six mission | none |
| Production and field use | 16–20 | USD 150B | cold lots and bounded formations | conditional |
| Challenge and renewal | 21–25 | USD 100B | renewed, resized or retired serials | renewal only |

Thus **USD 750B may be obligated before any production tranche exists**. This is not delay; it is the cost of refusing to mass-produce the wrong service architecture. `G5` production cannot open until all six systems supply at least one root to the rotating `G4` mission. Later success cannot erase valid adverse evidence or raid the failure reserve.[^3]

## Industrial consequence

The first market is not a fleet market. It is a national market for ninety-six unlike mechanisms, twenty-four complete Root Houses, distributed proving stations, public production cells, open interfaces and a 13.0-million-person Civil Service labor commons. It creates room for new primes and Lockheed-scale integrators without granting any firm the right to define, test and accept its own mission. The Department becomes a technology driver precisely because it buys rival physical principles, cold reconstruction and complete service—not because it predicts the winning exterior.
