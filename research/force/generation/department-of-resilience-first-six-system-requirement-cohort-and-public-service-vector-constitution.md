## Decision

Open the first physical requirement wave around one **compound first-reach chain**:

> enter a region without a working port; reach people through occupied damaged buildings; hold the fire boundary protecting those routes; create water, power and thermal service; complete definitive clinical episodes; and return every critical article to a known configuration.

This selects one problem field in each mission system without selecting a hull, aircraft, vehicle, reactor, factory, supplier or site. Twenty-four Root Houses each receive one field assignment, so four unlike mechanisms confront every field. The other eighteen fields remain part of every complete root.[^1]

Largest-demand-first fails because six independent rankings do not form an executable service route. Showcase machines fail because “heavy lifter,” “fire aircraft,” “utility ship” and “hospital ship” choose exteriors before service geometry. Infrastructure restoration fails because restored networks can hide missed households and failed clinical episodes. A common autonomy substrate fails because one control stack could become the decisive root across the force.

## Six noncompensatory service vectors

A vector has eight coordinates: useful output, rate, latency, duration, loss survival, receiver burden, public or rights completion, and return. These are gates, not a weighted score. A fast machine that externalizes labor, residuals, risk or configuration truth fails.

## What the program must invent

The fields admit genuinely different physical futures. No-pier transfer may emerge from stabilized offshore exchange, heavy buoyant carriage, machine-laid dry terminals or autonomous amphibious meshes. Building reach may come from prepared civic interfaces, exterior-climbing systems, temporary utility-and-lift spines or occupied-space machine swarms. Persistent fire control keeps atmospheric flux, closed hydraulic intervention, autonomous terrain work and distributed boundary actuation alive.

The lifeline competition deliberately places nuclear-thermal coastal carriers against inland geothermal trains, renewable-electrochemical swarms and synthetic-fuel cogeneration fleets. The clinical field retains federated islands, neighborhood care fabrics, mobile care lattices and rapid host-building conversion. Regeneration pits heavy arsenals against microfactory meshes, remanufacture networks and closed-loop mobile recertification.

These are mechanism hypotheses, not platform shortlists. The public program must also create dynamic load-transfer science, occupied-building robotics, high-duty fire structures, closed intervention metabolism, transportable firm heat sources, robotic pipe and conductor construction, reconfigurable acuity environments, local clinical production, autonomous damage-state reconstruction and portable sovereign metrology. This is the civilian analogue of a mission-driven aerospace and naval research economy: government buys a hard public effect and several rival physical principles, not a familiar vehicle category.

## Financial and evidentiary boundary

One of four fields in each mission system represents USD 138B of the full USD 550B mission-technology account. Its first-tranche ceiling is USD 27.5B. Only USD 14.3B may stage as private Root House options; USD 13.2B remains evidence-held. The USD 80.0B public/enabling accounts and USD 10.0B failure reserve remain portfolio accounts rather than prizes allocated to this cohort.[^7]

The inherited `FRAME / MAKE / BREAK / TRANSFER` ladder applies. If all twenty-four assignments produced valid evidence through `BREAK` and then failed, USD 12.2B would be payable and USD 2.15B would remain unexercised. That is a successful falsification outcome, not a reason to move money to the most photogenic survivor.
