## What the warrant moves

`CEW-1` creates a legal liability and notice to proceed for thirty packages—five for each active form. Those packages cover event method, instrumentation and validity, civil and receiver closure, destructive-article engineering and cold reconstruction. Their state becomes `OBLIGATED / ACTIVE`.

The other sixty packages stay `RESERVED / HELD`. Their money cannot be reprogrammed to an active form, used to cover an overrun or treated as savings. A cohort-one form receives exactly one-third of its root's conserved `FRAME` ceiling; equality applies within each root rather than as equal dollars across unlike roots.

The modeled fiscal boundary is:

Obligation is therefore permission to perform bounded design work, not evidence that the work is correct. Payment requires later artifact acceptance by a separate authority. Accepted artifacts still do not create observations. Only a future evidence authority can accept an observation, and only a separate Selection Court can select a form after the [selection covenant](department-of-resilience-six-root-pre-evidence-form-selection-covenant-and-return.md) is satisfied.

No weighted score can compensate for a failed gate. A missing reserve, shared controller or absent safety route holds that form and therefore prevents issuance of the whole symmetric warrant.

## Implementation as a separation architecture

Six independent implementation cells mobilize—one for each active form. Twelve sibling cells remain reserved. All eighteen cells have unique beneficial-control slots, technical environments and data enclaves; sibling-output access is zero. The portfolio reserve custodian, contracting authority, configuration registrar, implementation cells, package custodian, artifact-validity authority, finance disbursing authority, Evidence Court, Selection Court, safety authority and public ledger remain separate roles.

The state machine makes the nonconversions explicit. `ISSUED / RESERVED`, gate completion, obligation, notice to proceed and active work confer no payment, evidence or selection credit. Artifact submission may lead to validity acceptance, rework or termination. Validity acceptance creates payment eligibility only. Payment creates cash finality only. Evidence remains held for later adjudication and never loops backward into sibling mobilization.

Rollback is local and fiscal. A cell can stop, rework or terminate; unearned money returns to the protected reserve. A cohort-one failure cannot accelerate, redesign or enrich a later sibling. A root-wide safety warrant can impose a common source-blind burden, but cannot reveal the originating form or draw from competitive reserves. If separate safety finance is absent, the root holds.
