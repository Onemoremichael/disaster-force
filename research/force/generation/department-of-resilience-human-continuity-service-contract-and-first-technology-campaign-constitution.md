# Pass 169 — Human Continuity Service Contract and First Technology Campaign Constitution

**Research object:** `HCC-1`  
**Decision level:** `MS-HU` mission-system service contract  
**Verdict:** `CLOSE`  
**Decision date:** 10 September 2026

## Research disposition

The deepest live chain is `NIFS-1 → MSPT-1 → HCC-1`; this is the first pass at `HCC-1` resolution.

**Resolution test:** Changing the contract changes what counts as service for the Department's largest portfolio, the admission logic for 1,944 formation equivalents and the first USD 12.8B technology campaign.

The object therefore passes at first-order force-design resolution.

## Decision

`MS-HU` is a **five-route, seven-stage human-continuity system**. Its unit of service is not a patient, evacuee, shelter place or commodity issue. It is a **person–household–care relationship bundle** whose needs, authority, essential relationships, devices, therapies, destination and supported exit must remain coherent.

| Route | Complete service | Concurrent demand | N−1 inventory | Formation capital | Weighted completeness |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `R1` | Local support continuity | 333 | 703 | USD 543B | 56.4% |
| `R2` | Definitive clinical care | 77 | 179 | USD 383B | 46.8% |
| `R3` | Patient regulation and transfer | 80 | 186 | USD 294B | 48.1% |
| `R4` | Household relocation and reception | 314 | 702 | USD 1.12T | 53.9% |
| `R5` | Workforce replacement | 75 | 174 | USD 139B | 45.0% |
| **Total** | 15 formation families | **879** | **1,944** | **USD 2.48T** | **52.9%** |

Four rivals fail as the whole-system topology. A hospital-centered hub omits households and workforce rotation. A mass-support campus confuses occupancy with continuity. An evacuation network closes at transport rather than accepted service. A commodity-and-entitlement network cannot close clinical, safeguarding or receiving obligations. Each remains a possible subsystem.

The portfolio is not primarily a hospital fleet. `R1 + R4` account for 647 of 879 concurrent formation demands (73.6%), USD 1.67T of formation capital (67.2%) and USD 290B of the USD 414B weighted unclosed-demand signal (70.1%). Clinical care remains indispensable, but the dominant design problem is maintaining or lawfully moving the complete support bundle.

## One completion law

Every route must close seven serial stages:

1. establish person and service state;
2. stabilize the current setting;
3. assign a service and accepted destination;
4. reach and prepare;
5. move the person or project the service;
6. admit, operate and sustain; and
7. transition or return, then regenerate the force.

Safe local support is success; movement is required only when it improves the complete service state. A nominal bed, seat, vacancy, meal or shelter place cannot authorize dispatch. The receiver must accept the bundle it can actually sustain. Served credit issues only after supported discharge, durable transition or return and an explicit regeneration obligation.

This contract maps all 15 inherited `MS-HU` formation families once across Air Response, Health, Community Support and Civil Mobility executors. Five technical interfaces—entry, command, hazard control, lifelines and regeneration—remain external mission-system contracts. None receives quantity credit merely because it is shared.

## Continuity state without a surveillance premise

The minimum actionable state contains twelve correctable, offline-capable fields: protected identity or alternative; location; authority and consent; household and caregiver relationships; accessibility; clinical state; medicines and device power; nutrition and exposure; safeguarding; receiving acceptance; movement and handoff; and discharge or return.

Missing information is unknown, never “no need.” The affected person or guardian retains inspection, correction, refusal and a usable receipt. Clinical, civil, safeguarding, privacy, technical-release and independent-assurance powers remain plural. This is a route-specific continuity record, not a national identity system, social registry or centralized database requirement.

## Technology portfolio and sequence

The inherited USD 153B `MS-HU` technology envelope is conserved across twelve mechanism-neutral competitions over 25 years. The agenda includes distributed definitive care; autonomous diagnosis and laboratory logistics; local oxygen, sterile-products and medical-waste systems; remote clinical presence; supervised robotics; workforce learning; climate-safe occupied environments; adaptive food and hygiene production; accessible temporary buildings; continuity-preserving movement; and receiver-network admission.

These are development exposures, not feasibility findings or procurement authority.

`HU-04A`, continuity state, admission and matching, enters first with USD 12.8B. This does not make software more important than physical service. It makes every later physical campaign observable across all five routes and seven stages—and is forbidden from substituting records for actual care, shelter, food, movement or receiving capacity.

Four complete rival roots enter:

- a person-held continuity credential;
- a household edge continuity kit;
- federated service-node matching; and
- a staffed analog-optical continuity network.

They face twelve denial worlds at three independent sites across five routes: 720 route–world–root–site cells and 5,040 stage decisions. Trials include loss of cloud and backhaul, intermittent power, conflicting identity, caregiver separation, medicine or device misrouting, divided authority, receiver withdrawal, acuity change, accessibility loss, a compromised node and an uninhabitable origin at discharge. No root is selected. No capacity, force reduction, procurement or exterior form is admitted.

All 37 reconciliation audits pass.

## Closure

This pass closes the sole inherited `HCC-1` question: the complete `MS-HU` service contract and its first technology campaign are now constitutional inputs. The next admissible decision is which complete physical human-continuity service system should follow `HU-04A` into representative competition; that question is not opened here.

**Open-item ledger:** opened 0; closed 1; net −1; running total 0.

**Remaining unresolved:** physical performance rates, representative evidence, procurement quantities and exterior forms remain unadmitted.

**Authoritative:** the five-route, seven-stage human-continuity contract, twelve-field continuity state and `HU-04A` first-campaign constitution.  
**Died:** hospital, campus, evacuation and commodity topologies as complete `MS-HU` architectures.
