# Department of Resilience first-tranche industrial transaction and anti-convergence constitution

**Pass:** 207  
**Research object:** `PFP-2` — first-tranche separated option-and-proof market  
**Decision level:** whole-program industrial control and physical award  
**Verdict:** `CLOSE`

## Branch position and resolution test

The deepest live branch is `PFP-2`, the second of at most five physical-portfolio passes.

**Resolution test:** reversing this transaction would change who controls the first USD 200B, whether the Department creates one resilience prime or a contestable industrial commonwealth, and whether failure can terminate a favored technology; it therefore changes the mature institution and force.

## Decision

Constitute a **separated option-and-proof market**. Twenty-four bankruptcy-remote Root Houses each accept liability for one complete system root. They may grow into very large firms through technical depth, production, civil markets and exports, but no beneficial-control group may own, govern, collateralize or perform a decisive chain for a second root.

The Department does not award twenty-four platforms or ninety-six prizes. It awards cancelable options to produce evidence-bearing physical artifacts. Four field assignments compose each root, creating ninety-six traceable opportunities. Twelve decisive execution domains remain exclusive to each root, producing 288 ancestry declarations. Critical feedstock, interface-component and production-service dependencies create seventy-two further records; any provider, region or toolchain may touch at most two roots and every exposure requires an unrelated alternate.[^1]

Mission System Commands own complete-service denominators. Generating Services own technical release. Root Houses own designs but no public warrant. The Resilience Proving Service owns challenge, control, reconstruction and custody. The Root Disposition Board alone orders `BUILD PROOF`, `RESIZE` or `KILL`. The Capital Board opens and cancels options; the Program Comptroller pays accepted work and returns unused balances. A Continuity Receiver holds public step-in rights and may recharter a failed root only to a clean control group.

## Why the rival markets fail

Six lead system integrators preserve complete-root liability but place four roots behind each balance sheet, key chain and integration authority. One failure can remove an entire mission system. Ninety-six independent prize cells maximize mechanism diversity but leave no actor responsible for composing a complete service. Twenty-four perpetual franchises preserve nominal root separation while making adverse evidence and replacement nonbinding. A sovereign public arsenal keeps public rights but creates one national toolchain that builds and judges its own reconstruction.

Only the selected market passes all twelve conditions: public denominator, complete-root liability, twenty-four control groups, exclusive ancestry, the two-root provider cap, independent proof, paid valid failure, public step-in, fiscal conservation, no production inference, clean replacement entry and first-tranche executability.

Budget-weighted control concentration makes the structural difference visible. Independent Root Houses produce a Herfindahl–Hirschman index of **0.0463**, equivalent to **21.6** equal control groups after unequal mission budgets. Six system primes raise the index fourfold to **0.185**, equivalent to **5.41** groups. Ninety-six prize cells appear less concentrated at **0.0116**, but concentration cannot compensate for missing complete-root liability. The index is a diagnostic, not evidence that any real owner is independent.

## The first award is USD 57.4B, not USD 200B

The USD 200B tranche separates into four economic states:

| State | Authority |
| --- | ---: |
| Root House options that may stage after gates | USD 57.4B |
| Mission options held for evidence | USD 52.6B |
| Public service, common and sovereign technical accounts | USD 80.0B |
| Protected failure reserve | USD 10.0B |
| **Total first-tranche authority** | **USD 200B** |

The stageable private amount combines the inherited USD 43.0B non-lifeline endpoint ceiling and USD 14.4B lifeline first-wave authority. It is distributed across all twenty-four roots and all ninety-six root-field assignments. The remaining mission authority does not move to an apparent leader; it remains with its six owning systems. Public accounts fund methods, safety, interfaces, receiver emulators, challenge worlds, matched controls, reconstruction and custody. The failure reserve cannot supplement a winner or cure an overrun.[^2]

Every Root House option has four payment states:

| State | Share | Portfolio ceiling | Earned artifact |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `FRAME` | 15% | USD 8.61B | charter, service baseline, rights schedule and ancestry plan |
| `MAKE` | 35% | USD 20.1B | four measured mechanism articles and controlled bills |
| `BREAK` | 35% | USD 20.1B | endpoint challenge, control and valid evidence packet |
| `TRANSFER` | 15% | USD 8.61B | cold-build package, public custody and disposition record |

Payment requires an accepted artifact, not a favorable result. A valid adverse result is paid and remains binding. An invalid event may receive one exact-event replay from the protected reserve; outcome-aware repetition is prohibited. Killing every root after `BREAK`, for example, would leave USD 48.8B of accepted work paid, USD 8.61B of stageable options unexercised and the full USD 52.6B mission hold intact. Cancellation is therefore a designed fiscal capability rather than an extraordinary program failure.

## Public continuity without public monopoly

Twelve mission-limited foreground rights vest as work is accepted: controlled configuration, funded tooling, a root tooling capsule, reference articles, material genealogy, signing roots and keys, repair patterns, portable credentials, critical-material buffers, receiver interfaces, measurement methods and the evidence archive. Firms retain unrelated background knowledge and commercial use. Public possession creates no readiness; it allows a clean successor to reconstruct the work after insolvency, refusal, concentration or recovery failure.[^3]

Eight transaction states run from an unawarded denominator through control admission, fiscal option, public method, mechanism articles, endpoint rig, root disposition and a possible second-tranche proof option. No state is inferred from spending, schedule, urgency or corporate survival. A mechanism win cannot create root credit; a root disposition cannot create production; and an experimental article demanded during catastrophe remains outside the ready force.

The seventeen-sheet [companion model](../../../models/programmatics/department-of-resilience-first-tranche-separated-option-market.xlsx) instantiates five rival architectures, twenty-four root options, ninety-six field assignments, 288 ancestry cells, seventy-two dependencies, payment and cancellation exposure, ten authorities, twelve public rights, fourteen stress worlds and ten invalid mutations. Forty terminal audits pass after independent office recalculation; the full formula-error scan is empty and the native concentration chart survives. Every firm, owner, provider, tool, site and event remains source-empty. No option is awarded, production credit is zero and rendering remains held.
