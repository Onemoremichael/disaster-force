# Department of Resilience lifeline four-root `G3` physical proof-campaign constitution

**Research object:** `C3-LFG3-2`

## Frontier and resolution

The deepest live branch is the lifeline four-root `G3` branch, now in its second of five allowed passes. **Resolution test:** reversing the minimum proof estate would change the Department's first complete-system builds, public-range functions, use of the USD 36.0B lifeline tranche and eligibility for Cascadia `G4`; the object therefore remains visible at the top level.

Pass 176 froze four unlike configurations against one public service contract, but left “representative subsystem evidence” underspecified. This pass closes that ambiguity. It defines the smallest physical campaign able to distinguish a root failure from a bad test, expose coupled source–corridor behavior, and reproduce a result without the originating team. It authorizes no construction, location, supplier, physical result or force credit.

## Decision: three complete sectors per root

The unit of evidence is a **complete quarter-service sector**, not a source machine. Each sector must deliver 25,000 m³/day of quality-released water, accept 20,000 m³/day of wastewater or residuals, bridge 25 MW of firm electricity, deliver 20 MWth of occupied-space heating or cooling, retain 25,000 m³ of distributed water buffer, and black-start 60 MWh under disconnected control. Its ten mission positions comprise one root-native source, two water-and-residual groups, three 10 MW local-power cells, two 12.5 MW grid bridges and two 10 MWth thermal cells. Public interfaces, measurement boundaries and safe-stop authority are identical across roots; source physics, tooling, controls and decisive ancestry are not.

Each root receives three complete sectors:

- `P1` and `P2` are primary-team articles operated simultaneously. One carries the challenge while the other supplies a time-matched clean control; their roles reverse.
- `R1` is cold-built and commissioned by a second team at a separate sovereign range. It has no inherited traveler, operator, calibration chain, software signing path or repair history from `P1/P2`.

One sector cannot create a paired control. Two can create the pair but cannot separate reproducibility from shared craft knowledge. Four would duplicate the entire Pass 176 formation before the experiment yields a deployment decision. Three is therefore the minimum—not an average or preferred fleet quantity.

Across the four roots the bank contains 160 top-level article units: three sources, six water groups, nine local-power cells, six grid bridges, six thermal cells, three root-native evidence cells, two repair/reset groups, two civil/configuration groups, two closed-loop bases and one protected replacement bank per root. Applying the inherited controlled mass and crew coefficients produces approximately 385,000 dry tonnes and 12,500 deployed personnel; a 3.2 rotation factor implies 39,900 establishment billets. These are campaign-screen consequences, not validated design values.

## Experiment grammar

Every root must complete all eighteen inherited failure worlds. Each world receives one challenged `P` run and one simultaneous clean-control run: thirty-six valid primary events per root. Six decisive worlds—source loss, contaminated feed, grid absence, loss of wide-area control/time, denial of vendor signing, and denial of the primary repair depot—are then repeated on `R1`. The minimum is therefore forty-two valid physical events per root and 168 across the campaign.

Every valid event is replayed from outside-origin records by an authority that did not run or instrument the article. Nine reserve attempts per root may replace only events invalidated by predeclared range, safety, custody or instrumentation rules. They may not erase an adverse valid result. The campaign thus permits at most 204 attempts while keeping 168 as the evidentiary denominator.

A root's minimum evidence estate contains 159 governed packages: forty article build records, forty-two valid-event packets, forty-two outside replays, twelve ancestry declarations, twelve calibration chains, three repair/reset dossiers, one second-team reproduction dossier, one safety-and-rights case, one independent release record and five route/threshold dossiers. Across four roots that is 636 packages. A missing package is a missing observation, not a neutral result.

## Functional proving estate

This pass assigns functions, not sites. Each root needs nine governed functions: a root-native primary physics range; separate second-team reproduction arsenal; common public receiver twin; casualty and degraded-service cell; residual/environment laboratory; root-native measurement cell with sovereign shadow instruments; disconnected control cell; repair/reset depot; and protected replacement bank. The thirty-six function records may share geography only where custody, operating history, utilities and stop authority remain separable. Nuclear work uses licensed facilities and non-nuclear heat surrogates until nuclear causality is essential; geothermal, renewable-electrochemical and fuel roots receive equally native fixtures rather than being forced through the nuclear range.

## Program and clock

The campaign remains inside the inherited USD 36.0B `G0–G3` lifeline reservation—four equal USD 8.99B learning options. It preserves the established shares: 40 percent root mechanisms and prototypes, 20 percent public ranges and receivers, 20 percent root-native tooling and pilot lines, 15 percent independent evidence, and 5 percent protected failure reserve. These are maximum reservations, not prices or earned payments. A seven-year planning ramp of 8/14/18/20/18/14/8 percent gives annual authority of approximately USD 2.88/5.04/6.48/7.20/6.48/5.04/2.88B without changing the total.

The eighty-four-month critical path overlaps six bounded phases: requirements and ancestry (months 0–12); range and tooling commission (6–30); `P1/P2` construction (18–48); second-team `R1` construction (30–60); controlled trials (48–72); and repair, replay, discrepancy disposition and independent release (66–84). Schedule compression cannot waive a complete sector, simultaneous clean control, separate second-team build, outside replay or protected replacement.

## Release consequence

This constitution closes what must be built and observed for `G3`; it does not award `G3`. All 168 event outcomes, 636 evidence packages, thirty-six range functions, forty-eight ancestry identities and fifty-six inherited gates remain source-empty. One root's failure does not subsidize or contaminate another: its unused authority returns to the lifeline mission account, while valid adverse evidence remains attached to that root's frozen configuration. `G4` eligibility begins only after the independent board accepts every conjunctive gate for that root.
