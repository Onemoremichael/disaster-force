# Department of Resilience chartered root-house market, production and export constitution

**Pass:** 186

**Research object:** `MPC-1`

**Decision date:** 11 September 2026

**Verdict:** **CLOSE**

## Frontier and resolution

The deepest live branch is `MPC-1`, the first of five permitted passes after `DIC-1` closed Department-level industrial command.

**Resolution test:** reversing this market constitution would change whether the USD 1.00T program creates twenty-four independent complete-system firms or a few consolidated primes, whether any failed root can be reproduced, and whether exports strengthen or consume national readiness; the object passes at Department scale.

Pass 175 required twenty-four independent Root Houses but did not show how capital, debt, tooling, suppliers and exports could preserve that independence.[^1] Pass 185 separated public authorities but did not constitute the firms they govern.[^2] Ordinary procurement cannot close the gap. The Department is a monopsony buying uncertain technology, idle readiness and catastrophic surge. Left alone, that demand rewards common debt, proprietary interfaces, sole-source tooling and consolidation.

## Decision: a chartered root-house commonwealth

Five architectures face twelve noncompensatory gates. Six integrated civil primes pass four. Twenty-four protected cost-plus contractors pass eight but cannot reproduce or replace incumbents. A sovereign production monopoly passes eight but collapses rival roots into one public toolchain. A merchant mobilization and export market passes two because capacity follows commercial demand. Only a **chartered root-house commonwealth** passes civil authority, complete integration, root independence, public rights, outside reproduction, public fallback, supplier and site loss, capital separation, new entry, readiness reservation, responsible export and automatic recharter.

This is a market with a constitution, not a vendor list. Each of the twenty-four Root Houses receives one complete system root and complete-service liability. It may become a very large enterprise through technical depth, geographic production, prepared receivers, routine civil work and partner markets. It may not own, govern, collateralize or perform decisive work for another root. Scale is permitted vertically and internationally; horizontal root aggregation is not.

Seven adjacent enterprise classes prevent the Root Houses from becoming the whole economy: open-interface suppliers, receiver-system builders, civil capacity reserve operators, public arsenal operators, reproduction challengers, partner production houses, and public software and metrology custodians. Government does not choose one corporate form for all industrial work. It fixes the public seams and the powers no firm may combine.

## A charter reaches ownership and finance

Twelve charter clauses bind beneficial control, board rights, management services, voting capital, collateral, cross-default, decisive production, public rights, readiness claims and exports. One legal name is not independence. The workbook creates ninety-six control-separation records across beneficial control, governance capital, cross-default and decisive chain. It adds seventy-two shared-dependency records for feedstock, interface components and production services. No provider, region or toolchain may support more than two roots, and every critical exposure requires an unrelated qualified alternate.

Capital providers may invest across the wider economy, but a root must remain bankruptcy-remote from every other root. Debt at one Root House cannot seize public tooling or accelerate another root. A merger, control breach or common collateral triggers suspension and continuity receivership rather than a waiver. The successor may acquire the orphaned root; it may not already control another live root.

The Department buys five different economic objects: USD 200B of contest and option capital, USD 200B of proof and qualification, USD 250B of public productive estate, USD 230B of initial formations, and USD 120B of availability and regeneration options. These uses cross-classify the existing five accounts and five waves; they do not add money. Learning is paid even when valid evidence kills a lineage. Production money requires reproducibility. Readiness money requires exclusive reservation. Accepted service and regeneration remain later commodities.

The six mission allocations create twenty-five-year public exposures of about USD 13.7B–38.3B per root. These are option ceilings, not revenue or enterprise valuations. They can seed firms with national systems responsibility without pretending that all twenty-four must have the same sales or that public capital alone establishes a commercial market.

## Public fallback is productive capacity, not an escrow clause

The public receives mission-limited rights to foreground configuration, unique funded tooling, root-specific tooling capsules, reference articles, genealogy, signing roots, repair patterns, portable workforce credentials, critical material buffers and receiver interfaces. Firms retain unrelated background knowledge and commercial use. Public step-in activates only when continuity, safety, rights or concentration fails.

Paper rights are insufficient. Six mission systems each receive four functional public reproduction cells. Each cell may qualify no more than two roots; each root must cold-produce on two cells under different operators and at different sites. The resulting twenty-four-cell lattice creates forty-eight root–cell qualifications. This count follows the inherited two-root dependency cap: fewer cells would either leave a root with one fallback route or make one public toolchain serve too many roots. The cells are functions, not selected locations, and release no construction authority.

Twelve failure rules make market exit a capability transition. Insolvency opens receivership. Refusal opens escrow. Cold-production failure removes the production warrant. A supplier-cap breach disallows the dependency. Valid adverse evidence returns the root to `RESIZE` or `KILL`; it does not erase the evidence. The public estate preserves continuity without making the public operator the permanent winning design.

## Export is a second production system

The export object may be a formation, receiver package, module family, public interface, school, production license or mutual reserve. It is not counted as domestic readiness. Ten gates require conserved domestic replacement capacity, separate serials, recipient civil authority, custody, safety and rights, funded configuration support, recipient repair and evidence rights, trained workforce, independent ancestry, and end-of-life return.

No export market size or revenue is claimed. Partner production can enlarge industrial learning and give recipients sovereign operating capacity, but only after the domestic reserve exists. An export that removes replacement capacity or creates permanent dependence fails even if it is profitable.

## Industrial technology becomes a mission program

The Pass 185 sovereign-technology portfolio now receives a production application rather than a second budget.[^2] Its USD 100B crosswalk covers robotic cold-reconstruction factories, root-specific tooling capsules, denial-tolerant production cells, material and process genealogy, portable metrology, live beneficial-control graphs, demand-to-production compilers, serial reservation exchanges, universal qualification fixtures, receiver and workforce twins, causal factory-to-service telemetry, evidence-driven standards, paid failure, rapid recharter and self-restoring calibration roots.

The companion [workbook](../../../models/force/generation/department-of-resilience-chartered-root-house-market-production-and-export-constitution.xlsx) contains twenty-two sheets, 419 passing terminal audits and twenty-three restored mutation tests. Independent recalculation preserves the native chart, the full audit result, zero formula errors, zero chartered roots, zero fallback-ready roots, blocked export and zero released authority.

**Authoritative:** the chartered root-house commonwealth, one-root control law, eight enterprise classes, five capital instruments, public productive estate, twenty-four-cell fallback lattice, twelve failure transitions and ten export gates.

**Died:** six civil mega-primes, protected cost-plus incumbency, one sovereign production monopoly, post-event merchant mobilization and any claim that legal incorporation, escrow alone or export revenue preserves a root.
