# F36 fixture commissioning and first-run data constitution

## Executive judgment

The first F36 range must prove that it can distinguish a compliant article from a known-bad article before it is permitted to judge a supplier root. A calibrated sensor is not a commissioned test system. Commissioning is the end-to-end demonstration that registered truth can survive the reference hierarchy, physical fixture, clock system, data path, blind custody, decision rule and independent replay without being changed by the builder, the network or the institution that wants the program to succeed.

This constitution therefore treats the range as an article in its own right. It establishes twenty-four physical reference artifacts in eight classes and thirty-two commissioning events: sixteen for the closure fixture and eight for each of two independently resettable full-bore damage cells. The events bracket the pressure, flow, geometry, leakage, axial-load, time, water-quality and configuration decisions created in Pass 102. Every known truth must be classified correctly. An uncertain or incomplete result is `INDETERMINATE`, not a partial pass.

The calibration system has four public tiers: national primary cells, traveling transfer packages, fixture working references and run instruments. The fixture contribution may consume no more than twenty percent of an allowable decision-uncertainty budget. A result is accepted only when the measured value plus its expanded uncertainty remains on the safe side of the frozen limit. It is rejected when the uncertainty-bounded result violates the limit. The interval between those states is deliberately uncreditable.

The data system preserves two locally complete originals, one controlled by the evidence authority and one by an independent witness authority. It operates without wide-area connectivity, hashes every append-only record, preserves three clocks and reconstructs a decision deterministically from a frozen schema. Commands never substitute for observations. Derived features never replace native samples. At the proposed instrument plan, the complete seventy-two-closure and twenty-six-damage campaign requires approximately 271 terabytes after duplicate-original and derivative margins. Retaining original evidence is consequently an ordinary design obligation, not an exotic storage burden.

The first closure event, `FR-C-001`, is a declared nominal process-window article selected from a root-balanced sealed draw only after the closure fixture passes all sixteen commissioning events. The first full-bore event, `FR-D-001`, is the disclosed clean-transection case `D01` selected only after its root has a valid closure prerequisite. Each event proves that its evidence machine can execute one complete cycle. Neither event qualifies a root, establishes production reliability or releases a form.

The phase occupies eighteen months inside the thirty-month protocol and the forty-two-month F36 first-article program. Its USD 0.420B capital and USD 0.086B annual support are nested cross-classifications inside the Pass 102 boundaries of USD 1.480B and USD 0.192B. The money purchases public truth infrastructure: reference artifacts, transfer metrology, redundant time, denial-tolerant data, blank and challenge laboratories, reset capability and independent decision authorities. It is not an additional claim on the parent program.

Twenty-two content and physical gates keep writing, construction, commissioning, article admission and depiction separate. The planning package is complete. Reference manufacture, calibration transfer, fixture commissioning, the first closure event, the first damage event, production and rendering remain held or blocked until their own evidence exists. No exterior rendering is authorized by this pass.

## 1. The decision this constitution owns

Pass 102 specified the experimental questions, article counts, damage worlds, nonpausing restoration clock and evidence objects. It did not answer whether the proposed range could measure those questions without introducing a false pass, false rejection, hidden time pause or unreproducible record.

This constitution owns six decisions:

1. whether the reference hierarchy spans every acceptance boundary;
2. whether each complete fixture correctly classifies known truth rather than merely reading calibrated devices;
3. whether command and measured stimulus remain separable through transients and denial;
4. whether two independent local originals can reproduce the same frozen disposition;
5. whether blank, positive-control and custody systems protect sanitary decisions; and
6. whether the first root event may enter the admitted experimental record.

It does not validate a root, select an exterior architecture, adopt a national engineering standard or approve construction. It makes later testing capable of meaning something.

## 2. Commissioning as measurement-system proof

The commissioned object is not a pressure loop, a water laboratory or a database considered alone. It is the entire causal chain:

```text
REGISTERED TRUTH
      │
      ▼
REFERENCE ARTIFACT ── custody · stability · uncertainty
      │
      ▼
FIXTURE STIMULUS ─── command separated from observation
      │
      ▼
RUN INSTRUMENTS ──── independent clocks · native samples
      │
      ▼
LOCAL ORIGINALS ──── append-only · two authorities · offline complete
      │
      ▼
FROZEN RULE ──────── accept · reject · indeterminate · invalid
      │
      ▼
INDEPENDENT REPLAY ─ same packet · same disposition · retained dissent
```

A component calibration cannot demonstrate a correct decision if the mounted sensor resonates, the fixture bypasses load, the collection system drops a decisive transient, a timestamp is reconstructed from network time, or the analyst silently changes the rule. Each commissioning event therefore begins with a known physical or digital truth and ends only when two independent replays agree with that truth.

### 2.1 The five commissioning propositions

The range must establish all five propositions:

| Proposition | Demonstration | Failure consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| coverage | a reference exists on both sides of every decisive threshold | the affected claim cannot be tested |
| traceability | each run instrument resolves through a documented chain to a public primary cell | affected measurement is uncreditable |
| discrimination | the mounted system correctly classifies near-boundary good, bad and uncertainty states | fixture remains uncommissioned |
| continuity | native observations, clocks and custody survive network and power denial | denial event fails; prior affected runs enter review |
| reproducibility | two authorities replay the sealed packet to the same disposition | event is invalid until divergence is resolved |

The propositions apply again after a decisive fixture change, reference drift, software-baseline change, unexplained replay divergence, containment event or long storage interval.

### 2.2 Scope of public and article-owned equipment

The range may provide safe containment, registered stimulus, independent measurement, public references, quarantine and emergency shutdown. It may not provide root-specific alignment, preparation, curing, inspection, repair logic, process software or operator judgment that the deployed F36 system requires. Those capabilities remain charged to the article.

Commissioning therefore verifies two things at once: that the public range is capable enough to impose and observe the common service world, and that it is not quietly completing the supplier's system.

### 3.1 Reference design rules

Every artifact requires:

- an invariant or predictably evolving truth state;
- a primary truth method different from the run system where possible;
- a transport, storage and conditioning envelope;
- a custody log and tamper-evident identifier;
- an uncertainty budget at the relevant mounted condition;
- a reference interval and drift limit;
- a retirement rule and replacement lineage; and
- at least one deliberately nonconforming sibling near a decisive boundary.

A reference is not a golden specimen whose value is assumed. Its truth is periodically re-established. A damaged or drifting reference does not merely fail itself; it opens a bounded look-back review of every decision for which it supplied traceability.

### 3.2 Novel reference technologies worth driving

The Department should fund reference systems that current commercial markets have little reason to develop:

- self-surveying full-bore reference spools with embedded fiber strain, geometry fiducials and removable independent load witnesses;
- programmable microleak cartridges with traceable, contamination-compatible paths spanning tens of millilitres per day;
- traveling hydraulic transfer skids that reproduce static pressure, surge spectra, vacuum and accumulated volume without depending on the destination range controller;
- sealed synthetic-water challenge capsules with digitally committed composition and rapid field-assay companions;
- clock-denial appliances that introduce registered drift, discontinuity and packet reordering while preserving a private truth log; and
- configuration challenge modules that test signed firmware, recipe and material passports without exposing the tamper state before disposition.

These are not conveniences. They are the resilience equivalent of instrumented targets, calibration ranges and threat-representative test articles.

## 4. Four-tier public calibration chain

The calibration hierarchy is designed as a national public asset rather than a supplier service.

| Tier | Object | Function | Custodian | Mobility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `T0` | national primary cells | realize and compare pressure, flow, force, geometry, time and water reference values | national metrology authority | fixed; redundant sites |
| `T1` | traveling transfer packages | carry traceability and uncertainty to ranges without trusting range controllers | transfer-metrology detachments | deployable |
| `T2` | fixture working references | verify mounted system and detect drift before, during and after campaigns | independent fixture-metrology section | range-resident |
| `T3` | run instruments | observe the actual article, stimulus, environment and clock | evidence authority | installed/run-specific |

No tier may certify itself. `T1` returns periodically to an unlike `T0` cell; `T2` is bracketed by `T1`; `T3` is checked against `T2` in its mounted configuration. A supplier may witness and challenge the chain but may not own the only primary record.

### 4.1 Transfer cadence

Calibration is event-based as well as calendar-based. Transfer is required after:

- initial installation or relocation;
- a changed transducer, load path, fixture adapter or acquisition baseline;
- a containment or over-range event;
- failed pre- or post-run reference checks;
- clock or data-plane discontinuity;
- reference storage beyond its registered interval; or
- a forensic finding that makes earlier fixture behavior uncertain.

Post-run checks close the measurement bracket. A clean pre-run check does not protect a run from drift discovered immediately afterward.

## 5. Uncertainty and guard-band constitution

The range must publish a decision uncertainty budget before testing. The fixture's combined contribution is capped at twenty percent of the allowable decision uncertainty; the remaining budget is reserved for article state, method repeatability, environment, sample recovery and decision implementation.

For a one-sided upper limit `L`, measurement `x` and expanded uncertainty `U`:

- `ACCEPT` only if `x + U ≤ L`;
- `REJECT` if `x − U > L`; and
- otherwise `INDETERMINATE`.

For a one-sided lower limit, the signs reverse. For a bounded target, both edges are guard-banded. Expanded uncertainty is stated with coverage factor and contributors; it is never a decorative percentage added after the disposition.

| Quantity | Prospective mounted-system target | Boundary implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| pressure | ±1% of decision value | command is never substituted for measured article pressure |
| flow/volume | ±0.5% | accumulated flush and receiver continuity retain their own uncertainty |
| geometry | ±0.5 mm plus area-share propagation | point measurements cannot stand in for the minimum full section |
| leak | 0.02 L/h expanded uncertainty at 0.10 L/h | a measured 0.09 L/h is not automatically acceptable |
| axial load | ±2 kN around 200 kN | fixture load bypass is measured independently |
| displacement | ±1 mm | restraint and alignment claims remain separable |
| elapsed time | ±0.1 s over six hours | no reconstructed pause or interval compression |
| relative dynamic time | ≤100 μs | 1 kHz transient channels can be causally aligned |

The values are research targets, not current standards. The commissioning task is to prove a defensible budget at the mounted system, not simply to purchase instruments with better catalog specifications.

## 6. Waveform fidelity and stimulus invalidation

Each Pass 102 phase receives a registered command envelope, measured target, tolerance, rise time, hold or cycle duration, spectral requirement and abort boundary. Both command and observation are retained.

### 6.1 The rule against convenient over-test

An under-test cannot support acceptance. An over-test survival also cannot automatically substitute for the prescribed event because excessive stimulus may activate a different load path, damage mechanism, controller behavior or safety response. Before unblinding, an independent waveform authority may determine that a bounded over-test preserves the intended mechanism. Otherwise the run is invalid for acceptance even if the article survives.

### 6.2 Required waveform controls

The registered waveform packet includes:

1. command-series checksum and controller baseline;
2. independent measured pressure, flow, load and temperature at article boundaries;
3. rise time, overshoot, settling and spectral residuals;
4. fixture-state and adapter measurements;
5. sample-rate and antialias configuration;
6. synchronized event markers from both witness clocks;
7. deviation and abort records; and
8. adjudication before truth is unsealed.

The article is judged against what occurred, not what the controller was told to do.

## 7. Time as an independent measurement

Three clocks serve different authorities:

| Clock | Owner | Function | Failure posture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `A` evidence clock | evidence authority | timestamps native measurements and conserved service intervals | time-dependent claim becomes indeterminate |
| `B` witness clock | independent time authority | detects step, drift and event-order divergence | disagreement opens invalid-run rule `V03` |
| `C` builder clock | article team | controls root process and exposes its internal timing | never supplies sole official time |

Dynamic channels operate at one kilohertz or faster where transient causality requires it. Relative timestamp uncertainty is no greater than 100 microseconds; registered event boundaries resolve within ten milliseconds; accumulated six-hour interval error is no greater than 0.1 seconds. Each clock must hold over through network denial. Network time may discipline a clock before or after an event but may not reconstruct missing original time.

Clock disagreement beyond the registered bound does not become zero because the mechanical result looks plausible. The affected temporal claim is uncreditable.

## 8. Append-only evidence fabric

The evidence system must be able to operate as a temporary sovereign data enclave. A wide-area network, cloud service or supplier portal may improve distribution but cannot be required to complete the run.

### 8.1 Minimum event schema

Every decisive record uses a frozen twenty-eight-field envelope:

| Field group | Required fields |
| --- | --- |
| event identity | `event_id`, `campaign_id`, `root_id`, `article_id`, `run_id`, `fixture_id`, `fixture_baseline`, `artifact_id` |
| observation | `record_class`, `device_id`, `channel_id`, `unit`, `raw_value`, `transformed_value`, `uncertainty`, `quality_flag` |
| time and command | `local_monotonic_time`, `utc_time`, `time_uncertainty`, `command_id` |
| authority and integrity | `operator_id`, `authority_id`, `parent_hash`, `payload_hash`, `signature`, `custody_state`, `schema_version`, `reason_code` |

The registered record classes are `CFG`, `CAL`, `CMD`, `OBS`, `EVT`, `CLK`, `SAM`, `MAT`, `SUP`, `DEC`, `TRU`, `FOR` and `DEV`. New classes require a schema-version transition and replay test; they are not improvised during a run.

### 8.2 Two originals, not one copy and one backup

The evidence authority and independent witness authority each retain a locally complete native original written during the event. A third later replication may protect continuity, but it is not one of the two original authorities. The originals must independently contain:

- configuration and calibration state;
- command and native observations;
- clock ledgers and quality flags;
- samples, materials and custody transitions;
- decision rules and frozen dispositions;
- truth commitments and unblinding; and
- deviations, forensic openings and final admission.

If the two originals cannot replay to the same result, the disagreement is preserved and adjudicated. One is not silently declared canonical.

### 8.3 Data-volume model

The closure fixture produces an estimated 195,080 native samples per second: twelve pressure channels at 1 kHz, eight flow channels at 100 Hz, twenty geometry channels at 100 Hz, twenty strain/load channels at 1 kHz, twelve temperature channels at 10 Hz, eight acoustic channels at 20 kHz, six water channels at 10 Hz and ten energy/time channels at 10 Hz.

At one hundred instrumented hours per closure article, eight bytes per sample, a 2.5 multiplier for envelopes and metadata, and two originals, each article retains approximately 2.809 terabytes. Seventy-two articles retain approximately 202.259 terabytes.

Each damage cell produces 284,000 samples per second. A six-hour run under the same retention assumptions requires approximately 245.376 gigabytes; twenty-six runs require approximately 6.380 terabytes. Adding thirty percent for derived products, registered video, indexes and packet packaging yields a campaign estate of approximately 271.23 terabytes.

These numbers are deliberately transparent planning calculations. Lossless encoding may reduce physical media, while higher-rate video or distributed sensing may increase it. Neither changes the rule that native originals remain retained and replayable.

### 8.4 Technology program enabled by the evidence requirement

The Department should create an open resilience evidence stack with:

- rugged, synchronized edge recorders that keep dual original streams during isolation;
- content-addressed append-only packet stores with field-replaceable media and deterministic manifests;
- open sensor identity, calibration and uncertainty passports;
- real-time causal graphs linking commands, observations, samples and authorities without erasing native records;
- delay-tolerant reconciliation across terrestrial, airborne and orbital links;
- physically distinct signing modules for safety, evidence, metrology, water and receiver authorities; and
- reproducible replay engines that can rebuild a disposition years later on an independent implementation.

This is a general force capability. The same stack can preserve evidence for wildfire drops, mobile hospitals, temporary grids, desalination output, flood barriers and heavy-lift rescue systems.

## 10. Blank, positive-control and recovery constitution

Sanitary evidence has three independent failure modes: a dirty collection system can create a false rejection; poor recovery can create a false acceptance; and carryover can corrupt the next event. Commissioning must expose all three.

The water chain therefore requires:

1. source blank before fixture contact;
2. fixture blank after the configured cleaning process;
3. field blank traveling through custody without fixture exposure;
4. known low and high dissolved challenges;
5. known low and high particle challenges;
6. method blank and laboratory positive control;
7. sample split to an independent method or laboratory;
8. carryover blank after the highest challenge;
9. registered recovery interval for each analyte class; and
10. quarantine whenever a blank, recovery or custody control fails.

A clean assay after failed recovery does not support release. A failed blank does not prove the article contaminated the water. Both conditions block credit until the causal boundary is resolved.

## 11. Randomization and blind constitution

The physical campaign contains ninety-eight credit-bearing events: seventy-two closure articles and twenty-six damage runs. The thirty-two commissioning events are known-truth system proofs and remain sequenced to protect calibration and carryover logic. The credit-bearing campaign is randomized after the registry and constraint set freeze.

### 11.1 Commit–reveal seed

Three separated authorities each generate a private random contribution and publish its cryptographic commitment. After the article registry, blocking constraints and fixture schedule are sealed, all three contributions are revealed and combined into the campaign seed. A missing or mismatched reveal invalidates the draw. The final seed and deterministic draw procedure become public after execution and unblinding.

### 11.2 Blocking constraints

Randomization is stratified to:

- balance roots across fixture, calendar period, operator team and environmental condition;
- prevent more than two consecutive credit-bearing events from the same root;
- distribute blind defects and clean controls across the campaign;
- keep truth inaccessible to builders, operators and initial disposition teams;
- preserve sanitary carryover requirements; and
- prevent facility drift from mapping directly to root or condition.

Constraints are preregistered before the seed is revealed. They cannot be added afterward to improve a preferred schedule.

## 12. Invalid, failed and indeterminate events

The program needs three different negative states:

- `FAILED`: the event validly demonstrated that the article missed a service or acceptance requirement;
- `INDETERMINATE`: the event is valid but uncertainty or conflicting evidence does not support accept or reject; and
- `INVALID`: the event cannot answer its registered question because the test system, custody or procedure violated a prerequisite.

| Code | Invalidating condition | Default consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `V01` | stimulus outside registered tolerance without accepted mechanism equivalence | no acceptance credit |
| `V02` | decisive measurement loss or unregistered gap | affected claims invalid |
| `V03` | clock disagreement beyond bound | time-dependent claims invalid |
| `V04` | fixture configuration or adapter drift | run invalid; bounded look-back review |
| `V05` | blind integrity breach | blind inference invalid; safety evidence may remain |
| `V06` | sample or custody breach | sanitary and affected forensic claims invalid |
| `V07` | reference drift discovered after run | bounded look-back and reclassification review |
| `V08` | unauthorized decisive range assistance | deployability claim invalid |
| `V09` | schema, signature, hash or replay failure | packet cannot be admitted |
| `V10` | contaminated blank or failed positive control | sanitary decision invalid |

A safety stop is not automatically invalid. If the article causes the stop under a valid test, the result may be a service failure. Likewise, missing evidence is not converted into a pass because repetition is expensive.

Modular salvage is allowed only when the protocol preregisters a causally independent subclaim, the invalidating condition cannot affect it, and the evidence board records the limited credit. A salvaged pressure trace never becomes complete mission success.

## 13. Decision packet

Each event produces one packet containing eighteen governed objects:

1. requirement and decision-rule baseline;
2. fixture configuration and mounted survey;
3. article configuration and root ancestry;
4. reference and calibration package;
5. randomization record;
6. safety permit and stop boundaries;
7. blind-manifest commitment;
8. pre-run calibration and blank state;
9. command stream;
10. two raw independent originals;
11. builder process original;
12. three-clock ledger;
13. materials, energy, water and resource ledger;
14. sample and custody packet;
15. frozen operator and acceptance disposition;
16. unblinded truth package;
17. forensic opening and causal report; and
18. deviation record and final evidence-board admission.

The packet advances through `DRAFT`, `SEALED_PRE`, `EXECUTING`, `FROZEN_DECISION`, `UNBLINDED`, `FORENSIC_COMPLETE` and exactly one terminal state: `ADMITTED`, `FAILED` or `INVALID`. A packet cannot be backfilled into a prior state. Corrections are new signed records that preserve the original.

## 15. The first two credit-bearing events

### 15.1 `FR-C-001`: first closure event

`FR-C-001` is a declared, nominal, clean process-window closure. The root is selected through a sealed root-balanced draw after all closure commissioning events pass. The article uses its complete deployable closure kernel; the fixture supplies only public stimulus, containment and independent measurement.

Success requires:

- correct pre-run reference and fixture state;
- complete article-owned preparation, alignment, formation and inspection;
- compliant measured waveform;
- two original evidence streams with agreeing clocks;
- frozen `ACCEPT`, `REJECT` or `INDETERMINATE` disposition before truth release;
- complete pressure, sanitary, configuration and forensic packet; and
- deterministic independent replay.

An admitted `FR-C-001` proves that one declared article traversed the full evidence machine. It does not accept the root's twenty-four-article campaign or support a production-reliability claim.

### 15.2 `FR-D-001`: first full-bore damage event

`FR-D-001` is the disclosed `D01` clean-transverse-transection case. The root is drawn only from roots whose closure prerequisite is admitted. It begins at independently measurable injury and ends, if successful, only at joint potable receiver release inside the nonpausing 360-minute clock.

The event must demonstrate injury truth, detection, safe isolation, receiver continuity, access, removal, preparation, repair closure, proof, flush, assay, configuration closure, receiver release, forensic opening and packet replay. It does not admit the root's full damage campaign.

## 18. Release ladder

## 19. What this pass changes

### Established in planning

- the range is itself a governed article whose end-to-end discrimination must be proven;
- twenty-four reference artifacts and thirty-two mounted-system events span the current decision boundaries;
- a four-tier public calibration estate separates national realization from run observation;
- guard bands protect acceptance from measurement uncertainty;
- three clocks and two independent originals survive network denial;
- native retention for the full campaign is technically ordinary at approximately 271 terabytes;
- blind truth, safety, metrology, acceptance, forensics and evidence admission remain separate powers; and
- the first two events validate an evidence cycle without qualifying a root.

### Falsified

Six shortcuts no longer survive:

- calibrating individual instruments is equivalent to commissioning the range;
- a command waveform proves the article experienced that waveform;
- an over-test can always substitute for the prescribed test;
- a cloud copy can replace two locally complete original authorities;
- a clean assay can support release when blank or recovery controls fail; or
- a successful first article qualifies a technical root.

### Still provisional

Every reference value, uncertainty target, waveform tolerance, sample rate, storage multiplier, staffing implication, schedule and cost is a prospective research requirement. No reference artifact, fixture, clock estate or evidence plane has been built. Constituted engineering, metrology, safety, sanitary, receiver, cyber, configuration and fiscal authorities must approve physical execution.

### Burden created by success

The next bottleneck is no longer the definition of a test. It is the ability to execute the campaign at tempo without allowing fixture reset, metrology, water laboratories, forensic sectioning, blinded custody or evidence replay to become hidden serial constraints. A nominal thirty-month protocol can easily become a five-year queue if range throughput is inferred from fixture count alone.

### Next experiment

The next pass should define the **F36 range-complex throughput and regeneration architecture**. It should size closure bays, damage cells, reference cycles, cleanout windows, water laboratories, forensic lines, data-replay cells, blind shops, crews, spares and reset stocks against the seventy-two-closure and twenty-six-damage campaign. It should model stochastic invalid runs, bounded retest policy, unlike-cell common-cause failure and the minimum public capacity that preserves the thirty-month protocol without sacrificing independence.
