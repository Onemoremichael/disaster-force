# RBR-1 — Root A AM-06 whole-ladder closure, transfer ceiling and candidate-entry constitution

## Decision abstract

`RBR-1` defines what must happen after all ten physical classes constituted in [RBG-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-am06-source-water-ignition-entry-ladder-reset.md) through [RBQ-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-500kg-48m3-casualty-area-service-loss-and-outside-reconstruction-constitution.md) are accepted. Ten favorable class boards would not, by themselves, create one transferable method. The ladder changed its articles, modules, root definitions, water interfaces, geometry, atmosphere, receiver, service assumptions, evidence density, safeing and return law as the state became harder. Closure must prove that these changes form a traceable causal system rather than ten adjacent demonstrations.

The complete ladder contains 120 required exact-cell credits and forty invalid-attempt reserves. It has a 956-week primary and 990-week maximum execution floor. Its children authorize USD 18.88 billion of capital and USD 7.485 billion of mission-led technology. They constitute 3,276 candidate and comparison trials, 1,012 full-scale qualification cycles, up to 160 exact class attempts, 12,240 required-event gates, 48,960 required-event records and 3,216 required-event safeing paths. Their declared evidence boundaries sum to approximately 3.668 petabytes. These totals describe a research institution; they do not create a pooled performance statistic.

`RBR-1` creates `LCR-10`, a Whole-Ladder Closure Registry. It binds every rung, exact cell, reserve, architecture decision, article lineage, root version, service state, account, adverse result, reconstruction and return to one directed ancestry graph. The registry has ninety rung-by-terminal-root cells and 540 transfer obligations. A cell can be `DEFINED`, `TRANSFERRED`, `REOPENED`, `SUPERSEDED`, `ADVERSE`, `INDETERMINATE`, `INVALID` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`. `NOT_APPLICABLE` requires an affirmative reason; it cannot hide missing evidence.

The nine terminal physical roots are `PHO`, `ION`, `TOP`, `MOL`, `CAL`, `AQU`, `COU`, `DIV` and `PUB`. Ignition opportunity, dual ancestry, service independence, safeing, conservation, causal time and outside reconstruction remain cross-root constitutional truths. A terminal root may inherit an earlier observation only through a signed version edge that identifies unchanged meaning, changed hardware, changed calibration, changed estimator, changed uncertainty and the direction of permissible inference.

Document review cannot repair every discontinuity. `RBR-1` creates eighteen `TRC-9` translation carriers, two independent lineages for each of the nine adjacent rung edges. Each carrier executes four physical translations across two receiving campuses and two repetitions. The seventy-two cycles carry both the earlier and later root versions concurrently. They govern 17.64 tonnes of source or coded simulant and 904.32 cubic metres of water. A failed edge blocks transfer above that edge without erasing accepted local class results.

Every required exact-cell packet is reconstructed by three closure authorities that did not own the original class. The 360 decisions use native evidence and the root version graph, not class-board summaries. Thirty sealed contradiction cases—three per rung—then challenge favorable and adverse conclusions through withheld configuration, timing, service, account or calibration conflicts. Majority agreement cannot cure shared ancestry, and a favorable ladder board cannot average away a valid adverse event.

`RBR-1` defines the transfer ceiling. A closed `AM-06` ladder can establish that the public reference system distinguishes registered ignition opportunity, flame, transformation, energy, topology, water field, source-water contact, diversion, receiver state and public action across the bounded source, water, area, morphology and service-loss conditions. It can establish repeatable evidence, safe end, recovery and origin-independent reconstruction. It cannot establish operational suppression effectiveness, casualty reduction, performance on unknown fuels, moving fronts, buildings, terrain, wind, saltwater, contaminated floodwater, continuous feed, multiple simultaneous sites, deployment tempo, maintainability, cost at fleet scale or an exterior vehicle.

Operational candidates enter through `OCE-1`, not by inheriting a class label. Entry requires a registered mission effect, bounded environment, mechanism map to the nine terminal roots, independent public-rescue path, service-island design, logistics and regeneration concept, evidence interface, rights package and falsification plan. A candidate may reuse a qualified reference interface. It may not claim performance from similarity, shared supplier, common software or resemblance to a selected research architecture.

The closure program requires seventy-eight primary weeks and eighty-four maximum weeks, 850 execution positions, USD 1.8 billion capital, USD 220 million per year and USD 750 million in technology. It retains the 6,500-position and USD 1.25-billion-per-year ladder formation, creating 7,350 concurrent positions and USD 1.47 billion concurrent annual support. `RAS-3R8` contains the program without amendment and retains seventy maximum weeks, 250 positions, USD 9.32 billion capital, USD 280 million per year and USD 5.765 billion technology.

An accepted `AM06-LADDER-CLOSED` disposition authorizes only `RBS-1`, an operational candidate mission-envelope, duty-cycle, logistics and first architecture-competition constitution. It does not authorize a candidate article, fleet procurement, production, deployment, export or exterior rendering.

## 1. Why ten accepted classes are not one method

Each rung answers a local question under a local article and root constitution. Later rungs introduce mechanisms that can change the meaning of earlier evidence:

- `ER-02` adds high-volume water after `ER-01` defines ignition opportunity;
- `ER-03` adds blinded dual ancestry after clean-water custody;
- `ER-04` creates four combustion roots and 1,008-hour return;
- `ER-05` introduces candidate water, public water and runoff;
- `ER-06` changes source inventory, geometry, atmosphere and thermal boundary;
- `ER-07` separates water morphology from the interface that carries it;
- `ER-08` adds a fifth dry root for three-dimensional source topology;
- `ER-09` adds independent water-field and contact roots;
- `ER-10` adds diversion, public action, compound service loss and origin-free reconstruction.

A later method may be more capable without being backward-equivalent. A larger sensor field may disturb a smaller source. A high-rate water system may not reproduce a low-rate morphology. A new calibration may shift an old threshold. A service-independent clock may expose ordering that a former common clock could not. Closure must identify those changes explicitly.

## 3. `LCR-10` closure registry

### 3.1 Registered objects

The registry owns:

1. ten class packets and 120 required exact-cell packets;
2. forty reserve tokens and every issued invalid packet;
3. every selected and rejected architecture decision;
4. every article, module, carrier, rig and campus serial;
5. all root versions, calibrations, estimators and uncertainty rules;
6. source, water, product, energy, atmosphere, public-action and waste accounts;
7. service, contact, intervention and exposure ledgers;
8. all safety stops, valid adverse results and indeterminate states;
9. outside replay and reconstruction packets;
10. module, site and reference-estate return decisions; and
11. capital, workforce, technology and calendar authority.

### 3.2 Ninety root cells

Ten rungs cross nine terminal roots to create ninety cells. Every cell records:

- whether the root existed at the rung;
- predecessor and successor version;
- decisive native observation;
- calibration and reference objects;
- estimator and uncertainty;
- physical article and service state;
- outside reconstruction state;
- transfer direction; and
- terminal disposition.

Absence can be legitimate. `DIV` is not a physical root in `ER-01`. The registry must say why it is not applicable and identify the rung at which it becomes necessary. An empty cell is a defect.

### 3.3 540 transfer obligations

Each root cell has six obligations:

1. semantic continuity—what proposition means;
2. physical continuity—what article and interface instantiate it;
3. metrological continuity—what reference and calibration establish it;
4. computational continuity—what estimator and decision rule use it;
5. uncertainty continuity—what errors and dependencies bound it; and
6. authority continuity—who can issue, invalidate, reconstruct and accept it.

All 540 obligations must close or receive a bounded non-transfer disposition.

## 4. Version-edge law

Every directed root edge receives one of five treatments:

| Treatment | Meaning | Permitted use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `IDENTICAL` | proposition, article interface, calibration and decision rule unchanged | inference in both registered directions |
| `FORWARD_ONLY` | later method can decide later state using accepted predecessor | earlier result remains local; no backward claim |
| `BACKWARD_TRANSLATED` | concurrent evidence proves later version can reconstruct earlier state | bounded backward inference |
| `REOPENED` | changed mechanism defeats transfer | new physical bridge required |
| `SUPERSEDED_LOCAL` | later method replaces use above an edge but preserves old local result | no cross-edge performance claim |

No edge may be labeled identical because variable names or software modules match. The physical object, reference, estimator, uncertainty and authority must all remain unchanged.

## 6. Cross-ladder reconstruction

### 6.1 Three closure authorities

Every required exact-cell packet is reconstructed by:

- a campus that did not own the original event;
- an independent root-method team that did not own its class board; and
- a deployable reconstruction detachment using offline methods and its own references.

The resulting 360 decisions rebuild event identity, root versions, time, configuration, accounts, uncertainty, disposition and return. They use native records, not summaries exported by the origin class.

### 6.2 Thirty contradiction cases

Three sealed cases per rung introduce one known conflict while preserving two clean controls. Challenge families are:

1. wrong but plausible configuration ancestry;
2. clock discontinuity or reordered action;
3. hidden shared calibration;
4. service-state mismatch;
5. incomplete terminal account; and
6. favorable summary contradicted by native evidence.

The closure system must identify the conflict, localize affected claims and preserve unaffected truth. A challenge that poisons the whole ladder when only one edge is wrong exposes excessive common cause.

### 6.3 Disagreement law

Reconstruction decisions are `SUPPORTED`, `ADVERSE`, `INDETERMINATE` or `INVALID`. Agreement is accepted only after ancestry review. Disagreement triggers cause classification across custody, version ambiguity, calibration, computation, uncertainty, interpretation and missing observation. No majority vote, confidence average or senior-board waiver creates agreement.

## 7. Adverse, invalid and reserve conservation

The ladder board maintains three separate populations:

- valid evidence, favorable or adverse;
- invalid attempts with independently established cause; and
- unissued reserves.

A valid adverse event remains in its exact cell and cannot activate a reserve. An invalid attempt consumes its token and preserves its complete packet. A reserve can replace only its registered cell, root family, lineage and service condition. One apparently successful later rung cannot retroactively convert an earlier adverse result.

The closure board must publish:

- forty-token opening reserve custody;
- every activation and terminal state;
- all unissued tokens;
- every invalidity cause and independence decision;
- every valid adverse result;
- every repeated proposition and its authority; and
- zero unexplained reserve balance.

## 8. What a closed ladder establishes

Within the registered envelope, `AM06-LADDER-CLOSED` can establish:

1. physical ignition opportunity distinct from command;
2. water amount and distribution distinct from tank depletion;
3. dual ancestry through separation and return;
4. flame state distinct across photon and reaction-zone roots;
5. source transformation distinct from flame and heat;
6. distributed energy destinations and residual state;
7. three-dimensional source topology and redistribution;
8. water morphology, occupancy, kinematics and terminal position;
9. source-water contact, residence, displacement and entrainment;
10. diversion, receiver occupancy, separation and liquid destination;
11. public action and effect distinct from candidate action;
12. safe end under registered service denial;
13. fixed source, water, product, energy and atmosphere accounts;
14. module and site return at registered boundaries; and
15. reconstruction without origin services.

These are reference truths and public test capabilities. They define how an operational claim can be examined.

## 9. What does not transfer

The closed ladder does not establish:

- extinguishment probability or time for an operational fleet;
- casualty reduction or clinical outcome;
- performance on a fuel, chemical or material outside the registered source;
- continuous feed, reignition, buried source or a moving fire front;
- a structure, tunnel, forest, ship, refinery or neighborhood environment;
- wind, precipitation, saltwater, surf, flood sediment or unknown contamination;
- repeated sorties, multi-site command or national deployment tempo;
- autonomous navigation in public space;
- fleet maintenance, corrosion, training or logistics burden;
- acquisition cost, production rate or supplier capacity;
- interoperability with local departments or allied systems;
- legal authority outside the test constitution; or
- any exterior arrangement, hull, airframe, ground vehicle or field formation.

The ceiling prevents a common acquisition error: turning the ability to measure a reference event into a claim that an operational system already works.

## 18. Stop conditions

Closure stops or narrows if:

- a class summary substitutes for native exact-cell evidence;
- a root version changes without a directed edge;
- `NOT_APPLICABLE` hides a missing observation;
- one root uses another root's disposition as its decisive evidence;
- a translation carrier lets one version generate the other's truth;
- a failed edge is waived because both endpoint classes passed;
- reconstruction requires origin code, analyst, reader, key or conclusion;
- disagreement is resolved by majority vote;
- a valid adverse result is removed from the ladder population;
- an invalid event activates a reserve without independent cause;
- reserve ancestry or balance is unknown;
- a pooled success rate is used across unlike trials;
- the transfer ceiling includes an untested operational environment;
- a candidate claims qualification through resemblance;
- candidate form is selected before mission and logistics requirements; or
- parent capacity is absent.

## 19. Research stocktake after Pass 154

### What became authoritative

- ten class boards do not create a transferable method without whole-ladder closure;
- the complete child program contains 120 required credits, up to 160 attempts and a 956/990-week floor;
- USD 18.88B capital and USD 7.485B technology belong to the ladder before closure;
- nine terminal physical roots and seven cross-root truths need explicit version ancestry;
- ninety root cells and 540 transfer obligations make that ancestry testable;
- eighteen carriers and seventy-two physical cycles close the nine adjacent edges;
- 360 external reconstructions and thirty contradiction cases challenge class summaries;
- the transfer ceiling is a bounded reference-truth capability, not operational suppression performance; and
- operational candidates require a new mission, logistics, evidence and industrial entry dossier.

### What was falsified or narrowed

- later instrumentation is not automatically backward-compatible;
- a successful endpoint does not validate every path taken to reach it;
- pooled favorable percentages across unlike rungs are scientifically meaningless;
- an accepted research architecture is not an operational design baseline;
- outside reconstruction of the final rung alone does not close older root versions; and
- remaining `RAS-3R8` concurrency cannot support a full candidate competition without a preservation decision.

### What remains provisional

- every physical class result and accepted architecture;
- every version-edge treatment;
- translation-carrier design and observed performance;
- reconstruction agreement and contradiction detection;
- the exact transfer ceiling after evidence exists;
- preservation cost for the ten reference banks;
- operational mission environments and duty cycles;
- candidate families, forms, fleet quantities and industrial sources; and
- every rendering.

## 20. Next decisive pass

`RBS-1` should define the first operational candidate mission envelope before any exterior design. It should select one bounded disaster-control mission, specify effect, geography, arrival time, service loss, casualty interface, exposure, repeated-use tempo, logistics, regeneration, public authority and evidence, and then constitute rival architecture programs against that exact envelope.

Only after those requirements exist should the project produce internal layouts and, later, exterior renderings. The reference ladder now defines how to know whether a candidate works; it does not yet define what vehicle should be built.
