## Decision

Adopt a compound Experiment Definition Record. Each of the twenty-four technology orders receives one immutable, root-neutral **kernel**; each of its six nonfungible articles receives one **serial annex**. The kernel holds the shared proposition, adverse rule, receiver grammar and amendment law. The annex binds one builder-root slot, one `INT`, `BRK` or `RCN` role, one generation, one configuration, one instrument chain and one custody graph.[^1]

This is not a common machine or master model. It creates a comparable question while keeping the two builder roots and three serial roles physically and legally distinct. The architecture produces twenty-four kernels, 144 annexes, 216 kernel-gate records and 1,296 annex-gate records. Those 1,512 unique records become eighteen effective gates for each serial, or 2,592 serial-level tests. Closure is conjunctive; no score or favorable account compensates for an open gate.

## Nine seals before eligibility

Every kernel and annex must close the same nine seals at its own level:

1. **Proposition:** fix the root-neutral claim, decisive endpoint, world and adverse condition before result visibility.
2. **Title:** reserve a unique order–root–role–generation identity; disclose beneficial control and public custody transitions.
3. **Configuration:** hash the bill, process, software, fixture and interface state, including deliberate absences.
4. **Measurement:** predeclare variables, units, estimator, uncertainty and missingness; bind primary and physically unlike witness chains.
5. **Receiver:** let the public receiver fix the service denominator, matched counterfactual, completeness rule and rights receipt.
6. **Intervention:** name every planned, protective, facility, exogenous and unresolved causal class before execution.
7. **Abort and repair:** preserve unconditional stop authority while preventing repair from cleansing ancestry.
8. **Disclosure:** publish the twelve-object reconstruction packet and append-only amendment log.
9. **Conflict:** clear eighteen prohibited common-control edges across builder, receiver, fixture, metrology, originals, safety, validity, acceptance, payment and reconstruction.

`MAKE-ELIGIBLE` exists only after all eighteen effective gates close and no terminating defect remains. It authorizes consideration of a separate `MAKE` warrant. It is not an award, obligation, notice to proceed, material title or built article.

## Serial title and change law

The serial name is permanent. `INT` accumulates development and repair history and can never become `BRK` or `RCN`. `BRK` freezes before the fatal opportunity; decisive exposure consumes its role identity even if the hardware survives. `RCN` starts from virgin material under a clean team denied origin-builder keys, staff and live coaching.[^2]

Five change classes separate harmless display correction from causal corruption. A technical correction before material title cancels and reissues the affected record. A post-title deviation creates a new serial generation and rebalances its root–role comparison. A post-freeze action may restart only when the method proves that state and digest remained unchanged. After decisive exposure or outcome visibility, the original question, endpoint and role cannot change; the result stays attached to the serial.

Missing sources, hashes, identities or a curable conflict cause `HOLD`. Role retitling, silent overwriting, builder-defined denominators, candidate control of both measurement originals, origin assistance to `RCN`, outcome-conditioned redaction or incurable root common control terminates the serial or root pair.

## Measurement, intervention and public reconstruction

The thirty-six inherited coefficient packets each receive primary and witness chain roles, creating seventy-two source-empty chain positions. Independence must span sensing principle, power, clock, calibration and acquisition root. One agreeing chain cannot establish measurement. Every serial also owns a receiver-denominator record; a machine-bound output cannot replace complete service, rights, safety and residual custody.[^3]

Public protection acts without candidate permission. A correct protective intervention may establish safeing while remaining adverse to candidate performance. A facility-caused interruption may invalidate the opportunity, but it cannot erase candidate behavior already observed. Unresolved causality holds the comparison rather than averaging ambiguity into success.

The twelve-object disclosure packet publishes the kernel, serial and title ledger, configuration manifest, receiver denominator, intervention map, measurement and calibration declarations, decision grammar, rights receipts, conflict graph, raw-data custody schema and amendment/disposition log. Public disclosure supports reconstruction; it does not make the claim true. `AVA-TC` remains controlling for artifact validity and replay.[^4]
