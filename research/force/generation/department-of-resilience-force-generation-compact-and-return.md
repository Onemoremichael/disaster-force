## Decision: a five-circuit compact

A single Force Generation Command, seven sovereign services, an industrial mobilization state and an operational-command economy all fail. Each gains apparent speed by allowing one institution to define need, build the answer, judge the evidence or allocate its own output.

The Department shall instead operate through a **constitutional five-circuit compact**:

1. **Public purpose and capital.** The Civil Resilience Authority defines public effects, while the Capital Board opens, stages and cancels fiscal options.
2. **Technology and productive commonwealth.** Six Mission System Commands compete complete service roots; twenty-four Root Houses and the Industrial Mobilization Administration reproduce them through private and public paths.
3. **Formation generation.** Seven services, Strategic Sustainment and the Resilience Proving Service bind articles, crews, stocks, mobility, receivers and evidence into warranted serials.
4. **Readiness and allocation.** The Joint Capacity Authority maintains family floors; NMAC issues collision-free schedules subject to civil priority, rights review and sovereign custody.
5. **Employment, transfer and regeneration.** Operational commands deliver accepted effects; civil or host receivers accept transfer; sustainment and the Productive Commonwealth rebuild the exact force.

The compact is not a committee and not another super-command. Each circuit produces a bounded artifact needed by the next, but cannot issue its successor’s warrant. Fifteen institution classes retain exclusive powers.

Every transition has an issuing authority, positive artifact, rollback and prohibited inference.

Twelve firebreaks govern the entire system:

- spending does not create capability;

- a factory is not a formation;

These are the central intellectual product of the branch. They prevent the Department from recreating the symbolic aid inventory the project was designed to escape.

## One system, separate economies

The compact preserves four economic views:

- the finite USD 1.00T technology, proof, productive-estate and availability program;
- a USD 17.4T mature capital screen, including USD 9.41T of formation capital;
- a USD 2.11T mature annual operating screen; and
- separate domestic and partner markets for useful output.

They illuminate different burdens and cannot be added as though all were appropriations. The model’s transparent twenty-five-year path ramps operations with completed inventory. It produces USD 40.2T of operating exposure over the build and mature years, alongside the USD 17.4T capital screen. Fifteen sustaining years contain USD 1.13T in domestic sales and a USD 0.180T reference partner opportunity. Market revenue earns no readiness credit and partner demand earns no domestic recall right.

The enabling technology is constitutional infrastructure at industrial scale: executable authority twins, cross-circuit provenance, sovereign offline scheduling, public causal evidence banks, adaptive family-floor governors, cold-regeneration orchestration, receiver federations, rights-preserving population state, partner-sovereign replication kits, industrial-demobilization compilers, adversarial institutional ranges and machine-readable public compacts.
