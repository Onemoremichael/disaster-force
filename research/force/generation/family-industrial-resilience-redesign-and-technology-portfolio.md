# Family Industrial Resilience Redesign and Technology Portfolio

## Executive judgment

The Department of Resilience cannot purchase industrial resilience by adding suppliers to a list. It must redesign each capability family so that a civil-service lot can still be made, accepted, moved, admitted, operated and replaced after the infrastructure on which ordinary industry depends has itself become part of the disaster.

The [national industrial-mobilization exercise constitution](national-industrial-mobilization-exercise-constitution.md) established the measurement problem. Its M05 planning world—ten concurrent campaigns with simultaneous grid and transport-region loss—left twenty-two of the Department's forty-eight capability families below their required mission-service rate. The result was useful but incomplete. One common weakest-stage factor could identify families that failed; it could not identify the physical dependency that failed, the industrial form that should replace it, the technology that must be invented, or the public infrastructure required to keep that replacement alive.

This pass turns those twenty-two gaps into family-specific redesign programs. Each program receives:

- a primary industrial architecture aimed at its dominant dependency;
- a secondary architecture with a different physical root;
- a complete-chain cellular replication layer that closes the remaining weakest stage rather than hiding it in a portfolio average;
- one first article that must travel from a sealed cold state through commissioned public effect and regeneration;
- a share of USD 1.08 trillion in family redesign capital;
- access to twenty-four mission-led technology campaigns and a 128-site public industrial-resilience network; and
- an explicit evidence and stop structure that prevents planning capacity from becoming readiness credit.

The planning compiler brings all twenty-two gap families to at least 1.10 times the M05 requirement. Joined to the twenty-six continuing families, the full forty-eight-family design closes M05 on paper. It closes none of M06, the follow-on catastrophe with two lost industrial domains. That failure is preserved. M06 is not a budget target to be normalized away; it is the next architecture frontier.

The capital program is USD 1.8 trillion over twenty-five years: USD 1.08 trillion for family redesigns, USD 420 billion for technology, facilities and standards, and USD 300 billion in program reserve. The amount is analytically cross-classified inside the existing USD 5.25 trillion Acquisition and Industrial Mobilization Command allocation. It is not added to the established USD 15.179 trillion force-capital boundary. Mature annual operations are USD 138.8 billion inside AIMC's USD 310 billion operating design, including the USD 62 billion national exercise program established in the prior pass.

These values are strategic allocations, not estimates. No site has been selected, no design has been priced, no production line has been activated and no physical article has been admitted. All forty-eight families retain zero physical readiness credit. Exterior rendering remains blocked.

The important conclusion is institutional rather than numeric:

> **Industrial resilience is a designed property of a complete civil-service chain, not a count of factories.**

## 1. What the mobilization screen could not decide

Pass 98 represented each family through a twelve-stage chain and applied world-specific availability factors. This was the right first national screen because it made four evasions impossible: production could not stand in for service, one family could not offset another, downstream declarations could not recreate a lot lost upstream, and a successful first wave could not erase regeneration failure.

But the screen compressed different causes into the same arithmetic loss. A 0.70 stage factor might mean grid dependence, unique tooling, a common material precursor, missing metrology, a single port, an exhausted crew pool, receiver incompatibility or the absence of replacement stock. The numerical result could therefore answer “which family fails?” but not “what national asset should exist?”

That distinction matters because superficially similar redundancy can preserve the same failure. Two factories supplied by one process-gas complex are one root. Three shipyards dependent on one specialized foundry are one root. A domestic and allied supplier using one proprietary controller and one calibration service are one root. Ten vehicles that require one inaccessible receiving crane are one root. Additional nominal nodes can make a network larger while leaving it no more resilient.

The redesign compiler therefore treats each M05 gap as a dependency hypothesis. It asks seven questions before adding capacity:

1. Which physical stage governs the family under M05?
2. What hidden utilities, process assets, tooling, material ancestry, labor, route, receiver or regeneration dependencies sit behind that stage?
3. Which architecture removes the dominant dependency?
4. Which unlike architecture prevents the replacement from becoming a new single root?
5. What residual weakest-stage capacity remains after both interventions?
6. What cellular replication layer closes that residual weakness across the full chain?
7. What complete article could falsify the entire claim at once?

The answer is not “buy 10 percent more of everything.” Capacity is added where dependency analysis says it changes a conserved mission-service lot. A family can receive a large industrial redesign while retaining the same fleet count; another can require new fleet mass because movement and receiver burdens are the dependency. The governing unit remains the civil effect.

## 2. The redesign object: a conserved industrial service lot

The mission-service lot introduced in Pass 98 remains the common unit, but its denominator is tightened. For M05, required industrial capacity covers both first-wave service demand and the protected follow-on reserve. The 1.10 design floor is then applied above that combined requirement.

For family `f`:

`M05 required supply(f) = service demand(f) + protected reserve(f)`

`M05 design capacity(f,s) >= 1.10 × M05 required supply(f)`

The M05 reserve floor is 20 percent of base-family demand. The 1.10 ratio is therefore not a claim that ten percent excess production can preserve a twenty-percent reserve. It is ten percent headroom above the combined service-and-reserve obligation.

The reserve is tested after first-wave issue:

`post-service stock(f) = ready stock(f) - issued service lots(f)`

`reserve adequacy(f) = post-service stock(f) / protected reserve floor(f)`

This ordering prevents a warehouse inventory at the beginning of an exercise from being counted as follow-on readiness after the same stock has been issued. It also requires separate custody. A reserve physically co-located with the first-wave fleet, controlled by the same software authority or dependent on the same refrigeration, transport or process domain is not protected merely because an accounting system labels it “reserve.”

The model's M05 reserve ratios are deliberately greater than one because the 1.10 headroom is applied above the combined requirement. That margin is a design consequence, not physical evidence. Serial inventory, environmental condition, release authority, transportability and replenishment still have to be observed.

M06 raises service demand to 1.25 base-family demand, requires 25 percent protected reserve and removes two industrial domains. Every family remains below its required service-capacity ratio in the current design. The model does not automatically increase the replication layer to make M06 pass.

## 3. Eight industrial architectures

The twenty-two redesigns draw from eight reusable architecture families. They are not platform classes or procurement lots. They are ways of changing the topology of production and service.

| Architecture | Physical proposition | Dependency removed | Decisive first test |
|---|---|---|---|
| distributed process lattice | many qualified process cells with unlike utilities and local control | one regional loss cannot stop conforming output | restart a representative process in a second region |
| islanded utility and process twin | process plant wrapped in closed-loop power, heat, cooling, water, control and safe-state systems | regional utilities cannot stop or corrupt the line | black-start at full load and execute a controlled safe stop |
| receiver-native completion mesh | process-ready subassemblies completed, adapted and commissioned in prepared receiving arsenals | long-haul mass and unique receiver burden are removed before the damaged corridor | an independent receiver builds and admits one lot |
| regional multimodal fleet arsenal | distributed custody, self-loading interfaces, alternate routes and field repair | loss of one base, route or carrier class does not end service | blind dispatch after a route domain is removed |
| hot cellular service reserve | rotating crews, warm assets, protected consumables and independent service cells | labor absence or readiness decay cannot consume the whole family | no-notice activation of a second cell |
| circular material and regeneration network | qualified alternative feedstocks, recovery, remanufacture and protected replacement stock | first use does not destroy the second mission | recover a contaminated first lot and produce its replacement |
| transferable tooling and metrology kernel | portable fixtures, recipes, instruments, calibration and acceptance logic | a process can move without configuration drift | reproduce an accepted lot in a cold alternate facility |
| sovereign and allied dual-root license | two unlike process roots with public execution data and lawful cross-border transfer | ownership or domestic-domain loss leaves an executable path | an allied second team produces an accepted interchangeable lot |

No architecture is sufficient alone. A distributed lattice can reproduce a common material or software dependency. An islanded utility twin can make a single unique process more robust without making it replaceable. A receiver-completion mesh can distribute assembly while inheriting one upstream subassembly. A multimodal fleet can reroute cargo that no second factory can build. A hot reserve can preserve a service cell while depleting irreplaceable consumables.

For that reason each gap family receives two unlike architectures, not two instances of one pattern. Their stage additions are explicit in the workbook. The compiler then calculates the minimum residual stage ratio and adds one visible, separately costed cellular layer across the family. This layer is not a generic fudge factor. It is the capacity of a complete second chain—people, utility, process, acceptance, custody, movement, receipt, service and regeneration—needed to close the weakest remaining stage.

The sensitivity result demonstrates its role. Removing the governing replication cell from the F36 rapid water trunkline design reopens the M05 family gap and causes the audit to fail. The model therefore cannot pass F36 merely because upstream architecture additions look large.

## 4. The twenty-two family redesigns

The family briefs are organized by governing physical burden rather than by existing bureau. This is more useful than preserving today's acquisition categories because the same technology can change several public effects while each effect retains its own admission criterion.

### Process, utility and consumable families

Rapid nutrition production, cold-chain preservation, essential-goods fabrication, pharmaceutical and biologic microfactories, mobile nuclear energy, long-duration storage, fuel synthesis, floating nuclear desalination, inland brackish-water treatment and wastewater restoration all fail if their process environment is treated as background infrastructure.

Their redesigns make the process environment part of the system. The cold-chain lattice includes islanded refrigeration, thermal rejection, rotating cold stores and mobile repair cells. The pharmaceutical family combines transferable sterile production with portable independent batch release. Mobile nuclear energy and floating nuclear desalination require factory utility islands, high-hazard mobile safe-state control and two unlike component roots. Fuel and inland water trains require variable-feed chemistry, recovered catalysts or media, closed residual handling and a regenerative consumables path.

This is a deeper change than placing generators beside factories. A self-powered line can still fail through cooling water, process gas, feed preparation, sterile boundary, software timing, waste acceptance or operator certification. The islanded process twin must therefore be certified as one thermodynamic and control system. Its decisive trial is a full-load black start followed by declared utility and control losses and a bounded safe stop. Mission urgency cannot waive that stop.

### Fabrication and receiver-completion families

Shelter manufacturing, grid restoration, rapid bridge and causeway, port–rail–airfield restoration, rapid water trunkline and modular housing all carry too much finished geometry through damaged corridors if they are designed as factory-complete products.

Their common move is receiver-native completion. National industry produces compact, highly controlled elements: structural kits, conductor and pole packages, joints, valves, process cores, launch tooling, fixtures, test instruments and digital configuration. Prepared regional arsenals hold civil adapters, bulk local materials, robotics and trained commissioning cadres. The affected region completes the final geometry under a public acceptance standard.

The strategy does not assume that local improvisation is automatically resilient. Local material must be declared and qualified. Robotic assembly must preserve structural and process configuration. Receiving authorities retain consent and may refuse a burden that would export instability into the damaged system. A bridge is not admitted because components arrived; it is admitted when foundations, crossing geometry, load behavior, access and removal state meet the civil-service contract.

### Strategic movement families

Heavy airlift carriers, littoral response carriers, riverine access trains and autonomous ground-logistics convoys fail at the intersection of production, basing, route diversity and receiver burden. Their redesign therefore cannot be a simple increase in hulls, aircraft or vehicles.

The regional multimodal fleet arsenal distributes custody across unlike bases, preserves self-loading transfer among road, rail, air and water, maintains field repair and remanufacturable mission modules, and exercises routes only after one domain is removed. The heavy-air family must demonstrate an alternate-base sortie after simultaneous route and supplier loss. The littoral family must sortie without a functioning pier. The riverine family must transfer between basins from distributed road–rail launch sites. The autonomous convoy must continue after one control cell and one energy path are removed.

The aim is not universal equipment. It is universal transfer at governed interfaces. A mission-service lot can change carrier without losing identity, hazard state, configuration or deadline. Self-loading interfaces matter because a nominally multimodal fleet that depends on a unique crane, ramp, software broker or specialist crew remains one transport root.

### Regeneration and municipal-service families

Debris separation and material recovery, municipal service restoration and several process families are governed by what happens after first use. The debris fleet must convert a contaminated heterogeneous stream into registered feedstock without transferring hazard into the next product. Municipal service blocks need cross-credentialed crews, receiver-specific repair kits and second-city activation capacity. Both require hot cells that rotate through use rather than remaining cold emergency stock.

This family group makes regeneration an operational effect. The Department does not receive full credit for removing debris if the action strands unusable material and exhausts the fleet. It does not receive full credit for restoring one city's utilities if the same technicians, instruments and spares were pledged to a second region. The first article must expose that contention.

## 5. Technology as force structure

The redesign cannot be built entirely from present commercial catalogs. A coequal resilience establishment would use demand, long-duration contracts, public test facilities and repeated failure insertion to create technologies that markets have little reason to develop on their own.

The twenty-four campaigns in the executable model are organized around six missions.

**Industrial autonomy** combines certified black-start utility packs, closed-loop thermal rejection, distributed deterministic process control and high-hazard mobile safe-state control. The goal is not generic microgrids. It is a process plant that can preserve product quality, environmental containment and safe shutdown while external power, cooling water, timing and network services disappear.

**Transferable production truth** combines sterile process suites, reconfigurable joining kernels, public metrology pods, self-describing tooling and public technical-data execution. A process is transferable only when a second team can reconstruct its physical and software state without discretionary help from the original provider. The public artifact includes fixtures, calibration, uncertainty, operating windows, material ancestry and executable control behavior—not merely drawings.

**Material plurality and circularity** combines alternative-feedstock qualification, material passports, modular remanufacture and protected long-lead reserve architecture. The aim is bounded substitution rather than uncontrolled improvisation. A recovered or alternative material enters service only inside a declared family, with known hazard and property state and an acceptance method capable of detecting drift.

**Custody-preserving movement** combines mission-lot decomposition, self-loading multimodal interfaces and damage-aware routing. Optimization alone is insufficient. An executable route has carriers, transfer equipment, lawful operators, energy, hazard permissions, receiving slots and time. The lot must remain the same governed object while modes change.

**Receiver sovereignty** combines universal civil-utility adapters, portable public-effect commissioning metrology and remote or robotic commissioning. “Universal” means that a bounded set of adapters absorbs regional variation without exporting electrical, hydraulic, thermal, structural or cyber instability to the receiver. The receiver retains an independent measurement path and lawful refusal.

**Human and allied continuity** combines credential substitution, allied licensed process transfer and a two-domain-loss complete-chain demonstrator. Credentials become operational only when the replacement person has demonstrated the task and can act lawfully in the receiving jurisdiction. An allied source becomes a genuine root only when it can execute the process, accept the lot and transfer it lawfully under the loss world.

Each campaign carries an entry technology-readiness hypothesis, an explicit invention, a decisive trial and a stop condition. None receives readiness credit from expenditure, prototype appearance or schedule. The challenge is allowed to be ambitious because its failure is bounded and informative.

## 6. A 128-site public industrial-resilience network

The family programs need physical institutions that private firms cannot be expected to hold idle, duplicate across unlike regions or expose continuously to destructive testing. Pass 99 therefore adds a mission-dedicated 128-site network to the 136-node public technical commons defined in the AIMC design. The two populations are complementary; the new count does not silently replace or merge with the earlier one.

The network contains forty-eight islandable production-utility nodes, sixteen portable process-foundry campuses, twenty-four receiver-completion arsenals, twelve mobile metrology depots, eight multimodal loadout and custody yards, eight workforce-continuity centers, six material-substitution laboratories and six circular remanufacture centers.

The geographic objective is not equal distribution. It is unlike exposure. Sites are selected and governed so that one grid market, watershed, heat regime, seismic zone, port complex, cloud authority, ownership chain or specialized workforce pool cannot remove all roots of a family. A map with many dots can still represent one domain; independence must be demonstrated through ancestry and failure insertion.

Public ownership is not required for every machine or employee. Public custody is required for the parts of the system that make competition and recovery possible: complete process state, metrology, challenge articles, hazardous trial infrastructure, transferable tooling, interface standards, reserve identity and evidence. Private and allied operators can build and run large portions of the network under readiness options, but no incumbent may make its continued cooperation a precondition for national reproduction.

The USD 220 billion facility allocation is intentionally schematic. It provides scale and internal tradeoffs but lacks site, environmental, labor, utility, construction, security and lifecycle estimates. Facility programs remain open until representative processes have been identified and costed.

## 7. Capital and operating structure

The USD 1.8 trillion Pass 99 capital program is a cross-classification of the existing AIMC enterprise design:

| Use | 25-year capital |
|---|---:|
| family-specific industrial redesign | USD 1.080T |
| twenty-four technology campaigns | USD 0.150T |
| 128-site public facility program | USD 0.220T |
| standards and execution data | USD 0.050T |
| program reserve | USD 0.300T |
| **Total** | **USD 1.800T** |

The compiler allocates family capital by relative program complexity: the family's prior cost-scale class, the primary and secondary architecture factors, and the residual complete-chain layer. This is suitable for portfolio balance, not procurement. It makes the opportunity cost visible—heavy air, littoral and riverine redesigns claim large shares because movement, basing and field repair are industrial systems—but it does not estimate airframes, ships, buildings or production curves.

To prevent double counting, every Pass 99 use is also mapped to one of the nine AIMC workstreams. The cross-classification consumes USD 330 billion of sovereign design and test, USD 420 billion of pilot production and foundries, USD 280 billion of science and challenge programs, USD 270 billion of rival prototyping and replication, USD 100 billion of industrial mobilization and material reserves, USD 80 billion of standards and configuration, USD 20 billion of workforce and schools, and USD 300 billion of enterprise reserve. USD 3.45 trillion of the USD 5.25 trillion AIMC allocation remains assigned to its other responsibilities.

The annual operating design is USD 138.8 billion: USD 64.8 billion for family readiness and reserve rotation, USD 6 billion for facility readiness and calibration, USD 3 billion for standards and configuration, USD 3 billion for allied transfer and exercises, and USD 62 billion for the national mobilization exercise. USD 171.2 billion remains inside mature AIMC operations for the rest of the acquisition, research, data, workforce and industrial system.

These allocations are deliberately large because latent industrial capacity decays. Lines must run; alternate materials must be qualified; process recipes and software must be reconstructed; reserves must rotate; people must exercise; receiving arsenals must commission; and failed components must be remanufactured. A resilience industrial base funded only during disasters is a paper base.

## 8. What the planning result means—and does not mean

All twenty-two redesigned families reach at least 1.10 in the M05 stage compiler. The twenty-six continuing families are held at the same planning floor, producing a 48-of-48 M05 result. Every M05 reserve ledger also remains above its post-issue protected floor because the service-and-reserve obligation sits inside the denominator.

That result means the proposed architectures and residual replication layers are arithmetically sufficient under the declared stage-uplift hypotheses. It is valuable for program formation and capital allocation. It does not mean that a real process has the assumed yield, a black-start plant can reject the heat, a second crew exists, an alternate material qualifies, a route can execute, a receiver will consent, a public effect will persist or a consumed lot can be replaced.

The workbook protects this boundary in three ways.

First, capacity additions are isolated as explicit assumptions. They can be replaced by measured stage results without changing the force logic. Second, evidence and admission fields remain independent zeroes; no favorable planning ratio can alter them. Third, 2,123 deterministic controls test identities and boundaries but describe themselves as model controls, not physical findings.

The sensitivity tests confirm the structure. Raising the M05 floor from 1.10 to 1.20 causes the residual layer to increase and preserves twenty-two gap-family and forty-eight full-force planning closures. Removing F36's governing residual layer makes that family fail and produces an audit failure. Changing a generic evidence input cannot create a physical credit or admitted family. Reducing M06 demand changes the frontier and can produce closure; restoring the defined M06 world returns the full force to zero closures. The results are therefore responsive to causal inputs rather than typed conclusions.

## 9. F36 as the first complete-chain article

The rapid water trunkline system is the preferred first family exercise. This is not because water is the only decisive service. It is because F36 crosses an unusually wide share of the institution's unresolved problems without requiring the program to invent a glamorous exterior vehicle first.

The proposed first article is a ten-kilometre potable-water trunkline deployed from sealed cold inventory through a damaged corridor. It includes pipe and joint kits, route data, autonomous survey and preparation systems, transferable joining and test tooling, pumping and pressure control, temporary storage, potable release metrology, repair and recovery stock, receiver crews and public-health authority.

The exercise should begin only after a reference route and service contract are frozen. The route should contain at least four physically different segments: damaged urban pavement, soft or saturated soil, a water or debris crossing, and a receiver interface with an incompatible local standard. The white cell removes one transport path and one tooling or material root after the activation order. The family must then:

1. authenticate the order and reconstruct the exact configuration from public custody;
2. muster two non-duplicated credentialed teams;
3. black-start its joining, testing, pumping and control systems;
4. release sealed pipe, joint, adapter and repair lots;
5. produce or qualify one replacement component through an alternate process;
6. prepare the route and install ten kilometres without unique receiver heavy equipment;
7. preserve custody and configuration across at least two movement modes;
8. obtain receiver consent and connect through the bounded adapter set;
9. pressurize, flush, disinfect and independently measure potable service;
10. maintain the declared flow and quality interval;
11. repair an inserted defect without losing public-health control; and
12. recover, replenish and reseal the protected replacement lot.

The article fails if any stage loses the conserved service lot, if public-health release relies on builder self-certification, if the receiver supplies an undeclared decisive asset, if remote connectivity becomes necessary for safe operation, if an alternative component lacks accepted material state, or if regeneration cannot restore the family before the follow-on clock.

The next pass should specify the F36 range constitution: hydraulic service requirement, route geometry classes, pipe and joint families, installation rate, energy and water balance, disinfection and residual standard, crew and robotics task graph, receiver burdens, inserted failures, measurement uncertainty, environmental restoration, and protected reserve article. That work will create the first legitimate internal process-and-logistics section for a future rendering. It will still not authorize an exterior silhouette.

## 10. Organization and industrial roles

The redesign does not fit within a conventional emergency-management procurement office. It requires a standing relationship among the family program offices, technical integration offices, challenge agency, public facilities authority, industrial mobilization authority, logistics authority, receiving jurisdictions and independent evidence institutions.

The family program office owns the conserved service lot and the complete-chain baseline. It may choose technical means but cannot redefine the civil effect or certify success. The technical integration office owns cross-family interfaces and prevents several programs from claiming the same scarce utility, material, transport or workforce capacity. The challenge agency funds unlike roots and inserts failures. AIMC's facilities authority maintains public process and evidence infrastructure. The logistics authority preserves lot identity through movement and reserve custody. Receiving jurisdictions own consent and local admission. Independent evidence authorities own measurement, uncertainty and findings. The safety and environmental authority can stop hazardous activity without awarding performance credit.

Industrial firms occupy several roles rather than one prime-contractor hierarchy. A system integrator may compose a family increment. Process-root firms create decisive chemistry, fabrication, energy or control. Regional arsenal operators complete and adapt lots. Fleet operators move them. Public laboratories own common metrology and substitution standards. Challenge firms retain standing rights to attack an incumbent baseline. Allied firms operate lawful second roots. Remanufacture operators close regeneration.

The economic implication is a new class of resilience industrial company comparable in continuity and technical depth to major defense firms but judged against public-service performance. Some firms would build complete systems; others would specialize in mobile process utilities, transferable metrology, receiver completion, self-loading interfaces, autonomous civil works, circular materials, public technical data or force-scale exercises. Export value would follow from interoperable civil-service systems, licensed process roots, training and receiving infrastructure—not from surplus or retired military equipment.

The Department should resist creating one vertically integrated “resilience prime” with private custody of every interface. The point of the investment is not merely industrial scale. It is sovereign capacity to inspect, compete, reproduce, move, receive, repair and replace the force across generations.

## 11. Research limitations and evidence status

This pass is a normative systems design built from the repository's prior force architecture and models. It does not use an external cost, supplier, site, hazard-frequency or technology-performance dataset. That is appropriate for defining the first-principles institution but insufficient for estimation or acquisition.

The principal limitations are:

- stage uplifts are structured hypotheses rather than observations;
- the eight architectures may conceal common process, ownership, software, labor or geographic ancestry;
- capital weights are allocation devices without work-breakdown estimates, schedules or learning curves;
- facility counts have no siting, construction, environmental or utility study;
- workforce and credential burdens are described but not staffed at family-task level;
- M05 and M06 are design adversaries, not probabilistic forecasts;
- protected inventory has no serial age, condition, rotation or loss history;
- no receiver has negotiated or exercised the proposed burden;
- no first article has crossed the twelve stages; and
- no representative geometry has been earned.

These are not disclaimers to be appended after a confident answer. They define the next research program. The Department should commission dependency discovery, representative process trials, public-data reconstruction, receiver burden experiments and complete-chain exercises before refining unit counts or vehicle forms.

## 12. Pass stocktake and research frontier

This pass makes five advances authoritative at the planning level.

First, the twenty-two M05 gaps are no longer one residual category. Each has a named dominant and secondary dependency, two unlike redesign architectures and a first complete article. Second, the repository now has an industrial architecture grammar reusable across families without treating families as interchangeable. Third, protected reserve is explicitly calculated after first-wave issue and attached to separate custody. Fourth, technology and facilities are composed as force structure: twenty-four campaigns and 128 public nodes sit inside a reconciled capital and operating boundary. Fifth, the planning model closes M05 while deliberately preserving the zero-closure M06 frontier and zero physical admission.

It also supersedes one inference from Pass 98. The earlier 48/26/0 result remains valid as a scalar planning screen, but the twenty-two M05 failures should no longer be described as one weakest-stage class. They are family-specific dependency programs. Their common arithmetic symptom does not imply a common remedy.

The next admissible work is the F36 rapid water trunkline range and article constitution. It should determine the service requirement and internal physical section before selecting a carrier or producing exterior art. In parallel, the Department needs a dependency-discovery protocol capable of proving that nominally unlike roots do not share utilities, process assets, tooling, software, labor, ownership or receiving infrastructure.

The governing frontier is now sharper:

> **Can one family-specific civil-service lot be reconstructed from public custody, produced through an unlike root, moved through a damaged corridor, lawfully commissioned by a receiver and regenerated before the next demand—without any decisive dependency being supplied by the exercise itself?**

Until that question is answered physically, the industrial force remains a serious design and an unproved force.
