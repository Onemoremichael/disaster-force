## One package, one leased validity warrant

The validity unit is one funded package, never a whole form. Each of the thirty cohort-one packages must satisfy twelve noncompensatory gates, deliver its eighteen-field source capsule, complete deterministic native replay and a toolchain-disjoint clean reconstruction, reconcile six root-native semantic invariants, and use an available root estate carrying live non-displacement warrants. Independence must remain intact and comparative performance must remain sealed.

Only then may the relevant Root Artifact Court issue an Artifact Validity Warrant (`AVW`). It means that a bounded design artifact is identifiable, reconstructable, internally accountable and held under lawful custody. It does not mean that the design is physically correct, effective, superior, safe in untested conditions or selected.

Validity is leased to a configuration, ancestry, civic obligation and available estate. The compact specifies eighteen expiry routes. A changed configuration hash, causal denominator, decisive dependency, material assumption, root epoch, rights obligation, court quorum, toolchain, key set, truth schedule or claim ceiling revokes the affected authority and names the re-entry test. There is no permanent certificate.

The compact enumerates thirty-two nonconversions and thirty-six authority-separation rules. Its twenty-seven-instrument precedence stack ends at payment instruction. ## Reversibility and termination

Eighteen rollback routes remove only the authority created by an invalid act. Native or clean reconstruction failure returns one package; a lost site degrades its root; truth-capacity displacement revokes the local non-displacement warrant; an unsupported finance act freezes or recovers the claim. Source, divergence and adverse records remain preserved, and cohort-one failure cannot consume later-sibling money.

Sixteen nonwaivable termination routes govern fatal conflicts. Builder control of clean reconstruction, shared decisive tools, captured court or custody control, intentional performance leakage, falsified lineage, vendor operating dependency, truth-reservation displacement, false civil closure, unlawful payment, artifact-to-evidence conversion, premature selection, `MAKE`, `REP-4`, or encumbrance of the protected reserve terminates the specified authority. Central rescue, waiver and ratification after the fact are prohibited; only a clean successor under the same separation law may re-enter.

Forty integrated stress worlds test lawful partial progress, incomplete reconstruction, estate loss, common control, premature finance, prohibited conversion, reserve invasion and compound failure. All forty produce the prescribed `CONTROLLED`, `ROLLBACK` or `TERMINATED` result. These are constitutional tests, not empirical trials.
