## Decision: paired, asymmetric falsification precedes proof construction

Four architectures were tested: simulation-only screening, one aggregate score, independent endpoint campaigns and paired asymmetric trials. The Department selects the fourth. Each root receives two distinct endpoints:

- a **balance endpoint** measures the limiting account in its public-service denominator. A valid negative result sends the current `CF1` to `RESIZE`; it does not kill the underlying mechanism;
- a **keystone endpoint** tests whether the root's claimed causal mechanism survives its decisive denial. A valid negative result `KILL`s the lineage;
- a positive result on either endpoint supports only proof-article construction. It cannot release `G3`, earn force credit or satisfy Cascadia.

The asymmetry is constitutional. Scale error and mechanism failure are different findings. An average score would let surplus in an easy account hide failure in the root's reason for existing; a single pass/fail label would destroy useful mechanisms whose present configuration is merely too small.

Four valid events belong to each rig role. Every event requires five separately accepted packages: configuration, metrology, custody, safety-and-rights, and independent replay. Two additional attempts per root exist only for predeclared integrity failures; they cannot replace a valid adverse result or fund outcome-aware retry.

The endpoint method must be accepted before observation. It reports a normalized estimate and method-defined expanded uncertainty against a threshold of 1.00. If the lower bound is at least 1.00, the endpoint `SUPPORTS`. If the upper bound is below 1.00, a balance endpoint orders `RESIZE` and a keystone endpoint orders `KILL`. An interval crossing 1.00 is `INCONCLUSIVE` and leaves the root on `HOLD`. Missing events, packages, outside reproduction or stage authority also produce `HOLD`. An ancestry collision, terminal breach or attempt beyond the cap kills the lineage.

Only two supported endpoints, twelve valid events, sixty accepted packages, four outside reproductions and all three stages can produce `BUILD PROOF`. That phrase authorizes proof construction only.
