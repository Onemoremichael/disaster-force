# F36 `CISA-40` root build package and tolerance-stack constitution

## Executive judgment

Pass 111 established where the first `CISA-40` article must carry, touch, connect, measure and separate its functions. It did not establish whether either industrial root could make that geometry from named sections, joints, conductors, pipes, seals, machines and processes without silently spending mass, energy or tolerance that the program did not possess.

This pass creates `BPT-1`, the root Build-Package and Tolerance constitution. It is the first manufacturing-level hypothesis for two genuinely independent `CISA-40` roots. It defines preliminary sections, load paths, utility hardware, energy states, tolerance chains, material allowables, joining controls, coupon populations, nondestructive examination, destructive witnesses and release logic. It remains a research design, not a construction drawing or qualified article.

The pass falsifies two attractive claims in the predecessor. First, a “controlled design reserve” is not a physical component and cannot be entered at an arbitrary center of gravity. Each root therefore targets `30.900 t` of predicted manufactured dry mass and carries `1.100 t` as a controlled uncertainty allowance to the `32.000 t` dry boundary. Root A’s worst-case classified uncertainty is `0.800 t`; Root B’s is `0.860 t`. The corresponding root-sum-square values are `0.282 t` and `0.293 t`. The allowance remains unavailable for new scope until actual weighing and mass-property evidence retire uncertainty.

Second, the previous `1.400 t` mission-fuel entry cannot sustain the declared `1.50 MW` output for eight hours. It represented a convenient common inventory, not an energy closure. `BPT-1` replaces that fiction with three controlled 40-tonne states. The structural-proof state uses `5.100 t` of inert and trim ballast. Root A’s energy state carries `3.200 t` of liquid fuel and `3.300 t` of ballast; at a declared `11.83 MWh/t`, `38%` conversion efficiency and `0.45 MWh` deliverable storage it offers `14.835 MWh`, or `9.890 h` at full output. Root B’s state carries `4.400 t` of dimethyl ether and `2.100 t` of ballast; at `7.92 MWh/t`, `42%` and the same deliverable storage it offers `15.086 MWh`, or `10.057 h`. These are energy-accounting hypotheses; they are not demonstrated endurance.

The root structures are deliberately unlike. `ROOT_A` uses welded 690-MPa-class quenched-and-tempered steel: paired `250 × 150 × 10 mm` lower rectangular chords, `200 × 120 × 8 mm` upper chords, circular diagonals, four reaction frames and `300 × 250 × 16 mm` outrigger boxes. `ROOT_B` uses a continuous-fiber thermoplastic shell-grid: paired `320 × 180 × 10 mm` lower boxes, `260 × 140 × 8 mm` upper boxes, consolidated diaphragms, co-molded metallic nodes, post-tensioned ties and `360 × 220 × 12 mm` outrigger boxes. A common conservative beam screen gives `1.162 mm` and `1.533 mm` predicted midspan deflection under the 40-tonne, `1.50 g` reference case. It is a load-path screen, not finite-element qualification.

The two roots also diverge below their skins. Root A uses 350-mm-bore super-duplex metallic pipe, a bolted split collar, a segmental annular valve, laminated copper bus, variable-speed rotating generation, LFP storage and mechanically folded microchannel rejection panels. Root B uses 350-mm-bore continuous thermoplastic composite pipe, a conical bonded-clamp collar, a composite rolling-diaphragm valve, laminated aluminum bus, recuperated DME conversion, structural zinc-halogen storage and a different folding-panel mechanism. Common performance boundaries remain public; decisive manufacturing ancestry does not.

Thirty-two tolerance chains—sixteen per root—now connect fabrication variation to support contact, anchoring, lifting, hydraulic closure, valve deadleg, bus separation, cable routing, evidence independence, human access and thermal deployment. All pass analytically at nominal planning values. That does not authorize build. Ninety-six coupons, sixty-four NDE lots, twenty-four destructive witnesses and root-specific process records must convert declared allowables into observed evidence.

`BPT-1` is a fourteen-month, 164-person nested program. It draws `USD 0.212B` capital and `USD 0.061B/year` from the still-unspent Pass 111 allocation, leaving `USD 0.106B` capital and `USD 0.023B/year` inside that envelope. Sixty-six release gates distinguish planning closure from source evidence, article acceptance, `CRC-E01`, production and rendering. Only planning gates may close here. Exterior rendering remains prohibited.

## 1. Decision and epistemic status

`BPT-1` answers a narrower question than a vehicle design review: can two industrially independent builders receive the same public `CISA-40` service boundary and produce internally coherent build packages that expose their physical unknowns before expensive article manufacture?

Four evidence classes govern every numerical statement:

| Class | Meaning | Credit in this pass |
| --- | --- | --- |
| inherited requirement | controlled boundary from `CISA-1` or `BRD-1` | authoritative until reopened |
| design hypothesis | root-specific geometry, material, process or architecture proposed for challenge | planning only |
| calculated consequence | transparent arithmetic derived from requirements and hypotheses | planning only |
| physical observation | measured coupon, fixture, article, trial or teardown result | zero unless source object exists |

The distinction is constitutional. A large margin computed from a declared material allowable is not a measured strength. A tolerance sum is not an as-built survey. A fuel lower heating value and efficiency are not an eight-hour mission. A signed drawing cannot upgrade a hypothesis into an observation.

## 3. Correction of the mass constitution

### 3.1 Why a reserve is not mass

Pass 111 listed a `1.100 t` Root A design reserve and `0.300 t` Root B reserve as if they occupied specific coordinates. That makes the tabulated center of gravity look exact while concealing where future growth occurs. It also gives Root B only `0.300 t` of growth despite the same 32-tonne boundary.

`BPT-1` instead distinguishes:

- **predicted manufactured dry mass**: named physical systems with assigned locations;
- **classified uncertainty**: the bounded error around those predictions;
- **controlled manufactured-mass allowance**: the difference between the 30.900-tonne design target and 32.000-tonne public boundary; and
- **configuration inventory**: fuel, fluids, consumables and ballast installed for a named state.

The `1.100 t` allowance has no center of gravity because it is not yet hardware. A change that spends it must name a system, location, uncertainty class and displaced risk.

### 4.3 Energy–ballast technology program

The state ledger creates an unusual but productive design problem: mission fuel is consumed while the article’s proof mass and reaction envelope should remain controlled. `BPT-1` therefore establishes modular energy–ballast cells with four distinct states: live fuel, recovered process water, inert proof medium and empty-isolated. The cell must expose mass, content identity, restraint, leak state and location without relying on the root controller. It must not permit recovered water to enter the potable path.

This is a public technology program, not merely tank procurement. A successful cell would let future resilience platforms exchange energy inventory for water, absorb useful recovered mass, keep handling loads predictable and make consumable state machine-readable.

## 7. Support, outrigger and lift actuation

Root A’s support cylinders use a preliminary `110 mm` bore at `25 MPa`. The ideal bore for `150 kN` is `87.4 mm`; the larger hypothesis creates area for friction, pressure derating and control authority. Each support still requires a mechanical hold that does not depend on trapped hydraulic pressure.

Root B distributes the same public vertical reaction through electromechanical lifting crawlers and local screw-lock feet. Its key research burden is synchronization through a flexible shell-grid: local stroke equality is not equivalent to article-pose control.

Both roots must provide:

- positive stowed, deploying, deployed, locked, unloading and fault states;
- local force and stroke measurement independent of command position;
- manual energy isolation and controlled lowering after total power loss;
- physical capture against hose, cable or software failure;
- visible pin or lock engagement; and
- replaceable ground-contact faces with independent pressure maps.

The Root A 80-mm and Root B 75-mm lift pins see nominal double-shear stresses of `19.9 MPa` and `22.6 MPa` at `200 kN`. Those numbers do not qualify bearing, bending, clevis spreading, composite transfer or fatigue.

## 8. Hydraulic service build packages

### 8.1 Root A metallic service path

Root A uses 350-mm internal diameter super-duplex pipe with 10-mm wall and 370-mm outside diameter. Twenty-four attributable metres weigh approximately `2.131 t` before flanges, valves, supports and insulation. At the `1.05 MPa` proof pressure, nominal hoop stress is `18.375 MPa` against a declared `310 MPa` allowable.

Its collar is a split mechanical clamp with sixteen M20 property-class 10.9 bolts, `110 kN ±10%` preload per bolt, keyed axial transfer and two separately observable EPDM barriers. A purpose-built segmental annular slide preserves the 350-mm bore and limits declared deadleg to `0.33 m`.

### 8.2 Root B composite service path

Root B uses 350-mm internal diameter continuous thermoplastic composite pipe with 8-mm equivalent wall and 366-mm outside diameter. Twenty-four metres of pipe wall weigh approximately `0.346 t` at `1,600 kg/m³`; end fittings, liners, restraint and impact protection dominate the complete hydraulic allocation. Nominal proof hoop stress is `22.969 MPa` against a declared `180 MPa` allowable.

Its collar uses conical mechanical load transfer around a separately controlled bonded sleeve. A `120 mm` bond length gives `0.138 m²` nominal area and `1.812 MPa` mean shear at `250 kN` axial load, against an `8 MPa` declared design allowable. Dual barriers remain replaceable and inspectable. The proposed composite iris or rolling-diaphragm valve preserves the 350-mm bore with no more than `0.31 m` declared deadleg.

### 8.3 Sanitary separation

Neither root may claim sanitary acceptance from pressure tightness. Each path must preserve upstream blank custody, cleaning chemistry, flush volume, coupon identity, seal lot, valve cavity state, sample order, waste destination, downstream blank custody and post-teardown examination. Composite pipe must add liner continuity and permeation evidence; metallic pipe must add weld heat, passivation and crevice records.

### 10.3 Novel capabilities that become necessary

The build packages create six national technology programs:

1. **mass-state-aware energy cells** that exchange fuel, water and inert ballast while preserving measured reactions;
2. **self-sensing public load nodes** with force, strain, identity and geometry embedded at manufacture;
3. **structural electrochemical storage** whose load contribution, fire state and repair state remain independently observable;
4. **full-bore sanitary collars and valves** with fast remote closure, minimal deadleg and destructively inspectable barriers;
5. **source-aware, evidence-native protection** that safely handles rotating and converter-limited faults without network authority; and
6. **deployable high-flux thermal surfaces** that survive contamination, wind, icing, impact, reset and transport restraint.

Military-scale innovation matters here because the burdens interact. A lighter pipe changes CG; a different fuel changes ballast; added thermal surface changes deployed envelope; storage integration changes fire structure; automation changes evidence authority. A component demonstration without coupled configuration is inadequate.

Coupons must share material lots, machines, operators, process windows and environmental histories with the intended article. Perfect laboratory coupons from a different production lineage cannot qualify a difficult article joint.

### 14.2 Nondestructive examination

Sixty-four controlled NDE lots—thirty-two per root—cover material receipt, first article, repeated production, post-proof and teardown. Root A uses phased-array UT at full-penetration nodes, magnetic-particle examination at accessible steel surfaces, bolt-preload verification and dimensional survey. Root B uses phased-array or air-coupled UT, shearography, active thermography, tap/acoustic comparison only as a screening aid, and three-dimensional insert survey.

NDE performance must be demonstrated with known defects. A clean scan without probability-of-detection evidence or preserved raw data does not establish absence.

### 14.3 Destructive witnesses

Twenty-four destructive witnesses—twelve per root—are located beside decisive article processes. They include chord/node transitions, support nodes, lift nodes, anchor roots, collar bond or weld regions, pipe saddle transitions and bus contacts. Witness removal occurs after relevant proof and environmental exposure. The cut plan is frozen before results are known; failed indications trigger adjacent expansion rather than selective deletion.

## 15. Measurement and as-built constitution

Each root must deliver at least sixty-four key as-built characteristics spanning:

- chord section, straightness and station position;

Measurements must retain instrument identity, calibration, environment, method, operator, uncertainty, raw result and any transformation into the public datum. A rounded conformance entry without the underlying observation is not an as-built record.

## 16. Process-control and manufacturing travelers

The build package contains root-native travelers for material receipt, cutting or placement, forming, joining, machining, coating or environmental protection, pipe cleaning, electrical assembly, storage integration, software loading, calibration, proof, preservation and teardown.

Every traveler must answer:

1. what controlled object entered the step;
2. which people, machines, programs and consumables acted on it;
3. which actual process values were observed;
4. which acceptance boundary applied before the next irreversible step;
5. what nonconformance occurred and who had disposition authority;
6. where the physical item and native evidence moved next; and
7. which later tests can still reveal a hidden defect.

Root A travelers emphasize heat and weld ancestry, distortion, machining after weld, coating and bolt preload. Root B travelers emphasize tape and insert ancestry, thermal history, consolidation pressure, surface age, contamination, bondline, void state, preload and damage maps. A common PDF form may exist, but the evidence objects and causal processes must remain root specific.

## 17. Design for repair, reset and industrial independence

The two roots must survive more than first assembly. Each proposes replaceable support faces, anchor pins, seal cartridges, valve actuation modules, protection units, evidence recorders and thermal-panel hinges. Root A should permit local welded repair with controlled heat and subsequent NDE. Root B should permit scarf or thermoplastic patch repair with local reconsolidation and an inspectable damage boundary.

Industrial independence is lost if both roots require the same unique:

- material precursor or protected supplier line;
- tape, weld, consolidation or machining center;
- collar, seal or valve factory;
- control firmware signing service;
- protection relay or sensor ASIC;
- NDE algorithm or cloud license;
- calibration laboratory;
- energy-storage chemical feedstock; or
- proprietary file translator.

The public authority may standardize interfaces, evidence schemas and known-truth fixtures. It may not collapse decisive production ancestry for administrative convenience.

## 18. Safety and coupled-failure analysis

`BPT-1` treats the article as one coupled hazard system. At minimum it must expose:

- support or anchor deployment during occupied access;
- lifted mass with degraded synchronization or one lost node;
- high-flow water release into energized equipment;
- collar separation with stored hydraulic energy;
- rotating-machine overspeed, fuel release or DME vapor accumulation;
- LFP propagation or zinc-halogen electrolyte breach;
- hot exhaust or thermal panels near composite, seals or evidence cable;
- evidence-power fault mistaken for service-bus state;
- ballast identity error that shifts reactions;
- cleaning chemical incompatibility with pipe, liner, seal or waste container;
- remote command accepted after local custody changed; and
- repair that restores appearance but conceals a primary load or sanitary defect.

Safe state is not necessarily zero energy. Freeze protection, evidence retention, controlled ventilation, sanitary containment and communications may need energy after mission output stops. The article must declare which functions survive, for how long, from which independent source and under whose authority.

## 22. Failure modes that the package must expose

The build package fails if:

- a mass allowance appears as hardware at an invented coordinate;
- an energy state reaches 40 tonnes only by silently deleting required service inventory;
- fuel burn changes reactions without a declared mass-state law;
- a large simplified member margin hides an unanalysed joint or instability;
- a composite root books theoretical shell mass as finished-system mass;
- a pipe, bus, thermal panel or equipment skid becomes an undeclared structural member;
- a support or anchor is positioned by final forced assembly rather than datum control;
- worst-case tolerance fails while RSS is used to claim conformance;
- an adjustment is not measured and locked;
- sanitary acceptance is reduced to pressure or final sample alone;
- both roots share a decisive collar, valve, protection, NDE or process ancestor;
- a process command is substituted for observed heat, pressure, position or chemistry;
- a clean NDE image lacks known-defect capability;
- witness location is chosen after the article result is known;
- a repair deletes the original defect record;
- the evidence system depends on the service system it is meant to judge;
- the fourteen-month schedule moves evidence after irreversible assembly;
- nested research cost is added again to the Department total; or
- internal geometry is converted into exterior art before physical closure.
