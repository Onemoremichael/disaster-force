## Decision

The first complete physical human-continuity system is the **`HCDN-50` dual-state human-continuity district network**: fifty independently assignable `CDE-1K` districts, each able to maintain 1,000 person-equivalents at an origin, receive them elsewhere, bridge the household bundle between those conditions, or reset for reuse. One district can occupy only one state in one interval.

This is not a shelter fleet. It is the physical layer that makes the [five-route human-continuity contract](department-of-resilience-human-continuity-service-contract-and-first-technology-campaign-constitution.md) real at both ends of displacement. The system must carry person state; safe occupied environment; point-of-use water, power and residual custody; food and hygiene; accessibility; origin contact; continuity-preserving transport; receiver admission; clinical and safeguarding handoff; exit; and regeneration.

Selection used twelve non-substitutable gates, then strict dominance among complete candidates. No weighted score was permitted. Specialized care, mobility, receiver-only, local-only and workforce-relief systems remain valid later systems or subsystems, but each failed at least two gates as the *first* physical program. `HCDN-50` alone passed all twelve and dominated every alternative in demand, capital exposure, unclosed demand and formation-family reach.

## The system is a network, not a platform

Four mutually exclusive service states define the physical doctrine:

- `HOLD`: sustain a complete household service bundle at its origin or a nearby support point.
- `RECEIVE`: admit a displaced bundle into verified occupied service with a funded exit.
- `BRIDGE`: project service to the origin or move the intact bundle to an accepted receiver.
- `RETURN / RESET`: close the household case and regenerate people, articles, stocks and records.

This supersedes the earlier tendency to treat the mobile `PCC-50` and fixed National Receiving Lattice as separate program answers. Their physical and geographic work remains evidence lineage, but the first program must compete their boundary. Fixed and mobile are implementation variables inside one reversible service network.

The initial reference scale is deliberately inherited rather than re-certified: 7,530 m² of occupied area per district; 78.7 t for the independent structural screen; 15.7 t of prepared-site import; 21.9 t of receiver-weighted mobile mass; and 18–27 hours for installation. These `R2+` values are contradictions to beat, not requirements or proof. The mission endurance becomes 168 hours, extending the earlier 72-hour campus test to a seven-day complete-service obligation.

## Technology program

Five Pass 169 competitions form one USD 63.8B physical tranche: occupied-environment metabolism (`HU-03A`), food and essential-goods service (`HU-03B`), accessible structure and assistive support (`HU-03C`), continuity-preserving transport (`HU-04B`) and receiver transition (`HU-04C`). The preceding USD 12.8B `HU-04A` state-and-admission campaign remains separate interface substrate. Neither amount is additive to the USD 153B `MS-HU` envelope.

The tranche funds rival science, full-scale articles, denial trials and industrial reproduction—not incremental improvement to one prefab camp. It must be allowed to invent autonomous building conversion, self-routing utility spines, cellular occupied environments, robotic access and delivery, adaptive food and pharmacy systems, reconfigurable clinical transport interiors, local machine-readable assurance, and reversible civic-service cells.

## Representative campaign

Four complete roots enter without preference:

1. an instrumented prepared-building federation;
2. a deployable occupied district;
3. a hybrid civic-metabolism lattice; and
4. a robotic local-conversion network.

At each of three independent sites, every root receives two operating districts—one origin and one receiver—and one protected reference. The campaign therefore requires 24 operating and 12 reference district articles. Twelve denial worlds include seven-day grid loss, water and sewer loss, heat with smoke, flooded access, aftershock damage, communications denial, broken household custody, receiver withdrawal, supply interruption, workforce loss and compound origin/receiver loss.

Crossing four roots, three sites, twelve worlds and two role directions creates **288 complete missions**, **4,030 route-stage decisions** and 288,000 person-mission-equivalents. Those are repeated experimental exposures, not unique people. Human-occupied phases require independent structural, environmental, utility and civil release; hazardous stimuli use surrogates beforehand. Protected references never operate missions. Invalid attempts remain visible. No root, capacity, procurement quantity or exterior form is selected.
