## The three-hypothesis law

`REP-3` is a comparative technical plate, never a hero image. Each root must show exactly three mechanically divergent hypotheses together, at identical scale, view, detail, lighting and image area. Plan, elevation and causal section must expose the registered boundary. Assumed and unresolved geometry remains visibly hatched or transparent. Livery, brands, named places, people, dramatic mission scenes, fleet quantities and achieved-performance labels are prohibited. The plates cannot enter bid scoring or root disposition.

The eighteen authorized hypotheses deliberately vary the causal geometry: distributed twin-body, articulated-spine and semi-submersible pelagic exchange; telescoping, suspended-belt and mast-grid building insertion; winged, aerostat and rotorborne atmospheric intervention; radial, linear and nearshore geothermal source fields; courtyard, linear-spine and stacked clinical islands; and container-mesh, clean-core and mobile-train field regeneration.

All eighteen remain unissued and unselected. A comparative technical plate may expose form hypotheses; a single polished hero render may not create a favored configuration.

## What may be built

The representation boundary also fixes physical authority. Across forty-eight root–object records, thirty design-authorized objects require no production serial: full-scale causal articles, instrumented account sections, public interface fixtures, registered surrogate burdens and the limited exterior surfaces whose geometry is itself a causal variable. Eighteen objects remain held: operational packaging, production tooling and fleet units.

This is not permission to complete a silhouette. A surface may exist before evidence only where changing that surface changes the registered experiment. Transit enclosure, habitation, mission control, production detailing and aesthetic closure remain unresolved.

## Nonconversion and rollback

Fourteen view rules, eighteen nonconversion laws and twelve rollback triggers make visual authority reversible. Representation is not evidence; test geometry is not a candidate form; root survival is not a mission configuration; production is not readiness. A generated image can never become an as-built record.

Omitting one rival withdraws the entire root set. Unequal visual salience requires reissue. Apparent resolution of an unknown downgrades the work to `REP-2`. If an image influences a bid or disposition, that decision is void. Reversed evidence or failed cold reconstruction removes `REP-5` and every descendant. Discovered common ancestry returns the root to causal topology.

The undifferentiated equation of “rendering” with exterior selection is retired. The prohibition was necessary while requirements and causal sections were unresolved.
