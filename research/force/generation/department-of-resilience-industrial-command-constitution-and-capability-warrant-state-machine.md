# Department of Resilience industrial command constitution and capability-warrant state machine

**Research object:** `DIC-1`

## Frontier and resolution

The deepest live branch is `DIC-1`, the first of five permitted passes after the synchronized proof wave returned to Department-level industrial execution.

**Resolution test:** reversing the industrial command architecture would change who controls the Department’s USD 1.00T technology program, whether twenty-four rival roots remain independent and when any capability can become deployable national power; the object passes at Department scale.

Pass 184 established the Resilience Proving Service but did not show how its negative authority could coexist with mission commands, generating services, industrial houses, fiscal control and operating commands.[^1] A conventional hierarchy would resolve that tension by letting one executive trade one form of truth against another. This pass rejects that move.

## Decision: a constitutional industrial matrix

Five architectures face ten noncompensatory gates. A vertically integrated Materiel Command passes three. Six mission-system conglomerates pass three. Seven service arsenals pass five. A rotating lead-authority federation passes seven but still allows the current lead to own conflicting powers. Only a **constitutional industrial matrix** passes civil sovereignty, mission integration, technical depth, independent truth, root rivalry, fiscal and readiness conservation, adverse rollback, surge without waiver and transfer accountability.

The matrix is not consensus government. Each institution receives a narrow exclusive power and an explicit prohibition:

- the Civil Resilience Authority owns public priority, activation, acceptance and transfer;
- six Mission System Commands own complete service baselines and outcome integration;
- seven operating services own professions, article-family release and formation generation;
- twenty-four Root Houses design, build and reproduce controlled candidates;
- the Resilience Proving Service owns hostile worlds, counterfactuals, reconstruction, custody and root disposition;
- a Department Capital Board owns one-account option and stage authority;
- an Industrial Mobilization Administration owns tooling, material access, cold production and alternate lines;
- a Joint Capacity Authority owns serial readiness, exclusive reservation and the protected reserve;
- operational commands employ certified formations; and
- Strategic Sustainment Command owns national stocks, replacement and regeneration.

The Secretary and Resilience Staff may set portfolios, redirect priorities and cancel programs. They may not convert a missing technical, evidentiary, civil or readiness warrant into a positive one. Emergency urgency can redirect certified capacity; it cannot certify an unproved root.

## The capability is the governed object

The Department should not acquire through an organization chart. A capability advances through twelve serial warrants: public need, mission baseline, fiscal option, controlled configuration, technical and safety release, evidence disposition, production reproducibility, formation certification, exclusive reservation, lawful employment, service acceptance and force regeneration.

Each warrant has one issuing authority, one mandatory co-signer, an entrant artifact, an exit condition and a rollback floor. Later states cannot be inferred from contracts, inventory, source output, money spent or favorable evidence. The model expands the twenty-four roots into 288 root–warrant records. All issuer, co-signer, service-bundle and evidence identities are empty, so all roots remain `HOLD`.

This structure changes the meaning of integration. A Mission System Command can reject a disconnected machine but cannot judge its evidence. A service can deny unsafe technical release but cannot define the public objective. A Root House can create a system but owns no public warrant. The Proving Service can kill a lineage but cannot award Department capital or employ the force. The Joint Capacity Authority can deny double-counted readiness but cannot redesign the formation.

Twenty non-collapsible separation records apply beneficial-control, executive-command, decisive-toolchain and evidence-custody tests. Legal titles alone do not create independence.

## Failure is a state transition

Twelve rollback rules prevent success from becoming sticky. Configuration drift removes reliance from later evidence. A safety or rights stop returns the candidate to technical release. An adverse balance result orders `RESIZE`; an adverse keystone result orders `KILL` and returns unused authority to the failure reserve. Cold-production failure removes production and formation credit. A reservation conflict makes duplicated capacity zero. Civil revocation stops employment, while service rejection removes public credit even if equipment produced output. Regeneration failure keeps the formation outside the ready pool.

History remains public after every rollback. A program may re-earn a state; it may not erase the failed state.

## One industrial economy, five fiscal sovereignties

The Pass 166 fiscal constitution remains intact: USD 550B for mission systems, USD 150B for service technical bases, USD 150B for national common capacity, USD 100B for the sovereign technical estate and USD 50.0B for competitive failure reserve.[^2] Five time waves preserve the USD 1.00T total. Root and service ledgers are executor views, not additional appropriations.

The six published mission allocations total USD 549.9B at displayed precision. The model retains the USD 0.1B rounding remainder in the mission account rather than forcing root values to a false exact total. Pass 184’s USD 43.0B ceiling is also nested: USD 17.2B maps to mission systems, USD 21.5B to the sovereign technical estate and USD 4.30B to failure reserve. None is new money.

Pass 184’s Program Comptroller is therefore narrowed. It executes authorized proving stages and returns unused balances; it does not own the Department account or decide which mission receives capital. The Capital Board owns that higher fiscal warrant. This prevents the Proving Service from becoming both judge and portfolio sponsor.
