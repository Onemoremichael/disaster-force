## Frontier and resolution

The synchronized proof-wave branch is `C3-SPW-1 → C3-SPW-2 → C3-SPW-3`; this is its third of five permitted passes.

**Resolution test:** reversing the noncompensatory balance law or any of its twenty dimensioned baselines would redirect every non-lifeline proof build, the USD 239B learning portfolio and the roots that can enter the four-stack Cascadia barrier; the object passes.

Pass 181 froze twenty mechanism centers but did not prove that any one could carry its own mass, energy, labor, receivers or residuals. This pass closes that paper-design deficit without admitting a physical coefficient.

## Decision: every root carries seven balances

A `CF1` configuration is paper-balanced only when seven accounts close separately in two worlds: intact operation and loss of its largest independent cell. Intact capacity must be at least 1.15 times demand; the loss world must remain at or above 1.00. Surplus in one account cannot offset failure in another.

1. **Mass:** eight configuration-owned dry blocks plus mission stock fit the surviving access envelope.
2. **Energy:** firm generation and distributed black-start storage both survive one cell loss.
3. **Route flow:** every inherited service route retains its native denominator and passes separately.
4. **Labor:** shift-complete work continues with twenty percent of the deployed workforce unavailable.
5. **Safety:** protected positions cover everyone inside the declared hazard envelope after loss of one refuge cell.
6. **Receiver:** lawful public interfaces accept the offered service after one interface is denied.
7. **Residual:** treatment continues and a distributed buffer holds three days of output during receiver refusal.

This is more demanding than a platform mass budget. A machine that reaches the incident but borrows host power, leaves an unsafe worker envelope, overwhelms a hospital or port, or exports waste to the damaged jurisdiction remains an open configuration.

All 140 account cases and all ninety-two routes close on paper. Root-level largest-cell-loss floors range from 1.00 to 1.05. Two civil-terrain roots sit exactly at the 1.00 energy boundary; the model preserves that exposure rather than manufacturing reserve through rounding.

## Innovation and industrial consequence

The balance sheets now specify what technological ambition must buy. Variable-buoyancy carriers require controlled ground reaction and energy-dense turnaround. Building spines require safe robotic utility insertion in occupied structures. Hydraulic lattices need debris-tolerant exchangeable wet ends and active breach arrest. Mobile care must maintain therapy, identity and receiving state in motion. Disconnected microfactories must reproduce ancestry, calibration and material qualification. The closed-loop fleet must retain every counterflow when an outside receiver refuses it.

The USD 239B non-lifeline authority is cross-classified across mass 18 percent, energy 17, route flow 20, labor 10, safety 13, receivers 12 and residuals 10. This adds no money and does not replace Pass 181's four workstream view. It prevents a root house from spending its entire option on the visible mechanism while leaving the complete service chain unfunded.
