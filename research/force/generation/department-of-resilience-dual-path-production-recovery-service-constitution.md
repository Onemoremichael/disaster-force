# Department of Resilience dual-path production-recovery service constitution

**Pass:** 187

**Research object:** `MPC-2`

**Decision date:** 11 September 2026

**Verdict:** **CLOSE**

## Frontier and resolution

The deepest live branch is `MPC-2`, the second of five permitted market-and-production passes; `MPC-1` constituted the firms and public estate but left their recovery service undefined.

**Resolution test:** reversing this production-recovery constitution would change whether the Department buys paper rights and reserve inventory or a physically timed ability to replace any failed root after compound industrial loss, so the object passes at Department scale.

Pass 186 created twenty-four Root Houses, twenty-four public reproduction cells and forty-eight root–cell qualifications.[^1] It did not define what a cell must produce, how fast, with which labor and material, or after which losses. “Restart capacity” could therefore mean anything from a readable drawing to a duplicate national factory.

## Decision: a dual-path root recovery service

Five architectures face twelve noncompensatory gates. Escrow and contractor call-up passes three. An inventory-only reserve passes seven. One universal public mega-arsenal passes nine but creates common site and process roots. Two exact twin factories per root pass eleven but breach the USD 250B productive-estate boundary. Only a **dual-path root recovery service** passes all twelve.

Every Root House owes a distributed private survivor network. Every root also has two cold-qualified public paths in different cells, sites and operator-control groups. A public cell may serve at most two roots. This is not three standing copies of every factory. The Department owns root-specific tooling, keystone inventory, technical data, metrology and adaptable production capacity that can become the required root under denial.

The Industrial Mobilization Administration executes recovery. Mission System Commands retain the service denominator, operating services retain technical release, the Resilience Proving Service retains evidence and adverse disposition, and the Joint Capacity Authority alone credits capacity.[^2] A receiver cannot certify itself.

## The recovery unit is complete service

One **recovery service unit** is one complete system-native quarter-service unit inherited from the synchronized proof estate.[^3] It retains every route and interface: seventeen theater-entry positions, fourteen civil-terrain, seven hazard-control, ten lifeline, thirty-four human-continuity or seventeen regeneration positions. A partial machine, tonne of stock, unaccepted lot or borrowed formation earns zero units.

The twenty-four root units span 16.0–96.0 kt dry. The lifeline values divide the controlled two-million-person configurations by their inherited quarter-service denominator; the other twenty retain their first-principles balanced masses.[^4] Labor intensity and process energy remain explicit, editable acquisition hypotheses rather than observed facts.

## Two different recovery worlds

The private survivor world removes the largest private production site, one decisive supplier and 25.0 percent of qualified workers. Each Root House must distribute production across at least three sites, cap any site at 40.0 percent, black-start within seventy-two hours, deliver a conforming critical lot within thirty days, complete one recovery unit within 120 days and complete three by day 365.

The public world is harder. One Root House is wholly unavailable in each of the six mission systems at the same time. For every failed root, one of its two public cells, one decisive supplier, the largest internal bay and 25.0 percent of its workforce are also unavailable. The surviving path must black-start within 168 hours, cold-build a conforming critical lot within sixty days, complete one unit within 240 days and a second by day 365.

There are exactly 4,096 possible six-root cohorts. The lightest composite unit is 201 kt, the mean is 279 kt and the heaviest is 358 kt. The heaviest cohort requires 1.49 kt/day to deliver the first six units and 1.96 kt/day to deliver twelve by day 365. Six one-quarter-rate bays give every active cell 150 percent installed throughput and 125 percent after one bay loss; the corresponding six-cell envelope installs about 2.94 kt/day.

## The public estate becomes physical

Twenty-four cells contain 144 adaptive production bays. Each cell holds root-specific long-cycle sets equal to 15.0 percent of each assigned unit plus common convertible feedstock equal to 10.0 percent of its governing unit. Across the lattice that creates 465 kt of controlled inventory. Workforce gross-up for the registered denial creates about 87,700 qualified positions across all cells. The heaviest six-root cohort calls about 18,000 active workers and 123 MW of process power before reserve.

These are planning loads, not evidence. Site, operator, utility, tooling, supplier, workforce, cold-lot and acceptance identities remain empty. The workbook therefore reports arithmetic `PASS` while every authority state remains `SOURCE EMPTY`.

The existing USD 250B public productive-estate view now has physical content: USD 48.0B for cell sites and utility islands, USD 42.0B for bays, USD 48.0B for tooling capsules, USD 32.0B for material banks, USD 26.0B for reference articles, USD 18.0B for workforce systems, USD 14.0B for metrology and custody, USD 10.0B for transport and receiver bridges, and USD 12.0B for renewal. Its five-wave schedule is the same money viewed through time.

The inherited USD 100B sovereign-technology account drives the required novelty: compound factory-denial worlds, adaptive heavy-production bays, self-restoring metrology, live material genealogy, deficit-to-production compilers, portable credential twins, independent utility islands, evidence-driven reconfiguration and continuity-receivership twins. No new authority is added.

The companion [workbook](../../../models/force/generation/department-of-resilience-dual-path-production-recovery-service-constitution.xlsx) contains twenty sheets, 1,758 passing terminal audits and twenty-nine restored mutation tests. Independent recalculation preserves every audit, the native chart, zero formula errors and zero released production authority.
