## 1. The ancestry proposition

An ancestry result answers more than whether a marker was detected. It must establish which material entered, where it traveled, which phase carried it, which prior event could have contaminated it, where it ended and how much of the original inventory each terminal bin represents.

ER-03 therefore separates six questions:

1. **Identity:** which water and simulant lots were issued?
2. **Quantity:** how much of each lot entered and reached every terminal bin?
3. **Location:** where were the two materials at registered causal times?
4. **Phase:** was a code in water, simulant, an emulsion, a surface film, vapor, sample, quarantine or environment?
5. **History:** did the observation belong to the current event, a prior event, a decoy or laboratory contamination?
6. **Independence:** would the second root reach its result if the first root, its calibration and its software disappeared?

Detection alone can answer none of these reliably. A very sensitive reader may amplify old contamination. A code that strongly prefers one phase can understate entrainment. A shared sampler can make two unlike readers observe the same biased aliquot. A hidden event-to-code map can allow an analyst to infer the expected answer. RBJ-1 treats preparation, sampling, extraction, reading, interpretation and release as one end-to-end architecture.

## 2. Reference material and full-scale event

The nonenergetic simulant is a purpose-built reference fluid, not a convenient colored oil. Its procurement specification must bound density, viscosity, interfacial tension, wetting angle on every route and receiver material, film persistence, emulsification under registered shear, phase-separation time, water solubility, aerosol tendency, toxicity, environmental fate and decontamination response. The specification is a similarity envelope rather than a claim that one benign fluid reproduces every future combustion product.

The simulant enters through a separately measured, nonenergetic route that can create films, droplets and phase contact without using the future energetic source package. ## 9. Separation, decontamination and return

ER-03 does not demand perfectly pure recovered phases. It demands measured separation sufficient to prevent the recovery plant from manufacturing future ancestry. The recovered-water stream must carry no more than 25 milligrams of simulant per litre. Recovered simulant may contain no more than 0.005 water mass fraction. Material outside those boundaries enters quarantine; it cannot be relabeled as normal recovery.

Decontamination is a demonstrated state transition. Root-specific coupons, samplers, readers, separation cells, tanks and routes receive before-and-after observations. The next event cannot prepare merely because a cleaning recipe completed. Both roots must show prior-code carryover no greater than 0.001, while blank controls and the independent physical inventory close.

The ancestry-reference module receives a 672-hour wet and coded return. Required observations occur at 24, 72, 168, 336, 504 and 672 hours. Twenty-four decisions cover phase separation, hidden retention, reader baseline, sampler baseline, prior-code extinction, material compatibility, corrosion, seals, valve state, software baseline, evidence completeness and outside reconstruction. An early clean sample cannot shorten the return.

## Research stocktake

RBJ-1 changes the larger Department design in seven ways.

First, ancestry is a public physical capability. Code stock, preparation, samplers, readers, calibration, analysis, reveal and terminal custody are capital systems rather than metadata fields.

Second, independence must be tested in cohabitation. Two individually credible methods can share contamination, sampling or computation and fail as a pair.

Third, blinding is infrastructure. Separate preparation cells, anonymous reader outputs, immutable root packets and threshold reveal prevent event identity from becoming a hidden measurement input.

Fourth, nonenergetic work can be full scale. ER-03 controls the future 500-kilogram and 24-cubic-metre nominal inventory before heat is allowed to transform either material.

Fifth, return belongs to ancestry. A clean event result is incomplete until retained code, contaminated samplers, separation plant and analytical waste reach terminal state.

Sixth, technology authority must expand when a new truth system appears. The original parent had only USD 50 million of technology capacity left; the ancestry portfolio alone requires USD 210 million.

Seventh, a serious institution carries correction capacity. The seven-year parent boundary does not claim certainty. It makes room for common-cause failure instead of concealing it inside later rungs.
