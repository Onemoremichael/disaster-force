# Department of Resilience Productive Commonwealth sustaining economy and return

**Pass:** 190

**Research object:** `MPC-5`

**Decision date:** 11 September 2026

**Verdict:** **RETURN**

## Frontier and resolution

The deepest live branch is `MPC-5`, the fifth and final permitted market-and-production pass; `MPC-4` distributed the productive estate but left its workers, suppliers, recurring work and mature cash economy undefined.

**Resolution test:** reversing this constitution would change whether the Department owns a living industrial force or a capital estate that loses skills, suppliers and configuration knowledge between disasters, so the object passes at Department scale.

The branch cannot continue after this pass. It must return a complete industrial-service constitution to Department force generation.

## Decision: a dual-circuit Productive Commonwealth

Five architectures face fourteen noncompensatory gates. Standby retainers preserve nominal capacity but not work. An export-led merchant sector makes domestic readiness hostage to foreign demand. One public monopoly creates a national common root. Unregulated civil cross-subsidy provides activity without guaranteeing root-native skills, evidence or availability. Only a **dual-circuit Productive Commonwealth** passes all gates.

The first circuit is the finite USD 1.00T federal program. Contest capital, proof, public estate and initial formations spend USD 880B in the first ten years. The remaining USD 120B buys fifteen years of availability and regeneration at USD 8.00B/year. Payment is earned by physical service, not by corporate existence.

The second circuit is a market for useful output. It buys grid, water and thermal modules; fire, flood and civil equipment; clinical, housing and food systems; logistics and communications; regenerated material; receiver preparation; maintenance; training and assurance. Sales earn revenue and keep production hot. They do not earn readiness credit.

The Industrial Mobilization Administration schedules the service. Root Houses and public cells produce. A new **Resilience Labor Commonwealth** owns portable credentials and household continuity. The Resilience Proving Service owns evidence. The Joint Capacity Authority owns readiness credit. A continuity receiver inherits a failed firm’s configuration, workers, orders and supplier rights without erasing adverse evidence.[^1]

## Labor becomes productive infrastructure

The workforce model derives labor from each root’s complete-unit mass, labor intensity, annual recovery output, six productive hours per worker-day and 25.0-percent denial.[^2] Private Root Houses require about 112,000 qualified positions; public cells require about 87,500. The combined establishment is about 200,000.

Every worker carries five inseparable objects: an employer relationship, public task credentials, a mobilization cohort, portable benefits and a household-continuity package. Housing, health care, dependent care and relocation are therefore force infrastructure, not personnel amenities. Sixty percent of positions are resident campus cohorts, 25.0 percent are transferable reinforcement and 15.0 percent are instructors or cold-reconstruction personnel.

Eight skill guilds span systems safety, advanced manufacturing, civil works, power and process systems, clinical systems, heavy logistics, autonomy and communications, and evidence and metrology. A 10.0-percent annual renewal requires about 20,000 credential completions and a two-year pipeline of about 44,400 learners at a 90.0-percent completion assumption. Employer loss cannot cancel public task authority.

## A four-source supplier diamond

Each of twenty-four roots receives twelve decisive supplier families. Every root–family pair has four source roles: two private sources and one in each public path. The resulting 288 diamonds contain 1,152 source roles. Their four macro-theaters are different.[^3]

Loss of one macro-theater and one private provider therefore leaves at least two sources. A private provider may hold one decisive role per root and no more than two roots; 576 private source roles require at least 288 provider-control slots under this rule. The two public roles remain in sovereign cells. Every source role owes one accepted cold lot annually. Letters of intent, catalog availability and past production count as zero.

## Annual productive service

The Commonwealth runs even without a disaster. Every sustaining year requires ninety-six campus black-starts, 1,152 supplier cold lots, twenty-four route requalification activations, two six-root cohort mobilizations, twenty-four campus-denial reconstructions, eight district impairments, two macro-theater impairments and six receivership simulations.

Each root activates one private or public route annually, covering all seventy-two routes in three years. Two joint rotations cover all eight registered cohorts in four years. Campus and macro-denial groups recur in four years; districts recur in three. Their combined supercycle is twelve years. Annual success never substitutes for Pass 188’s full qualification campaign.[^4]

## The domestic market floor is a readiness condition

The model’s mature reference case requires USD 75.0B/year of domestic public-effect sales, USD 12.0B/year of optional partner sales and the USD 8.00B availability payment. Annual sector revenue is USD 95.0B against USD 88.2B of modeled cost, leaving USD 6.85B for renewal and expansion. Fifteen-year market sales are USD 1.31T and remain separate from federal program authority.

With exports at zero, USD 83.0B of revenue covers USD 82.8B of cost by only USD 0.249B. That narrow margin is intentional: export cannot be the hidden domestic subsidy. A domestic-demand fall to USD 60.0B produces a USD 8.00B deficit; the registered labor-and-material shock produces a USD 13.4B deficit. The response is to buy additional **useful output**, redesign the cost base or shrink credited authority—not add unexamined readiness money.

The USD 75.0B domestic floor is therefore an industrial service denominator, not a forecast. Actual buyers, prices, margins and orders remain `SOURCE EMPTY`.

## Branch return

Five `MPC` passes established the chartered twenty-four-firm market, dual-path recovery service, physical qualification campaign, ninety-six-campus geography and sustaining Productive Commonwealth. It cost a modeled USD 1.00T program, a USD 430B qualification campaign cross-classified within it, a USD 250B public estate, about 200,000 qualified positions, 1,152 decisive-source roles and a USD 75.0B annual domestic-output floor to learn that industrial capacity is a continuously exercised public service rather than stored plant.

The branch returns to Department force generation. Its transferable object is an **industrial availability service**. It is not a deployed formation, mission release or disaster-response effect.

The companion [workbook](../../../models/force/generation/department-of-resilience-productive-commonwealth-sustaining-economy-and-return.xlsx) contains twenty-five sheets, 4,206 passing terminal audits and forty-three restored mutations. Three live mutations turn the sovereign-floor surplus into a deficit and restore it. Independent recalculation reports no formula errors. All identities, lots, credentials, sales and events remain source-empty; released readiness authority is zero.
