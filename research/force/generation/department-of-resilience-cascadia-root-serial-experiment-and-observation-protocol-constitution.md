## Disposition before design

**Resolution test:** reversing the root allocation, blindness or adjudication law would change which six-system configurations can earn `G4`, how the USD 49.5B campaign reservation is spent and whether any system may approach production qualification.

It passes because the experimental unit and the authority to accept it are force-development decisions, not test administration.

## Decision: serialize complete service roots

A Cascadia root is a complete system configuration, not a lead vehicle, software release or prime-contractor team. Each of the six mission systems receives four unlike root baselines. Every baseline must carry the system’s entire Pass 173 load across its assigned routes. The campaign therefore creates twenty-four baseline identities and 1,448 serialized formation-equivalent positions: four times the 362-position campaign load.

The position register contains 248 theater-entry, 192 civil-terrain, 96 hazard-control, 152 lifeline, 512 human-continuity and 248 regeneration positions. Each serial binds one route, technical release owner, root baseline, configuration lineage, critical supplier, control ancestry, energy ancestry, evidence interface, reset state and disposition. Those fields initially read `UNBUILT`, `UNDECLARED` or `OPEN`. A root label is not evidence of diversity.

Fifteen system pairs create 180 observed root-pair exposures. The selected schedule contains 164 distinct pairings. Every pair sees at least ten of its sixteen possible cells; seven pairs see twelve. Fixed stacks and synchronized rotations expose only sixty distinct pairings; a simple site-shift design leaves some pairs at four.

### Freeze, blind and reveal

Eighteen package elements seal before activation: the root and position manifests; configuration hashes; route allocations; numeric service thresholds; lineage disclosures; crew and authority rosters; site state; calibration and time roots; evidence nodes; network-isolation and manual-service plans; safety stops; denial commitment; residual destinations; retry token; raw-custody path; and outside-replay destinations.

The operating stack receives zero activation notice inside a registered window. The denial director and safety authority know the physical inject. Operators, builders, program offices and public acceptance authorities do not. A cryptographic commitment and two sealed physical copies fix denial identity, order, tolerances and reveal material before data exist. Reveal occurs only after raw originals enter independent custody. Safety may stop a run; it may not rewrite the tested question.

Four stacks may execute concurrently within one range-class block. A root cannot enter the next block until its day-260 repair, configuration and recertification state is independently dispositioned. The calendar cannot convert an unresolved root into a fresh article by changing its identifier.

## The observation and adjudication object

The protocol freezes 222 decision templates: forty-two system-stage templates and 180 directed-interface-domain templates. Each is instantiated in sixty thread–epoch cells per execution and therefore produces the inherited 13,320 governed decisions. The workbook generates the complete selected-run packet deterministically rather than sampling it.

Every decision requires three separate signed dispositions:

1. the operating or generating authority issues, corrects or revokes a claim;
2. the sovereign evidence authority judges the frozen attempt `VALID` or `INVALID`; and
3. the receiving system or public-effect authority accepts, rejects or revokes reliance.

Campaign-wide, 159,840 decisions require 479,520 authority dispositions. The 21,600 exchange records retain 432,000 mandatory field instances. Twelve observation classes cover identity, configuration, time, physical state, capacity, path, quality, people and rights, custody, control and rollback, ancestry, and return and replay. Raw evidence must retain original state, calibration, causal time and custody. Derived features may assist judgment but cannot replace a missing original.

Credit exists only when a claim is current, the attempt is valid and reliance is accepted. Revocation removes dependent credit and forces the registered rollback. Missing thresholds, undeclared ancestry, broken custody, unrepeatable event order or absent acceptance remain visible failures; they do not become zeros.

## Invalid is not adverse

Each campaign execution receives one reserve token. Only an independent validity board may activate it, and only for the same run, roots, range class, denial and question. A breached blind, wrong configuration, out-of-tolerance inject, lost independent evidence plane, unreconstructable clock or broken raw custody can invalidate an attempt.

A root failure, operator error under the frozen protocol, unsafe response caused by the tested system, missed service, failed rollback, rights violation or exposed common ancestry is a valid adverse result. It cannot be repeated away. Twelve reserve tokens add no denominator or credit and draw only from the existing USD 2.48B failure reserve.
