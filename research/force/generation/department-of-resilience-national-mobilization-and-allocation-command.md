## Decision: a constitutional allocation command

Secretary discretion, a hazard priority table, a benefit-cost auction and a service dispatch federation all fail noncompensatory gates. The Department shall create a **National Mobilization and Allocation Command (NMAC)**, but deny it sovereignty over public value, readiness truth, proof or employment.

NMAC owns one narrow power: compile admitted claims into an executable, collision-free schedule. Nine other institutions retain exclusive powers:

- the Civil Resilience Authority defines public objectives, priority classes, acceptance and transfer;
- the Joint Capacity Authority certifies serial availability, family floors and reservation;
- six Mission System Commands compose complete service bundles;
- seven District Commands certify local crews, access, stocks and receivers;
- operational commands employ assigned formations;
- the Resilience Proving Service tests allocation worlds and evidence;
- a Rights and Scarcity Tribunal hears expedited challenges;
- an International Custody Chamber co-signs invitation and bilateral release; and
- a Public Allocation Ledger publishes claims, collisions, denials, warrants and appeals.

No actor issues its own input and clearance. The Secretary may change policy or cancel a mission but cannot create a formation, suppress a collision or recall an accepted international serial unilaterally.

## Allocation is lexicographic, not a score

NMAC applies seven ordered layers. A later layer operates only inside the feasible set left by every earlier layer:

1. **legality and rights** — invitation, accessibility, nondiscrimination and stop authority;
2. **existing custody** — occupied domestic and accepted international formations remain committed;
3. **minimum life-preserving service** — every admitted population receives a complete survival bundle before optimization;
4. **family and reserve invariants** — preserve every family floor, the 502-formation reserve and district-loss rule;
5. **collision elimination** — one serial, crew, stock set, route and receiver per interval;
6. **marginal irreversible-loss reduction** — assign remaining capacity by avoided irreversible harm per scarce-family-day; and
7. **recovery and transfer** — prefer missions that create lawful local capacity and release scarce formations.

The law refuses a monetary value-of-life scalar. Cost, speed and throughput inform choices only after rights, complete minimum service and hard readiness invariants pass. A technically efficient allocation that strands disabled people, exports residual harm or lacks a lawful receiver is infeasible—not merely lower-scoring.

## The twelve-seal transaction

Every order advances through twelve reconstructable seals: claim, admissibility, complete service bundle, candidate serials, collision graph, family-floor test, custody test, lexicographic solve, adversarial replay, public reason, appeal window and execution order. Emergency urgency may shorten clocks; it cannot remove a seal.

The claim grammar requires a population state, public effect, time interval, geography, complete family bundle, named receiver, authority, evidence and release condition. A request for aircraft, ships or pumps is not yet a public-service claim.

The model instantiates all **480** family–campaign cells underlying the ten accepted planning campaigns. Their demands sum exactly to the 2,420 committed account. All serial assignments remain empty, so arithmetic fit produces no execution authority.[^2]

## Reserve is not an eleventh campaign

The largest single inherited demand in each family creates a hypothetical eleventh event requiring **406** formations. The protected reserve contains 502—apparently ninety-six more. Yet family-by-family matching supplies only **390** complete formations. Six families are short by sixteen: flood/hydraulic control, ground fire, aerial hazard control, port opening, inland waterway and offshore support.

This is the central allocation result. Aggregate slack cannot cure a scarce-family deficit. NMAC must either deny complete admission, deliver a smaller lawful service, or request a **geographic-risk conversion warrant** for sixteen formations from the 745-formation geographic account. That warrant requires no active district impairment, independent routes, funded replacement clocks and public acknowledgement that `N−1` assurance has temporarily fallen. During district loss, the same shortcut is unavailable.

Accepted international capacity is equally hard. Its serials remain committed through employment, transfer, withdrawal and return. Bilateral release and domestic recertification—not domestic urgency—restore availability.
