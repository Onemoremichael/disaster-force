# F36 `CISA-40` internal build-release and measurement drawing constitution

## Executive judgment

Pass 110 made the first coupled F36 qualification object physical. `CISA-40` acquired a transport envelope, mass state, support and lifting requirements, paired full-bore hydraulic service, sanitary separation, island power, black start, receiver worlds and thirteen measurement planes. It did not show how those requirements occupy one article or what a builder must release before metal, composite, pipe, bus, software or fixture may be cut.

This pass creates `BRD-1`, the internal Build-Release Definition for `CISA-40`. It is a controlled coordinate, section, topology, record and authority system. It is not an exterior rendering and does not choose a production vehicle form.

The decisive result is that the 3.6 m transport width cannot directly supply the 5.0 m effective anchor-line span used in the Pass 110 overturning calculation. `BRD-1` therefore makes four deployable article-owned anchor outriggers mandatory. Their deployed centers sit at `Y = ±2.500 m`; their transport roots remain inside `Y = ±1.600 m`. A root that deletes the outriggers must replace the load model and requalify the receiver. It may not inherit the 156.757 kN combined-anchor result.

The article coordinate system is now fixed. The transport envelope is `X = 0.000–12.000 m`, `Y = −1.800–+1.800 m` and `Z = 0.000–3.800 m`. Eight support centers occupy four stations at `X = 1.000, 4.300, 7.700 and 11.000 m`, each at `Y = ±1.000 m`. Each support has a 1.000 m longitudinal by 1.600 m transverse bearing face, 1.600 m² effective area, 400 mm controlled leveling travel, a 150 kN working jack boundary and an independent 0–200 kN reaction channel. The maximum 1.50-g, one-support-out demand remains 70.923 kPa against the 75 kPa weak-ground limit.

Four lift-node centers are fixed at `(2.000, ±1.350, 2.800)` and `(10.000, ±1.350, 2.800) m`. The public load path requires 196 kN working capacity per node, 200 kN independent proof, local strain measurement and no receiver-owned crane. The governing challenged node remains 142.785 kN. Root A may use a strand-jack portal carried by the article. Root B may use four synchronized lifting crawlers and a tensioned transfer frame. Neither may borrow the other's unique fixture or control ancestry.

The two industrial roots now receive unlike internal structural hypotheses. `ROOT_A` uses a welded high-strength steel orthogonal space frame with four transverse reaction frames and replaceable mechanical utility collars. `ROOT_B` uses a continuous-fiber thermoplastic shell-grid, metallic receiver nodes and post-tensioned load ties with bonded sanitary collars. Both fit the same coordinate, load, service, measurement and access envelope. Their dry mass ledgers each close at 32.000 tonnes, but their materials, production machines, joining, inspection, repair and reset paths remain unlike.

The exact dry center of gravity is `X = 5.758 m`, `Y = −0.019 m`, `Z = 1.294 m` for the Root A planning build and `X = 5.809 m`, `Y = +0.033 m`, `Z = 1.308 m` for Root B. Common service inventory and four one-tonne ballast cells produce operating centers of gravity at `(5.899, −0.015, 1.189) m` and `(5.940, +0.026, 1.201) m`. These are model outputs, not measurements. Build release requires a root-specific mass-properties prediction, independent weighing plan and ballast map; article acceptance requires physical observation.

The 24 m length assigned to each hydraulic path does not fit as a straight line inside the 12 m article. `BRD-1` resolves the geometry without shrinking the test: each root article contains a 12 m full-bore service section and each receiver spine contains an independently instrumented 12 m return section for that path. Two paths therefore contain 48 m of 0.35 m bore and 4.6181 m³ total. Article and return sections must remain separately attributable. A common unmeasured receiver manifold receives no credit.

The power drawing becomes a two-section 480Y/277 V bus with a normally open tie, a separately protected 0.60 MW critical bus, a 2.5 MVA continuous step transformer and a 13.8 kV public feeder. The common boundary supports 1.50 MW continuous, 2.25 MW for ten minutes and 3.00 MW for ten seconds. A 30 kWh independent evidence battery and local clock preserve measurement through total service-bus loss. Root A is provisionally a liquid-fuel, rotating-machine and LFP-storage architecture. Root B is provisionally a different rotating-conversion train with structural zinc-halogen storage. These are technology competitions, not production selections.

The package contains forty-eight controlled drawing sheets, fifty-two root build records, twenty-four drawing checks, eighteen hazard boundaries and fifty-six release gates. It plans an eighteen-month definition program, 192 people, USD 0.318B capital and USD 0.084B/year, all cross-classified inside Pass 110 `CISA-1`. Drawing completeness may be authorized. Physical build release, article acceptance, `CRC-E01`, production and exterior rendering remain unavailable.

## 1. Authority and limits

`BRD-1` is allowed to define:

- public coordinates, datums, tolerances and interface zones;
- internal load paths and root-specific structural hypotheses;
- support, anchor, lift and transfer geometry;
- hydraulic, sanitary, electrical, grounding and containment topology;
- measurement locations, ranges, rates, uncertainties and evidence power;
- access, exclusion, rescue and teardown volumes;
- receiver and fixture reactions;
- material, process, software, calibration and custody records;
- drawing checks, change classes and build-release gates; and
- technology objectives created by unresolved physical burdens.

It is not allowed to claim:

- an attractive, efficient or preferred exterior shape;
- road, rail, ship or aircraft compatibility beyond the declared dimensional state;
- actual mass, center of gravity, stiffness, strength, leakage or electrical behavior;
- a qualified root, receiver, fixture, sanitary result or black start;
- complete `MQP-1`, `CRC-E01` or production admission; or
- authority to replace the public service and evidence definitions inherited from Pass 110.

Every drawing must protect a decision. A sheet without a quantity, coordinate, tolerance, stimulus, sensor, invalid state, hazard boundary, access need or custody object is decoration and receives no release credit.

## 5. Root-specific structural definitions

### 5.1 `ROOT_A`: orthogonal welded frame

Root A places two longitudinal lower chords at `Y = ±1.250 m`, `Z = 0.550 m` and two upper chords at `Y = ±1.250 m`, `Z = 2.850 m`. Transverse reaction frames coincide with all four support stations. Diagonal bracing transfers lateral and anchor loads without using hydraulic pipes, cable trays, skins or equipment bases as undeclared primary structure.

Its required records include material heat identity, weld procedure, welder qualification, joint traveler, distortion survey, nondestructive examination, coating lot, repair record and post-proof dimensional map. A weld hidden by permanent equipment before independent inspection is a build-release failure.

### 5.2 `ROOT_B`: shell-grid and post-tension frame

Root B uses four continuous longitudinal composite beams connected by thermoplastic transverse diaphragms at the support stations. Metallic node blocks carry support, anchor and lift interfaces. Independent post-tension ties carry the dominant lateral and denied-support load path. Utility containment skins may carry declared secondary shear but cannot conceal node or tie loads.

Its required records include fiber and matrix lots, tape-placement machine state, consolidation pressure and temperature history, insert preparation, bondline geometry, void inspection, tension history, acoustic baseline and repair coupon. A root cannot infer bond integrity from cure command values alone.

### 6.2 Leveling law

The public input is desired article pose, not eight independent jack commands. Each root must:

1. discover contact without exceeding 20 kPa;
2. establish all available support identities;
3. solve article pose and receiver deformation separately;
4. raise reaction under a published rate limit;
5. stop on force, displacement, synchronization or survey disagreement;
6. support a deliberately unavailable node; and
7. return all supports and disturbed ground to a known terminal state.

The 400 mm travel is recorded as `0–400 mm` with a 200 mm reference position. Build release reserves the entire swept volume. Hoses, conductors and guards cannot enter it.

\[
F_{anchor}=\sqrt{(235.360/2)^2+(517.791/5)^2}=156.757\ \text{kN}

This is a design reference, not a prediction of soil or anchor behavior. The receiver fixture must measure arm-root force, deployed-anchor force, local ground displacement and article pose. If one arm contacts the receiver between those planes, the trial is invalid.

## 10. Sanitary topology and custody

Sanitary release is an architecture, not a final sample.

### 10.1 Separation rules

- product, challenge fluid, cleaning solution, residual and waste occupy physically distinct identified paths;
- product and waste connectors are mechanically incompatible in diameter, key pattern and closure sequence;
- each branch dead leg is no longer than 0.350 m unless separately circulated;
- total uncirculated trapped volume is no greater than 25 L per path;
- every valve position has an independent observation at the boundary;
- a wrong command cannot create an undetected product-to-waste or path-to-path connection;
- waste receivers sit on independent load cells and remain inside containment;
- three loop volumes, 13.8544 m³, are the minimum flush accounting quantity;
- mechanical cleaning, chemistry, temperature and elapsed contact remain visible rather than being inferred from volume; and
- a missing blank, positive control, calibration bracket or custody signature produces `INDETERMINATE`.

The evidence battery does not start a root source, close a service breaker or satisfy the 0.40 MWh critical-service requirement. Its only purpose is to preserve the decision record through a service-power failure.

### 11.2 Conductor and bus boundaries

At 1.50 MW, 480 V and 0.90 power factor, the three-phase current reference is 2.005 kA. The 2.25 MW overload reference is 3.007 kA. The 3.00 MW transient reference is 4.009 kA. The public low-voltage boundary therefore requires:

| Item | Continuous | 10 min | 10 s | Observation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| combined service bus | 2.5 kA | 3.5 kA | 5.0 kA | phase current and temperature |
| critical bus | 1.2 kA | declared root limit | declared root limit | independent trip witness |
| step transformer | 2.5 MVA | 3.5 MVA | 5.0 MVA | winding and cooling state |
| 13.8 kV feeder | 100 A | 150 A | 200 A | independent waveform |

The 13.8 kV 1.50 MW current reference is 69.7 A at 0.90 power factor; the 3.00 MW transient is 139.5 A. Ratings above those references preserve measurement and control margin, not an implied distribution-service claim.

### 11.3 Root power hypotheses

`ROOT_A` uses two 1.0 MW variable-speed liquid-fuel generator units, a common DC link, two grid-forming converters and a 0.60 MWh LFP system targeted to deliver at least 0.45 MWh after protection and temperature limits.

`ROOT_B` uses a mechanically and electrically unlike pair of recuperated rotating conversion units, a split AC architecture and a 0.72 MWh structural zinc-halogen system targeted to deliver at least 0.45 MWh. The storage system is a technology objective; its mass, thermal behavior and power density are not assumed proven.

Both roots must reach local control in 30 s, the protected critical bus in 90 s, observable hydraulic isolation in 5 min, 0.60 MW service in 10 min and 1.50 MW service in 20 min.

## 12. Grounding and protection matrix

The public receiver may present no ground, a high-impedance ground, a solid ground, a phase reversal, a stale trusted reference or a fault-current envelope incompatible with the root setting. The article must identify the state before energization.

| Challenge | Required behavior | Independent witness | Unsafe result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| receiver ground absent | remain isolated | public ground monitor | contactor closes |
| ground impedance outside registered band | remain isolated or enter declared limited mode | injected known impedance | root display only |
| phase order reversed | block close | independent phase witness | energization |
| external source present during black start | detect and isolate | voltage waveform on both sides | backfeed |
| 480 V bolted fault | clear within registered envelope | source, feeder and trip time | thermal or touch hazard |
| converter-limited low fault current | differential or zone protection clears | two independent current planes | reliance on overcurrent alone |
| protection setting mismatch | reject configuration | signed setting and injected truth | automatic overwrite |
| trusted time or key denied | retain safe local operation | public local clock and key witness | remote dependency |
| sensor disagreement | safe separation | raw independent channels | averaged false agreement |

Root A's rotating-machine fault reference is provisionally 8 kA symmetrical at 480 V for the first cycle. Root B's converter-limited reference is provisionally 2.2 per unit. These values size the qualification fixtures; neither becomes an accepted root value until measured.

The 480Y/277 V bus has one declared neutral-ground bond. A second bond, an unidentified receiver bond or an open protective conductor blocks service. Ground conductors remain outside fluid restraints and do not use structural bearings as the only return path.

## 22. Technology programs created by the internal definition

### 22.1 Structural batteries as service mass

Root B's storage hypothesis requires a zinc-halogen system that also contributes containment or secondary structure at a system-level specific energy near 144 Wh/kg. The program should test whether structural contribution survives crash, fire, electrolyte leak, repair and teardown. Failure returns the root to ordinary nonstructural storage and reopens its mass ledger.

### 22.2 Self-verifying deployable anchors

The outriggers need to know deployed geometry, ground coupling, preload, force direction, settlement and loss of contact. A public technology line should integrate survey, force, soil response and actuator state without turning one sensor package into a shared root dependency.

### 22.3 Contamination-incompatible high-flow collars

Full-bore joints should make wrong sanitary connections physically difficult, keep two barriers observable and expose hidden seal state after service. Competing mechanical and bonded-clamp roots should pursue fast closure without sacrificing cleanability or destructive inspectability.

### 22.4 Adaptive protection for unlike sources

Rotating machines and converter-limited sources require different fault decisions at the same public feeder. The program should develop signed, locally testable protection that identifies receiver ground and source type, injects known truth and remains safe without a network.

### 22.5 Evidence-native utility construction

Future frames, pipes, valves, buses and fixtures should carry standard public measurement coordinates and separated evidence routes from first manufacture. Evidence cannot remain an instrumentation retrofit after the load and hazard geometry is frozen.

### 22.6 Machine-reproducible technical data

The second authority should regenerate geometry, mass, settings, process travelers and measurement maps from native public and root objects without proprietary viewers or remote licensing. That is a major public digital-engineering program rather than a document-conversion task.

## 23. Failure modes `BRD-1` must expose

The package fails if:

- the deployed anchor span is inferred from the load model but absent from geometry;
- stowed and deployed envelopes are mixed;
- the center of gravity is obtained by placing unrestrained ballast after review;
- a pipe, skin, cable tray or equipment base carries undeclared primary load;
- one support passes total reaction while concentrating pressure through a hard point;
- the denied support or lift node is restored through fixture contact;
- a 24 m hydraulic claim contains only 12 m of attributable full-bore path;
- article and receiver return sections share an unmeasured manifold;
- a sanitary release depends on a final sample while valve, waste or blank custody is missing;
- the critical bus depends on receiver power, remote time or the evidence battery;
- protection uses the wrong source fault model or silently changes settings;
- both originals share a clock, fuse, conduit, recorder, cooling loop or fire zone;
- access volumes require simultaneous incompatible hazard states;
- root drawings say “same as other root” for a decisive mechanism;
- summary drawing counts substitute for checked native sheets;
- a deviation is accepted without preserving the nonconforming state;
- teardown destroys the only evidence of a hidden joint or seal; or
- internal arrangement is relabeled as a production exterior.
