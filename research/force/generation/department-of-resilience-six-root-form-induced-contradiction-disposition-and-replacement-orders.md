## The six-domain test

Each form was examined against six noncompensatory questions inherited from the candidate articles and internal sections:

1. Can any candidate element control, safe, improve or judge more than one required causal path?
2. Can loss of one access cell erase the service or lawful escape path?
3. Does generated, retained, treated and released material remain attributable after loss?
4. Can a clean team reconstruct each path without origin-team memory or hidden service?
5. Does one frame, foundation, header, core or controller become a decisive ancestor?
6. Does the representation keep unresolved transit, habitat, circulation and production envelopes visibly unresolved?

A score of zero records no visible contradiction. One orders a bounded revision that preserves the defining topology. Two means the correction would change the feature that defines the form; any score of two withdraws that hypothesis. The scale is ordinal, not an engineering margin.
