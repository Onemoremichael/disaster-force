# Department of Resilience Site-Class, Bid-Admission and N−1 Award-Slate Constitution

**Pass 214 — `A2C-4`**  
**Verdict:** `CLOSE`  
**Decision date:** 11 September 2026  
**Scope:** source-independent site and bidder admission for the first `A2` estate; no site, firm, bid, award, obligation, payment, observation, capability or exterior form is selected

## Resolution test

If this result were reversed, the Department could concentrate its first physical truth estate in one correlated geography or let price, incumbency and early awards select its industrial structure; both the architecture and the executability of the 102-lot program would change.

## The decision

The Department should constitute **eighteen role-native site charters**, one per station nucleus, and admit bids only through a **whole-portfolio N−1 award slate**. A prestige national range, cheapest compliant sites, existing-federal-estate preference and contractor-supplied campuses all fail. Each converts convenience, sunk property or bidder power into technical truth.

The selected constitution separates four judgments that a normal procurement joins: physical site fitness, technical bid fitness, beneficial-control admissibility and price. None can compensate for another, and no actor sees enough of the transaction to steer all four.

## Eighteen site classes

Every site charter fixes ten dimensions: causal regime, scale envelope, challenge authority, raw-service boundary, transport access, environmental containment, rescue and refuge, custody, cold-reconstruction access and public exit rights. Twelve noncompensatory site gates govern each charter, creating 216 decisions. Actual names, coordinates, owners and conditions remain source-empty.

A site may host only one nucleus in the first wave. The three sites in a truth train must share no decisive failure basin across eight domains: geophysical or hazard source; water, air or residual path; electric or thermal utility; transport access; communications and time; workforce and decisive supply; rescue and containment; and title, custody or operating control. Separation is demonstrated by causal ancestry, not mileage. Three distant sites on one grid or under one emergency operator are one site for this purpose.

The six civil-substrate contracts remain balanced three-site packages; they do not authorize co-location. A substrate builder must work across three different systems and roles, while each physical site retains a separate public steward. Site surveys and access work remain inside the existing USD 0.720B substrate ceiling; a charter creates no additional appropriation.

## Bid admission without bidder rule

Every lot must attract at least two separately controlled admissible bid positions, creating a minimum pool of 204 positions. Each position faces fourteen conjunctive predicates: complete-lot responsibility; technical method; relevant scale; schedule; capital at risk; beneficial ownership; debt and cross-default; decisive subtier; tooling and executable build description; keys and calibration; qualified workforce; labor, safety and civil rights; data, custody and cold-reconstruction license; and replacement plus exit.

This creates 2,856 bid-admission decisions. Technical evaluators receive pseudonymous method records without identity or price. The control registrar receives full ownership, finance, dependency, tool and personnel graphs but does not judge performance. Price remains sealed until technical and control admission close. The comptroller then tests each bid against its own lot ceiling; unused authority remains with that lot and cannot cure another lot's failure.

The 204-position floor is necessary but not sufficient. The admitted pool must generate a complete 102-lot primary slate and remain solvable after removal of any one beneficial-control group. This **N−1 control-group test** is run for all forty-eight required group classes. It is stronger than naming a preferred alternate: the entire pool must be recomposable without violating candidate/public separation, two-lot decisive caps, system separation, method-lineage separation, rig–regeneration separation or substrate–integration separation.

Among pools that pass every hard gate, selection is lexicographic: maximize the number of feasible post-withdrawal slates; minimize the largest beneficial-control exposure; minimize evaluated price; then maximize critical-path slack. A lower price cannot buy loss of independence. The optimization method and all submitted records are published to the Evidence Court before identities are unsealed to the award authority.

## Reversible award sequence

Nine states prevent an early winner from becoming inevitable. `S0 CLASS` publishes the eighteen site classes. `S1 CHARTER` admits all eighteen named sites together. `B0 BLIND` receives pseudonymous technical bids. `B1 CONTROL` freezes complete ownership and dependency graphs. `B2 ADMIT` closes every applicable predicate. `B3 SOLVE` proves a primary slate and forty-eight N−1 removals. `B4 PRICE` opens prices only for admitted positions. `B5 PROVISIONAL` publishes the complete intended slate and machine-readable reasons. `B6 EFFECTIVE` makes all 102 awards effective simultaneously after challenge and revalidation.

Before `B6`, a provisional position may be replaced without termination liability. After `B6`, a control change, dependency collision or site failure reopens the complete slate; the affected lot does not silently novate. Site stewards, builders, technical evaluators, the control registrar, optimization custodian, award authority, Evidence Court and comptroller remain separate.

The inherited USD 15.6B ceiling is unchanged. The workbook carries 102 lot ceilings, eighteen charter templates, 180 site-dimension records, 216 site gates, 204 bid positions, 2,856 admission decisions, forty-eight removal tests and nine release states. Every implementation field remains empty or zero.

The twenty-three-sheet model contains 31,426 live formulas and passes eighty-seven terminal audits and twenty detected-and-restored mutations. Every sheet renders cleanly after independent office recalculation, the native chart survives and the formula-error scan is empty.
