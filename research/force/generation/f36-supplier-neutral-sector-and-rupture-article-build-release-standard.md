# F36 supplier-neutral sector and rupture-article build-release standard

## Executive judgment

The next F36 acquisition object should not be a prototype pipeline and should not be a beauty contest among three descriptions of pipe. It should be a **controlled bank of full-diameter evidence articles** capable of answering the failure questions that govern a rapid water corridor: can an industrial root make a sanitary pressure boundary at mission rate, can a field process close that boundary repeatably, can two routes survive representative civil terrain without becoming one failure domain, and can a blinded team detect, isolate, repair, retest and release a catastrophic breach inside six hours?

This standard turns the Pass 100 range constitution into a supplier-neutral build-release package. It preserves two provisional build roots and one protected challenge root:

- `R1`, factory-formed sectional conduit with machine-executed closures;
- `R2`, a decomposed mobile continuous-forge process; and
- `R3`, a fold-flat structural-ribbon challenge root.

Supplier-neutral does not mean physically identical. The three roots may use different materials, wall systems, forming processes, joints, tools and deployment mechanics. They must share the public service boundary, hydraulic class, sanitary release law, route and damage worlds, evidence schema, custody, authority and reproduction burden. A government standard should make unlike mechanisms comparable without turning the incumbent mechanism into the standard.

The package creates seven article classes. The two build roots each receive 63 controlled article units across 22 lots. The challenge root receives 70 units across 22 lots because its fold, barrier and bonded-closure uncertainties require more material and process specimens before integrated-sector authority. The complete bank contains 196 units in 66 lots:

| Article class | `R1` | `R2` | `R3` | Governing question |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Material and barrier lots | 12 | 12 | 16 | Does the pressure and sanitary system retain properties after manufacture, exposure, cleaning and aging? |
| Full-thickness pressure sections | 8 | 8 | 12 | Does the actual wall system survive proof, vacuum, cyclic and local-load states? |
| Closure process-window articles | 24 | 24 | 24 | Can the root repeatedly make and inspect closures at the field boundary? |
| Paired one-kilometre sector articles | 4 | 4 | 2 | Can both routes be built through weak-soil and crossing worlds with independent failure domains? |
| Full-bore destructive repair articles | 8 | 8 | 10 | Can catastrophic damage be returned to lawful service inside six hours? |
| Cold-reproduction mission lots | 2 | 2 | 2 | Can a second team reproduce an accepted state from the public package? |
| Reference and quarantine sets | 5 | 5 | 4 | Can the program preserve untouched, failed and ambiguous states for comparison? |

The integrated route object is two separate one-kilometre reach articles. Each contains both half-demand mains, so the sector campaign installs four route-kilometres per root. `SA-1` combines saturated weak soil, imposed settlement, grade and high-rate placement. `SA-2` combines road and rail protection, a water/debris crossing, an exclusion zone and the receiver approach. These are not shortened demonstrations of the whole ten-kilometre mission. They are deliberately dense mechanism tests that precede the full corridor.

Six registered damage states govern the destructive bank: clean guillotine transection; jagged crush and ovalization; puncture with axial tear; closure pullout; contaminated breach; and pressure-bound settlement/bending. Two additional tests per build root are sealed combinations selected after the articles enter custody. The challenge root carries two more repeats because crease and bonded-repair behavior are additional uncertainty, not permission for a weaker threshold.

The modeled first-article tranche is USD 8.120 billion in constant planning dollars, 20.99 percent of the Pass 100 USD 38.684 billion F36 capital allocation. It is a cross-classification of already authorized family capital, not a new claim on the Department total. Mature annual article-bank, range, metrology, destructive-test and reproduction support is USD 0.446 billion inside the inherited USD 2.321 billion annual readiness allocation. Construction, payment, physical evidence, production and rendering remain held.

The decisive institutional result is a five-step authority separation:

```text
PACKAGE COMPLETE
        │ content exists; no physical credit
        ▼
BUILD RELEASED
        │ requirements, configuration, safety, evidence and fiscal authorities concur
        ▼
ARTICLE ACCEPTED
        │ a serial article satisfies its class acceptance decisions
        ▼
CAMPAIGN CLAIM ADMITTED
        │ representative event + original data + custody + independent judgment
        ▼
PRODUCTION / FORM AUTHORIZED
        independent second-team lot and full F36 mission still required
```

No step inherits authority from the one before it. A complete drawing package is not a released build. A released build is not an accepted article. An accepted article is not an admitted mission claim. An admitted sector claim is not production authority. None of these states authorizes an exterior rendering.

## 1. The decision this standard owns

Pass 100 established the complete F36 mission unit: a ten-kilometre service corridor with two route-separated 0.35-metre half-demand mains, twenty installed and four reserve pipe-kilometres, article-owned industrial process, route systems, power, sanitary release, a 10,000-cubic-metre receiver buffer, full-bore repair and regeneration. It also established the correct next experiment: representative full-diameter sectors and a destructive repair article.

This pass owns the layer between that mission constitution and metal, polymer or composite in the range. It freezes:

- what every root must hold common;
- what each root may solve differently;
- the article classes, quantities and lot partitions;
- the full-diameter service and structural envelope;
- the closure process-window evidence;
- the geometry and hazards of the two representative sectors;
- the damage states and six-hour repair clock;
- the instrument channels, uncertainty and original-data burden;
- serial configuration, custody and destructive disposition;
- acceptance authorities and stop powers;
- the complete technical-data and reproduction rights package;
- a forty-two-month first-article calendar; and
- the capital and annual-readiness boundary.

It does not select a material, vendor, detailed joint, production process, deployment vehicle or exterior system form. Those choices must emerge from evidence.

## 2. Supplier neutrality as a constitutional boundary

There are three bad ways to specify an ambitious public technology.

The first is to prescribe the familiar solution so completely that a novel root can compete only by imitating it. The second is to write a high-level performance sentence and allow every builder to choose a favorable test. The third is to declare interfaces open while the original provider controls the only fixtures, process data, calibration files and legal rights needed to reproduce them.

F36 uses a different division.

### 2.1 Common public invariants

Every root receives the same:

- 10,000 m³/day full service and 5,000 m³/day per-route service identity;
- `F36-350` hydraulic class;
- source, receiver, isolation and sample-port envelopes;
- 0.70 MPa maximum allowable working-pressure target, 0.85 MPa registered surge and 1.05 MPa proof state;
- minus 0.085 MPa collapse challenge;
- route, soil, crossing, contamination and receiver worlds;
- six-hour detect-to-release repair clock;
- water-release and invalid-state grammar;
- instrument uncertainty and original-record requirements;
- serial custody and destructive-disposition law; and
- second-team reproduction and data-rights burden.

### 2.2 Root-specific implementation space

Each root retains freedom over:

- material chemistry and layer stack;
- circular or pressure-stabilized non-circular transport form;
- factory, mobile-forming or payout process;
- wall thickness and reinforcement architecture;
- thermal, chemical, mechanical or hybrid closure process;
- distributed or concentrated restraint;
- deployment and retrieval machinery;
- embedded sensing that does not replace independent instrumentation; and
- repair geometry, provided the restored article meets the common release law.

The rule is simple: the public institution owns the question and the evidence needed to answer it. Builders own rival answers until those answers cross a safety, interface or reproduction boundary that the public must be able to exercise without their permission.

## 3. The `F36-350` full-bore service class

The class is defined by service behavior, not a prescribed resin or metal.

| Boundary | Frozen build-release target | Why it exists |
|---|---:|---|
| Flow area at service state | at least 0.0962 m² | preserves the 0.35 m half-demand hydraulic identity |
| Local area under registered support world | at least 95% of reference | prevents hidden collapse or severe ovalization |
| Maximum allowable working pressure | 0.70 MPa | bounds steady, elevation and operating margin |
| Registered surge | 0.85 MPa | tests transient restraint and closure behavior |
| Hydrostatic proof | 1.05 MPa for 60 minutes | applies 1.5× working-pressure proof to the full wall system |
| Negative pressure | −0.085 MPa for 30 minutes | tests pump trip, draining and vacuum collapse |
| Pressure cycling | 50,000 cycles, 0.05–0.70 MPa | exposes closure, layer and fatigue defects before a field campaign |
| Axial system restraint | 200 kN | exceeds the pressure-end load with structural allowance |
| Differential settlement | 300 mm over 10 m | represents damaged and saturated route support |
| Unsupported void | 2 m while pressurized | forces the system to declare bridging or support requirements |
| Local puncture challenge | 20 kN registered indenter | creates a comparable damage mechanism |
| Debris impact | 5 kJ registered striker | tests protection, damage detection and safe isolation |

These values are research targets, not adopted codes. The pressure class is intentionally above the 0.289 MPa Pass 100 reference head. The axial threshold follows from the pressure end load on a 0.0962 m² flow area and preserves additional margin. The challenge does not assume the conduit alone carries every load: a root may use distributed anchors, sleeves or external restraint if those systems remain inside its serial manifest and survive the route world.

Sanitary acceptance is conjunctive. A pressure test cannot compensate for a failed barrier or contaminated repair. Each material system must publish its analyte and migrant inventory; survive manufacture, storage, soil, fuel, decontaminant and disinfectant exposures; complete a registered cleaning sequence; preserve sample custody; and meet the receiver's public release limits on blind pre- and post-exposure samples. Any exceedance, unknown material identity, missing sample interval or unexplained calibration drift creates an invalid state rather than a passing zero.

## 4. Seven article classes and sixty-six lots

An article is a serial physical unit. A lot is a separately constituted custody population made under one frozen material, process, software and facility state. Quantity is deliberately counted both ways because a program can possess many specimens while still possessing only one industrial event.

### 4.1 Material and barrier lots (`MB`)

`MB` lots contain coupons and layer specimens drawn across the beginning, middle and end of an accepted production run. They establish composition, thickness, bond, tensile and tear state, permeability, migration, cleaning exposure, environmental aging and forensic reference. `R3` receives sixteen units because folds, creases and multilayer bonds add distinct states.

### 4.2 Full-thickness pressure sections (`PS`)

These are complete wall systems at full diameter, including seams, reinforcement and production transitions. They face proof, negative pressure, pressure cycling, local support loss and destructive sectioning. A scaled tube does not qualify.

### 4.3 Closure process-window articles (`CW`)

The closure is treated as an industrial process, not a craft demonstration. Twenty-four articles per root cover low, nominal and high process-window corners; cold and hot ambient states; clean and contaminated preparation; new and substituted operators; equipment loss; and blinded nondestructive inspection. Each closure record binds tools, energy, surface state, recipe, operator, time, geometry, inspection and final disposition.

### 4.4 Paired one-kilometre sector articles (`SA`)

Each sector article is one route-kilometre at full diameter. Two units constitute the paired routes in a reach. Each build root therefore receives four units: two routes in `SA-1` and two routes in `SA-2`. `R3` receives two units initially and may build the second pair only after its material, fold and closure gates close; the modeled quantity preserves those conditional units inside its protected challenge ceiling.

### 4.5 Destructive repair articles (`DR`)

Each `DR` unit is a 120-metre full-bore cell with two independently powered isolation boundaries, known upstream and downstream water states, exchangeable ground and access beds, complete independent instrumentation and a receiver-release point. Damage is applied after steady credited service. The six-hour clock starts at the first independently detected excursion, not when the repair team is told where the cut is.

### 4.6 Cold-reproduction lots (`CR`)

The second team receives the controlled package, public fixtures, registered materials and qualified facilities, but no discretionary assistance from the original provider. It must create the same accepted decision, not merely a visually similar object. One lot reproduces pressure and closure acceptance; the second reproduces a paired sector and one destructive repair.

### 4.7 Reference and quarantine sets (`RQ`)

Untouched reference material, accepted witness sections, destructive remains and ambiguous exposed articles remain physically separated. A failed article is public technical capital. Repairing, cleaning or discarding it before the independent forensic decision destroys the counterfactual.

## 5. Two representative reach articles

The full ten-kilometre corridor remains the eventual mission. The first integrated articles compress its decisive mechanisms into two reproducible one-kilometre reaches.

### `SA-1`: ground-state and rate reach

Each of the two routes contains:

- 300 m of saturated weak soil with controlled bearing and groundwater;
- 200 m of imposed differential settlement, including a 2 m support void;
- 200 m of sustained 3 percent grade with high-point air and low-point drain behavior; and
- 300 m of open high-rate placement and retrieval.

The routes maintain 50 m separation except where the range registers physically distinct soil cells. One route receives an unannounced settlement event while the other remains in service. The article must expose route preparation, support, restraint, monitoring, access and repair burden at the same time that it measures placement rate.

### `SA-2`: crossing, exclusion and receiver reach

Each route contains four 250 m zones:

- a protected road crossing;
- an unlike rail-crossing solution;
- a water/debris crossing with impact and retrieval access; and
- a controlled-exclusion and receiver-approach zone with sanitary transfer, pressure control and accessible outlet boundary.

The two routes may not use the same decisive sleeve, bridge, anchor, lifting point, access lane or receiver completion hardware. A common flange or data schema is acceptable. A common physical completion kit whose loss disables both routes is not.

The sector articles include no borrowed grid, earthmoving equipment, joining cell, crane, laboratory, receiver pump or operator. The range supplies terrain, hazard insertion, safety control and independent measurement.

## 6. Destructive states and the six-hour clock

The destructive program uses six disclosed families and two sealed combinations for each build root.

| State | Physical insertion | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `D01` clean transection | complete guillotine separation | detection, isolation, end preparation, replacement and release |
| `D02` jagged crush | excavation strike, ovalization and embedded debris | damaged-length determination and removal boundary |
| `D03` puncture and tear | 20 kN puncture followed by axial propagation | leak localization and tear arrest |
| `D04` closure pullout | axial load through a credited closure | restraint failure recognition and root-cause custody |
| `D05` contaminated breach | full-bore opening with registered nonpathogenic chemical and particulate surrogates | exclusion, decontamination, flushing, blind assay and lawful restart |
| `D06` settlement while pressurized | 300 mm imposed displacement with support loss | service-state monitoring, isolation and structural requalification |
| `D07–D08` sealed combinations | two coupled states selected after custody transfer | resistance to test-specific preparation |

The detect-to-release interval contains eight conserved segments: independent detection; safe stop; isolation and buffer transition; access; removal and preparation; closure; proof and flush; and sanitary/receiver release. The six-hour total may not be met by starting access work before independent detection, stopping the clock during blind assay, or returning water before the configuration and release records close.

Full service continues from the receiver buffer during the repair interval. If repair exceeds six hours, the experiment records both the missed repair gate and the remaining full-service buffer. After the twenty-four-hour buffer boundary, the surviving route must still provide 5,000 m³/day.

## 7. Instrument and uncertainty constitution

Embedded root sensors may support operation, but they do not admit the claim. The independent evidence plane includes pressure, flow, geometry, strain, ground state, water quality, energy, process state, time and configuration custody.

The common minimum for each paired one-kilometre reach is:

| Channel family | Minimum channels | Design expanded uncertainty or resolution |
|---|---:|---|
| pressure and transient | 24 | `U95 ≤ 0.010 MPa` with high-rate transient capture |
| route flow and volume | 8 | `U95 ≤ 2.5%` of reading |
| conduit geometry | 32 | `U95 ≤ 2 mm` local dimension |
| axial/circumferential strain | 32 | `U95 ≤ 50 µε` |
| soil pressure and displacement | 40 | `U95 ≤ 2%` load; `≤ 5 mm` displacement |
| crossing load and pose | 24 | `U95 ≤ 2%` load; `≤ 0.1°` pose |
| leak/acoustic event | 24 | independently measured localization error `≤ 5 m` |
| water-quality and blind sample state | 20 | method-specific uncertainty below one-half of the decision margin |
| process and closure state | 24 | registered parameter resolution and invalid-state flag |
| electrical energy and power | 16 | `U95 ≤ 2%` energy; registered peak capture |
| denied-time and event order | 8 | relative event-order uncertainty `≤ 1 s` |
| configuration and custody witness | 16 | zero unidentified serial transitions |

Every channel has a measurand, location, range, sampling plan, calibration genealogy, invalid-state grammar, pre/post check and retained original. Summary files, dashboards and builder telemetry are derivative products. The original record remains local through network denial and carries enough metadata for a second team to recreate the decision.

## 8. Configuration custody

The configuration unit is the serial article plus its production and evidence state. Its minimum identity contains:

1. root, article class, lot and serial;
2. material and barrier passport;
3. facility, tool, fixture and process baseline;
4. software, firmware, recipe and calibration hashes;
5. operator and substitution record;
6. assembly, closure and inspection chronology;
7. as-built mass, dimensions and reference geometry;
8. exposure, cleaning, damage and repair history;
9. blind-sample and original-data custody;
10. deviation, concession and invalid-state history; and
11. final disposition as reference, accepted, destructive, quarantined, forensic, reproduced or discarded.

Custody transfers occur at build release, lot acceptance, transport, range receipt, blind damage, repair release, forensic opening and final disposition. Each transfer requires two-party serial reconciliation. A software update, material substitution, altered closure recipe, reused destructive section or unrecorded cleaning exposure creates a new configuration and reopens dependent decisions.

## 9. Acceptance authorities

F36 separates the powers to ask, build, operate, measure and release.

| Authority | Sole decision | May stop? | May award success? |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| F36 Program Office | requirement and package configuration | yes | package only |
| Industrial-root builder | build execution and declared conformity | self-stop | no |
| Range Safety Authority | personnel, pressure, energy and environmental safety | yes | no |
| Materials and Structural Authority | material, wall, closure and load acceptance | yes | article class only |
| Water Release Authority | sanitary stop, flush, assay and restart | yes | potable-release claim only |
| Independent Metrology Authority | channels, uncertainty, calibration and invalid intervals | yes | measurement admissibility only |
| Receiver Authority | local consent, pressure boundary and public-service release | yes | receiver acceptance only |
| Configuration and Custody Authority | serial identity, deviations and disposition | yes | configuration validity only |
| Independent Evidence Board | claim admission against the frozen event | yes | campaign claim only |
| Cost and Rights Authority | price, milestone artifact and rights completion | yes | payment only |
| Production Release Board | serial procurement after independent mission evidence | yes | production only |

No authority may borrow another authority's conclusion. The water authority cannot accept a structurally unidentified repair. The evidence board cannot waive receiver consent. The cost authority cannot pay for a dated milestone when the controlled article or rights package is absent. The program office cannot convert a useful failure into a passing claim.

## 10. Complete technical-data and rights package

Supplier neutrality is operational only if a qualified second team can execute the decision. Each root must deliver sixteen controlled data classes:

- public service and interface specification;
- complete bill of materials and material passports;
- bill of process, tooling and facility states;
- closure recipe and bounded process window;
- source, receiver, isolation and repair interface dataset;
- as-designed and as-built geometry;
- load, pressure, surge, vacuum and support cases;
- sanitary hazard, migration, cleaning and release plan;
- instrument, calibration and uncertainty plan;
- software source, firmware, build environment and software bill of materials;
- operating, stop, repair, retrieval and regeneration procedures;
- inspection, nondestructive examination and reject grammar;
- serial configuration and deviation ledger;
- raw evidence schema, time base and export definition;
- sub-tier ownership, process ancestry and capacity map; and
- teardown, limited-purpose manufacture, second-team reproduction, interface and evidence-data rights.

Root-specific know-how may remain controlled. It may not be discretionary. The Department must retain a perpetual public-service license sufficient to inspect, repair, compete, reproduce, qualify a second source and sustain an accepted article after provider withdrawal or ownership change. A black-box failure without teardown and reproduction rights is not an acquired learning artifact.

The overlap is deliberate, but article entry remains predecessor-controlled. Integrated sectors cannot receive credit before the relevant material, pressure, closure, metrology, safety and custody decisions close. The challenge root may continue coupon and full-bore work after a failure; it cannot use novelty to bypass sector entry conditions.

Payment follows accepted artifacts, not elapsed months. A useful negative result can earn the declared learning milestone when the article, original record, custody, teardown and data rights are complete. It cannot earn acceptance credit for the failed requirement.

The research package closes gates 1–9 at planning level. Gate 10 remains open because no constituted authority has approved an actual solicitation or safety case. Gates 11–14 remain open because no physical article exists. Therefore:

- package drafting and supplier engagement: `AUTHORIZED`;
- construction release: `HOLD`;
- article acceptance: `OPEN`;
- campaign evidence: `OPEN`;
- production release: `HOLD`; and
- exterior rendering: `BLOCKED`.

This is not bureaucratic caution. It preserves the difference between a rigorous plan and a demonstrated machine.

## 14. Technology programs created by the standard

The article bank exposes a portfolio of invention rather than one pipe procurement.

### High-rate machine closure

`R1` requires closure cells that align, prepare, join, inspect and serialize hundreds of interfaces without converting speed into latent leakage. That is a robotics, machine-tool, nondestructive-evaluation and configuration-computing program.

### Mobile continuous acceptance

`R2` requires decomposed forming cells that make accepted pressure boundary after cold start while measuring geometry, cooling, barrier state and material identity. The decisive advance is continuous acceptance, not merely continuous extrusion.

### Structural potable ribbon

`R3` requires a new materials system that can fold densely, deploy repeatedly, hold pressure, resist puncture and soil, retain a low-migration barrier, accept bonded repair and expose its state to independent inspection. Crease fatigue and barrier integrity must be solved as one system.

### Robotic civil-route production

The sector reaches require coordinated machines for survey, weak-soil preparation, support placement, crossing construction, conduit handling, anchoring, retrieval and access preservation. The program should develop a workfront system with multiple specialized machines rather than a single theatrical robot.

### Field metrology and water-release science

The independent evidence plane requires rugged pressure-flow standards, traveling geometry references, blind water-assay custody, locally retained originals, denied-time reconstruction and portable uncertainty evaluation. That infrastructure becomes useful across the Department's water, power, medical, fire and civil-engineering families.

### Full-bore robotic repair

The damage cells turn repair into a conserved industrial sequence. Detection, isolation, access, removal, closure, proof, flushing, assay and configuration return must be accelerated together. A fast clamp that cannot restore potable custody is not the target.

## 15. What changed and what comes next

### Now authoritative

The first integrated F36 hardware campaign consists of two paired one-kilometre reach articles and a separately instrumented 120-metre destructive-repair class. Supplier neutrality resides in common service, hazard, interface, evidence, custody and rights boundaries while materials and physical mechanisms remain root-specific.

### Falsified

Four shortcuts are no longer acceptable:

- one generic kilometre can represent route independence;
- identical joints or materials are required for comparability;
- a scaled tube can establish full-bore structural and sanitary behavior; or
- a builder-owned sensor and summary report can admit the builder's claim.

### Still provisional

All pressure, cycle, load, settlement, impact, uncertainty, quantity, duration and cost values are build-release research targets. They require review by constituted materials, structural, sanitary, metrology, safety, receiver and fiscal authorities before an actual construction release. No root has supplied hardware or measured performance.

### Burden created by success

This standard converts the F36 question from “what should the pipeline look like?” to “can three industrial roots deliver a serial, destructible and independently reproducible public-service boundary?” That demands public fixtures, range cells, blind damage teams, material and water laboratories, protected article arsenals, technical-data custody and second-team pilot lines. Those institutions are part of the force.

### Next experiment

The next pass should freeze the **closure-kernel and full-bore damage-cell protocols** at executable test-step resolution. It should specify physical fixtures, fixture neutrality, process-window sampling, pressure/transient waveforms, blind defect insertion, contaminated-breach surrogates, water-release assay timing, exact clock segmentation, forensic teardown, statistical acceptance and the minimum measured internal sections that could later support a requirements diagram. Exterior rendering remains blocked.

## Authority and limitations

This is a first-principles force-design standard under the project's full-authorization assumption. It does not analyze current statutes, politics or procurement culture. It uses internal program lineage rather than external sites. Its thresholds are prospective research requirements, not adopted engineering or drinking-water standards.

The companion workbook owns the article counts, common envelopes, channel and data calculations, schedule, capital cross-classification, annual readiness allocation, release logic and deterministic audits. The paper owns the institutional interpretation. Neither constitutes physical evidence.

The workbook contains twenty sheets and thirty terminal controls. Every control passes in the restored baseline, the final formula-error scan is empty and all twenty sheets have been rendered and visually inspected. Six disposable perturbations confirm that fiscal imbalance and missing article units fail the package, while authority, article, production and rendering inputs cannot bypass later evidence states.
