## Decision

Issue **Artifact Validity Constitution 1 (`AVC-1`)**. It creates two warrants that no office may combine:

1. an **Artifact Validity Warrant (`AVW`)**, issued by a root-native Artifact Court after independent reconstruction; and
2. a **Finance Disbursement Warrant (`FDW`)**, issued against one accepted milestone and its remaining ceiling.

Validity answers a narrow question: can an independent party identify the source, reconstruct the claimed design result, expose its assumptions and unknowns, trace it to the frozen destructive event and confirm its rights, safety, receiver and residual obligations? It does not answer whether the predicted machine works.

Five alternatives were tested. Builder self-certification fails independence. A central whole-form board creates a hidden selection forum. Hash-only automation proves identity but not meaning. Evidence Court acceptance converts design into observation. Only federated package validity keeps technical judgment at the package boundary while separating replay, acceptance and payment.

## The public artifact estate

`AVC-1` requires capabilities that ordinary contract administration does not possess:

- **content-addressed source capsules** carrying authorship, configuration, native tools, dependencies, material assumptions and machine-readable unknowns;
- **deterministic native replay** inside a sealed execution environment;
- **clean-room reconstruction** by a separately controlled cell using the public interface contract rather than the builder's tacit knowledge;
- a **causal trace graph** connecting every design artifact to its event, denominator, interface, safe state, receiver and reconstruction claim;
- a **performance-blind semantic firewall** that permits source custody and safety classification while denying sibling cells access to geometry, implementation, cost, schedule, maturity and relative performance; and
- an append-only **Public Artifact Exchange** that preserves superseded and failed records instead of letting a contractor overwrite lineage.

This is an enabling technology program in its own right. The Department must develop reproducible engineering environments, dependency capture for physical systems, machine-verifiable rights and residual ledgers, cross-tool semantic comparison and disconnected public archives. A common file format is insufficient, and a common solver would recreate the technical monoculture the rival-root program exists to prevent.

## Twelve noncompensatory gates

Each of the thirty packages receives twelve gates: source manifest; frozen configuration hash; executable native artifact; dependency, tool and material ledger; assumptions and unknowns; independent clean-room replay; interface and destructive-test trace; rights and safe-state closure; receiver and residual closure; challenge response and terminal archive; no sibling performance exposure; and separated builder-validity control.

The first ten govern artifact completeness. Failure returns the package for rework and releases no payment. The last two are constitutional. Performance leakage or common beneficial, technical, key, model, personnel or acceptance control terminates the affected form cell. Its unearned authority returns to protected custody and cannot accelerate another rival. If the Department cannot constitute all six root courts and reconstruction cells without shared control, `AVA` itself must be killed; a national validation prime is not an admissible fallback.

Today only the two constitutional preconditions are present for each package: sixty of 360 gate records pass and 300 remain open. No package or milestone is accepted.
