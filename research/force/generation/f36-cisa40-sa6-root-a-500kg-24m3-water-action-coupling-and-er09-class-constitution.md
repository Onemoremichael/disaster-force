# RBP-1 — Root A 500 kg / 24 m³ water-action coupling and ER-09 class constitution

## Decision abstract

`RBP-1` defines the ninth physical rung of the `AM-06` entry ladder created by [RBG-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-am06-source-water-ignition-entry-ladder-reset.md). It begins only after the 250-kilogram / 12-cubic-metre coupling class in [RBN-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-250kg-12m3-water-action-coupling-and-er07-class-constitution.md) and the 500-kilogram dry field-time class in [RBO-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-500kg-dry-source-field-time-atmosphere-and-er08-class-constitution.md) are physically accepted. Its question is:

> What complete coupling architecture can introduce 24 cubic metres of reference water into an independently verified 500-kilogram field, preserve both qualified water morphologies and the selected dry architecture, observe the evolving space-time intersection of water and source, control at least 15.768 cubic metres of necessarily liquid water, close seven independent physical roots, end safely and return after 1,008 hours?

The global water-to-source ratio remains 0.048 cubic metre per kilogram. The event does not remain similar. Relative to `ER-07`, source and water double while active area rises only 25 percent. Mean source depth, source per area, water per area, water volume flux and water mass flux each rise 60 percent. Equivalent field boundary rises only 11.8 percent, so both source energy and water inventory per boundary metre rise 78.9 percent.

The water issue is a 23.952-tonne controlled mass delivered at 0.2 cubic metre or 199.6 kilograms per second over 120 seconds. Heating it from the registered 20-degree-Celsius state to 100 degrees and vaporizing it would require 62,080.710 megajoules, 2.915 times the 21,294-megajoule source-energy screen. Even the impossible limit in which all source energy enters the water can vaporize only 8.232 cubic metres. At least 15.768 cubic metres remains liquid before condensate, retained inventory, public water, rain, wash water or uncertainty enters the account.

The inherited 40-cubic-metre normal receiver exposes the scale break. After the nominal 24-cubic-metre issue, it has 16 cubic metres of unused geometric volume. That is only 0.232 cubic metre above the minimum liquid remainder under the source-energy ceiling. A named 40-cubic-metre tank therefore provides almost no demonstrated margin for retained water, condensate, displaced source, foam, public intervention or measurement uncertainty. `RBP-1` does not declare it adequate. Receiver adequacy becomes a physical qualification result.

Accepted parent systems cannot be connected by an untested adapter. `RBP-1` creates four rival `CXT-24` coupling architectures around the frozen selected `FGT-625` field and `WIX-12` interface. `VXM-24` uses a volumetric cross-phase matrix. `PTR-24` uses protected tracer-transit reconstruction. `SBN-24` uses a surface-and-boundary flux network. `RCA-24` uses recoverable contact-core arrays. Each must carry both inherited dispersed and coherent water morphologies without changing the dry field, wet-interface mechanism, source inventory, ignition opportunity or parent root versions.

Two independent builder lineages at three campuses produce twenty-four complete coupling articles. Each article executes twelve nonenergetic wet trials, six under each morphology, for 288 candidate trials. All six architecture pairs then execute twenty-four blinded comparisons, producing 144 matched comparisons. Candidate and comparison work governs 162 tonnes of source-like simulant and 7,200 cubic metres of reference water before any architecture may populate the coupling-module bank.

Sixteen serviceable `AWC-24` modules execute 128 eight-state qualification cycles. The bridge separates dry docking, the accepted 250-kilogram / 12-cubic-metre anchor, field expansion, source increase at fixed water, water increase at fixed source, the exact 500-kilogram / 24-cubic-metre state, coupled timing and receiver perturbation, and root or service denial with full return. Every possible class event binds eighteen propositions, 144 gates, 576 evidence records, 1,536 active channels, forty safeing paths, five conserved terminal accounts, one contact ledger, one material-exposure ledger, two outside-origin reconstructions and a fixed 1,008-hour return.

`RBP-1` adds two physical truth roots. `AQU-24` owns the water field: identity, mass, swept volume, morphology, velocity, impulse, occupied volume and terminal water position. `COU-500W` owns the cross-phase interface: contact location, residence, wetting, rebound, penetration, displacement, entrainment, vapor-front passage and source-bearing-water departure. ## 2. Why composition is not yet coupling

RBN-1 can establish a selected water morphology and wet interface at 250 kilograms. RBO-1 can establish a selected dry field-time architecture at 500 kilograms. Those results do not establish that the two selected systems preserve each other's causal state when joined.

The combined event introduces at least six new interactions:

1. the wet interface can alter dry source topology before or during contact;
2. the dry field can destroy the water morphology that the interface creates;
3. water can move source into a region outside either parent's measurement field;
4. steam, aerosols and condensate can couple previously independent root paths;
5. receiver restriction can change field behavior upstream; and
6. the two-minute issue can create a liquid state that persists through a roughly twenty-four-minute source-energy interval and a much longer cooldown.

An interface compatibility drawing cannot answer these questions. They require a new physical architecture and a new evidence population.

Its scientific value is direct depth-specific physical evidence and preserved post-event specimens. Its principal risks are intrusive alteration, thermal conduction, preferential water paths, incomplete recovery and a field shaped around the measurement system.

## 11. Water-energy and phase limits

The registered sensible-plus-latent screen is:

\[
23{,}952\times2.59188=62{,}080.710\ \text{MJ}.
\]

The source lower-heating-value screen is 21,294 megajoules. The water heat-plus-vapor capacity is therefore 2.915 times the source-energy input.

\[
21{,}294/2.59188=8{,}215.658\ \text{kg}=8.232\ \text{m}^3.
