# Pass 192 — Synchronized formation-generation campaign

**Research object:** `DFG-2`

**Verdict:** **CLOSE**

## Branch and resolution

The deepest live branch is `DFG-2`, the second of five permitted Department force-generation passes.

**Resolution test:** reversing the build sequence would change whether the nation receives a balanced force or disconnected fleets, the timing of USD 9.41T in formation capital, the creation of 11.5 million affiliated billets and when the national promise can exist; the object passes at Department scale.

Pass 191 defined what a formation is. This pass defines how thousands can be generated without letting visible machines outrun crews, receivers, evidence or the least glamorous family.[^1]

## Decision: a balanced family-cohort braid

Five architectures face eight conjunctive gates. Article-first procurement creates parked equipment. Service-by-service buildup creates unusable seams. District autarkies duplicate scarce systems yet cannot project them. One national big-bang exercise discovers systemic failure after capital is sunk. Only a **balanced family-cohort braid** advances all forty-eight families, seven districts, crews, receivers, joint stacks and replacement paths together.

The braid has four overlapping movements:

1. **Seed lattice, years 1–3.** Establish 336 family–district paths: one for every family in every readiness district. Critical crews, household continuity and named receivers must lead article acceptance.
2. **Protected-force expansion, years 4–7.** Scale balanced family cohorts toward protected demand. No family may borrow success from another.
3. **National concurrency, years 8–10.** Cross the 3,667-formation instantaneous ready obligation while closing all sixty mission-system/campaign cells.
4. **Inventory closure, years 11–12.** Complete all 5,251 serials, then run all 384 intact and district-loss family cases as one synchronized proof.

Years 13–25 become a regeneration service. A replacement formation enters before its predecessor leaves the ready pool. Exercises, overhaul, exports and partner missions may not consume the protected floor.

## Campaign law

Every production year advances all forty-eight families. Scale is apportioned by each family’s inherited inventory, capital and billet burden, with exact final reconciliation. A formation cohort cannot advance unless its crew package, receiver path, sustainment set and evidence capacity advance with it. This converts the familiar “build the platform, add support later” model into one indivisible production object.

Geography is also constitutive. The 336 seeds are not token regional detachments; each is a recoverable family path with crew authority, receiver interface and district production access. Expansion remains round-robin so a national average cannot hide a missing family in one loss world.

Joint closure is deliberately late enough to contain real formations and early enough to change production. Six mission systems in ten campaign worlds produce sixty cells. Every cell requires interface evidence, a concurrent trial and civil admission. Year 12 then tests the complete family inventory against all 384 `N−1` cases concurrently. A failure quarantines the affected cohort and lineage; favorable results elsewhere cannot average it away.

## Scale and industrial consequence

The eleven-year reference build creates 528 family-year cohorts and closes exactly at 5,251 serials. It carries USD 9.41T of formation capital and 11.5 million direct affiliated billets. The steepest reference year reaches about USD 1.51T of formation capital. That is not a recommendation to appropriate USD 1.51T in one year; it is evidence that an eleven-year force build is a mobilization economy, not an acquisition program hidden inside the USD 1.00T technology fund.[^2]

The model therefore keeps three ledgers separate: technology and proving, formation capital, and recurring operations. It releases zero authority. All crew, receiver, trial and admission identities remain source-empty.

A twenty-year reference life would require about 263 complete replacement serials, USD 471B of formation-capital throughput and about 573,000 affiliated billet renewals annually. The values are sensitivities, not selected service lives. Their implication is constitutional: replacement factories, academies, receiver rehearsals and proving lanes are permanent operational formations.

The enabling technology includes formation-flow compilers, crew-before-metal academies, synthetic receiver districts, autonomous acceptance convoys, dynamic ready-floor governors, cross-district serial exchange, concurrent national proving fabric, cold replacement cells, human-continuity twins and adverse-cohort quarantine. These systems make force generation observable under denial rather than merely faster.
