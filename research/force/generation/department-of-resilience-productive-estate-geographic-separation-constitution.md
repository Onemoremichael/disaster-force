# Department of Resilience productive-estate geographic separation constitution

**Pass:** 189

**Research object:** `MPC-4`

**Decision date:** 11 September 2026

**Verdict:** **CLOSE**

## Frontier and resolution

The deepest live branch is `MPC-4`, the fourth of five permitted market-and-production passes; `MPC-3` qualified the campaign design but left seventy-two private sites and twenty-four public cells spatially ungoverned.

**Resolution test:** reversing this topology would change whether the Department owns a nationally survivable productive estate or ninety-six nominal campuses that a correlated regional failure can erase, so the object passes at Department scale.

Pass 188 proved that every production route requires its own nominal, denial and blind-replay events and that public capacity must carry six-system cohorts.[^1] It did not say where those routes can exist. Distance alone is not independence: two facilities can occupy different states yet share the same power restoration chain, drainage basin, skilled-labor market, freight gateway, timing service, supplier ring or emergency authority.

## Decision: an eight-by-three productive estate

Five architectures face twelve noncompensatory gates. Reusing incumbent clusters, assigning one region to each mission system and building twenty-four root parks all preserve decisive common causes. Ungoverned ninety-six-site dispersal creates the right count but cannot prove the relationships. Only an **eight-macro-theater by three-productive-district constitution** passes all gates.

A macro-theater is not a political region. It is a planning slot whose hazards, power restoration, water and thermal sources, freight gateways, labor market, communications, emergency authority, decisive suppliers, metrology and tool-regeneration chains must be shown independent of the other seven slots. Its real boundary and identity remain `SOURCE EMPTY`.

Each of the twenty-four productive districts contains four campuses: one public adaptive-production cell and three private survivor sites belonging to three other roots. Each macro-theater therefore contains three districts, three public cells and nine private campuses—twelve campuses total. The national estate contains ninety-six physical roles: twenty-four public and seventy-two private.

Every root occupies five different macro-theaters: three private sites and two public cold-production cells. No macro contains two roles from the same root. A complete macro loss therefore removes at most one role from any root: at least two private sites and one public path survive.

## Capacity is spatially overbuilt

Three private addresses do not create recovery capacity. The private network installs 150 percent of target production rate—50 percent at each campus—while normal work is distributed 40/35/25 percent. Loss of any campus leaves two sites and 100 percent installed rate. The public cell retains Pass 187’s six quarter-rate bays: 150 percent installed rate and 125 percent after one bay loss.[^2]

Every campus is also a service island. It must safe and preserve controlled work without the host utility, then meet the inherited seventy-two-hour private or 168-hour public black-start clock. Root-native power, water, thermal, waste, protection, process control and metrology cannot depend on a vendor cloud or on a decisive chain used by another campus role. Adaptable public bays may share a shell; they may not mix configuration ancestry.

Transport obeys the same rule. Each campus needs two unrelated heavy surface approaches, one root-appropriate gateway family and a rehearsed path for its largest tooling capsule, critical lot and complete-unit module. Shared corridors may supply throughput but never count as independence. Finished factory output earns zero until it can reach an accepted receiver.

## Exact public-path survivability

The intuitive cyclic placement was rejected: it survived macro loss but forced three active public paths into one surviving theater for some cohorts. The selected placement distributes each system’s four cells across different theaters and balances three cells in every theater.

The model enumerates all 4,096 choices of one failed root per mission system against loss of each of eight macro-theaters—32,768 cases. In 22,106 cases, the six surviving public paths can be assigned one per theater. The remaining 10,662 require at most two in one theater. No case requires three, and no failed root loses both public paths. A macro contains fifteen root-role exposures, all belonging to different roots.

This is a combinatorial proof, not geographic evidence. Site coordinates, titles, permits, operators, utilities, carrier paths, workforces and physical observations remain blank. Any substitution reruns every affected root, decisive-chain and cohort test and reopens Pass 188 qualification.

## Capital and industrial innovation

The inherited USD 250B public productive estate is conserved.[^2] Half is common platform capital; 25 percent follows governing root mass, 15 percent labor intensity and 10 percent process energy. The eight macro-theaters contain USD 28.9–33.3B each; loss of the largest places 13.3 percent of the public estate at risk without defeating a root. Private-site capitalization remains inside Root House contest and production instruments and is not charged again here.[^3]

The topology demands a technology program, not conventional real estate: a continental correlated-hazard twin; islandable industrial utilities; transportable heavy-production bays; autonomous oversized logistics; portable credential, housing and family-support systems; distributed metrology; rapid utility interties; climate-adaptive industrial shells; supplier-ancestry compilation; disconnected production control; receiver-compatible modularization; and self-repairing productive districts. A program dies if it creates the common dependency it claims to cure.

Independent recalculation preserves every audit and reports no formula errors. Released authority remains zero.
