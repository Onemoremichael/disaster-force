## Decision

Build a **federation of sovereign truth trains on a thin public substrate**. Commonality is limited to boundaries that protect people, evidence and neighboring property. Every load, actuator, working fluid, candidate-facing utility, decisive sensor, clock, safeing path, calibration chain and regeneration tool that can affect an endpoint remains native to one system, one station role and one method lineage.

A universal national shell fails: its utilities, controls and protection plant could manufacture correlated success. Six integrated system campuses fail because one casualty can erase challenge, control and reconstruction together. Eighteen unrelated monoliths preserve separation but make methods nonportable and spend the estate on duplicated civil works. Simulation-first cannot establish damage, residual or recovery truth. The selected architecture shares only nondecisive civil interfaces and preserves physical plurality where causation occurs.

## The substrate is a boundary, not a machine

Every station must supply eight public boundaries: a surveyed datum field; reversible hardpoints; separately isolable raw-service entries; protected refuge and rescue ingress; material and data custody portals; environmental containment; residual quarantine; and an emergency stop that removes energy without completing candidate safeing. Geometry and capacity remain rig-native.

The governing test is counterfactual: **if a common element can improve an endpoint, determine its value, keep the candidate operating or place it in its claimed safe state, that element is not substrate.** It must split into independent root-native implementations. A substrate failure may invalidate or halt an event; it may never make one pass.

## Eighteen nuclei and 144 physical methods

Each system receives physically separated challenge, matched-control and cold-reconstruction nuclei. The challenge nucleus owns the denied-condition inquiry and injects the governing loss. The control nucleus reproduces load and receiver burden without that loss. The reconstruction nucleus gives a second team only frozen public records and requires it to rebuild, calibrate and replay the claimed chain.

Each nucleus begins with one active lane and one regeneration lane. Its active lane contains four receiver-state bays. Each bay contains two unlike physical realizations of the station’s inquiry, so every nucleus holds eight realization paths. The arithmetic is exact: eighteen active lanes × four states × two lineages = 144 realizations; eighteen regeneration lanes produce thirty-six initial lanes. The fifty-four expansion lanes remain unbuilt.[^1]

The two lineages in a bay may share units, record fields and public connection geometry. They may not share decisive sensors, calibration artifacts, actuation physics, runtime, signer, build compiler or failure-recovery tool. Disagreement produces `HOLD`, never an average.

## Regeneration and industrial consequence

Every regeneration lane must independently perform four functions: isolate and decontaminate; disassemble and diagnose; rebuild or replace; and recalibrate, witness and return. It must recover all eight realization paths and its role-native rig boundary after one destructive event without borrowing decisive tooling from a sibling nucleus. Regeneration is not an alternate event lane and earns no endpoint credit.

This configuration directs the eighteen inherited technology campaigns toward physical inventions rather than generic facilities: dynamic hydrostatic loading, reversible occupied-structure attachment, plume-state tomography, high-duty thermal flight, rapid subsurface characterization, closed heat–water–brine metabolism, clinical load synthesis, sterile counterflow automation, and disconnected material qualification. Common data semantics remain public; common autonomy does not.[^3]

The USD 12.0B public-method reserve is divided equally across six systems and eighteen nuclei: USD 2.00B per system and USD 0.667B per nucleus. This is a loss-containment reservation, not a price forecast. With the selected private options, system ceilings are USD 2.50B entry, 2.52B civil terrain, 2.31B hazard control, 2.90B lifelines, 2.86B human continuity and 2.50B regeneration. They reconcile to the inherited USD 15.6B ceiling. No station may borrow another system’s reservation, and unused authority returns rather than financing a survivor.[^4]
