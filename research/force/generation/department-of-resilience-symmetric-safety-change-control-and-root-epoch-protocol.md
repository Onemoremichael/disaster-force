## Resolution test

If this result were reversed, the Department would either expose later rivals to a known hazard or disclose the early form's performance and implementation to them, changing the fairness and safety of the first physical portfolio.

The deepest active branch is `FSA`, with two prior passes consumed. It passes the resolution test because cross-cohort information determines whether the three-wave architecture can operate at all without invalidating the selection covenant.

## Decision

Adopt `CP4`, a content-minimized **Root Safety Change Warrant** (`RSCW`) and root-epoch protocol. An immediate protective stop may cross the cohort firewall. A design requirement may cross only when four independent authorities can express it as the same normative safety burden for all three siblings without exposing the source result.

“Symmetric” does not mean identical hardware. That would force pelagic exchange, occupied-boundary insertion, atmospheric intervention, geothermal access, clinical circulation and field regeneration toward common form. Symmetry means:

- the same protected subject and harm mechanism;
- the same threshold, unit and acceptance test;
- the same information envelope and effective root epoch;
- the same post-epoch qualification clock;
- no charge to competitive budgets; and
- no evidence or selection credit.

Three isolated translation cells implement that common burden differently for forms `A`, `B` and `C`. The source team receives no authority over a sibling.

## The information firewall

Every shared warrant carries twelve fields: warrant and root IDs; hazard class; protected subject; harm mechanism; threshold and unit; common acceptance test; effective epoch; the complete three-sibling set; clock treatment; expiry and review; and four approvals.

Ten fields are prohibited from the shared payload: source form, source contractor, raw telemetry, relative performance, rank, cost or schedule result, geometry, implementation suggestion, maturity claim and selection recommendation. They remain sealed with the source record. The propagated warrant describes a constraint, never how the source failed or how another form should solve it.

Four approvals are conjunctive:

1. the safety triage cell confirms a plausible physical or rights harm;
2. the rights authority confirms the protected subject and threshold;
3. the content challenge cell proves that prohibited information is absent; and
4. the configuration registrar confirms that the change preserves all three form identities and issues one root epoch.

No role may both access raw source data and translate a sibling. No role may both issue an epoch and accept its safety test. No Pass 228 role may compare performance. Twelve separated authorities preserve those incompatibilities.

## State and disposition

The twelve-state path is `DETECTED → STOPPED / QUARANTINED → CLASSIFIED`. From there:

- a pure performance result becomes `SEALED PERFORMANCE` and returns no payload to the campaign;
- an ambiguous hazard or non-normalizable change becomes `ROOT HOLD`;
- a valid safety matter proceeds through content minimization, rights approval, continuity approval, epoch issue, independent translation and a common safety test.

All three siblings move to the same root epoch together. Partial issue or a single-form epoch advance is invalid. Pre-change work on the affected safety endpoint earns no later evidence credit. The common post-epoch test is the only route back to the campaign. Expiry forces review so a precaution cannot silently become permanent architecture.

Twelve synthetic cases test the classifier. Four threshold-defined harms propagate, four performance results remain sealed and four source-specific or ambiguous records hold the root. These are protocol cases, not observations.

## Fiscal and clock neutrality

Mandated safety rework cannot reduce a form's reserved competitive ceiling or enter comparative cost. A separate safety-equalization warrant must fund it. Pass 228 authorizes no such money: its ceiling, obligations and payments remain zero. If an `RSCW` arises before separate authority exists, the entire root stays held rather than charging an early form, consuming a later sibling's reserve or hiding the cost.

The base 24-week qualification duration remains a planning hypothesis. A new epoch starts one common post-change qualification interval for the three siblings. Sunk source-form time does not create a comparative schedule penalty or advantage.
