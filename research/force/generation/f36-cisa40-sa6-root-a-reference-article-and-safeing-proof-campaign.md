# RAP-1 — Root A reference-article and safeing proof campaign

## Decision abstract

`RAP-1` defines the physical construction and acceptance campaign for the government-owned `REA-100` low-source reference article. The preceding `RAE-1` constitution established what an executable zero-source or 100-kilogram event dossier must contain. It did not establish that the article, safeing mechanisms, measurement channels, containment boundary or evidence system existed. RAP-1 creates the missing bridge between an internally complete design and a public institution capable of accepting real research hardware.[1–10]

The campaign builds eighteen serialized article units in four locked roles. Six operational articles are assigned one per Root A public rig. Six matched functional twins absorb blinded defects and overloads that would compromise operational articles. Three calibration skeletons provide known physical state and timing transitions. Three destructive witnesses preserve terminal sectioning, joint examination and state-memory evidence. Roles are frozen before results. A successful twin cannot replace an adverse operational article, and an attractive operational article cannot be diverted into destructive work.

Two unlike public build lineages divide the population evenly. `BL-X` uses a continuous welded reference construction with austenitic stainless wetted geometry. `BL-Y` uses a modular super-duplex cartridge construction with mechanical joints. Both must meet the same 40-millimetre internal diameter, 12-metre length, surface-state, pan, receiver and control envelopes. Each campus receives one operational article and one twin from each lineage. Build effect therefore remains observable rather than being hidden inside campus or rig effect.

The physical campaign creates 360 construction travelers, 720 adjacent-process coupons, 360 article-section nondestructive examinations, seventy-two frozen destructive cuts and eighteen configuration masters. The eighteen articles carry 216 final control elements and 648 serialized safeing paths. Path acceptance requires 3,348 trials: 2,160 operational boundary actuations, 864 blinded twin defects, 216 calibration excitations and 108 destructive mechanism trials. A repeated actuation is exposure on one serialized path, not an independent population observation.

Twelve fluid-capable articles execute sixteen inert-fluid route cards each, producing 192 route trials, approximately 16.945 tonnes of processed public surrogate throughput, 12 cubic metres of public-water challenge throughput and 1,152 physical samples. The surrogate is specified as a property envelope rather than a named recipe. It must match the declared density, viscosity and surface-tension bands while independently meeting noncombustibility, low-toxicity, compatibility and recovery requirements. Source material remains prohibited.

Metrology is article-owned. Twenty-four measurement families place six channel instances on each of eighteen articles, for 2,592 channels and 5,184 before-and-after calibration records. Six operational articles also execute 144 system-denial trials and produce 120 packet reconstructions at non-origin campuses. The resulting workbook contains 15,252 linked physical acceptance gates. Every physical state begins `OPEN`; none can be closed by planning completion.

The 24-month establishment requires 240 positions, $248 million in gross capital and $112 million per year in gross support. Gross 24-month exposure is $472 million. RAP-1 is fully nested inside the existing `RAE-1` boundary, leaving $112 million of parent capital and $56 million per year unallocated. Net portfolio extension is zero. A $310 million technology portfolio is cross-classified within the parent research allocation and is not added to RAP-1 capital. Article acceptance is the maximum available decision. `QE-01`, source entry, 150-millimetre route qualification, method release, candidate entry, production and exterior rendering remain blocked.

## 1. Research stocktake: the point at which design becomes physical

The Root A research chain now distinguishes technology search, public range, execution law, method logic, known-truth qualification, source-entry safety and event dossiers. `RFA-1` defined the drainage, firewater and recovery technology program. `RFP-1` created the candidate-neutral fire/hydraulic rig. `RFE-1` defined its clock, intervention law and source escalation. `RAM-1` established method cards and estimators. `RAW-1` created the nonenergetic qualification wave. `RAS-1` bounded quarter-source entry. `RAE-1` then compiled the smallest event classes into physical acceptance objects.[1–7]

This sequence exposed a new institutional gap. RAE-1 could require twelve final elements and thirty-six paths on each operational article, but a spreadsheet allocation was not a built mechanism. It could prescribe a 40-millimetre insert, but nominal diameter alone did not establish internal volume, surface retention, slope, trapped inventory or drain-down. It could require a direct final-state witness, but an installed sensor was not calibrated evidence that the final element reached its state. It could require two originals, but a storage promise was not proof that both originals survived service denial.

RAP-1 is therefore not a larger event plan. It is an article-making institution. It binds drawings, material lots, construction operations, coupons, examinations, installed channels, calibrations, safeing trials, fluid trials, samples, destructive cuts, evidence and independent acceptance to serialized hardware. The relevant analogue is not an equipment demonstration. It is the combination of a military arsenal, developmental test organization, operational evaluation unit and standards laboratory translated into resilience engineering.

The broader Department of Resilience needs this capability because a trillion-dollar industrial program cannot rely on vendor assertions or procurement specifications alone. Novel pumps, desalination systems, mobile substations, rescue aircraft and autonomous earthmoving machines will operate in mixed media, damaged infrastructure, extreme weather and uncertain public intervention. A mature force must be able to build neutral references, insert known failures, measure final physical state and reproduce a decision outside the origin institution. Those functions are part of force projection because they determine whether the force can trust what it deploys.

## 2. The acceptance proposition and authority boundary

RAP-1 asks one question:

> Can the Department build, instrument and independently accept six government-owned low-source reference articles whose physical geometry, safeing behavior, inert-fluid custody, measurement uncertainty and evidence survive registered boundary conditions and system denial?

The object is the public research article, not a source-entry method and not a field machine. A passing campaign may support assignment of six operational `REA-100` articles to the Root A rigs. It cannot issue `QE-01`, because event issue additionally requires an event-specific frozen baseline, neighbor state, rescue posture, holds, evidence plan and named issuer. It cannot issue `QE-02`, because source-package acceptance and source-entry authority remain absent. It cannot qualify the 150-millimetre route because the 40-millimetre article represents a different hydraulic regime.[7]

The authority hierarchy is deliberately asymmetric:

| Decision | RAP-1 may establish | RAP-1 cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| construction | an as-built serialized article within the public envelope | production design or fleet configuration |
| safeing | final-state behavior of named elements and paths under registered conditions | universal reliability or service life |
| fluid handling | inert-surrogate hold-up, containment and recovery | combustible-source behavior or fire performance |
| metrology | channel range, uncertainty, synchronization and replay | validity of an unexecuted source event |
| acceptance | suitability as a public research instrument | candidate admission, field deployment or exterior form |

This hierarchy prevents a familiar research failure: evidence acquired for one bounded question migrates into a larger claim because the names sound adjacent. RAP-1 requires every acceptance packet to state the object, configuration, medium, condition, population and prohibited extrapolations.

## 3. Article-bank architecture

### 3.1 Four roles instead of one overused prototype

A single prototype cannot simultaneously support calibration, nominal acceptance, blinded failure insertion, destructive examination and future event service. Each activity alters the physical state needed by the others. RAP-1 therefore creates four populations.

| Role | Units | Physical purpose | Governing restriction |
|---|---:|---|---|
| operational | 6 | future rig reference articles | no seeded defect or destructive cut before final disposition |
| functional twin | 6 | matched defects, overloads and repair learning | cannot replace an operational result |
| calibration skeleton | 3 | known time, state and geometry transitions | cannot earn operational article acceptance |
| destructive witness | 3 | terminal sectioning and mechanism reconstruction | intact evidence must be released before cutting |

The operational/twin pairing is one-to-one by rig and build lineage. A twin can receive an obstruction, failed energy source or false state indication while its operational pair remains intact. The comparison helps locate mechanism without exposing the operational article to an irreversible challenge. Calibration skeletons separate measurement-system truth from article performance. Destructive witnesses preserve the ability to examine wetted joints, valve seats, sensor mounts, seal compression and internal surface state without sacrificing the six-unit operational set.

The eighteen-unit bank has a maximum declared dry mass of 45 tonnes. Mass ceiling is a handling and fixture input, not an invitation to make every unit weigh 2.5 tonnes. Each article must publish its measured mass, center of mass, lift configuration, transport restraint and change history.

### 3.2 Two build lineages

Public reference hardware should not inherit one construction process as invisible truth. `BL-X` and `BL-Y` create deliberately unlike joint and assembly mechanisms inside a common measurement envelope.

`BL-X` uses continuous welded construction for the wetted insert and pan, direct-stem isolation and a welded structural frame. Its governing questions include weld geometry, thermal distortion, inaccessible internal finish and residual stress. `BL-Y` uses a mechanically joined cartridge path, replaceable isolation modules and a modular pan frame. Its questions include joint preload, gasket compression, module identity and repeated assembly.

The lineages are not assumed equivalent. Their internal diameter, line volume, roughness, surface energy, joint discontinuity, leakage, retained film and clean recovery are measured. Method results retain lineage as a blocking variable. If one lineage consistently holds more fluid, closes more slowly or generates more ambiguous final-state evidence, the difference remains visible. The program can then decide whether to narrow the common envelope, separate the reference classes or revise the design.

Each campus receives both lineages. This is essential. If all `BL-X` articles were at one campus and all `BL-Y` articles at another, a lineage difference would be inseparable from weather, fixtures, staff, utilities or local practice. Paired campus allocation makes build and campus effects identifiable within the six operational rigs.

## 4. Construction constitution and tolerance logic

Every article has twenty controlled sections: public datum, primary frame, surrogate vessel, capsule dock, 40-millimetre insert, dispenser, inner pan, normal and casualty routes, receivers, candidate/public-water isolation, final control assembly, safety energy, measurement frame, causal time/evidence, rescue access and environmental boundary. Eighteen articles therefore generate 360 section travelers and 360 section-specific examinations.

A traveler is a native record of physical state. It identifies material, process, operator, tool, calibration, predecessor state, result, hold release and configuration consequence. Completing the form after assembly is not equivalent. A state-changing operation must generate its record while the state remains observable.[8]

Forty adjacent-process coupons per article create 720 population records. Five classes cover wetted joints, seals, surface finish, penetrations and sensor mounts. Each article contributes eight coupons per class. The coupons establish process context and preserve material for later examination, but they do not replace article NDE. A perfect adjacent weld coupon cannot prove that an inaccessible article weld is sound.

Three simple calculations show why construction detail matters. The 40-millimetre, 12-metre insert has a nominal volume of 0.0150796 cubic metres and a nominal 800-kilogram-per-cubic-metre inventory of 12.0637 kilograms. A positive diameter deviation of only 0.05 millimetres changes that inventory by approximately 0.0302 kilograms. A 2-millimetre length deviation changes inventory by only about 0.0020 kilograms. Diameter therefore dominates simple geometry tolerance, but surface state can dominate both.

The wetted internal area is approximately 1.508 square metres. A uniform retained film of 10 micrometres represents about 0.0121 kilograms at the declared density. A 0.1-millimetre film represents about 0.1206 kilograms; a 0.5-millimetre film represents about 0.6032 kilograms. These quantities are material against a 0.75-kilogram unaccounted-source ceiling and a 1.5-kilogram expanded-uncertainty ceiling inherited from RAE-1. The campaign therefore measures roughness, surface condition, residual film, low-point geometry and drain-down rather than treating the line as an ideal cylinder.

Slope is similarly consequential. A 2-degree grade over 12 metres creates approximately 0.419 metres of elevation difference and about 3.29 kilopascals of hydrostatic head for the surrogate. That head can change terminal inventory, gravity diversion and valve timing. Positive and negative slope cards are therefore required physical trials, not optional environmental variations.

The three destructive articles receive seventy-two frozen cuts. Locations are committed before results so that investigators cannot preferentially cut only favorable regions. Intact geometry, leakage, state, calibration and evidence records must be released before teardown alters the article.

## 5. Safeing system and trial population

### 5.1 Final control elements

Each article carries twelve final control elements inherited from the entry constitution: source inlet isolation, dispenser isolation, gravity diversion, normal receiver isolation, casualty diversion, ignition-energy removal, candidate/public-water isolation, casualty-water diversion, normal electrical isolation, independent safety energy, environmental boundary/vent and evidence safe-state latch. Across eighteen articles this creates 216 element records.

Each element has primary, diverse and manual or passive paths. The 648 paths are physically serialized. A primary path uses registered command, independent energy and direct state sensing. A diverse path uses an unlike local trip, separate energy and unlike state indication. A manual or passive path does not depend on the network. Shared final geometry is permitted because all paths may act on the same element; shared decisive command, energy or state evidence is not.

### 5.2 Operational boundary trials

The six operational articles contain 216 paths. Every path receives ten actuations across five boundary families with two order realizations: nominal, low stored energy, service denial, environmental edge and upper accepted friction or contamination. The resulting 2,160 trials provide repeated exposure to the accepted envelope. They do not create 2,160 independent paths.

The low-energy condition begins at 60 percent of accepted stored energy. Environmental conditions include -20°C and 50°C external states with wet exposure and a separately accepted internal condition. Service-denial cases remove normal power, campus network or primary time while retaining the registered local survivor. The friction condition introduces an accepted mechanical resistance rather than an unknown field contaminant.

Acceptance attaches to final physical state and elapsed time. Source and water isolations target one to five seconds depending on function. Environmental routing targets ten seconds. Independent safety energy must remain sufficient for the thirty-second final-action interval. A command receipt or controller status cannot substitute for direct final-state evidence.

### 5.3 Blinded defects on functional twins

Every one of the 216 twin paths receives four defects: command withheld, motive energy absent, false state indication and mechanical obstruction. This yields 864 blinded presentations. The challenged path is expected to fail honestly. Campaign success requires detection of the failed path and safe completion through an unlike survivor.

The false-indication case is particularly important. A control system reports safe while the mechanism remains unsafe. The architecture must reject the report using the independent final-element witness and invoke another path. This tests whether the Department has built three paths or merely three ways to display one belief.

Defects are inserted only after twin baseline acceptance. The insertion authority, operator and result accepter are separated. Repair is prohibited until native evidence, state memory and physical condition are released. A repaired twin receives a new configuration identity and cannot erase the defect result.

### 5.4 Calibration and destructive path evidence

Three calibration skeletons contribute 216 known time and state excitations: two per path. These trials establish whether measurement roots can resolve the registered transition and timing interval. Three destructive witnesses contribute 108 terminal trials, one per path, followed by force, contact, seat, obstruction and memory examination. Together with operational and twin trials, total path population is 3,348.

No reliability claim is permitted. Ten passes on one path do not estimate fleet failure probability. RAP-1 may conclude that a serialized path completed a registered screen and that the public measurement system detected four governing failure classes. Service life, maintenance interval and fleet reliability require different populations and exposure models.

## 6. Inert-fluid route campaign

The fluid campaign uses the six operational articles and six functional twins. Each executes sixteen route cards. Dry and static-fill cards establish numeric zero and line inventory. Nominal, high-rate and low-rate cards bracket the 100-kilogram/120-second condition. Positive and negative slope cards expose gravity. An initial-residual card separates admitted mass from known starting inventory. Blinded flow bias and tomography blind-sector cards test measurement dependence. Normal and casualty routing cards close receiver custody. Inlet and dispenser delays expose terminal-source behavior. A public-water incursion card tests ancestry. A combined power, network and time denial tests local survival.

The processed surrogate throughput is approximately 16.945 tonnes. This is throughput, not a one-time stock requirement. Twelve article-specific lots each provide 250 kilograms of conditioned stock, for 3 tonnes on hand. A lot may be recovered and reused only after property, identity and contamination acceptance. Reuse creates a new custody state and cannot silently merge trials.

`SF-800` is deliberately a target specification rather than a published chemical mixture. Its density target is 800 kilograms per cubic metre with ±8 tolerance, dynamic viscosity 0.004 pascal-seconds with ±0.0002 tolerance, and surface tension 0.025 newtons per metre with ±0.002 tolerance. The chosen formulation must also be noncombustible, low-toxicity, compatible with both lineages, detectable by both ancestry roots and recoverable without creating a new environmental hazard. Those requirements may force novel surrogate-media research rather than selection from a commodity catalogue.

The water challenge uses twelve separate 5-cubic-metre public lots and processes 12 cubic metres across the campaign. Water and surrogate remain separate in supply, metering, sampling and recovery. Every route trial produces six samples: media before, media after, pan residual, receiver residual, water or blank control, and archive control. The result is 1,152 custody samples.

Mass closure must remain within the inherited 1.5-kilogram expanded-uncertainty ceiling. That condition is necessary but not sufficient. The trial must also preserve source-surrogate ancestry, water ancestry, environmental disposition, final routing and causal order. A numerically closed mass ledger with the wrong identity fails.

## 7. Metrology and causal-time architecture

RAP-1 gives every article 144 channels: six instances from each of twenty-four families. Across eighteen articles this creates 2,592 installed channels. The families cover vessel mass, integrated transfer mass, pressure, temperature, hold-up tomography, pan level, receiver mass, geometry/slope, primary/diverse/manual state witnesses, final-element reference, causal time, motive energy, normal and independent power, leak collection, occupied area, water flow, two ancestry roots, environmental release, image trigger and evidence health.

Six instances per family are not treated as six identical sensors. They provide spatial coverage, paired observation and retained reference capacity. Channel identity includes location, orientation, range, calibration standard, acquisition path, clock, power, evidence destination and configuration state.

Every channel receives before-and-after calibration, generating 5,184 records. Calibration records retain observed bias and expanded uncertainty. A timestamp beside an empty result cannot pass. A channel that exceeds its family uncertainty ceiling fails even if the trial result appears favorable. After-state calibration is mandatory because overload, wet exposure, temperature, obstruction and teardown can alter the measurement chain.

Continuous acquisition across the 144-channel article is approximately 0.52032 megabytes per second under the declared sample rates and eight-byte record size. A path trial retains about 1.0029 gigabytes after the full-rate window, tail, two originals, custody factor and imagery. A route trial retains about 5.315 gigabytes. Calibration, manufacturing, denial and replay records raise raw campaign provision to approximately 5.531 terabytes; a 30-percent forensic allowance raises provision to approximately 7.191 terabytes.

Storage is not the main challenge. Identity and causal order are. Every packet must show which article state, calibration, path, trigger, physical transition, sample and authority generated the data. A large archive with ambiguous joins is weaker than a smaller set of native records with explicit ancestry.

## 8. System denial and outside-origin replay

The six operational articles execute twelve denial cases twice, producing 144 trials. The cases remove normal power, campus network, primary time, primary controller, primary or diverse state witnesses, normal source or water destinations, one evidence original, origin-campus analysis, one environmental channel or operator interpretation. Each case names the survivor before execution.

Local safety remains mandatory. Loss of network does not relax final-state time. Loss of primary time does not permit unknown causal order. Loss of one evidence original requires the second to seal locally. Loss of origin analysis requires a sealed packet that another campus can reconstruct. If a denial makes the event undecidable, the result can be invalid; it cannot be promoted through post hoc interpretation.

Ten packet classes preserve configuration, path actuation, route mass, containment, calibration, ancestry, denial state, samples, native time and integrated conclusion. Each operational article sends each class to both non-origin campuses. The 120 reconstructions must reproduce the registered result within the declared numeric or categorical tolerance. The integrated conclusion cannot compensate for a missing native class.

This capability has direct operational value. A national resilience force will often deploy into regions where local infrastructure, data centers, communications and technical staff are impaired together. Field systems need locally complete safety and evidence, followed by later federation. RAP-1 makes that architecture a tested property rather than a software aspiration.

## 9. Industrial and technological program

The physical campaign creates ten technology programs with a $310 million cross-classified allocation:

| Program | Required advance | Force-level value |
|---|---|---|
| self-identifying surrogate capsule | contained connection with mass, property and custody identity | media and consumable control across distributed formations |
| distributed hold-up tomography | spatial line inventory without opening or draining | rapid custody after damaged piping and uncertain terrain |
| mechanical final-state memory | retained physical state after total energy loss | trustworthy local recovery after grid and network failure |
| local causal-time fabric | bounded unlike epoch across isolated instruments | reconstruction across disconnected response systems |
| reversible containment | known occupied area, closed recovery and rapid cleanout | repeated operation without sacrificing environmental custody |
| public surrogate-media science | safe matching of governing transport properties | high-fidelity development without full hazard escalation |
| portable distributed calibration | independent reference at the deployed article | field-grade metrology rather than laboratory-only certification |
| local immutable evidence vault | two sealed originals through service denial | accountable public operations in degraded regions |
| controlled-defect synthesis | repeatable command, energy, indication and obstruction failures | qualification against actual failure mechanisms |
| autonomous internal inspection | map diameter, finish, residue, joints and sensor geometry | rapid requalification of complex fluid infrastructure |

These technologies should not be procured as isolated gadgets. The Department should create long-horizon mission programs with public reference interfaces, multiple builders, government data rights, independent standards and regular severe-environment trials. This is how resilience acquisition can drive innovation at the same depth that military propulsion, sensing, communications and materials programs shaped their supplier bases.

The two-lineage article bank also defines an industrial policy. Competition occurs at the mechanism level rather than through superficial feature lists. Suppliers compete to meet common physical and evidentiary interfaces using unlike construction. Public test data exposes which mechanisms are sensitive to temperature, contamination, repeated assembly, service denial or recovery. The Department can then mature more than one viable supplier route instead of selecting one early architecture and losing industrial diversity.

The export product is larger than the article. It includes test methods, calibration standards, evidence schemas, failure libraries, certification services and interoperable interfaces. Partner nations could purchase equipment whose limits have been established under a common public regime, or establish compatible proving cells without duplicating the full U.S. complex. Trustworthy qualification becomes part of resilience statecraft.

## 10. Capacity and organization

The campaign requires 526 base work-centre days: 369 for path trials, forty-eight for route trials, thirty-six for denial trials, fifty-four calibration-equivalent days and nineteen replay/teardown days. A 15-percent rounded reserve adds seventy-nine, producing 605 days. Six rigs over two years at a 60-percent ready fraction provide 2,628 rig-days. Campaign demand is approximately 23 percent of that gross availability, leaving capacity for nonenergetic work, maintenance, configuration recovery and other Root A programs.

This arithmetic does not prove schedule feasibility. Work-centre conflicts, article delivery, calibration standards, staff qualifications, environmental windows and evidence review can create local constraints. The workbook therefore preserves each population and schedule stage rather than using the 23-percent figure as a global readiness claim.

The 240-position organization includes twenty-four in campaign integration, thirty-two in article engineering, thirty-six in manufacturing/quality, forty in controls/safeing, thirty-two in metrology/causal time, twenty-four in fluid/containment, twenty in evidence/replay, sixteen in safety/rescue, twelve in independent acceptance and four in program operations. These positions form a permanent public profession rather than a temporary vendor oversight office.

The critical institutional separation is between creation and acceptance. Manufacturing owns travelers and built state but cannot accept its own work. Metrology owns standards but cannot alter results. Safeing authority accepts path evidence but cannot issue `QE-01`. Evidence authority accepts native custody but cannot waive a missing physical state. The article board may accept one operational article. A separate configuration board may accept the six-article set. Event issue remains downstream.

## 11. Financial boundary

Gross RAP-1 capital is $248 million. The largest line is $54 million for eighteen reference-article units and article-owned fixtures. Channel suites require $32 million; manufacturing fixtures $30 million; safeing challenge fixtures $24 million; calibration laboratories $22 million; surrogate systems, containment/receivers and evidence/time $18 million each; controlled defects $12 million; teardown and safety/rescue $8 million each; and contingency $4 million.

Annual support is $112 million: $60 million workforce, $10 million rig operations, $7 million article maintenance, $8 million calibration, $5 million surrogate media, $6 million evidence, $5 million safety/rescue, $3 million teardown, $2 million independent boards, $3 million technology maturation and $3 million contingency. The 24-month exposure is $472 million.

RAP-1 is fully contained inside the `RAE-1` boundary of $360 million capital and $168 million per year. The remainder is $112 million capital and $56 million per year. It is not a savings claim because later event readiness, source custody and operational integration still require funding. It is an explicit unallocated parent balance. Net portfolio extension remains zero. The $310 million technology allocation is cross-classified within the parent research portfolio and is not summed with RAP-1 capital.[7,10]

## 12. Acceptance state and next research object

RAP-1 is internally complete and physically unexecuted. The companion workbook has forty-five sheets, 30,837 formula records, 120 passing assertions and no formula-error matches. Every worksheet is populated. It contains 15,252 formula-linked physical gates whose source states reside in the owning article, construction, trial, channel, calibration, denial, replay or sample ledger.

None of those facts accepts an article. As-built identities are blank. Coupons have no observed results. NDE has not occurred. Path triggers, motive states, final states and action times are unobserved. Surrogate and water lots are unaccepted. Calibrations, denial trials, replays and samples are open. Article acceptance and every later release therefore remain blocked.

The next research object should be `RAC-1`: the construction drawings, tolerance stacks and controlled build-release packages for `BL-X` and `BL-Y`. It should define internal coordinate drawings, material and joint specifications, surface-state bands, valve and actuator sizing, pneumatic and electrical energy budgets, sensor mounting, calibration ports, drainage geometry, access clearances, lift states, bill of material, supplier qualification, article travelers and build-order logic. It should also resolve which technologies require development articles before production-equivalent build can start.

Exterior rendering remains withheld. RAP-1 begins to constrain internal research geometry, but it still says nothing authoritative about candidate mobility, crew, field setup, operator protection, service interfaces, production packaging or operational form. Those constraints must emerge from later candidate and deployment research before a vehicle exterior can be credible.
