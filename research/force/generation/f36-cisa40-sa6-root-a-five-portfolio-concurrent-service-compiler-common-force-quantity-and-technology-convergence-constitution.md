## Executive finding

The Department of Resilience should not build five miniature armed forces for fire control, flood and water control, power and thermal service, medical continuity, and hard access. It should also not create one undifferentiated equipment pool and count the same machines against every promise. `RBX-1` defines the harder institution between those errors: a mission-service matrix whose common capacity is compiled from simultaneous public-service obligations, exact configurations, geography, time, crew, transport, reset, and industrial-root survival.

The immediate result is intentionally asymmetric. The current fire-control work provides a complete family bill for eleven national-common article families. The other four portfolios provide serious service kernels, national planning counts, and some mass, power, workforce, or payload quantities, but they do not yet provide family-by-family translations into those eleven articles. Similar function is not substitute capacity. A 10 MW source module in a `PRG-100` is not two `ENG-5/7` energy islands until electrical topology, fuel and heat path, fault behavior, controls, transport, crew, maintenance, release authority, and reset have been demonstrated in the receiving mission.

The compiler therefore produces a **known national-common floor**, not a final fleet:

| Quantity boundary | Current result |
|---|---:|
| reference operating kernels | 8 |
| effect portfolios | 5 |
| national-common families tested per kernel | 11 |
| translation cells | 88 |
| cells with an exact family bill | 11 |
| cells with cross-portfolio substitution evidence | 0 |
| fire-derived common articles | 200,752 |
| fire-derived common article capital | USD 1.198432T |
| fire-derived common infrastructure and production capital | USD 450.835746B |
| **known National Common Force capital floor** | **USD 1.649267746T** |
| admitted cross-portfolio quantity credit | **0** |
| admitted capital reduction | **USD 0** |

This does not mean that commonality is unimportant. It means that commonality is a research result, not an accounting preference. The USD 1.649267746T floor is only the already specified national-common content of the `C24` fire guarantee. It is not a ceiling and it does not imply that other portfolios add no common demand. Their uncompiled demand is recorded as **unavailable**, never as zero.

`RBX-1` also changes what should be invented. The Department should not seek a universal disaster truck. It should fund a **common capability grammar**: power, distribution, command, rescue, repair, sustainment, governed loads, transport interfaces, measurement, and release protocols that can be reconfigured without hiding mission-specific physics. A USD 1.000T, twenty-five-year technology-convergence envelope is defined to pursue that grammar through eight rival programs, plural industrial roots, destructive tests, representative missions, and explicit failure reserves. It is a policy-scale research boundary, not an addition to the national force account and not procurement authority.

The most ambitious technologies are treated as systems rather than icons. A nuclear water-energy carrier must close reactor safety, heat rejection, intake, desalination, residual custody, storage, long-distance conveyance, receiver water quality, maintenance, security, and civil release. Robotic pipeline deployment must confront corridor survey, pipe mass, joint integrity, crossings, pumping head, sanitation, repair, and recovery. Autonomous earthmoving must survive unknown soil, debris, people, buried utilities, communication loss, and safe handoff. The object of innovation is verified civilian service under damage.

No exterior rendering is authorized. The next research object is `RBY-1`: the first complete cross-portfolio article translation, beginning with power and distribution because `PRG-100` and the fire-control family bill expose the strongest measurable overlap.

## 1. The problem after `RBW-1`

### 1.1 The common-force paradox

`RBW-1` proved that title, technical family, reservation, and physical assignment are different facts. It moved fire-control common capacity into a National Common Force jurisdiction without removing any article from the `C24` guarantee.

That left a harder question. If power, medical, flood, and access formations also need energy islands, distribution, command, repair, sustainment, and transport, how many common articles should the nation own?

Three tempting calculations are invalid:

1. **Add every portfolio maximum.** This preserves service but may reproduce separate vertical institutions and duplicate compatible capacity.
2. **Take the maximum portfolio requirement.** This assumes the portfolios never peak together and erases compound catastrophe.
3. **Apply a sharing percentage.** This treats administrative intent as physical equivalence and assigns the same article to several obligations.

The valid calculation is a time-, place-, configuration-, and state-constrained reservation problem. It must answer which serialized article can serve which mission, when it can leave one reservation, how it reaches the next assignment, which crew accompanies it, what reset is required, and which other promise becomes uncovered.

### 1.2 Research question

What is the smallest National Common Force that can satisfy simultaneous fire, flood-water, power-thermal, medical, and hard-access obligations without double counting any article, person, route, base, receiver, reset line, or industrial root?

### 1.3 Scope

This pass:

- establishes five effect portfolios and eight reference operating kernels;
- imports ten inherited compound-demand vectors as planning stressors;
- creates an eleven-family translation register;
- defines article-equivalence and service-admission grades;
- compiles the known national-common quantity and capital floor;
- makes missing translation visible as a hard stop;
- defines the mathematical reservation problem;
- creates a USD 1.000T technology-convergence envelope;
- specifies institutional roles, new professions, and proving infrastructure; and
- selects the first cross-portfolio physical translation campaign.

It does not:

- add successor and predecessor force accounts;
- treat the canonical allocation as validated demand;
- infer equipment quantities from personnel or mass ratios;
- assume an article is interchangeable because its nominal output matches;
- reduce the `C24` service guarantee;
- choose a reactor, aircraft, ship, vehicle, pipe, or medical platform;
- authorize procurement or fielding; or
- authorize exterior rendering.

## 2. The unit of force remains public service

### 2.1 Five effect portfolios

The compiler begins with effects that civilians can experience and authorities can verify.

| Portfolio | Public-service proposition | Distinctive failure if reduced to logistics |
|---|---|---|
| fire control | hold a defined edge, protect evacuation and continuity, control contaminated water and return the force to readiness | delivered water or vehicles do not establish controlled fire behavior |
| flood and water control | prevent, remove, redirect, treat, store and lawfully discharge water while preserving people and critical systems | installed pump nameplate does not establish basin drawdown or safe discharge |
| power and thermal service | deliver stable electricity, cooling or heat, protection, black start and safe building connection | generator megawatts do not establish usable utility service |
| medical continuity | regulate patients, sustain staffed care, move patients, preserve clinical utilities and close referral and discharge | beds or transport seats without staff and utility continuity are not care |
| hard access and logistics | establish a complete service cell across failed airfield, road, waterway, port, utility, or receiver conditions | tonnes arriving at the wrong interface are not public effect |

The portfolios overlap in support but remain different in governing physics and authority. Potable-water release cannot be performed by an electrical authority. Aircraft safety cannot be waived by a receiving commander. Clinical triage cannot be converted into throughput optimization. A common force must join these authorities without collapsing them.

### 2.2 Eight reference operating kernels

One row is not one level of maturity. The kernels below are the strongest currently available quantitative anchors for the five portfolios.

| `FIN` regional cell | flood-water | 40 cells | 7 m³/s nominal; 12,682 t; 3,447 deployed; 12,926 billets | configuration reference; no common-family bill |
| inland `HCG-100` | flood-water | 10 groups | 100 m³/s nominal; 30 MW carried power; 79,391 t; 7,039 deployed | configuration reference; no common-family bill |
| coastal `HCG-100` | flood-water | 6 groups | 104 m³/s nominal; 73,912 t; 7,166 deployed | configuration reference; no common-family bill |

### 2.3 Why mass and personnel cannot fill the missing bill

A convenient proxy would assign common modules in proportion to dry mass or deployed people. `RBX-1` prohibits it.

- A flood formation carries enormous linear material that does not require one command node or repair shop per thirty tonnes.
- A hospital requires high labor density, utility quality, sterile custody, and clinical redundancy that dry mass conceals.
- A power formation carries fuel and conductors whose quantities depend on campaign duration and receiver topology.
- An access group carries complete alternate cells; dividing its payload by a generic frame mass would erase configuration positions.
- Fire-control holdings include national reserve, training, evaluation, reference, destructive, float, and casualty accounts that are not proportional to its twenty-four operational groups.

Proxy ratios are useful for detecting impossible statements. They are not a lawful basis for procurement.

### 3.2 Six layers of commonality

The Department should pursue commonality in an ordered stack.

| Layer | Can be common before vehicles are common? | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| measurement | yes | power quality, flow, temperature, contamination, structural state, location, custody |
| semantics | yes | service state, safe state, fault, reservation, return, evidence completeness |
| interfaces | often | voltage and protection envelopes, fluid boundaries, physical locks, data exchange, lifting and restraint |
| modules | only after qualification | power conversion, storage, control, pumps, repair packs, habitation |
| carriers | sometimes | governed load frames, movers, lift or corridor positions |
| complete formations | rarely | missions retain different crews, authorities, recovery, and effect equipment |

The highest-return early objective is not one chassis. It is an open, public, failure-tolerant interface and evidence regime that permits several suppliers and mission variants to compose.

### 3.3 Configuration grammar

Every candidate common article must publish:

- a physical envelope and handled states;
- operating and transport mass, center-of-gravity limits, lift and restraint paths;
- energy input, output, storage, grounding, protection, and heat destination;
- fluid compatibility, sanitary state, contamination class, drain and residual path;
- compute, command, degraded-mode, clock, and signed offline configuration behavior;
- crew roles, tools, personal protection, and human factors;
- inspection, repair, quarantine, decontamination, and reset intervals;
- receiver conditions, adapters, civil permits, and technical release authorities;
- production root, critical materials, software ancestry, and substitution limits; and
- test evidence, known unknowns, invalidating changes, and retirement rule.

This is the minimum grammar needed to discover whether two articles are equivalent. A shared connector is not sufficient.

## 4. Translation and admission law

### 4.1 Five grades

Each kernel–family cell receives one and only one grade.

| Grade | Meaning | Quantity credit |
|---|---|---:|
| `G0` | no registered requirement or relationship | 0 |
| `G1` | capability measured in a comparable unit | 0 |
| `G2` | interface-compatible candidate configuration exists | 0 |
| `G3` | exact article bill, crew, transport, and return are defined | 0 across portfolios |
| `G4` | representative substitution and loss campaign closes the public-service contract | eligible for one bounded cross-portfolio reservation |

`G4` is not permanent. Configuration change, supplier change, software change, corrosion state, contamination, new receiver, new duty cycle, or overdue inspection can demote an article.

### 4.2 Current translation result

The `C24` fire kernel provides eleven `G3` cells because its exact family bill exists. It does not provide `G4` cross-portfolio evidence. The seven other kernels have useful physical quantities but no exact translation into these families.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

| `FIN` cell | 11 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | exact family bill and return cycle |
| inland `HCG-100` | 9 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 0 | energy/distribution topology plus remaining family bill |
| coastal `HCG-100` | 10 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | energy relation and complete family bill |
| `PRG-100` | 8 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 0 | source, distribution, and control translation articles |
| `H-THC` | 11 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | clinical-service module and common support bill |
| `H-PMG` | 11 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | patient-state carrier and support bill |

`G1` entries only indicate comparable measures: the HCG carried-power statement; PRG source, distribution, and control; and TAFG payload/interface positions. They do not establish article compatibility.

### 4.3 The zero is a result

The current `G4` count is zero. That statement is more informative than a guessed commonality percentage because it protects every public promise and gives research a precise target.

The rule is:

> Unknown demand increases uncertainty; it never decreases required capacity.

An unavailable translation cannot be entered as zero, and an untested substitution cannot become a budget credit.

### 5.2 Successor compound worlds

The compiler adds six successor worlds that must eventually be populated with exact service quantities.

| World | Condition | Required proof |
|---|---|---|
| `X0` | normal readiness, training, overhaul, and protected reserves | no training or evaluation article supports an operational promise |
| `X1` | national fire lines plus heat-driven grid failure | power and cooling obligations coexist with every fire energy reservation |

No successor world currently compiles to a lawful article quantity. That is the next model’s work, not a reason to omit the worlds.

### 5.3 Time is part of quantity

For a common article, the occupied interval is:

\[
\tau_{occupied}=\tau_{preparation}+\tau_{transit}+\tau_{mission}+\tau_{withdrawal}+\tau_{quarantine}+\tau_{repair}+\tau_{recertification}
\]

An article is unavailable to another obligation throughout this interval. A short mission with a long decontamination or inspection path can consume more fleet capacity than a long stable mission. Quantity therefore cannot be inferred from peak deployed count alone.

## 6. The compiler

### 6.1 Decision objects

For article family `j`, configuration `c`, location `s`, time interval `t`, portfolio `p`, and world `w`:

- `I[j,c,s]` is owned inventory;
- `A[j,c,s,t,w]` is physically available inventory after maintenance, training, quarantine, casualty, and root exclusions;
- `D[p,j,c,s,t,w]` is translated article demand;
- `X[a,p,t,w]` is one if serialized article `a` is reserved to portfolio `p`;
- `K[a,p]` is one only if the article has `G4` substitution authority for that portfolio;
- `R[a,t,w]` is one if the article is in reset or otherwise unavailable; and
- `Y[p,w]` is one only if the complete public-service chain closes.

### 6.2 Conservation

For every serialized article and interval:

\[
\sum_p X[a,p,t,w]+R[a,t,w]\leq1
\]

For every demand cell:

\[
\sum_{a\in(j,c,s)}X[a,p,t,w]\geq D[p,j,c,s,t,w]Y[p,w]
\]

and:

\[
X[a,p,t,w]\leq K[a,p]
\]

These equations prohibit double counting and unqualified substitution. They also prevent an article in repair from remaining available in the readiness report.

### 6.3 Service-chain conjunction

Public service is conjunctive, not an average score:

\[
Y[p,w]=\prod_{k\in service\ chain(p)} y[k,p,w]
\]

If water quality, clinical staff, electrical protection, route access, command, or lawful receiver release fails, the chain fails even when most tonnes arrive.

### 6.4 Objective hierarchy

The compiler does not minimize cost first. It uses a lexicographic hierarchy:

1. satisfy every protected public-service obligation;
2. preserve casualty, technical-root, and protected-reserve constraints;
3. minimize the worst uncovered population-time burden;
4. minimize total new inventory and infrastructure;
5. minimize twenty-five-year public cost; and
6. maximize industrial learning and exportable open standards without creating a common root.

No financial advantage may compensate for failure at a higher level.

### 6.5 Three quantity outputs

For each family the compiler must publish three distinct values:

- **known floor:** accepted inventory already required by a constituted service promise;
- **compiled requirement:** additional inventory required after all portfolio translations and worlds close; and
- **conditional release:** predecessor quantity that can be retired only after the successor inventory, crew, bases, and service evidence exist.

The current model can calculate only the first.

### 7.1 Articles

The eleven `C24` common families total 200,752 articles and USD 1.198432T. This is the only itemized common-force inventory presently available.

- that the common fleet is geographically balanced;
- that any article is physically accepted; or
- that USD 1.649267746T is the mature National Common Force budget.

It says only that a smaller current number would silently break the existing planning guarantee.

## 9. Technology frontiers

### 9.1 Water-energy carriers

A floating nuclear-powered desalination carrier is a legitimate strategic hypothesis. It is not a reactor with a hose.

Its complete service chain includes:

- safe reactor operation, shutdown, decay-heat path, security, maintenance, and liability;
- intake that survives turbidity, biology, debris, contamination, and damaged ports;
- pretreatment, desalination, post-treatment, disinfection, laboratory custody, and product storage;
- brine, concentrate, chemical, and contaminated residual destinations;
- electrical export, islanding, grounding, and black-start behavior;
- high-flow ship-to-shore or ship-to-river transfer without one fragile terminal;
- booster energy, pressure control, route isolation, repair, and sanitary commissioning;
- receiver storage, district distribution, public access, and quality release;
- crew rotation, refueling or core life, drydock, weather avoidance, and casualty response; and
- consent, port and water authority, environmental decision, safeguards, and public communication.

At a declared 1,000 MW electrical output and 3 kWh per cubic metre process-energy screen, the arithmetic ceiling is 8 million m³/day before auxiliaries, downtime, intake limits, residual handling, and conveyance. At 5 kWh/m³ it is 4.8 million m³/day. These are energy quotients, not delivered-water claims.

Conveyance can dominate. Moving 100 m³/s at 3 m/s requires about 42 m² of flow area, equivalent to a single pipe roughly 6.5 m in internal diameter. A 1 m diameter steel line with a declared 20 mm wall carries roughly 493 kg of steel per metre before joints, lining, valves, protection, supports, and installation; 100 km is about 49,300 t of pipe. Drone deployment may transform labor and tempo, but it cannot repeal mass, head, crossing, joint, or sanitation requirements.

The technology program should therefore compete four whole systems:

1. deep-water nuclear water-energy carrier with multiple standoff transfer points;
2. shallow-draft modular water-energy flotilla for ports and inland waterways;
3. non-nuclear renewable or fuel-flexible processing carrier with distributed storage; and
4. source-side landable process blocks linked by rapidly built corridors.

No architecture receives credit from reactor power alone.

### 9.2 Rapid linear infrastructure

The future force needs utility construction at operational tempo. The target is a robotic **linear-infrastructure fabric** able to survey, clear, bridge, lay, join, test, energize, chlorinate or flush, sectionalize, inspect, repair, and recover multiple utility paths.

Promising research directions include:

- cooperative route robots that map soil, buried services, contamination, people, and exclusion zones;
- pipe and cable reels with automated tension, bend-radius, joint, and provenance control;
- temporary above-ground corridors that convert to buried or protected states without service interruption;
- trenchless microtunnel and directional-bore swarms for crossings;
- self-locating isolation valves, protection nodes, leak or fault localization, and autonomous bypass;
- composite or hybrid conduits with repairable liners and structural health sensing;
- robotic anchor, ballast, berm, culvert, and short-span construction;
- reversible sanitary commissioning and contamination-zone separation; and
- governed recovery so temporary infrastructure does not become abandoned debris.

The relevant measure is not kilometres laid. It is verified service delivered across the route, maintained through damage, and safely removed or transferred.

### 9.3 Power and thermal utility cores

The Department should develop a family of source-neutral utility cores rather than one generator fleet. A core would own protection, grid formation, synchronization, storage, black start, heat rejection, service metering, and receiver release while accepting several qualified primary-energy modules.

The difficult innovations are fault current without a strong grid, safe multi-vendor protection, very large mobile transformation, high-power connectors that tolerate dirt and repeated assembly, modular heat rejection, cold and hot weather performance, low-acoustic clinical operation, fuel-quality variation, cyber-independent controls, and automated commissioning that still leaves a human technical authority accountable.

A reactor, turbine, fuel cell, battery, geothermal exchanger, or surviving grid feed can become a qualified source module. None should control the entire utility architecture.

### 9.4 Autonomous civil works

Autonomy should change the scale and risk of route opening, earthmoving, debris handling, levee work, contaminated-zone operation, and infrastructure construction. The target is not an uncrewed excavator demonstration. It is a supervised fleet that can interpret uncertain ground, preserve buried utilities and evidence, operate near civilians, coordinate loads, recover failed machines, and continue with degraded communications.

The program needs representative rubble, mud, wildfire, ice, flood, unstable slope, contaminated soil, nighttime, and mixed human-machine ranges. Safe stop, local control, explainable exclusion zones, and recovery under machine failure are first-order performance measures.

### 9.5 Clinical continuity

Medical commonality should center on a **patient-state and utility contract**, not a universal hospital box. Modular clinical areas should declare acuity, staffing, gases, power quality, cooling, water, infection-control state, diagnostics, pharmacy, blood, imaging, sterilization, waste, referral, and evacuation behavior.

The common force may supply power, thermal, command, shelter, logistics, and governed loads. Clinical configuration and authority remain with the health service. A module cannot be transferred from general shelter to high-acuity care by relabeling it.

### 9.6 Governed mobility

The `LF-30` and `TIA-30` concepts should grow into a cross-mode load-state system. A payload would retain structural load paths, center of gravity, energy isolation, fluid isolation, environmental limits, identity, custody, and release state across road, rail, vessel, liftship, terrain carrier, and fixed receiver.

This could enable robotic cross-loading and rapid carrier substitution. It also risks a catastrophic common interface defect. The program therefore needs plural interface roots, incompatible-safe failure, physical keying, signed but offline-verifiable configuration, and destructive transfer tests.

## 10. Institution and command

### 10.1 The National Common Force is a force generator

The National Common Force should own:

- common-family requirements and open interface law;
- inventory, serial identity, custody, maintenance, and recapitalization;
- common crews and mission-specific qualification overlays;
- national dispatch and Service Availability Warrants;
- bases, arsenals, transfer nodes, and receiver-preparation grants;
- production roots, spares, repair, and regeneration;
- joint exercises and cross-portfolio substitution campaigns; and
- public inventory and readiness accounts.

It should not own:

- the definition of every mission effect;
- clinical, water-quality, electrical, aviation, reactor, or local civil release authority;
- independent test and evaluation;
- local political priority setting; or
- the right to move a reserved article without replacing the protected obligation.

### 10.2 Mission services retain service authority

Each mission service owns its service contract, distinctive equipment, professional standards, formation readiness, and mission acceptance. It submits translated common demand to the compiler and accepts only configurations qualified for its mission.

The relationship resembles a legally enforceable capacity market with physical custody. It is not an internal rental desk.

### 10.3 Independent evidence authority

A National Resilience Evaluation Authority should maintain reference articles, destructive ranges, blind faults, evidence originals, configuration baselines, and independent reconstruction. It may deny common-family admission even when both the mission service and common-force owner want a program to proceed.

The institution seeking readiness credit must not be the sole judge of its own equivalence claim.

### 10.4 Civil priority and appeal

Allocation among simultaneously valid missions is a public decision. The compiler can expose consequences; it cannot decide whose hospital, town, watershed, or partner receives service.

Every dispatch must therefore record:

- the protected service obligations;
- the population-time burden if each is delayed;
- legal and treaty constraints;
- vulnerable populations and continuity dependencies;
- the exact capacity displaced;
- dissenting technical judgments;
- the accountable civil decision authority; and
- the time and conditions for appeal or recompilation.

## 11. Workforce and industrial economy

### 11.1 New professions

The mature common force creates professions that barely exist today at public scale:

- public-service systems architect;
- cross-portfolio configuration officer;
- service-reservation controller;
- field utility commissioning authority;
- water-energy carrier engineer;
- rapid-corridor superintendent;
- autonomous civil-works commander;
- governed-load and cross-mode custody officer;
- field heat-rejection engineer;
- clinical-utility integration officer;
- regeneration scheduler;
- industrial-root assurance analyst;
- disconnected command and evidence engineer; and
- independent service-effect evaluator.

These are careers with apprenticeships, academies, licensing, promotion, field tours, depot tours, and teaching assignments—not temporary contract labor categories.

### 11.2 Planning workforce envelope

The technology-convergence program uses a provisional peak enterprise of 500,000 affiliated positions:

| Component | Peak positions | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| public laboratories, ranges, acquisition, standards, and integration | 120,000 | retain sovereign technical judgment |
| industrial design, production engineering, and pilot lines | 210,000 | build multiple physical roots and transition to scale |
| construction, utility, clinical, and operating partners | 95,000 | represent real service and receiver conditions |
| universities, technical colleges, and apprenticeships | 45,000 | create disciplines and recurrent workforce |
| allied qualification and export-standard teams | 30,000 | develop plural, interoperable supply systems |
| **peak affiliated enterprise** | **500,000** | not additive to operating-force counts without reconciliation |

The model does not price these positions separately; labor is contained within the technology envelope. A later workforce pass must separate direct public billets from funded industrial and academic effort.

### 11.3 Industrial structure

The Department should prevent the emergence of one monolithic resilience prime. System integration can exist at Lockheed Martin scale while the government retains interface law, test data, reference implementations, and the ability to re-compete modules.

Each critical family should pursue at least two physically meaningful roots. Separate logos using the same power electronics, resin system, reactor vendor, software kernel, skilled-labor pool, port, or test facility are one root for the relevant failure.

The economic objective is a standing market for civil systems that work under degraded conditions: domestic force recapitalization, state and tribal preparedness, utility hardening, allied fleets, development finance, and exportable receiver standards. Post-disaster emergency appropriations alone cannot sustain learning.

## 12. Bases, arsenals, and national geography

### 12.1 A matrix of custody

Common capacity should be distributed among:

- regional launch and receiver stations;
- mission-adjacent common detachments;
- separated national reserve arsenals;
- mode-specific transport and corridor depots;
- contaminated and clean regeneration campuses;
- industrial mobilization storage;
- schools and recurrent exercise sites; and
- independent evaluation and reference sites.

Central ownership does not require central storage. A fire group may hold nationally titled energy and transport articles under a standing warrant. What matters is unique reservation, local launch control, configuration currency, and a tested reallocation path.

### 12.2 Geography enters before optimization

The final compiler must represent travel time, weather, damaged corridors, load limits, crew staging, receiver acceptance, and base loss. National totals can pass while every relevant article sits on the wrong side of a disrupted continent.

Inventory must be indexed by location and accessible modes. A nominally available coastal carrier cannot satisfy an inland clock unless the complete transit, transfer, and receiver path closes.

### 12.3 Regeneration is a second geography

Repair, quarantine, wash, fuel, medical reprocessing, calibration, software signing, and recertification form a separate network. A fleet can deploy successfully and then collapse into one return queue.

The compiler therefore treats regeneration bays, specialist crews, replacement modules, evidence review, and production replenishment as capacity constraints equal to launch platforms.

## 13. Qualification campaign

### 13.1 From translation to substitution

Every proposed cross-portfolio credit must pass six stages:

1. **measurement bridge:** both missions express the requirement in compatible physical and service units;
2. **interface bridge:** a candidate article connects safely to each mission’s carriers, utilities, controls, and receivers;
3. **configuration bridge:** mission-specific modules and software can be installed, verified, and rolled back;
4. **crew bridge:** common and mission crews can operate, repair, and release the article;
5. **mission bridge:** representative service closes in both missions under damage and network loss; and
6. **reservation bridge:** simultaneous worlds, transit, reset, and root loss close with one article counted once.

Only the sixth stage creates quantity credit.

### 13.2 First campaign: power and distribution

Power is the best first seam because both the fire-control and `PRG-100` studies contain explicit energy quantities.

`RBY-1` should compete three relationships:

- adapt the existing `ENG-5/7` family into a mission-neutral utility core;
- adapt the `PRG-100` 10 MW source modules downward into smaller independently releasable islands; and
- create a new open-core family that both missions must beat.

The campaign should require:

- fire-control 20/35 MW duty with raw-water, command, repair, and onset constraints;
- `PRG-100` 100 MW net duty with five islands, ten feeders, storage, transformation, protection, and thermal allocation;
- hospital continuity duty with clinical power quality, low-acoustic behavior, cooling, and safe transfer;
- flood duty with wet, dirty, changing-load pump operation;
- access-cell duty after atmosphere or terrain carriage;
- loss of one module, control partition, fuel path, heat destination, supplier root, and depot;
- repeated cross-loading and rollback;
- full mission, return, repair, and re-dispatch; and
- blind independent reconstruction of every readiness claim.

The competition may conclude that several variants are required. A family can be common at the interface and evidence layers without using one physical prime mover or source.

### 13.3 Stop rules

A candidate receives no cross-portfolio quantity credit if:

- output is reached only by violating another mission’s quality or safety boundary;
- the changeover consumes the response clock;
- specialized crews are double counted;
- contamination or clinical state cannot be reset predictably;
- transport or receiver adapters become a single failure;
- a shared software or component root defeats nominal supplier diversity;
- one mission loses its protected reserve;
- the reset line cannot sustain the next dispatch; or
- evidence exists only in models or demonstrations chosen by the developer.

## 14. Governance of innovation

### 14.1 Failure is funded

The USD 200B reserve is released only to replace a failed architecture, develop a dissimilar root, or cross a scale transition whose prior basis has been falsified. It cannot cover ordinary overrun or conceal a service shortfall.

Each program must carry at least one lower-complexity rival and one dissimilar high-upside rival. The Department should be able to stop a celebrated platform while preserving the mission.

### 14.2 Public technical rights

For every common family, the government should own or irrevocably control:

- interface definitions;
- safety and evidence schemas;
- reference models and test fixtures;
- configuration and cryptographic signing rules;
- training and maintenance data needed for sovereign operation;
- field repair and depot rights;
- supplier substitution packages; and
- the right to disclose safety-critical failures.

Private companies can create valuable intellectual property around components and processes. They should not own the public ability to understand, repair, or re-compete a national service system.

### 14.3 Export architecture

Export should begin with interoperable service modules, receiver preparation, schools, metrology, and support agreements—not only complete flagships. Partner countries should be able to operate sovereign configurations and contribute modules to combined missions without surrendering their service data or relying on a permanent US software connection.

The export proposition is competence under environmental stress. It can become a major industrial instrument of alliance and development policy if the domestic force proves the systems continuously.

## 15. Stocktake

### 15.1 What became authoritative

- The National Common Force is sized from concurrent service obligations, not organization charts.
- Eleven fire-derived family quantities and USD 1.649267746T form a conserved known floor.
- Unknown portfolio translation is unavailable, never zero.
- Cross-portfolio quantity credit requires `G4` representative substitution plus a valid reservation schedule.
- The current translation field contains 88 cells, eleven exact family bills, and zero admitted substitution cells.
- Ten inherited demand worlds already disprove a simple maximum-of-portfolios rule.
- A common capability grammar should prioritize measurement, semantics, interfaces, modules, carriers, and only then whole formations.
- A USD 1.000T technology-convergence envelope can be governed as eight rival programs with a 25% failure and recompete reserve.

### 15.2 What was falsified

- functional resemblance as article equivalence;
- mass- or personnel-ratio estimates as procurement bills;
- one common pool serving several simultaneous readiness claims;
- summing differently scoped predecessor and successor budgets;
- reactor output as delivered water;
- kilometres of pipe laid as verified utility service;
- autonomy as removal of human authority and recovery; and
- common ownership as evidence of idle capacity.

### 15.3 Where the burden moved

The dominant uncertainty is no longer whether a common force is institutionally desirable. It is whether mission kernels can be translated into common families without importing new failure modes.

That burden moves to physical interface campaigns, mission-specific configuration bills, crew qualification, receiver integration, reset, and simultaneous reservation. It also moves to better medical and access service kernels, whose current mass and workforce statements do not yet expose complete support articles.

### 15.4 What remains provisional

- all non-fire common-family quantities;
- the eleven current family sizes and unit costs outside the fire reference;
- the canonical demand vectors;
- the national geography of bases and reserves;
- the 500,000-position technology-enterprise envelope;
- every technology-program allocation;
- the reactor and water-energy assumptions;
- any capital release or national total; and
- all exterior form.

### 15.5 Decision

Authorize `RBY-1`, the **Power-and-Distribution Cross-Portfolio Translation and Representative Substitution Constitution**.

It must turn the fire `ENG-5/7` and `PWR-20` families, the `PRG-100` source and distribution modules, medical critical-power duty, HCG pump duty, and TAFG carried-cell duty into rival physical configurations. It must issue exact bills, crews, transport states, receiver conditions, failure insertions, return cycles, and an article-level reservation experiment.

Do not reduce any quantity. Do not procure a common family. Do not select a carrier. Do not render an exterior.
