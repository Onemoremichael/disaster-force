## 1. The decision is an architecture, not a larger valve opening

The governing question is:

> Which complete source-transfer architecture can admit 500 kilograms, survive registered and faulted termination, receive the full low-density inventory, conserve mass and ancestry, return through a new source-exposed baseline, and remain independently decidable when normal control energy is lost?

The word **complete** is controlling. Route diameter alone is not an architecture. A candidate includes source metering, wetted path, compliant volume, isolation, diversion, receiver, measurement, local safety energy, passive final state, recovery interface, evidence originals and replaceable boundary. A favorable pipe calculation cannot cover an undersized receiver. A capable receiver cannot cover an unbounded stop transient. A fast control law cannot be its own decisive witness.

`RBE-1` therefore separates three authorities:

1. **candidate admission** permits a serialized nonenergetic article to enter the public competition;
2. **architecture admission** permits one frozen architecture baseline to populate the twelve `QE-04` cells; and
3. **event issue** permits one selected cell to receive one source package.

No authority implies the next. Parallel build and training may shorten calendar, but cannot manufacture evidence.

## 2. The inherited system is falsified at two independent boundaries

At nominal density 800 kilograms per cubic metre, 500 kilograms occupies 0.625 cubic metres. At the accepted low-density edge of 792 kilograms per cubic metre, the volume is approximately 0.631313 cubic metres. Adding the complete 5-kilogram expanded-uncertainty allowance as a conservative volume term produces approximately 0.637626 cubic metres before heel, foam, calibration liquid or unusable geometry. The inherited receiver supplies only 0.40 cubic metres usable. It fails before route selection begins.

The inherited 40-millimetre route also fails the same-schedule transient screen. For mass \(m\), density \(\rho\), schedule \(t\), route diameter \(D\), length \(L\) and wave-speed screen \(a\):

\[
V=\frac{m}{\rho},\quad \dot V=\frac{V}{t},\quad A=\frac{\pi D^2}{4},\quad v=\frac{\dot V}{A},\quad \Delta P_s=\rho a v.
\]

For 500 kilograms, 120 seconds, 40 millimetres and \(a=1{,}000\) metres per second, velocity is approximately 4.1447 metres per second and the instantaneous stop screen is approximately 3.3157 megapascals. At the 110-percent qualification bracket it is approximately 3.6473 megapascals. Both exceed the 2-megapascal design boundary.

Steady friction is not the controlling problem. The level-route Blasius screen is approximately 48.334 kilopascals and a two-degree grade contributes approximately 3.286 kilopascals. Increasing normal operating allowance would address the wrong mechanism. The competition must change time, geometry, topology or stop-energy physics.

## 6. Architecture admission without a weighted winner

A weighted sum could allow excellent convenience to compensate for unsafe termination. `RBE-1` prohibits that logic. Selection has three stages.

**Stage one: conjunctive physical gates.** All eighteen trials for an architecture–lineage pair across three campuses must pass pressure, isolation, receiver, evidence, mass-closure, final-state and damage gates. One valid adverse result is retained and denies that pair. Missing decisive evidence cannot become a pass.

**Stage two: architecture replication.** Both unlike lineages must pass. Campus spread, lineage spread and fault response remain reported separately. A national average cannot erase one failed site or one failed lineage.

**Stage three: minimax and lexicographic decision.** Among architectures that pass, the board first minimizes the worst registered safety margin across trial families. It then compares, in order, mass-closure uncertainty, terminal residual, recovery burden, fault-state complexity, replaceable-boundary burden, public energy dependence, workforce and cost. The board reports the complete vector. It does not convert it to one opaque score.

The result may be `NONE`, `ONE`, or `MULTIPLE`. `NONE` returns the program to technology development. `MULTIPLE` preserves more than one admitted baseline for later class comparison. The first event still draws from one frozen architecture population and cannot mix components across candidates.

## 19. Research stocktake

`RBE-1` changes the project in six ways.

First, scale escalation now changes architecture before it changes authority. The Department does not ask an accepted small system to absorb a known contradiction.

Second, innovation is organized as competing physical explanations. Time, bore, topology, passive energy storage and causal control receive the same public interface and fault burden. Novel candidates are welcome, but future promise cannot satisfy present evidence.

Third, the receiver becomes a first-class experimental object. Usable volume, trapped gas, inaccessible liquid, recovery geometry and casualty diversion matter as much as nominal capacity.

Fourth, selection law becomes part of engineering. Conjunctive gates and minimax review prevent convenience, speed or cost from paying for unsafe termination.

Fifth, the Department's industrial role becomes clearer. It can create new markets for passive fluid safety, rapid process cassettes, self-measuring receivers, local control, robotic recovery and sovereign evidence.
