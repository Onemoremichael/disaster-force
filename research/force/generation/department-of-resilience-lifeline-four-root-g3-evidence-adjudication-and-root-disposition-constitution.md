## One event, six independent judgments

Every valid event receives six signed judgments:

1. **Validity:** the frozen configuration, blind, inject, clock, calibration and raw custody either held or did not.
2. **Safety and rights:** the system remained inside the authorized safety envelope, issued no unsafe or unauthorized service and respected allocation and access rules.
3. **Public service:** water, residual, power and thermal outputs met the event-specific public-interface thresholds.
4. **Causal reliance:** the evidence distinguishes root behavior from range, receiver or external causes.
5. **Outside replay:** an independent team reconstructs the event from original records and reaches a compatible result.
6. **Reset:** the article returns to its frozen state within the inherited 720-hour ceiling, when reset is due.

The 168-event campaign therefore creates 1,008 event-axis judgments. These sit beside 636 package acceptances, forty-eight ancestry decisions, fifty-six gate decisions and four final root dispositions: 1,752 governed decisions. The counts are registers, not a weighted score. A missing judgment remains missing.

The operating root may make a service claim, but cannot validate its own attempt or accept its own evidence. Validity belongs to the Test Integrity Authority; safety and rights to the Civil Safety and Rights Authority; public service to route-specific public-effect authorities; causal reliance and replay to the Sovereign Evidence Authority; ancestry to the Manufacturing Independence Authority; reset to the Regeneration Authority; and money to the Program Comptroller. No person or subordinate organization may sign more than one of validity, claim and reliance. The `G3` board can aggregate accepted records but cannot cure one.

## Invalid, adverse and terminal

`INVALID` is narrow. A breached blind, wrong configuration, out-of-tolerance inject, failed independent evidence plane, unreconstructable clock or broken raw custody can invalidate an attempt only through an outside validity decision. The exact event may be repeated against the same root, article role and threshold. It consumes one of nine reserve attempts for that root and creates no denominator or credit. A tenth invalid attempt stops the campaign and leaves the root on `HOLD`; it does not make the machine pass or fail.

A valid adverse result remains in the denominator. A safely contained service miss, repair failure, reset failure or second-team reproduction mismatch fails the tested baseline but may identify a correctable configuration mechanism. The baseline becomes `FAIL—SUCCESSOR ELIGIBLE`; no repair can rewrite its result.

Six conditions terminate the lineage immediately:

- unsafe or unauthorized public issue;
- an unbounded radiological, thermal, pressure, chemical, electrical or environmental state;
- a rights violation caused by the tested allocation or control system;
- evidence tampering, withholding or outcome-aware reconfiguration;
- decisive-ancestry collision or convergence on another root; or
- any valid adverse result on the one permitted successor baseline.

These produce `KILL`. Honest technical failure remains public evidence; concealment and unsafe issue are not treated as learning-equivalent events.

## Successor means a new baseline

Each root lineage may constitute at most one successor inside this branch. A contained adverse result on `CF1` freezes that configuration permanently and permits `CF2` only after the causal mechanism, proposed correction and unchanged decisive ancestry are registered before construction. `CF2` starts at zero: all forty-two valid events, 159 evidence packages, twelve ancestry acceptances and fourteen gates must be earned again. Neither successful `CF1` events nor repaired hardware confer release credit.

Changing source physics or any of the twelve decisive ancestry domains is not a revision. It kills the old lineage and returns the root slot to the Pass 175 competition under a new identity. The replacement must remain unlike the three surviving roots. Cascadia `G4` still requires four released lifeline roots; “three of four” is not a diversity standard.

## Release is conjunctive

A root reaches `RELEASE` only when all forty-two required events are valid and pass, all 159 packages are accepted, all twelve ancestry identities are declared and unique, every outside replay agrees, all fourteen `G3` gates pass, and no terminal condition exists. Any missing record or unresolved replay conflict produces `HOLD`. One contained adverse result produces `FAIL—SUCCESSOR ELIGIBLE` on `CF1`; a terminal condition or an adverse `CF2` produces `KILL`. No average, confidence score, schedule urgency or performance advantage can compensate.

Originals must enter independent custody within seventy-two hours of an event. Validity closes by day seven, service claims by day twenty-one, causal reliance by day forty-five and draft root disposition by day sixty. The final board acts within ninety days of the complete packet. An incomplete packet at day ninety remains `HOLD`; time does not convert absence into failure or success.

## Failure economy

The USD 36.0B boundary remains unchanged. Its five program classes are cross-classified into five cumulative release stages: 9 percent for baseline constitution, 27 percent for ranges and tooling, 31 percent for paired `P1/P2` construction, 13 percent for cold `R1` construction and 20 percent for trials, replay and disposition. A root stopped after those stages has exposed approximately USD 0.809B, 3.24B, 6.03B, 7.20B or 8.99B respectively. Unreleased authority returns to the lifeline mission account; it is not divided among surviving roots.

The 5-percent failure reserve is USD 1.80B program-wide, or USD 0.450B per root. Dividing the root ceiling by nine reserve attempts yields an arithmetic maximum of about USD 50.0M per invalid attempt, not a price or entitlement. The reserve may fund identical-event repetition, safety teardown and restoration after a range-caused invalidity. It may not finance a valid-adverse retry, ordinary overrun, winner subsidy or root convergence.

Payment follows accepted work and evidence completeness, not favorable outcome. A valid adverse test can earn its completed evidence tranche; a hidden result cannot. A successor draws only from unspent root authority and separately released common reserve inside the conserved boundary. If that cannot finance the successor, the root remains failed until the parent program reallocates authority; the board may not borrow from another live root.
