**Resolution test:** reversing this selection would change which physical truths the Department builds first, how much of the USD 200B tranche enters loss, and whether one lineage in every mission system can be killed or resized before the full root estate is constructed.

The object passes because it controls the first irreversible physical portfolio rather than a subordinate component detail.

## Decision

Authorize a **six-root sentinel wave**: one deliberately vulnerable root in each mission system, tested through both its limiting balance and its causal keystone. Construct the public truth estate needed to judge those roots, but not the other eighteen root lanes.

Five alternatives were tested against eight conjunctive gates. Balance-first cannot kill a mechanism. Keystone-first cannot distinguish a useful mechanism from an undersized configuration. Receiver-bootstrap builds an apparatus before candidate and public work can falsify one another. Full-estate construction exposes all twenty-four roots before the Department has learned whether its method works. Only compound falsification preserves all six systems, both endpoint types, three independent rig roles, two unlike public realizations, bounded loss and a reusable estate without excess root construction.

This overturns the implicit assumption that “all-six” requires simultaneous construction of all twenty-four candidates. It also overturns the five-system limit of the earlier Proving Service: lifelines now receive the same challenge, counterfactual and reconstruction separation as the other systems.[^1]

## Public physical-truth estate

The Resilience Proving Service forms eighteen functional station nuclei: one challenge, one matched-control and one cold-reconstruction station in each system. Each begins with one sentinel lane and one regeneration lane. The result is thirty-six initial lanes; fifty-four additional root lanes remain unbuilt. Adding those later would produce the ninety-lane all-six estate.

All twenty-four receiver states and seventy-two method cards enter because no selected root may pass at its machine boundary while failing a public handoff. Each card receives two physically unlike implementations, creating 144 realization lineages. These are not 144 buildings. They are controlled physical worlds, inserts, fixtures and calibration chains distributed across the station nuclei.

The technology program is correspondingly first-principles: programmable wave-load fields and self-forming shore interfaces; sacrificial multistory skins and robotic utility insertion; closed atmospheric flux ranges and drop-field tomography; multi-geology heat–water loops and autonomous corridor construction; acuity-state clinical campuses and closed patient-path custody; and disconnected material-qualification factories. Public commonality ends at service meaning, interfaces, calibration and evidence portability. It may not supply candidate planning, estimation, control, safeing, energy management or build logic.

## Conserved authority and maximum loss

One of four roots per system receives one quarter of its system’s stageable cohort option. The six-root private ceiling is therefore USD 3.59B. The existing USD 12.0B public-method reservation is sufficient for the complete reusable method grid. Combined construction authority is USD 15.6B, nested inside the existing tranche rather than added to it.[^3]

Public authority releases 25/50/0/25 percent at method qualification, paired construction, valid endpoint trial and cold replay. Private authority releases 15/35/35/15 percent through `FRAME`, `MAKE`, `BREAK` and `TRANSFER`.

If the common method fails at its first gate, paid exposure stops at USD 3.54B. If all six roots are validly killed at `BREAK`, cumulative paid exposure stops at USD 12.0B: USD 9.00B of reusable public estate and USD 3.05B of accepted private evidence. USD 3.54B remains unreleased. The largest single-root kill exposure is USD 0.765B. The USD 10.0B protected failure reserve remains untouched unless a predeclared invalidity or public reconstitution event actually occurs.

## Cancellation law

A realization disagreement holds the affected method and every dependent event; the results cannot be averaged. A balance endpoint whose upper uncertainty bound is below 1.00 orders `RESIZE`. The same result on a keystone endpoint orders `KILL`. An interval crossing 1.00, a missing package or a lost realization produces `HOLD`. An ancestry collision, terminal rights breach or attempt beyond the two-attempt invalidity cap kills the lineage.

Valid adverse work is payable through the stage it completes, but `TRANSFER` is withheld after a kill. Unearned authority returns to the owning mission system and cannot subsidize a winner. Two supported endpoints still require cold outside reconstruction; even then the result is only `BUILD PROOF` eligibility. No system may open proof construction until all four roots have non-`HOLD` dispositions and every killed lineage has a genuinely unlike replacement.[^4]
