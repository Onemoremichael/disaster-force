## Resolution test

If this result were reversed, the first USD 15.6B physical wave could create either a competitive resilience industrial base or a hidden national prime able to manufacture every proof result; the number, control and separability of the first construction awards would change.

## The decision

The Department should procure the eighteen station nuclei and six sovereign truth trains as a **102-lot severable construction lattice**, not as one national engineering contract, six system campuses, eighteen station primes or hundreds of component purchases.

Those alternatives fail for different reasons. A national prime reunites every decisive root. Six system primes let one builder control candidate, challenge and reconstruction. Eighteen station primes preserve role separation but collapse the two method lineages and the regeneration chain inside each station. Component atomization separates parts while orphaning complete physical responsibility. The selected lattice keeps a complete accountable object at every lot boundary while preventing any one actor from owning the causal chain.

## Industrial constitution

A contractor name is not an independent root. Control follows beneficial ownership, debt covenants, decisive subcontractors, tooling, executable build descriptions, calibration standards, cryptographic keys and accepting personnel.

Six private candidate lots require six separate Root Houses. A candidate controller may hold no public lot in its system and no accepting authority. Among the seventy-two public decisive lots, one beneficial-control group may hold at most two nationally, never within the same system, never both method lineages, and never the rig and regeneration chain at the same station. This requires at least thirty-six decisive control groups. Boundary work requires at least six additional control groups; none may combine a station's substrate and integration lot. No group crosses the private-candidate, public-decisive or public-boundary classes.

The result is a floor of **forty-eight beneficial-control groups**: six candidate, thirty-six decisive and six boundary. This is not a small-business quota. It is the industrial topology needed to let firms grow to national scale inside a narrow physics domain without allowing one balance sheet, toolchain or accepting office to become the truth system. A nominally common product may cross roots only as a nondecisive commodity interface. If its failure, tuning or measurement can change an outcome, it is decisive and must separate.

## Acceptance is a construction product

Every lot owes five packet families: configuration and as-built state; ownership and ancestry; commissioning and metrology; safety, rights and residuals; and custody plus cold reconstruction. The lattice therefore creates 510 packet records. Each lot must also pass seven noncompensatory gates:

1. identity and beneficial control are admissible;
2. the issue baseline is frozen;
3. materials, interfaces and as-built state reconcile;
4. decisive paths are physically isolated;
5. native commissioning closes;
6. controlled denial or destructive witness is valid; and
7. an outside team can reconstruct the accepted state.

That creates 714 governed decisions. A requirement issuer, control registrar, physical commissioning authority, safety-and-rights authority, Evidence Court and comptroller hold separate signatures. No builder or station integrator may accept its own work. The integrator may connect public interfaces, but cannot change a rig, controller, calibration, safeing rule or evidentiary threshold.

## Release and money

Release proceeds in seven irreversible steps. `R0` constitutes all 102 empty lot records. `R1` freezes the complete control and dependency map; no partial award may precede a collision-free national slate. `R2` admits the six substrate packages. `R3` releases all six system-complete cohorts together: seventy-two public decisive lots and six private candidate options. `R4` admits the eighteen integrators only after every incoming object's as-built state is accepted. `R5 BREAK` performs station and system interlocks, including destructive denial. `R6 COLD RETURN` requires outside reconstruction and regeneration before transfer.

Public construction retains a 25/50/0/25 percent `FRAME/MAKE/BREAK/TRANSFER` schedule: valid adverse evidence at `BREAK` is binding but earns no additional public construction payment. Public exposure through `MAKE` is therefore USD 9.00B. Candidate options retain 15/35/35/15 percent: USD 1.79B through `MAKE` and USD 3.05B through `BREAK`. Combined exposure through `MAKE` is at most USD 10.8B. If all six candidates are validly killed at `BREAK`, total paid exposure is about USD 12.0B and USD 3.54B remains unreleased. Failure is financed as knowledge, but construction completion cannot be purchased with a favorable claim.

It creates independent industries in dynamic marine exchange, occupied-building utility insertion, atmospheric source-field control, coupled heat–water truth bodies, clinical-load synthesis, disconnected metrology, destructive reconstruction and public-boundary integration.
