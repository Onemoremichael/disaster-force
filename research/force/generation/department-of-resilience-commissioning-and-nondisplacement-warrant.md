The minimum modeled headroom remains five; about 3,020 active records pass and 216 lost-site records are correctly offline. Thus all thirty architectural capacity worlds pass without borrowing truth capacity.

That is a design proof, not measured non-displacement. `CNW-1` requires instrumentation of the six local resource classes, the actual truth cold-run schedule and six recovery clocks in every world. Any draw into the truth reservation, missing observer record or use of aggregate capacity in place of resource-specific capacity forces `HOLD`. Capacity cannot be inferred from the USD540M ceiling, installed equipment or a completed drill.
