## Decision

`AIP-1` establishes the minimum build-to-test program for the twenty-four technology orders issued by `PIL-1`. Each order receives two independently controlled builder roots. Each root produces three nonfungible serials:

- `INT` is an instrumented integration article. It supports development and calibration but carries no acceptance weight.
- `BRK` is configuration-frozen before its fatal cut and is consumed by that event.
- `RCN` is a virgin article reconstructed by a clean team from the public package, with origin-builder keys, staff and live coaching denied.

The serial identities cannot be reassigned. An integration article accumulates repair history and privileged knowledge; relabelling it `BRK` makes the destructive result endogenous to development. Rebuilding that same wreck does not prove public reconstruction. Therefore the minimum program is six full-scale articles per technology order, not one prototype used three times.

Across cohort one, this means 144 articles: forty-eight each of `INT`, `BRK` and `RCN`. Ninety-six serials are acceptance-bearing, but none has been built or accepted.

## Industrial constitution

Each technology order receives two root-bound tooling cells and two public-title proving fixtures: one challenge site and one matched-control site. The result is forty-eight builder lines, forty-eight tooling cells and forty-eight proving fixtures. Candidate ownership may not extend to the fixture, control article, event authorization, raw-data escrow, metrology chain or acceptance court.

This is a new industrial base, not a contracting pool. The Department buys severable technical data, replaceable process recipes, public fixture title and independent rebuild capacity together with hardware. Tooling follows the builder root; the challenge mechanism follows the Proving Service. Cross-use can reduce idle time only after an independence finding and cannot merge causal boundaries or metrology chains.

Thirty-six measurement packets preserve form-native instruments and return intervals, not favorable midpoints. The 144 serial-bound events comprise forty-eight development events and ninety-six fatal-cut or cold-reconstruction events. Source-specific critical paths range from about 4.5 years for `CT-C-B` and `RT-B-C` to eight years for `LF-B-A`; these are planning programs, not delivery promises.
