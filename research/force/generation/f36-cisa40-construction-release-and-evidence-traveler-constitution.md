# F36 `CISA-40` construction-release and evidence-traveler constitution

## Executive judgment

The `CISA-40` cannot earn construction release through a complete drawing package alone. A drawing describes an intended object. Construction creates a sequence of physical states, many of which erase access to the very surfaces, process histories and causal evidence later needed to judge the object. The governing problem is therefore not whether a builder can make a module. It is whether two unlike industrial roots can make, stop, inspect, repair, reject, reproduce and finally cut apart their modules without losing the public questions the articles exist to answer.

This pass creates `CRET-1`: the first construction-release and evidence-traveler constitution beneath `BRD-1` and `BPT-1`. It does not release either root to build. It defines what construction release would mean. An operation becomes a controlled state transition on a serialized object. An irreversible operation may begin only when its material identity, released instruction, qualified people and machines, calibrated fixture, bounded process variables, adjacent witness plan, inspection hold point, nonconformance route and downstream inspectability are all present. The event record must preserve both the command and the observed process. A signature without native measurements is not completion.

`CRET-1` assigns forty-four root-native construction operations, twenty-eight root-specific hold points, thirty-two controlled material lots, seventy-two known-defect standards, twelve full-scale subarticles, thirty-six minimum event fields, twelve independent reconstruction exercises and sixty destructive cut locations. Root A remains a welded 690-MPa-class steel, super-duplex hydraulic and copper-bus system. Root B remains a CF-PAEK shell-grid, composite hydraulic and aluminum-bus system. The two roots share public functions, coordinates and decision rules; they do not share decisive manufacturing mechanisms.

The pass also corrects an unresolved physical issue in the preceding energy configuration. Fuel consumption removes mass and can change support reactions and center of gravity during an eight-hour run. The proposed response is not generic ballast. Each root receives four independently weighed `EBC-1650` energy–ballast cassettes at longitudinal stations distributed through the module. Root A uses a double-wall liquid-fuel bladder inside a separately contained recovered-water chamber. Root B uses a pressure-rated DME cylinder within a structurally separate annular water chamber. A commanded, metered water transfer replaces consumed fuel mass at the cassette where it was consumed. The target replacement rates—approximately 0.334 tonnes per hour for Root A and 0.451 tonnes per hour for Root B—are hydraulically modest, but the safety, custody and center-of-gravity consequences are not. No shared wall may separate fuel from public water, and replacement water is non-potable process inventory until independently released.

The program is a sixteen-month, 188-person planning and subarticle-preparation increment nested inside the unused `BRD-1` boundary. It allocates USD 0.090B of capital and USD 0.020B/year, leaving USD 0.016B capital and USD 0.003B/year unassigned inside that parent boundary. Forty-eight planning gates can close. Twenty-four construction, physical-evidence and downstream gates remain open or blocked. No article, campaign, production or exterior-rendering authority is created.

The strategic result is consequential for the larger Department of Resilience. A mature resilience force cannot buy credible industrial capacity by issuing performance specifications and auditing final units. It needs a public construction grammar that makes material ancestry, process observability, defect-detection ability, repair consequence and independent reproduction into acquisition deliverables. In a military analogue, the traveler is not clerical quality paperwork. It is the equivalent of configuration control, range instrumentation, flight-test telemetry and munitions custody combined at the factory operation level.

## 1. Decision and boundary

### 1.1 The decision

This pass asks one question:

> What exact operation, evidence and stopping constitution must exist before either `CISA-40` root may cross from a released design package into irreversible construction?

The answer must preserve six public decisions:

1. whether the article that was built is the article that was authorized;
2. whether its decisive joints and interfaces were made inside bounded processes;
3. whether inspection could detect the defects that matter at the time detection mattered;
4. whether repair, rework or concession changed a load, sanitary, electrical, energy or evidence question;
5. whether an independent authority can reconstruct the article state without the builder's proprietary system; and
6. whether teardown can reveal hidden condition without destroying the causal record first.

### 1.2 What this pass does not decide

`CRET-1` does not establish material allowables, qualify a weld, bond, collar, bus, protection scheme, thermal panel or fuel system, or prove root independence. It does not authorize purchase of production tooling. It does not admit a `CISA-40` article to `CRC-E01`. It does not change the 12.0 × 3.6 × 3.8 m public envelope, the 40.000-tonne configured mass, the 30.900-tonne named physical target, the 1.100-tonne uncertainty allowance, the public support, anchor, lift, hydraulic, electrical, receiver or evidence interfaces, or the two-root principle established in [Pass 112](f36-cisa40-root-build-package-and-tolerance-stack-constitution.md).

The design remains intentionally internal. Permitted drawings are process-state maps, sections, joints, fixtures, measurement routes, tolerance loops and cut plans. Exterior form remains blocked.

## 2. Why drawings and end inspection are insufficient

Manufacturing does not simply reduce a drawing to matter. It introduces state. Plate obtains a heat history; weld restraint creates residual stress; a composite surface ages after preparation; a bondline becomes hidden; a seal is compressed; a conductor contact oxidizes; a software key becomes active; fuel enters a pressure boundary; and a measurement cable can be trapped behind an inaccessible closure. The final object does not reveal every state that produced it.

End inspection fails for three distinct reasons. First, some defects are not observable after closure. Second, a clean instrument image has no demonstrated meaning unless the instrument, procedure and analyst have confronted controlled defects of relevant type, scale, location and access. Third, even a detectable defect may no longer be separable from damage introduced by teardown, proof loading or later service.

The construction constitution therefore controls evidence before geometry disappears. It treats the builder's act, the public witness and the future forensic question as one coupled operation.

## 3. The constitutional object: a legal physical state

### 3.1 State-transition rule

Every controlled operation has the form:

`released pre-state + authorized inputs + observed transformation → measured post-state + evidence objects + disposition`

An operation is complete only when all six terms exist. A part can be physically joined and still remain in an incomplete legal state. It cannot enter the next irreversible operation merely because schedule or shop flow demands it.

### 3.2 Eight release predicates

Before an irreversible event, the traveler must show:

| Predicate | Required condition | Automatic stop if absent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| object | article, assembly, part, lot and configuration identities agree | identity mismatch or duplicate serial |
| authority | current instruction and drawing revision are released for this serial | superseded or unsigned baseline |
| capability | operator, machine, program and procedure qualifications are in force | expired or out-of-scope qualification |
| fixture | datum, restraint, calibration and uncertainty are valid | calibration lapse or failed pre-survey |
| process | limits, sampling rate and observed variables are defined | command-only record or unbounded variable |
| witness | adjacent coupon, reference standard or retained sample is assigned before work | witness selected after result |
| inspectability | required surface and sensor routes remain available after the event | closure would hide an unanswered question |
| consequence | hold authority, rejection rule and nonconformance branch are named | no authority empowered to stop the work |

All eight are conjunctive. Schedule pressure cannot trade one predicate against another.

### 3.3 Event, not form

A traveler event is a machine-readable evidence object. Its human-readable sheet is only one view. The native package contains time-series measurements, instrument metadata, imagery, programs, measurements, signatures, material splits and hashes. If the builder's system exports only a PDF, the event is incomplete.

## 4. Authority at the factory operation

Five authorities act at different times:

| Authority | May do | May not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| builder operation authority | prepare work, execute a released reversible or irreversible operation, declare an anomaly | accept its own public hold point |
| root engineering authority | own root-native process definition and technical disposition below public consequence | redefine a public interface or erase original state |
| independent inspection authority | witness, measure, challenge known-defect capability and accept inspection evidence | originate the inspected operation |
| public configuration and safety authority | stop work, control public characteristics, reopen baseline and quarantine an object | direct the builder to continue through uncertainty |
| independent reproduction authority | reconstruct events and article state from native objects | repair missing evidence by accepting narrative substitutes |

A hold point is an authority transfer. It is not a checkbox. The builder relinquishes the right to advance the object; the receiving authority either releases the next named operation or changes the object's state to quarantine, rework, reject or baseline-reopen.

## 8. Material splitting as conservation and experiment design

### 8.1 Five-way split

Every decisive received lot is split before article results are known into:

1. article material;
2. qualification and process-control coupons;
3. known-defect standards;
4. destructive witnesses; and
5. retained archive or quarantine material.

Scrap is a sixth recorded disposition, not an untracked remainder. At every transition:

`received = article + coupon + standard + witness + scrap + retained`

Mass is not always the correct unit. Tape may reconcile by roll, length and mass; fasteners by count and lot; resin by container and mass; software by hash and signed release; seals by count and batch. The ledger uses the unit that makes substitution or unexplained loss observable.

### 8.2 Thirty-two lot families

Each root controls sixteen decisive families. Root A includes primary steel, node steel, pins, structural filler, pipe, pipe filler, collars, fasteners, seals, valves, copper conductor, contact plating, insulation, fuel containment, storage cells and thermal surfaces. Root B includes structural tape, matrix resin, node metal, post-tension ties, pipe reinforcement, liner, collar reinforcement, bond material, seals, valves, aluminum conductor, contact treatment, insulation, DME containment, storage electrolyte/membrane and thermal surfaces.

Article-adjacent witnesses must be made from the same lot and through the same decisive process episode wherever physics permits. “Representative” stock from another heat, roll, resin age or machine campaign is not adjacent evidence.

### 8.3 What can be co-produced

Root A can co-produce weld run-on/run-off tabs, restrained T-joints, groove coupons, heat-affected-zone hardness strips, pipe weld rings, passivation coupons, collar preload witnesses, plated-contact pairs and coating panels. Root B can co-produce consolidated witness panels, induction seam strips, bonded insert plates, post-tension anchor tabs, pipe rings, lined sanitary sections, collar bond strips, treated aluminum contacts and thermal laminates.

Some questions cannot be answered by small coupons. A lift-node transfer, reaction-frame distortion, collar bending, electrical fault arc, panel deployment interaction or energy–ballast barrier requires a geometrically faithful subarticle. `CRET-1` therefore forbids coupon success from closing full-scale load-path or coupled-system gates.

## 9. Known-defect standards and demonstrated detection

A clean scan does not demonstrate detection ability. Each of the twenty-four joint families defined in `BPT-1` receives three controlled defect severities: below the declared decision threshold, at the threshold and above the threshold. This creates seventy-two standards.

Root A standards include incomplete fusion, planar crack analogues, lack of penetration, undercut, pore clusters, collar groove error, seal damage, bolt preload loss, passivation residue and high-resistance copper contacts. Root B standards include porosity, delamination, fiber waviness, foreign material, bondline starvation/contamination, insert debond, liner discontinuity, collar disbond, seal damage, tie preload loss and high-resistance aluminum contacts.

The defect standard has four identities: intended defect, metrologically established defect, blinded presentation and post-test retained object. Fabrication intent alone is not truth. The defect must be independently measured by a method whose uncertainty is small relative to the severity separation.

For high-consequence detection claims, the planning criterion is at least twenty-nine independent realizations at the governing threshold with zero misses to support a one-sided 95-percent lower bound of approximately 90 percent under the simple independent Bernoulli model. Repeated readings of one defect are not independent realizations. The workbook therefore treats the seventy-two initial standards as library architecture, not as statistical qualification. A later campaign must create the replicated population and model operator, instrument, access, geometry and root effects explicitly.

One missed above-threshold defect is an automatic stop. The affected inspection procedure cannot release article work until cause is known, the method is revised, new blinded standards are presented and already accepted surfaces within the affected scope are reassessed.

These counts are screening hypotheses, not life qualification.

## 11. The `EBC-1650` coupled energy–ballast cell

### 11.1 Why the cell exists

The 40.000-tonne Root A and Root B energy configurations in `BPT-1` balance dry hardware, service inventory, fuel and ballast at the start of operation. Fuel use then removes 3.2 or 4.4 tonnes over the modeled run. If the fuel is concentrated or consumed unevenly, support reactions and CG can depart from the state used for qualification. Treating ballast as a static line item is therefore inadequate.

### 11.2 Four distributed cassettes

Each root uses four cassettes, nominally centered at longitudinal stations 2.5, 4.8, 7.2 and 9.5 m. Each bay accepts no more than 1.650 tonnes of controlled fluid inventory and reports mass through redundant load measurement independent of fluid meters. Root A begins with approximately 0.800 t fuel and 0.825 t controlled ballast per cassette. Root B begins with approximately 1.100 t DME and 0.525 t ballast per cassette. The exact distribution is trimmed to the as-built CG and locked in the configuration record.

Fuel is drawn proportionally across the four stations unless a fault-isolation sequence is under test. Recovered non-potable water enters the paired chamber at the same station. The nominal full-output replacement rates are:

- Root A: `1.5 MW / 0.38 / 11.83 MWh/t ≈ 0.334 t/h` total;
- Root B: `1.5 MW / 0.42 / 7.92 MWh/t ≈ 0.451 t/h` total.

The corresponding average water flows are only about 0.093 and 0.125 kg/s, but rate adequacy does not close safety. Meter bias, trapped volume, slosh, DME pressure state, water temperature, simultaneous isolation and load-cell drift govern the custody claim.

### 11.3 Unlike containment

Root A uses a removable double-wall fuel bladder inside a rigid cassette. The replacement-water chamber is structurally and hydraulically separate, with interstitial leak detection between fuel and water boundaries. Root B uses a pressure-rated DME cylinder supported within an annular water ballast shell. The cylinder and water shell have no common fluid barrier. The annulus cannot be credited as secondary fuel containment unless tested for the full DME hazard independently.

Neither architecture permits a single flexible membrane to be the only separator between fuel and water. No replacement water becomes potable inventory. It remains a quarantined process fluid until sampled and released through the sanitary authority.

### 11.4 Control and degraded states

The control law holds total cassette mass within a planning band of ±0.010 t and cross-cassette imbalance within ±0.020 t while reporting both meter-derived and load-cell-derived custody. A discrepancy greater than 0.005 t per cassette, unexplained mass gain, interstitial alarm, barrier pressure anomaly, incompatible valve lineup, lost independent weighing or inability to establish safe isolation stops automatic transfer.

Generation may continue in a bounded degraded state only if the public safety authority has pre-authorized the state and support/CG limits remain measured. The article no longer claims 40-tonne mass-preserving equivalence. The degraded event is evidence, not a hidden operational success.

## 12. Nonconformance is a branch, not a note

Five nonconformance classes govern:

| Class | Meaning | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `N0` | documentation defect with no physical or configuration consequence | correct with full history; independent document check |
| `N1` | reversible discrepancy before decisive transformation | restore released pre-state and repeat readiness checks |
| `N2` | bounded repair or rework with retained inspectability | root engineering proposal, independent inspection, new adjacent witness and repeat hold |
| `N3` | primary load, pressure, sanitary, protection, fuel barrier or evidence-path consequence | quarantine; public board disposition; no “use as is” |
| `N4` | public interface, requirement, root-independence or baseline consequence | revoke construction release and reopen governing baseline |

Every branch preserves the original observation, the pre-repair surface, the proposed causal explanation, the authority decision, the repair process, added witness material, repeated inspection and downstream consequence. A repair cannot overwrite the original scan or traveler. Adjacent parts made since the last demonstrated-good state enter the affected population until bounded by evidence.

Root A repair must address removed base metal, new heat cycles, distortion, coating removal and inspectability. Root B repair must address damage-map expansion, surface preparation age, parent laminate knockdown, scarf geometry, cure/consolidation history and re-inspection access. Repeated repair beyond a declared count becomes `N3`, even if each local result appears acceptable.

## 13. Destructive examination without destroying the question

Teardown order is frozen before article outcome is known. Each root has thirty planned cuts distributed across primary nodes, lift/anchor transfers, hydraulic closures, sanitary surfaces, bus contacts, energy barriers, thermal interfaces and evidence routes.

The cut law has four stages:

1. preserve intact functional state through external survey, leak, resistance, insulation, waveform and contamination observations;
2. immobilize fluids, energy and movable parts without cutting the question surface;
3. remove a sacrificial perimeter that leaves the governing interface intact for imaging and metrology; and
4. section the preserved interface only after non-destructive and surface evidence is complete.

Mirror halves are assigned before cutting. One goes to public forensic custody, one to the root authority or independent laboratory. Kerf loss, orientation, tool, coolant, contamination blank, images and chain of custody are recorded. If only one specimen exists, destructive order proceeds from least intrusive to most intrusive. A cut that would sever both the causal feature and its reference datum is prohibited.

For sanitary questions, product-contact surfaces are sampled before introducing cutting coolant or abrasive debris. For electrical contacts, torque/preload, resistance and thermal images precede disassembly. For composite damage, 3-D NDE registration precedes sectioning so polished micrographs can be mapped to the intact volume. For welds, the scan coordinate, indication and metallographic plane share a common datum.

## 15. Independent reconstruction

The independent reproduction authority executes twelve exercises:

1. reconstruct a received lot into every resulting object and remainder;
2. rebuild a structural joint's process trace and adjacent witnesses;
3. align a raw NDE volume to the article coordinate system;
4. determine which surfaces were hidden at each closure event;
5. reproduce a support/anchor/lift datum chain;
6. rebuild a sanitary cleaning, blank and sample chain;
7. reproduce a collar's geometry, preload and cycle history;
8. reproduce bus contact state, protection settings and a fault event;
9. reconcile each energy–ballast cassette's mass and fluid custody over a simulated run;
10. reconstruct a nonconformance through repair and repeated inspection;
11. map an intact observation to a destructive cut and retained item; and
12. create a terminal article manifest of installed, removed, consumed, repaired, destroyed and archived objects.

Success means a second authority can produce the same state and identify the same open questions from controlled native objects. It does not mean that it can repeat the builder's narrative. Any missing parent object, ambiguous coordinate transformation, unverified hash, proprietary-only measurement or irreconcilable material balance is a failed reconstruction.

## 16. Process windows and qualification debt

`CRET-1` distinguishes a proposed window from a qualified window. Proposed bounds allow fixtures and experiments to be designed; they do not release article construction. Each decisive process must map variables to mechanisms and consequences.

For Root A welding, the map includes heat input, preheat/interpass, hydrogen control, restraint, pass sequence, cooling, hardness, toughness, distortion and NDE access. For Root B consolidation and joining, it includes material condition, tool state, placement, temperature, pressure, time, surface preparation, exposure age, contamination, crystallinity/void state, fiber direction, insert transfer and NDE access. Hydraulic paths add liner, purge, chemistry, passivation, cleaning and trapped-volume variables. Electrical joints add surface preparation age, plating, preload, contact resistance and thermal response. Energy systems add barrier pressure, leak detection, valve state, weighing and incompatible-fluid logic.

A qualified window needs physical evidence at nominal and challenge edges, not merely successful nominal production. Interactions matter: maximum heat with maximum restraint, minimum bondline with maximum surface age, low preload with oxide growth, or thermal deployment under cable damage may govern. Later design-of-experiments work must preserve those coupled hypotheses.

## 19. Falsification and automatic-stop register

`CRET-1` is falsified, or construction is automatically stopped, if:

- a part, lot, program, drawing or configuration identity does not reconcile;
- article stock is cut before coupon, standard, witness and archive splits are assigned;
- a decisive process is recorded only by commanded settings rather than observed values;
- a governing process variable leaves the proposed or qualified window;
- a known above-threshold defect is missed;
- a hidden surface is closed before its hold release;
- a fixture or instrument is outside calibration or fails its pre/post check;
- material conservation fails in its governing unit;
- a repair erases original state, lacks a new adjacent witness or removes inspectability;
- Root A and Root B share a decisive material, process, NDE or data dependency without a controlled common-cause disposition;
- fuel and public water share a single fluid barrier;
- energy–ballast load-cell and meter custody differ by more than the controlled tolerance without cause;
- an electrical fault, fluid leak or interstitial alarm lacks a demonstrated safe state;
- a destructive cut would erase the only intact observation or reference datum;
- a native object exists only in a proprietary system unavailable to the reproduction authority;
- either evidence original depends on the article service power it judges;
- the new allocation exceeds the unassigned `BRD-1` capital or annual boundary;
- construction, campaign, production or exterior form is inferred from planning closure.
