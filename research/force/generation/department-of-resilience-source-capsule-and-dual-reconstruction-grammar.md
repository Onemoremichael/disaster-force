# Source-capsule and dual-reconstruction grammar

## Resolution and branch position

The deepest active branch is `AVA`, with one prior pass consumed. **Resolution test:** if this result were reversed, the Department would either impose one engineering stack across all six roots or accept archived builder files as public reconstructability, changing the industrial architecture and the first physical program's validity law.

This pass closes the question registered by Pass 231: **what source-capsule and dual-reconstruction grammar can make all five package types independently rebuildable across six root-native technical environments without imposing a common solver, leaking protected performance or confusing deterministic replay with physical proof?**

## Decision

Adopt **Dual Reconstruction Grammar 1 (`DRG-1`)**. A package becomes reproducible only when two non-substitutable paths succeed:

1. **native replay** recreates the claimed result inside the sealed original environment; and
2. **clean reconstruction** recreates the public meaning under disjoint people, beneficial control and tools, then commits its result before native output is disclosed.

The two committed results are compared semantically across six invariants. Bit identity is neither required nor sufficient. Native replay without clean reconstruction proves only that the builder's environment is deterministic. Clean reconstruction without native replay cannot identify whether it rebuilt the warranted configuration. Agreement proves design reproducibility, not that the predicted machine works.

## The source capsule

Each of the thirty packages carries eighteen required fields, creating **540 capsule records**:

| Family | Required contents |
|---|---|
| identity | package and warrant scope; authorship and beneficial control; configuration and content hash |
| executable | native artifact; toolchain image; dependency and license bill; data and calibration lineage; seeds, solver and numerical controls |
| physical | units and datums; material and constitutive sources; interfaces, tolerances and boundaries; manufacturing, process and assembly assumptions |
| civic | safe state and abort logic; receiver and residual dispositions; rights, access and affected-population obligations |
| epistemic | assumptions, unknowns and uncertainty bounds; causal event, denominator and claim trace; acceptance, expiry, disclosure and supersession law |

The capsule is content-addressed and append-only. Correction issues a superseding record; it never overwrites the failed one. Executable internals may remain in protected escrow, but public dependency, physical-interface, uncertainty, rights and disposition semantics must survive vendor exit.

## The only common technical layer

The **Artifact Interchange Kernel** standardizes six meanings: identity; units and datums; boundary and interface; uncertainty; causal trace; and rights and access. It may transport a typed obligation, never the answer.

Three payloads are constitutionally prohibited: a common solver or algorithm; common geometry, controller or implementation; and predicted performance, rank or maturity. This boundary lets a pelagic free-surface model, occupied-structure model, atmospheric intervention model, geothermal source model, clinical-process model and manufacturing-genealogy model remain genuinely unlike.

Each package is compared through six invariants: identity and boundary; conservation and causal closure; interface, receiver and residual; safe state and protected subject; uncertainty and sensitivity; and rebuild and supersession. The result is **180 invariant records**, but their root-native interpretations remain different. A common schema therefore cannot become a national engineering prime in software form.

## Reconstruction sequence and divergence law

Native replay has eight stages per package, or **240 records**: resolve warrant and hash, instantiate the sealed environment, resolve dependencies, load provenance, execute the frozen case, reproduce intermediate states, reproduce the terminal result and seal the transcript.

Clean reconstruction has ten stages per package, or **300 records**: receive only the public contract, declare disjoint control and tools, reconstruct boundaries, reimplement the causal model, rebuild physical assumptions, recreate the challenge case, calculate invariants, commit before disclosure, compare semantic deltas and seal the disposition.

The law yields four outcomes:

- native failure returns the capsule for rework;
- native success plus clean failure creates `DIVERGENT / HOLD`, preserving both records for adjudication;
- two complete paths plus six invariant matches create `REPRODUCIBLE`, making the package eligible for an `AVW`; and
- common control, toolchain convergence, performance leakage or a kernel that requires a common solver creates `TERMINATED`.

No outcome creates an observation, survivor, form selection, `MAKE`, capability, production or `REP-4` state. Destructive physical evidence remains a separate event and court.

## A technology program, not document control

`DRG-1` requires new public capabilities: hermetic engineering environments that survive vendor exit; physical dependency bills binding software, calibration, materials and process assumptions; dissimilar-tool semantic comparators; machine-verifiable causal and civil-obligation graphs; commit-before-disclosure enclaves; performance-blind output escrow; and disconnected archives capable of replay after institutional failure.

The inherited **USD26.9M** reconstruction account and **USD14.3M** public-escrow account form a **USD41.3M** planning ceiling. Inside it, 55 percent of reconstruction funds clean-room cells, 25 percent qualifies root-native instruments and 20 percent reserves divergence. Public escrow assigns 45 percent to the Artifact Exchange, 30 percent to disconnected custody, 15 percent to cryptographic lineage and 10 percent to kernel stewardship. These are protected planning allocations, not efficiency findings or new money.
