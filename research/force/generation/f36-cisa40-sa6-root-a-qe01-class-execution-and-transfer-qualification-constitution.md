# RAZ-1 — Root A QE-01 class execution and transfer-qualification constitution

## Decision abstract

RAZ-1 defines how the Department of Resilience would turn the single null event constituted by [RAY-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-first-qe01-event-and-regeneration-constitution.md) into a complete, transferable `QE-01` evidence class. Its subject is not another event design. It is the higher-order institution that schedules twenty-four separately governed events, preserves the consequences of invalid and adverse attempts, regenerates every used article–rig pair, reconstructs every active packet outside its origin campus and decides whether method, truth-root, rig and campus differences remain inside predeclared bounds.

The class crosses six public rigs, two nonenergetic methods and two independent truth roots:

\[
6\ \text{rigs}\times2\ \text{methods}\times2\ \text{truth roots}=24\ \text{indivisible cells}.
\]

Every cell requires one accepted valid-success credit. No average, spare event, favorable rig or unused token may fill a different cell. The public RAY-1 draw becomes the class seed. A fixed transform rotates the six canonical rig sequences and independently flips the method and truth-root factors so that canonical rig 1, wave 1 equals the already selected event. Leadership does not receive a second opportunity to choose the class order after learning the first result.

The class executes in four six-event waves. Each wave contains three events per method and three per truth root. Each rig receives all four treatments once. Across the twenty-four positions, every treatment appears once or twice in each wave position; across the eighteen same-rig reuse transitions, every possible directed treatment pair appears once or twice. This is a balanced operating schedule, not a claim that order effects are absent.

Every attempt remains a complete RAY-1 event. RAZ-1 therefore governs 720 event-gate positions—thirty possible attempts times twenty-four gates—without copying or weakening the underlying event constitution. It also creates 720 outside-origin reconstruction decisions: twelve packet classes, two non-origin destinations and thirty possible attempts. The twenty-four required credits alone demand 576 event-gate acceptances and 576 replay decisions.

Six reserve tokens raise attempt capacity from twenty-four to thirty. They are not extra credits. A reserve token can activate only after an independent validity board classifies an attempt `INVALID`; it must target the same rig–method–root cell, use a new issue and packet root, and retain the original attempt in the evidence and statistical denominators. A valid `ADVERSE` result cannot be replaced. At a planning validity rate of 0.88, the probability of obtaining at least twenty-four valid attempts from thirty is approximately 93.94 percent. That is a capacity calculation, not a prediction of installed performance.

Reuse is part of class inference. A credited event does not carry the pair’s old readiness credential forward. Each of twenty-four pair-cycle records binds the actual credited token—including a replacement token if one is used—to a prior baseline, issue, safe state, regeneration, re-entry and a distinct new baseline. Eighteen between-wave transition audits require at least 264 hours from one `T0` to the next on the same canonical rig: day-seven disposition followed by a new 96-hour configuration freeze. Source and water residuals must be numerical zero.

Transfer qualification is deliberately decomposed. Twelve unlike-root contrasts compare `TR-A` and `TR-B` at a fixed rig and method. Twelve cross-method contrasts compare `AM-05` and `AM-06` at a fixed rig and root. Six rig decisions then require four cell credits, three accepted transitions and four accepted contrasts apiece. Four treatment decisions require every rig and campus. Three campus decisions require eight balanced credits and every required remote reconstruction. Passing one layer cannot compensate for failure in another.

Closure is conjunctive. Thirty-two class gates govern the seed, schedule, attempts, event packets, metrics, regeneration, transitions, replay, contrasts, higher-order decisions, denominator, replacement legality, retained invalid evidence, duplicate-credit exclusion, adverse-result protection, evidence volume, exceptions, changes, quarantine, credential expiry, finance and downstream authority. Four final decisions may close the class, qualify `QE-01` transfer, return the six-rig pool and permit constitution of the next research object. They cannot issue `QE-02`, admit source, choose a candidate, authorize production or determine exterior form.

RAZ-1 allocates USD 16 million in capital, USD 6 million per year in support and USD 36 million in cross-classified technology work. Its eight-month stage exposure is USD 20 million. These allocations use the remaining `RAE-1` capital, annual-support and technology limits exactly. The release-aware chain from RAC-1 through RAZ-1 reaches month 66, compared with a 90-month sequential chain. The 24-month difference is revocable preparation time, not evidence borrowed before release. The original gross `RAE-1` reference remains USD 696 million; its USD 88 million arithmetic difference from cumulative stage exposure is not time or source authority.

The companion [49-sheet workbook](../../../models/force/generation/f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-qe01-class-execution-and-transfer-qualification-constitution.xlsx) begins with every physical event, credit, contrast and release decision open. Its 180 terminal audits test workbook coherence only. A 121-assertion executable review completes the ideal class, forces event and credential failures, activates a lawful replacement, confirms that the invalid original remains in a 25-attempt denominator, follows the replacement token through pair and transition records, stresses finance and downstream authority, and restores the model. It finds no formula-error matches. No physical event has occurred.

The next admissible object is the first `QE-02` source-lot, issue, safety and event constitution. Its task is to decide how 100 kilograms can enter one already qualified cell without allowing null-event transfer evidence to become source-bearing evidence by vocabulary. Exterior rendering remains blocked.

## 1. Research stocktake: from one event to a class

The preceding Root A chain progressively changed the unit of proof.

| Stage | Evidence object | Question resolved | Maximum available decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RAE-1` | executable low-source dossier | what objects and authorities make a 100 kg event issuable? | define event classes; no hardware |
| `RAP-1` | eighteen-role article campaign | what physical population can prove construction and safeing? | reserve article roles |
| `RAC-1` | construction constitution | can two unlike roots be built from controlled records? | release development construction |
| `RAB-1` | destructive qualification campaign | can process, defect and integrated evidence be separated? | admit a lineage to lot research |
| `RAQ-1` | production-equivalent bank | can three teams reproduce both roots? | constitute eighteen bank positions |
| `RAX-1` | campaign readiness federation | can six rigs, articles, crews, campuses and evidence become current together? | issue one pre-source event |
| `RAY-1` | first null event and regeneration | can one selected pair observe zero and earn a new baseline? | grant one `QE-01` credit |
| `RAZ-1` | complete null-event class | can all rigs, methods and roots reproduce and transfer the null proposition? | qualify `QE-01`; no source authority |

The key transition is from event truth to class truth. RAY-1 asks whether one named event was valid and what it observed. RAZ-1 asks whether the same proposition survives changes in rig, method, root, campus, order, crew, regenerated baseline and evidence destination. Those questions cannot be answered by repeating the first event informally.

This distinction generalizes to the mature Department. One successful mobile power deployment does not establish fleet availability. One hospital formation does not establish transfer across receiving systems. One fire-control sortie does not establish a doctrine across aircraft, bases, fuels and airspace cells. A serious resilience force requires class constitutions that convert isolated demonstrations into bounded, transferable capability without inflating the claim.

## 2. The class proposition

RAZ-1 asks:

> Can six public rigs execute both nonenergetic methods under both unlike truth roots, retain valid zero-state results through complete regeneration and outside-origin replay, and remain inside registered root and method contrasts without deleting invalid or adverse evidence?

An affirmative answer would establish five bounded claims:

1. all twenty-four rig–method–root cells can execute the RAY-1 null-event law;
2. each used pair can receive a distinct post-event baseline before reuse or return;
3. both truth roots and both methods can reach mutually bounded zero-state decisions at every rig;
4. each active packet can be reconstructed at two non-origin campuses; and
5. the class record can preserve invalid attempts and any adverse valid result without allowing replacement to change the question.

It would not establish source handling, fire behavior, casualty-water response, candidate performance, mature reliability, production rate, fleet availability or field service. `QE-01` is the zero-source class. Its value is that later source-bearing results acquire a credible baseline and an institution capable of detecting state corruption.

## 3. Factor-preserving schedule generation

### 3.1 Four treatments

The class uses a two-by-two factorial treatment space.

| Treatment | Method | Credit root | Method bit | Root bit |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| `T1` | `AM-05` | `TR-A` | 0 | 0 |
| `T2` | `AM-05` | `TR-B` | 0 | 1 |
| `T3` | `AM-06` | `TR-A` | 1 | 0 |
| `T4` | `AM-06` | `TR-B` | 1 | 1 |

The bit representation matters. A general relabeling could map the selected first event to canonical `T1` while destroying the separate meaning of method and root. RAZ-1 permits only two independent exclusive-or flips: one for method and one for root. Thus the transform preserves the factorial structure.

### 3.2 Canonical sequences

Before the RAY-1 draw exists, the class freezes six canonical rig sequences:

| Canonical rig | Wave 1 | Wave 2 | Wave 3 | Wave 4 |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `T1` | `T2` | `T3` | `T4` |
| 2 | `T1` | `T2` | `T4` | `T3` |
| 3 | `T2` | `T3` | `T1` | `T4` |
| 4 | `T3` | `T4` | `T2` | `T1` |
| 5 | `T4` | `T1` | `T3` | `T2` |
| 6 | `T4` | `T3` | `T2` | `T1` |

Let the RAY-1 selected index be \(s\in\{1,\ldots,24\}\). The selected rig and treatment are

\[
r_s=\left\lfloor\frac{s-1}{4}\right\rfloor+1,
\qquad
t_s=((s-1)\bmod4)+1.
\]

The rig rotation is \(r_s-1\). The method and root flips are the two bits of \(t_s-1\). Applied to every canonical cell, those three operations make the first scheduled event equal the RAY-1 selection while retaining all balance properties of the canonical design.

For the worked verification seed, `s=24`, so the selected cell is `RFP-C2 / AM-06 / TR-B`. The transform rotates canonical rig 1 to physical rig 6 and flips both factors. All twenty-four output cells remain unique.

### 3.3 Balance is a protected resource

The design meets four non-substitutable balance conditions:

- every wave contains six events;
- every wave contains three events under each method and three under each truth root;
- every rig executes all four treatments exactly once; and
- every directed transition between unlike treatments appears one or two times across the eighteen between-wave transitions.

No random reordering occurs after the seed. This is intentional. A small class cannot repair arbitrary convenience changes through later regression. Balance has to be treated like test hardware: reserved before results and withdrawn when altered.

## 4. Attempt law and the ethics of replacement

The model distinguishes a cell, an attempt and a credit.

- A **cell** is one required rig–method–root proposition.
- An **attempt** is one issued physical event with its own packet, baseline and result.
- A **credit** is the single accepted valid-success attempt that closes a cell.

This grammar prevents two common distortions. First, a second attempt cannot cause the first to disappear. Second, an unfavorable valid result cannot be recoded as an execution failure merely because a reserve token exists.

Twenty-four primary attempt tokens are bound to the transformed schedule. Six reserve tokens begin dormant. A reserve token becomes available only if all of the following are true:

1. the replacement board has explicit authority;
2. the named original token is independently `INVALID`;
3. the reserve targets the same cell as the invalid original;
4. the invalid original retains a packet root and accepted record; and
5. the new attempt receives a new issue, event packet, metric record, replay set and credential window.

A seventh invalid attempt exhausts class capacity. It does not relax the requirement for twenty-four cells.

At nominal independent validity \(p=0.88\), thirty attempt positions give

\[
P(X\ge24)=\sum_{k=24}^{30}{30\choose k}p^k(1-p)^{30-k}
\approx0.9393926331.
\]

The independence assumption is a planning simplification. Common-cause failures in rigs, methods, roots, campuses, crews or evidence systems could make the true completion probability lower. The reserve is therefore capacity for investigation and lawful repetition, not evidence that the class is reliable.

Even a perfect 24-for-24 valid-success record has limited statistical content. Under a simplifying independent Bernoulli interpretation, the one-sided exact 95-percent lower bound for an all-success probability is

\[
0.05^{1/24}\approx0.882703.
\]

If one original attempt is invalid and a replacement succeeds, the final class may still have twenty-four credits, but the observed validity denominator is twenty-five attempts with twenty-four valid events. RAZ-1 reports that 0.96 rate and a one-sided Wilson lower bound. It never calculates validity from credits alone.

## 5. Every event remains a RAY-1 event

RAZ-1 does not create a shortened “class event.” Every active attempt must close the twenty-four RAY-1 packet gates governing credential, eligibility, selection, issue, dossier, crew, configuration, source zero, water zero, uncertainty, measurement, neighbors, safeing, command, causal clock, holds, evidence, blanks, aborts, intervention, result, recovery, regeneration and re-entry.

The class workbook stores one state, evidence root and acceptance time for every event gate. It reads the accepted RAY-1 packet; it does not reproduce the lower-level ledgers that produced that packet. This boundary avoids two errors:

- class administrators cannot waive a failed event-level fact; and
- event operators cannot grant class transfer on the strength of one event.

Attempt-level metrics separately require numerical unlike-root source and water estimates, their uncertainty terms, exact zero source and water ledgers, native and allocated evidence volumes and an intervention class. Blank ledgers are rejected. A numerical zero is evidence only when its measurement and custody chain also passes.

Each event allocates 117.659 GB of native evidence and 152.9567 GB of governed storage. Twenty-four required events therefore require 2,823.816 GB native and 3,670.9608 GB allocated. Planning for all thirty attempt positions requires 3,529.770 GB native and 4,588.701 GB allocated. The storage allocation is intentionally larger than required demand so invalid originals and replacement packets coexist.

## 6. Regeneration and carryover

The experimental object after an event is not the same object that entered it. Even a null event can alter seals, surfaces, drainage state, instrument zero, calibration, software state, crew condition and evidence media. RAZ-1 therefore treats the path from one event to the next as evidence.

Every credited cell receives one pair-cycle record containing:

- the actual credit token;
- its exact cell and physical pair;
- the prior accepted baseline;
- event issue and safe state;
- completed regeneration and re-entry;
- a distinct new baseline; and
- the accepting evidence root.

If a replacement earns the credit, the pair-cycle, crew, root-deployment and transition records follow the replacement token. They do not remain attached to the failed primary merely because it occupied the original schedule position.

The minimum reuse interval is

\[
168\ \text{hours to day-seven disposition}
+96\ \text{hours for a new freeze}
=264\ \text{hours}.
\]

The eighteen transition audits also require zero predecessor source residual, zero predecessor water residual, accepted prior disposition, a named successor baseline and retained evidence. The schedule’s directed carryover balance gives these observations interpretive structure; it does not prove that carryover is absent.

## 7. Independent reconstruction as force structure

For each active attempt, twelve packet classes travel to two non-origin destinations. The destination pair is deterministic from the origin campus: an origin at `PTC-A` reconstructs at `PTC-B` and `PTC-C`, and similarly for the other campuses. Each reconstruction requires the same result as the origin packet and an unlike evidence root.

This yields twenty-four decisions per active attempt. The required class uses 576. Full attempt capacity reserves 720.

Replay is not an archive copy. Its purpose is to determine whether another public institution can reconstruct the event’s state, timing, zero decisions, validity, outcome, recovery and evidence identity without relying on origin-site interpretation or a live national network. Disagreement blocks the affected attempt and remains evidence about the institution.

The larger Department implication is substantial. A force capable of operating through national infrastructure failure needs deployable epistemic independence: portable standards, self-describing instruments, local originals, offline computation, remote custody and boards that can reproduce a decision away from the operating unit.

## 8. Transfer qualification

Cell completion precedes comparison. The transfer board receives only exact, accepted cell credits.

### 8.1 Root contrasts

At each of six rigs and two methods, `TR-A` and `TR-B` are compared. Twelve decisions require absolute source difference no greater than 0.05 kg and absolute water difference no greater than 0.0125 m³. These are registered class margins derived from the RAY-1 zero ceilings; they are not observed performance claims.

### 8.2 Method contrasts

At each of six rigs and two roots, `AM-05` and `AM-06` are compared under the same margins. Another twelve decisions result.

### 8.3 Rig, treatment and campus decisions

Six rig boards require four cells, three transitions, two root contrasts and two method contrasts each. Four treatment boards require six rig credits and representation at all three campuses. Three campus boards require eight credits, four `AM-05` credits, four `TR-A` credits and all required remote reconstructions for attempts originating on that campus.

The hierarchy localizes discrepancy. A failed root contrast points to a different research branch than a failed method contrast. A campus replay failure is not silently averaged with correct measurements. A rig transition failure does not become a generic class shortfall. This is how a mission-led technology program learns where to invest.

The model’s eleven class states run from `UNCONSTITUTED` through `SEEDED`, `SCHEDULED`, `ISSUED`, `EXECUTING`, `ADJUDICATING`, `TRANSFERRING`, `REGENERATING`, `REPLAYING`, `QUALIFIED` and `CLOSED`. Every state requires its registered sequence number and evidence root. Administrative declaration cannot jump the chain.

## 10. Capacity and concurrency

The class needs forty-eight required rig-days and reserves twelve more. Four waves permit six concurrent events only if the six rigs, 240 event-duty seats, three teams per truth root, independent evidence paths and regeneration positions are genuinely simultaneous. The capacity reference provides 120 rig-days, 300 concurrent seats, four teams per root, twelve regeneration positions and 6 TB of governed evidence allocation.

These are acquisition requirements. They do not show that the current proving institution can supply them. Physical issue must measure availability and conflicts at the class date.

The schedule adds eight months after RAY-1:

- months 59–64: four balanced waves, investigations and reserve windows;
- months 65–66: contrasts, remote reconstruction completion, class boards and record freeze.

The class is not compressed to the arithmetic minimum. Reserve windows exist because investigation must precede replacement. A fast replacement issued before causal classification would protect calendar appearance at the expense of evidence.

## 15. Limitations and falsifiers

RAZ-1 is a research constitution, not executed evidence. Its most important limitations are:

1. the 0.88 validity rate and independence assumption are planning inputs;
2. the canonical schedule balances observed treatment order but does not eliminate all period, fatigue, weather or campus interactions;
3. the 0.05 kg and 0.0125 m³ contrast margins are registered requirements, not empirically calibrated equivalence limits;
4. the 264-hour reuse minimum assumes day-seven disposition and a complete 96-hour freeze are sufficient, which must be tested physically;
5. evidence-volume rates are inherited from RAY-1 and do not yet include every investigation branch or long-term migration cost;
6. 24-for-24 success would still provide weak production-reliability information;
7. higher-order decisions remain conditional on correct cell-level validity judgments;
8. the workbook records accepted roots and times but is not itself a cryptographic ledger, scheduler or evidence repository;
9. the eight-month execution window depends on real concurrent crews, roots, assay paths and campus capacity; and
10. no null-event result can validate source-bearing thermal, fire, hydraulic, contamination or rescue behavior.

Immediate falsifiers include a missing scheduled cell, broken method or root balance, a directed carryover outside the registered range, an invalid reserve target, an attempted replacement of valid adverse evidence, a failed RAY gate, nonzero residual, reused baseline, premature `T0`, missing relief, shared replay root, out-of-margin contrast, duplicate credit, expired credential, unreconciled allocation or any nonzero source, candidate, production or exterior-form authority.
