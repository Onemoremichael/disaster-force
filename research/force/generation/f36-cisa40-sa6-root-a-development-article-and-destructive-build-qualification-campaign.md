# RAB-1 — Root A development-article and destructive build-qualification campaign

## Decision abstract

RAB-1 defines the first physical qualification campaign for the two REA-100 construction roots established by RAC-1. It specifies two complete development articles, the public fixtures that create and interrogate them, separate process-window populations, blinded defect populations, measurement-system studies, integrated exposure histories, automatic-stop laws, quarantine branches, destructive cuts, statistical decision rules, outside-team reproduction and the institutional authorities required to decide whether either lineage is ready for a production-equivalent article lot. It introduces no physical observations and accepts no hardware.[1–8]

The primary methodological correction is that two complete articles cannot qualify a construction system. DA-X1 contains five permanent orbital-welded joints. DA-Y1 contains five keyed, monitored double-barrier joints. Even if all ten joints completed every planned exposure, five observations per lineage would permit only a weak claim about failure probability. With zero failures in five independent trials, the one-sided 95-percent upper bound on the underlying failure probability is approximately 45 percent. The complete articles therefore serve as integrated-system witnesses and terminal forensic objects. They do not serve as the statistical population for process qualification.

RAB-1 adds five physically distinct populations around those articles. Each lineage receives sixty process-window joint specimens: forty-eight cover all sixteen corners of a blocked four-factor experiment in triplicate, and twelve sit at the declared center condition. Twelve lineage–defect cells each receive twenty-nine at-threshold positives, twenty-nine above-threshold positives, twenty-nine below-threshold negatives and twenty-nine clean controls. That produces 1,392 unique blinded presentations. Seventy-two additional safeing assemblies, 144 instrument-mount specimens and thirty-six surface-and-drain specimens separate mechanism evidence from the two full articles. Including the articles, the physical campaign contains 1,766 planned objects. Repeated cycles remain exposure on one object and never become fictitious independent samples.

The statistical claim is deliberately bounded. If an inspection method detects all twenty-nine at-threshold positives in one lineage–defect cell, the one-sided 95-percent lower bound on its detection probability is approximately 90.19 percent. If it falsely rejects none of the twenty-nine below-threshold negatives and twenty-nine clean controls, the corresponding upper false-reject bound is approximately 5.04 percent. If all sixty process-window specimens in one lineage complete the registered screen without the named failure, the one-sided upper failure bound is approximately 4.87 percent. No observations may be pooled across unlike lineages or different defect mechanisms to rescue a failed cell. These are screening and discrimination bounds, not fleet reliability, production yield, service life or maintenance intervals.

The two integrated articles remain physically unlike. DA-X1 uses the BL-X fused-metallic path and is terminally destroyed after its intact trials. DA-Y1 uses the BL-Y serviceable cartridge path and receives fifty assembly–disassembly cycles in addition to the shared exposures. Both complete one 3-megapascal liquid proof, 1,000 normal-pressure cycles, fifty surge-screen cycles, twenty thermal cycles between −20°C and 50°C, thirty drain-and-recovery trials, sixty final-element state trials, four independent-energy cases and twenty integrated system cases. The BL-X cycle ledger records inspection checkpoints inside its existing 1,000-cycle pressure history; it does not add a hidden second thousand cycles.

Thirty-three serialized units across eighteen fixture families establish the range boundary. The public institution acquires coordinate and optical survey, robotic bore mapping, surface-state measurement, orbital-weld execution, clamp and preload execution, liquid proof, gravimetric leak capture, full-line thermal exposure, cyclic pressure, drain and recovery, safeing work, low-state energy, instrument and causal-time integration, blinded NDE, precision sectioning, microscopy, handling and evidence replay. Each fixture requires calibration identity, boundary identification, uncertainty closure, safety release and an evidence root before a named authority can commission it.

The measurement system is qualified before the article. Twelve measurement families each receive a ten-part, three-operator, two-repeat study, creating 720 raw gage records plus a system-level analysis decision. Preliminary expanded-uncertainty budgets close for bore diameter, joint step, deployed length, proof pressure, proof leakage, drained residual, spring work, stored energy, clamp preload, surface roughness, contact angle and causal-time offset. The four-to-one ratios for proof leakage, bore half-band and drained residual are design hypotheses. Commissioning evidence remains open.

Destruction is part of the measurement architecture. Forty-eight zones on each article are committed before integrated results: five joints, six segment bores, twelve final elements, twelve instrument mounts, six support contacts and seven ports or drains. The ninety-six resulting section records preserve cut coordinate, image root, dimensional result, material result, examiner and acceptance identity. Blind NDE predictions remain sealed until destructive truth records are complete. Investigators cannot select only favorable joints or clean-looking surfaces after seeing the trials.

RAB-1 requires 218 peak assigned positions drawn from the existing 240-position RAP-1 establishment. The 18-month physical campaign requires $118 million of capital and $58 million per year of support, for $205 million of stage exposure. Together, the RAC-1 and RAB-1 stage allocations leave $66 million of capital and $16 million per year inside the parent RAP-1 boundary. A $180 million technology map is cross-classified inside the parent $310 million research allocation. It supersedes the $125 million construction subset and adds $55 million of covered technology scope without increasing the Department capital total.

The strongest possible future RAB-1 decision is narrower than qualified equipment. A lineage may become eligible to enter RAQ-1, where production-equivalent articles would be built after the accepted process window freezes and a second team reproduces the package. Either lineage may advance, require another development branch or be rejected. The program cannot authorize the eighteen-unit RAP-1 article bank, a source event, production, a field candidate or exterior rendering.

## 1. Research stocktake

The Root A chain now contains four different epistemic objects. RAE-1 defines an executable low-source event dossier and the authority required to issue it. RAP-1 defines the eighteen-article bank and physical campaign needed to accept a reference article. RAC-1 defines the six-module construction topology, public geometry, two unlike joint roots, drawing system, material splits, process routes, tolerances, pressure boundary, thermal accommodation, safeing-work budget and article-release predicates. RAB-1 now defines how physical work could challenge that construction constitution.[1–3]

The distinction matters. A drawing can be internally consistent while impossible to build repeatedly. A joint can meet its nominal dimensions while hiding an unacceptable process sensitivity. An inspection method can report plausible images while missing the defect at the acceptance threshold. A full article can complete one demonstration while its result is dominated by a favorable operator, material lot or fixture. RAB-1 separates those possibilities instead of asking one prototype to carry every claim.

This pass also closes a structural gap in the larger Department of Resilience concept. A coequal rescue-and-stabilization force would need public proving institutions able to create and break unfamiliar technology before industrial selection. The Department cannot rely on supplier certificates to qualify autonomous pipeline systems, field desalination, mobile power, large fire aircraft, temporary cooling plants or medical utility networks. It needs permanent professions in process science, metrology, controlled defects, destructive forensics, range safety and evidence custody. RAB-1 is a small-scale construction of that institution.

## 2. Qualification proposition

RAB-1 asks:

> Do the BL-X and BL-Y public construction roots, their measurement systems and their released build packages possess enough physically observed and reproducible evidence to enter production-equivalent article-lot construction?

The unit of decision is one lineage in one frozen configuration and one tested domain. A positive decision requires a commissioned range, an accepted measurement system, conserved material ancestry, completed process-window specimens, cell-specific defect discrimination, one complete sacrificial article, precommitted destructive correlation and outside-team reproduction. A missing layer cannot be inferred from the others.

| Claim class | Permitted future statement | Prohibited inference |
|---|---|---|
| measurement capable | the named chain resolves the declared interval in the commissioned fixture | transfer to another fixture, medium or range |
| process screen completed | the serialized specimens completed the registered factor and destructive screen | production yield or capability index |
| defect discrimination bounded | the method met the cell-specific detection and false-reject bounds | pooling across lineage or defect family |
| article integration completed | one serialized article completed the registered integrated history | service life or fleet reliability |
| destructive correlation completed | committed NDE calls correspond to committed physical sections | state of regions that were not examined |
| construction transfer reproduced | an outside team followed the released package and preserved evidence | unrestricted supplier interchangeability |

The campaign can also produce a negative result with high value. A measurement-invalid outcome means the article cannot be judged. A fixture-invalid outcome means the stimulus cannot be attributed. A process-invalid outcome means the construction window must be narrowed or redesigned. A detection-invalid outcome means the inspection method cannot govern acceptance. An article failure may reveal a coupled mechanism absent from the subarticle population. These are distinct branches and cannot be compressed into one generic test failure.

## 3. Population architecture

### 3.1 Integrated articles

DA-X1 and DA-Y1 are complete six-module, 12-metre, 40-millimetre-bore research instruments. Each contains five lineage joints, twelve final-element assemblies and 144 installed channels. They share the COMMON-40 source, receiver, containment, measurement, safeing, evidence and water-boundary interfaces defined in RAC-1. Their joint mechanisms, construction records and forensic questions remain unlike.

Neither article belongs to the future eighteen-unit RAP-1 bank. Both are intentionally consumed. Treating them as later operational or reserve articles would create a selection bias toward the least damaged development hardware and would preserve configurations built before the final process decision. RAB-1 instead values them for what their destruction can reveal.

### 3.2 Independent mechanism populations

| Population | Count | Primary purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| complete development articles | 2 | integrated exposure and terminal forensics |
| process-window joints | 120 | factor interaction and construction repeatability |
| blinded defect presentations | 1,392 | detection and false-reject bounds by cell |
| safeing mechanism specimens | 72 | final action under energy and command denial |
| instrument-mount specimens | 144 | physical interface and evidence transfer |
| surface-and-drain specimens | 36 | retained-film, geometry and recovery mechanisms |
| **total** | **1,766** | |

The populations cannot substitute for one another. A safeing assembly contains enough mechanism to measure work and final state but not enough system context to prove integrated routing. A complete article contains the context but only one realization of each joint station. A controlled defect specimen provides known truth but is not a production-yield sample. The research design preserves these roles.

One physical joint cycled one thousand times remains one joint. The exposure may reveal damage initiation, drift or sudden failure, but it does not create a thousand independent manufacturing observations. Every RAB-1 statistical denominator counts separately serialized material and process ancestry. Repeated acquisitions from one blinded standard estimate operator, access and instrument effects; they do not enter the primary detection bound as new specimens.

A repair similarly cannot restore independence. The failed configuration remains in the record. Repaired hardware receives a new branch identity and may support mechanism learning, but it cannot erase the original failure or quietly re-enter the original population.

## 4. Process-window experiment

Each lineage receives a blocked four-factor design. Sixteen corner conditions realize the full \(2^4\) space, with three independently serialized specimens at every corner. Twelve center specimens measure short-run repeatability and reveal gross curvature between the factor extremes. The result is sixty process-window joints per lineage.

For BL-X, the factors are purge oxygen or equivalent oxidation condition, measured heat input, axial mismatch before joining and end-gap state. For BL-Y, they are retained preload relative to the declared center, seal compression, face and key misregistration and monitored-interspace evacuation response.

The actual low and high values remain qualification hypotheses until fixture commissioning and material trials. RAB-1 preserves coded −1, 0 and +1 states in the common experiment so that a later revision can change a physical window without rewriting the design.

The campaign does not calculate a production capability index from sixty experimental specimens. The corner population is intentionally non-random and overrepresents boundary conditions. It can identify main effects, interactions, gross nonlinear response and unacceptable corners. It cannot estimate a stable factory distribution. Production capability belongs to later lots built under the frozen process.

Every specimen retains material lot, machine, operator, preparation, environmental state, execution record, NDE result, destructive result and configuration branch. A process corner fails if the named governing state exceeds its limit, if evidence is missing or if the fixture boundary cannot be reconstructed. A successful center cannot rescue a failed corner that remains inside the proposed public window.

## 5. Blinded controlled-defect architecture

The fused lineage contains six governing defect families: purge loss and internal oxidation; incomplete fusion or root discontinuity; bore hi-lo and internal step; heat-input or heat-affected-zone excursion; porosity or a connected leak path; and surface contamination or failed recovery.

The serviceable lineage contains six different families: low retained preload; incorrect seal compression; key or face misregistration; particulate or film contamination; blocked or cross-connected monitored interspace; and assembly relaxation after cycling.

These mechanisms cannot be replaced by generic good and bad examples. The challenge authority must create a traceable physical variable, verify its state through a truth method and seal its identity before inspection. Thresholds are committed before presentations begin.

Every lineage–defect cell contains four groups of twenty-nine:

- at-threshold positives test whether the method resolves the exact governing boundary;
- above-threshold positives show whether stronger indications are recognized;
- below-threshold negatives test specificity close to the boundary; and
- clean controls reveal process or inspection bias unrelated to the defect.

The 116 presentations per cell and twelve cells produce 1,392 unique primary observations. The challenge authority randomizes order and commits the assignment hash. Inspectors record a blind call, indication location, confidence class, instrument state, access condition and evidence root before any key is opened.

An accepted block requires all twenty-nine presentations to be completed and correctly classified. The workbook deliberately fails a signed row with twenty-eight correct calls. This strict campaign rule is stronger than the statistical summary because each miss matters physically. The exact bound describes the inference after a successful block; it does not excuse a miss.

## 6. Statistical interpretation

For zero misses in \(n\) successes, the one-sided 95-percent lower confidence bound on detection probability is:

\[
L = \alpha^{1/n}
\]

with \(\alpha = 0.05\). At \(n = 29\), \(L = 0.901855\). For zero false rejects in \(n\) negative presentations, the upper bound is:

\[
U = 1-\alpha^{1/n}
\]

Combining the below-threshold and clean groups gives \(n = 58\) and \(U = 0.050339\). For sixty process specimens with zero named failures, the same upper-bound equation gives \(U = 0.048703\).

The required zero-failure population for an upper bound no greater than a target \(p^*\) is:

\[
n \geq \frac{\ln(\alpha)}{\ln(1-p^*)}
\]

At \(p^* = 0.05\), the rounded-up requirement is fifty-nine. The sixty-specimen lineage population crosses that narrow screening threshold. It still does not establish the much smaller failure probabilities expected of mature public equipment.

No cell can borrow observations from another defect family. No lineage can borrow observations from the other construction root. A repeated acquisition from one specimen cannot enter the primary denominator twice. These restrictions prevent a large global sample count from hiding a weak local mechanism.

The fixture is part of the experiment. An observed response is the combined behavior of article, fixture, boundary controls, instruments, software, clocks and operators. RAB-1 therefore makes each fixture a serialized public object.

Commissioning requires five independent objects: calibration identity, boundary-identification result, uncertainty result, safety release and evidence root. A timestamp is not one of those objects. The model turns a timestamp beside any missing prerequisite into FAIL.

Boundary identification repeats after repair, overload, sensor replacement, controller revision, unexplained zero shift or any event that could alter stimulus transfer. A valid article cannot compensate for an unidentified fixture.

## 8. Measurement constitution

RAB-1 creates twelve measurement-system plans: bore diameter, joint step, deployed length, proof pressure, proof leak mass, drained residual, spring work, stored electrical energy, clamp preload, surface roughness, contact angle and causal-time offset.

Each system receives a ten-part, three-operator, two-repeat crossed study. The 720 raw records preserve part, operator, repeat, observed value and acceptance identity. The study result remains open until a system-level analysis identifier and observed gage R&R fraction are present. The preliminary maximum is 10 percent. A value above that ceiling fails the measurement-system release.

The gage study and uncertainty budget answer different questions. Gage R&R estimates observed repeatability and reproducibility across the declared study. The uncertainty budget combines reference, repeatability and environmental components with a coverage factor. Neither substitutes for the other.

Preliminary expanded uncertainty is calculated as:

\[
U_{95}=k\sqrt{u_\mathrm{ref}^2+u_\mathrm{repeat}^2+u_\mathrm{environment}^2}
\]

with \(k=2\) for the planning screen. The bore budget is 0.0117 millimetres against a 0.0125-millimetre ceiling. Joint-step uncertainty is approximately 0.0247 millimetres against 0.025. Proof-leak uncertainty is approximately 0.00108 kilograms against 0.00125. Drained-residual uncertainty is approximately 0.0469 kilograms against 0.05. These narrow margins make fixture commissioning an early design activity.

The test uncertainty ratio is four for proof leakage, the bore half-band and drained residual under the declared ceilings. A later calibration that increases expanded uncertainty must reduce the acceptance claim or improve the instrument chain. The program may not preserve the original limit by ignoring uncertainty.

## 9. DA-X1 construction

DA-X1 uses the six-module fused-metallic path. Its five article welds receive measured purge state, native energy record, travel record, internal root map, external NDE and an adjacent-process witness. The witness shares parent lot, preparation, machine, operator and process window with the article joint.

Segment mapping precedes joining. Root imaging occurs before any permitted surface recovery. Complete-bore mapping occurs after all five joints exist. NDE calls are sealed before terminal sectioning. Any repair preserves the original root and creates a new configuration branch.

The process-window experiment determines which combinations of oxidation condition, heat input, mismatch and end gap remain eligible. It does not permit a favorable article weld to expand a failed window. The public design authority either narrows the window, changes the joint preparation, changes the measurement requirement or rejects the lineage branch.

## 10. DA-Y1 construction

DA-Y1 uses five keyed double-barrier cartridge joints. Preload, seal compression, key state, bore step, monitored-interspace response and teardown identity are independent records. No inferred assembled-correctly label replaces them.

The article receives fifty assembly–disassembly cycles in ten blocks of five. Preload is measured before and after each block. Seal and key identity remain serialized. Interspace evacuation, isolation and leak indication are challenged after reassembly. A joint that requires undocumented adjustment becomes a new configuration.

Serviceability is not presumed superior. Repeated assembly creates its own risks: preload scatter, seal damage, embedded contamination, key wear, bore discontinuity and loss of monitor-path independence. RAB-1 exists to expose those mechanisms under the same public geometry used by the fused path.

The pressure history comprises ten 100-cycle normal blocks and ten five-cycle surge blocks per article. The normal blocks peak at the inherited 0.25-megapascal relief boundary. The surge blocks reach the 1.326-megapascal conservative instantaneous screen. The 3-megapascal liquid proof remains a structural screen and uses liquid to limit stored energy.

The preliminary residual-set ceiling is 0.5 millimetres. The thermal program asks whether the constructed article follows the RAC-1 free-growth strategy, not whether its material survives every future climate.

An accepted case requires effect, safe state, mass ledger, evidence and stop-law state to close together.

## 12. Safeing, energy and evidence

Each article receives five conditions across twelve final elements: normal energy, 60-percent stored energy, normal-power loss, causal-time loss and the manual or passive route. That produces sixty article-level safeing trials per lineage. Seventy-two extra mechanism specimens allow destructive and boundary work without consuming the full articles.

Mechanical stored energy creates the final action. Electrical energy preserves observation, time, local records and release electronics. A battery is not credited as the only means of reaching the safe state. A spring is not credited as evidence that the state was reached.

The energy program includes full charge, 60-percent state, normal-power loss and a combined recorder-and-release peak. Initial, consumed and final energy must reconcile within the declared two-watt-hour measurement interval. Evidence survival remains a separate observation.

Forty-eight article-level evidence-family releases govern the twenty-four channel families on both articles. Each row requires six installed channel instances, a calibration root, a causal-time root and two native originals. The workbook rejects a signed family if any one of those physical objects is absent.

## 13. Destructive qualification

Destruction is scheduled after every intact observation that the cut could alter. It is not delayed until investigators decide which regions look interesting.

Each article has forty-eight committed zones:

- five joint sections;
- six segment-bore sections;
- twelve final-element sections;
- twelve instrument-mount sections;
- six support-contact sections; and
- seven port-or-drain sections.

The joint cuts reveal root fusion, heat-affected material, seal compression, key registration, particulate state and connected leak paths. Segment cuts reveal wall state, internal finish and retained film. Final-element cuts reveal seats, spindles, springs and direct witnesses. Mount cuts reveal boss geometry, insertion depth, cable boundary and local distortion. Support cuts reveal slide behavior and fretting. Port and drain cuts reveal deadlegs, slope and trapped inventory.

Twenty-four correlation decisions compare blind prediction with destructive truth for the twelve defect families across positive and negative populations. Prediction and truth remain in separate evidence roots until the challenge authority opens the key.

An unanticipated destructive finding has priority over a prior successful screen. The configuration branches even if the finding did not create a leak or stop during the intact history. The program exists to learn from hidden states before they enter production-equivalent articles.

## 14. Failure, quarantine and repair

Sixteen automatic-stop laws cover gross leakage, pressure departure, permanent geometry change, joint slip, preload collapse, uncommanded final state, failed safeing, lost independent reference, lost evidence originals, clock divergence, changed fixture boundary, temperature excursion, blind-key exposure, identity mismatch, unauthorized repair, lost personnel boundary and unknown energetic or chemical state.

A stop produces three immediate consequences:

1. the rig enters its registered physical safe state;
2. the article, fixture and evidence roots enter quarantine; and
3. the governing authority determines the next configuration branch.

The failure cannot be deleted because another specimen succeeds. A replacement comes from a predeclared reserve lot and retains the failed event in the denominator where the statistical protocol requires it. Invalidity is reserved for a predeclared reason such as an unidentified fixture boundary; it cannot become a convenient label for an unfavorable article result.

Twelve quarantine classes assign custody and decision rights for material, fixtures, geometry, welds, clamps, proof, surfaces, safeing, energy, evidence, unauthorized change and personnel or environmental safety. This prevents a program manager from turning every anomaly into local rework.

No authority may build, inspect, reveal the challenge key and accept the same claim. The separation is a design feature, not an administrative precaution. It allows the Department to sponsor ambitious technology while retaining a public means of disproving it.

The outside teams matter for the same reason. One team attempts to reconstruct each build route from the released package. Another attempts to reconstruct the findings from released evidence. Their inability to reproduce the result is a failure of the public technical system even if the original team understands its own work.

## 16. Schedule and capacity

The campaign uses eighteen ordered monthly stages. Months one through three freeze protocols and commission safety-critical facilities. Months four and five establish standards and conserved material lots. Months six and seven execute process-window waves. Months eight and nine build and inspect the blind defect population. Months ten and eleven construct the complete articles.

Months twelve through fourteen execute geometry, proof, safeing, thermal, pressure, drain, energy and evidence work. Month fifteen conducts outside-team build and evidence replay. Month sixteen destroys the committed zones. Month seventeen opens the blind key and correlates inspection with truth. Month eighteen convenes the article board and decides whether RAQ-1 may begin.

Sixteen work centres carry explicit demand and availability. Availability applies unit count, an 18-month horizon, 220 workdays per year and a 65-percent ready fraction. The highest planned utilizations remain below 70 percent. That reserve is intentional because failed specimens, fixture recommissioning, safety holds and evidence review consume capacity unevenly.

The capacity calculation does not prove schedule feasibility. Long-lead material, personnel qualification, facility co-location, destructive-laboratory queues and blind-custody constraints may create local conflicts. The model exposes each work centre rather than replacing them with one global utilization percentage.

## 19. Technology and industrial consequences

The program should not treat current measurement and manufacturing limits as fixed. Like a major military research establishment, the Department can create new technical fields around its recurring mission problems.

Autonomous internal metrology should traverse long, assembled fluid paths and reconstruct bore, ovality, step, port depth, surface condition and retained material without dismantling the system. A self-identifying fixture should bind calibration, boundary condition, software, actuator, sensor, clock and safety state into one physical configuration record. Robotic destructive mapping should preserve the exact relationship between an NDE indication and the section that reveals its truth.

Monitored cartridge joints can become an exportable resilience technology if seal state, preload and interspace independence are directly observable. Weld execution twins can connect measured energy, purge and travel to the final root without confusing a machine command with a physical result. Mechanical state memory can show where a valve or isolation actually finished after total power loss.

Controlled-defect synthesis is equally strategic. A public library of realistic weld, seal, contamination, sensor, clock and actuator failures allows multiple companies to compete against the same hidden physical problems. The challenge institution becomes a national asset comparable to a weapons range or standards laboratory, but directed toward rescue, recovery and environmental infrastructure.

The industrial product is not only the REA-100 article. It is the combination of public interfaces, reference materials, fixtures, uncertainty methods, challenge libraries, evidence schemas, forensic capacity and qualification services. Partner countries could adopt compatible proving cells or purchase equipment whose bounded claims can be independently reconstructed.

| Outcome | Consequence |
|---|---|
| both lineages pass | both enter separate RAQ-1 production-equivalent lots |
| only BL-X passes | BL-X may enter RAQ-1; BL-Y remains in a redesign branch |

There is no requirement to name a winner. Industrial resilience may justify carrying both roots if they fail differently and both remain viable. Conversely, institutional preference for competition cannot rescue an unsafe or unmeasurable lineage.
