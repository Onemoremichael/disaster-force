# RBI-1 — Root A water-only reference system and ER-02 class constitution

## Decision abstract

`RBI-1` defines the second physical rung of the `AM-06` entry ladder created by [RBG-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-am06-source-water-ignition-entry-ladder-reset.md). It begins only after the dry ignition-reference class in [RBH-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-dry-igniter-reference-architecture-competition-and-er01-class-constitution.md) is accepted. Its question is:

> Can the public test institution issue, distribute, recover, locate, account for, safe, return and independently reconstruct one 24-cubic-metre clean-water event in every exact rig–truth-root cell while energetic source is observed as zero, ignition is physically prohibited and no drainage candidate receives performance credit?

The event introduces approximately twenty-four tonnes of working mass, not a minor service. At the inherited public boundary of 3 cubic metres per minute, issue lasts eight minutes. The ideal hydraulic-power screen is 60 kilowatts at 1.2 megapascals; a 70-percent pump-efficiency screen requires approximately 85.714 kilowatts and 11.429 kilowatt-hours for the issue interval. Distributed over the registered 6.25-square-metre reference field, the volume corresponds to an eight-millimetre-per-second surface flux. A water-only rung therefore creates serious pressure, stored-energy, overflow, route, custody, environmental and recovery questions even though it contains no fire.[1–8]

RBI-1 creates two unlike end-to-end water truth roots. `WTR-G` uses four independently weighed 6-cubic-metre batch cells and gravimetric supply and return. `WTR-V` uses four surveyed displacement cells, encoded swept volume and an independent transit-time flow chain. Both conform to the same public interface, but they do not share the physical principle that decides volume closure. Builder lineage remains orthogonal to truth root across six rigs and three campuses.

Eighteen serialized water-reference modules form the bank: twelve active exact-cell modules, four same-lineage operational spares and two dry sealed forensic references. The sixteen wettable modules each complete four full-volume qualification cycles—nominal delivery, a 25-percent return restriction, primary network and time denial, and controlled abort with casualty diversion. The campaign therefore requires sixty-four 24-cubic-metre cycles before class issue. Qualification throughput is 1,536 cubic metres. The twelve required class events add 288 cubic metres; four invalidity-only reserves raise class capacity to sixteen events and 384 cubic metres. Maximum governed throughput through the reference system is therefore 1,920 cubic metres. Throughput is not the same as new-water acquisition because qualification stocks may be reconditioned and reused only after complete account closure.

The class owns eighteen 25-cubic-metre water-lot positions. Each position separates 24 cubic metres for issue, 0.25 for assay, 0.25 for line conditioning and 0.50 for quarantine and discrepancy investigation. Twelve primary and four reserve lots can issue; two references remain protected. The nominal installed lot envelope is 450 cubic metres. Qualification water uses six separate 30-cubic-metre campus stocks and can never become class water without a new identity and full release.

Each credited event binds forty-eight gates, 192 evidence records, 192 active measurement channels, twelve physical safeing paths, twenty return decisions and twelve outside-campus replay decisions. The module receives a 336-hour wet return with observations at 24, 72, 168 and 336 hours. Source must be numeric zero. Ignition must be independently isolated, removed or physically inhibited. Water must be a measured numeric quantity; a scheduled 24-cubic-metre command is not delivery evidence.

The primary calendar is thirty-four weeks from water-system construction start: twelve weeks for root-native modules and custody cells, eight weeks for sixty-four qualification cycles, four weeks for review and cross-campus reconstruction, six weeks for primary issue, two weeks for the last wet return and two weeks for class review. Four invalidity reserves add two issue weeks, creating a thirty-six-week maximum.

This corrects the parent ladder. RBG-1 assigned thirteen primary weeks and fifteen maximum weeks to each rung. RBH-1 required thirty-seven and thirty-nine weeks; RBI-1 requires thirty-four and thirty-six. The first two rungs therefore consume seventy-one primary weeks or seventy-five maximum weeks, not twenty-six or thirty. Even if the remaining eight rungs retained the inherited allowance, the ladder floor becomes 175 primary weeks and 195 maximum weeks. The thirty-six-month, approximately 156-week boundary is short by nineteen primary weeks and thirty-nine maximum weeks. Calendar compression cannot be purchased by omitting qualification or overlapping dependent issue.

RBI-1 assigns 330 execution positions while retaining sixty positions to sustain the accepted dry-reference bank. It requires USD 260 million capital, USD 48 million per year during the eighteen-month stage and USD 90 million of cross-classified technology. Direct stage exposure is USD 332 million; technology is not added again. RBH-1 and RBI-1 together consume USD 440 million of the USD 1.1 billion `RAS-3R` capital increment and USD 185 million of its USD 235 million technology account. During RBI-1, USD 56 million of the USD 67 million annual increment supports water execution and dry-reference sustainment, leaving USD 11 million of concurrent capacity. Annual authority is a reusable rate, not a capital balance.

An accepted `ER-02` class does not introduce a coded tracer, source, ignition or flame. The next admissible object is `RBJ-1`, an `ER-03` dual-ancestry reference competition joined to a formal `RAS-3R` schedule, technology and concurrency rebase. No `ER-03` issue can occur until that correction closes. Candidate admission, operational procurement, export and exterior rendering remain blocked.

## 1. Water is a deployed mass system

The parent force design often described water as a service rate. ER-02 exposes the physical stock and state changes behind that abstraction. At the registered reference density of 998 kilograms per cubic metre, one event carries a planning mass of:

\[
m_w=(24\ \mathrm{m^3})(998\ \mathrm{kg/m^3})=23{,}952\ \mathrm{kg}.
\]

The commanded flow is:

\[
Q=\frac{24\ \mathrm{m^3}}{480\ \mathrm{s}}=0.05\ \mathrm{m^3/s}=3\ \mathrm{m^3/min}.
\]

At the public 1.2-megapascal boundary:

\[
P_h=\Delta pQ=(1.2\times10^6)(0.05)=60\ \mathrm{kW}.
\]

With a 0.70 pump-efficiency screen, input power is approximately 85.714 kilowatts. Eight minutes of issue requires approximately 11.429 kilowatt-hours before return pumping, conditioning, instrumentation and evidence loads. These values define a test envelope; they do not select a pump.

The 6.25-square-metre field is divided into sixteen equal witness zones. Each zone covers 0.390625 square metre and receives 0.1875 cubic metre per minute, or 3.125 litres per second, under the uniform reference condition. The equivalent surface flux is 0.008 metre per second. The field is a calibration artifact with a sealed catchment, not a drainage candidate. It cannot earn gravity, cellular, robotic or closed-loop candidate credit.

## 2. Two unlike water truth roots

### 2.1 `WTR-G`: gravimetric batch chain

`WTR-G` uses four double-contained 6-cubic-metre issue cells on separate weigh frames. Density and temperature convert measured mass to reference-state volume. Return, retained module water, samples and quarantine inventory are independently weighed. The chain is strong against tank-geometry error and weak against structural load transfer, platform tilt, load-cell creep and density error.

### 2.2 `WTR-V`: swept-volume chain

`WTR-V` uses four surveyed displacement cells with encoded piston or level travel. A physically separate transit-time flow chain witnesses the issue interval. Return volume uses surveyed geometry and independent level state. The chain is strong against weigh-frame coupling and weak against seal bypass, trapped gas, wall deformation and geometric drift.

Neither root may use the other root’s decisive calibration, state estimator, correction table or final disposition. Both may share public interface geometry, the event card and nondecisive environmental context. Their final 24-cubic-metre estimates must differ by no more than 0.024 cubic metre, and each must satisfy its own uncertainty and account tests. Agreement between two chains does not rescue either chain if its physical truth containment fails.

Each operational module contains a root-native issue-measurement section, a sixteen-zone reference diffuser, the 6.25-square-metre reference field, a sealed 4-cubic-metre-per-minute catchment, a 30-cubic-metre return cell, quarantine connection, twelve safeing interfaces, environmental witnesses and two native evidence originals. The return path carries 33.3-percent hydraulic margin above the 3-cubic-metre-per-minute issue rate. The 30-cubic-metre cell carries 6 cubic metres of geometric headroom above one event.

The module is deliberately unlike a candidate system. It does not optimize source capture, fire suppression, heat rejection or damaged-terrain recovery. It exists to establish whether the public institution can create and close a full-volume water proposition without hidden help.

## 4. Sixty-four full-volume qualification cycles

Every wettable module completes four 24-cubic-metre cycles.

| Cycle | Physical challenge | Required answer |
|---|---|---|
| `WQ-1` | nominal issue and full return | volume, rate, pressure, field distribution and terminal account close |
| `WQ-2` | 25-percent return-capacity restriction | the system retains control at 3 cubic metres per minute without hidden overflow |
| `WQ-3` | campus network and primary-time denial | local control, unlike time and two native originals remain decisive |
| `WQ-4` | commanded abort and public casualty diversion | flow stops, diversion is measured and candidate-like credit is prohibited |

Sixteen modules times four cycles produce sixty-four required valid cycles. A valid adverse result denies the module or challenge. It cannot be repeated away. An invalid attempt requires independent causal invalidity, a new identity and a prospectively controlled position. The two forensic references remain dry and sealed; wetting them to repair schedule destroys their role.

The six campus qualification stocks each hold 30 cubic metres. A cycle issues 24 cubic metres and sends every destination to return, retention, sample, quarantine, casualty, vapor estimate, environment or unresolved discrepancy. Reuse requires a closed account, accepted water quality, accepted tank state and a new cycle identity. The campaign therefore governs 1,536 cubic metres of throughput without pretending that it consumes 1,536 cubic metres of new water.

Each cycle binds thirty-two gates, 128 evidence records, 192 channels, twelve safeing paths and four outside-campus reconstruction decisions. All sixty-four must close before class issue.

The bank contains twelve primary, four reserve and two protected reference lots. A lot is a custody identity, not merely water in a tank. Release binds origin, treatment history, density, temperature, conductivity, turbidity, background chemistry, container, seal, route, assay, conditioning volume and terminal disposition. One reserve lot is assigned only after independent invalidity. A reserve cannot replace a valid adverse event, cross truth-root family or become an informal buffer for unexplained loss. Protected reference lots do not issue.

## 7. Volume ledger and terminal custody

Every event closes the identity:

\[
V_{issue}=V_{return}+V_{module}+V_{sample}+V_{quarantine}+V_{casualty}+V_{vapor}+V_{environment}+V_{unresolved}.
\]

The terms remain separate even when one is observed as zero. A mass or volume difference cannot be moved into “evaporation” without an independent estimate and uncertainty. Water found in the secondary basin is not return. Water in the casualty vault is not reference-path success. A sample remains part of custody. Environmental migration beyond the tertiary site cell denies the event and may stop the class.

The account reports both point discrepancy and expanded uncertainty. A wide uncertainty interval cannot hide a biased point account. A small point discrepancy cannot compensate for missing custody or a shared decisive sensor. Each truth root closes separately before the class compares them.

## 8. Safeing and wet return

Twelve physical safeing paths govern every cycle and event: upstream supply isolation, truth-root isolation, module-inlet shutoff, passive reverse-flow check, pressure relief to quarantine, return isolation, casualty diversion, secondary-basin seal, local pump stop, motive-power isolation, protected manual cutoff and evidence-state latch.

The 336-hour wet return includes immediate isolation and drainage; a twenty-four-hour retained-volume and leak survey; a seventy-two-hour corrosion, seal and sensor review; a 168-hour dimensional and calibration check; evidence and safeing replay; and a 336-hour dry-baseline, material-account and independent-authority decision. Elapsed time alone cannot release a module.

An operational spare inherits no cell credit. It must retain truth-root family and builder lineage, then repeat geometry, volume, pressure, valve-lineage, catchment, calibration, safeing and evidence acceptance under a new serial state. A common-cause failure quarantines the root or bank rather than consuming spares sequentially.

## 13. Stop conditions and bounded authority

RBI-1 stops or narrows if a truth root cannot close without the other root; the 24-cubic-metre state is inferred from a command; module or tank geometry changes without regression; intentional coding enters before ER-03; a qualification stock becomes an event lot without release; a valid adverse cycle or event is repeated; a reserve crosses cell, root or lineage; casualty water is credited to normal performance; unexplained volume is relabeled as vapor; environmental migration lacks a terminal disposition; a forensic reference is wetted; the wet-return clock is waived; outside replay requires origin services; source is not observed as numeric zero; ignition remains physically available; or annual, capital, technology or schedule authority is absent.

`ER-02-QUALIFIED` establishes only the clean-water issue, custody, return and evidence class. It cannot establish two-fluid ancestry, source behavior, ignition, flame, thermal control, drainage-candidate performance or operational readiness.

The next admissible object is `RBJ-1`. It must compete unlike ER-03 ancestry architectures and formally rebase the remaining ladder calendar, technology portfolio and concurrent formation load. No ER-03 event may issue from a financial or scheduling spreadsheet alone; its coded materials, readers, cross-talk boundaries and physical articles must be constituted first.

## Research stocktake

RBI-1 changes the larger Department design in six ways.

First, water volume is operational mass. One apparently simple event controls roughly 24 tonnes through pressure, distribution, containment and recovery.

Second, high-volume water truth needs unlike physical roots. Two meters with shared tank geometry or shared correction software do not create independent evidence.

Third, water qualification has its own industrial article bank. Reference modules, custody cells, conditioning systems, valve lineages and environmental receivers are purpose-built capital equipment.

Fourth, return is part of the capability. Wet baselines, corrosion, hidden retention, sensor drift and account closure persist long after pump stop.

Fifth, full-volume qualification dominates the hidden throughput. The class may issue at most 384 cubic metres, while the module campaign controls another 1,536 cubic metres before class issue.

Sixth, decomposition changes the parent program. The first two rungs alone consume seventy-one primary weeks and most of the registered technology account. Ambition requires enlarging or redesigning the institution when the physics demands it, not preserving a placeholder envelope.
