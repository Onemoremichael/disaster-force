## Decision abstract

`RBQ-1` defines the final physical rung of the `AM-06` entry ladder created by [RBG-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-am06-source-water-ignition-entry-ladder-reset.md). Its question is:

> What complete casualty system can govern a 500-kilogram source distributed across 25 square metres, introduce 48 cubic metres of reference water in 120 seconds, preserve both water morphologies, survive compound loss of host services, accept and separate the necessarily liquid inventory, protect public intervention, reconstruct the event entirely outside the origin institution, end safely and return after 1,008 hours?

This is not a proportional extension of `ER-09`. Source is unchanged, water doubles and area quadruples. Mean source depth and source per area fall to one quarter of the prior state. Water per area and local water flux fall by half. Boundary length doubles, so water per boundary metre remains approximately 2.708 cubic metres per metre while source energy per boundary metre halves. Yet the area-controlled heat-release screen rises fourfold to 58.5585 megawatts and the dry-duration screen falls from 24.242 to 6.061 minutes. The casualty case combines a broader, shallower, faster thermal field with twice the liquid inventory.

The water issue is a 47.904-tonne controlled mass delivered at 0.4 cubic metre or 399.2 kilograms per second. It requires 24 cubic metres per minute. The inherited casualty receiver can accept at most 4 cubic metres per minute and the normal supply can deliver 3 cubic metres per minute. The issue therefore exceeds those interfaces by factors of six and eight. Named storage volume does not cure inlet restriction. `ER-10` requires autonomous issue energy, zoned high-rate diversion and receiver architecture built around the transient.

Heating and vaporizing all 48 cubic metres from the registered state would require 124,161.420 megajoules, 5.831 times the 21,294-megajoule source-energy screen. Even the impossible limit in which every joule enters the water can vaporize only 8.232 cubic metres. At least 39.768 cubic metres remains liquid. The 120-cubic-metre casualty receiver has 72 cubic metres of named volume after issue and only 32.232 cubic metres of analytical margin above that minimum liquid state before condensate, retained source, foam, rain, public water, debris, unusable layers or uncertainty. The 40-cubic-metre normal receiver is geometrically short by 8 cubic metres before any of those additions.

`RBQ-1` creates four rival `CAS-48` casualty architectures. `CSM-48` is a cellular source-water matrix with isolated local sinks. `PGR-48` is a perimeter gravity ring with passive radial diversion. `SPS-48` is a split-plane system joining overhead distribution to below-grade sector receivers. `AMR-48` is an autonomous mobile hydraulic formation whose independent pump, sensor and collector units can reconfigure around a damaged field. ## 5. Four casualty architectures

### 5.1 `CSM-48`: cellular source-water matrix

`CSM-48` divides the 25-square-metre field into twenty-five one-square-metre cells. Each cell has local morphology witnesses, source topology, contact observation, passive threshold diversion and isolated receiver custody. Cross-cell transfer is measured at every shared edge.

Its scientific strength is graceful loss: a failed cell need not erase the field. Its risk is a large device population, seam-induced flow, coordinate common cause and hidden transfer through damaged partitions.

### 5.2 `PGR-48`: perimeter gravity ring

`PGR-48` preserves an open field and uses a surveyed perimeter ring, radial surface gradients, gravity-fed sector sumps and an elevated emergency reservoir. Distribution and collection remain physically separate.

Its strength is low active-service dependence and direct boundary conservation. Its risk is unresolved interior contact, debris-blocked grades, asymmetric steam lift and false confidence from clean perimeter closure.

### 5.3 `SPS-48`: split-plane system

`SPS-48` separates the intervention and recovery planes. A protected overhead manifold establishes water morphology while a below-grade grid records source, water and mixed-phase arrival in independent sectors. Surface hardware remains sparse.

Its strength is control of arrival without instrumenting the active plane heavily. Its risk is coupling through the structure, loss of overhead geometry, below-grade backflow and a common spatial transform joining two otherwise unlike systems.

### 5.4 `AMR-48`: autonomous mobile hydraulic formation

`AMR-48` uses independent mobile cells with onboard water, energy, navigation, sensing, local compute and collector custody. Units are assigned temporary zones, form a distributed issue pattern and carry captured liquid to isolated receivers.

Its strength is reconfiguration under facility loss and direct translation to field response. Its risk is unit-to-unit interference, path obstruction, exhaust and battery effects, local navigation divergence, incomplete collection and hidden dependence on a fleet coordinator.

## 22. Admission, invalidity and stop law

`RBQ-1` stops or narrows if:

- `ER-08` or `ER-09` is not physically accepted;
- a candidate changes an inherited water morphology or root definition;
- the complete 500-kilogram source or 48-cubic-metre water state is absent;
- the 120-second issue cannot be independently measured;
- the 25-square-metre topology is inferred from a smaller field;
- receiver adequacy is claimed from named volume;
- any exact event uses the normal 3-cubic-metre-per-minute supply as its hidden source;
- service loss begins after decisive observations are already secured;
- an island depends on undeclared campus power, network, time, water, cooling, receiver, key or analyst service;
- public rescue is delayed, discouraged or credited to the candidate;
- any root survives by using another root's conclusion;
- a terminal account converts missing state to zero;
- outside reconstruction receives origin code, intermediates, interpretation or conclusion;
- disagreement is averaged or voted away;
- a valid adverse event is repeated to seek a favorable result;
- a reserve crosses morphology, denial world, campus or lineage;
- any safeing function lacks its four required paths;
- module return closes before 1,008 hours;
- site recovery closes before 2,016 hours; or
- parent capacity is absent.

Possible event dispositions are `SUPPORTED`, `ADVERSE`, `INDETERMINATE`, `INVALID_RANGE`, `INVALID_ARTICLE`, `SAFETY_STOP` and `REAL_MISSION_CONVERSION`. A safety stop never becomes a pass. A real mission supersedes the experiment and creates no class credit. An invalid event consumes its token and may use only its bound replacement.

The class may close as `ER-10-QUALIFIED`, `ER-10-BOUNDED-ADVERSE`, `ER-10-INDETERMINATE` or `ER-10-INCOMPLETE`. Only the first authorizes whole-ladder closure work.
