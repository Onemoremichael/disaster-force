# RAY-1 — Root A first QE-01 event and regeneration constitution

## Decision abstract

RAY-1 defines the first use of the nationally mobilized Root A article–rig system constituted by RAX-1. It answers a narrow but foundational question: what must the Department of Resilience observe, preserve, decide and rebuild when one accepted article–rig pair crosses from readiness into an event?

The first event is not source-bearing. `QE-01` is a null event: zero introduced source, zero introduced water and no permitted ignition. This is not a ceremonial rehearsal. It is the first attempt to prove that the complete campaign can distinguish an intentionally empty source state from leakage, background, residual inventory, weather, instrument noise, record substitution and common-cause evidence error. A system that cannot observe zero has no standing to interpret 100 kilograms, 500 kilograms or a maximum credible release.

The event consumes readiness. RAX-1 proves that six articles, six rigs, their people and their evidence systems can become ready together. It does not grant an unlimited bank of future events. Once the selected pair is issued, its state changes from available to reserved, committed, executing, safeing, recovering and regenerating. It returns to available only after a new technical baseline, new credentials and six independent re-entry decisions are accepted. Pre-event readiness cannot be copied across `T0`.

RAY-1 therefore rejects the intuitive idea that a successful first run should accelerate the next run by presumption. The event is itself an intervention in the article, rig, instruments, media, crews, site and evidence estate. Even a clean null event can shift seals, wet lines, change surface condition, consume samples, expose sensors, alter software state, fatigue personnel and reveal configuration disagreement. Regeneration is not cleanup after the experiment. It is part of the experiment’s claim.

Twenty-four candidate cells are eligible for the first issue: six rigs, two source-bearing methods (`AM-05` and `AM-06`) and two independent truth roots (`TR-A` and `TR-B`). Every cell must be current before one is chosen. Six rig contributors then participate in a precommitted commit–reveal draw. The workbook records the verified commitments, reveals, resulting seed and selected index; it does not pretend that spreadsheet formulas constitute a cryptographic system. This prevents program leaders from selecting a familiar rig, favored method or convenient truth root for the inaugural event.

The selected cell receives an atomic, expiring issue that reserves one article, one rig, one crew, one campus window and one evidence estate. Its event dossier binds 192 accepted objects to the issue. Forty named event-duty positions, twenty-four configuration-freeze domains, twelve physical source-zero checks, ten physical water-zero checks, twenty-two uncertainty components, ninety-six measurement-window records, three neighbor proofs, thirty-six safeing polls and twenty command transactions must close around the same event.

Two unlike measurement roots judge source and water independently. For source, each root combines six observed standard-uncertainty components into a `k=2` expanded uncertainty. At half of the design allocations, the result is approximately 0.0364 kg against a 0.10 kg ceiling. For water, each root combines five components; the comparable result is approximately 0.0102 cubic metres against a 0.025 cubic metre ceiling. Numerical ledgers must reconcile to observed zero. A checkbox labeled “none” is not evidence.

The event clock has twenty-four phases from the `T-96 h` configuration freeze through day-seven disposition. Sixteen validity rules are adjudicated before ten outcome rules. A valid adverse result remains part of the campaign even if the article is quarantined or never returns to service. Public-protection intervention does not disappear into an invalid label; it receives one of twelve predeclared classifications. Twenty abort conditions always remain available to the safety authority.

Closure is conjunctive. Twenty-four release gates must all pass, three bounded event decisions must be accepted and no open exception, unauthorized change, quarantine or expiry may remain. The first decision closes the event record. The second grants one `QE-01` credit. The third returns all six article–rig pairs to available status. None qualifies the QE-01 class, permits `QE-02`, introduces source, selects a candidate, authorizes production or determines exterior form.

RAY-1 allocates USD 12 million of capital, USD 6 million per year of support and USD 24 million of cross-classified technology work. Its four-month stage exposure is USD 14 million. The complete release-aware chain from RAC-1 through RAY-1 reaches month 58, compared with an 82-month sequential chain. After this pass, the RAE-1 parent retains USD 16 million of capital, USD 6 million per year and USD 36 million of technology authority for later class execution and source-entry work.

The next admissible object is `RAZ-1`: execution and transfer qualification of the complete twenty-four-event QE-01 class. One successful null event proves only one event and one regenerated pair. It does not establish that the other rigs, methods and truth roots can reproduce the result.

## 1. Research stocktake: readiness meets use

The Root A chain has reached a transition that most acquisition programs obscure. Construction, qualification and readiness produce a claim about what could be done. An event changes physical state and produces a claim about what was done. Those claims require different constitutions.

| Stage | Evidence object | Governing question | Maximum authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RAE-1` | executable quarter-source dossier | what is the lowest physically honest source-bearing event? | define a 40 mm reference path and event classes |
| `RAP-1` | article and safeing proof campaign | what population and institution can judge it? | reserve roles, lineages and proof obligations |
| `RAC-1` | construction constitution | can two unlike article roots be built under public control? | release development construction packages |
| `RAB-1` | destructive qualification campaign | do processes and defect screens survive physical challenge? | admit construction lineages |
| `RAQ-1` | production-equivalent article bank | can independent builders reproduce both lineages? | accept eighteen bank positions |
| `RAX-1` | national campaign readiness | can articles, rigs, crews, campuses and evidence become current together? | issue one pre-source event |
| `RAY-1` | first null event and regeneration | can the system observe zero and return the used pair through a new baseline? | close one QE-01 event and one pair-return cycle |

RAY-1 is deliberately smaller than the source ambitions that motivated the chain. That narrowness is not timidity. It reflects a first-principles view of experimental force development. Before a civil protection institution handles dangerous inventories, it must demonstrate that it can exclude them, identify residuals, observe the absence of transfer, preserve causal order, intervene without corrupting the record and restore the test system after use.

This is the same institutional seriousness that the mature Department of Resilience would apply to full-scale assets. A desalination ship must prove water quality before claiming production. A mobile reactor formation must prove de-energization and decay-heat control before claiming restart. A firefighting aircraft must prove its empty, tare and residual states before its dropped mass can be trusted. An autonomous pipeline train must show that an apparently dry route is actually empty before it opens to a new fluid. Zero states are operating states, not blank spaces between missions.

RAY-1 also clarifies what “force projection for rescue” means institutionally. The Department does not own impressive hardware that remains ready by reputation. It owns a governed cycle of availability, commitment, execution, recovery, regeneration and re-entry. Every use consumes a bounded portion of force readiness; every return requires evidence. This turns regeneration capacity, spare crews, calibration depth, evidence replay and public test infrastructure into force structure.

## 2. The null event as an affirmative experiment

`QE-01` begins with three prohibited inputs:

- introduced source mass: `0.000 kg`;
- introduced candidate or public water: `0.000 m³`; and
- commanded or permissive ignition: none.

The prohibition alone is insufficient. A valid null event must answer four separate propositions:

1. no source package, transport container or unintended source path entered the boundary;
2. no candidate water, public firewater or hidden receiver inventory entered the measured event system;
3. the measurement architecture could have detected a nonzero condition at or below the registered ceiling; and
4. the event sequence, safeing response and evidence chain operated as they must for later source-bearing events.

These propositions create an observed zero rather than an assumed zero. Physical exclusion addresses the first two. Unlike-root uncertainty analysis addresses the third. Full execution and evidence obligations address the fourth.

This distinction matters because complex facilities often record zero when a channel is disconnected, saturated, out of range, improperly tared or not causally aligned with the event. A numerical zero without an accepted measurement state can mean absence, blindness or default initialization. RAY-1 accepts zero only when the relevant channels are live, the root-specific uncertainty components pass, blanks are accepted, the event windows are complete and two independent evidence originals preserve the values.

The event is also an end-to-end exercise of the future source path without source. The selected article, rig, method card, source boundary, water paths, final control elements, safeing paths, command system, casualty receiver, rescue posture and evidence graph all occupy their event configurations. The source route remains physically empty and blocked. This maximizes learning about integration while retaining a strict prohibition on hazardous entry.

Success has a precise meaning: the system executed the null method, remained inside the registered zero ceilings, entered its commanded safe state, preserved a validity-complete record and regenerated the pair. It does not mean that the system would succeed with source. Source adds thermal, chemical, hydraulic, fire, contamination, retention and rescue phenomena that null execution cannot validate.

## 3. Selection without inaugural-event bias

The inaugural event is unusually vulnerable to favorable selection. Program leaders have incentives to choose the best-known rig, the most experienced crew, the easiest method or the measurement root most likely to agree with prior expectations. Even if the choice is defensible operationally, it makes later inference weaker.

RAY-1 defines twenty-four equally eligible cells:

\[
6\ \text{rigs}\times2\ \text{methods}\times2\ \text{truth roots}=24\ \text{cells}.
\]

Every cell binds a specific rig, campus, article, method, credit root and current RAX-1 pair credential. The candidate register does not average readiness. If one cell is open, failed, excluded or expired, the draw cannot proceed. This prevents the program from creating a favorable subset while describing the selection as random.

Six contributors—one associated with each rig—submit commitments before any reveal. They later reveal their contributions, independent verification confirms that each reveal matches its commitment, and the accepted contributions are combined under the registered `PUBLIC-DRAW-V1` algorithm. The result maps to an index from 1 through 24. Board acceptance completes the selection.

The workbook is a custody and decision record, not a cryptographic oracle. It stores commitment roots, reveals, computed commitments, evidence roots and accepted times. The actual cryptographic computation must occur in an independently inspectable implementation. Treating a convenient spreadsheet hash or pseudorandom function as secure would create false assurance.

This selection architecture has a larger use in the Department. Surprise inspections, no-notice mobilization exercises, destructive sampling and depot readiness checks all need methods that deny local leaders the ability to prepare only the inspected unit. A public commit–reveal service could become common infrastructure for resilience acquisition and operations.

## 4. Event issue as an atomic reservation

RAY-1 treats event authority like a scarce operational reservation. The issue is valid only when the selected cell is accepted, the complete dossier and crew are bound, the RAX campaign date is current, no disqualifying exception remains and `T0` falls within the accepted issue window.

The issue reserves five coupled resources:

| Resource | Why reservation is necessary |
|---|---|
| article–rig pair | prevents simultaneous claims on the same physical system |
| named crew | prevents one qualified person from appearing in overlapping duties |
| campus slot | protects environmental, rescue and utility capacity |
| evidence estate | ensures channels, media, clocks and replay capacity are exclusive |
| board authority | binds one configuration and time window to one decision branch |

The reservation is atomic: partial issue has no meaning. An article cannot be committed while its evidence estate remains available to another event. A rig cannot be scheduled while the rescue team is assigned elsewhere. A qualified crew roster cannot compensate for an expired calibration chain.

The issue also expires. If `T0` occurs before acceptance or after expiry, the event fails issue validity. The board must release the resources, investigate any physical changes and create a new issue. It cannot backdate authority around a completed event.

This logic is central to a future large resilience force. During a national catastrophe, ships, aircraft, mobile power plants, hospital formations, pipeline trains, communications units and engineering teams will compete for ports, corridors, fuel, crews, receivers and evidence systems. A mature command system must reserve complete service chains rather than dispatch individual assets into resource conflicts.

## 6. Physical proof of zero source and zero water

Twelve source-zero checks establish the exclusion chain. They remove bulk source storage and source transport from the event, confirm that the source package is absent, identify and tare a serialized null capsule, install two upstream barriers, verify the interspace between them, show the route dry, confirm blank normal and casualty receivers and preserve an environmental witness.

Ten water-zero checks perform the analogous work. Candidate and public water paths are isolated; normal supply, return and casualty routes are blocked; receivers and terrain are observed dry; weather is inside the registered hold; and background water inventory is accepted.

The source and water chains remain separate because one can contaminate inference about the other. Rainfall, condensation, washdown residue, groundwater intrusion or fire-protection leakage can add water without adding source. Residual fuel, calibration material or sample carryover can add source signature without a bulk package. The event must classify these cases rather than compress them into a generic “clean” result.

The measurement constitution uses two unlike truth roots. Each root owns its instruments, standards, calculations and evidence path. Agreement between roots supports the event. Disagreement remains visible and blocks release; a preferred root cannot overwrite the other.

For source root (r), expanded uncertainty is calculated as

\[
U_{S,r}=2\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{6}u_{i,r}^{2}}.
\]

The six standard-uncertainty allocations are 0.020, 0.020, 0.010, 0.010, 0.015 and 0.010 kg. If observed performance reaches half of every allocation, the expanded uncertainty is approximately 0.0364 kg. The decision ceiling is 0.10 kg.

For water root (r),

\[
U_{W,r}=2\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{5}u_{i,r}^{2}}.
\]

The five allocations are 0.005, 0.005, 0.005, 0.002 and 0.005 m³. Half-allocation performance yields approximately 0.0102 m³ against a 0.025 m³ ceiling.

These are acquisition requirements, not claims about installed instrument performance. The workbook opens with every observed uncertainty blank. Only physical calibration and accepted evidence can populate them.

Twenty-four measurement families are observed through two roots and two windows, creating ninety-six records. The paired windows distinguish the pre-event baseline from the event/post-command observation. Three neighbor proofs confirm that adjacent cells and shared campus systems remain outside the event claim. Seven source-ledger lines and eight water-ledger lines reconcile all registered entries, exits, recoveries and residuals to numerical zero.

## 7. Event clock, command and intervention law

The twenty-four-phase causal clock begins at `T-96 h` and ends with the day-seven regeneration board. Important transitions include the `T-72 h` dossier bind, `T-48 h` source exclusion, `T-36 h` water exclusion, `T-24 h` environmental and neighbor baseline, `T-12 h` crew release, `T-6 h` standards acceptance, `T-2 h` containment readiness, `T-30 min` evidence arming, `T-10 min` issue poll, full-rate acquisition at `T-1 min`, initial state at `T-10 s`, the null command at `T0`, route actuation at five seconds, ignition-prohibited confirmation at fifteen seconds, decision at thirty seconds, safeing at two minutes, classification at fifteen minutes and later evidence, sample, replay and disposition states.

Observed sequence must match the registered sequence. A favorable end state reached through an unknown order is not equivalent to the authorized event because response time and causality are part of the claim.

Twenty command transactions record the issuer, receiver, command identity, expected response, observed response, command time, response time and evidence root. Command receipt is distinct from physical completion. This prevents control-system acknowledgment from being mistaken for valve state, energy isolation or safe configuration.

Twelve final control elements each have three independent safeing paths, yielding thirty-six polls. Normal actuation, independent safety energy and local/manual or mechanically independent action remain separately observable. A shared status display does not make the paths independent.

Twenty abort conditions authorize immediate protection. Twelve intervention rules then classify what happened. The structure separates three questions:

1. was intervention necessary to protect people, the public or the facility?
2. did intervention prevent a valid event record?
3. what does the intervention imply about candidate outcome?

The safety authority never needs permission to intervene. The validity board cannot relabel an embarrassing but interpretable intervention as missing data. A valid adverse result remains admissible. This protects both people and inference.

Thirty-two holds divide eight decision domains into four questions: prerequisite present, observed state acceptable, evidence present and independent acceptance complete. The repeated form is intentional. It prevents one ceremonial go/no-go meeting from hiding the distinct reasons an event is not ready.

## 8. Evidence, validity and outcome

RAY-1 defines twelve evidence obligations, including two native originals, causal-time roots, command traces, full-rate measurements, safeing state, source and water ledgers, sample custody, environmental record, intervention record, recovery state and configuration reconstruction. Six blank samples bracket source, water and environmental pathways.

Evidence is created during the event, not reconstructed afterward. Native records retain device and channel identity, acquisition timing and original values. Derivatives may be useful for analysis but cannot replace originals. The modeled native estate is 117.659 GB for the event; a 30 percent forensic and reconstruction allowance brings allocated capacity to 152.9567 GB.

Sixteen validity rules are decided before ten outcome rules. Validity asks whether the intended question was actually posed and observed. Outcome asks how the article and system behaved. The ordering blocks a common analytical failure: inspecting a favorable outcome first, then relaxing validity requirements to preserve it.

The event-result record therefore allows four meaningful branches:

- valid and acceptable;
- valid and adverse;
- invalid with a preserved physical record; or
- incomplete because required evidence never existed.

Only accepted validity allows a QE-01 credit. An adverse result can still earn a valid credit because it answers the registered question. An invalid event cannot be repaired through statistical imputation, a second run or expert confidence. A replacement event would need a newly issued cell and preserved linkage to the invalid attempt.

Outside-origin replay is required before release. Twelve packet classes are reconstructed away from the event campus. Remote reviewers verify roots, rebuild causal order and apply the registered decision rules without private explanation from the origin team. Disagreement opens an exception. This protects the national program from site-local interpretation and prepares the evidence architecture for operations during network partition or campus loss.

## 9. Recovery is ordered; regeneration is a new proof

Immediately after the event, the pair is not available. Ten recovery states must occur in order:

1. new source terminated at numerical zero;
2. all source and water paths isolated;
3. casualty state resolved;
4. remote physical map accepted;
5. accountable inventories captured;
6. atmosphere and thermal state accepted;
7. source and water ledgers closed;
8. native evidence packet frozen;
9. cleaning and repair released; and
10. serialized baseline restored.

The ordering protects evidence from well-intentioned cleanup. Draining, washing, ventilating, repairing or resetting too early can destroy the physical state needed to classify a result. Public safety remains paramount, but the intervention and resulting evidence loss must be explicit.

Technical baseline restoration has a 72-hour design ceiling. Final event disposition occurs on day seven. These are program targets, not guaranteed performance. A hard case may exceed them and remain quarantined.

Thirty-six regeneration tasks cover article structure, route components, water paths, control elements, safeing paths, instruments, standards, software, evidence media, receivers, rescue equipment, campus utilities and crew state. Completion requires observed state, evidence root and independent acceptance. Twenty-four regression tests then re-exercise the critical relations disturbed by the event.

Six re-entry decisions cover article, rig, pair, crew, campus and evidence estate. All six must pass before the selected pair returns to available. The other five pairs also require confirmation that the event did not consume shared standards, personnel, utilities, media or authority needed for their readiness. RAY-1 therefore closes a six-pair return decision rather than assuming five untouched pairs remained unaffected.

Twelve event credentials are reissued with new start and expiry times. None inherits its pre-event timestamp. The new baseline references the event disposition and regeneration evidence so a later investigator can determine exactly what changed.

This principle scales directly to operational force design. A floating desalination and power plant needs pier, membrane, reactor, grid, crew and water-quality re-entry after a mission. A high-rate wildfire aircraft needs structural, propulsion, tank, door, sensor and crew regeneration after repeated thermal and load exposure. An autonomous earthmoving formation needs wear-state mapping, tool replacement, soil decontamination, software review and operator recertification. If these systems are funded as coequal national power, their return pipelines must be built with the same seriousness as their deployment systems.

## 10. Release, authority and the refusal to extrapolate

Twenty-four release gates gather the complete event chain. They cover inherited RAX readiness, cell eligibility, draw commitments, selection, event reservation, dossier, crew, issue, configuration freeze, zero-source and zero-water checks, uncertainty, measurement windows, neighbor proof, safeing polls, commands, clock, holds, evidence, blanks, abort and intervention classification, accepted result, and regeneration/re-entry.

Every gate is conjunctive. Twenty-three passing gates and one open gate produce an open event. A board acceptance timestamp cannot turn an open source state into PASS. The release board reads operating sheets; it does not type the operating result.

Three final decisions have separate meanings:

| Decision | Required condition | Authority created |
|---|---|---|
| `RAY-CLOSED` | all 24 gates accepted | close one event record |
| `QE01-CREDIT` | one validity-accepted event result | add one credit to the QE-01 class |
| `PAIR-RETURNED` | all six re-entry decisions accepted | restore the campaign pair pool |

None creates `QE02-AUTHORITY`. Ten boundary conditions keep `QE-02` blocked, including incomplete QE-01 coverage, no accepted source lot, no source-bearing event issue, no thermal or fire observation, no source-retention evidence, no casualty-water classification, no source-exposed recovery proof, no transfer result and no board release.

This refusal to extrapolate is strategically important. Large national programs often mistake a successful demonstration for class qualification, a qualified class for production readiness and production readiness for field service. RAY-1 allows only the smallest claim supported by one event. Ambition appears in the completeness of the learning system, not in the size of the headline.

These programs show how a Department of Resilience could drive invention rather than wait for commercial markets. The null event creates demand for sensors that distinguish absence from blindness, interfaces that refuse operation when authority is incomplete, robots that map post-event state before people enter, and configuration systems that generate a justified re-entry campaign from actual exposure.

The same technologies would serve the future response force. Zero-state metrology can verify an evacuated chemical corridor or a decontaminated hospital zone. This is the productive relationship between force structure and innovation. The institution states a hard, public, measurable mission; competing industrial teams pursue unlike architectures; public ranges expose failures; evidence remains available to later teams; and acquisition proceeds only when the physical claim advances. The program does not prescribe today’s components as the limit of tomorrow’s force.

The workbook opens in the honest physical state: operating observations are blank and event gates are OPEN. Its 150 terminal assertions all pass, showing that the workbook’s own counts, equations, boundaries and references are coherent. A separate dynamic verification harness performs 123 checks. It enters complete evidence, tests a successful closure, then perturbs selection range, cell eligibility, commitment, issue window, configuration, uncertainty, measurements, clock order, evidence, ledgers, validity, recovery, replay, exceptions, release and finance. All 123 checks pass. A formula-error scan finds zero errors.

The testing found and corrected two meaningful defects during this pass. First, an out-of-range selected index could propagate a reference error; the model now rejects it through a clean failed selection state. Second, uncertainty summaries initially compared each root’s passing component count with the combined count for both roots. The corrected decision requires six source components or five water components per root. The fix reinforces the governing concept: unlike roots are separate claims, not duplicate rows that can be pooled.

These tests validate calculation and control logic only. They do not populate physical results. The model is neither a safety approval nor a prediction of event success. Its financial values are program design references, not appropriations or vendor estimates.
