# RBH-1 — Root A dry-igniter reference architecture competition and ER-01 class constitution

## Decision abstract

`RBH-1` defines the first physical rung of the `AM-06` entry ladder established by [RBG-1](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-am06-source-water-ignition-entry-ladder-reset.md). Its question is deliberately narrower than fire:

> Can the public test institution create, locate, measure, interrupt, remove and independently reconstruct a prescribed ignition-energy opportunity across twelve exact rig–truth-root cells while energetic source and water remain physically excluded and observed as zero?

The event contains no fuel, candidate water or flame. It cannot establish ignition probability, flame stability, fire control or thermal performance. It establishes whether “valid ignition opportunity” is a measured physical proposition rather than a command-log label.[1–8]

Three unlike ignition architectures enter a public competition: a galvanically isolated point-discharge cartridge, a remote radiant-energy head and a distributed resistive thermal element. Each enters through two independently controlled builder lineages at three campuses. Eighteen articles each execute six challenge families, producing 108 required valid architecture trials. A conjunctive screen eliminates any candidate with uncommanded discharge, energy outside the registered window, unacceptable spatial or temporal error, failed abort, persistent hot state, service-coupled evidence or incomplete outside-campus reconstruction. Weighted scoring cannot compensate for a failed safety or truth condition.

After selection, eighteen production-representative ignition assemblies are built: twelve active, four same-lineage operational spares and two sealed forensic references. Twelve exact rig–root cells receive one primary dry event. Four reserve tokens replace only independently invalid attempts and preserve root and cell identity. A valid adverse result remains in the class. Required execution is twelve events; the maximum is sixteen.

Each architecture trial binds twenty-four gates, ninety-six evidence records, eight physical safeing paths, 160 measurement channels and two outside-campus reconstructions. Each `ER-01` event binds thirty-six gates, 144 evidence records, twelve physical safeing paths, eighteen return decisions and twelve outside-campus replay decisions. The maximum program provisions approximately 23.2 terabytes of native and governed evidence and reserves 30 terabytes for indexes, repair copies, forensic work and disputed packets.

The dry event preserves numeric zero for source and water through physical isolation, independent mass and volume observations, background samples and custody seals. Zero is not inferred from “not scheduled.” The igniter receives one ten-second opportunity beginning at the inherited `T+15 s` phase. Command, delivered energy, onset, duration, location, local atmosphere, interruption and terminal hot state remain separate observations.

RBH-1 allocates 260 positions from the proposed 720-position `RAS-3R` formation. The fifteen-month stage uses USD 180 million capital, USD 42 million per year and USD 95 million of the cross-classified technology account. Stage exposure is USD 232.5 million; technology is not added again. This leaves USD 920 million of the `RAS-3R` capital increment, USD 25 million per year of incremental support and USD 140 million of technology for the later water, ancestry and fire rungs.

The primary calendar is thirty-seven weeks from architecture-campaign start: twelve weeks of trial execution, four weeks of selection review, twelve weeks of selected-bank build and acceptance, six weeks of primary issue, one week of dry return and two weeks of class review. Four invalidity reserves add two issue weeks, creating a thirty-nine-week maximum. Remaining stage time protects redesign, common-cause investigation and second-lineage rebuild; it is not hidden event authority.

Every physical observation, candidate decision and class field begins open. Planning completion does not discharge an igniter. An accepted `ER-01` class can authorize only `RBI-1`, the `ER-02` water-only hydraulic and custody constitution. It does not authorize water issue, energetic source, flame, candidate admission, production, export or exterior rendering.

## 1. The epistemic object

The earlier method defined an ignition opportunity as a ten-second registered interval. That temporal definition is necessary but insufficient. A command may be issued while the final element remains cold, energy may appear in the wrong place, an unintended path may energize early, or the evidence system may record only the command bit.

RBH-1 separates nine identities:

1. **command** — the authorized instruction and issuer;
2. **source energy** — energy admitted to the ignition system;
3. **delivered energy** — energy observed at the registered interaction boundary;
4. **spatial state** — location, footprint and orientation relative to the future source surface;
5. **temporal state** — onset, duration, interruption and terminal time;
6. **atmosphere** — local composition, temperature, humidity and flow;
7. **safeing state** — isolation, discharge prevention and residual temperature;
8. **evidence state** — two native originals and outside-campus reconstruction; and
9. **event validity** — whether the registered dry proposition occurred.

None can substitute for another. Electrical input is not delivered energy. Visible light is not thermal coupling. A safe abort is not a valid full opportunity. A complete command log is not physical final-state evidence.

## 2. Three candidate architectures

| Candidate | Physical concept | Principal advantage | Principal uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| `IA-1` | galvanically isolated point-discharge cartridge | compact, local and directly instrumentable | electrode condition, electromagnetic interference and placement sensitivity |
| `IA-2` | remote radiant-energy head | no energized hardware at the future source surface | line-of-sight loss, window fouling and beam-location evidence |
| `IA-3` | distributed resistive thermal element | controlled area and slower spatial averaging | stored heat, slow abort and residual hot-state custody |

The competition does not presume that one technology must serve every later rung. It selects the reference architecture for `ER-01` and creates transfer evidence. A later fire rung may reopen architecture if vapor geometry, water, smoke or thermal exposure changes the proposition.

Each candidate enters through `BL-A` and `BL-B` at each of three campuses. The eighteen articles are not replicas from one production root. Builder lineages must use separately controlled process records, calibration chains and acceptance authorities while conforming to the same public interface and evidence specification.

## 3. The 108-trial architecture campaign

Every article executes six valid trials.

| Family | Challenge | Required answer |
|---|---|---|
| `IT-1` | nominal prescribed opportunity | energy, onset, duration, placement and terminal state close |
| `IT-2` | placement at the accepted geometric edge | independent coordinates remain inside the registered window |
| `IT-3` | low/high accepted atmosphere boundary | delivery remains measurable without silent correction |
| `IT-4` | one nonessential service denied | local control, isolation and evidence remain sufficient |
| `IT-5` | campus network and primary time removed | causal order and two native originals remain complete |
| `IT-6` | interrupted opportunity and emergency removal | energy ceases, residual state is bounded and reconstruction remains possible |

The program therefore requires \(3\times2\times3\times6=108\) valid trials. Invalid trials remain recorded. A repeat requires independent causal invalidity and a prospectively assigned reserve position. A valid adverse result cannot be replaced.

Each trial uses a noncombustible public reference plate with calorimetric, electrical, optical, geometric and terminal-temperature witnesses. The plate is a measurement article, not a simulated fuel. It cannot yield ignition or flame credit.

## 4. Conjunctive qualification and selection

The following values are registered research hypotheses rather than accepted performance. Physical trial fields begin blank.

| Variable | Planning acceptance window |
|---|---:|
| delivered-to-commanded energy ratio | 0.95 to 1.05 |
| onset error from registered phase | no more than 10 ms absolute |
| ten-second duration error | no more than 50 ms absolute |
| interaction-location error | no more than 5 mm |
| repeated coordinate error | no more than 2 mm |
| cessation after abort command | no more than 100 ms |
| residual reference-surface temperature at 60 s | no more than 80 °C |
| residual reference-surface temperature at 10 min | no more than 45 °C |
| pre-command delivered energy | below the registered measurement floor |
| uncommanded discharge | none |

Every candidate must also pass physical isolation, single-fault safeing, final-state indication, local operation, two complete originals and two outside-campus reconstructions. Missing measurement is not zero. An average inside the energy window cannot erase a single valid excursion.

Candidates that pass all hard screens enter a minimax review. The review compares the largest normalized deviation across energy, time, location, residual heat, reset time, service dependence and evidence loss. Ties break first on lower residual hazard, then lower required service count, then shorter measured reset, then a precommitted public draw. Acquisition price cannot purchase relief from a scientific or safety gate.

## 5. Selected assembly bank

The selected architecture produces eighteen serialized `IGN` assemblies.

| Role | `BL-A` | `BL-B` | Total | Authority |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| active exact-cell assembly | 6 | 6 | 12 | one rig–root cell each |
| operational spare | 2 | 2 | 4 | none before same-lineage rebaseline |
| sealed forensic reference | 1 | 1 | 2 | no operational authority |
| **total** | **9** | **9** | **18** | |

An operational spare can replace isolated hardware loss only after geometry, energy path, calibration, isolation, terminal temperature and evidence are rebuilt under a new serial. It cannot erase a valid adverse event or an architecture-wide defect. A forensic assembly remains sealed even when schedule tightens.

The twelve active assignments cross truth root with builder lineage exactly as RBF-1 did: rigs `S0`, `S2` and `S4` use `BL-A` for `TR-A` and `BL-B` for `TR-B`; rigs `S1`, `S3` and `S5` reverse the pairing. Every campus contains all four root–lineage combinations.

## 6. ER-01 exact-cell class

The class contains twelve primaries and four dormant invalidity reserves, two per truth-root family. Each primary binds one rig, one root, one selected assembly, one reference plate and one evidence packet before issue. Reserve activation preserves the exact cell and root family.

A dry event must show numeric zero source and zero water through:

- closed and sealed energetic-source boundary;
- independent source-vessel and receiver observations;
- isolated candidate- and casualty-water boundaries;
- measured initial and terminal receiver volumes;
- background air and surface samples; and
- source and water custodians’ separate acceptance.

The igniter opportunity begins at `T+15 s` and ends at `T+25 s` unless an abort acts sooner. A valid full opportunity closes all ten seconds. A valid interrupted trial can qualify safeing during the architecture campaign but cannot create an `ER-01` class credit.

Each event has thirty-six gates across prior authority, cell identity, assembly, reference plate, source exclusion, water exclusion, atmosphere, geometry, energy metrology, time, safeing, evidence, return and independent review. It also binds 144 named evidence records, 160 measurement channels, twelve safeing paths, eighteen return decisions and twelve replay decisions.

| Domain | Channels |
|---|---:|

| calorimetric delivered energy | 24 |
| high-speed optical and spectral state | 24 |
| geometry, pose and reference coordinates | 16 |
| local atmosphere and flow | 16 |
| independent causal time | 16 |
| safeing and final-element state | 16 |
| environment and neighboring state | 16 |
| **total** | **160** |

Command and physical channels use independent acquisition paths. A high-speed camera may support location and onset but cannot be the sole delivered-energy root. Electrical measurement may support source energy but cannot establish interaction at the reference plate. The primary and unlike truth roots must remain capable of disagreeing.

Every trial and event creates two native originals. Another campus reconstructs energy, onset, duration, location, abort state and terminal condition without receiving the origin’s final answer. The maximum program provisions approximately 12.96 TB for architecture trials and 10.24 TB for sixteen possible class events. Thirty terabytes provides about 6.8 TB for indexes, repair copies, disputed packets and forensic reconstruction.

## 8. Safeing and return

Twelve physical safeing paths govern each class event: energy-source isolation, local inhibit, independent trip, passive or mechanical disconnect, discharge prevention, residual-energy drain, geometry withdrawal, hot-state isolation, atmosphere alarm, evidence latch, remote stop and manual protected stop.

The selected assembly receives a seven-day dry return. Return includes immediate isolation, one-hour terminal temperature, twenty-four-hour electrical and geometric inspection, three-day drift review, evidence reconstruction, safeing-path replay and day-seven baseline. This clock belongs to the ignition assembly and reference plate. It grants no credit for the future 1,008-hour source-and-water module return.

Early cooling cannot close return. A low terminal temperature is one observation; geometry, insulation, isolation, energy calibration and evidence must also close. Failed return quarantines the serial and triggers same-lineage rebaseline or common-cause review.

## 9. Calendar and capacity

Six public candidate benches, two per campus, can execute eighteen article trials per week in aggregate, but the campaign limits each article to one challenge per week so drift and reconfiguration remain visible. Six trials per article therefore occupy six execution weeks. Setup, two boundary repeats, non-origin replay and review expand the registered architecture campaign to twelve weeks.

Selection review takes four weeks. The selected eighteen-assembly bank then receives twelve weeks for build, calibration, independent acceptance and forensic sealing. The twelve-cell class issues at two events per week over six weeks. The last assembly completes seven-day return in week seven and the class board closes two weeks later.

| Boundary | Primary | Maximum with four invalidity reserves |
|---|---:|---:|
| architecture campaign | 12 weeks | 12 weeks |
| selection review | 4 weeks | 4 weeks |
| selected bank build and acceptance | 12 weeks | 12 weeks |
| class issue | 6 weeks | 8 weeks |
| last return and class review | 3 weeks | 3 weeks |
| **total from campaign start** | **37 weeks** | **39 weeks** |

The fifteen-month stage is not sized to the optimistic critical path alone. It preserves time for candidate redesign before selection, common-cause quarantine, second-lineage reconstruction and independent review without authorizing a later rung.

The industrial objective is a public ignition-reference field, not a single proprietary igniter. Common mechanical, electrical, optical, state-reporting and evidence interfaces allow multiple builders to compete. Public reference plates, calibration methods and outside-campus replay prevent a supplier from owning the definition of successful delivery.

The technologies transfer to controlled backburning, industrial-fire research, hazardous-material neutralization, remote flare replacement, battery-fire testing and degraded-service thermal control. The Department’s demand creates specialist suppliers in fault-safe energy delivery, spatial metrology, residual-heat sensing and evidence-grade local control.

## 12. Decision states, falsifiers and next object

RBH-1 has five final states: `NO-ARCHITECTURE`, `ARCHITECTURE-SELECTED`, `ER-01-QUALIFIED`, `CLASS-OPEN` and `QUARANTINE`. Architecture selection does not imply class transfer.

The program stops or narrows if an architecture discharges without command; command and delivered energy cannot be separated; energy, placement, timing or terminal temperature exceeds its registered window; a candidate requires origin services for replay; a valid adverse trial or event is repeated away; a reserve crosses cell or root identity; a spare enters without same-lineage rebaseline; source or water zero is inferred from schedule; one root’s algorithm is used by the other; cooling is treated as complete return; fiscal authority is absent; or a result is used to issue water, source or flame.

`ER-01-QUALIFIED` can authorize only constitution of `RBI-1`, the full nominal `ER-02` water-only hydraulic and custody rung. RBI-1 must treat 24 cubic metres as a full deployed mass system with route, metering, retention, ancestry, return and environmental disposition. Energetic source, flame, candidate admission, production, operational procurement, export and exterior rendering remain blocked.

## Research stocktake

RBH-1 adds five conclusions to the Department design.

First, opportunity must be measured at the interaction boundary. Command evidence establishes intent, not physical delivery.

Second, ignition architecture is a public infrastructure question. Common interfaces, independent roots and multiple builders matter before fuel ever appears.

Third, dry trials can be demanding without borrowing fire authority. They reveal energy, geometry, timing, abort and residual-state failure at much lower consequence.

Fourth, safe removal is part of the capability. A device that delivers energy but cannot rapidly prove isolation and terminal state is incomplete for disaster work.

Fifth, the innovation program begins with measurement limits. The Department funds new calorimetry, spatial reconstruction, local causal evidence and fault-safe energy delivery because later fire claims otherwise cannot be made honestly.
