# F36 `CISA-40` `SA6` Root A method-card and causal-estimator constitution

## Executive judgment

The [Root A technology constitution](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-drainage-firewater-and-recovery-technology-constitution.md) created three unlike drainage, firewater and recovery candidates built through six industrial roots.[1] The [public-rig specification](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-full-scale-fire-hydraulic-rig-physical-specification.md) then defined six candidate-neutral public instruments.[2] The [execution constitution](f36-cisa40-sa6-root-a-fire-hydraulic-rig-execution-and-source-escalation-constitution.md) froze 306 events, 96 matched pairs, 294 tonnes of base source, 74 tonnes of reserve source and a common twenty-four-phase clock.[3] Those documents establish what is to be compared and where it can be compared. They do not yet establish what a method is, how the public institution knows that a challenge physically occurred, how source and water remain attributable, or how event records become candidate-level causal claims without hiding adverse or incomplete evidence.

This pass creates `RAM-1`, the Root A method-card and causal-estimator constitution. Its governing decision is:

> Establish ten physically distinct public method families on one common governance clock, then carry every observation through an explicit event-to-pair-to-root-to-candidate estimator in which missingness, public intervention, uncertainty and industrial ancestry remain visible.

`RAM-1` treats method design as sovereign public infrastructure. A test method is not a contractor procedure attached to an article. It is a public measurement system comprising a physical state, a source or medium, a challenge realization, a command sequence, independent witnesses, safeing logic, feature transformations, uncertainty, recovery and an amendment boundary. A Department of Resilience that cannot own those objects would depend on suppliers to define what success means.

Ten methods span dry geometry, water-only hydraulics, safe surrogate behavior, debris, dual-tracer ancestry, quarter-source unignited work, a quarter-source fire anchor, full-source paired fire, controlled destructive work, and recovery/reset/replay. The same twenty-four phases bind all ten. Common phase identity does not imply common physical hazard, instrumentation or authority. The method card specializes each phase while preserving a comparable chain of custody.

All 306 `RFE-1` events now compile to one method and one set of twenty-four method-phase procedures. The distribution is 12 `AM-00`, 12 `AM-01`, 12 `AM-02`, 12 `AM-03`, 12 `AM-04`, 102 `AM-05`, 6 `AM-06`, 96 `AM-07`, 24 `AM-08` and 18 `AM-09` events. The apparently asymmetric counts are intentional. `AM-05` carries the 96 quarter-source paired events in addition to six development anchors; `AM-07` carries all 96 full-source paired events; destructive and recovery propositions are separate methods rather than pooled afterthoughts.

The challenge system contains thirty-two initial tolerances and twenty-four challenge-realization records. Each challenge requires measured decisive state, a registered dwell, blindness and an independent witness. A fixture label or command cannot establish that an adverse grade, blocked drain, half-full receiver, debris field, pool-fire boundary, reduced water capacity, partial thermal barrier or compound service denial existed in the physical world.

Twenty-four measurement functions each receive a primary chain and an independent witness chain, producing forty-eight calibration objects. Thirty method-linked sample sets preserve identity, pre-method state and post-method transformations. Two certificates on one transducer path do not count as independent evidence. The public institution must be able to lose a candidate channel, a campus network or a primary time source while retaining the decisive proposition.

Thirty typed decision features cover source termination, source capture, mass closure, pool and thermal state, water use and ancestry, environmental custody, public intervention, recovery, time integrity, channel availability, causal order and evidence completeness. They are not combined into a weighted score. A strong result in a convenience measure cannot compensate for a hard safety, validity or custody failure.

Inference is hierarchical and intentionally modest. An event record holds raw values. A matched-pair estimator subtracts control from challenge only when both events are valid. Two site effects form one builder-root result only after both pairs are independently accepted. Two unlike builder roots form one candidate result only after both root results are independently accepted. Site spread and builder spread remain visible. No level averages the available subset when its required counterpart is missing.

Public source isolation, casualty-source transfer, quench and casualty-water capture are competing terminal events. They are successful public safety actions and adverse candidate evidence. They are not ordinary right-censoring. The estimator does not imagine how the candidate might have performed after the public institution stopped the event.

Mass and water closure remain interval-valued. Each term retains ancestry, time support, calibration and transformation uncertainty. Midpoint substitution is prohibited when a decisive term is unresolved. Covariance may be introduced only through a prospective amendment that identifies the shared physical source. The initial thirty uncertainty chains use root-sum-square only for declared uncorrelated components and retain an expanded value beside the governing budget.

One synthetic training case makes the anti-erasure law concrete. `RFE-E083`, the `A06` challenge event at `PTC-B` for root `AF-1-BA`, loses its independent dual-tracer witness before water closure. The event remains in the record and becomes invalid for the proposition. Reserve token `RFE-R030` links to it, and replacement `RAM-X001` receives a new identity. Neither the original nor the replacement earns physical credit in this planning pass.

The constitution also creates twelve mission-led technology campaigns. They pursue real-time source inventory tomography, dual-coded fluid ancestry, instrumented terrain skins, radiant-field tomography, self-describing instruments, traceable distributed time, a public intervention recorder, remote casualty reconnaissance, rapid source-water microanalysis, a public causal-state compiler, robotic post-event material recovery and outside-site executable replay. Several do not exist at the required integrated scale. That is a program requirement, not a reason to omit them.

The thirty-six-month method-realization program requires 342 public positions, USD 220M capital and USD 142M per year. USD 178M capital and USD 118M per year overlap registered Root A infrastructure, leaving USD 42M and USD 24M per year as net-new gross requirement. The corrected registered portfolio is `BRD-12R` at USD 2.350B capital and USD 430M per year. The uncredited ceiling is `BRD-12U` at USD 2.965B and USD 583M per year. Program-period exposure is USD 646M.

At baseline, all planning gates pass, every physical gate remains open and every downstream authority remains blocked. All 3,851 terminal audits and sixty-one disposable sensitivity checks pass. ## 1. Decision, scope and institutional meaning

### 1.1 Decision question

`RAM-1` answers:

> What public methods, physical tolerances, calibration chains, samples, commands, safe states, features, missing-data rules, uncertainty rules, estimators, evidence objects, amendment rules, technology campaigns, workforce and fiscal commitments are required to turn the frozen Root A event population into reproducible causal evidence?

This is narrower than selecting a Root A candidate and broader than drafting laboratory instructions. It creates the rule system that must exist before the first physical method can be qualified.

### 1.2 Why a method is a force asset

The Department of Resilience concept assumes a civilian institution with force-scale industrial authority. Such an institution needs more than vehicles and crews. It needs public competence to discover whether its systems work under damaged, contaminated, denied and time-critical conditions. Method ownership therefore sits beside logistics, command, maintenance and training as an operational capacity.

A mature method enterprise performs five public functions:

1. It defines propositions before suppliers see results.
2. It creates physical challenge states that are independent of candidate telemetry.
3. It records native evidence under public custody.
4. It separates life safety from candidate scoring without concealing either.
5. It preserves enough method and evidence context for another site to reproduce the decision.

The alternative is structurally weak. If each candidate builder supplies its own procedure, calibration, feature extractor and explanation for missing data, the government purchases competing narratives rather than comparable systems. `RAM-1` establishes a common public language while retaining the physical differences that each method requires.

### 1.3 What is fixed

This pass fixes:

- ten method families and 240 method-phase procedures;
- compilation of all 306 inherited events and one complete 51-event worked root;
- thirty-two challenge tolerances and twenty-four realization records;
- forty-eight calibration chains and thirty sample objects;
- twenty command transactions, twenty abort channels and ten safe-state actions;
- thirty typed features, 300 method-feature assignments and thirty registered equations;
- 306 event, 96 pair, 48 root and 24 candidate estimator rows;
- six competing terminal events and sixteen missing-data rules;
- thirty uncertainty chains, twelve validity rules and fourteen outcome classes;
- ten recovery-entry states, twelve evidence objects, fourteen amendment classes and sixteen regression triggers;
- twelve technology campaigns, a thirty-six-month schedule and a 342-position public establishment; and
- gross, overlap, ownership, corrected and uncredited fiscal views.

### 1.4 What remains unearned

`RAM-1` does not qualify a method, accept a tolerance, calibrate a chain, certify a sample procedure, drill a command or abort path, accept an extractor, execute an event, close a pair, calculate a physical candidate effect, select a candidate, freeze Root A, authorize production or authorize exterior form.

The planning ledger is complete because every obligation has a location and an owner. The physical ledger remains open because no physical observation has been supplied.

The design principle is simple: governance should be common where comparison requires it, and physics should differ where the proposition requires it.

## 3. Challenge realization as physical truth

### 3.1 Four records per challenge are not enough

Each of the eight paired challenges has four initial-condition tolerances. A tolerance record specifies the variable, nominal state, allowed deviation, unit, dwell and decision class. The challenge also has three realization objects:

- decisive-state acceptance;
- blindness acceptance; and
- independent-witness acceptance.

These objects answer different questions. The first asks whether the required physical state existed. The second asks whether candidate and local operators could infer the sealed condition before insertion. The third asks whether a public chain observed the state without using candidate telemetry. One object cannot substitute for another.

### 3.2 Challenge examples

`A01`, adverse grade, is not satisfied by placing a cassette marked “reverse grade.” The local grade must be 1.0 percent reverse within 0.05 percentage point, datum displacement must be 25 mm within 2 mm, cross-grade must remain within its registered band and surface temperature must remain within one kelvin for the registered dwell.

`A03`, a half-full receiver, requires measured usable fill, initial liquid density, receiver temperature and level trend. A commanded fill percentage cannot establish inventory if gas space, temperature, geometry or leakage alters the true state.

`A06`, water-capacity shock, combines a candidate vault at half usable fill, a twenty-minute candidate-water window, 2,000 kg of source and a registered water-service condition. The result cannot be attributed to limited water capacity if the physical vault fraction drifted outside tolerance before source admission.

`A08`, compound service denial, requires zero normal energy availability, zero campus-network availability, zero public primary-time availability and a thirty-minute water window. The method therefore tests not only the candidate article but the institution’s ability to observe causality with distributed local time and local originals.

### 3.3 Blindness has a limited purpose

Blindness protects comparison against anticipatory tuning. It must never conceal a condition from the authorities that need it for safety. The challenge authority knows the state, the safety system knows the safeing envelope, and the evidence system records the truth identity. Candidate and local operating teams are denied only the information that would let them tune behavior to a known challenge before insertion.

## 4. Measurement, sampling and ancestry

### 4.1 Forty-eight chains across twenty-four functions

The measurement architecture inherits 576 public channels per rig from `RFP-1`.[2] `RAM-1` groups those channels into twenty-four decision functions: initial source mass; source flow and residual; surface depth and extent; terrain datum and reaction; drain flow and inventory; candidate receiver inventory; water supply and return; casualty source and water; temperature; heat flux and radiation; flame and gas; structural load and strain; chemistry; robot state; safety power and action; weather and plume; high-speed imagery; thermal imagery; event time; custody and configuration; facility utilities; environmental witness; casualty-system state; and independent field video.

Each function receives a primary chain and an independent witness chain. Independence means a physically unlike principle, separately powered witness or separately timed chain where common cause has been addressed. Duplicate certificates, mirrored packets or two displays driven by one acquisition path do not create independent evidence.

### 4.2 Sampling is part of the method

Every method has three sample objects:

1. identity and control material before preparation;
2. a pre-method split before candidate or public contact; and
3. post-method material from applicable receiver, casualty, surface, air and solid residues.

The sample plan records transformations and ancestry. It does not backfill a missing decisive field. A post-event laboratory result may refine chemical identity, but it cannot invent the absent time history needed to decide whether source crossed a boundary before public action.

### 4.3 Source and water require separate ledgers

Root A joins source control and firewater. A single liquid-volume ledger would confound at least four inventories: admitted source, candidate water, public water and environmental custody. The method therefore requires dual ancestry. A source-equivalent mass can appear in a water stream; a water quantity can enter a source receiver; public casualty capture can safely take custody of both. Every transfer must retain its origin and time support.

For a source interval, let admitted mass be \([S_L,S_U]\) and accepted destination intervals be \([D_{i,L},D_{i,U}]\). The residual interval is conservatively bounded by:

\[
R_S=[S_L-\sum_iD_{i,U},\;S_U-\sum_iD_{i,L}].
\]

For water, gross candidate supply \(W_G\), accepted candidate return \(W_R\), candidate storage change \(\Delta W_C\), public casualty custody \(W_P\) and environmental custody \(W_E\) must retain ancestry. Net retained candidate water is not inferred from one tank level if other paths remain possible.

Intervals are retained until a registered rule resolves them. If a decisive interval crosses a hard bound, the result is unresolved or adverse according to the method; it is not replaced with the midpoint that produces the preferred answer.

## 5. From native observations to causal claims

### 5.1 Thirty features, six domains

The feature dictionary contains six linked domains:

- **Source:** terminal time, capture fraction, accounted fraction, unaccounted mass, public casualty-source use, neighbor transfer and pool extent.
- **Fire and thermal:** off-boundary pool state, heat-release estimate, public-boundary radiant flux and dose, and candidate heat-rejection load.
- **Water and ancestry:** gross candidate water, net retained water, vault utilization, ancestry completeness, mixed source-water mass, public casualty-water use and uncontrolled runoff.
- **Environment and public action:** environmental source mass and public intervention.
- **Recovery:** safe refusal, remote-map completeness and serialized recovery time.
- **Validity:** time skew, usable-channel fraction, cause-order confidence, evidence completeness and expanded mass and water discrepancies.

Every feature has a unit, preferred direction, governing bound where applicable, decision class and expanded-uncertainty budget. A diagnostic feature may have no scalar bound. That does not make it disposable; it means the feature explains mechanism rather than independently deciding success.

### 5.2 No weighted composite

Weighted scores are attractive because they create one rank. They are dangerous here because a candidate might offset an environmental release with rapid terminal source control, or offset public intervention with low water use. `RAM-1` instead uses conjunctive hard limits and transparent diagnostic features. Candidate comparison can examine effect sizes and spreads only after every applicable safety, validity and custody requirement is satisfied.

### 5.3 Event estimator

The event level contains the native decision inputs beside identity, method, root, site and pair. Validity is linked from the immutable event compiler. Outcome remains blank until adjudication. State is:

- `OPEN` when validity is unresolved;
- `OPEN` when an event is valid but outcome remains unresolved;
- `PASS` when a valid event has an assigned outcome; and
- `INVALID` when the validity board rejects the proposition.

“Pass” at this layer means the event record is procedurally complete, not that the candidate succeeded. Candidate outcome remains one of fourteen explicit classes, including clean success, bounded degraded success, safe refusal, source failure, fire failure, water failure, ancestry failure, environmental failure, recovery failure, public intervention, reset failure, unsafe excursion and no candidate outcome.

### 5.4 Matched-pair estimator

For valid control \(Y_{rsc0}\) and valid challenge \(Y_{rsc1}\), where \(r\) is root, \(s\) site and \(c\) challenge, the registered effect is:

\[
\Delta_{rsc}=Y_{rsc1}-Y_{rsc0}.
\]

Signed values remain visible for terminal time, unaccounted source, net retained water, radiant dose and public intervention. The sign is interpreted with the feature’s preferred direction. The pair remains blank unless both members are valid. Pair acceptance is a separate physical decision; calculation alone cannot close its gate.

Alternating order in the inherited campaign reduces systematic first-use and second-use bias.[3] It does not erase carryover risk. Method phases still require restored serialized baseline, and the estimator retains the registered order and recovery state.

### 5.5 Builder-root estimator

Each root-challenge cell has one `PTC-B` pair and one `PTC-C` pair. When both are accepted:

\[
\bar{\Delta}_{rc}=\frac{\Delta_{rBc}+\Delta_{rCc}}{2},
\qquad
S^{site}_{rc}=|\Delta_{rBc}-\Delta_{rCc}|.
\]

The mean is a registered summary, not a claim that two sites estimate a population distribution. The site spread remains beside it because site sensitivity is operationally important. A response system that succeeds only at one prepared campus has not established a transferable method.

### 5.6 Candidate estimator

Each candidate has unlike `BA` and `BB` industrial roots. After both root effects are accepted:

\[
\bar{\Delta}_{kc}=\frac{\bar{\Delta}_{kBA,c}+\bar{\Delta}_{kBB,c}}{2},
\qquad
S^{builder}_{kc}=|\bar{\Delta}_{kBA,c}-\bar{\Delta}_{kBB,c}|.
\]

The builder spread exposes dependence on manufacturing ancestry. A low mean with a large spread is not equivalent to consistent performance. `RAM-1` does not yet define a production selection rule because no physical results exist and no candidate is admitted.

### 5.7 Competing terminal events

The estimator distinguishes six terminal conditions:

1. candidate bounded terminal state;
2. public source isolation;
3. public casualty-source transfer;
4. public quench;
5. public casualty-water capture; and
6. decisive evidence loss.

Public action is not a nuisance variable. It is an observed transition with issuer, threshold, time, inventory and consequence. Once public source isolation or quench acts, later autonomous candidate success cannot be observed under the registered proposition. Treating that condition as routine right-censoring would assume the unobserved future is exchangeable with uninterrupted events. That assumption is not credible because the public action is triggered by the candidate’s evolving state.

The constitution therefore records public intervention as adverse candidate evidence while preserving its success as a safety action.

## 6. Missing data, uncertainty and validity

### 6.1 Blank and zero are different states

The workbook uses blank for unresolved information and zero only for an observed numeric value. Visual verification caught and corrected a formula-link defect that initially displayed unresolved validity as zero in event and pair estimators. The corrected formulas preserve blank through both layers. This is not cosmetic. A false zero can be interpreted as absence of intervention, mass discrepancy or time skew and can silently create a favorable result.

### 6.2 Sixteen missing-data rules

The missing-data constitution covers absent decisive functions, expired calibration, loss of both originals, low channel availability, excessive time skew, absent challenge witness, interval-valued source or water terms, uncertain intervention time, absent proprietary channels, missing supporting channels, missing environmental witness, incomplete recovery map, broken sample custody, an invalid pair member and a missing higher-level input.

The recurring rule is anti-imputation:

- decisive absence invalidates or leaves the relevant proposition unresolved;
- supporting absence can use only a preregistered redundant estimator and carries expanded uncertainty;
- a valid pair member remains preserved if its mate is invalid;
- higher levels do not average the available subset; and
- a replacement receives a new identity.

Candidate proprietary data may supplement the packet. Its absence carries no penalty when the public decisive set is complete, and its presence cannot rescue an incomplete public decisive set.

### 6.3 Uncertainty chain

For each feature, initial standard uncertainty includes calibration, spatial, temporal and transformation terms. Where the method declares them uncorrelated:

\[
u_f=\sqrt{u_{cal,f}^2+u_{spatial,f}^2+u_{time,f}^2+u_{transform,f}^2},
\qquad U_f=2u_f.
\]

The factor-two expanded value is a planning convention, not a universal confidence claim. If two components share a clock, geometry reference, chemistry model or calibration source, covariance must be identified through amendment. It may not be ignored merely because the root-sum-square result is smaller.

### 6.4 Validity before outcome

Twelve conjunctive validity rules ask whether the registered proposition was tested: configuration identity; source identity; initial state; challenge realization; decisive measurement; time integrity; public-boundary integrity; mass and water closure; intervention identity; custody continuity; recovery integrity; and independent adjudication.

A valid failure is scientifically valuable and remains in every denominator. An invalid event answers no candidate question. A no-flame result after a valid ignition opportunity can be valid evidence. A facility-caused departure can invalidate attribution. A candidate-caused sensor loss can be both an adverse candidate fact and a validity problem for a separate proposition. The independent board must state both layers rather than compressing them into “bad run.”

## 7. Command, safeing, recovery and change control

### 7.1 Command transactions

Twenty transactions cover configuration freeze, baseline acceptance, source admission, public isolation readiness, candidate-control readiness, native-evidence arming, challenge insertion, method start, ignition opportunity, candidate source/water/robot actions, four public interventions, remote reconnaissance, casualty extraction, packet freeze and restoration release.

Every consequential action has one issuer, one timestamp, one observable final element and one predetermined failure response. The candidate does not issue public safeing commands. The test conductor cannot approve calibration or environmental custody. Evidence collection never delays immediate life safety.

### 7.2 Abort channels and safe state

Twenty abort channels cover source identity, clock integrity, native originals, challenge realization, terminal source, boundary crossing, vault level, casualty admission, pool extent, thermal boundary, neighboring source, ancestry loss, safeing capacity, robot corridor, casualty location, breathing air, air release, structural reaction, manual stop and uncertain casualty capacity.

Ten public-safe-state actions stop new source, isolate the candidate path, divert uncontrolled source, bound candidate and casualty water, remove ignition and heat, retain thermal control, preserve remote evidence, keep responders outside, freeze configuration and enter recovery by measured state.

The safe state has two simultaneous purposes: protect people and preserve the causal record. If those purposes conflict, life safety wins and the conflict is recorded.

### 7.3 Recovery as evidence

Recovery uses ten ordered states: new source terminated; public and candidate paths isolated; casualty state resolved; remote map accepted; accountable inventories captured; atmospheric and thermal state accepted; mass and water ledgers closed; evidence packet frozen; cleaning and repair released; and serialized baseline restored.

Elapsed time does not advance the state. Each transition has exit evidence. Human entry remains held when the remote map is incomplete. Cleaning remains held until custody and packet freeze permit it. The next method cannot borrow an assumed baseline from calendar spacing.

### 7.4 Amendment and regression

Fourteen amendment classes preserve prior versions. They cover pre-freeze correction, post-freeze clerical defect, safety-critical discovery, source-lot substitution, tolerance change, sensor replacement, feature-software change, missing-data-rule change, candidate change, site-procedure change, reserve assignment, outcome correction, recovery-process change and public-intervention change.

Sixteen regression triggers map changed objects to affected methods. A new drain cartridge reopens flow, ancestry, mass and recovery evidence. A new clock reopens timing and feature extraction. A candidate software revision reopens every candidate-controlled method. A repair can return to service only after its affected method set is accepted; a generic maintenance sign-off is insufficient.

## 8. Technology program: inventing the missing public capabilities

The mission should not be constrained to instruments available as commercial products today. Military institutions routinely create sensing, communications, autonomy, propulsion and analytical capabilities because the mission requires them. A coequal resilience institution should do the same.

### 8.1 Real-time source inventory tomography

The required advance is an interval-valued reconstruction of source distributed across surfaces, drains, receivers, aerosol and retained solids. It would combine distributed mass references, flow fields, level geometry, chemical signatures, imaging and residual sampling. The first falsification experiment uses known distributed releases with deliberately obscured paths and asks whether the system contains truth within its reported interval.

This capability could later transfer to flood contamination, industrial spills, wildfire retardant custody, post-earthquake utility leaks and hazardous-material response.

### 8.2 Dual-coded fluid ancestry

Root A needs to distinguish source, candidate water, public suppression water and environmental custody at event timescales. A mature system may require families of safe optical, electrochemical or molecular codes; inline readers resistant to smoke, foam and mixed chemistry; and algorithms that retain fractionation uncertainty. Blind mixture trials, cross-contamination and sensor loss are the first tests.

### 8.3 Instrumented terrain skin

Replaceable terrain panels should measure depth, wetting, temperature, local reaction, slope and material state over a large field. The technological challenge is not simply adding sensors. It is producing a calibrated, repairable surface whose geometry and response remain traceable after fire, debris, impact and cleaning. Such skins could become standard public range infrastructure for flood, debris-flow, wildfire and collapsed-utility research.

### 8.4 Radiant-field tomography

Sparse point heat-flux measurements are insufficient when smoke, partial barriers, reflective surfaces and sensor loss create asymmetric fields. Radiant-field tomography would combine unlike sensors and registered physical models to reconstruct flux and dose at public boundaries with uncertainty. Known emitters and controlled fires test whether the field estimate remains conservative under occlusion and failed channels.

### 8.5 Self-describing instruments and traceable time

A self-describing instrument binds serial, calibration, position, orientation, local clock, method role and software identity at acquisition. Traceable distributed time preserves microsecond causal order without a campus network or primary time source. `A08` makes these capabilities operational requirements. False-identity insertion, device swaps, oscillator drift and origin-site loss are mandatory falsification cases.

### 8.6 Public intervention recorder

The recorder must bind threshold observation, authority vote, command issuance, final-element response, transferred inventory and consequence into one independently witnessed object. That enables the same action to be credited as public safety and scored as adverse candidate evidence without ambiguity.

### 8.7 Remote casualty reconnaissance and robotic recovery

Remote reconnaissance must map people, heat, gas, liquids, structure and access routes before human entry. Robotic recovery must sample, map and recover source-bearing solids without breaking custody. Their first experiments combine occluded casualties, heat, debris, sensor denial and intentionally ambiguous material identity.

These are not merely test tools. They are prototypes for a national operational capacity that can enter industrial fires, flood-contaminated districts, damaged hospitals and infrastructure collapse while protecting responders.

### 8.8 Rapid microanalysis, causal compilation and outside-site replay

Rapid source-water microanalysis must classify changing mixtures at drain and receiver timescales. The causal-state compiler must transform native records into versioned features whose inputs, algorithms, exclusions and uncertainty are machine-checkable. Outside-site replay must reconstruct event state and decision from portable originals when the origin campus is unavailable.

Together these capabilities create an evidence institution able to learn during disaster operations without letting urgency erase scientific custody.

## 9. Program, workforce and industrial economy

### 9.1 Thirty-six-month realization program

The schedule begins with method, feature and change-law freeze. Calibration, timing, sampling and ancestry prototypes start in parallel. Command, abort and safeing integration follows. Physical qualification then ascends through geometry and water, surrogate/debris/tracer, quarter source, full-source paired fire, destructive work, recovery and outside-site replay. ## 12. Failure modes the constitution is designed to expose

The method system should reject at least these plausible but invalid shortcuts:

- treating a commanded challenge as a realized challenge;
- calling two certificates on one acquisition path independent;
- inferring source custody from liquid volume without ancestry;
- assigning zero to a missing decisive observation;
- using a public quench as ordinary censoring;
- replacing a valid adverse event with a reserve attempt;
- averaging one surviving site or builder when the other input is missing;
- letting a diagnostic composite offset a safety failure;
- accepting a repaired object without method-specific regression;
- permitting local knowledge to defeat challenge blindness;
- releasing human entry because enough time elapsed; and
- permitting a workbook or candidate to authorize its own physical gate.

These are institutional failure modes, not spreadsheet errors. The workbook exists to make them legible before physical execution.
