# F36 physical module and interface qualification constitution

## Executive judgment

Pass 108 established the force and campaign required to prove complete-campus regeneration without standing down the real alert path. It did not establish what physical objects must exist before the first `CRC-6` event can begin. “Modular” remained descriptive. The interfaces among structure, lifting, water, power, heat, sanitation, evidence and custody had no representative article bank, cold-state test or independent reproduction requirement.

This pass defines `MQP-1`, the F36 Module Qualification Program. Its purpose is not to select the exterior form of a replacement campus. It is to prove that two unlike industrial roots can independently build, transport, join, operate, inspect, separate and reset the physical service cells that make a complete campus.

The qualification unit is not a connector by itself. It is an **interface transaction**: two identified articles enter controlled states; named people and tools create a joint; the joint carries a declared service across a declared environment; independent instruments observe it; a fault or clean control receives terminal disposition; and both articles return to known custody. Hardware, process, tooling, software, access volume, contamination control, evidence and reset are one object for qualification.

`MQP-1` controls 130 serial qualification units:

- sixty root-native interface-pair articles;
- thirty-six full service-cell articles;
- four utility-island assemblies;
- six receiver-world spines;
- four integrated campus slices;
- eighteen reference, destructive-truth and quarantine articles; and
- two emergency-adapter articles.

Those units support ninety-four decision-bearing physical trials. Sixty are interface trials. Eighteen are paired service-cell reproduction trials. Four qualify utility islands, six qualify receiver spines, four qualify integrated campus slices and two challenge cross-root emergency adapters. Twenty-four sealed faults and twenty-four clean controls are allocated before execution. Every physical observation remains zero in this planning package.

The program holds ten interface families common at the level of public effect and evidence: structural load transfer; lifting and transport; water and hydraulic service; electrical protection and black start; thermal and ventilation service; sanitary, sample and waste separation; data, time, identity and signing; metrology and known truth; human access and maintainability; and environmental containment and decontamination. The two industrial roots may use different materials, joints, voltages, protocols, geometries and processes. A common standard may define what must cross the boundary and how failure is observed. It may not silently prescribe the same decisive implementation in both roots.

Three receiver worlds prevent qualification against one ideal pad: a recoverable industrial hardstand with damaged utility approaches; a settled or weak-ground site requiring an independent foundation and utility island; and a remote corridor site with long service runs, limited heavy access and no trusted grid or national network. Each world is a requirements class, not a real location.

Cold readiness is tested as a state transition. One-third of the interface articles enter fresh baseline trials. One-third carry sealed faults or destructive endpoints. One-third undergo at least twelve months of controlled cold storage, loss of external data and utility support, transport exposure, deployment, operation, disassembly and reset. A favorable fresh prototype cannot qualify an arsenal-held system.

The program is scheduled over forty-eight months. It requires USD 3.000B of capital draw cross-classified inside the Pass 108 USD 13.900B standing-capital program. Its annual operating draw is USD 0.720B, or USD 2.880B across four years, cross-classified inside the USD 1.086B annual readiness program. Neither amount is added again to the twenty-five-year boundary. The six operational shipsets remain protected; consuming them as qualification articles would falsify the rotational reserve.

Planning may authorize the `MQP-1` registry, article allocation, test sequence, finance crosswalk and release package. It may not claim a qualified interface, service cell, root, receiver, operational shipset, `CRC-E01` start, production configuration or exterior form.

## 1. Why “modular” is not a requirement

Modularity often means that an object can be moved or that its envelope resembles a transport standard. Neither property establishes public service. A portable laboratory that cannot reproduce time, water quality, evidence custody and waste separation at a new receiver is portable equipment, not a regenerable campus service. A power module with an electrical plug but no protection study, grounding state, black-start sequence or safe isolation process has a connector but not an interface.

The F36 campus is a coupled system. Its closure bays, damage cells, laboratories, forensics, metrology, replay and blind-truth functions depend on common utilities and on decisions that must remain trustworthy after total campus loss. The physical interfaces therefore carry two simultaneous obligations:

1. transmit the required material, energy, information or load; and
2. preserve the causal evidence needed to decide whether that transmission was safe and conforming.

An interface that delivers water but loses sanitary identity fails. An anchor that carries static load but cannot be surveyed after settlement fails. A data link that passes packets but shares the same hidden signing root across both industrial roots fails. A lifting point that works only with one arsenal's unique fixture fails the force even if it passes a factory demonstration.

`MQP-1` replaces the label “modular” with enumerated transactions, articles, faults, receiver environments and terminal records.

## 2. Governing physical invariants

Every qualification trial must preserve eight invariants.

### 2.1 Complete identities

Both sides of the interface, every adapter, tool, reference, software baseline, person and evidence original receive immutable identities before test. Replacement does not overwrite history. An inaccessible item remains in the balance.

### 2.2 Declared boundaries

The trial identifies what crosses the interface, in which direction, under what normal and abnormal states, and which quantities must not cross. Product water and waste, trusted time and ordinary data, structural reaction and transport restraint cannot share an undefined boundary.

### 2.3 Observable safe isolation

The interface can enter a safe isolated state without an external cloud, the lost campus or a unique manufacturer representative. Isolation must be visible through an independent physical or locally complete measurement path.

### 2.4 Root autonomy

Each industrial root brings its own decisive material process, production tooling, executable baseline, inspection method and reproduction rights. Public fixtures and service definitions may be common. A hidden common sub-tier that can defeat both roots receives zero independence credit.

### 2.5 Receiver independence

The article owns all temporary foundation, utility, access, contamination, evidence and reset equipment needed by its assigned receiver world. The receiver supplies only the preregistered civil boundary. Unlisted local help is a trial failure, not ordinary field ingenuity.

### 2.6 Two-original evidence

At least two locally complete evidence originals are created under separate authorities. A summary database, telemetry stream or manufacturer log cannot substitute for native observations and custody.

### 2.7 Reversible deployment or declared consumption

Every item ends in a known reusable, repairable, consumed, quarantined or destroyed state. Reset cost and time are measured. A successful service period followed by an indeterminate return state does not support rotational readiness.

### 2.8 No protected-force consumption

The six `CRS-2` operational shipsets, the alert line group, the alert commissioning team and the alert affiliate pool remain outside qualification custody. `MQP-1` has its own articles and test rosters.

### 3.1 Interface serial states

Each interface-family and root combination receives three independent serial pairs:

- `S0 FRESH`: the declared baseline built from the frozen package;
- `S1 CHALLENGE`: a fault-bearing or destructive-truth pair controlled by the independent challenge authority; and
- `S2 COLD_RESET`: a pair sealed into the cold-storage, transport, deployment and reset sequence.

The three states cannot be created by repeatedly reclassifying one favorable prototype. Their material, work, inspection and evidence histories remain separate.

### 3.2 Service-cell pairs

Each of the nine campus services receives one primary and one independent-reproduction cell from each root. The second cell uses the public technical-data package but a different production lot, named build team and inspection chain. Reproduction is not a second run by the prototype team.

### 3.3 Protected articles

Reference and destructive-truth articles do not become spares after the result is known. Quarantine preserves disputed material and causal evidence. Qualification articles do not transfer automatically into operational shipsets because their fatigue, contamination, modification and disclosure states differ.

### 4.1 Normalized physical envelope

Before detailed dimensions exist, each interface package declares a normalized envelope rather than pretending that one number governs every physics domain:

- `1.00` is the maximum declared continuous service state;
- `1.10` is the registered abnormal transient screen;
- `1.25` is the planning proof state for non-consumable qualification where the service-specific safety authority accepts that relation; and
- destructive articles continue to a preregistered terminal condition rather than earning service credit.

These factors are acquisition placeholders. Structural, electrical, hydraulic, thermal and sanitary authorities must replace them with physical quantities, uncertainty and failure criteria before build release. No common scalar permits unsafe equivalence across domains.

## 5. Three receiver worlds

Receiver qualification cannot assume one prepared campus pad.

### `RW1` Damaged industrial hardstand

The bearing surface is usable but utility approaches, drainage, grid protection and communications are damaged or untrusted. The build may use surveyed hardstand capacity. It must create its own safe utility boundary, time, signing and environmental controls.

### `RW2` Settled weak-ground island

Differential settlement, limited bearing strength and disrupted drainage require an independent load-spreading foundation, adjustable datum and island utilities. The world tests whether structural and utility alignment can remain observable as the receiver changes.

### `RW3` Remote corridor receiver

The site has long source and discharge runs, restricted heavy access, no trusted grid, no national network and limited local maintenance support. The formation must stage, assemble and sustain the campus slice from its own transport and evidence estate.

The six receiver-spine articles assign each root to every receiver world. No root may qualify only on the easiest site. A receiver spine contains the foundation, alignment grid, utility approach, drainage and waste boundary, local time and identity node, lifting interface, access envelope and custody perimeter required to judge service cells.

### 6.1 Trial ladder

The physical ladder is sequential.

1. `Q0 PACKAGE`: requirements, rights, hazards, references, tools, roles and terminal states are frozen.
2. `Q1 PRESTATE`: serial identity, material ancestry, calibration, contamination, software and custody reconcile.
3. `Q2 INTERFACE`: the joint is created, challenged, measured, isolated and disassembled.
4. `Q3 SERVICE CELL`: the full service operates through its interfaces and receives an independent packet.
5. `Q4 RECEIVER AND ISLAND`: multiple services operate on a receiver spine with island utilities.
6. `Q5 CAMPUS SLICE`: at least three coupled campus services, including one evidence service, reach admission together.
7. `Q6 COLD RESET`: aged articles deploy, operate, return to known state and reproduce their acceptance result.

Failure at a lower level blocks the affected higher-level branch. Calendar progress does not waive the predecessor.

## 7. Service-cell obligations

The nine Pass 108 service lines remain indivisible until physical evidence earns another result.

### 7.1 Closure-bay cell

The cell includes preparation, alignment, formation, inspection, rejection, rework, serialization, evidence and safe tool service. Qualification must show that its structural, power, ventilation, data and material interfaces reproduce the admitted closure process outside the original campus.

### 7.2 Damage-cell cell

The cell includes injury stimulus, isolation, repair, flush, sanitary decision, receiver release, teardown and forensic preservation. A pipe or fixture alone is not the cell. The temporary receiver must safely carry abnormal water and waste states.

### 7.3 Utility-island service

The utility island establishes black-start power, water conditioning, hydraulic loops, heat rejection, communications, time, grounding, drainage and waste containment. It is a service line and also a common dependency. Root-specific implementations must prevent it from becoming the shared failure that defeats every other cell.

### 7.4 Sanitary-laboratory cell

The cell owns sampling, blanks, recovery controls, rapid assays, residual measurement, clean and contaminated workflow, waste and result custody. Product and waste paths require physical incompatibility or independently detected cross-connection.

### 7.5 Forensic-line cell

The cell receives failed articles without corrupting identity, fracture state, contamination or configuration evidence. It must support controlled opening, imaging, sectioning and retained material under receiver conditions.

### 7.6 Metrology cell

The cell brings independent references, uncertainty budgets, mounted-system checks and traveling standards. A root cannot qualify using only instruments it produced or calibrated through its own hidden chain.

### 7.7 Evidence-replay cell

The cell holds native records, executable configuration, time mapping and two-original replay. It must reconstruct the decision without national network access or the lost campus.

### 7.8 Blind-truth cell

The cell creates, seals and later reveals fault and clean-control truth. The builder and operator cannot learn the assignment through shared software, logistics or labeling.

### 7.9 Site-custody cell

The cell controls perimeter, item movement, quarantine, waste, key material, visitors, transfer and final restoration. It is the physical institution that keeps every other service's evidence attributable.

## 8. Root independence and the commonality ceiling

A public interface standard is necessary for force integration but can destroy diversity if it fixes the hidden implementation.

`MQP-1` divides commonality into three layers.

### Common public layer

The Department owns service definitions, receiver worlds, hazard envelopes, measurement coordinates, serial grammar, evidence objects, safety authority, access outcomes and reproduction rights.

### Root-native layer

Each root independently selects material systems, joint mechanics, voltage and conversion architecture, control implementation, production process, tooling, inspection sequence, firmware ancestry and repair method. Those differences are declared enough to audit without being forced into one design.

### Adapter layer

Emergency adapters may connect unlike root-native interfaces after an actual loss. They are separately serialized, tested in both directions and stored outside both roots. An adapter cannot count toward routine root independence, cannot carry an undeclared safety function and cannot cause both roots to depend on one supplier or executable baseline.

The two adapter trials ask whether controlled emergency federation is possible. Failure does not necessarily reject either root. It rejects the claimed cross-root recovery option.

## 9. Cold storage, transport and reset

The `S2 COLD_RESET` cohort contains twenty interface pairs, one for each interface-root combination. The minimum planning sequence is:

1. accepted new-state baseline;
2. twelve months of sealed storage with recorded environmental and preservation state;
3. loss of external grid, national network and remote manufacturer support before release;
4. registered transport shock, vibration, handling and custody exposure;
5. no-notice receiver assignment;
6. joining with field tools and named qualification personnel;
7. continuous and abnormal service trials;
8. safe isolation and disassembly;
9. inspection, repair, replenishment and configuration reconciliation;
10. return to controlled storage; and
11. independent replay of both initial acceptance and post-reset state.

The program records calendar time, direct work, queue, hold, rework, consumed stores, rejected material, contaminated material, tool use and unavailable dependencies separately. Reset is a physical result, not a checkbox after a successful trial.

Twenty-four fault tokens and twenty-four clean controls are committed before execution. Each fault family appears once in each root.

| `MF01` | substituted lot or article identity | ancestry and custody rejection |

| `MF03` | denied lift point or altered center of gravity | handling-plan rejection or safe alternate |
| `MF04` | contaminated hydraulic dead leg or wrong valve state | isolation, assay and causal localization |

| `MF06` | fouled heat-rejection path | thermal-limit detection and degraded-state control |
| `MF07` | sanitary product-to-waste cross-connection | physical or independently measured rejection |

Clean controls measure false rejection and operator behavior. A clean control discarded because it is inconvenient remains a failed control. Unlimited diagnostic retries are prohibited. Replacement trials receive new identities linked to the first terminal packet.

As in Pass 108, twenty-four favorable fault detections would yield only an 88.3-percent one-sided 95-percent lower bound under a simple independent-trial approximation. Repeated mechanisms and shared teams make that calculation optimistic. The qualification bank supports interface and process decisions, not fleet reliability.

## 11. Technology programs created by the requirement

The interface constitution is deliberately ambitious. It creates mission-led invention targets rather than restricting the force to current commercial connectors.

### 11.1 Self-surveying load grids

Deployable foundation grids should measure their own geometry, reaction distribution, settlement and anchor state through independently readable local references. Robotic grading and adjustable load paths could convert weak or altered receivers without a bespoke civil project.

### 11.2 Multi-physics trust collars

A service boundary can combine physical keying, valve or breaker state, seal identity, local measurement, time and configuration evidence in one replaceable collar. The collar does not make one universal connector. It makes the interface state observable across root-specific mechanics.

### 11.3 Contamination-incompatible fluid architecture

Clean water, process water, test water, residuals and waste should use geometries and control sequences that resist unsafe cross-connection even when labels, software or lighting fail. Autonomous flushing and dead-leg mapping could turn sanitary reset into measurable work.

### 11.4 Portable sovereign time and metrology

Commissioning teams need transportable time, geometry, electrical, hydraulic, thermal and chemical references that remain useful without satellite timing, national networks or a manufacturer's certificate service.

### 11.5 Robot-readable access and disassembly

Interfaces should expose safe energy state, lift geometry, tool approach, fastener condition and contaminated surfaces to people and machines. Autonomous inspection and disassembly are valuable only if the independent evidence chain can judge their work.

### 11.6 Reversible representative faults

The program needs sealed physical and digital faults that reproduce causal mechanisms without uncontrolled release or irreversible damage to unrelated assets. Fault design becomes its own public technical discipline.
### 11.7 Cellular common services

Utility, metrology, replay, forensics, blind truth and custody create the singleton burden behind six complete shipsets. A future reduction is possible if two or more cells demonstrate safe quorum service, independent failure, portable references and mixed-cell acceptance. `MQP-1` records the evidence needed for that decision but grants no fractional credit.

## 12. Program organization

`MQP-1` uses authorities distinct from the Pass 108 exercise and alert rotations.

- the F36 requirement authority owns public service and receiver worlds;
- Root A and Root B build authorities own their separate articles and processes;
- a public fixture authority owns receiver spines and common stimuli;
- an independent metrology authority owns references and uncertainty;
- a blind challenge authority owns faults and clean controls;
- a safety and environmental authority may stop exposure without awarding success;
- a custody authority controls serial state, quarantine and transfers;
- an independent commissioning authority issues trial packets;
- a reproduction authority repeats service-cell builds; and
- a campaign admission board decides whether `CRC-E01` may start.

No authority may both create the condition and issue the terminal decision about that condition. Contractors provide named people and facilities but do not own public evidence or the admission grammar.

- required service crosses the boundary inside its physical and uncertainty limits;

- safe isolation, inspection and disassembly work without undeclared help;
- injected fault or clean control receives correct terminal disposition;
- two native evidence originals replay independently; and

### 15.4 `CRC-E01` start

- all sixty interface trials close;
- all eighteen service-cell reproduction pairs close;
- four utility islands, six receiver spines and four integrated slices close;
- both adapter trials receive terminal decisions;
- all twenty-four faults and twenty-four clean controls close;
- the cold cohort returns to accepted known state;
- no root-independence or no-borrow exception remains open; and
- the campaign board can reproduce every governing result without a builder's private record.

Summary counters cannot satisfy these gates by themselves. Article acceptance must reconcile to all 130 serial rows; interface, service, receiver, island, slice, adapter, fault, control and cold-reset totals must reconcile to their trial-level physical observations and terminal states. A complete-looking executive report with open source records remains a failed admission package.

## 16. Failure modes the program must expose

`MQP-1` must reject the program if:

- one transport envelope is mistaken for complete interface compatibility;
- both roots depend on the same decisive material, machine, firmware, key service or inspection method;
- a field joint passes only with factory personnel or another arsenal's unique fixture;
- sanitary safety depends on labels or software that can be wrong together;
- a receiver supplies unregistered foundation, grid, water, drainage, network, labor or evidence capacity;
- a fresh prototype passes while the cold-reset article cannot deploy or return to state;
- an interface is called safe because telemetry disappears before the abnormal state;
- a service cell passes using references produced by the same root under test;
- a clean control is rejected to improve fault-detection appearance;
- qualification consumes an operational shipset or alert roster;
- a disputed article is repaired before cause evidence is secured;
- adapter success is relabeled as root independence; or
- calendar month 48 is treated as evidence of physical completion.

## 17. Stocktake after Pass 109

### What becomes authoritative

- an interface is a complete service-and-evidence transaction, not a connector;
- `MQP-1` requires 130 separately controlled qualification units and 94 physical trials;
- ten interface families and nine service cells define the pre-`CRC-E01` physical boundary;
- every interface family must appear in both roots, in fresh, challenged and cold-reset states;
- three receiver worlds replace qualification against one ideal pad;
- twenty-four sealed faults require twenty-four clean controls;
- the six operational shipsets remain outside qualification custody; and
- USD 3.000B capital and USD 0.720B/year are cross-classified inside Pass 108.

### What is falsified or narrowed

- transportability alone does not establish modularity;
- a universal connector can reduce rather than increase industrial resilience;
- successful fresh prototypes do not establish arsenal readiness;
- an adapter cannot count as an independent industrial root;
- reset cannot be inferred from successful service; and
- the protected operational inventory cannot double as a test bank.

### What remains provisional

- every physical dimension, load, flow, voltage, temperature and chemical limit;
- the sufficiency of three receiver worlds;
- the twelve-month cold-storage minimum;
- the 130-unit and 94-trial counts;
- the month-48 schedule;
- the USD 3.000B and USD 0.720B draws;
- the independence of proposed root implementations; and
- every physical, reliability, production and exterior-form claim.
