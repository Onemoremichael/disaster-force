# Department of Resilience national first physical launch compact and return

**Pass:** 210  
**Research object:** `PFP-5` — National First Physical Launch Compact  
**Decision level:** whole-program launch authority  
**Verdict:** `RETURN`

## Branch position and resolution test

The deepest active branch is `PFP`, with four passes consumed; this is its fifth and terminal pass.

**Resolution test:** reversing this compact would change whether the USD 200B first tranche is executable, whether paper authority can masquerade as capability, and which physical construction may begin; it therefore changes the top-level program.

## Decision

Adopt a **warranted launch lattice** and return the frontier to first `A2` construction. The Department may constitute accounts, publish denominators, open competitions and issue bounded development warrants. It may not infer a firm, article, fact, payment, capability or readiness state from those acts.

A consolidated launch office fails because requirement, money and schedule become a shadow capability command. Six system program offices preserve service ownership but fragment the compound mission. Twenty-four prime-led demonstrations let candidates define proof. A proving-estate-first launch builds expensive apparatus before the first endpoint portfolio is chosen. The selected lattice alone keeps the five fiscal accounts, twenty-four roots, six system vectors, public methods, independent proof and receiver acceptance separate while allowing them to start together.

## The compact

1. **One tranche, five accounts.** The USD 200B ceiling remains USD 110B mission systems, USD 30.0B service technical bases, USD 30.0B common capacity, USD 20.0B sovereign technical estate and USD 10.0B protected failure reserve. No nested view adds money.
2. **One national portfolio, one first cohort.** Ninety-six root–field opportunities remain authoritative. Twenty-four form the compound first-reach cohort; seventy-two remain unissued, not rejected.
3. **One root, four fields.** Each Root House retains complete-root liability across four field assignments. Its first-cohort `A1` is one mechanism experiment, never complete-root credit.
4. **Public questions, plural physical truths.** Six `A0.1` packages, twenty-four receiver states and seventy-two method cards may issue. Their 144 unlike realizations remain unbuilt and unaccepted.
5. **Shared language, separate execution.** Twelve public seams govern semantics and portability. Thirty directed mission interfaces govern operational exchange. Neither may supply candidate planning, estimation, control, safeing, energy management or build logic.
6. **Typed succession.** Fiscal option, frozen method, development issue, award, controlled article, endpoint rig, valid event, disposition, payment and capability are different states. Every transition needs its own named warrant and may roll back without erasing valid adverse evidence.
7. **Failure is purchased knowledge.** Accepted `FRAME`, `MAKE`, `BREAK` and `TRANSFER` artifacts may be paid even when they kill a lineage. The failure reserve funds exact-event invalidity recovery and reconstitution, not overruns or winners.
8. **Construction returns upward.** This compact authorizes competition architecture. It does not select `A2` endpoints, estate formations, sites or serial articles. Those first physical choices now become the parent-level question.
