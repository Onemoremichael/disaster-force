# Civil mobility and continuity

This subsystem owns the movement of people into complete service when remaining in place is unsafe or impossible.

It is deliberately separate from logistics. Moving a service package toward a damaged region and moving a person away from one are not inverse versions of the same problem. Population movement carries consent, accessibility, clinical regulation, household and caregiver unity, custody, destination admission, continuity and return obligations that cargo movement does not.

## The complete chain

```text
HOUSEHOLD CONTACT
        ↓
ACCESSIBLE COLLECTION
        ↓
PROTECTED TRANSFER
        ↓
MULTIMODAL LINE HAUL
        ↓
DESTINATION ADMISSION
        ↓
CONTINUITY OF CARE, RECORDS AND FAMILY
        ↓
FUNDED RETURN OR LAWFUL TRANSITION
```

[The National Civil Mobility and Continuity Force](national-civil-mobility-and-continuity-force.md) establishes the first national reference: observed network gates, five mutually exclusive planning cohorts, purpose-built fleets and transfer nodes, route-restoration groups, commands, bases, workforce and fiscal boundary.

[Civil Mobility Assurance Under Deep Uncertainty](mobility-assurance-under-deep-uncertainty.md) attacks that reference across broad parameter worlds and tract-scale collection. It reverses the earlier architectural emphasis: the restorable movement mesh remains necessary but is not robust as the universal answer; protected-local continuity becomes the governing doctrine.

The [Harris County campaign](../../proving/campaigns/harris-county/README.md) then shows that local protection is also incomplete without an assured household–building–street interface. That failure produces the [Neighborhood Continuity and Access Cell](neighborhood-continuity-and-access-cell.md), a provisional formation joining household contact, powered and unpowered building access, neighborhood service, route assurance and selective collection. The conventional reference is brigade-scale; the technology rival depends on unproved disconnected records, building-access productivity, prepared-district interfaces and distributed communications.

The [New York campaign](../../proving/campaigns/new-york/README.md) is the different-city falsification of those rivals. Its live status and stop conditions are maintained in the [active frontier](../../frontier.md), not repeated here. Neither architecture is admitted, and no rendering is authorized.
