# Civil mobility and continuity

This subsystem owns the movement of people into complete service when remaining in place is unsafe or impossible.

It is deliberately separate from logistics. Moving a service pod toward a damaged region and moving a person away from one are not inverse versions of the same problem. Population movement carries consent, accessibility, clinical regulation, family unity, custody, destination admission and return obligations that cargo movement does not.

[The National Civil Mobility and Continuity Force](national-civil-mobility-and-continuity-force.md) establishes the service and first reference force. It replaces Pass 32's abstract corridor multiplier with observed federal network gates, five mutually exclusive planning cohorts, purpose-built fleet and transfer-node classes, a workforce and fiscal account, and 500 joint route-receiver trials for each of four rival architectures.

The current reference is [Civil Mobility Assurance Under Deep Uncertainty](mobility-assurance-under-deep-uncertainty.md). It attacks the apparent Pass 33 winner across 1,000 broad parameter worlds, adds 2,430 tract records in four collection pilots, and configures one `CTN-10` transfer formation and one `UCM-8` continuity module.

The result reverses the architectural emphasis. The restorable mesh falls from 98.8-percent success in the fixed Pass 33 distribution to 7.6 percent of the declared broad worlds. Protected-local continuity passes 99.8 percent, becoming the national doctrine; the accessible mobility and restoration force remains a necessary rescue, selective-relocation and return subsystem.

[The Harris County continuity and selective-mobility campaign](../../proving/campaigns/harris-county-continuity-and-selective-mobility-campaign.md) tests that doctrine on one corrected metropolitan road graph. Protected local service alone is not enough. The corrective `H3` rival adds faster building access, collection and route assurance, wins all 300 paired worlds, yet passes the complete admission rule in only 55.3 percent. It advances only as a test architecture.

Collection is now a fifth force layer alongside local protection, transfer, line haul and reception. `CTN-10` also changes category: the first complete-system screen yields 29,263 m² of campus, 4,513 tonnes and 2,289 deployed staff, rather than the prior 220-person crew hypothesis. Across 128 nodes, that correction adds 264,832 direct positions before training and reserve multipliers.

The [Neighborhood Continuity and Access Cell](neighborhood-continuity-and-access-cell.md) now configures that fifth layer. `NCAC-35` binds a 35,000-person demand class to 34 protected districts, 300 household teams, 100 vertical-access teams, five collection squadrons, seven route-assurance detachments, utility islands, civil authority, rights, communications, and sustainment. The `A2` conventional reference contains 7,587 deployed people and 6,388 packed tonnes. It survives all declared physical component losses but verifies only 79.6 percent of households by hour 36 when commercial communications fail.

The `S3` technology target closes the isolated arithmetic by changing disconnected-record retention, household and vertical-access productivity, prepared-district mass, and distributed communications. Those are research requirements, not operational capabilities.

The next work is now specified rather than merely requested. The [New York vertical heat-blackout protocol](../../proving/campaigns/new-york-vertical-heat-blackout-replication-protocol.md) freezes `A2` and `S3` before geographic replication. Its first [`L0` catchment rehearsal](../../proving/campaigns/new-york-l0-catchment-rehearsal.md) produces 844 candidates, twelve provisional references and three commitments, then blocks formal freeze pending street topology, independent reproduction and salted custody. The [full-mission test program](../../proving/test-enterprise/ncac-full-mission-test-program.md) defines the 888 element trials, 60 utility sequences, 48 occupied-district trials, twelve developmental missions, thirty confirmatory missions, independent authorities and $115.83 billion design-cost envelope needed to prove or reject the formation.

All twenty `NCAC` gates remain open. Tract catchments are not jurisdictions; deployment screens are not movement plans; costs are not estimates; simulations are not field reliability; and renderings remain prohibited.
