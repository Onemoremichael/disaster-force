# Neighborhood Continuity and Access Cell

**Status:** first formation configuration; `R2+` exploratory arithmetic

**Decision:** advance `A2`, the assured conventional cell, as the development reference and `S3`, the distributed technology-target cell, as the rival invention program. Admit neither formation to the force.

**Design class:** `NCAC-35`, a seven-day cell for approximately 35,000 people inside a bounded demand envelope

**Companion model:** [Neighborhood Continuity and Access Cell model](../../../models/neighborhood-continuity-access-cell-model.xlsx)

## Executive judgment

The missing neighborhood formation is not a liaison element, a bus fleet, a rescue task force, a shelter detachment, or a public-works package. At the declared workload it is a **brigade-scale joint civil formation** that owns the seam from household contact to protected local service or complete movement.

The first conventional configuration requires approximately **7,587 deployed people**, **6,388 tonnes of packed materiel**, **18,391 cubic metres of packed volume**, **275 forty-foot-equivalent positions**, **7.2 MW of average electrical service**, **650 m³/day of water**, **$6.866 billion in capital**, and **$1.586 billion in mature annual operations**. These values are design-study consequences, not estimates or recommendations.

`A2` closes the base case and eight physical loss cases. It can lose one contact company, one vertical-access company, one collection squadron, one route-assurance detachment, five protected districts, two power blocks, one water island, or one communications node and retain arithmetic closure. It still fails when commercial communications and cloud services are unavailable. Only **79.6 percent** of the household-contact requirement is verified by hour 36.

The failure is not caused by insufficient long-distance transport. It is caused by a coupled loss of prior records, household reachability, field productivity, and authoritative reconciliation. The force therefore needs a capability that is partly physical and partly institutional: a **disconnected household-to-service operating system** whose records, rights, field teams, building access, utilities, route state, destination acceptance, and return obligations survive together.

`S3` closes all ten required arithmetic cases by changing four parameters: it retains more lawful registry value while isolated, increases household-contact productivity from 1.6 to 2.4 households per team-hour, increases assisted vertical movement from 0.50 to 0.80 people per team-hour, and reduces prepared-district imported mass from 15.742 to 12 tonnes. Those values define a technology program. They do not receive operational credit.

This pass also overturns the apparent adequacy of the previous campaign workforce and operating account. Fourteen `A2`-equivalent cells would contain approximately **106,212 deployed people** and cost **$22.209 billion per year** before regional transfer nodes, line haul, destination operations, higher command, or regeneration. That exceeds the entire Pass 36 `H3` campaign hypothesis of 56,554 deployed people and $19.405 billion per year. Multiplication is not a force proposal; it is a contradiction test. The campaign account must be reopened from tasks upward.

## Why the cell exists

The [Harris County continuity and selective-mobility campaign](../../campaigns/harris-county-continuity-and-selective-mobility-campaign.md) established three facts.

First, strong local protection prevents avoidable movement demand. The movement-led architecture manufactured hundreds of thousands of additional journeys because it could not provide safe local service.

Second, protected-local service is not self-executing. A resident may be separated from a functioning district by a failed elevator, a flooded block, a mobility-device constraint, an inaccessible vehicle, an unverified building, a missing caregiver, a closed culvert, or an unavailable receiving service.

Third, delay accumulates before regional transport. `H3` had effectively no line-haul queue in the reference campaign while its collection queue still peaked near 73,750 people. More distant seats could not repair missing block-to-node service.

The `NCAC` is created to own that gap. Its public output is not deployments, contacts, route-kilometres, vehicle trips, megawatts, or registrations. Its output is:

> a person receiving complete, lawful, accessible service by a specified time—locally when safe, or at an admitted destination when movement is necessary.

## The design object

### A demand class, not a jurisdiction

`NCAC-35` is named for a design demand of approximately 35,000 people, not for resident population, municipal boundary, command rank, or vehicle count.

This pass uses `N01`, one analytical catchment inherited from Pass 36. It contains 61 census tracts and 339,390 residents. The Pass 36 `H3` architecture assigned 30,397.2 demand people to that catchment: 24,931.4 receiving local protection and 5,465.8 requiring movement. A 1.15 design factor creates the following bounded contract:

| Requirement | Design value | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Total demand | 34,956.8 people | service obligation, not resident population |
| Protected-local service | 28,671.1 people | complete occupied service, not nominal shelter space |
| Complete movement | 6,285.7 people | collection through destination admission |
| Household records | 14,268.1 households | using 2.45 people per demand household |
| Assisted vertical movement | 1,503.1 people | 4.3% design share; unvalidated proxy |
| Critical local route release | 60 route-km | vehicle-specific lawful operability |
| Average / peak power | 7.2 / 11.0 MW | load registry and diversity remain open |
| Water / wastewater | 650 / 520 m³/day | source, use class, and discharge remain open |
| Communications coverage | 100 abstract units | not a geographic radio-frequency design |

`N01` is not a proposed operating area. Its node is not a site. The tract assignment is useful only because it creates a bounded workload against which rival formations can fail.

### The complete service rule

The cell must close eight serial capability accounts:

1. protected-local service;
2. household verification;
3. assisted vertical access;
4. accessible collection;
5. local route release;
6. average power;
7. daily water; and
8. disconnected communications coverage.

The architecture passes a case only when every ratio is at least one. An average cannot erase a missing capability. Excess route capacity cannot substitute for an unreachable resident; a verified household cannot substitute for a safe building; eventual admission cannot substitute for a missed contact deadline.

Formally, for architecture \(a\) and loss case \(f\):

\[
M_{a,f}=\min_{k\in K}\left(\frac{C_{a,f,k}}{D_k}\right)
\]

where \(K\) is the set of eight capabilities, \(C\) is surviving credited capacity, and \(D\) is the declared requirement. A case passes only if \(M_{a,f}\ge 1\).

This is a bottleneck test, not a reliability estimate. Loss cases have no assigned probability and several capacities remain assumption-driven.

## Four rival formations

The study compares rival architectures rather than optimizing a single preferred organization.

| ID | Formation | Governing wager | Base | Required cases | Isolated contact | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `T0` | Thin interface detachment | existing specialist systems can be coordinated by a small seam layer | fail | 0 / 10 | 40.8% | reject |
| `I1` | Integrated baseline cell | organic neighborhood functions can close normal operation | pass | 1 / 10 | 66.7% | retain as base-only comparator |
| `A2` | Assured conventional cell | conventional teams and equipment can provide physical loss tolerance | pass | 9 / 10 | 79.6% | development reference |
| `S3` | Distributed technology-target cell | better disconnected information, access productivity, and prepared interfaces can close isolation with fewer people | pass | 10 / 10 | 102.2% | unproved RDT&E target |

### `T0`: the coordination fallacy

`T0` resembles a politically easy answer: create a small organization that connects existing response assets. It carries 20 districts, 120 contact teams, 40 vertical teams, three collection squadrons, four route detachments, and thin utility and communications capacity.

It fails every base capability. Its apparent economy—about 3,721 deployed people and $4.173 billion in capital—is purchased by omitting the mass and labor needed to produce the promised effect. It is not a lean force. It is an incomplete one.

### `I1`: a force that works only when nothing important is lost

`I1` has enough capacity to close the no-loss case. It fails every required degraded case. This is the difference between capacity and readiness. A formation that operates only with all companies, districts, utility blocks, communications nodes, and registries intact is not an assured national instrument.

### `A2`: physical assurance without informational assurance

`A2` contains 34 protected districts, 300 household-contact teams in five companies, 100 vertical-access teams in five companies, five collection squadrons, seven route-assurance detachments, ten power blocks, nine water islands, seven communications nodes, and a 720-person command, civil-authority, rights, data, communications, and clinical allowance.

It is the first conventional architecture whose physical margins are deliberately configured around the loss cases. Its only required failure is `F9`, commercial communications and cloud unavailability.

### `S3`: technology target, not preferred future

`S3` does not merely add autonomy or substitute software for people. It redistributes capability: 35 lighter prepared districts, 270 higher-productivity household teams in six companies, 65 higher-productivity vertical teams, six collection squadrons, seven route detachments, ten power blocks, nine water islands, eight communications nodes, and 600 fixed staff.

It carries a 25-percent technology cost factor and costs more in capital than `A2` despite using approximately 555 fewer deployed people. Its value is epistemic: it identifies which parameter changes would make disconnected neighborhood service plausible. It is not evidence that those changes are attainable.

## Failure architecture

Ten cases are required for the first screen. The base case is included because an architecture that cannot meet nominal demand should not be rescued by redundancy rhetoric.

| Case | Removed or changed | Failure being tested |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `F0` | none | base mission capacity |
| `F1` | one contact company | distributed household reach |
| `F2` | one vertical-access company | high-burden building access |
| `F3` | one collection squadron | pickup and accessible vehicle loss |
| `F4` | one route detachment | local access work and release |
| `F5` | five protected districts | clustered local-service loss |
| `F6` | two power blocks | N-2 electrical service |
| `F7` | one water island | N-1 water service |
| `F8` | one communications node | overlapping field coverage |
| `F9` | commercial communications and cloud | disconnected registry and field work |

`F10` combines the loss of one contact company, one vertical company, one collection squadron, one route detachment, and commercial communications. It is diagnostic rather than required. Even `S3` fails it because contact reaches only 87.7 percent of the hour-36 requirement. This prevents the technology target from being mistaken for an unbounded assurance claim.

The loss cases are deliberately legible. They should later be replaced by empirically fitted joint failures involving fuel, software, authority, workforce, inspection, repair, suppliers, and multiple infrastructure layers. Until then, “N-1” describes only the component explicitly removed.

## Time is a separate obligation

A final completion percentage can conceal dangerous delay. The model therefore advances the same workload in six-hour steps to hour 168 and tracks protected local service, verified households, vertical completion, route release, movement eligibility, collection, destination admission, and the resulting protected share.

| Case | 24 h | 36 h | 48 h | 72 h | Contact at 36 h | Time to 95% | Mean unprotected h/person |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `A2` base | 73.8% | 93.9% | 99.5% | 100% | 100% | 42 h | 17.39 |
| `A2` isolated | 71.9% | 93.9% | 99.5% | 100% | 79.6% | 42 h | 17.65 |
| `S3` isolated | 90.9% | 96.3% | 99.6% | 100% | 100% | 36 h | 12.79 |
| `S3` compound | 88.6% | 93.9% | 99.0% | 100% | 87.7% | 42 h | 13.54 |

The `A2` isolated case illustrates why contact is an independent requirement. Aggregate service eventually closes because assumed production and movement continue. Yet at hour 36 approximately 2,912 household records remain unverified. The model cannot legitimately infer that those households are safe merely because later aggregate flows reach 100 percent.

This creates two simultaneous clocks:

- an **effect clock**, measuring when complete service is delivered; and
- a **knowledge-and-authority clock**, measuring when the force can establish who has been reached, what they require, what authority governs action, and whether the record can survive reconciliation.

A mature readiness standard must close both.

## Formation design

### Command principle

The cell is a joint formation generated by the Community Stabilization, Civil Mobility and Continuity, Land Engineering, Infrastructure Restoration, and Health Protection Services. No single occupational community owns enough of the mission to define it alone.

In employment, the `NCAC` answers to a geographic joint effect command. The affected civil authority sets service and movement priorities. Engineering, clinical, environmental, building, transportation, and safety professionals retain technical release authority. Independent civil-rights and operational-test authorities remain outside the production chain.

The command relationship is intentionally plural. Operational unity does not require concentrating every kind of authority in one commander.

### Eight operating groups

| Group | Organic mission | Non-delegable output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Civil command, rights, and continuity group | civil priorities, consent, exceptions, household binding, custody, privacy, benefits, destination, return | lawful person-service record |
| District continuity group | activate and support prepared `PCC-D1` districts | complete local protected service |
| Household access group | building/block reconnaissance, contact, welfare, needs and service state | verified household disposition |
| Vertical continuity group | stairs, refuge, assisted movement, medical power, temporary lifting and rescue interface | safe vertical access or supported remain decision |
| Collection group | dispatch, accessible loading, short-haul movement, cleaning, energy, clinical separation | person delivered to the correct next service |
| Route-assurance group | reconnaissance, pumps, debris, surfaces, crossings, traffic control, inspection and marking | lawful vehicle-specific route release |
| Utility and communications group | islanded power, storage, water, residuals, field networks, edge compute and backhaul | continuously available cell services |
| Sustainment and regeneration group | field base, food, fuel, maintenance, spares, decontamination, waste, relief, casualty replacement and recovery | seven-day operational continuity |

These are mission groupings, not settled battalions. Trials may show that some functions should be combined, embedded in districts, pooled above the cell, or duplicated. The burden of proof lies with any proposed dependency: moving an element outside the cell cannot reduce mass merely by moving it off the table.

### The workforce consequence

The `A2` workforce screen is:

| Function | Deployed people | Share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Protected-district operations | 1,836 | 24.2% |
| Household contact and building screening | 1,656 | 21.8% |
| Vertical and assisted extraction | 1,104 | 14.6% |
| Accessible collection | 676 | 8.9% |
| Local route assurance | 605 | 8.0% |
| Civil authority, rights, clinical regulation, data, and communications | 720 | 9.5% |
| Cell sustainment and regeneration | 990 | 13.0% |
| **Total** | **7,587** | **100%** |

The result is not a forecast of federal employees. It demonstrates that a 70-person rescue-team analogue cannot own this workload. FEMA's typed urban search-and-rescue task force is a valuable analogue for multidisciplinary, round-the-clock, self-sufficient organization, but its collapse-rescue mission and 70-person Type I staffing are not an `NCAC` rate basis ([FEMA resource typing](https://rtlt.preptoolkit.fema.gov/Public/Resource/View/8-508-1262?p=19)).

The eventual personnel system must distinguish:

- deployed positions from home-station generation;
- on-shift crews from relief, command, maintenance, training, and medical loss;
- public authority from contracted technical labor;
- permanent professionals from regional reserves and locally affiliated workers; and
- occupation count from qualification, currency, and team composition.

Until those distinctions are configured, 7,587 is best read as a warning against staff-free hardware concepts.

Functional mass totals approximately 5,324 tonnes. A 20-percent packing, handling, restraint, dunnage, protection, and configuration factor increases the movement burden to 6,388 tonnes and 18,391 m³.

The route-assurance system is the largest functional equipment burden. This is conceptually important. “Neighborhood mobility” is not mainly passenger vehicles; it is the work required to make a damaged local network legally and physically usable by specific vehicles.

The U.S. Access Board's accessible-egress guidance identifies an accessible path as a continuous system through the building to a public way and recognizes refuge and standby-power elevator conditions. It does not establish post-disaster operability, but it clarifies that accessibility cannot begin at the curb ([U.S. Access Board](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/guides/chapter-4-accessible-means-of-egress/)). NIST's work on mobility impairments and elevator use similarly supports a building-state, communication, and procedure research agenda rather than a universal movement rate ([NIST Technical Note 1923](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.TN.1923)).

### Deployment screen

The packed `A2` configuration corresponds to approximately:

- 1,409 notional air positions under the model's mass-or-volume ceiling;
- 275 forty-foot-equivalent positions;
- 256 twenty-five-tonne road-equivalent moves;
- 49.6 hours in the coarse air screen after a 24-hour decision and marshal allowance; or
- 59.5 hours in the coarse offshore screen on the same basis.

These timings are not executable movement plans. They omit item dimensions, centers of gravity, hazardous segregation, cold-chain needs, aircraft and vessel compatibility, ramps and cranes, origin availability, queues, crew duty, receiving geometry, onward movement, and retrograde. Their purpose is to prevent “rapidly deployable” from remaining free of a transport burden.

They also imply echeloning. A cell cannot wait for all 6,388 tonnes before producing effect. Future configuration must divide alert, advance, service-entry, expansion, sustainment, and relief packages while preserving complete minimum effects at every stage.

## Power, water, communications, and residuals

The cell's utility system is a network, not a generator-and-purifier shopping list.

`A2` carries ten 1 MW-class power blocks. After loss of two, eight blocks at 0.90 MW credited output provide the 7.2 MW average requirement. A separate 11 MW peak and 28.8 MWh usable battery requirement expose starting, thermal, charging, medical, communications, and process loads. No source technology is selected. Fuel, emissions, black start, switching, protection, grounding, fault duty, transients, feeder geometry, cable, maintenance, and operator workload remain open.

Nine nominal 100 m³/day water islands provide 720 m³/day after one loss at 90-percent credited output against the 650 m³/day requirement. That balance does not close source water, treatment train, quality, pressure, storage, distribution, wastewater, stormwater, decontamination, or residual disposition.

The Department of Energy defines a microgrid around a controllable electrical boundary capable of grid-connected or islanded operation. That is an architectural starting point, not evidence for this 7.2 MW formation ([DOE Microgrid Systems](https://www.energy.gov/oe/microgrid-systems)). The relevant proving question is whether the entire occupied cell can black start, sectionalize, protect, shed load, survive source and controller faults, and operate for seven days under representative heat, humidity, water, contamination, and labor conditions.

Communications must be treated the same way. Deployable broadband and edge computing are technically plausible research directions, as shown by NIST's deployable public-safety network and DistressNet-NG programs, but neither validates `NCAC` coverage, public usability, identity handling, cyber security, spectrum access, or offline civil-record reconciliation ([NIST deployable systems](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/public-safety-700-mhz-broadband-deployable-systems); [NIST DistressNet-NG](https://www.nist.gov/ctl/pscr/distressnet-ng-resilient-mobile-broadband-communication-and-edge-computing)).

## Civil rights are part of the configuration

A high-capacity movement force can become coercive, exclusionary, or surveillant if rights are treated as policy added after optimization. `NCAC` therefore carries ten controls as service requirements:

1. affected civil authority sets outcome priorities;
2. every non-immediate journey records consent or specific lawful authority and review;
3. caregivers, dependents, service animals, and essential devices remain bound to the household;
4. unmet accessibility requests enter a named, timed exception-and-remedy queue;
5. clinical need and destination acceptance travel with the person;
6. only operationally necessary data persist, with access and expiry logs;
7. disconnected records preserve provenance and lawful exceptions during reconciliation;
8. a named receiver confirms accessible services, staff, utilities, and authority;
9. custody, benefits, housing, medication, and essential-property obligations persist; and
10. origin release, funded return, or durable alternative settlement remains part of the mission.

These controls cannot be traded against throughput. If a vehicle completes more trips by separating households, or a database reconciles faster by deleting a refusal or local exception, the system has failed even when its arithmetic improves.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `T0` | $4.173B | $0.903B | $26.740B | 3,721 |
| `I1` | $5.900B | $1.331B | $39.172B | 6,133 |
| `A2` | $6.866B | $1.586B | $46.525B | 7,587 |
| `S3` | $8.832B | $1.592B | $48.633B | 7,031 |

The model separates direct capital, base/depot/training capital, program reserve, loaded personnel, equipment operations and maintenance, base operations, exercises, and stock rotation. Unit costs are declared comparative assumptions. No vendor pricing, independent cost estimate, schedule risk, learning curve, retirement, recapitalization, or industrial mobilization model supports them.

The apparent small annual operating difference between `A2` and `S3` is not a finding that advanced technology is cheap. The 25-percent capital factor and lower assumed workforce approximately offset in the screen. Research, integration, software assurance, obsolescence, training, data stewardship, licensing, and specialized depot costs could reverse the result.

The correct current decision is not to choose the cheaper architecture. It is to fund rival evidence that reveals whether labor, capital, access quality, safety, and common-mode risk can actually be traded.

## Mission-driven technology portfolio

The failure analysis creates seven programs. Each changes a mission parameter and carries an explicit kill condition.

| ID | Program | Required change | Representative proof | Kill condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `NC-01` | Disconnected household continuity ledger | retain at least 50% lawful registry verification without cloud/backhaul | 72-hour disconnected reconciliation across five field companies and one rights authority | lost consent or household links, unrecoverable conflict, or undeclared surveillance field |
| `NC-02` | Building access mission system | 1.6 → 2.4 households/team-hour without weaker evidence | mixed low-, mid- and high-rise district under heat, darkness, water, blocked curb and device loads | missed people, unsafe entry, or injury shifted to residents/workers |
| `NC-03` | Powered vertical continuity system | 0.50 → 0.80 completed people/team-hour | occupied stair, refuge, temporary-hoist, device and medical-power trials | falls, crush, egress obstruction, separation, or dependence on one building system |
| `NC-04` | Common-cabin collection family | increase mixed-fleet flow while preserving household and device continuity | complete dispatch-load-travel-unload-clean-energy-maintenance cycle | incompatible restraint/egress, excessive dwell, or proprietary interface |
| `NC-05` | Dynamic civil route-assurance work cell | 11.52 → at least 15.5 released route-km per detachment through hour 48 | flood, debris, culvert, pavement, signal, crossing, control and independent-release trial | counting cleared geometry without lawful vehicle-specific operability |
| `NC-06` | Neighborhood utility island | N-2 power and N-1 water with black start and sectional isolation | seven-day hot-humid occupied operation under source, feeder, controller and treatment faults | common software, fuel, protection, treatment, or operator mode defeats the cell |
| `NC-07` | Prepared district adapter | 15.742 → 12 t imported per district with release inside 24 h | five building/site archetypes under damaged utilities, accessibility, clean-air and sanitation states | mass reduction externalizes unsafe structure, labor, waste, or host dependence |

This is how a Department of Resilience should drive innovation. A program is not justified because autonomy, nuclear energy, artificial intelligence, robotics, or advanced materials are fashionable. It begins because a complete public-service thread fails. The technology must change the failed parameter without moving the burden outside the measurement boundary.

The most important first program is `NC-01` joined to `NC-02`. A disconnected ledger without field access becomes stale administrative data. Faster teams without lawful, reconcilable records create unaccountable motion. They must be tested as one human-technical system.

## Evidence and admission

Twenty gates remain open:

| Gate family | Required evidence before force admission |
| --- | --- |
| Geography and people | named catchment and alternatives; parcel/building inventory; observed demand, refusals, languages, digital reach, and shadow movement |
| Buildings and access | hazard-specific safety release; occupied vertical trials; accessible collection duty cycles |
| Routes and districts | vehicle-specific route operability; complete protected-district and adapter trials |
| Utilities and communications | electrical one-line and N-2 trial; water/residual N-1 trial; disconnected coverage, edge, identity, cyber, power, and spectrum trial |
| Civil outcomes | consent, review, accessibility, family integrity, custody, privacy, remedy, clinical continuity, destination admission, and return |
| Formation reality | occupational roster; weighed and dimensioned bill of material; seven-day sustainment, maintenance and regeneration |
| Assurance and economy | joint common-mode distribution; independent cost and industry evidence; 168-hour full-mission demonstration; preregistered metropolitan replication |

Relevant official guidance supports the shape of these gates without closing them. NIST's Community Resilience Planning Guide links social functions to buildings, infrastructure systems, dependencies, and recovery time; it is a planning method, not a force-sizing model ([NIST SP 1190 Volume II](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.1190v2.pdf)). FEMA's rapid visual screening methodology is a useful model for structured screening and professional escalation, but its pre-event seismic purpose cannot be treated as post-event flood or building-release evidence ([FEMA P-154](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/2020-07/fema_earthquakes_rapid-visual-screening-of-buildings-for-potential-seismic-hazards-a-handbook-third-edition-fema-p-154.pdf)). FHWA's safety-service patrol guidance exposes detection, traffic control, clearance, debris, communications, and coordination functions, but ordinary incident patrol productivity does not establish disaster route release ([FHWA](https://ops.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/fhwahop10014/s1.htm)).

### Readiness test

The first full-mission demonstration should run for 168 hours with a representative occupied population and should:

- remove one five-district service cluster;
- remove one route-assurance detachment and one collection squadron;
- deny commercial communications and cloud services;
- inject conflicting household and destination records;
- impose one power-source, one feeder, one water-island, and one software fault;
- require residents with wheelchairs, service animals, caregivers, medical devices, language needs, and refusal/consent changes to complete the same service chain;
- include independent building, route, clinical, environmental, rights, cyber, safety, and operational-test authorities; and
- end with destination admission, household reconciliation, property/benefit continuity, return or durable continuity, waste disposition, maintenance recovery, and a second-ready-force assessment.

Passing separate demonstrations of buses, microgrids, search teams, building inspection, route clearance, registration, or shelters cannot substitute for this mission thread.

## Research consequence

Pass 36 defined the `NCAC` requirement. This pass establishes its first formation consequence and changes the repository's frontier.

The next inquiry should not immediately draw a vehicle or scale `NCAC-35` nationally. It should produce a **preregistered second metropolitan campaign and a representative field experiment design** around the two linked failures that govern the cell: disconnected household continuity and building access. A different hazard and built form should test whether the `A2`/`S3` distinction survives outside the Harris-derived catchment.

Pass 38 supplies those designs. The [New York vertical heat-blackout replication protocol](../../campaigns/new-york-vertical-heat-blackout-replication-protocol.md) freezes `A2` and `S3` before outcome-blind geographic selection across tower, mid-rise and campus/service-friction forms. Pass 39's [`L0` catchment rehearsal](../../campaigns/new-york-l0-catchment-rehearsal.md) creates twelve provisional references and three commitments, but refuses formal freeze until street topology, independent reproduction and salted custody are complete. The [NCAC full-mission test program](../../innovation/ncac-full-mission-test-program.md) turns this paper's open gates into a staged evidence enterprise with 888 element units, 60 utility sequences, 48 occupied-district trials, twelve developmental full-cell missions and thirty confirmatory missions for a frozen selected candidate. It reports no performance outcome and closes no gate; it replaces an underspecified “demonstration” with a falsifiable research commitment.

In parallel, the program should:

- replace the 61-tract analytical catchment with named but non-selected site alternatives and building inventories;
- split the cell into alert, service-entry, expansion, sustainment, and relief echelons;
- produce occupation- and qualification-level tables of organization;
- configure prepared, mobile, and expeditionary district/transfer interfaces;
- fit household-contact, vertical-access, route-release, collection-cycle, utility-failure, and reconciliation distributions from trials;
- reopen the Harris campaign workforce and finance from these task-level results; and
- define industrial product families only after common cabins, access systems, utility blocks, route work cells, and data interfaces have earned `R3` evidence.

The mature force is now more ambitious and less platform-centric than the original thought experiment. At neighborhood scale, national resilience power is the ability to know who needs service, reach them through damaged buildings and streets, keep them safely in place where possible, move them completely where necessary, preserve their rights and continuity, and regenerate the formation to do it again. The equipment matters because it serves that chain. The chain—not the equipment—is the weapon system analogue.

No rendering is authorized. All twenty gates remain open. The immediate authorized research action is protocol registration and `L0` data rehearsal, not national scaling.
