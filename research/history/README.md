# Research history

This directory preserves discovery order without making chronology the structure of the argument.

The [research log](research-log.md) records each pass, its scope, changes, checks, and next frontier. The [supersession map](supersession-map.md) identifies the later finding that changed each important earlier result. The [pass termination method](pass-method.md) governs resolution, ledgers, five-pass branch budgets and mandatory human review. [Human Review HR-01](human-review-211-220.md) validates Passes 211–220, releases the first ten-pass halt and registers the next top-level question. [Pass findings](pass-findings.md) preserves the former cumulative strategic manuscript, whose pass-by-pass structure made discovery visible but became unsuitable as the main synthesis.

These records are valuable for lineage and audit. They are not automatically authoritative. Current judgments live in the [strategic study](../synthesis/strategic-study.md), the live research state is centralized in the [active frontier](../frontier.md), and technical authority remains with the bounded paper and model that own a decision.
