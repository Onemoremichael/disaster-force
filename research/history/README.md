# Research history

This directory preserves discovery order without making chronology the structure of the argument.

The [research log](research-log.md) records each pass, its scope, changes, checks, and next frontier. The [supersession map](supersession-map.md) identifies the later finding that changed each important earlier result. The [pass termination method](pass-method.md) governs resolution, ledgers, five-pass branch budgets and autonomous adversarial review. [Review HR-03](human-review-231-240.md) validates Passes 231–240 before [Registration `FR-04`](frontier-registration-post-edb-experimental-realization.md) opens experimental realization. Passes 241–245 take `EXR` from the immutable pre-`MAKE` record through topology, transaction, commissioning and one-shot adjudication, then return it with no open item. [Registration `FR-05`](frontier-registration-post-exr-strategic-synthesis.md) opens strategic synthesis. Passes [246](../synthesis/department-of-resilience-strategic-synthesis-architecture-and-completion-stocktake.md), [247](../synthesis/department-of-resilience-cross-chapter-claim-and-quantity-reconciliation.md), [248](../synthesis/department-of-resilience-invited-resilience-partnership-compact.md) and [249](../synthesis/department-of-resilience-integrated-strategic-manuscript-and-atlas-authority.md) establish the publication architecture, reconcile claims, close invited doctrine and rewrite the manuscript. [Pass 250](../synthesis/department-of-resilience-technical-visual-atlas-issuance-and-terminal-return.md) issues the governed atlas, closes 70 of 70 editorial gates and returns `SYN` to `FR-05`. [Review HR-04](human-review-241-250.md) validates both five-pass branches and closes the interval. [Pass findings](pass-findings.md) preserves the former cumulative strategic manuscript, whose chronology remains provenance rather than publication structure.

These records are valuable for lineage and audit. They are not automatically authoritative. Current judgments live in the [strategic study](../synthesis/strategic-study.md), the live research state is centralized in the [active frontier](../frontier.md), and technical authority remains with the bounded paper and model that own a decision.
