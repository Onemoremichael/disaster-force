# Research history

This directory preserves discovery order without making chronology the structure of the argument.

The [research log](research-log.md) records each pass, its scope, changes, checks, and next frontier. The [supersession map](supersession-map.md) identifies the later finding that changed each important earlier result. The [pass termination method](pass-method.md) governs resolution, ledgers, five-pass branch budgets and autonomous adversarial review. [Review HR-03](human-review-231-240.md) validates Passes 231–240 before [Registration `FR-04`](frontier-registration-post-edb-experimental-realization.md) opens experimental realization. Passes 241–245 take `EXR` from the immutable pre-`MAKE` record through topology, transaction, commissioning and one-shot adjudication, then return it with no open item. [Registration `FR-05`](frontier-registration-post-exr-strategic-synthesis.md) opens strategic synthesis, and [Pass 246](../synthesis/department-of-resilience-strategic-synthesis-architecture-and-completion-stocktake.md) replaces accumulation with fourteen publication chapters and seventy noncompensatory closure gates. [Pass findings](pass-findings.md) preserves the former cumulative strategic manuscript, whose pass-by-pass structure made discovery visible but became unsuitable as the main synthesis.

These records are valuable for lineage and audit. They are not automatically authoritative. Current judgments live in the [strategic study](../synthesis/strategic-study.md), the live research state is centralized in the [active frontier](../frontier.md), and technical authority remains with the bounded paper and model that own a decision.
