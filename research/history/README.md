# Research history

This directory preserves discovery order without making chronology the structure of the argument.

The [research log](research-log.md) records each pass, its scope, changes, checks, and next frontier. The [supersession map](supersession-map.md) identifies the later finding that changed each important earlier result. The [pass termination method](pass-method.md) governs resolution, ledgers, five-pass branch budgets and autonomous adversarial review. [Review HR-01](human-review-211-220.md) validates Passes 211–220, [Review HR-02](human-review-221-230.md) validates Passes 221–230 while replacing external approval with a non-blocking research-lead checkpoint, and [Review HR-03](human-review-231-240.md) validates Passes 231–240 before [Registration `FR-04`](frontier-registration-post-edb-experimental-realization.md) opens experimental realization. [Pass 241](../force/generation/department-of-resilience-cohort-one-experiment-definition-and-pre-make-issue-constitution.md) closes the pre-`MAKE` experiment-record constitution, [Pass 242](../force/generation/department-of-resilience-cohort-one-campaign-topology-and-release-sequence-constitution.md) closes physical topology, and [Pass 243](../force/generation/department-of-resilience-cohort-one-simultaneous-make-and-public-title-transaction-constitution.md) closes the atomic `MAKE`, public-title and performance-blind industrial transaction. `EXR` now moves to site-to-event commissioning. [Pass findings](pass-findings.md) preserves the former cumulative strategic manuscript, whose pass-by-pass structure made discovery visible but became unsuitable as the main synthesis.

These records are valuable for lineage and audit. They are not automatically authoritative. Current judgments live in the [strategic study](../synthesis/strategic-study.md), the live research state is centralized in the [active frontier](../frontier.md), and technical authority remains with the bounded paper and model that own a decision.
