# Research history

This directory preserves discovery order without making chronology the structure of the argument.

The [research log](research-log.md) records each pass, its scope, changes, checks, and next frontier. [Pass findings](pass-findings.md) preserves the former cumulative strategic manuscript, whose pass-by-pass structure made supersession visible but became unsuitable as the main synthesis.

These records are valuable for lineage and audit. They are not automatically authoritative. Current judgments live in the [strategic study](../synthesis/strategic-study.md), and current technical authority remains with the bounded paper and model that own a decision.
