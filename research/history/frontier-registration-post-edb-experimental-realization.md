# Frontier Registration FR-04 — Experimental Realization

**Registration:** `FR-04`  
**Branch:** `EXR`  
**Status:** open before Pass 241  
**Budget:** at most five numbered passes

## Registered parent question

Can the Department convert six `BUILD-TO-TEST / UNMEASURED` Engineering Definition Baselines and USD25.0B of planning exposure into an executable, publicly reconstructable proof campaign that issues, makes, instruments, breaks, cold-rebuilds and adjudicates 144 nonfungible articles without allowing design completion, builder identity, cost or imagery to become evidence?

This question passes the resolution test. Reversal would either authorize physical construction without a frozen falsifiable object or prevent the first cohort from entering physical realization, changing the Department's first proof wave, industrial mobilization and evidence constitution.

## First registered item

Which immutable Experiment Definition Record, serial-title law, configuration freeze, instrumentation chain, receiver-denominator record, intervention boundary, abort-and-repair law, public disclosure packet and conflict check must close before any of the 144 articles can move from `PROGRAMMED / UNBUILT` to `MAKE-ELIGIBLE`; and which defect must hold or terminate issue?

`EXR` begins with one open item and zero consumed passes. Its fifth pass must `CLOSE`, `KILL` or `RETURN` to the registered parent question above.

## Boundary

- `MAKE-ELIGIBLE` is a preconstruction state. It is not an award, obligation, notice to proceed, built serial or accepted result.
- Every `INT`, `BRK` and `RCN` serial retains distinct title, configuration ancestry and permissible history.
- The Resilience Proving Service, not a candidate builder, controls receiver denominators, intervention worlds, raw-data custody and acceptance.
- USD25.0B remains a planning exposure. The four Pass 184 jurisdictions remain purpose-bound.
- `REP-2` article, fixture and integration-section plates may issue only as inseparable parts of the registered experimental record. `REP-3` retains the three-hypothesis law. `REP-4` through `REP-7` remain held.

## Early termination route

Kill `EXR` and return to the registered parent if no pre-`MAKE` record can keep the tested proposition immutable across builder, proving and reconstruction actors; if serial title or beneficial control cannot be independently resolved before cost exposure; or if a visual, administrative or financial state must be treated as evidence to mobilize the campaign.

Registration opens one item, closes none and sets the running open total to one. `HR-03` resets the consecutive no-`KILL` counter to zero. No rendering, obligation, construction, observation, evidence, selection, production or readiness authority is created here.
