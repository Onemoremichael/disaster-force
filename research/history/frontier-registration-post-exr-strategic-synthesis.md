## Boundary

- Pass count, file count and formula count are not measures of completion.
- A chapter is publication-ready only when its requirement, architecture, quantitative boundary, cross-chapter integration and reader-facing asset all close.
- A planning model, design constitution or internally reconciled number remains labeled as such. Synthesis cannot promote it into an observation, forecast, acquisition baseline or measured capability.
- Superseded findings remain traceable but do not receive equal narrative weight with controlling decisions.
- Technical renderings remain held until the owning systems have first-principles requirements, causal topology, bounded configuration and an explicit representation class.
- The strategic study remains the publication spine; the research frontier remains the live decision edge; the history remains the audit trail.
