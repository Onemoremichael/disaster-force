# Mandate

The mandate layer asks two questions in order: **what must remain true for people after catastrophic failure, and who is sovereignly accountable for making it true?** It refuses to begin with an agency, hazard program or platform.

The analytical chain starts with [first principles](first-principles.md), bounds the observed problem in the [empirical baseline](empirical-baseline.md), replaces the average event with [design reference missions](design-reference-missions.md), and gives the promise a measurable vocabulary in the [service-level library](service-level-library.md). The [mission-engineering method](mission-engineering-method.md) traces every service from source to safely used effect; the [force-design framework](force-design-framework.md) translates concurrent demand into formations, readiness, movement, reception, sustainment and regeneration.

Only after that chain is explicit does [Department of Resilience](department-of-resilience.md) derive the cabinet institution, seven operating services, commands, professions, acquisition and science system, components and civil-authority rules. The [National Utility Projection System](national-utility-projection-system.md) is the first strategic-function test of whether that institutional structure can turn water and power modules into assured national capacity. The [research program](research-program.md) defines how the whole inquiry must mature.

The mature organizational answer begins with the [Department constitutional order and national-power state machine](department-of-resilience-constitutional-order-and-national-power-state-machine.md). Five coequal houses and seventeen typed warrants replace a single descending command hierarchy: political direction, public effect, mission integration, professional release, industrial rivalry, independent proof, readiness custody and lawful employment remain separately sovereign. The [domestic public-service sovereignty and activation compact](department-of-resilience-domestic-public-service-sovereignty-and-activation-compact.md) makes the guaranteed mode executable through a dual request/objective-breach trigger, receiver custody, direct tribal and territorial paths, a nonterritorial service easement and a six-day local bridge. The [workforce, service-citizenship and reserve compact](department-of-resilience-workforce-service-citizenship-and-reserve-compact.md) now constitutes a Civil Service Commonwealth: voluntary paid entry, seven civil statuses, twelve rights, ten duties and eleven separated personnel warrants turn 13.0M affiliations into bounded professional duty without conscription, contractor readiness or military identity. The next mandate resolution is Department public knowledge, sensing, forecast, data rights and civil privacy.

Mandate documents own requirements, authority and method. They do not certify technical performance or admit platforms.
