## Branch and resolution

The deepest live branch is `DCA-5`, the fifth and final permitted Department constitutional-architecture pass.

**Resolution test:** reversing this compact would replace or abandon the five-house Department, change who can create every public-service state and reopen whether constructive civil power is a constitutional instrument or one executive command; the object passes at Department scale.

The branch must now return. Four passes constituted the Department, domestic duty, its people and its knowledge. This pass determines whether those compacts compose one institution or merely coexist as names.[^1]

## Decision: a warranted constitutional compact

The mature Department shall be a **warranted constitutional compact**. Five coequal houses exchange seventeen typed public-service warrants. The domestic covenant, Civil Service Commonwealth, Public Reality Commons, force-generation compact and Strategic Power Compact add subordinate predicates to those warrants; none becomes a sovereign organization above or beside them.

Four alternatives fail. A unified department lets direction manufacture fact, readiness and success. A council of houses preserves separation but turns every incident into negotiation and makes silence a veto. An automated constitutional compiler mistakes a representation of law for authority. An emergency integration command makes exception the real constitution. The selected form instead uses one rule: **integration is artifact succession, not organizational coordination**. A downstream actor receives a bounded warrant, not the upstream actor’s power.

The five houses remain:

1. **Civic Covenant:** public guarantee, deficit, effect contract and lawful fiscal option.
2. **Capability Commonwealth:** mission baseline, rival controlled roots, professional technical release and production availability.
3. **Evidence Court:** adverse proof, civil rights, remedy and public reason.
4. **Force Estate:** formation, readiness, conservation, allocation and regeneration.
5. **Employment Federation:** domestic or invited activation, complete service, civil acceptance, transfer and exit.

The Secretary owns priority, budget proposal, convening, disclosure, certified alternates and suspension of unsafe work. The office cannot certify performance, declare readiness, override receiver or host custody, turn a forecast into activation, retain an expired warrant or convert output into effect. Political responsibility is therefore broad; epistemic and operational self-certification are not.

## One Department, not a second Department hidden in its compacts

The seventeen-warrant machine remains the only top-level state sequence: enacted guarantee; service-deficit order; effect contract; fiscal option; mission baseline; controlled root; technical release; evidence disposition; production availability; formation warrant; readiness state; allocation order; lawful activation; campaign service; civil acceptance and transfer; effect disposition; regenerated serial.

The three newer compacts refine that sequence. Thirteen domestic warrants govern breach, receiver routes, easement and extinction. Eleven personnel warrants prevent affiliation, pay, license or availability from becoming a ready crew. Twelve knowledge warrants keep observation, protected query, forecast, warning and operational use distinct. They create no eighteenth Department warrant and no new institution class.

Every top-level warrant has one issuer, one receiver, an adverse authority, a clock, rollback and a statutory same-scope alternate. Silence holds the last valid state. Expiry removes present authority without erasing history. Incapacitation activates the alternate; powers do not migrate. Emergency may reorder certified capacity or choose a certified fallback but cannot waive proof, rights, readiness or sovereign consent.

This compact also resolves the supposed trade between speed and separation. Preparation is permanent: 5,251 formations, 3,667 ready serials, 1,008 domestic receiver routes, 1,350 invited corridor claims and disconnected civil-state nodes exist before an event. Constitutional friction applies to state creation, not to improvising an institution after impact. The correct military analogy is not a larger incident staff. It is standing logistics, trained formations, rehearsed authorities and precomputed branches whose use still requires a lawful release.

## People, knowledge, force and economy

The Department retains 13.0 million affiliated positions: 5.57 million national career, 2.94 million ready reserve, 2.43 million sovereign covenant, 2.07 million productive service and 20,700 partner fellows. Service citizenship is voluntary paid civil membership with enforceable duty and nonwaivable labor, professional and rights protections. It is not nationality, conscription or military status.[^2]

The Public Reality Commons remains a distributed utility, not an intelligence service. Open physical reality, protected civil state, ephemeral service state and sealed evidence stay nonfungible. Three unlike forecast roots must preserve disagreement. A model may inform a published threshold; it cannot issue a warning or activation. Missing observation remains missing, and unobserved effect remains `UNKNOWN`.[^3]

The force retains 5,251 formations: 2,420 committed, 502 protected reserve, 745 geographic buffer and 1,584 in requalification, maintenance or overhaul. Domestic continuity holds 1,745 commitments and invited stabilization 675. Forward partnership and productive statecraft gain no U.S. readiness credit from partner capacity or market output.[^4]

The fiscal constitution changes no predecessor total. Three public accounts add: **USD 1.00T technology**, **USD 17.4T capital** and **USD 50.0T ramped operations**, producing **USD 68.4T** over twenty-five years. The USD 6.19T Public Reality Commons, domestic covenant, receiver commons, activation system and evidence system are overlapping jurisdictional views inside those accounts. Adding them again would be a constitutional error, not conservatism. Markets remain separate and never net appropriations or create readiness.

The fifteen-sheet [model](../../models/programmatics/department-of-resilience-constitutional-compact-and-return.xlsx) instantiates all five houses, seventeen warrants and institution classes, the compact registry, authority matrix, force and workforce conservation, nested-account treatment, twenty-five-year path, twelve stress worlds and eight invalid mutations. Twenty-eight terminal audits pass after independent office recalculation; one native chart survives export; the formula-error scan is empty. Every identity, observation, warrant and effect remains source-empty. Implementation authority and effect credit are zero. No site, serial, platform exterior or rendering is released.
