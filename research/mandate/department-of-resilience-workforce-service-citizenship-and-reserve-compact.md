## Decision: a Civil Service Commonwealth

Six architectures face twelve conjunctive tests. An all-federal career corps cannot preserve sovereign employment or variable depth. Contractor call options create no public duty. Universal conscription violates voluntary civil membership and professional refusal. A state-militia federation lacks national portability and direct tribal standing. A military reserve analogue imports command justice and coercive identity. Only a **Civil Service Commonwealth** joins scale, civil status, sovereign plurality, labor rights, professional independence, mobilization duty and family continuity.

“Service citizenship” is membership in a public profession, never nationality, immigration status or a superior class of civil rights. Entry is voluntary and paid. Accepted membership creates enforceable obligations; mobilization is not optional after a valid warrant. Discipline remains civil, labor and professional law—never court-martial, rank justice or weapons authority.

Seven statuses compose the Commonwealth:

1. the National Career Service;
2. the National Ready Reserve;
3. state and territorial Covenant Services;
4. Tribal Sovereign Services, direct and not state-subordinate;
5. the Productive Commonwealth Service;
6. invited Partner Fellows, confined to bilateral missions; and
7. learners and civil auxiliaries, who receive training and protection but create no readiness credit.

The inherited 13.0M affiliated establishment resolves into 5.57M national-career positions, 2.94M ready-reserve positions, 2.43M combined sovereign-covenant positions, 2.07M productive-service positions and 20,700 partner-fellow positions. Tribal, state and territorial allocations inside the covenant account remain source-empty; affiliation is not authority. The 44,400-person industrial learner pipeline is nested, not added.[^2]

## One membership, separated warrants

No employer may manufacture professional or operational authority. Eleven warrants separate application; membership; occupational license; Department qualification; billet assignment; fitness and household continuity; availability; formation manifest; mobilization; campaign employment; and release/requalification. A formation is ready only when every required role holds the current chain. Payroll, a badge, a professional license or an available person is not a crew.

The rights charter binds every status: voluntary entry; equal protection; collective representation; due process and appeal; lawful and unsafe-order refusal; professional truth; transparent compensation; health and injury coverage; dependent care, housing and relocation; employment return; privacy; and dignified separation. Ordinary collective action remains protected. Once an exact event warrant is accepted, continuity arbitration and safe handoff govern interruption; no person can be imprisoned into service, and the institution—not the individual—owns the duty to field a qualified replacement.

The duty covenant requires truthful credentials, recurring qualification, declared availability, lawful mobilization, receiver custody, evidence preservation, noncoercion, regeneration and return. A reserve call may expose at most 365 duty days in any rolling 730-day window. Every campaign employment warrant still expires at thirty days and requires fresh legislative review after day 180. Productive-service mobilization activates a production or repair duty, not field command; field employment requires a second qualification. Partner Fellows cannot be used domestically or recalled from an accepted invited mission outside the bilateral release law.

## Mass without militarization

The Commonwealth is organized by profession and service, not rank. Portable public credentials survive employer failure. Independent professional colleges set competence; services generate crews; the Joint Capacity Authority admits readiness; NMAC schedules only valid manifests; receivers admit work; safety and rights authorities can stop it; unions represent members; and civil courts and a Service Rights Tribunal provide remedy. Command is temporary authority over an admitted task, not ownership of a person.

The six-day domestic continuity bridge now has a workforce consequence: each covenant receiver must maintain locally callable qualified crews, while the national force closes the inherited maximum 5.5-day arrival gap. Neither a federal employee count nor a volunteer registry can satisfy that bridge.[^3]

Technology becomes constitutional infrastructure: privacy-preserving credential wallets; reconstructable skill and crew graphs; household-continuity twins; fatigue and exposure governors; offline mobilization ledgers; robotic training ranges; multilingual mixed-reality qualification; causal skill-decay models; portable-benefit clearing; and adversarial labor-rights simulation. These are major national systems, not human-resources software.

## The workforce changes the fiscal constitution

The inherited component mix and loaded billet-year factors yield a mature people-and-household floor of about **USD 1.36T/year**. Pass 193’s top-down forty-percent people share allowed only USD 844B/year and is retired. Preserving its non-personnel readiness floor of USD 1.27T/year produces revised mature operations of about **USD 2.63T/year**.

Applying the inherited 19.05 mature-year ramp raises twenty-five-year operations from USD 40.2T to about **USD 50.0T**. Technology and capital remain USD 1.00T and USD 17.4T, so the Department public-outlay screen becomes about **USD 68.4T**. A ±20-percent loaded-rate sensitivity gives USD 63.2–73.6T. These are constant-2026-dollar planning screens, not appropriations or wage forecasts. The alternative is a hollow force whose machines, maintenance and people are each budgeted by assuming the others are cheaper.
