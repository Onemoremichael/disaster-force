## Decision

The Department shall be a **constitutional national-power matrix**: five coequal houses exchange typed warrants, and no institution owns both a state and the state that validates it. This closes the whole-department organizational form.

It supersedes the top-level `Department → Establishment → Force → Effect` diagram wherever that diagram implies one descending chain of command. Those remain useful capability states, not sovereign organizational layers. Four alternatives fail conjunctive tests:

- A unified executive department lets political direction become technical fact, readiness and retained success.
- Seven sovereign services preserve professions but fragment complete public-service chains and joint employment.
- Six mission-system agencies preserve integration but absorb professions, industrial roots and their own evidence.
- A hazard or regional federation duplicates permanent capacity, cannot conserve national readiness and makes geography an authority source.

## Five houses, seventeen institution classes

1. **Civic Covenant** owns public obligation and lawful scarcity: Secretary and Resilience Staff; Civil Resilience Authority; nine Civilian Effect Trustees; Department Capital Board.
2. **Capability Commonwealth** creates reproducible options: six Mission System Commands; seven Generating Services; twenty-four Root Houses; Industrial Mobilization Administration.
3. **Evidence Court** makes adverse truth durable: Resilience Proving Service; Rights and Scarcity Tribunal; Public Allocation Ledger.
4. **Force Estate** converts accepted capability into conserved readiness: Strategic Sustainment Command; Joint Capacity Authority; National Mobilization and Allocation Command; seven Readiness District Commands.
5. **Employment Federation** governs use and exit: Operational Campaign Commands formed for admitted events; Sovereign Compact and Custody Chamber.

The Secretary remains politically responsible. The office sets portfolio priority, submits budgets, convenes the houses, compels disclosure, orders independent alternates and suspends unsafe employment. It cannot certify performance, declare readiness, override civil or host consent, recall an accepted invited serial, count partner capacity as U.S. readiness or preserve a failed branch for prestige. This is not weak executive leadership; it is leadership denied the power to manufacture facts.

## National-power state machine

Seventeen warrants join the houses: enacted guarantee; service-deficit order; effect contract; fiscal option; mission baseline; controlled root; technical release; evidence disposition; production availability; formation warrant; readiness state; allocation order; lawful activation; campaign service; civil acceptance and transfer; effect disposition; regenerated serial.

Each warrant names an issuer, receiver, negative authority, sufficient claim, rollback and expiry. Silence holds the last valid state. Disagreement returns to the exact warrant. Expiry removes present authority without erasing history. Emergency may reorder certified capacity or invoke a certified fallback; it cannot waive proof, rights, readiness or consent. Incapacitation activates a statutory alternate for the same warrant—powers do not migrate.

Eighteen nondelegation laws make the architecture executable. Appropriation is not capability; mission ownership is not technical release; industry is not formation; formation is not readiness; readiness is not allocation; claim is not activation; access is not command; output is not effect; partner capacity is not U.S. readiness; revenue is not appropriation; history is not current authority. These laws prevent any successful constituency from becoming the Department.

## Integrated operating and fiscal constitution

The matrix inherits rather than reopens the mature topology: six mission systems integrate forty-eight formation families; seven services preserve professional and technical depth; nine effect ledgers retain independent civil acceptance; four strategic modes distinguish domestic guarantee, invited stabilization, forward partnership and productive statecraft. Twenty-two active mission-system/service pairs reconcile all 2,420 committed formation demands inside 5,251 owned formations.[^1]

The fiscal state is equally non-fungible. The USD 1.00T technology program, USD 17.4T mature capital and about USD 40.2T ramped operations produce about USD 58.6T of twenty-five-year public outlay. A separate USD 1.31T useful-output market screen does not net appropriations or create readiness. Nested receiver, proving, activation and measurement views remain analytical cross-classifications, not new spending.[^2]

The accompanying twenty-one-sheet model tests five architectures, all seventeen warrants, eighteen nondelegation laws, eight deadlock rules and twelve constitutional stress cases. Twenty-two terminal reconciliations pass; five deliberately invalid mutations fail and are restored. Institution identities, warrant evidence and effect claims remain source-empty. Released implementation authority and strategic effect are zero. No site, platform exterior or rendering is authorized.
