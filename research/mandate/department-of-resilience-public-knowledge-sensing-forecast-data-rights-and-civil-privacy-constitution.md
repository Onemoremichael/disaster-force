## Decision: a Public Reality Commons

The Department shall operate a **Public Reality Commons**: a distributed civil measurement, forecast and warning utility whose products can inform action but never supply action authority. Five alternatives fail fourteen conjunctive tests. A resilience intelligence agency joins collection, inference and executive use. One national digital twin turns model convenience into a common mode and hides disagreement. Service-owned silos make the same person, place and outage change truth at an organizational boundary. A commercial fusion cloud makes public duty dependent on private custody and secondary use. Open-data absolutism protects scrutiny by exposing people, critical systems and sovereign knowledge.

The Commons instead separates four nonfungible planes:

1. **Open reality** holds physics-first environmental, infrastructure, capacity and public-effect observations, methods, coverage and uncertainty. Public funding creates a presumption of public release; exact safety-sensitive detail may be delayed or bounded.
2. **Protected civil state** holds identifiable person, household, relationship, health, disability, property and community records with the originating tribal, territorial, state, local or person-authorized custodian. Approved computations travel to data; raw records do not travel to a national lake.
3. **Ephemeral service state** contains the minimum incident token needed to find, admit, serve, reunify or transfer a person or asset. It is tied to one easement and expires with that purpose.
4. **Sealed evidence** preserves methods, warrants, model versions, decisions, uncertainty and minimized proof for independent replay and remedy. It cannot be searched as an operational population file.

The Commons is not a sixth house or a hidden command. Its roles are functions assigned across the existing seventeen institution classes, not a new sovereign class. Source custodians observe; public observatories maintain physical fields; sovereign civil-data trusts answer bounded queries; three unlike model roots produce each decision-grade forecast; Civilian Effect Trustees own effect and breach judgments; warning authorities issue warnings; the Resilience Proving Service reproduces methods; the Rights and Scarcity Tribunal governs protected use and remedy; NMAC and campaign commands consume only released products. No actor may collect, model, warn and activate.

## The civil knowledge contract

Within the inherited seventeen-warrant Department state machine, twelve subordinate knowledge warrants separate source charter, observation, protected collection, linkage, model qualification, forecast issue, public warning, protected query, service-token issue, mobilization recommendation, operational use and evidence retention or destruction. They refine existing Department transitions rather than add sovereign powers. Every product names its source ancestry, purpose, population and geography, clock, model version, uncertainty, missing coverage, known failure domains, issuer, permitted receivers, expiry and challenge path.

A probability is not a fact; a forecast is not a warning; a warning is not activation. Decision thresholds are published separately from models so an operator cannot tune a model until a desired action appears scientific. Forecasts retain the unlike roots and disagreement; averaging cannot erase a dissenting tail. Every warning later receives a public score against what became observable, including false alarms, misses, lead time, subgroup reach and action burden. An unobserved outcome remains `UNKNOWN`, never zero.

Fourteen civil data rights bind all modes: warning without compulsory identification; service without a permanent dossier; minimum necessary collection; source and inference visibility; correction; contest and human review; uncertainty disclosure; purpose and time limits; direct tribal and territorial custody; voluntary consent outside a constituted public duty; protected relationships; no penalty for data absence; deletion of operative copies; and remedy. The evidence plane may preserve a content-minimized receipt after deletion, but not reconstruct the expired record.

Fifteen nonconversion laws enforce the boundary. Observation is not identity; location is not personhood; association is not consent; missing data is not low need; model fit is not legitimacy; forecast is not fact; warning is not command; need score is not eligibility; service token is not identity credential; emergency is not secondary-use permission; openness is not protected-data release; commercial access is not title; security restriction is not adverse-evidence suppression; retained evidence is not operative reuse; accuracy is not authority. Law-enforcement, immigration, political, insurance-pricing, advertising and unrelated eligibility reuse are prohibited rather than balanced against convenience.

## A defense-scale knowledge instrument

The inherited domestic topology becomes a measurable obligation. Seven district observatories maintain 504 district–campaign–effect forecast cells. Three unlike roots create 1,512 decision-grade forecast paths. The 1,008 primary and alternate receiver routes receive sovereign query enclaves; 336 family–district interfaces receive common semantics; all 5,251 formations receive disconnected mission-state nodes. These are jurisdictional slots, not claims that an installation or model exists.[^2]

About 600,000 knowledge positions are cross-classified inside the 13.0M Commonwealth: formation detachments, receiver and trust seams, effect-forecast teams, family integration, root lineage, district observatories and common rights, proving and communications functions. Affiliation, a sensor feed or a model artifact creates no operational credit.

The technology program is intentionally ambitious: identity-avoiding sensors that measure hazard rather than people; recipient-held emergency beacons; query-to-data computation with publicly verifiable execution; disconnected probabilistic state compilation; machine-readable uncertainty and negative-coverage fields; three-root public model foundries; cryptographically enforced purpose expiry; sovereign offline data trusts; causal warning simulators; and adversarial knowledge ranges able to inject spoofing, missing communities, compromised models and unlawful queries. The system must make ignorance visible and misuse physically difficult, not merely promise responsible artificial intelligence.

The companion [workbook](../../models/programmatics/department-of-resilience-public-reality-commons.xlsx) establishes a nested base jurisdiction of about **USD 175B technology**, **USD 680B capital** and **USD 280B/year** at maturity. Applying the inherited 19.05 mature-year ramp yields about **USD 6.19T** across twenty-five years, roughly 9.05 percent of the Department’s USD 68.4T public-outlay screen. A low–high design range is USD 5.20–7.69T. Nothing is added to the Department total: the model forces explicit reservations inside the USD 1.00T technology, USD 17.4T capital, USD 2.63T mature-operation and 13.0M people accounts.[^3]

The workbook tests the four planes, twelve warrants, fourteen rights, fifteen nonconversions, forecast-root obligations, workforce, capital, operations, twenty-five-year economy, twelve stress worlds and six deliberate mutations. Every identity, observation, model, query, warning and service token remains source-empty. Released knowledge, activation and effect credit are zero. No site or platform exterior is authorized.
