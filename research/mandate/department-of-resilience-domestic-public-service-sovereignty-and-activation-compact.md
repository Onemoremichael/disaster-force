## Decision

Guaranteed domestic continuity shall operate through a **federally guaranteed, receiver-governed public-service covenant**. The floor attaches to people and nine nonfungible civil effects; it does not attach to a governor’s request or transfer general jurisdiction over a place. A receiver request is sufficient but not necessary. An independently verified objective breach may also open the transaction.

Four alternatives fail twelve conjunctive tests. A governor-request system lets an absent, failed or unwilling executive extinguish a national duty. Automatic federal preemption preserves the duty by destroying civil custody. Incident-by-incident bargaining starts after the clock and fails under receiver incapacity. A voluntary federation makes equal protection depend on prior opt-in. The selected covenant alone preserves an equal floor, non-veto activation, state/local custody, direct tribal sovereignty, territorial equality, incapacity continuity, rights, expiry and district-loss survival.

This closes the gap left by Passes 167 and 196: “constitutional duty” and “domestic allocation” become an executable transaction rather than labels.[^1]

## The compact

Ten articles govern domestic employment:

1. Every person in a state, tribe or territory holds the same continuity floor.
2. Receiver authority, interfaces, remedies and alternates are constituted before an event.
3. Tribal nations and territories possess direct sovereign paths; state permission is unnecessary.
4. Either authenticated receiver request or verified objective breach may trigger action.
5. Civilian Effect Trustees—not executives or operators—verify the breach.
6. A **nonterritorial public-service easement** binds authority to named people, effects, interfaces, routes and time.
7. A capable receiver selects among safe methods, owns admission/custody and may stop an unsafe act, but cannot extinguish the floor.
8. Rights, burden, priority, access, compensation and remedy remain independently reviewable.
9. Receiver incapacity transfers only the exact unavailable service function for the exact clock.
10. Civil acceptance, restitution, transfer, evidence and regeneration extinguish federal employment.

The easement carries no general police, land-use, zoning, electoral or governing power. Security support remains separately warranted. Evacuation capacity does not confer coercive removal authority. Functional receivership does not replace a polity. Success creates neither title nor permanent jurisdiction.

## Activation and extinction

Four paths enter the same fourteen-state machine. A receiver request reaches full campaign authority within six hours. Independently verified breach has a twelve-hour ceiling. Receiver incapacity has an eight-hour ceiling. Contested refusal plus imminent nonfungible harm can release a pre-certified bridge by hour four, but full service still requires a Rights and Scarcity Tribunal necessity/easement warrant.

Thirteen domestic warrants join standing covenant, preparedness, watch, request, breach, incapacity, deficit, provisional bridge, easement, allocation, employment, civil acceptance/transfer and regeneration. Every warrant has an issuer, receiver, sufficient claim, rollback and expiry. Campaign and easement warrants expire after thirty days. Six consecutive renewals require fresh legislative review at day 180; repeated executive renewal cannot become permanent jurisdiction.

Sixteen prohibited conversions enforce the boundary: guarantee is not police power; breach is not automatic command; request is not technical truth; refusal is not extinction; incapacity is not government replacement; easement is not land title; emergency is not waiver; delivered output is not acceptance; acceptance is not strategic effect; scarcity is not weighted cancellation of a nonfungible floor.

## The six-day domestic bridge

The inherited eight domestic campaigns contain 1,745 committed formations.[^2] Adding the twelve-hour objective-breach ceiling to their preparation and transit clocks, then subtracting usable warning, produces post-onset national-service gaps of zero to 5.5 days. Cascadia is controlling. The covenant therefore rounds the local continuity obligation upward to a **six-day bridge**.

This is not household self-reliance substituting for national capacity. It is a public receiver estate: continuity islands, bridge stocks, service interfaces, accessible household reach, offline authority and pre-certified operators that preserve immediate water, sanitation, health, thermal safety, communications, hazard control and civil coordination until national formations arrive.

Seven readiness districts and eight campaigns create fifty-six district–campaign cells. Nine effects expand them to 504 deficit registers and 1,008 primary/alternate receiver paths. Forty-eight families across seven districts require 336 family–district interfaces and 672 dual seams. These are design obligations, not installed assets.
